# Release to the DM: the landing

Campaign: sim-03. Year 0, the evening of the arrival; Year 1 opens from it. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-21. Everything the Understory senses and knows at the moment it stands up in this world, and nothing else. Every distance, direction and sight line here is measured from the map. The first morning story and its painting are written from this file, per the first-morning section of `engine/rules/morning-story.md`.

## The moment

Evening, in autumn. The light is going. It has rained lately and will again.

## The body's senses

- Underfoot: deep wet leaf litter over living soil, cold, giving a little. Bare feet. Pale rock breaks the surface a few paces off, slick with wet.
- Smell: rot and rain, leaf mold, wet stone, fungus. Woodsmoke on the air, thin, from the south-east.
- Sound: drip from the canopy. Water running a short way and then going down into the ground with a hollow note. No voices. No beasts close by.
- Sight: very large old trees, fallen ones lying where they fell with young growth coming up through them. Fungi up everywhere, on the logs and through the litter. Hollows and sinkholes among the trees, some dry, some with water standing at the bottom, one or two too deep to see into. Through the gaps in the canopy, cloud breaking up, and where it breaks, high overhead, one small pale blue light, steady.
- Touch and weight: the body is whole, unhurt and unhurried. It is one body and it feels each of its edges.

## What its powers tell it

Nothing yet. It has no tended ground and no tended line, so the ground-ear is silent and there is nowhere to send a slow word from. Its hands in this soil would feel that the soil is alive.

## Its sense of others like it

It knows that others like it came down when it did. It feels nothing of any of them: no direction, no nearness, no weight. That sense comes only face to face, and no one is near.

## What it remembers

What its backstory outline holds and no more. The rest is fog.

## What is in view

A tall person's eye here sees level ground out to about 3 miles where nothing stands in the way; in forest it sees things at ground level within about half a mile.

- A worn way, wide enough for two abreast, a few hundred yards to the south. About a mile of it is in view, running from the north-west to the south-east. Beside it, coppiced stools and cut stumps; in the clearings, turf grazed short.
- Smoke, about 2 miles to the south-east: a town's, by the amount of it. The town itself is not in view.

Nothing else is in view.

## What it does not know

Who made the way or the smoke. The names of the people and the town: the player left both unnamed, so there is no name to keep back in an (oog) line, and none is to be supplied. Anything about its three designed mortals beyond what the Day 3 record holds; none of them has seen it, hears it or serves it. Anything its people know of the country: that reaches it when one of them tells it.

## What it carries

As its card has it: linen, a cloth bag, seed in twists of cloth, a sealed crock of starter, a short bone-handled knife. All ordinary.
