# The steward (unnamed)

Campaign: sim-03. Created by the player on creation Day 3, 2026-11-20. Unnamed at the player's wish: "I'd like to learn their names in play, from them, the way a stranger would."

Who she is, in the player's words: "An older woman, a gravekeeper and orchard keeper by trade, who runs my ground when I'm in the earth. She is pragmatic, sharp, and tired of people. She wants her ground to outlast her, and she is not sure she wants to be a steward at all. She is the only person orders travel through, and she carries them to the council as advice, never command."

At landing: "a woman at her work who has never met me." She does not know the god exists. She holds no office under it.

The steward's pressing (`player/power-card.md`, added Day 3) is hers alone, always her choice, and sleeps until she knows who it is for. The god will not ask it of her.

What she will do is hers. Nothing here says she takes the office.
