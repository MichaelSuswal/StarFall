# The track warden (unnamed)

Campaign: sim-03. Created by the player on creation Day 3, 2026-11-20. Unnamed at the player's wish.

Who he is, in the player's words: "A blunt former hunter who keeps the paths and the watch. He distrusts anything that doesn't walk on the ground, and so he'll distrust me. He wants the town safe more than he wants my approval. He's the one who will hear first that something is wrong at the edges."

At landing he does not know the god exists. The god will not use the steward's place to hide anything from him.

What he will do is his. That he distrusts the god, and that he hears first of trouble, are the player's hopes, heard and not promised.
