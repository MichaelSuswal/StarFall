# The unnamed people: starting state

Campaign: sim-03. Population as agreed on creation Day 3, 2026-11-20. Stores, treasure, trade, strength and mood are the Keeper's to set on Night 3a.

population: about 5,000, in one town and the farms and clearings around it
stores: the autumn is nearly in. Cured mushrooms, roots, orchard fruit, a little grain from the gardens and more bought, and smoked pork to come when the mast is done: enough for an ordinary winter, thin by the thaw if it runs long. Firewood and charcoal are stacked high.
treasure: small. Stacked charcoal, cured mushrooms, seed in twists kept by the houses, a little worked bronze. Nothing held in common but the track and the watch.
trade: small. Charcoal, wood and cured mushrooms go out by the track; salt and grain come in, the salt dear. Fewer buyers came this autumn than usual.
strength: no war band. A watch of about 40 on the paths, and about 300 who hunt and could hold a path with bow and spear if the council called them.
mood: careful and a little grim, as always. Busy with the mast and the last cutting. No one is waiting for anything.

Stores, treasure, trade, strength and mood were set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a, 2026-11-20, as the state of the people on the evening the god comes down.
