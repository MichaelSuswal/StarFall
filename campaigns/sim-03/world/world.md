# The world, as the Understory knows it on waking

Campaign: sim-03. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-21, for the DM, in words the player may read. Public facts only: what this god can see and know where it stands on the first evening, and what the agreed record of its people already holds. Everything else is learned in play.

## The season

Autumn. Every year of this campaign opens in autumn.

## The land as it is first seen

Measured from the map. The landing is in grid cell B4.

Old forest on broken pale limestone: very large old trees, fallen ones lying where they fell, deep wet leaf litter, ribs and ledges of pale rock, sinkholes, streams that run a while and go into the ground. Low hill country, between about 300 and 1,100 feet. The climate is temperate and wet, with a hard winter and a late spring.

From where the Understory stands, a tall person's eye sees level ground out to about 3 miles where nothing stands in the way, and in forest like this sees things at ground level within about half a mile.

In view:
- A worn way, wide enough for two to walk abreast, a few hundred yards to the south. About a mile of it shows, running from the north-west to the south-east.
- Near it, coppiced stools and cut stumps, and turf grazed short in the clearings.

Farther off, seen only as smoke:
- A town, about 2 miles to the south-east. Its smoke shows above the trees. The town itself is not in view.

Nothing else is in view.

## The people, as the agreed record has them

The record is `campaigns/sim-03/civs/unnamed-people/record.md`, written on Day 3. In short: about 5,000 people, human, in one town and the farms and clearings round it; woodland keepers more than farmers, who coppice, burn charcoal, graze the clearings, forage and grow mushrooms, keep small gardens, orchards and a few pigs on the autumn mast; ruled by a council of the heads of about a dozen old houses. The people and the town are unnamed until play names them. No one among them knows that anything has arrived, and no one serves it. Their starting state is in `campaigns/sim-03/civs/unnamed-people/state.md`.

What the people know of the country beyond their own valleys reaches the Understory only when one of them tells it.

## Tools and making

The world is before the medieval era. There are no steel forges. People work in wood, stone, bone, clay, fiber and bronze.

## Mortal magic

None is in view from the landing. What every god knows of it: it is simple, and it costs the worker.

## The land that is known, and the land only rumored

Known by sight: the forest it stands in, as far as it can see. Reported by its own sight: the worn way, and the smoke to the south-east. Rumored: nothing yet. The file is `campaigns/sim-03/player/map-knowledge.json`, and the map is drawn from it on request.

## What the people call the world, and the light above it

For the DM: these reach the Understory when a mortal first says them in its hearing, and not before.

Its people call the world the Standing Ground. They call the blue light that stands overhead the Cold Coal.
