# Hierarchy: bent, and empty at landing

Campaign: sim-03. Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-20.

This god did not refuse helpers. It turned down the default body of them and designed three people in its place, none of whom knows it at landing. The game remembers out loud that this god does not play the usual way.

In the player's words:

"No priests. No court. I want a council of the heads of the old houses, about a dozen, who really do rule and argue among themselves. I don't want to rule them."

"My helpers, few and chosen. I decide on three. I don't want a chain of command."

"If this conflicts with 'a god comes down to lead,' then I'm leading from the edge, and I'll accept whatever that costs in speed."

## What the god has

At landing: nothing. No seat beyond a burial ground and orchard it has not yet reached, no tended ground, and no one who knows what it is.

Designed, to be won in play (`civs/unnamed-people/people/`): a steward, a seed keeper, a track warden. All unnamed. Once the steward serves, orders travel from the god to her and from her to the council as advice, never command. The other two hear nothing from the god except through her or in person. Their standing instruction, once they serve: follow the god's conduct and last decisions, and otherwise tend the town's ground and keep the peace.

## The people's own order

- A council of about a dozen heads of the old houses. It rules the town and may decide against the god. It is not the god's.
- Small disputes go to whoever both sides trust, usually an elder of a third house. Large ones go to the council. The god takes no part.
- The grave words are said by the eldest of the family or whoever the dead trusted. No priest.

## What this means for every agent

- No text gives this god a court, priests, officers, or command over the council (failures checklist, check 4).
- Until play has made the steward a steward, no one acts for the god. Unplayed time runs on `player/conduct-profile.md` alone, cautious and in character, and the people go their own way. The DM said this plainly and the player accepted it on 2026-11-20.
- No agent writes any of the three as knowing the god, serving it or carrying its word before play has made it so. The steward's pressing sleeps until she knows who it is for.
- A decision of the god's reaches the town only by what the god says and does in person, until the steward serves.
