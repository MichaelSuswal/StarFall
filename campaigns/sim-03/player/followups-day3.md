# Follow-up questions for Day 3

Campaign: sim-03. Written by the Keeper after Night 2, for the DM to weave into Day 3. Few. Each comes from something the god's card or conduct leaves open that bears on its people. The DM writes the player's answer under each before the close.

1. In the fiction: "There will be a season when you are in the ground and cannot be asked. Who do they go to then?"
   (oog) fallback: Your slow return says that while you mend or grow back you do nothing, "and no one acts for you unless helpers do." You lean toward few helpers, chosen carefully. When you are gone for a season, who, if anyone, speaks or decides in your place?

2. In the fiction: "They keep fires. Where do you sit in a house with a hearth?"
   (oog) fallback: Your weakness names fire, and a line of fire that stops you. Your people will cook and keep warm with it. How do you mean to live among hearths: is there anything you ask of them, or do you take it as it comes? Nothing on the card changes either way.

3. In the fiction: "Some of them will kneel before you have said a word. What do you do with the ones who taught them to?"
   (oog) fallback: You will ask a kneeling mortal to get up. If the people you come to already have someone whose place it is to keep rites, what do you want done about that office?
