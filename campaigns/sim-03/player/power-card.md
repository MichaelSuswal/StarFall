# Power card: the Understory

Campaign: sim-03. Written: creation, Day 2, 2026-11-20. The text below is the text read back at the close of Day 2 and agreed by the player. The god has no name of its own yet; mortals will name it. It is filed under "the Understory".

## Form

One body, in one place, as for every god here (the campaign allows one body).

It looks like a thin, patient person of no particular age, with pale hands that are always a little damp. It smells of leaf mold and wet stone. Small fungi come up wherever it rests for long, and anyone can see them. It has no look of rank. It goes barefoot.

Senses: the player has described none beyond the ordinary. Senses not described are unsettled and will be asked about in play, never inferred.

Pace: not discussed at the table. The pace table's rates for a god apply (`engine/rules/time-and-pace.md`).

God-sense, as set for this campaign: it knows another god only face to face, close, as a weight or unease that grows surer with time and can be wrong. It tells nothing of the other's name, nature or powers. It knows that others like it came down when it did.

When the body is broken or scattered: see the power "The slow return" below. It carries a cost and a trigger, so it is written as a power.

### Definitions

- **Tended ground.** Living soil the god has worked with its own bare hands until its threads have taken. About three days of the god's work for an acre (five worker-days by hand, at a god's work rate). Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0001.
- **Tended line.** A strip a pace wide, laid by walking it barefoot and slowly, about a mile a day. Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0002.
- **How long they last.** Both last while the soil lives. Fire to the mineral soil, salting, poison or stripping ends them, and the ground must be tended again. Hard frost silences them until the thaw; they are still tended after.
- **What a line crosses.** A line carries over any living surface soil, however thin, whatever lies under it. Leaf litter and soil over rock carry. A line can run down into a hollow or a sinkhole and across its floor if living soil runs unbroken down the side and across. A sheer drop of bare rock breaks it, and the line is laid around the rim. A cave floor carries only as far in as its soil is alive. A line breaks at bare rock, paving, floors, open water, and running water wider than a stride that no roots cross. Marsh carries if it is alive. Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0003.
- **Touched.** The god's bare hands on the soil of a plot, once, while that soil is alive.
- **Dead or sterile ground.** Ground where nothing grows within a bowshot of the god: salted, burnt to mineral, poisoned, bare rock, deep cave, and open water out of reach of soil. A town with gardens and weeds is living ground. Frozen ground is asleep, not dead. Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0004.

### Things carried

All ordinary, with no power attached, at the player's request.

- Undyed linen, a loose shirt and trousers, worn soft and many times washed. No shoes.
- A plain cloth bag, carried over one shoulder.
- A few handfuls of mixed seed, sorted into twists of cloth. The player holds them to be kinds that breed true and does not know whether they will grow where the god lands. Nothing about the seed is settled by this card.
- A small sealed clay crock of living starter, a culture the god keeps fed.
- A short knife with a bone handle, for cutting cord and roots, not for fighting.

## Powers

### The ground-ear
Status: main
Description: With bare hands or feet in tended ground, you hear what touches the surface of all tended ground joined to you by an unbroken line, and about a bowshot beyond its edges, at any distance along the line. You hear weight, number, gait, direction, digging, felling, fire, and a body going into the soil. No words, faces or names. A tread is known only if you have stood near that walker in the flesh. The living layer only, nothing beneath stone, behind a sealed place or in lifeless ground. What happens, never why. Footfalls linger a few days. Digging, fire and burial linger until the next winter.
Drawback: The listening spends your speech. For as long as you listened, and never less than a day, you cannot hold speech with people. Words come slow and wrong, and you cannot make or keep up a conversation that matters.
Limits settled at creation:
- An hour to sink in and an hour to come out. It is not a glance.
- While listening the god is blind, deaf and numb in the body. Its body sits in plain sight and can be seen, moved or harmed. Lifting its skin from the soil ends the listening.
- The day of lost speech is a floor: listen for the minimum and a day is lost; listen for longer than a day and as long as was listened is lost. Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0005.
- Shoes and gloves block it. Soil in a pot is joined to nothing.
- Light and dark make no difference.
- Distance: no limit along an unbroken tended line; nothing beyond a bowshot off tended ground.
- Water, rock and frost: as under Definitions.
- Being watched: nothing hides the listening body.
- Other gods: another god's tread sounds like any tread. The ground-ear never tells a god from a mortal. Anything this power does to another god is unruled until it happens.
- The god's own people get no exemption.
- It rules no mind.
- Ground worked by more than ordinary craft, a mortal's or a god's, is unruled until it happens.
- Use across a year: limited only by the time each listening takes and the speech it spends.
- Information: it shows effects, never causes, and nothing beneath the living layer. Priced by shape on Day 2, for the Keeper's review on Night 2.
- Falling and carrying: not applicable to this power.
- Intent, the player's first wording: "While I stand with my bare hands or feet on living soil, I can listen through the roots and fungal threads, with no limit on range, and learn anything that touches the ground within my forest: footfalls, digging, fires, deaths, who has walked where and roughly when. It's a perceptive power, and it lets me stay in one place and still know the land." Trimmed by the DM, announced and accepted, to tended ground, the surface, and a short memory.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The slow word
Status: main
Description: From tended ground you send one image or one mood, no words or names, to one plot that is tended or touched. It comes up as a flush, a smell, or a dream for whoever sleeps nearest, in three to ten days along a line, ten days to a month to a touched plot with no line. One in the ground at a time. It rules no mind, and the reading belongs to whoever reads it. You do not learn how it landed unless you listen or go. Passers-by can see or smell it. Frost, fire or digging kills it before it comes up. Sent to ground another god holds, or to a god, it is unruled until it happens.
Drawback: You send what you have. The mood or image is gone from you while it is in the ground. Send calm and you are without calm until it fruits or dies; send a memory and you cannot recall it until then. If it is killed in the ground, it comes back a season late.
Limits settled at creation:
- A plot is a field, a garden or a grave.
- It is always sent from tended ground, whatever the plot. A touched plot needs no tended line to it. Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0006.
- It rules no mind: a dream can be shrugged off, a mood passes, a pattern can be read wrong or kicked over.
- Being watched: anyone who passes can see or smell it, and what they make of it is theirs.
- Other gods: sent to ground another god holds, or to a god in person, it is unruled until it happens.
- The god's own people get no exemption and read it as anyone would.
- Use across a year: one in the ground at a time, each taking three days to a month.
- Light and dark, falling, carrying: not applicable. Water, rock and frost: as under Definitions for the line; frost kills a word not yet up.
- More than ordinary craft: a plot worked or warded by more than ordinary craft is unruled until it happens.
- Intent, the player's first wording: "I can seed a mortal's field, garden, or grave with something living and small that will carry a message or a mood to them over days: a flush of mushrooms in a pattern, a smell, a dream. It's slow and ambiguous, and it can be misread. This would be my way to act where I am not, without having a second body."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: The reach to plots only touched, not tended, was bought with the weakness under Weaknesses taken.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The slow return
Status: main
Description: Your body is slow to die and slow to mend. Ordinary wounds close no faster than a mortal's. A body destroyed or scattered grows back from the living soil nearest the largest part of what is left, and takes no less than a full season. It is no escape: held, buried or walled in, you stay there, and remains kept from living soil do not return until soil reaches them. While mending from ruin or growing back you do nothing, and no one acts for you unless helpers do.
Drawback: Each return from nothing leaves behind a memory or a habit, and you are not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- What is lost on a return stays unspecified, at the player's request: "I'd like that to stay unspecified, so I find out by noticing a gap."
- Wounds from fire or salt follow the weakness below, not this power.
- Other gods: what another god can do to the body or its remains is unruled until it happens.
- Intent, the player's first wording: "I'd like a broken body to be slow to die and slow to mend. I'd rather be held, buried, or left in a hole than be gone, and I want to come back in whatever soil is nearest, no faster than the season allows. I understand the cost: while I'm hurt, I'm a hole in my own standing conduct."
- The drawback was proposed by the DM from the player's Day 1 answer 16 ("if one is lost, something of me goes with it, a memory or a habit") and accepted. Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0007.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The steward's pressing
Status: added Day 3 (2026-11-20). A people's edge that is a power of the god's, priced on the god.
Description: Once a season, your steward, with her bare hands in ground you have tended, may press one image or one mood into it. No words, no names. It lies there until you next listen on ground joined to it, and you know it for hers. Unheard by the next winter, it is gone. Fire, salt or digging on that spot kills it.
Drawback: At any listening in which you take up what she left, you lose one day of speech more than that listening would have cost. She loses a day of her own speech each time she presses; that half was set by the DM by shape and is subject to the Keeper fitting it to the world.
Limits settled at creation:
- It keeps the ground-ear's limit: no words, faces or names. The player's first ask, a spoken question, was refused for that reason and not argued.
- It is always her choice. Nothing in the power obliges her. The god will not ask it of her (conduct profile).
- It is hers alone. It does not pass to another steward without being priced again, and it ends with her office or her life.
- It sleeps until she knows who it is for. At landing she does not know the god. The player: "It means the power is dormant until I've earned her trust, and I'd rather it be that way than have her helping a god she was told about on the first day."
- Tended ground only, with bare hands. The god hears it only by the ground-ear, with all of that power's terms: joined ground, the hour in and out, the lost speech.
- It rules no mind and tells the god nothing but the one image or mood. The reading is the god's.
- Other gods, and ground worked by more than ordinary craft: unruled until it happens.
- Intent, the player's first wording: "that my steward can, once a season, speak to me through the ground I have tended, as a spoken question to me, not a reply I hear. It would be a one-way message from her to my ground, heard when I next listen."
- Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 3.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: The defaults the player turned down on Day 3 (no court, no priests, no command over the council, few helpers) were counted toward the price.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

**Fire, salt and dead ground.** Volunteered by the player and given a price at the table.

- Wounds from fire or salt do not close until the god has lain a full season in living soil.
- Each day on dead or sterile ground (see Definitions) weakens the god, and by the third day it can do nothing but leave.
- A line of salt or of fire stops the god's body in both directions. It keeps the god out of a threshold, and a closed ring of it keeps the god in.
- How it can be used against the god: anyone who learns it can bar a door, hold a threshold, pen the god in a ring, drive it off by salting or burning ground, or keep a wound open by keeping it from living soil. The player accepts that it may become known: "A god whose weakness is common knowledge among the right people is more interesting to play, and I'd rather earn trust than hide a handle."
- What it bought: the slow word's reach to plots only touched, not tended.
- The player's first wording: "Fire, salt, and a dead or sterile land hurt me more than they hurt mortals. I would rather you wrote that down than have me hide it."
