# Appearance card: the Understory

Written: creation, Day 2. Campaign: sim-03. For the paintings. Only what the player said.

- A thin, patient person of no particular age. Human-seeming.
- Pale hands that are always a little damp.
- Barefoot, always.
- Undyed linen: a loose shirt and trousers, worn soft, washed many times.
- A plain cloth bag over one shoulder. In it: seed in twists of cloth, a small sealed clay crock, a short knife with a bone handle.
- Small fungi come up wherever it rests for long.
- It smells of leaf mold and wet stone (not paintable, kept for scenes).
- No look of rank. In the player's words: "I don't have a throne look. I look like someone you'd step around on a path."
- When it listens through the ground it sits or kneels where it is with bare hands or feet in the soil, unseeing.

Not described, and not to be invented: face, hair, eyes, skin beyond the pale hands, height, voice, sex.
