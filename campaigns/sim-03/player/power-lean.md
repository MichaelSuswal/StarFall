# Power lean: the Understory

Campaign: sim-03. For the Keeper. Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 2. The DM does not know the world, the other gods or the mysteries; everything here is priced by shape.

## The lean

Low at landing, by the DM's estimate. Every active power runs on tended ground, and the god lands with none. Until it has worked soil with its own hands it has a slow body, a heavy weakness and nothing else. The weakness taken (fire, salt, dead ground, a salt or fire line that holds it both ways) is larger than what it bought.

Rising with years. The ground-ear has no limit on distance along an unbroken tended line, so its reach is whatever the god has had the time to lay, about a mile of line a day and about three days an acre. After many years of tending this could cover a large country. That is time spent, not a power grown, but the Keeper should weigh where it leads.

## Effects other gods perceive

None was granted at the table. Two things about this god are visible to anyone, gods included: small fungi come up wherever its body rests for long, and a slow word shows as a flush, a smell or a dream that passers-by can notice. The listening body sits in plain sight.

Anything acting on another god is written on the card as unruled until it happens.

## For the Keeper's review on Night 2

- **Mystery standard, the ground-ear.** Priced by shape as: the surface of the living layer only, nothing beneath stone, behind a sealed place or in lifeless ground; effects, never causes; footfalls held a few days, digging, fire and burial until the next winter. The player's first want is to learn what is buried, so the Keeper should check that "a body going into the soil" and "digging", held until the next winter, give away nothing they should not.
- **Mystery standard, the slow return.** The god regrows from the nearest living soil. Check against whatever lies in the ground.
- **Salt.** The weakness names salt, and the player wished on Day 1 for salt to be scarce. The card says nothing about how common salt is. How much the weakness bites depends on that.
- **Seed.** The god carries a few handfuls of seed the player holds to breed true, as ordinary possessions with no power. The player wished on Day 1 for clean seed to be scarce. The card settles nothing about whether it grows or what it is worth.
- **Rates set at the table.** Three days of the god's work to tend an acre, a mile a day to lay a line. Set by analogy to hand planting and the god's work rate; the pace table has no entry for this work.
- **The memory or habit lost on a return.** The player asks that it stay unspecified, to be found by noticing a gap. The rules give a mechanism for this only for NPC gods; how it is enacted for the player's god is the Keeper's to set.
- **Rulings settled at creation, needing ledger ids:** the definitions of tended ground, tended line, what a line crosses, and dead or sterile ground; the floor on the lost day of speech; that a slow word is always sent from tended ground and a touched plot needs no line; the drawback of the slow return.
- **Senses and pace** were not discussed. Senses are unsettled; pace is left at the pace table's rates for a god.
