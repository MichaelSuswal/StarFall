# Backstory outline: the Understory

Campaign: sim-03. Written: creation, Day 2. The player's words, verbatim. An outline only; the god's past is foggy to the god itself.

## What I am

I am a mycelial god, the slow body under the forest floor. My one body is a body I can walk around in. It looks like a thin, patient person of no particular age, with pale hands that are always a little damp. It smells of leaf mold and wet stone, and small fungi come up wherever I rest for long. I don't have a throne look. I look like someone you'd step around on a path.

## Name

I don't have one yet. The mortals can give me one, and I expect it will be wrong. For now, call me the Understory.

## Who I was and what I lost

Before the fall I was the quiet part of something large, the connective tissue between other lives. What I lost is the connection itself. I came from a place where I was never alone and never one thing, and I am now a single body that feels each of its edges. I want it back, and I know I can't have it the way it was. That is why I'll try to build it again slowly, out of mortals, trees, and old ground.
