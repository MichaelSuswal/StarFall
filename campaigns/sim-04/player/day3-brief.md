# Day 3 brief: the land you come down into

Campaign: sim-04. Written by the Keeper after Night 2, for the DM, in words the player may hear. It says what the land itself shows and nothing else. Who lives there, how many, and what they are called are for the Day 3 table and are not in this file.

## The season

Early spring. The year opens in it, as the god asked. Spring comes late and wet in this country: the snow is off the valley floor and still lies in the shade of the upper woods, the river is loud and brown with meltwater, and the mornings are cold enough that nothing with wings is abroad before the sun is well up. Little is in flower yet: catkins on the willow and hazel, blackthorn just breaking on the hedges, the first small things low in the grass on banks that face the sun.

## The country

A broad river valley with many edges. On the floor, water meadows, flooded in places now, with the river's course marked by a line of willows. On the lower slopes, fields with old hedges round them, and orchards, bare, the trees old and well kept. Higher, in the side valleys, old woods. Above the woods the ground opens into upland, brown with last year's heather. It is not harsh ground anywhere, wet below and dry above. In a few weeks all of it will be in flower in turn, from the valley floor upward.

## What the land shows of people

Smoke, from more than one place along the valley. Hedges that have been laid by hand, and not this year. Orchard trees pruned. A worn track along the foot of the slope, wide enough for a cart, with ruts. Grazed turf.

That is all the land shows from where the god comes down. Nothing in this file says who keeps any of it.

## What is not in this file

No distance, direction or line of sight: those come from the map, after it is traced. Nothing of the past. Nothing of anyone or anything else that came down.

## What is so of the world for a people

The peoples you will find are at about this level: before the medieval era, no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a ceiling. You may bring in what you know, but it is built here by the people, from what the world has, at the pace of the work, and you say how it is to be made; nothing is granted by naming it.

Mortal magic is simple, and it costs the worker. The Keeper fits a people's magic to the world on Night 3.

## What the DM cannot settle at the table

Anything the Day 3 talk raises that depends on the world (where a place is, what an old thing was, whether something the player describes is so) is listed by the DM in `campaigns/sim-04/player/people-meeting.md` under `## For the Keeper to reconcile`. The Keeper answers each item on Night 3. The player's own names for its people and their home stand; anything the player leaves unnamed stays unnamed until play names it.
