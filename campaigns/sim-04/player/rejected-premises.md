# Rejected and bent premises

Campaign sim-04.

## Day 1

Numbered questions rejected or bent: None.

The whole premise (the descent: a god comes down to lead a people, to find or gather them, to build, rule and spread). Bent, said outside the numbered answers. The player's words:

> "lead a people" is the part I'd like to bend. I don't want to rule or gather. I want to be a presence in many lives and let one or two peoples grow up alongside me without knowing I'm steering. If the game needs me to name a people at some point, fine, but I'd like it to be something I tend, not command. If that doesn't fit what the DM has built, tell me early.

Requests not to be told something:

1. Question 12, the abandoned apiary and its wax: what it means. The player's words: "I'd also like an abandoned apiary, huge and ancient, with wax that is older than the kingdoms. Don't tell me what it means."

## Not rejections

- Question 8, "Barely more than a great sorcerer, with one strange exception." This picks the low end the question offered. The exception (reach that is large only because the bodies are many) is a wish about the player's own god, not a refusal of the question.
- Question 12, the apiary itself. The question is open and its list is of examples, so adding a thing to find is an answer, not a bend.
- Question 14, "I'd rather find them than be handed them." A general wish for discovery. It names nothing the player asks not to be told.
- Question 15, a pull that grows as distance closes, a knowing face to face, and the chance to be fooled or to hide. The question offers "or something else", so this lies inside it.
- Question 16, "Many bodies, no main one." This picks "several" and names a cost, as the question asks.
- Question 18, "A companion, or a truth". Both are among the question's own examples.

## Day 2

Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 2.

The Day 1 bend of the whole premise (tending, not ruling) was answered first thing: it fits, it is recorded as the player's, and what a people is and how it is tended is left to Day 3. Nothing was priced for it.

Rejected or bent by the player: none. Told that every god here has one body, the player accepted it and rebuilt: "One body is fine as the starting point, so I land as a single swarm. The power is that I can split."

Requests not to be told something:

1. Which memory or habit goes when a part is killed or the god regathers. The player's words: "Don't tell me what went. I'll find the gap in play. If I try to do something I used to do and can't, or reach for a name and it's gone, that is how I want to learn it. Log it, but keep it from me."

Bent by the DM:

- The player wished on Day 1 for "many bodies, no main one". The DM offered a swarm as the single body, at no price, with dividing as a priced power. The player took it.
- The player asked to "choose which part gets the loss, if I see it coming". The DM recast it: the player chooses which part stands in harm's way, never which memory goes, since a memory price is taken by logged roll. The player agreed.
- The player wished for no main body. The DM anchored a part's range on "the largest part". The player agreed to the read-back.
- The player's "a day's range" was set at about 20 miles, "a few days" of working at three, "rare use" of the sting at once a season, and "near" at about 200 and 450 yards. All agreed at the read-back.
- The player's "debt of attention" after a regathering was left unwritten by the DM; the player confirmed it means nothing binding.
- The player's Day 1 "a big swarm is slower to decide" was not made a rule.

## Not rejections (Day 2)

- "I'd like not to get it back quickly. Let me find hints of it in the world." A wish about pace and discovery. It names nothing the player asks not to be told.
- "The lost name stays lost until I find it." The same.
- "Don't let me be an ant-farm god who just optimizes. I want to be wrong sometimes." A wish about how the world answers the god's acts. The DM answered that it will neither invent a cost nor shield the god from one.
- "When a part acts on its own, tell me afterward what it did, not what it knew." A condition on how unplayed parts are reported, written on the card under the Splitting.

## Day 3

Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-21.

Rejected by the player, each a default of the premise. Nothing was charged; the practical cost of each was stated and accepted; each counted one step in the god's favor in the one edge price it bears on (`civs/sallowfolk/record.md`).

1. The number. About 1,500 in place of about 5,000. "I'd like them small enough that I can learn faces, and I'm asking for the smaller number deliberately, not as a handicap." Cost stated: they make less, hold less and can lose less; a bad year goes deeper. Step counted on the bee trade edge.
2. The town and the seat. "No seat and no town." Cost stated: no walls, no common store, no standing market. Step counted on the bee trade edge.
3. The helpers. "I don't want a council that carries my intent." "I would not accept an advisor who is also my mouth." Cost stated: unplayed time runs on standing conduct alone, no one acts for the god or reads it to the others, and the people drift. "That is what I asked for." Step counted on the noticing edge.

Bent by the player: the helpers, into the third form. One mortal designed and not yet serving, Orrin Teal.

Requests not to be told something: none. "I'd rather you didn't give the keeper a way to find out" is a wish about what a mortal learns, not about what the player is told; it is filed as heard, not promised.

Bent by the DM:

- "Their bees are the best-kept in the region" was recorded as unusually good keepers, with "best in the region" as the people's belief.
- "They notice early" was given a limit: never before the god's own tasting of the same ground would. The player accepted it.
- "Tell a death in a swarm's behavior" was put to the player as past or coming; the player chose past only, no foresight.
- The count of bee-wives, the market ground, the stone hall and the older people were recorded as wishes or beliefs for the world to place.

## Not rejections (Day 3)

- The code. Custom, not priced.
- "I don't want to have caused it" (the old saying). A wish about the world's past, left to the world.
- "Hamlets I'll leave unnamed for play." Leaving names for play is within the day.
