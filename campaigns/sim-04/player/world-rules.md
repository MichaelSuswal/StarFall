# What is so of every god here

For the DM, in words the player may hear. These are facts of being a god in
this world, known from the moment of landing.

## Knowing another god

A god knows that others like it came down when it did. Beyond that, a god feels another god only when they stand close enough to speak. Then it is a weight and a stillness in the air about the other, nothing more: no name, no nature, no measure of what the other can do. The feeling comes slowly and can be wrong, and a god that takes pains can pass for something lesser for a while. Farther off than that there is nothing to feel, however near the other may be.
