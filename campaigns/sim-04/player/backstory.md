# Backstory outline: the Thrum

Campaign: sim-04. Written: creation, Day 2. The player's words, verbatim. An outline only; the god's past is foggy to the god itself.

## What I am

A swarm god. I'm not one body. I'm a hive-mind of bees, wasps and the small pollinating things, with no single shape that is "me." Mortals will meet me as weather in the flowers, not as a face. (Day 1.)

One body is fine as the starting point, so I land as a single swarm. The power is that I can split. (Day 2.)

## Name

Those who don't know what I am can call me the Thrum, because that is what they'll hear. You can call me that too. The lost name stays lost until I find it.

## Who I was and what I lost

I can't see it clearly, which feels right. I know I was not one thing. I was a place where something was kept, a grove or a comb, and I remember a lot of patient work with no audience. What I lost is a name, and something like a queen: a center that told me which part was me. I'd like not to get it back quickly. Let me find hints of it in the world, such as an old apiary, a sealed door, or a word the locals don't recognize.

## Note for the Keeper

The hints the player names (an old apiary, a sealed door, a word the locals do not recognize) are wishes, not facts. The DM confirmed none of them and does not know what the world holds.
