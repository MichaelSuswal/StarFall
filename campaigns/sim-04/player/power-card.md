# Power card: the Thrum

Campaign: sim-04. Written: creation, Day 2, 2026-11-21. The text below is the text read back at the close of Day 2 and agreed by the player, recast in the third person without changing a term. The god's own name is lost; it is filed under "the Thrum", which is also what those who do not know what it is may call it.

## Form

One body, as for every god here (the campaign allows one body). The body is a swarm: bees, wasps and the small pollinating things, with no face, no chief insect and no voice. A swarm that is one presence in one place is form and costs nothing; dividing it is the power "The Splitting" below. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.

It does not attack unprovoked and does not speak. When spoken to, all of it turns toward the sound. All of it together fills a doorway, a swarm that could not be walked through without it parting. A single part, a fifth, fills a hat. Spread loose across a field it can look like a thin haze; drawn tight it is about the size of a barrel a person could carry.

Senses: it hears speech. Its other senses are unsettled and will be asked about in play, never inferred.

Pace: it flies about 20 miles in a fair day (provisional, for the Keeper). It does not fly at night, in rain or in cold.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: the god knows that others like it came down when it did. Beyond that it feels another god only when they stand close enough to speak, as a weight and a stillness in the air about the other, nothing more: no name, no nature, no measure of what the other can do. The feeling comes slowly and can be wrong, and a god that takes pains can pass for something lesser for a while. Farther off there is nothing to feel.

When the body is wholly destroyed: see the power "The Regathering" below.

### Definitions

- **A part.** One of up to five divisions of the swarm, each a thinking swarm.
- **Full number.** The whole swarm, unsplit and unhurt.
- **Small pollinators.** Bees, wasps, and the flies, moths, butterflies and beetles that work flowers.
- **The chosen valley.** One valley, chosen in play once the god has seen it, its bounds set by the Keeper from the land.
- **A tended hive.** A hive of ordinary bees the god has worked or wintered, counted in seasons since it first did.
- **Near.** Within about 200 yards of a part and about 450 yards of the whole of the god (provisional, for the Keeper). Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
- **A day's flight.** About 20 miles in a fair day (provisional, for the Keeper). Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.

### Things carried

Nothing but what bees carry: pollen, nectar, wax scraps. No object from before the fall.

## Powers

### The Splitting
Status: main
Description: The god lands whole. It may divide into as many as five parts and rejoin by touching. A part is a fifth of the god in every way; two fifths together are two fifths. Parts range up to a day's flight from the largest part.
Drawback: Each part out thins the god. Parts do not share what they see or know until they touch again. A part killed is gone, and one item of memory or habit goes with it for good, picked by logged roll from the god's record; the god is not told what went. The bees regrow to full number over a season of brood in fair weather.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The god chooses which part stands in harm's way, never which memory goes. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
  - A lost part is priced at one item, by analogy with the first rung of the standing return scale. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
  - A part shut in a skep or a box is held, not killed.
  - A part kept more than seven days beyond a day's flight from the largest part goes wild and counts as killed.
  - Dividing is visible as a swarm dividing, which mortals have seen bees do.
  - Parts do not fly at night, in rain or in cold. Drowning, smoke, fire, cold or a wet week kill a part more easily than the whole.
  - In scenes the player may play any part, one at a time; each knows only what it has itself seen since it last touched another. A part not being played carries on with the purpose it left with, by the god's standing conduct, cautious and in character. Afterward the player is told what that part did, not what it knew; what it learned reaches the rest when it touches them again. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
  - What another god feels from a part is unruled until it happens.
  - The player said on Day 1 that a large swarm decides slowly. It is not a rule; it is the player's to play.
  - Not applicable: falling, scarcity, minds, things of more than ordinary craft.
  - Intent, the player's first wording: "One body is fine as the starting point, so I land as a single swarm. The power is that I can split." "Start with a small number, maybe three to five, that I can send out as separate thinking swarms. Each is weaker alone and can act independently inside a day's range. Losing one should cost me a real memory or a habit, not just strength, so I'm permanently a bit different. I'd like to be able to choose which part gets the loss, if I see it coming." And from Day 1: "each body I send out thins me. I get less certain which of my bodies is thinking, and a big swarm is slower to decide. A hive dying should hurt. I'd like it to be possible to lose a part of myself for good."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Working
Status: main
Description: The small pollinators near the god act as one thing at its will: fly a pattern, hold a path, strip a field of pollen or fill it, move heat and stores to bring a hive through winter.
Drawback: The bees pay. What they do for the god they do not do for themselves. A hive worked more than three days in one season goes into winter short, whoever's hive it is, the god's own tended hives included.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It works on small pollinators only, never birds, beasts or people.
  - It moves honey and stores; it never makes any.
  - Bees holding a path sting as ordinary bees sting, no harder.
  - It stops at night, in rain and in cold. In winter it reaches only the hive the god is sheltering in or beside.
  - Anyone who knows bees can see that worked bees are not behaving as bees do.
  - Its reach is "near" as defined, and no more in a year than that reach and the days allow.
  - The god's own people's hives pay the same as any.
  - Pollinators that are more than ordinary creatures, hives kept by more than ordinary craft, and bees another god has a hand on are unruled until met.
  - Not applicable: falling, minds.
  - Intent, the player's first wording: "The wild bees are just bees. I can make them act as one thing: carry a message by pattern, guard a path, strip a field of pollen or fill it, and bring one hive through a winter by moving its heat and stores." On the cost chosen: "The bees pay. ... I'd rather this fall on someone else's honey than on my own idleness, because it is the exact 'debt a mortal incurs on my behalf' I asked for."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Tasting
Status: main
Description: Where the god or a part feeds, it knows the health of that ground before mortal eyes would: that it is sick, and how far the sickness reaches. Never what caused it, and never who. Provisional for the Keeper.
Drawback: The god reads by eating and takes a share of what it reads. The part that tasted is sick for days and loses bees. Ground foul enough can kill the part that tastes it, at the usual price of a part.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Only ground the god has fed over. Nothing in winter.
  - To a watcher it is bees foraging.
  - Effects, never causes: priced by shape against the mystery standard, for the Keeper's review.
  - Not applicable: falling, carrying, minds, other gods.
  - Intent, the player's first wording: "I can read a place by what is flowering and who is feeding on it. I'd like to taste a land's health that way, which means I know about a blight, a poisoned stream or a dead orchard before any mortal does."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Sting
Status: main
Description: One person, stung with intent, takes either a fever of some days or a night of bad dreams carrying one feeling of the god's choosing, picked at the time. A feeling, never a picture, a word or a command. The fever is real and can kill the weak; the god does not control that.
Drawback: It marks them. For the rest of their life the stung one feels the god near, the whole of it or any part, as a hum under other sounds. They are not told what the hum is.
Limits settled at creation:
  - One such sting in a season, counted across all the god's parts.
  - It must reach skin.
  - It works on mortal persons, the god's own people the same as any.
  - It does not rule a mind.
  - The mark's reach is "near" as defined for a part, about 200 yards.
  - To a watcher it is a bee sting.
  - On another god it is unruled until it happens.
  - Not applicable: falling, carrying, water, scarcity, things of more than ordinary craft.
  - Intent, the player's first wording: "I'd like a small way to sting that carries intent, like a fever or a nightmare, for rare use." On the cost chosen: "I would rather be known to one person than useless for a week."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Listening
Status: main
Description: Across the chosen valley the god feels what every small pollinator there is doing, all at once: movement, numbers, alarm, hunger, smoke, a crowd, a field gone quiet, a thing the bees avoid. Effects, never causes; never speech, faces or who. Provisional for the Keeper.
Drawback: While the god listens and for a day after, it cannot tell its own parts from the wild ones; parts that are out get no direction and act on what they last knew. And it shows: each time the god opens it, every listener in the valley holds still together for a breath, and anyone who knows bees or watches closely can see it, another god among them.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Only where flowers are in season. Blind in winter, thin in early spring and late autumn, blind at night, indoors, underground, over open water and bare stone.
  - It does nothing until a valley is chosen. Changing valleys takes a full year in which the god hears neither.
  - It shows what the listeners do, so it knows a god from a mortal no better than a bee does.
  - A part that gets no direction keeps acting on what it last knew; it does not hold still.
  - Pollinators that are more than ordinary creatures are unruled until met.
  - Priced by shape against the mystery standard, for the Keeper's review. If the Keeper adds a limit, it is put to the player openly.
  - Not applicable: falling, carrying, minds.
  - Intent, the player's first wording: "I'd like to be able to listen through every bee, wasp and pollinator in my chosen valley at once, not just the parts of me I've sent out. A whole watershed of small ears." With the player's suggested terms: "it works only where flowers are in season, so winter blinds me; the listening is shallow, patterns and not words; and I can't tell my own parts from the wild bees without effort". On the cost: "The reveal is the part I like. It means I have to decide each time whether knowing is worth being noticed."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Regathering
Status: main
Description: When every bee of the god is dead, it gathers again from the hive it has tended longest, wherever that hive then stands. If it has tended none, from the oldest wild hive in the chosen valley. If no valley is chosen either, from the wild hive nearest where it fell. It takes a full season of brood.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit, by logged roll, not told to the god. As the player asked, and beyond what the scale requires, so it buys nothing: the god comes back at a fifth of full number and needs a further season to regrow.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The standing return scale, first rung: a season, one item. The month at two items was offered and declined.
  - The return ground is the hive tended longest, as the player wished, in place of the nearest ground of the god's kind. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
  - Parts that died in the same day as the last of the god are covered by that one item; parts lost earlier have already been paid for. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
  - A swarm shut in, smoked quiet or scattered is held, not broken. This happens only when all of it is dead.
  - The "debt of attention" to that hive is not a rule. The player means nothing binding by it and plays it.
  - Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched, minds, things of more than ordinary craft.
  - Intent, the player's first wording: "If every part of me goes at once, I'd wish to regather from the hive I have tended longest, wherever it stands then, not from a place I chose or the spot I landed. It should come back slowly, over a full season of brood. I want it to cost me: I come back smaller, with a gap I can't name, and a debt of attention to that hive. If I have tended no hive that long, let me regather from the oldest wild hive in my chosen valley. It must be a place I could have been reached by something I did."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
