# Power lean: the Thrum

Campaign: sim-04. For the Keeper. Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 2. The DM does not know the world, the other gods or the mysteries; everything here is priced by shape, against this god's own table prices and nothing else.

## The lean

Up, by the DM's estimate, and said so to the player. Six blocks are on the card: the Splitting, the Working, the Tasting, the Sting, the Listening and the Regathering. On Day 1 the player asked to be "barely more than a great sorcerer". The Listening is most of the overage: region-wide knowledge with no travel. The DM offered to drop or shrink it; the player kept it at full strength and accepted the lean, "as long as the reveal stays as written".

What holds it down: no weakness was taken and nothing was bought. Every active power is seasonal, stops at night, in rain and in cold, and nearly all of it is dark in winter. The god has no voice and no hands. The Listening does nothing until a valley is chosen, exposes the god each time it is used, and cuts its parts loose for a day. Each part lost costs a memory for good.

## Effects other gods perceive

Granted at the table: the stillness of the Listening. Each time it opens, every small pollinator in the chosen valley holds still together for a breath, and another god who is watching the bees can see it as anyone can. Worked bees visibly do not behave as bees do. Nothing was granted that acts on another god; the card marks each such case unruled until it happens, including what another god feels from a single part of a divided body.

## For the Keeper's review on Night 2

- **Mystery standard, the Listening.** Priced by shape: what the valley's pollinators are doing, effects only, never speech, faces, who or why; blind indoors, underground, at night, in winter. "A thing the bees avoid" and "a field gone quiet" are among what it shows. Check that these give away nothing they should not.
- **Mystery standard, the Tasting.** Priced by shape: that ground is sick and how far, early, never the cause. Check against whatever makes ground sick in this world.
- **The Regathering's ground.** It returns from the hive it has tended longest, or the oldest wild hive in the chosen valley, or the wild hive nearest where it fell. This replaces "the nearest ground of the god's kind" at the player's wish. First rung taken: a season, one item. The month at two was offered and declined. Check "the oldest wild hive in the chosen valley" against whatever stands there.
- **A lost part.** Priced at one item of memory or habit by logged roll, by analogy with the first rung of the return scale; parts that die the same day as the last of the body are covered by the Regathering's one item. The rules do not cover a divided body.
- **Requests not to be told.** The player asked not to be told which memory or habit goes, for a lost part and for a regathering: "Log it, but keep it from me."
- **Rates and reaches set at the table, all provisional, for the Keeper.** Flight: 20 miles in a fair day, none at night, in rain or in cold. Near: about 200 yards of a part, about 450 yards of the whole. A hive worked more than three days in a season goes into winter short. A part more than seven days beyond a day's flight from the largest part goes wild. A sting with intent: once a season across all parts. Regrowth to full number: a season of brood.
- **Limits that may rest on a scarcity.** The Working moves honey and stores and never makes any, and its cost falls on hives' winter stores. The player wished on Day 1 for sweetness to be scarce. The card says nothing of how scarce honey is; how hard the cost bites depends on that.
- **More than ordinary creatures.** The player wished on Day 1 for a few wrong animals, a moth and a wasp among them. The card marks pollinators that are more than ordinary, and hives kept by more than ordinary craft, as unruled until met.
- **Table rulings awaiting ledger ids.** The swarm as a single body (Form); near and a day's flight (Definitions); the price of a lost part, the choice of which part stands in harm's way but never which memory goes, and how unplayed parts run (the Splitting); the return ground and same-day coverage (the Regathering).
- **World rules given.** Bodies and god-sense were stated to the player. The pricing terms for mortal magic did not come up and were not stated.
