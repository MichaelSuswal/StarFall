# Appearance card: the Thrum

Written: creation, Day 2. Campaign: sim-04. For the paintings. Only what the player said.

- A swarm of bees, wasps and the small pollinating things. No single shape, no face, no chief insect.
- All of it together fills a doorway: a swarm that could not be walked through without it parting.
- A single part, a fifth of it, fills a hat. It may be divided into as many as five such parts.
- Spread loose across a field it can look like a thin haze. Drawn tight it is about the size of a barrel a person could carry.
- Mostly it is nothing a passer-by would notice: bees working a field in an order that looks like a plan.
- If someone stands still, the swarm gathers around them and waits. It does not attack and does not speak.
- Up close, when it is spoken to, all the bees turn the same way, like a crowd leaning toward a sound.
- It carries nothing but what bees carry: pollen, nectar, wax scraps.
- A hum a little too steady, and a sense of being counted (not paintable, kept for scenes).
- In the player's words: "Mortals will meet me as weather in the flowers, not as a face." "It is polite in the way weather is polite."

Not described, and not to be invented: the color or kinds of the insects beyond bees, wasps and small pollinators, any figure or face formed by the swarm, eyes, a voice.
