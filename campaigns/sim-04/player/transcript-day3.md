# Transcript: creation Day 3 (sim-04)

## DM:

The fall has slowed. There is country under you now, and the voice of The Between is nearer than it was.

"Your card came back from its night. Nothing was added to it and nothing taken. What was marked unsure is sure: the day's flight, the reach, the tasting and the listening all stand as you agreed them."

"Now look down. Early spring, as you asked, and it comes late and wet here. The snow is off the valley floor and still lies in the shade of the upper woods. The river is loud and brown with melt. The mornings are cold enough that nothing with wings is abroad before the sun is well up. Little is in flower yet: catkins on the willow and hazel, blackthorn just breaking on the hedges, the first small things low in the grass on banks that face the sun."

"A broad river valley with many edges. Water meadows on the floor, flooded in places, the river's course marked by a line of willows. Fields on the lower slopes with old hedges round them, and orchards, bare, the trees old and well kept. Old woods in the side valleys. Above the woods the ground opens into upland, brown with last year's heather. Wet below and dry above, and none of it harsh. In a few weeks all of it will flower in turn, from the valley floor upward."

"And it is kept. Smoke, from more than one place along the valley. Hedges laid by hand, and not this year. Orchard trees pruned. A track along the foot of the slope, wide enough for a cart, with ruts. Grazed turf. That is all the land shows from here, and I will not tell you whose any of it is."

"Those who fall come down to a people. The usual one is about five thousand strong, with a town or a city, a seat, a court or a council, and those who run things when the one who fell is elsewhere. You said you would tend and not command, and that if a people had to be named you would name one. Today it is named. So. Who are the people you tend, in country like this? How do they live, and what do they do with their days? And when you are in another field, or not there at all, who among them runs things?"

(oog) Any order, any length, as yesterday. I will ask as you go, and price whatever gives them a real edge over other peoples. A few things to know first. The peoples here begin before the medieval era, with no steel forges; you may bring in what you know, but it is built here by the people, from what the world has, and you say how it is made. Mortal magic, if your people have any, is simple and it costs the one who works it. What you tell me about the land or its past is recorded as a wish or as your people's belief; where anything stands is the world's, and you will see it when you land. The names of your people and of their home are yours to give, or to leave unnamed for play.
