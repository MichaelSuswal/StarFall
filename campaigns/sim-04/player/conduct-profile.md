# Conduct profile: the Thrum

Campaign: sim-04. Unplayed time, and any part of the god the player is not playing, runs on this: cautious and in character by default, never reckless.

## Now

As of creation Day 3, 2026-11-21.

Wants: to learn what the place it came from kept, and why. To see a valley run well and badly under its tending and know which of those was its doing. To be known by a few and not by many. A people small enough that it can learn faces.

Refusals: it will not sting anyone to death on purpose or choose a fever for a person it has decided is in the way. It will not starve a neighbor's hives to feed its own people's. It will not drive a mind with a sting, even with a feeling, to get a decision it wants. It will not burn a hive, its own or anyone's, to win a fight. It will not have anyone speak for it.

How it goes about things: it tends and does not rule; it means to be read, not heard, and would rather be misread than spoken for. It asks the price before it takes a thing and takes the cost that falls where it can be seen and answered for. It weighs each act of knowing against being noticed. It expects to be wrong sometimes and wants to answer for what its tending does. It counts the Working's cost against itself each time it uses it near its people's hives.

In a year not played: it keeps out of sight, a swarm among swarms, and lets no one see it for what it is, Orrin Teal included, until the player chooses otherwise at the table. It tends what it has tended. It uses the Working only for the short-hive duty: when a hive of the Sallowfolk has gone into winter short by its doing, it moves stores to it from a surplus hive in its reach and does not work that hive again that year. It opens the Listening no more than it must. It stings no one. It takes no part when a quarrel goes to the bees.

Helpers: refused on creation Day 3. None at landing. One mortal is designed and not yet serving, Orrin Teal; he does not know it. No one acts for it while it is away, and its people drift by their own lights.

Unsaid wants are kept sealed and are not in this file.

## 2026-11-21, creation Day 2

### What it wants (the player's words)

To learn what the place I came from kept, and why. To see one valley run well and badly under my tending, and know which of those was me. To be known by a few and not by many.

### What it will not do (the player's words, given as standing conduct)

I will not sting someone to death on purpose, or choose a fever for a person I've decided is in the way. I will not starve a neighbor's hives to feed my own people's. I will not drive a mind with a sting, even a feeling, to get a decision I want. I won't burn a hive, mine or anyone's, to win a fight. These hold whatever they'd gain me.

### How it goes about things (from the Day 1 and Day 2 tables)

- It tends and does not command. "I want to be a presence in many lives and let one or two peoples grow up alongside me without knowing I'm steering." (Day 1.)
- It is a god that has to be read, not one that speaks. (Day 1.)
- It asks to be priced and will not take a thing for free: "I'd rather pay a real cost than get it for free."
- It chose that the cost of its working fall on the bees, and so on whoever keeps them, over its own idleness, knowing a keeper may notice: "it is the exact 'debt a mortal incurs on my behalf' I asked for."
- It chose to be known to one stung person for life over being useless for a week. It will not pick the feeling a sting carries in advance.
- It took the listening at full strength with its showing: "I have to decide each time whether knowing is worth being noticed."
- It declined to buy more with a weakness: "I don't want to buy more power with a cost I haven't felt yet."
- It does not want to be an optimizer. "I want to be wrong sometimes." "A tender should have to answer for what the tending does."
- After a regathering it expects to feel pulled to the hive it came back from and to worry about it, and wants that to be its choice to honor or ignore.
- It does not want its lost name or its lost center back quickly.
- When a part has acted on its own, the player is told what it did, not what it knew.

## 2026-11-21, creation Day 3

### Standing conduct the day produced (the player's words)

- On a hive that goes into winter short by its doing: "What I'll do is move stores from a surplus hive in my reach to the short one and not work that hive again that year." "The cost should sit on me, as something I carry and she doesn't know about." The DM told the player that moving stores is itself the Working and counts as a day of work on both hives.
- On the Working near its people's hives: "I'll count that against myself every time I use it near their hives."
- On unplayed years: "I keep out of sight. I stay a swarm among swarms, tend what I've tended, use the Working only for the short-hive duty and nothing else, and open the Listening no more than I must. I don't let myself be seen by Orrin or anyone else until I choose it at the table."
- On the sting in unplayed years: "I would not sting anyone in an unplayed year, so the hum isn't given to anyone without my being present to see what it does."
- On being spoken for: "I'd rather be misread honestly than interpreted by someone who says they speak for me."
- On the people's own quarrels: when one goes to the bees, "I don't intervene in it."

### What the day showed

- It asked for the smaller people deliberately: "small enough that I can learn faces".
- It asked for edges to be priced and took each weakness without bargaining, and said the Working's cost on its own people is something it will count against itself.
- It did not want to have caused the luck its people believe in.
- It would have a mortal watched before it is ever seen by him, and set who he is and nothing he will do.
