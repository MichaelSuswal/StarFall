# Conduct profile: the Thrum

Campaign: sim-04. Unplayed time, and any part of the god the player is not playing, runs on this: cautious and in character by default, never reckless.

## Now

As of creation Day 2, 2026-11-21.

Wants: to learn what the place it came from kept, and why. To see a valley run well and badly under its tending and know which of those was its doing. To be known by a few and not by many.

Refusals: it will not sting anyone to death on purpose or choose a fever for a person it has decided is in the way. It will not starve a neighbor's hives to feed its own people's. It will not drive a mind with a sting, even with a feeling, to get a decision it wants. It will not burn a hive, its own or anyone's, to win a fight.

How it goes about things: it tends and does not rule; it means to be read, not heard. It asks the price before it takes a thing and takes the cost that falls where it can be seen and answered for. It weighs each act of knowing against being noticed. It expects to be wrong sometimes and wants to answer for what its tending does.

Helpers: not yet known. Day 3.

Unsaid wants are kept sealed and are not in this file.

## 2026-11-21, creation Day 2

### What it wants (the player's words)

To learn what the place I came from kept, and why. To see one valley run well and badly under my tending, and know which of those was me. To be known by a few and not by many.

### What it will not do (the player's words, given as standing conduct)

I will not sting someone to death on purpose, or choose a fever for a person I've decided is in the way. I will not starve a neighbor's hives to feed my own people's. I will not drive a mind with a sting, even a feeling, to get a decision I want. I won't burn a hive, mine or anyone's, to win a fight. These hold whatever they'd gain me.

### How it goes about things (from the Day 1 and Day 2 tables)

- It tends and does not command. "I want to be a presence in many lives and let one or two peoples grow up alongside me without knowing I'm steering." (Day 1.)
- It is a god that has to be read, not one that speaks. (Day 1.)
- It asks to be priced and will not take a thing for free: "I'd rather pay a real cost than get it for free."
- It chose that the cost of its working fall on the bees, and so on whoever keeps them, over its own idleness, knowing a keeper may notice: "it is the exact 'debt a mortal incurs on my behalf' I asked for."
- It chose to be known to one stung person for life over being useless for a week. It will not pick the feeling a sting carries in advance.
- It took the listening at full strength with its showing: "I have to decide each time whether knowing is worth being noticed."
- It declined to buy more with a weakness: "I don't want to buy more power with a cost I haven't felt yet."
- It does not want to be an optimizer. "I want to be wrong sometimes." "A tender should have to answer for what the tending does."
- After a regathering it expects to feel pulled to the hive it came back from and to worry about it, and wants that to be its choice to honor or ignore.
- It does not want its lost name or its lost center back quickly.
- When a part has acted on its own, the player is told what it did, not what it knew.
