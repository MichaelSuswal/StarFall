# Hierarchy: refused, with one designed and not yet serving

Campaign: sim-04. Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-21.

This god refused helpers on 2026-11-21. The game remembers out loud that this god does not play the usual way.

In the player's words:

"Nobody, in the sense you mean. I don't want a council that carries my intent. They run themselves, by the old way."

"I'm not asking you to give them a ruler, a priest or a chosen one. If you want to offer me helpers, I'd consider a keeper of one hamlet who has noticed the swarms and quietly wonders why, someone I can watch before I speak to them. I would not accept an advisor who is also my mouth."

"I'd rather be misread honestly than interpreted by someone who says they speak for me."

## What the god has

At landing: nothing. No seat, no tended hive, and no one who knows what it is.

Designed, not yet serving (`civs/sallowfolk/people/orrin-teal.md`): Orrin Teal, about sixty, widowed, a keeper of nine skeps in the highest hamlet and a hedge-layer in winter. He does not know the god. How he may be won is for play; the player set no way and set nothing he will do. Even won, he is not to be the god's mouth.

## The people's own order

- Each hamlet has a reeve, chosen from among its older households.
- The reeves meet at the river crossing when something touches the whole valley. They decide for themselves. The meeting is not the god's.
- Inside a hamlet the reeve hears both sides with the households of the quarrel present and the elders sitting by, and proposes a settlement, which the households take or do not. Only what touches the whole valley goes on to the reeves' meeting.
- A quarrel over a borrowed swarm goes to the bees: the households say the matter aloud to the hive and take what the hive does next as the answer. The god takes no part.

## What this means for every agent

- No text gives this god a court, priests, officers, a mouth, or any say in the reeves' meeting.
- No one acts for the god. Unplayed time runs on `player/conduct-profile.md` alone, cautious and in character, and the people drift by their own lights. The DM said this plainly and the player accepted it on 2026-11-21: "That is what I asked for."
- No agent writes Orrin Teal as knowing the god, serving it or carrying its word before play has made it so.
- A decision of the god's reaches the Sallowfolk only by what the god does in person, and they may read it wrong.
