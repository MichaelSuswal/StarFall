# Follow-up questions for Day 3

Campaign: sim-04. Written by the Keeper after Night 2, for the DM to weave into Day 3. Few. Each comes from the god as it now stands and bears on its people. Each has a form for the fiction and an (oog) fallback. The DM writes the player's answer under each before the close.

## 1. A god with no voice

In the fiction: "You do not speak. When someone below is meant to carry what you intend while you are elsewhere, how do they come to know what it is?"
(oog): Your god has no voice and is read, not heard. If anyone among the people is to act on its intent in time you do not play, how do they learn that intent, and how would they know they had read it wrong?

Answer (Day 3 table, 2026-11-21): "They don't, except crudely: a pattern in the bees that a keeper may read as a warning, a welcome or a sign. That's the only language I have. They'd learn they read me wrong when the thing doesn't happen or something else does. I'm fine with that. I'd rather be misread honestly than interpreted by someone who says they speak for me. The one exception is a stung person, who feels a hum when I'm near and gets nothing else." No one among the people is to act on the god's intent in unplayed time: helpers were refused.

## 2. Whose hives

In the fiction: "What you ask of the bees, the bees do not do for themselves, and someone's winter is shorter for it. Among those you tend, who keeps hives, and who answers to whom when one goes into winter short?"
(oog): The Working's cost falls on hives, your own people's the same as any. Do the people you tend keep bees, and how do they settle it among themselves when a hive is short and nobody knows why?

Answer (Day 3 table, 2026-11-21): They keep bees, in straw skeps and hollow-log hives. "I do. The keeper will blame the weather, the bees or herself, and I'll have no way to say otherwise in words. What I'll do is move stores from a surplus hive in my reach to the short one and not work that hive again that year. Beyond that, nobody answers to anybody, and I'd rather you didn't give the keeper a way to find out. The cost should sit on me, as something I carry and she doesn't know about." The DM told the player that moving stores is the Working and counts as a day of work on both hives, and that it will neither hand the keeper a way to find out nor shield the god from one.

## 3. Being alongside

In the fiction: "You said they would grow up beside you without knowing. On the day you land, do they know there is anything to know?"
(oog): You bent the premise toward tending a people who do not know they are steered. At the start, what do they believe about you, if anything: nothing at all, a good year in the bees, something they have a name for?

Answer (Day 3 table, 2026-11-21): They do not know there is anything to know. "They do not know I am a god." They hold an old saying, "older than me, that swarms settle on the right houses", and that there is a luck over the valley. "I don't want to have caused it. If the world gives it a cause, I'll find out in play. What they believe it means, the world decides."
