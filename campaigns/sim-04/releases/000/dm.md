# Release to the DM: the landing

Campaign: sim-04. Year 1 opens. Written by the Keeper on creation night 3b, 2026-11-22. Everything the Thrum senses and knows at the moment it comes down, and nothing else. Every distance, direction and sight line is measured from the map. The first morning story and its painting request are written from this file, by the first-morning rule in `engine/rules/morning-story.md`. Nothing here is resolved; the board is set and the player moves.

## The moment

First light, early spring. The Thrum comes down as one swarm onto a low rise at the edge of a water meadow, on the floor of a broad river valley, in grid cell B4. It is cold: cold enough that nothing with wings is abroad before the sun is well up.

## Its body, as it finds it

One swarm, whole, at full number. By its own card it does not fly at night, in rain or in cold, so at this hour it is clustered and slow, and it stays so until the sun has warmed the air. Nothing is wrong with it. Its past is fog, as its outline gives it.

## What its senses give it

- Cold, and the warmth at the heart of its own cluster. Its own hum, and no other like it.
- Wet ground. The river is loud and brown with meltwater. Snow is off the valley floor and still lies in the shade of the upper woods.
- Woodsmoke on the air, faint.
- Little in flower: catkins on the willow and hazel, blackthorn just breaking on the hedges, the first small things low in the grass on banks that face the sun. For a thing that reads the world by bloom, the country is nearly silent, and will not be for long: in a few weeks it flowers in turn, from the valley floor upward.
- Of its own kind: nothing. No weight and no stillness in the air about it. Nothing like it is near enough to feel, and at any greater distance there is nothing to feel at all. It knows, as every god here knows, that others like it came down when it did. It knows nothing more of them.

## What is in view

From the valley floor a tall mortal's eye sees level ground out to about 3 miles and things at ground level within about 2; higher ground shows on the skyline.

- The valley itself: water meadows, flooded in places; hedged fields and bare orchards on the lower slopes; old woods in the side valleys; brown heather upland above them on both sides.
- A river about a mile to the south, about 3 miles of it in view, running from the west toward the south-east, its line marked by willows.
- High brown ground on the skyline to the south, its nearest edge about 18 miles off.
- High brown ground on the skyline to the north, its nearest edge about 21 miles off.
- Lower ground along the valley to the west, about 25 miles off.
- Smoke, and nothing of the places it rises from: about 3 miles north; 6 south-west; 10 south-east; 11 north-west; 12 east; 15 west; 18 north-east; 20 south-east; 22 east; 23 north-west; 32 south-east; 35 east.
- Hedges that were laid by hand, and not this year. Orchard trees pruned. Grazed turf.
- Low in the north-east, a blue light larger than the moon, a little more than half a fist held at arm's length above the skyline, bright enough to throw a faint shadow before the sun is up.

Not in view from this ground: any track or road, any building, any person.

## What it knows

- That it has come down among people, to tend, as the player set it. It has met none of them.
- No name of any place, river, hill or person. No one has spoken to it. The names the player gave on Day 3 stay out of the fiction until someone says them in its hearing; they go to the player in one (oog) line.
- Its own powers, as its card gives them. Two things the season and the hour bear on: it cannot fly until the day warms; and its Listening does nothing until a valley is chosen, and is thin in early spring wherever it is opened.
- Nothing of the land beyond what it sees.

## Its people this morning

They do not know it exists. Lambing has begun; everyone is watching the blossom for frost; stores are thin, as every early spring. Their state is in `campaigns/sim-04/civs/sallowfolk/state.md`. The Thrum knows none of this until it sees or hears it.
