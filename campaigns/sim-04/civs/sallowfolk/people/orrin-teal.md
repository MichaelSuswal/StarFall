# Orrin Teal

Campaign: sim-04. Created by the player on creation Day 3, 2026-11-21. Designed, not yet serving.

Who he is, in the player's words: "Orrin Teal, about sixty, keeps nine skeps in the highest hamlet, where the upland starts. Widowed, a hedge-layer in winter, dry and unhurried." From the player's first sketch of him: "a keeper of one hamlet who has noticed the swarms and quietly wonders why, someone I can watch before I speak to them."

What he has noticed, as the player wishes it: "wild swarms in his hamlet settle each spring in the roofless stone hall by the old track and never take, as if they were reading something and moving on. I'd like that to be his private puzzle, not a custom." The hall, where it stands, and what wild swarms do there are the world's. This is a wish for the Keeper to place; the DM confirmed none of it and told the player that if the world places no such hall, the player will be told what he has noticed instead before the god lands.

At landing he does not know the god exists. He holds no office under it and carries nothing for it.

What he will do is his. "I set who he is. I don't set what he'll do."
