# The Sallowfolk: starting state

Campaign: sim-04. Population as agreed on creation Day 3, 2026-11-21. Stores, treasure, trade, strength and mood are the Keeper's to set on Night 3a.

population: about 1,500, in a dozen or so hamlets and farmsteads, with no town

Set by the Keeper on creation night 3a, 2026-11-22, for the morning the god lands (early spring).

hamlets: thirteen, with their farmsteads; the highest stands where the upland starts
stores: thin, as every early spring. Grain enough for about seven months of the year in an ordinary year, and it is the hungry gap now; the rest is bought with honey and wax. Last autumn's honey and wax are mostly traded or eaten. Mead in the cellars.
treasure: little, and all in small things: wax, tapers, mead, a few bronze tools to a household. No common store.
trade: honey, wax, mead and tapers go out a few times a year, up the track and down the river; salt, iron and bronze come back the same ways. Rating 3 of 5 for a people this small (2 for their number and place, one step for their keeping).
strength: none trained and no walls. About 320 adults who would turn out with billhooks.
mood: steady and inward. Lambing has begun; everyone is watching the blossom for frost.
