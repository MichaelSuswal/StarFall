# The world, as the Thrum knows it on waking

Campaign: sim-04. Written by the Keeper on creation night 3b, 2026-11-22, for the DM, in words the player may read. Public facts only. Everything here is what the Thrum's own senses give it where it stands, or what the player settled at the creation table. At the moment of landing no one has spoken to it.

## Where it stands

Grid cell B4. A low rise at the edge of a water meadow, on the floor of a broad river valley.

## The land as it first sees it

Measured from the map, by the sight rule every landing uses.

The ground is a broad river valley of water meadows, hedged fields, orchards and old woods between two heather uplands. The valley floor is low, a few hundred feet up; the mildest country there is, with late wet springs. The valley runs about 77 miles east to west and about 50 north to south.

From the valley floor a tall mortal's eye sees level ground out to about 3 miles, and things at ground level within about 2 miles. Higher ground shows on the skyline as far as its height carries.

In view:
- A river, about a mile to the south, with about 3 miles of its course in view, running from the west toward the south-east. Its line is marked by willows, and it floods the meadows in spring.
- High brown ground on the skyline to the south, its nearest edge about 18 miles off.
- High brown ground on the skyline to the north, its nearest edge about 21 miles off.
- Lower ground along the valley to the west, about 25 miles off.
- Smoke in a dozen places along the valley, nothing of the places themselves: about 3 miles north; 6 south-west; 10 south-east; 11 north-west; 12 east; 15 west; 18 north-east; 20 south-east; 22 east; 23 north-west; 32 south-east; 35 east.
- Low in the north-east, a blue light larger than the moon, standing a little more than half a fist held at arm's length above the skyline. It is there at first light and does not set with the night.

## The season and the hour

Early spring, late and wet, as the Day 3 brief gives it. The year opens in this season every year. The hour of landing is first light.

## Its people

About 1,500, in thirteen hamlets with their farmsteads and no town, as their record gives them (`campaigns/sim-04/civs/sallowfolk/record.md`, `state.md`). Hedge-layers, orchardists, grazers of the upland turf and keepers of bees. They run themselves. They do not know the Thrum exists and are waiting for nothing. Their own tellings of their past are in their record as the player agreed them; whatever more the old tell reaches the Thrum only as they tell it, in play.

## What the world's people can make

About this level: before the medieval era, no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a ceiling. What the Thrum knows of more may be brought in; it is built here by the people, from what the world has, at the pace of the work, and nothing is granted by naming it.

## The land it knows, and the land only rumored

Known: the valley it stands in, as far as it can see, and nothing more. Its map holds exactly this (`campaigns/sim-04/player/map-knowledge.json`): the valley seen; the two uplands and the lower ground to the west as skylines; a stretch of river; smoke in twelve places, each a rumor with a grid cell.

Rumored: nothing yet. No one has told it anything.

## Names

For the DM: a name reaches the Thrum only when it is first said in its hearing. Until then none of these is used in the fiction; the names the player gave on Day 3 are given to the player in one (oog) line, as the first-morning rule says.

- Its people call themselves the Sallowfolk and their valley Sallowdene. Both are the player's names.
- Its people call the whole world Lowlight.
- Its people call the blue light the Taper.

Every other name, of a river, a hamlet, a hill, a track or a person, is learned in play. The hamlets are unnamed and left for play.

## What is not in this file

Anything the Thrum could not know where it stands.
