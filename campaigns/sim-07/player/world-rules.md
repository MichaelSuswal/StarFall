# What is so of every god here

For the DM, in words the player may hear. These are facts of being a god in
this world, known from the moment of landing.

## Knowing another god
A god knows another god only close at hand, about as near as one can make out
a face. It comes as a nearness and a weight, and once the two stand before
each other it cannot be mistaken. It comes to both at the same nearness. At
any greater distance a god feels nothing of another: no pull, no direction.
