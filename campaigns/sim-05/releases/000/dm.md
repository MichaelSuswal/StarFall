# Release to the DM: the landing

Campaign sim-05. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3, 2026-11-23. Everything Ostrane senses and knows at the moment of landing. The first morning story and its painting request are written from this, by the first-morning section of `engine/rules/morning-story.md`. Nothing here is resolved: the god has landed and has done nothing yet.

Read first: `player/corrections.md`, "After Day 3", items 11 to 14, which give what the Day 3 brief promised and the land does not hold.

## The moment

Early spring. First light, the hour before sunrise. The god stands where it came down, on a low grassy rise at the edge of a high plain. Grid cell C1. No one is near. No one has spoken to it.

## What its body tells it

- Cold. Frost lies white in the hollows and is already wet on the rise. The ground gives a little underfoot: the low ground is sodden with melt, and the tracks are only beginning to dry.
- The air is still and smells of wet grass, old snow and, faintly, wood smoke.
- Cranes are calling somewhere toward the river. A lark is up.
- The coat is damp at the hem. The chain is in its hand and the satchel on its shoulder, as its card gives them.
- Its sense of the ground reaches one chain's length round it and no farther, as its card gives: a low rise of firm turf over gravel, falling gently away on every side. It holds no ground.

## What it senses of others like it

Nothing. At a distance it feels nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. It knows that others like it came down when it did, and nothing more. It will know one only by meeting it (`player/world-rules.md`).

## What is in view

By the eyes of a tall mortal, in clear air at first light. Every distance and direction is measured from the map. Level ground is in view out to about 3 miles; things at ground level are picked out within about 2 miles.

- Flat open grassland on every side, last year's growth lying brown with green coming through it.
- An old paved road, about a mile to the east. About 3 miles of it are in view, a straight pale line running from the north-east toward the south.
- A braided river, about 2 miles to the north: many channels between gravel bars, running high and the color of clay. About 2 miles of it are in view, going off to the north-west.
- A worn track along the river's near bank, about 2 miles to the north.
- One thread of smoke, about 3 miles to the north-east, rising straight in the still air. It is a town's. Nothing of the town itself is in view from here: no wall, no roof, no ford.
- A line of bare hills on the skyline to the north, about 10 miles off at its nearest, and behind it snow heights, very high.
- More snow heights on the skyline far to the west, about 100 miles off, small and sharp.
- In the sky, a blue star, well up to the south, paling as the light comes.

Not in view from the landing: the ford where the road meets the river; any gap in the line of hills; any person or beast nearer than the smoke.

## What it knows

Only the above. `player/map-knowledge.json` holds it: the plain as seen, the road, river, track and heights as things in view with plain descriptions, the smoke as a rumor in C1. The god knows no name for any of them.

Names the player gave on Day 3 are the player's own and are not yet the god's: it has heard no one say them. What the god's people know of their land, by their own names, is not in this release. It is in `civs/carrow/knowledge.md`, for what a mortal may say when the god meets one; a name from it becomes the god's when a mortal first says it in the god's hearing.

## Its people, as far as the god knows

It expects a people, as every god does. It does not know them and they do not know it. It lands with no helpers in fact (`player/hierarchy.md`): no one acts for it, and nothing is done in its name until an office is won in play.

## Held for the player

- The open conduct line "Start the first line from the gap in the hills" carries "check with the player before acting". It is not run and is not assumed. The other first actions in the conduct profile carry no mark and are the player's to play.
- No message, envoy or question is waiting for the god.

## Counts

No recurring thing is counted in this release. If the god or its people come to notice one, its count for the year comes from `tools/phenomena.py` and from nowhere else.
