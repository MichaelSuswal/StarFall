# The world as Ostrane wakes to it

Campaign sim-05. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3, 2026-11-23, for the DM. Public facts only: what the god knows at the moment of landing, from its own senses. No one has spoken to it yet. Everything else is found in play.

## Where and when

- Landing: grid cell C1.
- Season: early spring. Each year opens in early spring.
- Hour of landing: first light, the hour before sunrise.

## The land as it first sees it

Measured from the map, by the eyes of a tall mortal standing where the god stands.

The god stands on a low rise at the edge of a plain: high grassland, flat, with wet ground toward a river. Last year's growth lies brown with green coming through it. Level ground is in view out to about 3 miles, and things at ground level are picked out within about 2 miles.

- An old paved road, about a mile to the east, about 3 miles of it in view, running from the north-east toward the south.
- A braided river, about 2 miles to the north, about 2 miles of it in view, running off to the north-west.
- A worn track, about 2 miles to the north, along the river.
- One thread of smoke, about 3 miles to the north-east: a town, seen only as its smoke.
- A line of bare hills on the skyline to the north, about 10 miles off at its nearest, with snow heights behind it.
- More snow heights on the skyline far to the west, about 100 miles off.

The ford where the road meets the river is not in view from the landing. Neither is any gap in the hills.

## The land it knows, and the land only rumored

- Seen: the plain it stands on.
- In view, and so known as shapes with no names: the old paved road, the braided river, the worn track, the line of hills under snow heights, the far snow heights.
- Rumored: the smoke to the north-east (C1). The god does not yet know what makes it.
- Nothing else. `player/map-knowledge.json` holds exactly this.

## Its people

About 5,000, with a town. Farmers, drovers, ferrymen and carters of a ford on an old road: wood and stone, a market, a granary, a wall of earth and timber. They are there before the god, with their own history, and do not know it (`civs/carrow/record.md`, `player/people-meeting.md`). The god lands with no helpers in fact.

Names the player gave on Day 3 (the Carrow, Carrowford) are the player's own. The god has not yet heard them from anyone.

## Technology

The starting level: before the medieval era, no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a ceiling. What the god knows beyond it is built here by the people, from what the world has, at the pace of the work.

## Magic

Nothing of it is in view at landing. For pricing: it is simple, and it costs the worker.

## What the people call the world

For the DM: these are the people's words. A name like these reaches the god only when it is first said in its hearing, and is written into its knowledge then.

- The world: the Wide Ground.
- The star: the Old Candle.

What the Carrow know of their land and their neighbors, by their own names, is in `civs/carrow/knowledge.md`.
