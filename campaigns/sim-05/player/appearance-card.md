# Appearance card: Ostrane

Written: creation, Day 2

Called the Chain or the Measure.

A single human-seeming body. Tall and spare, built like a man who walks everywhere. Skin the grey of old iron. Eyes of dull copper. Hair cropped. A ridge of fine scale-like lines along the forearms, like survey marks. Stands very straight.

The chain: a hundred equal links of one foot each, part of the body, carried and laid by hand. It is not a weapon.

Clothes and gear, all ordinary: a long coat of undyed wool, plain and dark, with deep pockets, worn at the elbows and cuffs. A leather satchel on a cross strap, holding a small wooden mallet, a bundle of iron-shod stakes and a sack of chalk. A lead plumb bob on a waxed line. A folding rule of boxwood and brass. A hand-ruled tally book with a stub of pencil on a cord. One old, much-worn stake with notches down one side, kept in the inner pocket of the coat and not usually seen.

Manner: patient and exact. Whistles when working something out.

Not described by the player, and not to be invented: age of the face, voice, height in figures, anything of the senses.
