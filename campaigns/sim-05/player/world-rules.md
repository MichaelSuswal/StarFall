# What is so of every god here

For the DM, in words the player may hear. These are facts of being a god in
this world, known from the moment of landing.

## Knowing another god
At a distance you feel nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. You know one only by meeting it. When another god stands before you, the knowing that this one is no mortal comes on slowly, by nearness alone, and one who works at seeming mortal may hold it off for a while. Within arm's reach it is sure, and no god passes for a mortal at a touch. The knowing tells you what stands there. It tells you nothing of its temper, its powers or its mind.

## Bodies
One body. A god is in one place at a time.
