# Rejected and bent premises

Campaign sim-05.

## Day 1

None.

Requests not to be told something: none.

## Not rejections

- The closing remark ("on the record as a conqueror", "a seat, a court and captains"). It takes the premise as stated, to lead, build, rule and spread, and bends nothing. It is kept under `## Also said` in `survey-day1.md`. "I'll decide on helpers once I know what they cost" is a decision put off, not a refusal of helpers and not a question asked of the DM.
- Question 2, river country opening onto high plains, with the coast as a second choice. Picks among the lands offered. The passes, forts and harbor are wishes, and the DM confirmed none as fact.
- Question 6, "Closer to a rumor most people doubt, with a wise-woman in the odd valley." A point inside the range offered.
- Question 8, "well above a mortal sorcerer", short of the one-eyed man. A point inside the range offered.
- Question 10, "I don't want it to come from monsters." An answer to what mortals should fear, not a refusal of the question.
- Question 11, "Salt and iron, or whichever one". Answered inside the question; the player leaves the choice between them open.
- Question 12, old roads and a sealed door. Both are among the question's own examples.
- Question 16, one body. An offered option. "I send captains" is a wish about the player's own god.
- Question 18, "I can't picture it yet, which is the point." A general wish for discovery. It names nothing the player asks not to be told.

## Day 2

None rejected.

Requests not to be told something: one, kept in the sealed record.

### Bent by the DM

- The return. The player wished a return of years, with a cost growing with distance from its marks. The DM wrote it on the standing return scale instead (the season rung), with the distance paid in the walk back. The player agreed.
- The whole map's price. The player offered three terms as its price (paced ground only, half absent while reading, the ground without the people). The DM wrote them as Limits and asked a separate drawback. The player chose blind beyond.
- The trade. The player offered the chain at a quarter of walking pace against a third, to buy counts of people on the map. The DM rewrote the weakness as three quarters of whatever rate the Keeper confirms, and narrowed the counts to groups of a hundred or more, as round figures. The player agreed.

### Not rejections

- "Most gods want worship. I want compliance with a standard." A statement of what this god wants from its people. It takes the premise, to lead, build, rule and spread, as given. What it means for the people is Day 3's.
- "I'll leave the court, the captains and the helpers for Day 3." A decision still put off, not a refusal.
- "I can't feel land I haven't walked", "sea, sky and the inside of a mind". The player's own limits on its god, written on the card as Limits.
