# Backstory outline: Ostrane

Written: creation, Day 2, 2026-11-23. The outline, in the player's words. The rest is the Keeper's, and the god's past is foggy to the god.

> What is falling is a claim. I'm not a thing that owns land. I'm the act of measuring it and holding it, with the chain, the stake and the boundary stone's authority all in one.

> I was the boundary-keeper of something old. It wasn't a king. It was the one who walked the edge of a place every year and put the stones back up. The place had a name I've lost, and it had a people. The lost thing is the roll, the single written record of where the line ran. I remember that there was a line. I remember the sound of the stakes going in. I don't remember what was inside it, and I don't remember why I stopped walking it. That's why I fall to claim ground. I want one line to be kept long enough that it can't be lost the way that one was.

Of the old stake the god carries: "One old stake, much worn, with notches cut down one side that I can't read. I keep it in the inner pocket of the coat. I don't know where I got it."

More was said privately and is kept sealed.
