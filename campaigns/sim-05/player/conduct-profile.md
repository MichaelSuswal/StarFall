# Conduct profile: Ostrane

## Now

As of creation Day 2, 2026-11-23.

Wants: compliance with a standard, not worship. To be the unit, so that people say "that's the Measure's mile" the way they say "that's a fair weight". To claim and hold ground by pacing and marking it, and to have a line kept long enough that it cannot be lost. On the record from Day 1 as a conqueror who wants a seat, a court and captains, and an empire whose laws, roads and habits outlast it.

Refusals: it will not break a given word to its letter. It will not put to the sword a people who have surrendered under a line it has agreed. It will not salt or burn ground it means to hold, or let its own captains do it in its name.

How it goes about things: patient, exact, ruthless when it pays. It does not rage; it counts. Early to everything, and calm while it waits. Bad at mercy offered for its own sake. Keeps its word to the letter and expects the same to the letter. Means to be careful what it promises and to write its treaties short. Means to scout ground before taking it, send captains ahead and hire a surveyor. Wants to be able to trade, and sometimes cede, ground by treaty. Holds ground openly.

Fears: ground it cannot read; "the one mapped place that proves a map wrong". Deep water at night unsettles it.

Helpers: not yet decided. The player put the decision off until it knows what helpers cost, which is Day 3's to settle.

Unsaid wants are kept sealed.
