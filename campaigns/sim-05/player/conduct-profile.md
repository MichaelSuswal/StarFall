# Conduct profile: Ostrane

## Now

As of creation Day 3, 2026-11-23.

Wants: compliance with a standard, not worship. To be the unit, so that people say "that's the Measure's mile" the way they say "that's a fair weight". To claim and hold ground by pacing and marking it, and to have a line kept long enough that it cannot be lost. On the record from Day 1 as a conqueror who wants a seat, a court and captains, and an empire whose laws, roads and habits outlast it.

Refusals: it will not break a given word to its letter. It will not put to the sword a people who have surrendered under a line it has agreed. It will not salt or burn ground it means to hold, or let its own captains do it in its name. It will not tear down the Carrow's own measures.

How it goes about things: patient, exact, ruthless when it pays. It does not rage; it counts. Early to everything, and calm while it waits. Bad at mercy offered for its own sake. Keeps its word to the letter and expects the same to the letter. Means to be careful what it promises and to write its treaties short. Means to scout ground before taking it. Wants to be able to trade, and sometimes cede, ground by treaty. Holds ground openly. Tells the truth when asked what it is. Speaks or writes its orders short and plain. Means to earn its offices one at a time.

How it means to be found: a stranger walking up from the river with a chain, who begins by asking to see the oldest disputed fence.

First things it means to do, in the order the player gave them. Ask to see the oldest disputed fence. Set its foot and its chain in iron on the market wall beside the Carrow's measures, with the ratio cut between them, saying plainly how the two compare. Try to win the Warden of Marks first of the offices, offering it to the best fence-viewer. Start the first line from the gap in the hills, a wish the world places (check with the player before acting).

Fears: ground it cannot read; "the one mapped place that proves a map wrong". Deep water at night unsettles it.

Helpers: designed, not yet serving. The god lands with no helpers in fact (`player/hierarchy.md`). Until an office is won in play, no one acts for the god while it is away, and unplayed time runs on this profile alone: cautious, in character, and with nothing done in its name by any mortal.

Unsaid wants are kept sealed.
