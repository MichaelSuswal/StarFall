# Power card: Ostrane

Written: creation, Day 2, 2026-11-23. Read back to the player and agreed.

## Form

Ostrane, called the Chain or the Measure. One body; a god is in one place at a time. Human-seeming: tall and spare, built like a man who walks everywhere. Skin the grey of old iron, eyes dull copper, hair cropped, a ridge of fine scale-like lines along the forearms, like survey marks. It stands straight and cannot stand to see a crooked line. It whistles when it is working something out and cannot stop it.

The form by itself does what a strong walking man's body does. Senses the player has not described are unsettled and are asked about in play.

Knowing another god: at a distance the god feels nothing of another god, no pull and no direction. It knows one only by meeting it. The knowing comes on slowly, by nearness alone, and one who works at seeming mortal may hold it off for a while. Within arm's reach it is sure, and no god passes for a mortal at a touch. It tells what stands there and nothing of its temper, its powers or its mind.

When the body is broken: see The return, under Powers.

### Definitions

- The chain: a hundred links of one foot each, a hundred feet in all, part of the god's body like an arm. It cannot be stolen; cutting it is a wound. Swung at someone it is an ordinary chain.
- Paced: walked on the god's own feet, on ground, the chain laid length after length, the line closing on its own start. Not ridden, not carried, not done by another.
- Marked: a stone or stake set at the start and at every turning of the line, by the god or by its people behind it.
- Kept: the god's people walk the line and stand its marks back up at least once in each year.
- Claimed ground: what lies inside a line that is paced, closed, marked and kept.

### Things carried

All ordinary. None does more than an ordinary object.

- A long coat of undyed wool, plain and dark, with deep pockets, worn at the elbows and cuffs.
- A leather satchel on a cross strap, with a small wooden mallet, a bundle of iron-shod stakes and a sack of chalk.
- A lead plumb bob on a waxed line.
- A folding rule of boxwood and brass, marked in feet and in links.
- A tally book, ruled by hand, with a stub of pencil on a cord, for counts and distances.
- One old stake, much worn, with notches cut down one side that the god cannot read, kept in the inner pocket of the coat. The god does not know where it got it.

## Powers

### True ground
Status: main
Description: Where the god stands, it knows the true distance, slope and bearing of the ground around it, the same in dark, fog or rain. It is the surface as it lies, with what is built on it as shape: a wall, a ditch, a road.
Drawback: The true measure. The god cannot state, write, draw or put its name to a measure it knows to be false, whether a distance, a bearing, a boundary, a count or a weight. It may keep silent.
Limits settled at creation:
- Distance: on claimed ground it reaches to where clear daylight sight would reach from that spot. Off claimed ground it reaches one chain's length around the god (the drawback of The whole map). The god lands holding nothing, so it is one chain's length everywhere until its first line closes.
- Light and dark: the same in dark, fog or rain.
- Falling and footing: it needs the god's feet on the ground. It is silent on a boat, a saddle, a cart, a floor above the earth, or falling.
- Water: shallows the god could wade read as ground; deep water is blank.
- It shows nothing under the surface (no ore, salt or water below) and nothing inside a closed building.
- Other gods, the god's own people: it shows no person, no animal and no god.
- Being watched: nothing shows on the god when it works.
- Mind: it rules none and reads none.
- More than ordinary craft: a place made or held by more than ordinary craft may read wrong or not at all. Unruled until it happens.
- Use: always on, no count of uses.
- It shows what is there, never why it is so. Priced by shape against the mystery standard; for the Keeper's Night 2 review.
- The drawback binds the god's own mouth and hand. What its captains say on their own is theirs.
- Not applicable: carrying.
- The player's first wording, kept as intent: "Wherever I stand, I know the true distance, slope and bearing of the ground, the way you know where your own hand is."
- Text, limits and price settled at creation. Ledger: L-0001.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The paced claim
Status: main
Description: When the god paces a line closed and it is marked, the ground inside begins to feel the god's. Everyone who lives inside feels it as a settled, ordered sense that the ground is held, and by whom. It comes on over a season of living there.
Drawback: No quiet ground. The feeling tells everyone inside exactly where the god's line runs and that they are within it: its own people, an enemy, a spy, a guest. The god can never hold ground secretly, and it cannot deny a claim it has paced.
Limits settled at creation:
- Mind: it is a feeling and never an order. It makes no one obey, stay, love the god or change a decision. An enemy living inside feels it and may hate it.
- It gives no title in anyone's law and no yield from the land.
- It claims only what the god personally paces.
- Water: a line cannot be paced across water the god cannot wade.
- Being watched: pacing is plain to anyone watching.
- Other gods: another god who enters claimed ground perceives that it is held. Anything more, on a god, is unruled until it happens.
- The god's own people: they feel it as everyone inside does.
- What breaks it: a stretch of the line unmarked for a full year opens the line. The feeling fades over a season, until the god paces the gap again.
- Use: no limit on how much or how often but the god's own legs.
- Pace: chaining a line is slower than walking it. Set at the table at a third of the god's walking pace, before the weakness below. Provisional, for the Keeper.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying.
- The player's first wording, kept as intent: "When I pace a boundary with the chain, the land inside it starts to feel mine. Those who live inside it feel it too, as a settled, ordered sense. It isn't coercion. It's more like the pull of a well-kept road."
- Text, limits and price settled at creation. Ledger: L-0002.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### What is held
Status: main
Description: A claimed line resists being unmade. Its marks do not drift, sink or get lost by accident or weather. Those who live inside do not forget or mistake where it runs, so a false survey, a forged deed or a bribed witness does not shift what people on that ground know. Mortal working aimed at moving, hiding or erasing the line costs its worker a good deal more than it otherwise would.
Drawback: The letter holds it. A boundary the god has agreed by its own given word, spoken or written, it cannot cross under arms and cannot pace across, until the other party releases it or throws down the marks themselves. Their breaking faith elsewhere does not release it.
Limits settled at creation:
- It does not stop feet: an army walks across a held line as across any ground, and marks can be pulled up by hand.
- It does not stop a judge being bought. The bought ruling does not change what the ground's own people know.
- It holds only while the line is kept.
- The letter binds the god's body and the god's own word, not a word a captain gave.
- Other gods: against another god's working, unruled until it happens.
- More than ordinary craft: how much more a mortal working against the line costs its worker is the Keeper's to set. Provisional, for the Keeper.
- Use: it works on every claimed line at once, with no count.
- Not applicable: light and dark, distance, falling, carrying, water, being watched.
- The player's first wording, kept as intent: "What I hold, I hold. A border I've walked and my people have kept resists being argued, bribed or magicked out of existence more than ordinary ground does."
- Text, limits and price settled at creation. Ledger: L-0003.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The whole map
Status: main
Description: Wherever the god is, on ground or off it, it may turn its attention to all its claimed ground at once and know it as it is at that moment, by the same sense as True ground and with the same blanks: nothing under the surface, nothing inside a closed building, deep water blank. It knows what has been done to that ground: a wall breached, a road cut, a bridge down, a fire, a flood, a field trampled flat. By the trade below, it also knows bodies of people gathered or moving together a hundred or more, as a round figure.
Drawback: Blind beyond. Off claimed ground, True ground reaches only one chain's length around the god.
Limits settled at creation:
- Claimed ground only. A line that opens leaves the map at once.
- No who and no why. No faces, no banners, no telling the god's own from others, nothing of what they carry. No exact counts, and no group under a hundred.
- While the god reads, its body is half absent: it can walk and answer slowly, and it cannot fight, bargain or work the chain.
- The god may leave the read when it chooses. Anyone speaking its name within the body's hearing pulls it out of the read, friend or not. (Added at the player's request, as wording.)
- Distance, light and dark, water: any distance, day or night; deep water is blank.
- Other gods: it shows no god as a god.
- Mind: it rules none and reads none.
- Use: as often as the god likes.
- It shows effects, never causes. Priced by shape against the mystery standard; for the Keeper's Night 2 review.
- The three terms the player offered as its price (paced ground only, half absent while reading, the ground without the people) are conditions of the power and are written here as Limits; they bought nothing.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, being watched.
- The player's first wording, kept as intent: "I want to see every inch of my claimed ground at once, wherever I am. I'd know every army crossing, every wall breached and every road cut, in real time, as a map that updates in my head."
- Text, limits and price settled at creation. Ledger: L-0004.
Rulings:
Trades: the round counts of people were bought by the weakness The slow chain (see Weaknesses taken). Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0005.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The return
Status: main
Description: When the god's body is broken, it gathers again over a season at the nearest standing mark of its own claimed line, walking out of fog with the chain. The walk from that mark to wherever it was needed is added.
Drawback: Each return costs the god an item of memory or habit, a single item, taken by a logged roll from the god's own record, the things it has done and the lines of conduct it holds. The god does not choose it.
Limits settled at creation:
- The rung is the season rung of the standing return scale, fixed at creation and never chosen at each return. The faster rung was offered and declined.
- A body that is held or shut out is not broken, and this power does not answer it.
- If the god holds no claimed ground on the day, it gathers at the nearest standing boundary mark of anyone's setting. Provisional, for the Keeper.
- The player's wish, kept as intent: "I wish to be re-gathered at the nearest set mark of ground I hold, a stone or stake of my own line. I'd go there slowly, as a body walking out of a fog, and I'd keep my chain." The player offered a return of years with a cost growing with distance; the standing scale is the same for every god, so it was written on the scale instead.
- The player asks that the roll be logged where the Keeper can show it, so the god knows what was taken once it is gone. Passed to the Keeper.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, being watched, other gods, the god's own people.
- Text, rung and price settled at creation. Ledger: L-0006.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The slow chain
Ostrane's chaining runs at three quarters of the plain chaining rate, whatever the Keeper confirms that rate to be: a quarter of its walking pace as the rate stood at the table. Nothing hurries it, no helper, craft or power. Anyone who knows it can outpace the god's claiming, and a line half paced is no line. It bought the round counts of people on The whole map: groups of a hundred or more, as a round figure only. Offered by the player at a quarter against a third; rewritten by the DM against the confirmed rate, and the improvement narrowed, with the player's yes. Settled at creation. Ledger: L-0005.
