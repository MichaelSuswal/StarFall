# Power lean: Ostrane, for the Keeper

Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 2, 2026-11-23. Measured only against this god's own budget as priced at the table.

## The budget as priced

- True ground: small. Price: the true measure (cannot state a false measure).
- The paced claim: small to middling. Price: no quiet ground.
- What is held: small to middling. Price: the letter holds it.
- The whole map: large. Price: blind beyond, which cuts True ground to one chain's length off claimed ground.
- The return: the season rung, the first rung of the standing scale, at its standing price. The faster rung was offered and declined.
- Weakness taken: the slow chain. Bought: round counts of people on the whole map, groups of a hundred or more.

## Lean

About even at landing, and below even in its first seasons: the god lands holding no claimed ground, so the map is empty and True ground reaches one chain's length. It leans up as holdings grow, because the map and What is held scale with ground and nothing but the god's legs limits how much it claims. The round counts are the strongest single piece. The slow chain is a light weakness for what it bought; I narrowed the improvement instead of asking a second weakness. I read the whole as a slight lean up over a long game.

## Priced provisionally, for Night 2 review

1. True ground and The whole map are information powers, priced by shape: effects, never causes; surface only; nothing under the ground, nothing inside a closed building, no persons except the round counts. The DM does not know the mysteries.
2. "A place made or held by more than ordinary craft may read wrong or not at all" (True ground) is left unruled until it happens.
3. What is held: "costs its worker a good deal more than it otherwise would" rests on the given rule that mortal magic costs the worker. How much more is the Keeper's to set.
4. Limits that say what the powers do not find (no ore, salt or water below the surface; no yield from a claim) are written by shape and name nothing as real.
5. The chaining rate: a third of walking pace, set at the table without the pace table, provisional. The weakness is three quarters of whatever rate the Keeper confirms.
6. The return when the god holds no claimed ground: the nearest standing boundary mark of anyone's setting. Provisional.
7. The letter binds the god's body and own word, not a captain's word. Generous to the player; the player did not ask for it. Noted in case it reads as a loophole granted in advance.

## Granted that other gods perceive

- A god who enters claimed ground perceives that the ground is held. Anything acting on a god is unruled until it happens.
- The god's name spoken within its body's hearing pulls it out of a read of the map; anyone may do this, a god included.

## Requests passed on

- The player asks that the return's roll be logged where the Keeper can show it, so the god knows what was taken once it is gone.
- The god took the first rung of the return scale.
- Sealed answers given today are in the sealed unsaid file, with one that bears on the return's roll and one that asks a decision of the Keeper.
