# Corrections and answers for the DM

Written by the Keeper after Day 2, 2026-11-23, for the DM to put to the player at the opening of Day 3, before anything is played. In words the player may hear.

## The terms the table marked provisional

1. The chaining rate. Confirmed as set at the table: chaining a line runs at a third of the god's walking pace over the same ground, by the pace table. With The slow chain, Ostrane chains at a quarter of its walking pace. Nothing on the card changes. Ledger: L-0007.

2. Where the god gathers if it holds no claimed ground. Confirmed as written: the nearest standing boundary mark of anyone's setting. Nothing on the card changes. Ledger: L-0008.

3. The shape of True ground and The whole map. Confirmed, with one Limit to add, which needs the player's yes because it is new on the card:

   Under True ground, Limits: "It reads the ground and what stands on it. It gives no distance, height or bearing for anything in the sky."

   The whole map works by the same sense and takes the same Limit. The reason, for the player: the power was described and priced as a sense of ground, and the player's own account of the god sets the sky outside it ("I'm weak at the things that need no ground, such as sea, sky and the inside of a mind"). The Limit says so on the card, so that it never has to be argued in a scene. If the player agrees, write it as a dated entry, citing ledger L-0009. If the player does not agree, write the player's argument to `player/rulings-pending.md` and the Keeper rules on it tonight.

## A figure the table left to the Keeper

4. What is held. It is a figure for a Limit already agreed and needs no new yes; tell the player, and write it as a dated entry. Ledger: L-0010.

## The player's ask about the return

5. The player asked that the roll that takes an item be logged where the Keeper can show it, so that the player knows what was taken once it is gone. Granted. The roll is logged, as every roll is. After a return has happened, the Keeper tells the DM which item went, and the DM tells the player in (oog) and strikes the item by a dated entry. In the fiction the god no longer holds the item and does not know what it was. Nothing is shown before the return, and the god never chooses the item. Ledger: L-0011.
