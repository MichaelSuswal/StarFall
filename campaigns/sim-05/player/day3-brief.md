## The country the god comes down into

River country opening onto high plains. The god comes down on a low rise at the edge of a plain, above a river.

What the land shows from there, in early spring:

- On one side, flat open grassland, last year's growth lying brown with green coming through it.
- Below, the river: many channels between gravel bars, running high and cold and the color of clay with the melt.
- A line of hills, with a gap in it.
- Snow still lying on the far heights. The low ground is wet, and the tracks are beginning to dry.
- The straight line of an old paved road coming down to the river at a ford.
- A worn track along the bank.
- One thread of smoke.

That is all the land shows. Who made the smoke, how many they are, what they are called and how they live are Day 3's to settle with the player. Nothing here is a distance or a direction: those come from the map when it is drawn, and anything above that the finished map does not hold will be corrected in `player/corrections.md` before the first morning.

## The season

Early spring, as the player asked.

## What is so of the world for a people

- The peoples the god will find are at about this level: before the medieval era, no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a ceiling. The god may bring in what it knows, but it is built here by the people, from what the world has, at the pace of the work, and the god says how it is to be made; nothing is granted by naming it.
- Mortal magic, for pricing: it is simple, and it costs the worker. If the player's people have any, the Keeper fits it to the world tonight.
