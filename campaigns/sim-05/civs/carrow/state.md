# The Carrow: starting state

Campaign: sim-05. Population as agreed on creation Day 3, 2026-11-23. Stores, treasure, trade, strength and mood are the Keeper's to set on Night 3a.

population: about 5,000, with a town, Carrowford
stores: grain, beans and salted meat for about three months, enough to reach the early barley if the spring is kind, with the seed corn set apart; thin after the winter
treasure: modest: the market chest, the town's iron and bronze, cattle and horses, and debts owed at the ford
trade: ferry and ford tolls, cattle, horses, hides and flax, for salt and iron, which come by the old road and came short last year
strength: a paid watch of about thirty for the gate, the market and the ford; a muster of about six hundred drovers and farmers under a captain called up in danger. The eighty drilled soldiers are not yet raised
mood: stubborn and steady; worn by the fence quarrels; wary of strangers
