# The Judge of the Ford

Unnamed by the player; filed by office. Written at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-23.

Holds "an old seat that hears weights, debts and fence cases, held now by someone respected and tired." Has never heard the god's name. The player wants "a judge who can't be bought and a people who know it"; ruled at the table as character, oath and custom, not a binding, able to fail in play. What the Judge does is the Judge's.
