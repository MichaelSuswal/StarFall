# The Reeve of Carrowford

Unnamed by the player; filed by office. Written at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-23.

Holds an office of the town's own law, "an elected office, a year at a time, and the town knows how to fill it." Runs the town day to day: grain, labor, disputes, the market. Has never heard the god's name. Nothing else about the holder was set by the player. What the Reeve does is the Reeve's.
