# The best fence-viewer in Carrowford

Unnamed by the player; filed by office. Written at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-23.

A woman, one of the town's fence-viewers, who "settle where a hedge or ditch runs." In the player's words: "I'd take the best fence-viewer in the town and make her the first Warden, so the office grows from something they trust. She'd be one who already hates a moved stone. I'd like her to be one of the ones who disagrees with me."

Who she is, is set: the best of the fence-viewers, one who hates a moved stone. That she takes the office, and that she disagrees with the god, are heard and not promised. She has never heard the god's name.
