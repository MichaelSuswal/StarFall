# The world, as Vesper's god-self knows it on waking

Campaign: sim-02. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3, for the DM, in words the player may read. Public facts only: what the god can see from where it comes down, what the player agreed of its people, and what is so of the age. Every distance and direction is measured from the world as it was finally laid. Nothing here is hearsay, because no one has yet told the god anything.

## The season

Late autumn, at dusk. The leaves are mostly down. Frost has held in the shade all day and there is new snow on the highest ground. The light is going early.

## Where the god comes down

On the south side of a closed upland valley, on cropped turf above the river. The valley runs from south-west to north-east. It is long: its head, where the mountains begin, lies about 110 miles to the south-west, and its mouth, where the ridges draw in and the river leaves, about 60 miles to the north-east. Ridges shut it in on both sides, and other valleys lie beyond them. The god has seen none of those.

In view from where it stands:

- The river, cold and quick, about a mile below to the north-west, with a worn track beside it.
- A crossing on set stones, and above the stones a small town of stone houses with a low wall and one gate: Ostrey, about a mile and a half to the north-west. Smoke stands straight up from it.
- A lit window near the crossing, outside the wall.
- Smoke from two more places down the valley floor.
- Walled fields in stubble on the valley floor, and stone walls running up the hillsides far above where any animal now grazes.
- Under the turf close by, about a mile to the north, a line of fitted stone, wider than the track and straighter, running along the valley both ways and out of sight.
- At the valley's head, high folded mountains, range behind range, white on top.

## The people

The Haldren: about 800 hill and river folk, some 300 in Ostrey and the rest in a few steadings along the valley floor. Sheep, some cattle, barley and oats in walled fields, peat and wood for the winter; wool, cheese, cured meat and rough cloth. No one person rules them; a moot of householders meets in autumn. They do not know a god has come. Their record and starting state are in `civs/haldren/`.

## The age

The peoples here work at a level before the medieval era: bronze, no steel forges. Salt is dear in the valley, and so is light for the dark months: oil and tallow are thin every year and are saved.

## What the god knows of the land beyond

Nothing yet. Its map holds the valley it stands in, the ridges and the mountains it can see, the town, the river and the line of fitted stone (`player/map-knowledge.json`). What the Haldren know of the land reaches the god when someone tells it.

## Names

The Haldren call their valley the Hald Valley. For everything past it they say the Lamplands.
