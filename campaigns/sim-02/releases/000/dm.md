# Release to the DM: the moment of landing

Campaign: sim-02. Year 1 opens here. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3 from the traced world: everything Vesper senses and knows at the moment it comes down, and nothing else. Every distance, direction and sight line is measured from the world as laid. The DM writes the first morning story and the first painting request from this file, `world/world.md`, the player's own files and `civs/haldren/`, per `engine/rules/morning-story.md`.

## The moment

Late autumn, dusk. The air is still. The god comes down alone onto cropped turf on the south side of the Hald Valley, about a mile above the river and about a mile and a half south-east of Ostrey. It has its coat and its lantern. No one sees it arrive.

## What its senses give

- Cold, as a mortal feels it: frost that has not lifted all day in the shade and is forming again on the turf, the air dry and sharp.
- Smell: peat smoke and wood smoke, sheep, wet stone from the river.
- Sound: the river over stones, about a mile off and below; a dog, once, from the town; nothing else.
- Sight, by the last light and its own hood-flame, which lights a few paces: the things listed under "In view" below. Past its own circle, in the dusk, it sees what a tall mortal sees.
- Of other gods: nothing. In this world a god knows another only face to face, and no one is here. It knows what every god knows from landing, that others like it came down when it did, and no more than that.

## In view

- The river, about a mile to the north-west and below, running from the south-west toward the north-east, with a worn track on its near bank.
- A crossing on set stones, and above it Ostrey: a cluster of stone houses with a low wall and one gate, about a mile and a half to the north-west. Smoke rises straight from several roofs.
- One lit window near the crossing, outside the wall, a little above the stones.
- Smoke from two more places on the valley floor, both farther down the valley, to the north-east.
- Walled fields in stubble on the valley floor. Stone walls running up both hillsides, far above present grazing.
- A line of fitted stone under the turf, about a mile to the north, between the god and the river: wider than the track, straighter, running along the valley in both directions until the ground hides it.
- The ridges that close the valley, on both sides.
- At the valley's head, about 110 miles to the south-west, the first of the mountains, with snow on them and more behind.
- In the sky to the south-east, above the ridge, one light unlike the other stars as they come out: larger, blue, and steady.

## What it knows

- Its own name, form and powers, as its card gives them, and the outline of its past, as the player gave it. Its past is foggy to it.
- Nothing of the people below: not their name for themselves, their number or their customs. The player knows the Haldren as agreed on Day 3; the god has yet to meet one. They do not know a god has come.
- The land: only what is listed above. Its starting map is in `player/map-knowledge.json`: the valley seen; the ridges, the mountains, the town, the river and the line of fitted stone as reported by its own sight. `tools/map.py draw sim-02 --zoom known` draws it.

## What is waiting

No message, no envoy, no question addressed to the god. No encounter is held. No one in the valley is waiting for anything.

The player's standing conduct for this first evening is in `player/people-meeting.md`: go first to a door; do not cross until asked; if not asked, stand and wait; tell no one what a kept flame is. Which door, and everything said at it, is the player's.

## For the DM's handling

- Winter, for the god's Day 3 power, runs from the first hard frost to the thaw where a hearth stands. In the Hald Valley the first hard frost of this year fell eleven nights before the landing, so it is already winter in that sense.
- Recurring things in the world are counted by `tools/phenomena.py` and released by the Keeper only when the god or its people would notice one. None is released here.
- The names the Keeper has given the valley and its river appear on the map when the god has seen them. The river's name has not yet been told to the god.
