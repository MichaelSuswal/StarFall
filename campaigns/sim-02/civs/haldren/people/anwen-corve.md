# Anwen Corve

Campaign: sim-02. Created by the player on creation Day 3. Who she is, is set by the player. What she does is hers, and is never set by the player or the DM.

Called Old Corve by the neighbors. Haldren. A widow, near the end of her working life. Her family keeps the ford lamp, a lamp for travelers, "because it's what her family does"; by the family's own account it has kept it "for longer than the family can account for." "The lamp is the one thing she's never let go out." She has no knack ("Corve certainly doesn't").

The player's wish, for the world to place: that her house is near the ford and is the lit window the land showed.

Heard, not promised: the player hopes she "doesn't ask what I am." The player's last word on it: "What she does when the coat stops at her door is hers to decide, and I'll find out when you do."

She does not know what a flame of the god's is. The player means to tell her, if ever, "late and deliberate."
