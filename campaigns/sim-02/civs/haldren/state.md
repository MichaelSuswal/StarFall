# The Haldren: starting state

Campaign: sim-02. Population as agreed on Day 3; the rest set by the Keeper on creation Night 3, in words the player may read. This is the state of the people on the evening the god comes down. The civ agent keeps it from Year 1.

population: about 800, in a few steadings along the valley floor and one town, Ostrey (about 300 in Ostrey, about 500 in the steadings)
stores: the harvest is in and it was a middling one. Barley, oats, cheese and cured meat to carry an ordinary winter with a little over; a long winter would be felt by the thaw. Peat and wood are cut and stacked. Lamp oil and tallow are thin, as they are every year, and are saved for the dark months.
herds: about 2,400 sheep and about 300 cattle, brought down to the lower slopes
treasure: little. Some worked bronze, a few good cloths, one house's old silver. Nothing held in common but the town's wall and the moot's bench.
trade: small. Wool, cheese, cured meat and rough cloth go out; salt comes in, dear, and a little bronze. The year's trading is over; the high ways are closing with the snow.
strength: no standing guard. About 150 who could hold a wall or a ford with spear, sling and bow if called by the moot. The gate-warden and two watchers keep Ostrey's gate.
mood: settled and watchful. The autumn gathering is done and its quarrels are parked for the winter. The town and the outlying steadings are cool with each other, as usual. No one is waiting for anything.
