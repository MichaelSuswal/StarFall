# Rejected and bent premises

Campaign: sim-02. For the Keeper's follow-ups. Written by the DM at each day's close; later days add their own section below. Full answers are in `survey-day1.md`.

## Day 1

No premise was rejected outright. The survey's standing assumption, that the god will rule a land and a people, was not rejected: the player answered question 4 ("Past the borders of your own land") on its own terms.

One premise was bent.

1. **Question 8, how much a god stands out.** The question offers one scale, from the one-eyed man in the kingdom of the blind to barely more than a great mortal sorcerer. The player split it into parts and placed them at different ends, and added a condition the question did not ask about, that a god should stay in real danger from mortals: "Closer to the one-eyed man, but only in certain ways. A god should be far more durable and perceptive than a mortal, and should see things mortals can't. Direct force should be weak, so a god's real strength is patience and being believed. A mob with torches should still be a real threat to me."
   For the Keeper: the player asks for gods who stand out in perception and durability and not in force, and for mortals in numbers to remain a threat.

One request not to be told something. It is honored and is not turned into a follow-up question.

2. **Question 13, peoples who aren't human.** The player answered the question in part (one or two peoples, seen at the edges, "like a night-active folk in the high passes who trade but don't talk about themselves") and then asked to learn no more for a long time: "They shouldn't be a faction I have to manage. I'd like to be left wondering about them for a long time." No follow-up on what these peoples are should go to the player.

## Not rejections, noted so they are not read as one

These are the DM's observations, not the player's statements. Nothing here has been inferred into a decision.

- Question 18: "I'd like it to take a long time." A wish about the pace of discovery, not a request not to be told. Not a rejection.
- Question 12: the player wants the enormous buried thing "ambiguous, with no one sure whether it's a skeleton or a structure." That is a wish about what mortals know, not a request about what the player is told.
- Question 11: the player named a category ("Light, in the broad sense") where the question asked for a thing, then listed the things inside it. Answered within the question; noted only because the Keeper may want the list rather than the category. Salt named second.
- Question 16: the player accepted several bodies only as a one-way split ("leaving a part of myself behind in a place, which then can't come back to me") and does not want several avatars at once. Within the range offered. Bodies are set per campaign and given before Day 2 (DESIGN 3.13); this is the player's one voice on it.
- After question 19 the player described, in a few lines, the god it is picturing (dusk, lamps, thresholds, who gets let in) and said it will describe it properly on Day 2. Kept verbatim at the end of `survey-day1.md`. Not survey input and not a Day 2 answer.
