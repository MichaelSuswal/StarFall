# Day 3 brief: the land the god comes down into

Campaign: sim-02. Written by the Keeper after creation Night 2, for the DM, in words the player may hear. This is the kind of country and what the land itself shows. It is not a map: there are no distances, directions or sight lines here, and the DM gives none. Those come later, from the world as it is finally laid.

## The season

Late autumn. The leaves are mostly down. Frost holds in the shade until midday, and there is new snow on the highest ground. The light goes early, and earlier each day.

## The country

Upland river country. A cold, quick river runs along the floor of a valley that is closed in by its own ridges, so that the valley has its own weather: mist lying on the water in the morning, wind that comes down off the heights at dusk. Other valleys lie beyond the ridges, each shut away from the next. The valley floor rises into hill country, and behind the hills stand high, folded mountains, range behind range. There is forest on the slopes, bare oak and beech low down and dark fir above. Where the river leaves the hills, far down the valley, the ground goes low and wet.

## What the land shows

- Smoke, from more than one place along the valley floor, going straight up in the still of the evening.
- Walled fields in stubble, and stone walls running up the hillsides farther than anyone now seems to graze.
- Turf cropped short by animals on the lower slopes.
- A worn track beside the river, and a place where the river is crossed on set stones.
- Under the turf on the valley side, in places, a line of fitted stone, wider than the track and straighter, running up the valley and out of sight.
- A lit window, or what may be one, as the light fails.

That is what the land shows. Who lives here, how many they are, what they call themselves and their valley, what they say came before them, and what lies over the ridges are not in this brief. The player names its own people and their home on Day 3; whatever the player says of the land or its past is heard as a wish or as its people's belief, and the world decides.

## What is so of the world for a people

- The peoples here work at a level before the medieval era, with no steel forges. Peoples who walked here from far away may have brought more.
- Mortal magic, for pricing: it is simple, and it costs the worker. The Keeper fits a people's magic to the world on Night 3; a mortal with a knack whom the player describes is priced at the table and marked for the Keeper to reconcile.
- The god's own rules are unchanged from `world-rules.md` and its power card.

## What the DM cannot settle at the table

Anything that depends on the world's past or on what lies beyond the valley (how long the people have been here, what they say came before them, what is over the ridge, whether a thing the player describes exists) is written as agreed and listed in `player/people-meeting.md` under `## For the Keeper to reconcile`. The Keeper answers each item on Night 3 and makes the smallest change that fits, with a line for the DM in `player/corrections.md` if anything must change.
