# Follow-up questions for Day 3

Campaign: sim-02. Written by the Keeper after creation Night 2, for the DM to weave into Day 3. Few. Each comes from something the god's own card or conduct leaves open about its people. The DM writes the player's answer under each before the close.

## 1. One hearth, and who else

On Day 2 the god said it does not want a temple or a following: it wants one hearth, tended by someone who did not ask for it. Day 3 assumes a people. In the fiction: "A hearth is kept, and the light is seen. Who else comes to it, in time? Do you hope for a people at all, and if you do, how many, and what are they to you?"
(oog) fallback: The game's default is a people of about 5,000 with a town and advisors. You may take that, shrink it, or refuse it. Which do you want, knowing what your god said it wants?

## 2. Who asks you over a town's gate

The vow holds at "a walled town at its gate," and asked over means an invitation from someone who lives in the place or keeps its door. For a house that is plain. In the fiction: "A house has someone at its door. A town has many. When you stand at a gate, whose word opens it to you? Any who live within, or one who keeps it?"
(oog) fallback: For a town or any shared place, who among your people can ask the god in, and who can withdraw the asking? This is about how your people are ordered, and it sets how the vow reads at their gate.

## 3. What your people know of your fire

A hearth lit from the lantern and kept is a window the god can look through and a door it can step out of. In the fiction: "Those who keep a fire of yours: do they know what they keep? Do you mean them to know that you can look out of it, and step out of it?"
(oog) fallback: Do you want your people to know what a hearth of yours is, to half know it, or not to know? This shapes what they believe about you and how they treat their fires. It changes nothing on the power card.
