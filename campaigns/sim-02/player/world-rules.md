# What is so of every god here

For the DM, in words the player may hear. These are facts of being a god in
this world, known from the moment of landing.

## Knowing another god

A god knows another god only face to face: in person, within sight and speaking distance, each seen by the other. The knowing is mutual, immediate and sure. Neither can pretend to the other not to be a god. It tells only that the other is a god, nothing of its name, its nature or what it can do.

At a distance there is nothing to feel. A god that keeps out of sight is not known for what it is. What every god knows from the moment of landing is only this: that others like it came down when it did.

## Bodies

One body. A god here has one body and is in one place.
