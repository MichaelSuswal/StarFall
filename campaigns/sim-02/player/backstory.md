# Backstory outline: Vesper

Campaign: sim-02. Creation Day 2. The outline only, in the player's words, verbatim from `transcript-day2.md`. The rest is the Keeper's to write on Night 2, in secret. The god's past is foggy to the god itself.

## The name

"Among gods, Vesper, because it was what I was named before, and it's the part I still hold."

## Who it was

"I kept a place of passage. I don't remember its shape, only that it was somewhere that things came through to be sorted, taken in or turned away, and that I decided which. I was good at it, and I wasn't kind in the usual sense. I was fair, which is rarer. Hospitality was the only law I answered to."

## What it lost

"I lost the place itself, and I lost the people I let through, whose faces I remember only as lit windows. Worse, I lost the reason I was ever the one at the door. I feel I was chosen, or I chose, and I can't recall which. I'd like that fog to clear slowly in play, in pieces, because of things I do."

## What it fears about its past (from the conduct answers, the player's words)

"It also fears that what it lost was a place it was locking as much as keeping, and that part of the fog is a mercy."

## For the Keeper

The player asked that the fog clear "slowly in play, in pieces, because of things I do." On Day 1 (question 18) the player asked to find out "something about what my kind actually are, or what happened in this world before we arrived," and to have it "take a long time." These are wishes about pace, recorded here so the secret rest is written with them in view; nothing is promised.
