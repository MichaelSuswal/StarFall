# Power lean: Vesper

Campaign: sim-02. For the Keeper, from the DM, at the close of creation Day 2. The DM has seen no other god's build, so this is a judgment of the card on its own, not against the other seven.

## The lean

At or a little below the middle at arrival. Rising toward the middle, and possibly above it, as the number of kept hearths grows over years.

## Why

- Nothing on the card reaches anywhere on the day it lands. Both main powers run on flames of its own, and it arrives with only the lantern. Every window and every door has to be kindled by its own hand and then kept alive by a mortal who chooses to. A cold hearth is lost for good.
- Once a network exists, the Window is strong: sight and hearing at any distance, as often as it likes, by day or night. It is bounded to what the fire lights, shows effects never causes, and carries a learnable tell. The DM priced it by shape against the mystery standard and asks the Keeper to review it.
- The Door is real mobility at no cost in hearths, but one step a day, only at local dusk at the exit, nothing living carried, and nowhere to go until a hearth is kept.
- It has no power that harms, compels, hides, heals or feeds. Its force is a mortal's. The player asked for this on Day 1 (question 8).
- Absence is the strong defensive piece: a broken body cannot be held, and the god returns at the next dusk. Its costs are real (a hearth spent each return; no kindling and no stepping while lanternless; the lantern itself in whoever's hands). A mortal who understands the lantern holds the god. If the Keeper thinks absence runs too strong against the other builds, the hearth spent on return is the lever the DM would adjust, and the player has already accepted it.
- The weakness is heavy. The body cannot cross an unasked threshold, cannot be carried across one, and a town that learns this can shut it out forever. In exchange it got a modest gift: fuel-free light in one room, at the cost of its own sight while it does so.
- The god's fire is capped so it is not a free light source for a people: fuel-free only in the lantern, in hand, in hood-light, or in one room.

## For the Keeper to weigh

- Information power: the Window may show whatever a kept flame lights, anywhere. The DM wrote "what a fire lights and never why" and flagged the great old things. If a limit is needed, the card has room.
- Absence return through a relit lantern: a mortal act can bring the god back, in front of the one who did it. The DM considers this in the spirit of the player's Day 1 answer 19 and priced it at nothing beyond the exposure; the Keeper may disagree.
- Nothing on the card acts on another god, and the DM granted no effect other gods perceive. Every case touching another god is written "unruled until it happens."
- The player asked for the whole world's fires and accepted the trim without argument, then argued two readings (local dusk at the exit; a subtle sign) and won both on fairness. No trim was made beyond the initial pricing; nothing is logged in `rulings-pending.md`.
- Night 1 produced no follow-ups for Day 2, and the DM asked none of its own beyond the guide's checklist.
