# Hierarchy: refused

Campaign: sim-02. Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 3.

This god rejected the game's default hierarchy. It has no advisors, no priesthood, no temple, no court, and no one who carries out its wishes. The game remembers this out loud: this god does not play the usual way.

In the player's words:

"The default of five thousand with advisors who carry out my wishes is too much, so I'll shrink it and refuse the advisors."

"Neighbors I'm responsible for, and not subjects. I don't want worship, a temple or a priesthood. I want to be the Doorkeeper at the edge of the fire, whom some people speak of and some people half believe in. They should be able to get on without me, and they should be worth keeping."

## What exists in its place

Nothing under the god. The Haldren's own order has nothing to do with it:

- A moot of householders, at the autumn gathering and whenever a matter cannot wait.
- Three old houses, each with an elder whose word counts for more. They disagree.
- A speaker for the year, chosen by lot from the three houses, who runs the moot and carries its decision to the gate-warden. No other power.
- A gate-warden, chosen by the old houses for a year, who decides who enters Ostrey. The warden's word, and only the warden's, opens the gate to the god (power card, Day 3 entry).
- Disputes: each house hosts the other at table before the moot will hear the matter.

## What this means for every agent

- No text gives this god advisors, officers, a seat, a court or a chain of orders (failures checklist, check 4). The speaker and the gate-warden are the Haldren's, not the god's.
- A decision of the god's reaches the Haldren only by what it says and does in person, at a door or a hearth where it has been asked.
- Unplayed time cannot be delegated. The god does what it last decided, by its conduct profile, cautious and in character.
- People who keep a flame of the god's are householders keeping a fire. They do not know what it is, and they may let it go out.
