# Vesper (the avatar)
Kind: god
Written: creation, Day 2

A tall, narrow hooded figure a little over seven feet, thin enough to look like a doorframe someone has dressed. A long coat the color of the last light on a wall: a dull amber that goes brown and then grey the longer the eye stays on it. The hood is always up. Under the hood there is no face, only a small steady flame the size of a fist, a lamp-flame with no lamp, that does not flicker in wind. The flame lights whatever stands before it and does not light the figure itself; the coat and hood stay in their own shadow. The hands are ordinary, long-fingered and a little too still. In one hand a plain iron lantern, which swings slowly as though its bearer were walking, even when the figure stands dead still. The figure does not shift its weight, turn its head quickly, or hurry. It stands at thresholds and waits.

When painted: the single warm light source is the hood-flame or the lantern; the figure is seen from a low, close view, at a doorway, at dusk or in the dark; whatever it faces is lit, the figure is not.

Names mortals use: the Doorkeeper, the Lamp-in-the-Hood, Dusk Coat, the late visitor.
