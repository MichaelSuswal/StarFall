# Day 3 brief: where the Cairn lands

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper at the close of Night 2, for the DM, to open Day 3. The player may hear all of it. It describes the country as the land itself would show it and nothing more. The exact ground is laid out tonight after Day 3 closes; treat what follows as the look of the place, not a map.

## The country

High country. Upland moor and broken hills, with long valleys running down between the tops. The soil is thin and there is stone everywhere: outcrops on the ridges, scree under them, boulders in the heather. Trees keep to the valley floors and the sheltered folds, birch and rowan and stunted pine, and give out well below the tops. The streams are many, small and cold, and run fast.

The god comes down at the head of one of the long valleys, where it narrows and climbs toward a pass. Above is the bare top the player set aside for later: wind, rock, and a notch in the skyline where the pass goes over. Below, the valley widens as it falls away. It is high enough here that smoke goes up thin.

## The season and the weather

Late autumn, as the player asked. The bracken has gone brown and the heather is past. There is frost in the mornings in the valley head and the first snow already lies on the tops. The light is low and short. Winters here are long and hard, and the pass above will not stay open through one. Summers are short and bright.

## What the land itself shows

- Smoke, thin, from more than one place lower down the valley. People live here.
- Drystone work: low walls and folds on the valley sides, some kept, some fallen.
- A worn way running up the valley toward the pass.
- Grazed ground: cropped turf, and the droppings of sheep or goats.
- No town in sight from the valley head.

Who the people are, how many, how they live and what they believe is not in this brief. That is Day 3's own work, made between the player and the DM, and it is not decided yet.

## Further off, as far as the god could know on landing

Nothing. It knows a coast lies some weeks' walk away because it asked for one, and not in which direction.

## For the DM

- Open from the valley head: the pass above, the smoke below, the cold coming on.
- Do not describe anything past this valley. Do not name the valley, the pass or any people; names come from the people tomorrow and from the Keeper tonight.
