# How the Cairn meets its people

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the DM at the close of creation Day 3. Lines in quotation marks are the player's words.

## What was agreed

The Carrowfolk were there first, with their own history: about ten generations in Carrow by their own telling. They were not waiting for a god and did not arrive with one. They have no god and believe "not much, and not loudly."

The Cairn comes down in the night at the valley head, where the valley narrows toward the pass. It settles and stays still.

"How they learn I'm here: a cairn at the valley head that wasn't there the night before. I land, I settle, and I stay still. The first shepherd up in the morning finds a figure the size of a big boulder with lichen on it and decides what to make of it. I don't want to walk down in the first week. I'd rather be found, and watched, and quietly tested. They'll push on me and walk around me and leave an offering or a rock, and the first person who adds a stone and then somehow doesn't lose their way home will do more than any speech."

The player expects to be taken for one of the valley's own cairns: "they'll see me as one more of their own cairns, a very large one, and I think they'll take it in stride better than any other sort of thing." That is the god's expectation. What the Carrowfolk make of it is theirs.

## Standing conduct for the first morning

Decided by the player: the Cairn does not walk down in the first week. It stays still at the valley head and lets itself be found.

Not decided: what it does when first spoken to, pushed, or asked a straight question. The power card governs what it can say. The player has said it would go quiet before it would lie.

## Reconciliation with the deep history

Not done by the DM, who cannot read it. Three things in the player's account of the people touch the land or its past and are the Keeper's to reconcile on Night 3:

- The people's saying that the valley "was already kept when they got here," and "the ones before." Recorded as what they say.
- The player's wish for "a few of the oldest cairns on the pass" that are "far older than the valley's memory, built of slabs nobody in the valley could move." The DM told the player this is the land's to answer and promised nothing.
- What lies over the pass and who trades salt and iron there.
