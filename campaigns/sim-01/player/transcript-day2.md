# Day 2 transcript: the god

Campaign: sim-01. The table talk of creation Day 2, as relayed. Each post is kept as it was sent.

## DM:

Still falling. The night you fell through was long, and something below was listening the whole way down. It kept some of what you asked. You can feel that much, the way you feel weather turning: there is high country under you, stone and thin soil, and cold coming on it.

You are not the only thing it listened to.

A voice has been beside you for a while now. It belongs to this place between, and it will not follow you down.

"You asked for high country. When you picture the first place you stand, is it the bare top itself, with the wind on it, or a valley under the tops, with smoke going up?"

"And you. The world took your wishes for itself and asked nothing about the one wishing. So tell me what is falling. What shape does it have, when it has one? What were you, before this, and what did you lose on the way? Say as much as you can still see. The rest is going to fog, and that is ordinary here."

"Then tell me what you can do that the people below cannot. Take it in any order. I will ask as you go."

(oog) Today is the god itself: its form, its past in outline, what it wants, and what it can do. Describe it freely and at any length. I will ask questions, and I will put a price on anything that is a power, because every power here comes with a drawback. Step out with (oog) any time you want to talk plainly.

## Player:

Valley under the tops, high enough that the smoke goes up thin. I want to land at the head of it, where the valley narrows toward a pass. The bare top is for later.

What is falling is a heap of stones that thinks it is a person. When it has a shape, it is a figure built the way a cairn is built, flat grey slabs balanced on one another, with a lichen skin in orange and pale green. It stands a bit taller than a tall man and never looks finished. Wherever one stone sits on another there is a seam of dim light, like banked coals, and the light moves a little when I am paying attention to something. Two hollows near the top serve as eyes. I have no mouth. I speak by the sound of stones settling, and people hear it as words if they have been quiet for a while.

Before this, I was a marker. I don't remember being a person. I remember being the thing travelers touched on the way past, one stone on a long line of stones across some other country, and every hand that added a rock to me was a small prayer that said "someone has been here and got through." I knew where the line went. I knew every place it had been laid. The line is gone now, and so is the country, and I don't know how it ended. What I lost on the way down is my name, and the end of the road. I can still feel that it ran to a place where something was waiting for it. I don't remember what.

What I want is to put a line across this country. I want roads and passes marked so that people can cross without dying, and I want the marking to mean something, so that when someone stacks a stone they are telling the truth to whoever comes next. The marker's whole nature is honesty. A cairn that lies gets people killed. I want that to stay true of me. Past that, I want to find out where the old roads here go, because I suspect some of them were mine, or something like me.

What I can do that mortals can't:

1. I know where I have been laid. Anywhere a stone has been set in my name, I can feel the ground around it, the weather, and who passes. It's a sense, not sight. It's dim and slow, and it works only at marked places.

2. People who walk by my stones and add one, even without understanding, are a little harder to lose. They find the way back, or the way on. It doesn't stop a storm or a bad step. It stops being lost.

3. I can't be moved easily. A stone figure that decides to stay put stays put, and mortals will find that out if they try to push me.

For the edge, I'm going to ask for something too strong, and I'd like you to price it. I want to be able to speak through any stone I've been laid in, anywhere in the land, at the same moment. Not just sense, but a voice. Standing at a pass in the snow, a traveler hears me say "the left path, not the right." And all my stones can do that at once, if I choose.

I expect a cost. Here is what I would offer without being asked: each time I do it, I forget something, one stone's worth of memory, and I don't get to choose which. It should be the slow loss of the road I already can't remember. If you want the price to be harsher, tell me, but I'd like to know what the voice costs before I use it.

## DM:

"The head of a valley, under a pass. The world heard that."

The voice is quiet for a while, the way a person is quiet when counting.

"A marker, then. One stone on a line, and the line gone. You are not the first thing to fall through here that was made by other hands. Three things you would know of yourself, so I will say them. You have one body down there, and only one. You know that others like you are falling now, and nothing else about them. You will feel nothing of them from far off. But if one stands near enough to speak to, you will know it plainly for one like yourself, and it will know you. Not what you are, or what you can do, or what you want. Only that."

"So I ask you this. A heap of stones that holds still is a cairn to any herdsman. Suppose one like you came down off the road at dusk and sat across your fire. Would you want it to know you for what you are? Or would you sooner pass for something smaller, for as long as you could?"

"And you said a cairn that lies gets people killed. Is that a thing you prefer, or a thing you are? Could you say a false word if you chose to?"

(oog) Pricing. I will take your four in order and say what I propose for each. Argue any of it.

One question sits under all of them: what makes a stone yours? "Set in my name" could mean a cairn you built with your own hands, or any heap a shepherd raises and calls yours. The second kind multiplies without you, so it matters a great deal. My proposal is two grades. Any honest cairn raised in your name, by anyone, carries power 2, the not-being-lost. Only a cairn you have laid your own hand on carries your sense and your voice. That keeps the line something you walk, at the pace of walking.

1. The sense. As you wrote it, with these limits. At a hand-laid cairn you feel what a blind man leaning on it would know: weather, the ground, footfalls, how many, heavy or light, beast or person, about a stone's throw around. No faces and no words. One place at a time. A cairn holds roughly a day of what it felt, fading, so you can learn that a party passed this morning but not last week. Drawback: while you listen at a far stone, your body is only a cairn. It stands where it is and knows nothing of what is around it until you come back, and coming back is not quick.

2. Not lost. As you wrote it: it does nothing against storm, cold, hunger or a bad step, it only keeps the way. Drawback, and this one comes from your own words: it holds only while the cairn tells the truth. A cairn of yours that is moved, or raised to mislead, does not go quiet. It leads people wrong, with your weight behind it, and you do not feel that from afar.

3. Staying put. Granted: once you settle, nothing mortal shifts you. The drawback is the same stone. You are as heavy as you look. You do not swim, you sink. Thin ice, small boats, rope bridges and rotten floors are not for you, and you ride nothing. Tell me also what happens if you fall from a height or are pulled apart: do you scatter and gather again, and how long does that take?

4. The voice. It is strong mainly because nothing else in this world carries a word across a country faster than a walker. I will allow it, shaped like this. It speaks only through hand-laid cairns. It is one breath of words, a sentence, said once. It goes one way: you do not hear an answer, beyond what the sense would give you later. Anyone near who has been quiet hears it. You cannot choose the ears.

On the cost, I am going to turn your offer rather than take it as given. The old road is already fog to you. A price paid out of fog would cost nothing you could feel, and you told the world you did not want magic with a clean meter. So the forgetting takes from the new line. Each time you speak through a stone, one hand-laid cairn somewhere on your line goes out of you, and you do not choose which. It still stands. It still keeps travelers from being lost. But you can no longer feel there or speak there, until you walk back and lay your hand on it again, and you do not learn which one went until you reach for it and find nothing. If you speak through many at once, one goes out for every three that spoke. Speaking through all of them at once would cost a third of your line.

That keeps your idea, the slow loss of a road, one stone at a time, not chosen. It moves it to a road you will have built and would miss.

Two more things when you answer. If honesty is a thing you are and not only a thing you prefer, so that you cannot say a false word or set a false marker, that is a real weakness here, and it can buy you something. Tell me what you would want it to buy. And you lost your name on the way down. What do people call a thing like you when they do not know its name?
