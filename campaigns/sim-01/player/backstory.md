# Backstory outline: the Cairn

Campaign: sim-01. Creation Day 2. The outline only, in the player's words, verbatim from `transcript-day2.md`. The rest is the Keeper's to write on Night 2, in secret. The god's past is foggy to the god itself.

## What it is

"What is falling is a heap of stones that thinks it is a person."

## Who it was and what it lost

"Before this, I was a marker. I don't remember being a person. I remember being the thing travelers touched on the way past, one stone on a long line of stones across some other country, and every hand that added a rock to me was a small prayer that said "someone has been here and got through." I knew where the line went. I knew every place it had been laid. The line is gone now, and so is the country, and I don't know how it ended. What I lost on the way down is my name, and the end of the road. I can still feel that it ran to a place where something was waiting for it. I don't remember what."

## What it suspects

"Past that, I want to find out where the old roads here go, because I suspect some of them were mine, or something like me."

This is the god's belief. It is recorded as a belief and is not confirmed or denied by anything on this page.

## What the outline leaves open

Stated by the player as unknown to the god: its name; how the line and the country ended; where the road ran to; what was waiting there.
