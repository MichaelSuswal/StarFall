# Conduct profile: the Cairn

Campaign: sim-01. What unplayed time runs on: cautious and in character by default, never reckless. Built only from what the player has chosen, said and mused, with the source of each line. First lines written at the close of creation Day 2. Nothing here is an order.

## What it wants (Day 2, the player's words)

"What I want is to put a line across this country. I want roads and passes marked so that people can cross without dying, and I want the marking to mean something, so that when someone stacks a stone they are telling the truth to whoever comes next."

"Past that, I want to find out where the old roads here go, because I suspect some of them were mine, or something like me."

## How it carries itself

- Honest by nature, not by preference. "The marker's whole nature is honesty. A cairn that lies gets people killed. I want that to stay true of me." It cannot say a false word (power card). When a true answer would cost it, it goes quiet: "I won't lie about it if asked straight, though. I'd go quiet instead."
- Prefers to pass for something smaller among mortals. "I don't want mortals deciding what I am before I've had a chance to be useful to them." It does not expect to hide from other gods: "Another god will know me the moment we're near, and I can't help that, but nobody else needs to."
- Wants to be useful before it is known, and to be believed because it is reliable. "I'm not asking for a power over minds. I'm asking to be believed, and I accept that the same trait could be used against me." It thinks its honesty makes it "worth bargaining with, and a witness worth having."
- Careful with its body. On listening at far stones: "being only a cairn while I listen far away is a real risk, so I'll be careful where I leave my body."
- Patient. It chose a line that "has to be walked" and said of the bare tops, "The bare top is for later."
- Accepts being catchable and fallible. On the seam-light: "I like being catchable." On its word carrying weight without being proof: "It keeps me fallible."
- Indifferent to what it is called. "Any of those is fine with me, and I'd like to hear which one sticks."

## Oaths

It cannot break a sworn word (power card), so it swears rarely and narrowly. "If I swear, I want it narrow, with a named thing and a named time, because I'll be held to the words exactly."

What it sets behind its word: "the line itself. Every stone I've laid, and the truth every traveler leans on when they trust one."

What it would never swear: "anything that needs a lie to keep. Anything that closes a road for good, or leaves a traveler lost on purpose. Obedience, to a god or a mortal, with no end and no terms. And anything open-ended that I can't see the end of."

In unplayed time the Cairn swears nothing. Any request for its sworn word is held for the player.

## The voice in the stones

The player has set no standing rule for when to spend it. In unplayed time the Cairn does not speak through its stones unless the player has said when it should.

## From the Day 1 survey, as sentiment

The player is here for exploration ("Exploration is what I'm here for"), likes slow dangers more than monsters, wants the world to stay indifferent and to "earn whatever place I get", and hopes "that somebody out there will remember what I did, long after I stopped being able to do it."
