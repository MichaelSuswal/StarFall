# Day 3 transcript: the people

Campaign: sim-01. The table talk of creation Day 3, as relayed. Each post is kept as it was sent.

## DM:

The falling is nearly done. You can see where it ends.

High country, upland moor and broken hills, with long valleys running down between the tops. Stone everywhere: outcrops on the ridges, scree under them, boulders in the heather. You are coming down at the head of one of the long valleys, where it narrows and climbs toward a pass. Above is the bare top, wind and rock, and a notch in the skyline where the pass goes over. The first snow already lies up there. Below you the valley widens as it falls away. Birch and rowan and stunted pine keep to the floor and the sheltered folds. The bracken has gone brown and the heather is past.

There is smoke lower down, thin, from more than one place. There are low drystone walls and folds on the valley sides, some kept and some fallen. A worn way runs up the valley toward the pass. The turf is cropped, and something with small hooves has left its droppings on it. You can see no town from here.

The voice you left behind is fainter now, and it speaks as if across a width of water.

"People live under that smoke. I do not know them yet, and neither do you. They are made today, between us. So tell me who you hope they are. How do they live, and what do they do with their days? What is always true of them, and what would they never do?"

"You spoke all yesterday of travelers. Whoever is down there has built walls. Are the people you would lead the ones who keep a valley, or the ones who cross it?"

"And when they first learn you are here, how would you have it happen? Do they wake to find a cairn at the valley head that was not there the night before, and make of it what they will? Or do you walk down?"

(oog) Today is the people. Describe them freely: who they are, how many, how they live, their customs and skills, what they believe about gods before you. Ordinary culture costs nothing. Anything that would give them a real edge over other peoples gets priced against a weakness, the way your powers were. The game's defaults are about 5,000 people, a town or city, and advisors who carry out a god's wishes. You can keep or refuse any of those, and nothing is held against you for it.
