# Power lean: the Cairn

Campaign: sim-01. For the Keeper, from the DM, at the close of creation Day 2. The DM has seen no other god's build, so this is a judgment of the card on its own, not against the other seven.

## The lean

Below the middle at arrival. Rising toward the middle, and possibly a little above it, as the line of hand-laid cairns grows over years.

## Why

- Nothing on the card works on the day it lands. Three of the four powers need cairns, and it arrives with none. Its reach grows only at walking pace, an hour per cairn.
- It has no power that harms, compels, hides, heals, feeds or moves fast. Staying put is purely defensive, and what a god can do to it is unruled.
- The voice is the one strong thing. No other means in this world, as far as the DM knows, carries a word across a country faster than a walker. It is one sentence, one way, heard by anyone near, and each use puts one hand-laid cairn out of the god's reach, one in three for a many-stone speaking. The cost falls on the same network that gives the power its value.
- The sense is narrow (no faces, no words, one place at a time, a day's memory) and leaves the body unaware while in use.
- Not lost spreads without the god, through honest cairns anyone raises. It is small per person and could be large across a people over decades. Its drawback gives enemies a lever: a moved cairn misleads with the god's weight behind it and the god does not feel it.
- Two heavy weaknesses were taken for small returns: it cannot say a false word, and it cannot knowingly act against a sworn word. In a world where deception is open to everyone else, both can be turned against it by anyone who learns them. What they bought is a felt sincerity (not belief) and the plain fact that its oaths hold it.

## For the Keeper to weigh

- If the other builds run stronger at arrival, this god has room for hidden powers at the fuller end of "weaker than its main power".
- The DM gave the felt weight of its word an effect on other gods as well as mortals: they feel that it means what it says, and remain free to doubt. If that reaches further than the Keeper wants between gods, it is the one item on the card the DM would bring back to the player.
- The player asked for the voice knowing it was strong, and accepted every price without argument. No trim was made.
