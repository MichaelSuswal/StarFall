# The Cairn (the avatar)
Kind: god
Written: Year 1, from the god's own form as negotiated on Day 2

A standing figure built the way a cairn is built, of flat grey stone slabs balanced one on another, a head taller than a tall man, uneven and unfinished-looking, with no face and no mouth. Orange and pale green lichen covers the slabs. In each seam where one stone rests on another there is a thin line of dim light, like banked coals. Two dark hollows near the top serve as eyes.
