# The world, as the Cairn knows it on waking (public facts)

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper at the lock. Only what the god knows as it lands, and what was agreed about its people on creation Day 3. Facts are added here through play, as the god learns them.

## Where it is

The head of a long upland valley, where the valley narrows and climbs toward a pass. Moor and broken hills all round, thin soil, stone everywhere, birch and rowan on the valley floor. A fast cold stream starts here. Above, the bare tops and a notch in the skyline where the pass goes over. Below, the valley widens as it falls away, with smoke from more than one place.

## The people in the valley

The Carrowfolk: about 1,200 herders in a dozen or so hamlets and summer shielings. No town, no lord, no wall. They call the valley Carrow. They run sheep and small hardy cattle, grow oats and barley on the valley floor, and some of the young cross the pass in the short summer to trade wool and cheese for salt and iron. They count everything and settle debts by a public reckoning. They never leave a body unburied on the hill and never turn a stranger out onto the tops after dark in winter. They raise cairns at the passes and on the hill tracks, a stone added by every traveler. They have no god and do not know one has arrived. Their full record is in `civs/carrowfolk/record.md`.

## Tools and making

Bronze, wood, stone and bone. No one forges steel. Iron is known, comes from elsewhere, and is dear.

## Magic, as the valley knows it

Rare. One old woman in the upper hamlet has a knack. Most people have never met anyone else who does. What it costs her is plain to see and no one can say how it works.

## The season

Late autumn. Frost in the mornings, first snow on the tops. Each year opens in this season. The pass will not stay open through the winter.

## The sky

One blue star, larger and nearer-looking than any other light, stands low in the south-east and does not move through the night or the year. It is the only star in the sky. The moon passes it. The Carrowfolk call it the Wether's Eye.

## The land it knows

Seen: the valley head and the upper valley. Seen from where it stands: the tops above, the notch of the pass, and three tall shapes on the skyline there.

Rumored or reported: nothing yet. No one has spoken to it.

It knows that a coast lies some weeks' walk away and not in which direction.

## What the people call the world

The Carrowfolk have no word for the whole world. Their word for all the country anyone has heard of is the Fold.
