population: about 1,200, in thirteen hamlets and their summer shielings in Carrow; no town; the largest hamlet about 160
stores: fair for the season; oats and barley in, enough to the fifth month with care; cheese and salted mutton laid by; hay short in the two highest hamlets
treasure: little; wool and cheese are the trade goods; about 500 pounds of salt in the valley, most of it in the lowest hamlet
trade: four salt trains came over the pass this past summer and about thirty of the young crossed with wool and cheese; salt and a little iron came back; the pass is shut now until the fourth or fifth month
strength: about 190 who could fight, with staves, slings, wood-axes and a few bronze spearheads; no walls but folds; no standing fighters; no one who gives orders
mood: settled into the dark half of the year; counting debts ahead of nothing in particular, since the autumn gathering is just over; they have no god and do not know one has arrived
