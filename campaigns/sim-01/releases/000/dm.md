# Release 000 to the DM: the landing

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper at the lock, for the first morning story (Year 1). Everything the Cairn senses and knows at the moment of landing and through its first night and dawn. Nothing here is beyond what the god could know. Write `years/001/morning-story.md` and the painting request from this, `world/world.md`, the power card, the conduct profile and `player/people-meeting.md`.

## When and where

Late autumn, Year 1. The Cairn comes down in the night at the head of the valley its people call Carrow, where the valley narrows toward the pass. It settles and is still. That is its standing conduct for the first week: it does not walk down. It lets itself be found.

## What its senses give it

Sight (the two hollows see as eyes):
- By night: frost forming on the turf and on its own slabs. Snow on the tops above, pale in the dark. One blue star, low in the south-east, steady, the only star in the sky. A half moon setting behind the western ridge about midnight. No light in the valley below after the first hour.
- At first light: the valley falling away south-east below it, brown bracken, grey walls, birch bare on the floor. A stream that begins within a stone's throw of it and runs down the valley. Drystone folds on both sides, the nearest about two hundred paces below, with a dozen ewes in it. A worn way coming up the valley, passing within twenty paces, and going on up to the notch of the pass. Smoke rising thin and straight from three places, the nearest about two miles down: a cluster of low roofs.
- Looking up at first light: the notch of the pass on the skyline, half a day's climb above, and on it three tall dark shapes, too regular for outcrops, standing against the sky. It cannot tell from here what they are.
- A few paces from where it stands, beside the worn way: an ordinary traveler's cairn, knee high, of small stones.

Hearing: it was not settled on Day 2 whether the Cairn hears as a person does. Ask the player in (oog) before the first scene that turns on sound, and write the answer to the power card as a ruling. Until then, report sound plainly as a person would have it (the stream, wind in the notch, sheep, a dog far down the valley at dawn) and mark in (oog) that this is open.

Touch and cold: it feels frost settle into its seams and does not mind it. It feels the ground through its lowest slabs: firm, thin soil over rock.

God-sense: nothing. No one like itself is near. It knows only that others came down when it did.

Its powers: it has no hand-laid cairn yet, so Knowing where it is laid and The voice in the stones have nothing to reach. The small cairn by the way is not its own. By its card, that cairn becomes an honest cairn of its own when it lays its hand on it, or when someone adds a stone and means it for the god.

## What it knows without being told

What it agreed on Day 3: that a people is in this valley, about how they live, and that they do not know it has come. It knows that a coast lies some weeks' walk away and not which way.

## Who is nearest, and what is likely at dawn

The highest hamlet, nine houses, is about two miles down the valley. Its people call it Tophouses. The ewes in the nearest fold belong to it, and someone comes up for them most mornings in this season. On this first morning it is a girl of about fifteen with a dog, soon after sunrise. What she makes of a cairn that was not there yesterday is hers and the civ agent's. The old woman with the knack lives in that hamlet. Nothing was promised about her.

No one in the valley has ever had a god. They add a stone to a cairn for luck and for the dead who are still walking.
