# The unnamed people: starting state

Campaign: sim-06. Population as agreed on creation Day 3, 2026-11-24. Stores, treasure, trade, strength and mood are the Keeper's to set on Night 3a.

population: about 5,000, in an unnamed market town of reed and clay houses on raised banks, with its channel, flat and hill folk
stores: grain and dried fish enough for the winter if the autumn catch is ordinary; salt in plenty; reed and clay without stint; charcoal and ore thin until the hill carriers come down
treasure: little held in common: the hall's fired weights, a chest of bronze and scrap silver, and salt kept as a store of worth; more in private hands than anyone admits
trade: salt, pots, reed goods and dried fish out; ore, charcoal, timber and bronze in; a market every few days, quarrelsome since last spring
strength: a watch of sixty with spears; up to three hundred more at the call, few trained; no wall
mood: practical, suspicious and short-tempered at market; fed, housed and not afraid
