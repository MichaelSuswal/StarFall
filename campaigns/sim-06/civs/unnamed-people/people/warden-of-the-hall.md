# The Warden of the Hall

Unnamed by the player; filed by the player's name for the office. The town has its own name for it. Written at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-24.

The chief assayer, who tests weights for the town and sits on its council. Holds an office of the town's own law, and has never heard of the Weigher. Who the holder is, in the player's words: "I want someone patient and slightly humorless, who will argue with me." And: "The Warden is the one who will argue with me, and I want that person alive, in office, and not beholden to me." This is not the vacant keeper's place; that is a separate office.

That is who the Warden is, able to fail in play. What the Warden does is the Warden's.
