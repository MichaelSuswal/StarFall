# The Clerk of the Seals

Unnamed by the player; filed by the player's name for the office. The town has an office under another name that this one is to grow from. Written at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-24.

Holds an office of the town's own law, and has never heard of the Weigher. Designed to keep "the register of every sealed bargain and every licensed factor". The Clerk feels no breaking; the Weigher tells the Clerk. Who the holder is, in the player's words: "I want this one close to me. It is the office I would license first." Holds no licence at landing.

That is who the Clerk is, able to fail in play. What the Clerk does is the Clerk's.
