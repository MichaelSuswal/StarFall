# The boy at the gate who asks about the star

Unnamed: his name has not been said aloud in the Weightward's hearing. Filed by the civ agent, Year 5 night, only so far as the Weightward saw.

A boy of the town, nine or ten, of the clay-bank people, who stands in the line with his mother on market mornings. He asked the Weightward at the gate whether the star is its own in the first month of Year 3, and asked the empty post in the second, and asked it again on the eighth of the eighth month of Year 3 after the star dimmed, and on the seventeenth of the second month of Year 4 after it dimmed again. Each time it gave him no answer and read the next scale. Each time he said it was. The line told him to hush.

In the eighth month of Year 4 he was not at the gate. His mother said he had been put to a boatwright at the far channels for the season. A girl of perhaps six in the line asked it in his words on the twentieth, and was told to hush by her grandmother.

Temper: stubborn, not rude, and sure.
