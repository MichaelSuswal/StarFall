# The master of the weavers

Unnamed: the name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. One of the eleven. Fixed by the civ agent at the close of Year 3.

A man of about 56, thin, of the town's own people, who weighs wool and says he knows what a man is when he weighs him. Voted for the offer in Year 1, against the first motion at the weighing and for the third, and counted the nest's figures against each other aloud and said they did not agree.

History: spoke against the seat for the first time in the ninth month of Year 2: a seat for a thing that does not sleep is a throne whatever the term, and the ring's right to put it down is a paper against it. At the winter sitting said only that he had not weighed this one. Did not vote on the old six. Said he would call it down when it first did a thing that was not a weight.

Temper: dry, exact, slow to commit, and when committed hard to move.

Year 3 again, from the fifth day of the first month, to the fourteenth day of the seventh month: watched the post on the fourteenth of the second month to see what it did, and said on the seventeenth that it weighed a basket. At the post on the seventeenth of the sixth month said a hair is not a figure and that the hall should say by how many ones or say nothing.
