# The head of reed and fish

Unnamed: the name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. One of the four trade heads of the council. Fixed by the civ agent in Year 2.

A woman of about 60, of the channel people, the eldest of the four trade heads, lean and dark from the sun. Hard to hurry and harder to insult.

History: voted for the offer in Year 1. At the weighing she asked the Assay-master whether he had opened the chest alone, and he said alone, both times. She said the channel folk had said for years that the bench ran heavy against the fish, and voted for the first motion (the old six as the town's weight), against the second, and for the third. In the ninth month she voted for the seat.

Temper: dry, patient, fond of the Stallreeve. Does not say what she is told to say.

This year: the question of the chest; the vote for the old six.
