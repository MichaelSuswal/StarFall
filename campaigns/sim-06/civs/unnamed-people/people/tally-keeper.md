# The Tally-keeper

Unnamed: her name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. The town's own office, the one the player calls the Clerk of the Seals (see clerk-of-the-seals.md): the keeper of the kept halves of the split sticks. Fixed by the civ agent at the close of Year 1.

A woman of 36, of the clay-bank farming people, broad and quiet, with a farmer's hands. Careful and dry. She learned the office from her mother's brother, who kept the sticks before her, and took it three years ago. She reads notches faster than most people read faces and trusts them more. The halves she keeps sit in a reed-lined chest under the hall and she has not lost one.

History: she was one of the three who watched the stranger on the first day and said nothing. She went home and began cutting a stick of the gate's readings that night, unasked: the date, whose scale, true or out, and by how much in the stranger's cups. She keeps one half in the hall and one half in her own house. She told no one for some weeks. The Assay-master found the halves and said nothing.

Temper: patient, watchful, a little afraid of mistakes. She does not volunteer. Asked a plain question she answers it plainly. She believes a word is only worth what its stick is worth.

This year: kept the stick of the gate's readings; cut the council's invitation on a split stick for the Stallreeve in the eleventh month; asked the stranger to name a mark so she could cut it, and met silence.
