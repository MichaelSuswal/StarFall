# The tide-reckoner

Unnamed by the player; filed by what she is called. Written at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-24.

"An old woman of the salt flats who can tell the height of a flood several days ahead by what she feels in her feet. It costs her the feeling in her hands for a season after each telling. She cannot cure and cannot curse, and she can be wrong about a flood that is hours too late."

A mortal's knack, priced at the table: her own cost is the price. She cannot teach it at the player's telling, and it ends with her unless play makes another. Confirmed as priced by the Keeper on Night 3, nothing changed (L-0024). She has never heard of the Weigher. What she does is hers.

Year 2: called the winter water in the fifth month four days ahead by the same child, right in height and a few hours off the hour. The feeling went out of her hands for the season. In the seventh month she told the Stallreeve she did not need her feet for an ordinary flood.

Year 3: called the winter water in the fifth month four days ahead by the same child, right in height and a few hours off the hour, and lost the feeling in her hands for the season. In the seventh month told the Stallreeve she did not need her feet for an ordinary flood. A netmaker asked the stranger at the gate whether it can tell the height of the water as she does; what it answered is the Keeper's.
