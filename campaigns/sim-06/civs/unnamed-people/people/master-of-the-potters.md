# The master of the potters

Unnamed: the name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. One of the eleven. Fixed by the civ agent in Year 2.

A woman of about 45, broad, of the clay-bank people, precise. She fired the nest of eleven new standards in one kiln so they would shrink alike, and kept them unmarked in the potters' hall until the weighing.

History: asked by the stranger at the end of Year 1 to bring the nest to the hall and not the gate, she carried it up herself in a reed basket on her back. At the weighing the nest was found not to agree with itself, about a thirtieth to a twenty-fifth either side of true and sitting about a twentieth heavy against the old weights. She asked that it be left in the hall under the Assay-master. She did not vote on the first or second motions, voted for the third, and in the ninth month voted for the seat. She fired a second nest in spring, twenty-two pieces in one kiln and eleven chosen by the gate's scale; whether it agrees with itself is the Keeper's.

Temper: proud of her work and not hurt by being shown it was wrong, only slowed. Says nothing in the ring until she has something to say.

This year: the nest, the second nest, the vote for the seat.
