# The fish seller at the head of the line

Unnamed: his name has not been said aloud in the Weightward's hearing. Filed by the DM on the Year 4 played day, from the day sheet, only so far as the Weightward saw.

A man of the channel folk, a fish seller. He stood at the head of the line at the gatepost on the fifth day of the first month of Year 3, a market morning, with a hand balance of wood and cord. The Weightward tried it against its brass and called it out: the buying pan heavy by about one part in sixty. He was angry for a breath, said the reading back, paid his pinch of fish, took the balance and stood aside to work the cord himself. He asked for no stick.
