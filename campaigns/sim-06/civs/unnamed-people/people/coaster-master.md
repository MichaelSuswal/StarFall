# The coaster master of the southern harbor towns

Unnamed: her name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. Fixed by the civ agent in Year 2, only so far as the town saw.

A woman of about forty, master of a coaster of the southern harbor towns that came up the main channel to the town's own landing in the first month of summer, as happens some summers and did not happen last year. A crew of about a dozen hands and a mate. For some days she traded salt, pots and reed mats and paid in goods from the south coast, weighed in the town's scales, and asked the news of the town, who bought what and what a pot fetched, as such masters do. A man of her crew stood at the gatepost one market morning watching the readings and went away without a scale. On the next market day she joined the line before light like anyone, and when the line moved up she stood at its head in front of the stranger.

That is what the town saw. Her own words are the Keeper's, and so is whatever she meant. The thread is held; see the report.

Year 2 told again: stood at the head of the line before the Weightward in the tenth month and asked and was asked the things the release gives, and called a god ashore a landlord. Took her coaster down the channel after three more days. Gave no name and was not asked for one. She will tell every house on the coast, the line says, that the town has two weights. No coaster came up in Year 3.

Year 3, from the fourteenth day of the seventh month, to Year 4, the fourteenth day of the tenth month: came up the channel in the evening of the twelfth day of the tenth month of Year 4 in the same hull as two summers before, the boatmen knowing her by her stem and a mended strake. Her crew traded at the landing on the twelfth and thirteenth. She stayed by her boat. She asked the landing who sits in the Weightward's seat now and which is the town's weight, and told it nothing. On the fourteenth, a market day, she came up from the landing in the dark and stood at the head of the line before the gate opened. Her words and her business are not written: the thread is held.
