# The ship owner of the channels

Unnamed: the name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. One of the eleven. Fixed by the civ agent at the close of Year 3.

A man of about 50, of the channel people, big, quick, proud of knowing his boats, who has done well out of southern goods and means to do better. He voted for the offer in Year 1 and for the terms, against the old six at the weighing, for the third motion. At the landing in the fourth month of Year 2 he put a bargain to the stranger before Bren and two boatmen: every boat read before it was unloaded, at his landing and no other, for a measure of salt a boat. It refused the landing and no other and put its terms back. He took them and said "I came down here to buy a year of you, and you have sold me a morning and kept the rest."

History: in the sixth month a boat of his own came in with salt that was one part in six sand and grit under a clean top. He said "Read the rest," bore half of the sixth himself and docked his captain the other half. Said before the ring that it read his own boat against him in front of the water and that he would sooner it did that than the buyer. The water says he paid it for a show. Was for the yard let in and for the seat. His gift of a measure of salt a month to the Tally-keeper's sticks she took as the hall's.

Temper: proud, practical, hard to shame and quick to pay. Likes a bargain better for having lost the best of it.
