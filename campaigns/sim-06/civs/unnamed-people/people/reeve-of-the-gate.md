# The Reeve of the Gate

Unnamed by the player; filed by the player's name for the office. The town has its own name for it. Written at the close of creation Day 3, 2026-11-24.

Holds an office of the town's own law, and has never heard of the Weigher. Runs "the market itself: stalls, fees, disputes between buyers and sellers, and the watch", with runners. Who the holder is, in the player's words: "I want someone practical and well liked."

That is who the Reeve is, able to fail in play. What the Reeve does is the Reeve's.
