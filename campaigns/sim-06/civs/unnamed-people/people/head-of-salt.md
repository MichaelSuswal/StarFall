# The head of salt

Unnamed: the name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. One of the four trade heads of the council. Fixed by the civ agent in Year 2.

A man of about 55, weathered and quiet, of the salt-rakers. Slow to speak and long when he does. He holds that a store of worth is made by agreeing what the weight is, not by finding out, and that a stick cut once cannot be recut.

History: voted against the offer in Year 1 and spoke longest. Spoke longest again against the terms before the harvest and lost, eight to three. At the weighing he moved that the hall's pieces stand as the town's weight and lost. He voted for the clay head's motion to wait, for its clause that every debt and bargain made before the day stand as made. The salt-rakers greeted him for a month with "read at the gate."

Temper: stubborn, not stupid. Will say a thing he thinks is true in a room that does not want it. Does not forgive a joke and does not repay one.

This year: spoke against the terms; moved the second motion at the weighing; spoke against the seat in the ninth month.
