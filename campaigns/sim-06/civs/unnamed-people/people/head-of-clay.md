# The head of clay

Unnamed: the name has not been said aloud in the stranger's hearing. One of the four trade heads of the council. Fixed by the civ agent at the close of Year 3. His office is the head of the clay trade, the diggers, the potters' clay and the kilns' trade.

A man of about 50, of the clay-bank people, round and unhurried, fond of bread and of putting it out. He wanted the stranger at the hall from the first night and says so. He offered it bread and a jar of water at midday on the day of the weighing and had it put away when it said it took no gift.

History: moved the clay head's third motion at the weighing (wait, fire the nest again, the chest opened only before two of the eleven, the loft looked in by three). Went up the old keeper's loft stair on the third day as the ring's witness. Was told in the second month of Year 2, with the Assay-master, what the Stallreeve had been told by the boatmen. Moved at the winter sitting that the seventh be looked for in every way open to the town. Brought the seat forward with the master of the potters and spoke for it in the ninth month.

Temper: patient, hospitable, slow to say what he thinks and clear when he does. Says a stranger that says what it cannot do is rare.

Year 3: moved that the old six be the town's weight with the others, and would not move to wait again, since the whole south coast had seen the ring wait.

Year 3, from the fourteenth day of the seventh month, to Year 4, the fourteenth day of the tenth month: stood as one of the pair for the chest on the second of the first month of Year 4.
