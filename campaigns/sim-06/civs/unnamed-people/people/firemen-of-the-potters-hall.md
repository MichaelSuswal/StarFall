# The firemen of the potters' hall

Unnamed: filed by office by the DM, Year 5 play day, 2026-11-26 (Pacific), from the day sheet. A pair of men of the clay-bank people who stack and tend the master of the potters' kiln. They carry green pieces for her; no other hand does. They say nothing to the Weightward unless spoken to, and then the plain thing.

Year 3, the seventh month, the twenty-fifth day: carried the boards of the third nest and its spares round to the kiln after the spares were weighed green. Nodded to the Weightward and said nothing. The first day of the eighth month: at the kiln door behind the master, with boards.
