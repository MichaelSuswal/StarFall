# The head of the raking household

Unnamed: her name has not been said aloud in the Weightward's hearing. Fixed by the civ agent in Year 4, only so far as the Weightward saw.

A lean woman of about fifty-five of the salt-rakers, with hands cracked white by salt, who heads the household whose baskets were found bulked in the sixth month of Year 2. Her household of sons, their wives and children rakes pans at the near edge of the Flats. The head of salt is their patron.

On the fourteenth day of the second month of Year 3, the lowest tide of the autumn, she met the Weightward at the edge of her pans, told the day before by the tide-reckoner's child at the Stallreeve's sending. Her household had turned out every basket of the season's store on planks by the drying shed with a sieve beside each. She said they had been made to look and had looked, that the flats' salt had come to the landing clean since, and that it was welcome to anything it wanted to see. She watched it weigh a basket of its own choosing, called clean salt, put a pinch of her salt in its pan, and said the household would tell the yard what it read, since there was no stick that day.

Temper: proud, plain, a little hard. Does not forgive being looked at and does not hide from it.
