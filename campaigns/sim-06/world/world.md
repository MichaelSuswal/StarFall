# The world, as the Weigher knows it on waking

Campaign sim-06. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-24, for the DM, in words the player may read. It holds only what the god knows at the moment it lands: what its own senses give it, and what it brought with it from creation. No one has spoken to it yet.

## Where and when

The landing is in grid cell B3. The season is early autumn. The hour is the hour before first light.

## The land as it first sees it

Low wet country: a wide delta of channels, reed islands and old clay banks. It stands on an old raised bank, about 12 feet up, on a path that runs along the bank's top. From there a level stretch of ground shows out to about 3 miles, and things on the ground show within about 2.

In view as the light comes:
- The path, at the point where it stands, about 4 miles of it in view, from the north to the south-east.
- A river channel about a quarter mile to the east, about 4 miles of it in view, running from the north to the south.
- A town about a mile to the south-east.
- A road about a mile to the south-east, by the town, about half a mile of it in view.
- A low rise of hills on the skyline to the east, their nearest edge about 23 miles off.

The pale flats and the open water are not in view from the bank.

## The sky

A round blue light stands in the north-east, about a fifth of the way up the sky, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by.

## Its people

About 5,000, a mixed river and reed people of an unnamed market town: channel folk who fish and cut reed, salt-rakers of the flats, clay-bank farmers and potters, and hill people who bring ore and charcoal. They live in houses of reed and clay on raised banks, joined by raised paths and flat-bottomed boats, and the market is their center. They do not know the god is coming, and nothing in the town is yet its own. Their record is `campaigns/sim-06/civs/unnamed-people/record.md`.

## The level of craft

The peoples here are at about this level: before the medieval era, no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a ceiling.

## What it knows of the land, and what is only rumored

Known: what it sees from where it stands, listed above and held in `campaigns/sim-06/player/map-knowledge.json`. Rumored: nothing. No one has told it anything yet.

## Names

For the DM: a name below, like any name a mortal uses, reaches the god only when it is first said in its hearing. Until then it stays out of the fiction.

The town's people call the world the Wide Ground. They call the blue light the Low Lamp.
