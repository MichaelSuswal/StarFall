# Message m-0002

Year sent: 2
Sender: the Weigher, at the market town on the levee
Recipient: the old weigher of the hill kin, at Ruddfold
Language: mortal tongue
Carrier: the hill carriers going home, with the words cut on a stick by the Tally-keeper
Route: from the town up the Old Rudd Way to Ruddfold
Form: spoken, with a cut stick

## Text
"The Weigher will weigh your stones in front of any of the ring who want to see it, at the gate, any market morning. No fee but a pinch. Bring the stones in your own bag, and I'll call them in my own cups."

With it: that the old weights and his stones agreed to the beam's tremble, and that the Weigher would like to hear what his people think the seventh was. "I ask nothing more."

## Carried with it
Nothing.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
None given.

## Fate, as the Weigher knows it
He came down with the carriers in the second month of Year 3 with his stones in his own bag, and said the word had reached him.
