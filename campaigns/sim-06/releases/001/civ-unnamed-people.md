# Release: civ unnamed-people, Year 1, the year resolved

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open, as the town knows them. You read this at the opening of Year 2 with your own Year 1 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The meeting at dusk stands as you wrote it from the Assay-master's side. The stranger answered what you marked as answered, in the words you had, and met the other asks with silence.
- Every reading the stranger gave in the year was right, the fourteen of the Assay-master's winter test among them.
- The cup, month 6: the man did not get it. The stranger's hand was on the nest before his. It said nothing and did not strike him. The channel folk took hold of him and the watch held him. Asked by the Stallreeve at the gatepost whether it asked a price of him, the stranger gave no answer. The council set a price in measure for a theft tried on a guest of the yard; the Tally-keeper holds the stick and the price lies unclaimed. This is written into your year file and state.
- The potters' sorting by weight: it did not do what they hoped. By summer the kilns that sorted had cracked as many loads as before. What the potters make of that is yours.
- The walker reached the hill kin and came back. Whether a witness comes down with the carriers is for next autumn.
- The stranger sits late at the landing by the channel folk's lamp. Whether it sleeps, the town does not know.
- The invitation at the gatepost in month 11 has no answer yet. The stranger's answer, when it comes, will be told to you at the opening of Year 2. Until then the town knows only that the half-stick lies at the foot of the post.
- Seven lines of your Year 1 files were reworded by the Keeper for the scan (a place name, a count, a capital letter) and one winter night of the dimmed star is now told as one night. No meaning of yours was changed.
