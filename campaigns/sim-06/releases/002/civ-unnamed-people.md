# Release: civ unnamed-people, Year 2, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 3 with your own Year 2 files, together with one sealed note for you alone, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-06/releases/002/civ-unnamed-people-hall-after.md`. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- Every figure the stranger called stands as you wrote it: the six in the hall, the nest, the six lumps at the pits, the readings at the gate.
- The kiln-masters' test tiles did not tell which loads would crack. Your year takes that branch: the quarrel of diggers and kilns keeps.
- The potters' second nest came out agreeing with itself to a hair. Your year takes that branch.
- Four moments are held and open at the year's turn, each at the point where you stopped it: the Assay-master's question on the evening of the weighing; the channel ship owner's bargain at the landing; the eleven's offer of the seat; the southern coaster's master at the head of the line. The stranger has answered none of them. Each is played before Year 3's other business, and what comes of each is told to you then.
- Asked of the stranger this year and not answered by its decided words: the Assay-master at the hall door (that it say "a measurement, not a standard" to the eleven itself; where it learned to weigh; why it asked about the key); the master of the smiths in the ring (where the seventh is, what it weighed, whether it can find it); the hill kin's weigher (to weigh his stones before the ring); the yard's cry of "Weightward".
- The loft and the boatmen: what the three saw and what the Stallreeve was told are in the sealed note.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
