# Release to the DM: the landing

Campaign sim-06. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-24. Everything the Weigher senses and knows at the moment it lands, and nothing else. Every distance and direction is measured from the map.

## The hour and the season

The hour before first light, in early autumn.

## What the god senses

Firm clay under its feet, on the top of an old raised bank, with the wet on either side. The air has the first edge of cold. Mist lies on the water and thins over the bank. It smells reed, wet clay and woodsmoke. The reeds stand tall and tawny. Water moves in a channel close by, low and slow after the summer. Rain is not far off.

It carries what its card says it carries. The scales hang where they hang, with their dent.

It feels no other like itself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, as a weight in the air that settles over a moment into knowing, and nothing here gives it.

## What is in view

It lands in the dark. By the light in the sky it can see the bank under its feet and the pale line of the path. The rest shows as the light comes.

Standing ground: about 12 feet up, on a marsh country's old bank. A level stretch of ground shows out to about 3 miles; things on the ground show within about 2.

- A path, at the point where it stands: about 4 miles of it in view, from the north to the south-east.
- A river channel about a quarter mile to the east: about 4 miles of it in view, running from the north to the south.
- A town about a mile to the south-east: its roofs, and smoke from more than one fire.
- A road about a mile to the south-east, by the town: about half a mile of it in view.
- A low rise of hills on the skyline to the east, their nearest edge about 23 miles off.

Not in view from the bank: the pale flats and the open water, which lie beyond the reeds. No person is in view at the point where it lands.

## The sky

A round blue light stands in the north-east, about a fifth of the way up the sky, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by. The god has no name for it.

## What it does not know

Any name. No one has spoken to it. What the town's people know of their land and say of it, by their own names, is in `campaigns/sim-06/civs/unnamed-people/knowledge.md`: use it for what a mortal says when asked, and enter a name in the god's map knowledge when a mortal first says it.

No count of anything that recurs is given here. When the god or its people would notice such a thing, take its count from `tools/phenomena.py` and from nowhere else.

What belongs before the landing is in `campaigns/sim-06/player/corrections.md`, under the land against the Day 3 brief. The lines for the sealed requests of Day 3 are in `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-06/player/requests-answered.md`.

## Settled for you

- The house you described stands in the town, on the highest firm bank where the track meets the main channel, with a gate and no wall. It is the town's own house.
- The town already has a flood drum on that bank.
- The salt pans are on the flats beyond the town.
- The town has flat-bottomed boats and a few coasters, and both of the ship-owners you described sit on its council.
- The offices you designed stand under the town's own names for them, which you will hear from the town.
- The tide-reckoner is as priced at the table, with nothing changed.
- Holding a licence costs a factor nothing. Each day on which a factor sets one or more seals costs that factor the night's sleep that follows. Lifting a seal costs nothing, and a factor feels no breaking.
- The town holds debt-bonded folk, as you supposed. Its present terms are its own, and you will hear them from the town.
