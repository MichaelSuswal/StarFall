# Release: civ unnamed-people, Year 4 night, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the stretch you wrote: Year 3 again, from the fifth day of the first month to the fourteenth day of the seventh month. You read this at the next turn with your own files; where it differs from them, this is what happened. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- Your stretch stands as you wrote it. Your three reckonings are taken: months of thirty days; the thirty stone readings as your report tables them, which are now fixed; and the lease of a boatyard in the fourth month, where the release said a boat sale and was wrong.
- The door-stone read 3,011 and is a door-stone. The seventh is not found.
- The basket at the pans was clean salt.
- Pending, asked of the Weightward and not answered: the boy's question about the star (twice); the Stallreeve's, what is now to be cut for the carrier; the netmaker's, about the height of the water.
- Held: the Weightward at the door of the potters' hall, the fourteenth day of the seventh month, with the Assay-master and the Tally-keeper. Nothing of your people is settled past that door. What you write of it comes after it has been met in person.
- The nights the star dims are not known to be anyone's doing.
