# Threads

## Open

- The weighing of the old weights. Opened Year 1 by the god's offer to the Assay-master. The eleven voted seven to three to ask it; the Stallreeve has brought the asking and half a split stick to the gatepost. State: held at that moment, unanswered. The god has not seen the old weights.
- The Weightward's seat. Opened Year 1. Empty since the spring before the landing; the council's to fill. The yard has asked the god to take it. The god means to fill it in the open, only once the Assay-master knows and has agreed to watch.
- The gate. Opened Year 1. The god reads scales at the gatepost for a fee in measure. The Stallreeve uses the readings and rules himself.
- Clay. Opened Year 1. The potters weigh diggers' clay by the basket; weight has not told good clay from bad; some say the scale is no use for clay.
- The new nest of eleven clay standards, fired by the master of the potters, unmarked, waiting.
- The hand at the nest. Opened Year 1. A price in measure is set and unclaimed; the Tally-keeper holds the stick.
- The Tally-keeper's stick of the gate's readings, and her asking for a mark.
- The Assay-master's five questions from the dusk meeting, unanswered, and his name, not given.
- The dimming of the blue light, seen in Year 1.
- The lost house, the lost name and the debt. Left alone until the god says.

## Closed

- The salt quarrel of the landing day. Opened and closed Year 1: the bench ruled the salt short by a seventh of a weight and the raker made it good.
- Whether Bren's balance is a true scale. Opened and closed Year 1 by ruling L-0031: it is.
