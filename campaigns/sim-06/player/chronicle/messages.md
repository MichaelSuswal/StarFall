# Messages

Told as far as the first month of Year 3, at the gatepost.

- m-0002. Sent Year 2, the second month. From the Weigher to the old weigher of the hill kin at Ruddfold. Carrier: the hill carriers going home up the Old Rudd Way, with the words cut on a stick by the Tally-keeper. Content: it will weigh his stones at the gate, any market morning, before any of the ring who want to see it, for a pinch; that the old weights and his stones agreed to the beam's tremble; and that it would like to hear what his people think the seventh was. Fate, as far as told: no word back by the first month of Year 3.

None received, through the first month of Year 3.
