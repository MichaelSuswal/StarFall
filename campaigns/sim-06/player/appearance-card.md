# The Weigher (the avatar)
Kind: god
Written: creation, Day 2, from the god's own form as negotiated
Copy text: the first paragraph below is what the painting prompt copies word for word; nothing after it is copied

A tall, lean figure in a long coat with many deep pockets, the coat the first thing seen. The face is a middle-aged mortal's, plain, with steady eyes that watch as a man watches a scale while the pans settle. The hands are stained with ink and green verdigris. A pair of small, plain brass scales, slightly dented, hangs at the belt.

Not painted: the face is hard to describe afterward. No color was given for the coat, hair, skin or eyes, and no sex; these are unsettled and are asked in play, never inferred.
