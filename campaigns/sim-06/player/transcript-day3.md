# Transcript: creation Day 3

Campaign: sim-06. Date: 2026-11-24 (Pacific).

## DM:

You are still turning as you fall, one face to the light and then the other, and the world has come up close enough to have a color. The voice of The Between speaks once more before the ground does.

(oog) First, the night's business. The Keeper reviewed every term on your card that was marked provisional and changed none of them. The four powers priced by shape, effects and never causes, stand as written. The price that rests on what true measure is worth stands. The Scattering stands as a slower return, at one item, gathering at the nearest true scale. The things you carry, the glass, the wax and ring, the ink, the testing stones, and the word "coin", stand as ordinary possessions that do only what such things do; a lost or broken one is replaced only by work in play, never for the asking. I have written dated entries on the card lifting those marks. No Limit was added to any power. The dent and the empty pocket were settled out of your sight, with the rest of your past: the dent is there and you did not put it there on the way down, the pocket is empty and does nothing, and what either is for is found in play, if it is found. You made no sealed request yesterday; one line of your private answer went to the Keeper to weigh, and it was received and ruled. No cost question was carried over. Of the questions you asked me yesterday, what your people weigh first is yours to say today, since the people are yours to describe.

One fact of the world before you describe anyone. The peoples there are at about the level before the medieval era, with no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a ceiling. Anything you know beyond it is built there by your people, from what the world has, at the pace of the work, and you say how it is to be made; naming a thing grants nothing. And whatever you say today about the land or its past is written down as your wish or your people's belief. The world decides, and you will see.

The country under you is low and wet. Several rivers come down and part into many channels before they reach a wide, sheltered water, and between the channels lie islands of reed, tall and gone tawny with the season. Toward the open water the ground goes to pale flats, bare and white-crusted where the tide leaves them. Behind, the land lifts into low hills. The firm ground is old raised bank, clay, with the wet on either side of it. It is early autumn. There is mist on the water, the first edge of cold in the air, and rain not far off. Smoke rises from more than one fire. Along the top of an old bank runs a worn track.

"A god comes down to lead a people," the voice says. "To build, to rule, to spread. Yours are some five thousand, with a town, a seat that is yours, a court or council, and hands that run things when you are elsewhere. Yesterday you spoke of a counting-house with a hall of weights, of factors, assayers and clerks, of guild-masters and ship-owners who argue in front of you. Now say who they are. What kind of people are they, how do they live in country like this, and what fills their days? And when you are out of the house, who runs it, by what offices, and how does a word of yours reach the far end of the town?"
