# Transcript: creation Day 2

Campaign: sim-06. Date: 2026-11-24 (Pacific).

## DM:

You are still falling. The world below is nearer than it was, and it has stopped listening; whatever it took from all those voices, it has taken. The voice that speaks to you now belongs to this place, The Between, and it is not asking about the world any more. It is asking about you.

Yesterday you spoke of a market at dusk, of a world that is one long conversation about price, of a promise that has weight. You said you would act at a distance through agents, seals and contracts, and not through copies of yourself. Those were words about a world. Today is for the one who said them.

Some things are so of every god that lands there, and you know them the way you know you are falling. You will have one body. You will not feel another god from far off: only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, will you feel one as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing that this is one like you. It tells you nothing more, not who the other is and not what it can do. And where mortals work magic, it is simple, and it costs the worker.

(oog) Those three are given, the same for every god. Anything past them, a sense that reaches further than a few paces, say, would be a power of yours, and priced. Nothing was carried over from yesterday that needs an answer, and the Keeper had no follow-up questions for you. Today is a conversation. Describe the god in any order and at any length; I will ask, price and rule as we go, and at the end I will read the whole card back to you for a yes.

So. What is it that is falling?
