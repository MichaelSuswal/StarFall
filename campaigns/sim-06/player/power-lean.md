# Power lean: the Weigher

For the Keeper's Night 2 review. Campaign: sim-06. Written at the close of creation Day 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific). Measured only against this card's own budget as priced at the table.

## The budget as priced

- The True Measure: a close-range knowing of weight, count and purity. Price: the god cannot give a false measure (silence stays open). Later bound to the scales by the trade.
- The Good-Faith Reading: a yes or no on each party's intent at the moment of agreeing, by choice, in the god's presence. Trimmed from a reading of minds. Price: both pans show, with the witness case added so the price is paid when the god stands aside.
- The Seal: the single long-range thing on the card, and the player's "one thing that is clearly beyond mortal reach" (Day 1, question 8). Price: a blow at each breaking and the Seal and the Reading dark until the next dawn. The cap of twelve is a limit, not a price.
- Seals by factors: bought by the weakness "the scales as the instrument". Narrowed to four licences, face to face.
- The Scattering: a slower return than the standing rungs, at the first rung's price.

## Lean

About level, leaning slightly down. Every power is information; none moves, makes, harms or compels. The return is slower than the standing first rung at the same item price. The scales can be stolen, which can remove the first power outright, and a breaking can be forced by anyone party to a seal. Against that, the Seal has no limit of distance and the True Measure has no limit of use. The player asked for prices throughout and argued only one of them.

## Provisional, for the Keeper

1. Information powers priced by shape, effects and never causes:
   - The True Measure tells what a thing is now, never its maker, source or past.
   - The Seal tells which seal and which party, never where, how or why, at any distance.
   - The Scattering's hearing: each weighing on a true scale, weight and count only, kept afterward as drift and not as figures.
   - The Good-Faith Reading: a yes or no on intent, nothing of the content.
2. A scarcity the DM cannot confirm: on Day 1 the player wished honest weights, "a trusted standard of measure", to be scarce (question 11). The True Measure, the definition of a true scale and the return ground all rest on true measure being worth something. They are written without naming any scarcity as real. If measure is not scarce, the first power is worth less than priced; if it is very scarce, more.
3. The return: priced by the DM as a slower return, one item of memory or habit, a season within twenty miles of a true scale, a year beyond, no less than three years and a Keeper-ruled ground if no true scale stands anywhere. The return ground is set at the table as "the nearest true scale"; whether that gives an advantage the nearest ground of the god's kind would not is the Keeper's to judge. `powers.md` says the Keeper logs which rung each god takes: this one took neither standing rung.
4. No god-specific work rate was set. Pace was not discussed.
5. Things carried that the DM cannot check against the world's craft: a magnifying glass, wax and a seal-ring, ink on the hands, stones for testing metal, and the word "coin" in the card's text. Recorded as the player said them.

## Granted that other gods perceive

Nothing was granted outright. Three matters are written "unruled until it happens": The True Measure on a thing in another god's keeping, The Good-Faith Reading on another god (and whether "both pans show" lets a god party read this one), and a god as party to a sealed bargain. The player asks to be told, the first time the last is ruled, what was ruled and why.

## Rulings made at the table, for ledger ids

1. The first asking divided into The True Measure, The Good-Faith Reading and The Seal.
2. The Good-Faith Reading trimmed to the bargain, never the mind.
3. The Seal's price changed at the player's argument: the True Measure is not darkened by a breaking. Undisputed after the change.
4. The trade: the scales as the instrument, for Seals by factors, with the DM's narrowings (cited on the card in two places, one matter).
5. The Scattering priced as a slower return, with its hearing trimmed.

## Asked of the DM and passed on

The player asked the DM to decide the story of the dent in the scales and what the always-empty pocket is for. The DM does not write a god's past; both are in `backstory.md` for the Keeper. The lost name is there too.

## The offer of silence

The player was offered a heavier price on The True Measure, silence closed as well, and declined. In conduct it then vowed part of it back freely: no silence that lets a partner believe a false measure. That vow is conduct, not a card term.
