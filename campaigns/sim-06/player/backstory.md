# Backstory outline: the Weigher

Campaign: sim-06. Written at the close of creation Day 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific), in the player's words. The rest is the Keeper's, written in secret on Night 2. The god's past is foggy to the god itself.

## Who it was

"I kept a reckoning. I think I was the one who stood at the door of a house where things were counted, and I know the work better than the place."

## What it lost

"What I lost is the name of that house and the faces of the ones I counted for. I remember that I was owed something and that I owed something, and I have lost the sums. I know there is a debt, and I do not know whether it is mine or someone else's."

## Its name

"'Weigher' is what I answer to. I do not think it is my name. Under it there is a shorter word, the sound of a pan settling, and it went with the house. If anyone asks, I say I am the Weigher and that the rest was left at a door I cannot find."

## How it falls

"I do not fall like a star. I fall like a coin dropped down a well, turning over and catching the light on each side."

## Left by the player for someone else to decide

The player asked the DM to decide these. The DM does not write a god's past, so they are passed to the Keeper for Night 2. The god knows neither answer.

- The dent in the scales: "I would like the dent to have a story, and I will let the DM decide what it is." The DM told the player: "You will know only that it is there and that you did not put it there on the way down."
- The empty pocket: "One pocket is always empty, and I do not know why. I would like the DM to decide what that pocket is for." On the card it does nothing.
