# Power card: the Weigher

Bynames: the Weigher (the player's own; it is what the god answers to). Its name is lost to it: "a shorter word, the sound of a pan settling, and it went with the house." Filed under: the Weigher.

Agreed and read back at the close of Day 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific). The player's answer: "Yes, with no strikes. I accept the card as read, including every term marked (new)."

## Form

One body, as every god has in this campaign: a tall, lean figure in a long coat with many deep pockets. The face is a mortal's, middle-aged and plain, hard to describe afterward. The eyes look at a person the way a man looks at a scale as the pans settle. The hands are stained with ink and verdigris.

Senses: sight and hearing are used by the powers below. Nothing else about its senses was described; they are unsettled and are asked about in play.

Pace: not discussed.

God-sense: the god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing that this is one like itself. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.

When the body is broken: see the power The Scattering.

### Definitions

- The god's scales: the small, plain, slightly dented pair of brass scales at its belt.
- The god's presence: within a few paces of the god and in its sight and hearing.
- A true scale: a scale that has been checked against the god's scales, in the god's presence, and still reads true.
- The god's mark: the act of the god's own hand setting a seal, or of a licensed factor's hand. The wax and the ring are only its sign. A copied sign carries nothing, and the god can set a seal without the ring.
- A factor: a mortal holding the god's licence to set seals.

### Things carried

- The long coat, with many deep pockets.
- A set of nested brass weights.
- Wax and a seal-ring.
- Chalk.
- A coil of knotted counting-cord.
- A magnifying glass.
- A few small stones of known purity, for testing metal.
- One pocket is always empty, and the god does not know why. It does nothing.
- The scales are not ordinary. They are the instrument of The True Measure and are written under Weaknesses taken.

## Powers

### The True Measure
Status: main
Description: Anything set in the pans of the god's scales, or weighed on another scale in the god's presence while its scales are on it or in its hand, is known to the god: its true weight, its true count and its true purity. The god cannot be shorted at a scale or passed a plated coin. The power tells what a thing is now, never who made it so, how, where it came from or what it was before.
Drawback: The god cannot give a false measure, spoken, written, signed, sealed or by a nod. It may keep silent or refuse to answer. The drawback holds even when the scales are out of its hands.
Limits settled at creation:
- Light and dark: what is in the pans is known in any light; what is weighed beside them must be in the god's sight.
- Distance: the god's presence, no further.
- Carrying: the scales must be on the god or in its hand.
- Other gods: anything in another god's keeping is unruled until it happens.
- The god's own people: it works on them as on anyone.
- More than ordinary craft: a thing made or held by more than ordinary craft may not read true. Unruled until it happens.
- A mind: it rules none.
- Use across a year: no limit on how often. It goes only where the god and its scales are.
- A scarcity: whether it touches one is for the Keeper's Night 2 review (`power-lean.md`).
- Not applicable: falling, water, being watched.
- Silence: the player was offered a heavier price, silence closed to the god as well, and declined it: "Leave it open to me. I can keep quiet and I can refuse to answer."
- First wording, kept as intent: "Anything weighed in my presence, or sealed under my mark, becomes known to me. I mean its true weight, its true count and its true purity, and also whether the bargain struck over it was struck in good faith or in lies. I can't be shorted at a scale or fooled by a plated coin." And of the drawback: "I also cannot lie about a measure. If I say a thing weighs a pound, it does, and that binds me when a lie would be convenient."
- The first asking was divided at the table into The True Measure, The Good-Faith Reading and The Seal, since they cost differently. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
Rulings:
Trades: its instrument, the scales, is the weakness that bought Seals by factors (see Weaknesses taken).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Good-Faith Reading
Status: main
Description: When a bargain is struck in the god's presence and the god chooses to read it, the choice being its own each time, it knows of each party to the bargain a single thing, yes or no: at the moment of agreeing, did this party mean to keep the terms as spoken. Not why, not what the lie is, not what the party means to do instead.
Drawback: Both pans show. Every party to a bargain the god reads knows as surely whether the god means to keep its terms. When the god reads a bargain it is only witness to, each party learns of the others what the god learned.
Limits settled at creation:
- It reads the bargain, never the mind, and compels no party.
- It does not catch someone who honestly believes a false thing, nor a go-between dealing honestly for a master who is not.
- Distance: the god's presence, no further.
- It does not need the scales.
- The god's own people: it reads them as anyone.
- Other gods: unruled until it happens.
- It is dark from a breaking of any seal until the next dawn (see The Seal).
- Use across a year: no limit on how often. It goes only where the god is.
- More than ordinary craft, a scarcity: it touches neither as written; for the Keeper's Night 2 review.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, being watched.
- The drawback was chosen from a pair offered at the table. The other, a blind spot for the god's own house, was declined: "I pick two, both pans show."
- Trimmed by the DM, said so at the table, from a reading of minds to a reading of the bargain. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
- First wording, kept as intent: "and also whether the bargain struck over it was struck in good faith or in lies"; and "the good-faith reading is close to reading minds. So price it."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Seal
Status: main
Description: The god's mark, set by its own hand, in the presence of every party and with their consent, on a bargain whose terms are spoken or written, or on a closed vessel, bale or chest whose contents the god has measured. From anywhere, the god feels the moment the seal is broken: a party knowingly doing what the terms forbid or letting a due day pass, or the vessel opened. The god knows which seal and, on a bargain, which party; never where, how or why. A seal ends only when its terms are fulfilled, when it is broken, or when it is lifted by hand on the thing itself. Burning the paper breaks nothing and frees nothing.
Drawback: Each breaking lands on the god like a blow, pain and no wound, and until the next dawn The Seal and The Good-Faith Reading are dark. The True Measure is untouched. While The Seal is dark the god can set no seal and feel no breaking; at dawn it knows which seals are gone, and not which party broke them.
Limits settled at creation:
- At most twelve seals stand at once, bargains and vessels, the god's own and its factors' together.
- Distance: felt from anywhere.
- A mind: it compels no party, and it shows nothing to mortals when broken.
- Other gods: a god as party to a sealed bargain is unruled until it happens. The player asked: "the first time it happens, I am told what was ruled and why."
- When the body is scattered: the god feels nothing of its seals; the seals stand, and on its return it knows which are gone (see The Scattering).
- The god's own people: a seal binds and is felt the same whoever the parties are.
- More than ordinary craft, a scarcity: for the Keeper's Night 2 review.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, being watched.
- The price was first set at the table as the cap and a darkening of all three powers. The player argued it was priced twice: "My offer is that a breaking darkens the Seal and the Reading until dawn and leaves the True Measure alone." The DM agreed and the price was changed as asked. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
- First wording, kept as intent: "A seal I put on a contract also lets me feel when it is broken, wherever the breaker is." And of its price: "a limit on how many seals I can hold at once, or a cost to me each time one is broken".
Rulings:
Trades: none of its own. Seals by factors, below, extends it.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Seals by factors
Status: main
Description: The god may license a factor, face to face, to set seals in its name. A factor's seal is a seal in full: it counts against the twelve seals, its setting and its breaking are felt as any seal's are, and its breaking costs the same. The god does not know its terms, or a vessel's contents, until it is told. A factor may lift a seal that factor set. A licence stands until the god revokes it face to face or the factor dies, and at most four licences stand at once. Neither The True Measure nor The Good-Faith Reading goes with a licence.
Drawback: The weakness that bought it: the scales as the instrument (see Weaknesses taken). And every factor's seal takes a place among the twelve seals, and its breaking lands as The Seal's drawback gives.
Limits settled at creation:
- Dormant: it sleeps until the god has licensed someone in play.
- What holding a licence costs the mortal, if anything, is for the Keeper's Night 3 fitting.
- When the body is scattered: licences stand.
- Other gods: unruled until it happens.
- Narrowed by the DM so that the single weakness covers it: licences given and revoked face to face only, four at most, terms and contents unknown to the god until told. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, being watched.
- First wording, kept as intent: "For the trade, I want my factors to be able to set seals in my name. I would count those seals against my twelve and give each factor a standing licence that I can revoke. I do not need more than twelve. I want the reach to come through my people, and not through a bigger pocket for me."
Rulings:
Trades: bought by the weakness "the scales as the instrument".
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Scattering
Status: main (the return)
Description: When the god's body is broken, it is scattered into weights, brass, coin and grain. It gathers at the nearest true scale. If a true scale stands within a day's walk, twenty miles, of where the body broke, the return takes a season. If the nearest is farther, a year. If none stands anywhere, the Keeper rules where the god gathers, and it takes no less than three years. What the god was carrying comes back with it, the scales too if they were on it. While scattered the god hears each weighing made on a true scale, the weight and the count called and nothing of who or why, and can do nothing. It comes back with the drift of it, which scales were busy, which fell silent, what rose and fell, not every figure.
Drawback: Each return costs one item of memory or habit, chosen by a logged roll from the god's own record. The player is told afterward, in (oog), which went; in the fiction the god no longer holds it. And the time: a season at the least, a year or years where true scales are far or gone.
Limits settled at creation:
- A body held is not broken.
- The walk from the gathering ground is added.
- While scattered the god feels nothing of its seals; they and the licences stand, and on its return it knows which seals are gone.
- That a truly measured thing "carries a piece" of the god is so in the telling only: it gives nothing to whoever holds one and no hold over the god.
- If another holds the scales when the body breaks, they stay where they are.
- The hearing while scattered was trimmed by the DM, said so at the table, to the drift and not every figure. It is an information power priced by shape, for the Keeper's Night 2 review.
- Priced at the table as a slower return than the standing rungs, at the first rung's price of one item, the time growing with the distance to a true scale. Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, water, being watched.
- First wording, kept as intent: "I am scattered into the weights, the brass, the coin and the grain, and whatever has been truly measured in my name carries a piece of me. I come back together at the nearest scale that has been checked against mine, slowly, and the weaker the true houses nearby, the longer it takes. While I am scattered I can hear every measure struck in my markets, but I can do nothing about them. If no true scale is near, I am lost for a long time. I would rather that be a real risk than a convenient escape."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The scales as the instrument
The True Measure comes only through the god's scales. If they are taken or lost, The True Measure is gone until the god holds them again; in any other hand they are only brass. They can be bent, sunk or melted like any brass. If they are destroyed, The True Measure is gone until the god's next return, when they come back with the body. If another holds them when the body breaks, they stay where they are. Anyone who learns this can take the god's edge by taking the scales.
The drawback of The True Measure, no false measure, holds whether or not the god has the scales.
The dent in them has a story the god does not know.
It bought: Seals by factors.
The player's words: "I take them as the instrument of the True Measure, and I accept the weakness. They are a relic in fact. A thief takes my edge, and I will have to guard them."
Settled at creation. Ledger: pending, Night 2 (the same matter as the narrowing under Seals by factors).

## Trades
Offered once, on Day 2. Taken: the scales as the instrument, for Seals by factors. Not taken: more than twelve seals ("I do not need more than twelve"), a longer reach for The True Measure, a far pull when another god's goods cross the god's markets. Trades are closed for the rest of creation and may be raised again only in play.
