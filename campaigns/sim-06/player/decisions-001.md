# Decisions, Year 1

Campaign sim-06. Handed off by the DM at the close of the Year 1 played day, 2026-11-24 (Pacific). Decisions only, in the order given. The player's words are quoted where they set the terms.

1. The Weigher is in the town as a stranger and stays. It does not say it is a god. Asked who it is, it says: "I'm a weigher, as I said. I came down the old bank at first light and I haven't been in this town before today. People who ask what to call me have said the Weigher, and I've not argued with it. I had another name once, and I can't give it to you, because I've lost it."

2. The dusk meeting with the Assay-master, held at the door shut. What the Weigher does in it, as the player gave it: "I'll answer how I knew as truthfully as I can without the rest. My scales tell me what is in the pans, and what's weighed beside them while I'm near. That's the whole of it, and I'll show it again if asked. I'll say nothing about seals or the Reading, and I won't say the word god." It tells no lie. "I will tell him I want to see the Weightward's old weights set right, and that I'll weigh them in front of the council, not in a back room. I'd like that to be the offer he takes to the eleven, and I'd like it to be his offer, not mine." If asked for a name to trade, it has none but the Weigher.

3. The yard. It reads scales by the gate of the town's house, off the bench, where the yard can see, and told those waiting it will be back tomorrow. The terms: a small fee in measure, grain, fish or a fired weight, never metal; the order people stand in, no one let ahead for offering more; the reading only, which pan and by how much in its cups, no repair by its hand, though it will show an owner where to adjust.

4. The keeper's place. It means to fill the Weightward's vacancy in the open, by checking the town's standards against its own scales, "but only once the Warden knows what I am doing and has agreed to watch." (The Warden is its own word for the Assay-master.)

5. The Clerk's office (the town's Tally-keeper, as the god does not yet know it) is the office it would license first, "and only after I have seen that person work. I make no promise today." No licence is given in unplayed time.

6. Anything touching the lost house, the lost name or the debt "is left alone until I say. That one is firm."

7. The nest: seven brass cups, each twice the last, one to sixty-four. Whatever it says a thing weighs, it says in those.

Standing at the close: no seal set, no licence given, no promise made beyond returning to the yard. Fees in hand: dried fish, a fold of barley, two small fired weights, and a clay cup it bought.
