# Rulings pending: for the Keeper

Campaign sim-06. Written by the DM. Each case: the power, the use, the player's intent, the arguments.

## 1. Year 1, 2026-11-24. Is Bren's hand balance a true scale?

Power: the card's definition of a true scale, "a scale that has been checked against the god's scales, in the god's presence, and still reads true", which The Scattering gathers at.

The use: in the yard the Weigher read Bren's wood and cord balance by setting the same brass from its nest in each of her pans in turn, with its own scales on the clay beside it. It read out of true (left pan heavy by one part in twenty). She took up the cord herself; the Weigher tried it again the same way and the pans agreed. The player did not say it was making a true scale and has not been told one stands. The DM told the player in (oog) that it has not counted hers as one, and that the question goes to the Keeper if the player means otherwise.

The player's intent on Day 2: "I come back together at the nearest scale that has been checked against mine". The DM's wording added "in the god's presence".

The DM's argument for the player: the check was made by the god's own hand, in its presence, with its scales on it and The True Measure telling it what her pans held; the brass it used is the brass of its own scales' nest; and after the cord was taken up the balance read true. On intent, a scale the god itself has tested and found level is what the player meant by "checked against mine".

Against, for the Keeper to weigh: the test was against the nest and not pan against pan with the god's scales; and the same reading would make every scale the god read level today a candidate, though none of the others was adjusted and tried again.

Not urgent: nothing turns on it until the body is broken.
