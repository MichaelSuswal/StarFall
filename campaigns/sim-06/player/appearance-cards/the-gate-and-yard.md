# The gate and yard of the town's house
Kind: place
Written: Year 1, from what the god saw standing in the gate on the landing day

A gate on the top of a high clay bank: two timber posts and a lintel standing alone, with no wall and no fence on either side, the gate open. Beyond it a yard of beaten clay, and a long, low house with clay walls and a reed roof, the largest roof in the town, its one door facing the yard. To one side of the yard a drum as wide as a cart wheel hangs on a frame of heavy timber.
