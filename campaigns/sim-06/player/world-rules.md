# What is so of every god here

For the DM, in words the player may hear. These are facts of being a god in
this world, known from the moment of landing.

## Knowing another god
A god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing: this is one like me. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.
