# The Dry Run World (public facts)

A test world for exercising the clock. Not a real campaign.

The player's god, called the Weir-King by its people, woke on a shingle bar where the river Ald meets the sea-marsh. Its people, the Riverfolk, number about 5,000 in a dozen villages along forty miles of the Ald. Technology: pre-medieval, no steel. Magic: rare, a wise-woman or two per valley.

Known land: the Ald valley from the marsh to the first falls (three days' walk). Rumored: hill country beyond the falls; a salt road to the south; "the burning hills" far to the east.

Season each year opens on: early spring.
