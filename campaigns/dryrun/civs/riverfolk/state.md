population: 5014
stores: one winter's barley and a tenth over; salt about 6 tons and dried fish about 4 tons held for village trade; nets, boats and the weir timbers kept (weir catch not counted, Keeper to add)
treasure: little; salt and dried fish are the trade goods
strength: 410 men who can fight with spear and sling; no walls; a weir across the Ald at Oxmouth
mood: warming to the Weir-King, still wary; divided at Oxmouth and Wellbrook over the old posts
