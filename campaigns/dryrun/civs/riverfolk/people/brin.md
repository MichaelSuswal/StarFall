# Brin of Aldford

Boatman, aged 22 in Year 1. Lean, a head taller than Hask, red-brown hair, quick eyes. Born at Aldford; the best swimmer on the middle river. Nature: curious, talkative, bold, hopeful. His wife is with child, due in winter. Volunteered when the headman asked. Set out in week 14 of Year 1 with Hask.
