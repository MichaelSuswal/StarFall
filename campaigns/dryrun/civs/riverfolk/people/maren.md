# Maren, headman of Oxmouth

Aged 61 in Year 1. Broad, grey, voice like gravel, a salt-boiler's widow who rose by being right about tides. Nature: decisive, proud of the mouth, believes in signs that favor her. Fixed the weir's giving gate and the tenth. Holds that the old posts are the Weir-King's first weir. Rival to Hobb of Wellbrook.
