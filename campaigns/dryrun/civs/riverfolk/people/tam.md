# Tam of Aldford

Net-maker, aged 31 in Year 1. Square, quiet, hands scarred by cord. Nature: loyal, slow to forgive, not a talker. His wife Ede died in childbed in week 9 while he begged the god at the water; the god stood in the river and said nothing. Tam has not spoken to the god since. He keeps the daughter, unnamed in the god's hearing, and mends nets for the whole valley.
