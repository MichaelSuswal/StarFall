# Hask of Fallstead

Boatman, aged 38 in Year 1. Wide and weathered, two fingers lost from the left hand to a net winch. Born at Fallstead; poles the river to the foot of the falls and once cut ash a day above them. Nature: patient, literal, distrusts stories, keeps a knotted tally cord. Wife and a mother at Fallstead. Chosen by his headman for steadiness. Set out in week 14 of Year 1 with Brin.
