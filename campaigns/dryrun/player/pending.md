# Pending questions to the god
Questions someone has asked the god that the player has not answered (DESIGN 12.9). The DM puts each to the player before 1:30.

1. Year 1, week 5, Wellbrook: Hobb the headman and a knot of fishers asked the Weir-King at the staithe for a law of giving at the weir he had ordered: what the river must be given back for what the weir takes. He said nothing (standing conduct). The world did not wait: Maren of Oxmouth set a giving gate and a tenth returned alive as the code's answer when the work began in week 16. The question still stands as asked.
