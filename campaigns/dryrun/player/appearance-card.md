# The Weir-King (the avatar)
Kind: god
Written: Year 1, from the form line of player/power-card.md and what the first year showed of him (the dry run skipped Day 2, so the card was written on the first morning)

A tall man, a head above most men, bareheaded, with river-grey eyes and hair slicked dark with water. He is never dry: water beads on his skin and runs from his hair and his clothes in any weather, as if he had that moment come up out of a river. He wears plain undyed wool, darkened and clinging with wet. He carries nothing. Wherever he stands, the ground about his feet is wet.

Draft. Written by the DM on the first morning from the power card's form line; the details beyond that line (height, dress, carrying nothing, the wet ground at his feet) are the DM's and are for the player to confirm or change on the first day. Day 2 of creation was skipped in the dry run.
