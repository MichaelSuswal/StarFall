## Open

### The weir at Oxmouth
Opened: Year 1 (decision 2: a weir and fish-trap at Oxmouth before the autumn run).
State at the held moment, week 16: begun on the line Maren chose, about three hundred yards above the shingle bar, sixty yards bank to bank at low water; two rows of oak posts with stone and brush between, wicker hurdles upstream, a wicker trap-pen midstream, a giving gate four yards wide on the west side, lifted on the dark of every moon and the first night of each run, a tenth of each haul returned alive. About sixty at the work at a time. The god has felt the mouth narrowing from forty miles off; he has not seen the work.
Waiting on: the work finishing; the autumn run.

### The boatmen's errand
Opened: Year 1 (decision 3: two boatmen past the falls, no farther than ten days, and back).
State at the held moment, week 16: Hask of Fallstead and Brin of Aldford carried a dugout round the falls at first light in week 14 and went up, to pole a day above, hide the boat, and go east on foot into the wood. Not yet due back.
Waiting on: their return and their word.

### The lights and the counting
Opened: Year 1 (decision 4: watch the sky-lights each night; the elders to count at every village).
State at the held moment, week 16: two lights seen by the god this year, the fourth night after waking over the sea to the west, and week 10 over the wood to the north. The elders of all twelve villages count the same two. The god has watched from wherever he stood, not from the bar (see the (oog) note in years/002/morning-story.md).
Waiting on: the next light; the elders' counts.

### The woman at the falls
Opened: Year 1, week 16, dusk, the held moment.
State: a woman in a hill traveler's grey wool and leather has walked openly down the east bank path and stopped on the stones at the water's edge, ten paces from the god, and looks at him. Her hands are wet though she has not touched the river. The moving pull is where she is. She has not spoken. Two children on the Fallstead staithe have seen her. Nothing else has happened.
Waiting on: the god.

## Closed

### The walk to the falls
Opened: Year 1 (decision 1: walk the Ald from the marsh to the first falls, touching the water at every village, and learn the river).
Closed: Year 1, week 13, at Fallstead and the first falls. The god walked the whole river from the bar, about a week at each village, hand in the water at every staithe, and has river-sense of the Ald from the lip of the falls to the sea. He has stayed at the falls since.
