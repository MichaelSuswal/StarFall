# Who's who

Everyone the god has met or heard named, with status, as the god knows them (chronicle.md 5). Beliefs carry the year they were formed.

## Hobb, headman of Wellbrook
Status: alive.
Who he is to the god: the headman who put the first question to him. Last seen: Wellbrook staithe, Year 1, week 5, with a knot of fishers behind him, asking for a law of giving at the weir the god had ordered: what the river must be given back for what the weir takes. The god said nothing. The question stands (player/pending.md). Year 1.

## Maren, headman of Oxmouth
Status: alive.
Who she is to the god: the headman of the village at the river's mouth, a widow; the Riverfolk have the one word for the office. Known from the word that came up by boat in week 16: she chose the weir's line about three hundred yards above the shingle bar, and set the giving gate and the tenth returned alive herself, as the code's answer to the weir. Oxmouth is glad of the god. Year 1.

## Tam, net-maker of Aldford
Status: alive.
Who he is to the god: came to him at the Aldford water at nightfall in week 9 and begged him to save his wife Ede in a hard labor. The god stayed in the river and said nothing. Tam went back to the house. He has not come down to the staithe since. Last seen: Aldford, Year 1, week 9. Year 1.

## Ede of Aldford
Status: dead, Year 1, week 9, before dawn, in childbirth; the god learned it the same night.
Who she was to the god: Tam's wife, for whom Tam begged at the water. The god never saw her. Year 1.

## The daughter of Tam and Ede
Status: alive. Born Year 1, week 9, the night her mother died. Name not known to the god. Year 1.

## The Aldford wise-woman
Status: alive. Name not known to the god.
What she said of him, as Aldford reports it: that he is the river's and the river does not come to the bed. Year 1.

## The headman of Fallstead
Status: alive. Name not known to the god.
Named Hask and Brin for the errand past the falls and had the dugout built, weeks 11 to 13. Year 1.

## Hask of Fallstead
Status: away on the god's errand; not yet due back at the held moment.
Who he is to the god: a boatman of about thirty-eight, wide and weathered, two fingers gone from the left hand; one of the two sent past the falls. Last seen: the falls, Year 1, week 14, at first light, carrying the dugout round the falls with Brin and going up. His wife and his mother, names not known to the god, watched him go; Fallstead is anxious for him. Year 1.

## Brin of Aldford
Status: away on the god's errand; not yet due back at the held moment.
Who he is to the god: a boatman of about twenty-two, lean and quick-eyed; the second of the two sent past the falls. Last seen: the falls, Year 1, week 14, at first light, with Hask. Year 1.

## The figure on the rock shoulder
Status: unknown.
Seen once, Year 1, week 15, at dusk, from the pool below the falls: standing on the rock shoulder at the top of the falls on the east side, against the last light, upright and still, too far and too dark for a face or a sex, for about the time it takes to draw a net; gone when the god looked again. The first pull lay north-east, toward the shoulder, at that moment. Year 1.

## The woman at the water
Status: unknown; present at the held moment.
Seen Year 1, week 16, at dusk, walking openly down the east bank path from the wood's edge to the stones at the pool's side below the falls, and stopping at the water's edge ten paces from the god. Tall and plain-faced, in a hill traveler's grey wool and leather, a pack and a staff, no weapon the god can see. Her hands hang at her sides and are wet, though she has not touched the river. The first pull is where she is. She has not spoken. Year 1.
