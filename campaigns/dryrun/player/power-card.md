# Power card: the Weir-King (dry run)

Form: a tall man, river-grey eyes, never dry; water beads on him in any weather.
Main power: River-sense. Knows the state of any running water it has touched (depth, flow, what moves in it, who crossed and when, within the past day). Drawback: cannot cross open ice; is weakened (half strength, dulled senses) more than a day's walk from running water.
Shared with all gods: immortal, no need of food or sleep, the god-tongue, god-sense (a pull at any distance, direction only).
Rulings: none yet.
