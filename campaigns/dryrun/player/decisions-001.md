# Decisions, Year 1 (dry run)
1. Walk the Ald from the marsh to the first falls, touching the water at every village, and learn the river.
2. Have the Riverfolk raise a weir and fish-trap at Oxmouth before the autumn run.
3. Send two boatmen up past the falls to see what the hill country holds; they are to go no farther than ten days and come back.
4. Watch the sky-lights each night from the shingle bar and have the elders at each village count them too.
