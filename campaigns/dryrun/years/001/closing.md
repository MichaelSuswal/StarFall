# Closing, Year 1

The last light goes off the marsh and the Ald runs black under the shingle bar. The Weir-King stands where he woke a year ago, water beading on him in the cold, and feels the river the whole way up to the falls: every village fire reflected, every net drawn in, the two boats that went past the falls and have not come back. Above him the sky-lights hang where they always hang. The year turns.
