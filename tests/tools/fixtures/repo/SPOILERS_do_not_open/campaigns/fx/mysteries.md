# Mysteries: fx

## EPIC. The Silent Bell

### Truth
The bell under [f05] was cast to hold a sound that must not be heard. Roll r-0001 set its age.

### Body
The bell, the ruin [f05], the moor [r03].

### Cost if ignored
The bell cracks in Year 9 and the moor goes deaf.

### Clock or trigger
Year 9, or the first hard frost after anyone strikes it.

### Clues
- At [f01], the bell-founders refuse moor tin. Shows: a custom. Readings: old feud; poor tin; fear.

### Branches
- Someone strikes it. The world does: every dog on the moor runs south for a season.

### Never found
Who cast it.
