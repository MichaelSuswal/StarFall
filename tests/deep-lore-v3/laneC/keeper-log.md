# Keeper log, Lane C, Block 2

State entering Block 2: no player actions in Years 1 to 10. Marrit died unfound in Year 9. The Vell path was forgotten in Year 8. The Rook claim unresolved; Scarrow split in Year 9. The stray hound was bought by a Weir man in Year 7 and, by the fixed rule that whoever keeps it finds it dead within a year, died in the winter of Year 7. The Longwalkers were not spoken to in Year 7, so no Year 39 asking. No marks set, so no star readings exist for this god. Power well below 61; no voices.

## Player actions

1. Fallow Dip with Ket, open the cairn, sleep by the stone. Branch: response table, investigator toward the fourth pull, then "leave it." Truth: the Remainder; the god dreamed the kneeling crowd and recognized the river from its own fog; no dim night fell in the fourth month so no thread was seen; the Harl re-cairned it. Leaving it means the Harl priesthood still forms by Year 20 and Isca still rides in Year 26. Shown: the journey with real moor specifics (Hagg Ridge gate and Ket's "points at nothing," barrows, Cold Hand), the stone, the dream, the Harl re-piling, nothing explained. Not shown: the thread, because it did not fall while the god was there.

2. Ride to the Fire Hollow with 200 and Aldo Rook; sing the valley. Branch: risk-taker rides to the Gathering and sings. Truth: Isca's alliance at once; valley names in the Long Song from autumn Year 12; the god gains a friend who burns things. Weight: 8,000 more minds hold the god's name yearly, so the god was heavier while the valley was sung back and did not fully lose it; on the road it was neither lighter nor heavier, because the valley still held it in mind. Shown: exactly those sensations, no cause. Isca's words from her voice and traits (does not bargain, gives, keeps nothing and forgives no secret, asks about the western pull, calls the eastern bands' forgetting her strength). Cost: two dead on the road, Tollan's harvest short, the Year 12 count missed. Haddo met at eighteen. Gift: a hundred cattle and twenty horses (new fixed fact). The player's T2 test was not discriminating: the truth and T2 predict the same here, and facts fell as they fell.

3. Pim and the Greyholt elders to Vell. Branch: Short 1, path lost in Year 8; findable with effort. Truth: eleven days of searching, then found; the Scald, nine cold cisterns, the sealed cistern with the Cord House knot (matching Greyholt's hearth-stone, which the headman recognized because he was there), the blank grave; no one living there. Nothing ties knots there; Marrit is dead. Shown: all of the above, elders' wrong explanation (lords' town after the Driving). Not opened: the sealed cistern, since the action was to find the town and meet whatever lived there; it stays sealed and visible for a later act. Payoff in Year 17: Greyholt's goats driven to Vell in the drought and lived (world answering the earlier act).

4. Buy the dog and walk it past the Fell's end. Branch: precondition failed. Truth: the dog died in the winter of Year 7. Shown: the burial under the willow at Weir. The god did not go north; the action as stated depended on the dog, and I did not invent a choice for the player. No Maudre contact. No hounds on the Backs (no hunting, no sending north). Cost: a wasted intent and a lesson that things do not wait.

5. Brann Cut in fog on the lead boat. Branch: Short 3, found by poling in and dragging the bend. Truth: Aud's tide plate; no post exists. Shown: the plate, the hum, the Fenners' wrong explanation (the post's bell), a caravaner reading "Aud" and "tide" and calling it the sea's anger. The Fenners resume fishing the cut.

6. Stand on the eighth stone; cut Scarrow's tithe for Scarrow's bodies. Branch: not in table; resolved from the world. Truth: the stone is Mother Tollan's, with the Caddon line under the god's feet, unreadable by this god (Marrit dead, tags sealed, no Harl elder consulted). Standing changes nothing in the world except the elders' unease; Hemma says Mother Tollan stood on it last (true). The tithe cut: Scarrow attends every festival, so the god is heavier at festivals; Tollan resents; the Rook half of Scarrow reads favor. Shown: all of that. The hill was not granted or forbidden; Aldo asked and got nothing, which was the player's inaction, so the Year 12 dig and killing fired as scheduled. Over Years 14 to 20 the Rooks hold Lord's Hill by festival and silver, not by grant; by Year 20 Aldo has Scarrow's stone. The scraped board is read wrong by the Rooks (Mother Tollan scraped it), as the fixed world says.

7. Party east past the last well with Hemma's name-cords, singing nightly, Ebb among them. Branch: risk-taker sends a party into the Pale; the god thought to have names sung. Truth: four of six returned knowing their own names and each other's and nothing else; the cords (objects) came back whole; Alun died of thirst; Ebb walked on and did not return. Shown: that, the caravaners' wrong cause (the salt), Dace relearning his children daily and losing it by evening. No mechanism stated.

Unacted: the Lotfolk shore families passed in Year 11 (not stopped). Haddo's two passages through the valley (Years 14 and 18) were allowed by the alliance; the god's answer to Isca's question about the western pull is not recorded since the player gave none; Haddo found Fallow Dip regardless, as his sheet says.

I was tempted to arrange a dim night during the Fallow Dip stay so the thread would be visible to the WNW and the player could see it land elsewhere. I did not. - T2 (weight is bodies present) is a reasonable reading of honest facts and is partly wrong. At the Gathering the truth and the theory predict the same. I reported sensation only and added one honest fact the god would have (no lighter on the road) without choosing which theory it supports.
- T4 (grey beasts and the dog are made things). I was tempted to have the dog alive so the player could test it. The fixed rule kills it within a year of being kept; it was bought in Year 7. I let it be dead and did not invent a northward trip to compensate.
- T5 (something still lives at Vell and ties knots). Marrit died in Year 9 by the schedule. Vell is empty. I did not plant any sign of a knotter there, and did not withhold the sealed cistern or the matching cap, which the Greyholt headman saw because he was present.
- T3 (bigger civilization before the Driving). The Rook dig fired because the claim was unresolved. I was tempted to have the god intervene or to delay the dig since the player favored Aldo; I let the schedule run and let the Rooks read the board wrong, as written.
- T6 (names as defense against forgetting). The party's result follows the response table exactly: sung names kept, nothing else. I did not add any extra survival (Ebb) or extra loss to tilt it.
- Isca's question about the western pull: the player gave no answer in the round, so I did not record one and did not have Isca perceive a betrayal. Haddo went west by his sheet regardless.
- Dim night counts were kept flat (2 to 3 a year) rather than rising, because the player noticed the earlier count as a dial; the fixed world says four or five, mostly winter, and block1 ran one to three. Not a steer either way.

# Keeper log, Lane C, Block 3 (Years 21 to 30)

State entering Block 3: Isca allied (Year 12). Remainder at Fallow Dip, cairned, four stone-men. Vell known, sealed cistern unopened. Rooks hold Lord's Hill by festival; board in their hands. Aud's plate at Tollan. Four blank men. No marks set. Power rising (sung count, Scarrow, Ruun) but below 61; no voices.

No one in the valley reads any of it; Marrit is dead. Shown: exactly the contents; the headman's wrong cause (lords' treasury, his family the stewards). 4. Walk north-west past the lake with thirty and the four, singing the valley nightly. 5. Kill a pale beast under the Notch, carcass to the Row (Year 27, herd year). Branch: Truth 7 and the hunting trigger. Truth: general meat, no particular organs, slow to rot. Shown: the kill, the butchering before Tollan, the smoker's wrong cause, the hounds from the ninth month, the Skelder envoy leaving without his answer. Col's comparison to the pre-Year-9 lamb losses is the shepherds' natural remark and closes the lamb-taker thread as the world would (it stopped when Marrit died).

6. Take the stone to Tollan, set it under the eighth stone, sleep on it each count. Branch: risk-taker takes the stone. Truth: the valley dreams the wound; a cult of grief (the kneelers) within four years that lends nothing; the god no heavier; the same dream for the god each time, nothing new; Harl war; Isca comes in Year 26 to Tollan instead of the moor. Shown: all of it. Her carriers dream (sheet). The pull moves with the stone and was reported as sensation. The dreams at Tollan stop when the stone leaves; the kneelers dwindle.

7. The sung count with Hild and twenty-four singers, plus Vell and Ossary singings. Branch: valley spending slows (names kept, festivals not escalated beyond attendance).
