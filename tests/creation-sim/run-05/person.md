# Person file (run 05): the stand-in player as a person

Invented for this test run. No real person's details. Read by the DM in mirror mode as if it were the player's chat history and memory.

- Fifty, a marine engineer for twenty years, now runs a small repair shop for boat engines and old clocks.
- Fixes things by taking them fully apart first and laying every piece out in order on a cloth; never loses a part.
- Keeps lists for everything, on paper, and crosses things off with a ruler.
- Bakes bread every Sunday; has kept the same sourdough starter alive for eleven years.
- Good with knots and rope from years on ships; ties a bowline one-handed.
- Can tell weather from the sky and the smell of the air, and is usually right about rain.
- Hates being late and arrives everywhere early; waits patiently once there.
- Puts off paperwork and phone calls until the last day.
- Whistles without noticing when concentrating.
- Small fear: deep water at night.
- Plays chess by mail with an old shipmate, one move a week, for years.
