# Play Test List

Michael's list of things to test when he plays StarFall 1.0. While he plays, ask him the questions under each item, a few at a time, at natural pauses (after a morning story, after a ruling, at the end of a day), and record his answers under "Answers" with the date and year of play. Never use the questions to steer play, and never ask about something that would spoil a sealed fact.

## 1. God override
The player refuses or bends a default (the premise, the seat, the helpers, the people's size, a world rule), and the game argues it, prices it fairly, records it, and still honors it years later. Next creation test: whether NPC gods take the same option when no lean is forced.
- When you turned down or bent something, did the price feel fair, and did you get the reasons?
- Years later, does the game still honor it, with nothing pulled back toward the default?

Everything settled at the table (card terms, rulings, edge cases, conduct) holds in play.
