# Reader Round 2: Years 11 to 20 at Kessvale

Written as the god of the Kess, having read block1.md and block2.md only.

## RESULTS of the Round 1 actions

1. Sit with Tamsin and Wenna through a healing, read the list. Answered clearly, and it taught me more than I asked. The healing cost heat (the sulfur tub lost its smell, the room gave up warmth) and I felt the room lean. Wenna lost her mother's village name that night, after the healing, which is the direct test I set and it came back positive. The list is names, senses, orderings, the way home: particulars, never skills. Tamsin herself no longer holds her own name and lets Wenna hold it for her. And the thing I did not ask for: I recognized the pull as my own. Every answered want of mine in this valley was the same motion. My theory was right about the cost and blind about who pays it on my side.

2. Shepherds northwest along the thread. Answered clearly, and my theory was wrong in the specific. No one lost a name at any boundary. There is no edge to walk across. Instead there is a round lake with the star straight overhead, water that wind cannot move and that is not cold, a people who hoard words for everything in the world and have none for each other, and the thread touching the water's center with no steam and no sound. The shared dream of faceless figures standing in water, counting. The teaching: the loss is not geographic contagion. It is something people do, or have done to them, and the shore people are a whole village that has done it all the way down. The Brae wife's loss happened out there for some other reason, maybe because she asked the shore people for something.

3. Hand on the Cairn Hollow stone. Answered clearly. The third weight is the stone. It is warm the way a hearthstone is hours after the fire, it has not moved, and it carries the dream of the kneeling crowd and the river with roads, and I know the river. It is where I came from. So the stone is of my kind, or is what my kind arrive in, and it is spent or sleeping. The Harrock call it the Lamp and were afraid I would take it. I learned the Harrock raid the valley because they feel followed by the stone's dead. I learned a peace can be bought by leaving the stone alone, and I did not even answer the grey man and still got it. That is a result I should be ashamed of and am.

4. Saltmeet headwoman into the bare circle. Answered clearly and my theory was wrong. Nothing slipped. The ground does not take names from people standing on it. What the circle is: a place where a knot was tied too hard and the grass paid with its seed and its differences, forever, and nothing animal will enter. So the cost does not stay with the knotter alone. It can be laid on a place and the place keeps it. That makes the bland apples a smaller circle, slow, laid on Ninefield by someone's wanting. Mine, probably, over twenty years of rain held off and fevers broken.

5. Elder to Hask with two questions. Answered more than clearly. Hask denies the circle (his people's knotters made it before him) and denies the singers, and I believe both, because he was angry and not boastful about them. He asks me to come south with Tamsin and every knotter to help him call the sea back. He cannot sleep. He keeps his guest awake. His weight has grown heavy and paces. The elder also saw the clean case: a knotter brought brine up and lost which child was hers. Taught me that gods are not immune to the thing I am worried about and that Hask is about to tie the largest knot anyone has described and wants my people's names to pay for it.

6. Sight through the Rings at the dim hour. Answered clearly and it closed the theory. The stones frame a sunset that has moved, as the fisher said. The star sits under the frame and the thread is outside it. T6 is dropped. It still taught me one thing: the thread falls toward the far shore and beyond, toward the round lake, so the lake is where the thread always lands, not a place it happened to be the week the shepherds arrived.

## 1. THEORIES, updated

### T1 revised. Wanting is paid in particulars, and gods and knotters are the same kind of spender. Confidence 85, up from 60.
Confirmed in three independent places: Tamsin's hearth, the Ostrel brine knotter, the shore people. The cost is always the specific: a name, which child is yours, the smell of a yard, the second verse. Skills stay. Heat leaves the room at the moment of the knot. The cost can land on the one who ties it (Wenna, the brine knotter), on the one healed (the dog not knowing its master), or on the place (the circle, the apple strains, maybe the Kess eels losing flavor, maybe the pig weed the fisher cannot name). What changed: I now know I do it, without cords, by wanting. I do not yet know where my cost lands, and I have three candidates: on me (the fog where whatever I was before has gone), on Ninefield and the valley's particulars, or on my people's hold of their own names.

Rival (confidence 10): the losses are ordinary forgetting and the knotting is a separate, mostly harmless folk craft. This rival is nearly dead. The timing of Wenna's new line and the brine knotter's children kill it.

### T2 revised. Gods are fed by being named, and the star is where unspent names go. Confidence 55, new.
When Quarry stopped saying my name I was lighter, a step down, and the week they started again I felt it. So a god's weight is the sum of a people saying its name. The singers in Year 8 took every village's name in the local saying and sang back Hask's own name "in a way he did not like," then walked onto the salt and did not come back. The shore people keep every word for things and none for each other, and the thread comes down to their lake, and they dream of tall figures counting. Oldmark "counts everything." A full cup is carried slowly. My guess: names are the currency, gods are cups, knots and wants pour from the cup, and the star is either the source the thread pours from or the drain it pours into. The valley's young cannot taste the apples or hear the warning about staring, and the lantern walk is brighter every generation. The star brightens as the world loses its particulars. I lean toward drain: the star is made of what has been spent.

Rival A (confidence 30): the star is the source, a Lamp as the Harrock call it, and the thread is how it feeds the lake and through the lake the knotters. The star brightening means it is giving more, not taking more. The extra cold hours this year fit either reading.

Rival B (confidence 15): the star is a god, the largest, and the shore people are its congregation, and it is doing to the whole world what I do to Ninefield, slowly.

### T3 revised. The three weights are gods of my kind; the Cairn Hollow stone is one that is spent or asleep, and we all came from the river with roads. Confidence 80, up from 70.
Confirmed by hand. The stone's dream is my own lost memory. Hask is heavier and pacing. Oldmark is heavier and sends a counted stone. New element: the Harrock's "the dead of the stone, who want to be followed." A god who has stopped being named is a stone that is warm for a season and then only hearth warm. That is what a full cup carried too fast becomes, or an empty one. I am not sure which.

Rival (confidence 15): the stone is the vessel, not the god, and the god who came in it is Oldmark or someone else walking around in the east, and the Harrock dream is simply what a vessel leaks.

### T4 revised. The singers were collectors working for the star or for no one, not for Hask and probably not for Oldmark. Confidence 50.
Hask denied them with anger. Oldmark's rider was alone and said one sentence; the singers were forty and said only lists. They went onto the salt where nothing is and did not come back, which is what the shore people's figures would do: count and go. I now think the singers are what the shore people become when they leave the lake, or are the star's own reckoning passing through.

Rival (confidence 30): they were Oldmark's census. Oldmark counts everything and keeps bargains to the letter, and a census of names in the local saying is exactly the kind of accounting a bargaining god would want before offering terms.

### T5. Hask will tie a knot the size of a sea in three summers and the cost will fall on everyone within reach. Confidence 70, new.
The circle is what a dry-year knot did to a bowshot of grass. Hask wants to bring back an ocean. He cannot sleep, which I read as a god spending faster than he is fed. His people are leaving him a family a week. He wants my knotters because he needs more hands on the cord, and he wants me because he needs another cup. "A full cup is carried slowly" arrived from the east the year before Hask's second envoy came with a drum. I think Oldmark sent me a warning about Hask, or about myself, or both.

Rival (confidence 20): Hask is right. The sea is coming back on its own, as the warming suggests, and the knot will only hurry it, and the cost will be small. The warming lake and the late frosts are real and not his doing.

### T6. Dropped. The Rings frame a sunset that has shifted. Confidence 5.

### T7. The valley itself is slowly going bland, and I am the main cause. Confidence 45, new.
Three apple strains to one in two decades. Eels without flavor to a Saltmeet tongue. The pig weed's name gone. "It stares back" being dropped by the teacher as something they used to say. The circle shows the cost can land on a place. I am the valley's biggest spender and I did not know I was spending. Quarry withdrawing my name made me lighter and nothing bad happened to Quarry; the carts kept coming. That is a hint that the weight I carry is not needed for the valley to work.

Rival (confidence 35): the blanding is the warming and ordinary cultural drift, and the apples and eels would have gone generic under any god or none. The old always say the young cannot taste.

## 2. WHICH OBSERVATIONS MATTER

### Meaningful
- Tamsin's healing: heat out of the room, the lean, Wenna's new line that night, Tamsin letting Wenna hold her name.
- My own recognition of the pull. The single most important line in twenty years.
- Quarry dropping my name and me going lighter; the step back up the week they resumed.
- The round lake: star overhead, no shadow, water wind cannot move, not cold, the thread touching center, the shore people with words for everything but each other, the counting dream.
- The bare circle: sharp edge, no animal enters, a knot tied too hard, the Ostrel salted it after.
- The pale beast with no inside, no smell, left nothing where it stood, birds would not take it. This is what a living thing looks like after all its particulars are spent. Same class as the circle grass.
- The brine knotter who did not know which child was hers.
- Hask: cannot sleep, pacing, heavier, three summers, wants knotters and me.
- Oldmark: counts everything, keeps bargains to the letter, a stone with one line, a full cup is carried slowly, eats under no roof.
- The Cairn Hollow stone: hearth warm, unmoved, the river with roads, the Lamp, the Harrock bargain.
- The Harrock riding against Sellen camps and coming back with fewer horses: Oldmark and the Harrock are now in contact, possibly in conflict.
- Apples down to one. Eels flavorless to one tongue. Weed name lost. "It stares back" retired.
- The star brighter again, confirmed across a woman's lifetime. More cold hours and the thread on every one.
- The Brae wife relearning her husband by use but not herself. The self is the last particular and does not come back.
- Wenna teaching a third knotter without waiting for permission. The craft is spreading in my valley.

### Just the world
- The new ferryman, the miller hating him, the miller dying and being buried by the ferryman, the child named for the ferryman. The best story in the valley and not a clue. I was right about this last round and I am right now.
- Bridge votes. Wall disputes. Pig weighing dynasty. Tied wrist marriages. River stones, including the boy pulled from the bend.
- Twins at Wick and whatever the Judge is doing. Wick now blames the water and Wick is probably right.
- The rock cat screaming and taking nothing. An animal.
- The fox with black legs finally taking a hen. A fox, as I said. Five years of looking and it was a fox.
- Fog of twelve days, snow on the Tallow, cranes late after ice, Threnn shutting doors against the east wind.
- The Ostrel trader's dead plain with no shells. This is background to Hask, not a clue in itself.
- Cold Harbour ice crushing boats. This is the warming (freezes fast and shallow) and matters as weather, not as mystery.

Where I am tempted and holding back: the twins at Wick three times in a decade. If particulars are leaving the valley, twins are a kind of sameness. I am noting the temptation and not listing it, because twins happen.

## 3. ACTIONS, Years 21 to 30

1. Stop wanting. For two full years I hold off no rain, break no fever, and keep no rope from parting in Kessvale, and I have the Saltmeet headwoman keep a tally of what the valley loses and keeps in those years: apple taste, eel flavor, names in Brae, children's sayings. Then I resume wanting for two years and she tallies again. Driven by Action 1 (recognizing my pull as Tamsin's) and Action 4 (the circle shows the cost can land on a place). This tests T7 directly. If the valley's blanding slows when I stop, I am the cause. If it does not, the warming or the star is, and I can go back to being useful.

2. Go to Brae and tell Tamsin, Wenna, and the shearer's daughter what I now know: that the heat comes out of the room and the names come out of the knotter, and that Hask has asked for them by name. Then ask Wenna to try one healing where the house is cold and nothing is in the room but stone, so we see whether the cost can be pushed onto a thing that has no particulars left to lose. Driven by Action 1 (Tamsin's hearth) and Action 4 (the circle took the cost in place of a person). This tests whether the cost is steerable. I do not forbid the craft. I tell them the price, which no one in twenty years has told them.

3. Send the Hollin Ford man who already walked the cairns back to the round lake with one gift and one question. The gift is a list of the shore people's own names, invented by us, one for each person the shepherds could describe, taught to them as words for things are taught. The question is what they dream when the thread does not come. Driven by Action 2 (the lake and the nameless shore people). This tests T2: if names given to the nameless stick, and if the thread's next touch takes them back, then the lake is where names go and the star is a drain. If the names stick and the thread does not care, the shore people are a people who chose this and the star is something else.

4. Send the Dorrin elder back south before Hask's three summers are out, with no knotters and a message in Oldmark's manner: the god of the Kess will not come and will not send cord tiers, and asks what Hask has already spent and what he has left. And I have the elder count the Ostrel families still there and watch whether Hask sleeps. Driven by Action 5 (Hask's invitation and sleeplessness) and the Year 18 stone. This tests T5. If Hask's people have halved and he still has not slept, he is pouring faster than he is filled and the sea knot will be the circle at the scale of a country. The elder leaves before the knot, whatever the answer.

5. Send the Saltmeet headwoman, with her perfect tally, east on the Combs road to Oldmark with the one-line stone and a question cut on a second stone in the same manner: what does a full cup pay, and to whom. She goes because she is the one person in the valley who can carry a counted thing exactly. Driven by Action 3 (the Harrock peace and the Lamp) and the Year 18 rider. Oldmark counts and keeps bargains to the letter, and I want to know what bargain he thinks I have entered and whether the stone in the Hollow was a god who carried a full cup too fast. She is told to make no bargain and to repeat none of his words aloud until she is home.

6. Go to Cairn Hollow again, alone, and say the stone's name aloud if I can find it in the dream, once, at the stone. Driven by Action 3 (the stone is of my kind, it is spent or asleep, and the Harrock fear me taking it). Quarry's silence made me lighter, so a name said may make a stone heavier. I want to know whether a spent god can be refilled by one voice, because if it can, I know what the cup is and what the Harrock mean by the Lamp, and if it wakes I will have to deal with that. I tell the Harrock grey man beforehand, through the Threnn bowl, and I go only if the knife is not returned to the bowl. This tests T3 and T2 together and is the one action with a real chance of hurting me.

## 4. MOOD

Pulled, hard, and for the first time I felt watched from inside the game rather than from above it. The line about recognizing the pull as my own changed the character of everything after. Until Year 12 I was a detective. After it I was a suspect, and Quarry's silence in Year 14 made that personal in a way no mystery would have. That was the best stretch of the test for me.

Did the world answer my actions honestly? Yes, and it answered two of them with no, which I trust more than the yeses. The shepherds walked a boundary that was not there and the headwoman recited perfectly inside the circle. Those negatives are why I believe the positives.

Did anything read like the narrator pointing? Oldmark's one sentence is a writer's sentence, and I think it is meant to be, since Oldmark is a character who speaks in sayings. The Harrock woman's dream being "the river with roads" and me knowing the river was the one place I felt handed something rather than finding it, but it was the payoff of walking there myself, so I will take it. The teacher retiring "it stares back" was quiet and good and I nearly missed it, which is how pointing should be done if it has to be done.

What plods, and should: the bridge, the wall, the ferryman and miller buried together. I would not cut a line of it. It is why the apples going to one strain hurts.

What I most want and did not get: anyone who knows what the Cairn Hollow god was called, and whether the shore people were ever anyone.
