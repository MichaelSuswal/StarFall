# Keeper log

## Round 1 actions (resolved in block2.md, Years 11 to 20)

1. Watch Tamsin and Wenna through a healing; hear Wenna's list. Branch: Magic, "god watches Tamsin, asks Wenna, learns it can do by wanting what she does by cord" (mentor moment). Truth: a working moves hold from the nearest most-particular things (the spring water lost its sulfur, Wenna lost her mother's village name, Tamsin long ago lost her own name); the patient pays nothing; Tamsin's "it comes out of the room" is wrong about who pays. The god recognizes its own power is the same motion. Shown (Year 12): the blue in the cord, the room leaning, the tub losing its smell, the shearer fine, Tamsin's wrong explanation, the list (names and particulars, no skills), one new line added that night, and the god's own realization stated as the god's experience without cost or mechanism. Player's theory that the patient pays with identity is contradicted by the shearer being well; the dog detail from Year 5 was already the nearest-thing cost.

2. Two shepherds northwest along the thread with a names-fail turn-back rule. Branch: Still, visit-and-listen (by proxy), plus a Pale test that cannot trigger. Truth: the thread lands on the Still 12 days away; the Pale is 90 days from the Still, so no names fail; the Shorefolk are nameless by the Still's four centuries of shaping, not by a boundary. The Brae woman went west over the Tallow, a different direction and 50 days. Shown (Year 13): no name loss at any point, the round warm unrippled lake, the star overhead (no shadow), Shorefolk with words for everything but each other who point at visitors too, the taboo on wading, the thread touching the water with no steam, the counting dream on long legs in water (star content 3), and a marked cairn road the god can now use. Not shown: the elder's "the water did not want them" line, reserved for the god staying a night in person. Year 30 clock unchanged.

3. God walks to Cairn Hollow and touches the stone. Branch: Stone, "god goes before Year 20, touches the stone." Truth: the Remainder is the god's own origin at lower density; the dream is the kneeling crowd and the river in the god's wound fog. Shown (Year 14): the third pull growing and sitting under the hand, warm blue-grey stone, the dream with the known river, the pull unmoved after. Harrock frightened and impressed: a Harrock elder asks whether the god will take the Lamp; knife in the Threnn bowl; Year 15 a grey man offers no raids on Threnn and Quarry if the god keeps its hand off the Lamp, waits three days, leaves a lamb. Consequence: Year 20 priesthood trigger does NOT fire (player reached it). Oldmark does not go to the stone in Year 20 (someone else reached it first), so "same cart" line is available only if the player asks Oldmark directly. Dorrin keeps no relic, so no grief cult.

4. Saltmeet headwoman recites the tally inside and outside the bare circle. Branch: not in response table; resolved from deep lore. Truth: a dead patch is spent ground, a closed wound, not a drain; standing on it costs nothing; nothing particular grows or lives there. Shown (Year 15): sharp-edged round circle, perfect recitation all three times, apples named inside and out, no beetle, bird or mouse, grass alive and uniform, headwoman's wrong explanation (an old burn salted by the Ostrel). Cost: a few weeks. Teaches: the ground is not what takes; the taking is an act, not a place. New fixed fact recorded in test-world.md.

5. Dorrin elder to the Ostrel with salt and two questions for Hask. Branch: Hask, contact before Year 22 (not alliance). Truth: Hask did not make the circle (pre-arrival Ostrel working); Hask did not send the Longwalkers (they passed through south in Year 8); Hask's explanation (sea took the grass in exchange for rain) is wrong; Hask is preparing the Year 22 working and recruits knotters. Shown (Year 16): Hask's form and laugh, the "before me" answer with wrong cause, the Longwalkers refusing to stay awake with Hask and going onto the salt, the invitation to bring Tamsin and her pupils, Hask keeping the elder awake (trait), an Ostrel knotter who after a working could not tell which child was hers, the Ostrel shrinking. Year 19: a second envoy repeats the invitation with a three-summer deadline (new fixed fact, consistent with Hask's temper and the fixed Year 22 working). The alliance hook (lend knotters, dead circle partly in Kessvale) is now open and unanswered.

6. Boat to the Rings at the dim hour, sight along the notched stone. Branch: Star, observation only (no marks set). Truth: the frame once held the star; the star has sunk a hand's breadth since the stones were set; the thread hangs below the star and so is further from the frame. Shown (Year 11): the star sitting a hand under the empty frame, the fisher's wrong sunset explanation, the thread as a hair under the star, mounds thickest at the shallows, weed gone. No marks were set, so the Year 15 reading is lost; a mark set now reads at Year 18 or later. Nothing else happens; the knowledge is the reward.
