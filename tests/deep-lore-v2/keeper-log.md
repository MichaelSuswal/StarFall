# Keeper log

## Round 1 actions (resolved in block2.md, Years 11 to 20)

1. Watch Tamsin and Wenna through a healing; hear Wenna's list. Branch: Magic, "god watches Tamsin, asks Wenna, learns it can do by wanting what she does by cord" (mentor moment). Truth: a working moves hold from the nearest most-particular things (the spring water lost its sulfur, Wenna lost her mother's village name, Tamsin long ago lost her own name); the patient pays nothing; Tamsin's "it comes out of the room" is wrong about who pays. The god recognizes its own power is the same motion. Shown (Year 12): the blue in the cord, the room leaning, the tub losing its smell, the shearer fine, Tamsin's wrong explanation, the list (names and particulars, no skills), one new line added that night, and the god's own realization stated as the god's experience without cost or mechanism. Player's theory that the patient pays with identity is contradicted by the shearer being well; the dog detail from Year 5 was already the nearest-thing cost.

2. Two shepherds northwest along the thread with a names-fail turn-back rule. Branch: Still, visit-and-listen (by proxy), plus a Pale test that cannot trigger. Truth: the thread lands on the Still 12 days away; the Pale is 90 days from the Still, so no names fail; the Shorefolk are nameless by the Still's four centuries of shaping, not by a boundary. The Brae woman went west over the Tallow, a different direction and 50 days. Shown (Year 13): no name loss at any point, the round warm unrippled lake, the star overhead (no shadow), Shorefolk with words for everything but each other who point at visitors too, the taboo on wading, the thread touching the water with no steam, the counting dream on long legs in water (star content 3), and a marked cairn road the god can now use. Not shown: the elder's "the water did not want them" line, reserved for the god staying a night in person. Year 30 clock unchanged.

3. God walks to Cairn Hollow and touches the stone. Branch: Stone, "god goes before Year 20, touches the stone." Truth: the Remainder is the god's own origin at lower density; the dream is the kneeling crowd and the river in the god's wound fog. Shown (Year 14): the third pull growing and sitting under the hand, warm blue-grey stone, the dream with the known river, the pull unmoved after. Harrock frightened and impressed: a Harrock elder asks whether the god will take the Lamp; knife in the Threnn bowl; Year 15 a grey man offers no raids on Threnn and Quarry if the god keeps its hand off the Lamp, waits three days, leaves a lamb. Consequence: Year 20 priesthood trigger does NOT fire (player reached it). Oldmark does not go to the stone in Year 20 (someone else reached it first), so "same cart" line is available only if the player asks Oldmark directly. Dorrin keeps no relic, so no grief cult.

4. Saltmeet headwoman recites the tally inside and outside the bare circle. Branch: not in response table; resolved from deep lore. Truth: a dead patch is spent ground, a closed wound, not a drain; standing on it costs nothing; nothing particular grows or lives there. Shown (Year 15): sharp-edged round circle, perfect recitation all three times, apples named inside and out, no beetle, bird or mouse, grass alive and uniform, headwoman's wrong explanation (an old burn salted by the Ostrel). Cost: a few weeks. Teaches: the ground is not what takes; the taking is an act, not a place. New fixed fact recorded in test-world.md.

5. Dorrin elder to the Ostrel with salt and two questions for Hask. Branch: Hask, contact before Year 22 (not alliance). Truth: Hask did not make the circle (pre-arrival Ostrel working); Hask did not send the Longwalkers (they passed through south in Year 8); Hask's explanation (sea took the grass in exchange for rain) is wrong; Hask is preparing the Year 22 working and recruits knotters. Shown (Year 16): Hask's form and laugh, the "before me" answer with wrong cause, the Longwalkers refusing to stay awake with Hask and going onto the salt, the invitation to bring Tamsin and her pupils, Hask keeping the elder awake (trait), an Ostrel knotter who after a working could not tell which child was hers, the Ostrel shrinking. Year 19: a second envoy repeats the invitation with a three-summer deadline (new fixed fact, consistent with Hask's temper and the fixed Year 22 working). The alliance hook (lend knotters, dead circle partly in Kessvale) is now open and unanswered.

6. Boat to the Rings at the dim hour, sight along the notched stone. Branch: Star, observation only (no marks set). Truth: the frame once held the star; the star has sunk a hand's breadth since the stones were set; the thread hangs below the star and so is further from the frame. Shown (Year 11): the star sitting a hand under the empty frame, the fisher's wrong sunset explanation, the thread as a hair under the star, mounds thickest at the shallows, weed gone. No marks were set, so the Year 15 reading is lost; a mark set now reads at Year 18 or later. Nothing else happens; the knowledge is the reward.

## Round 2 actions (resolved in block3.md, Years 21 to 30)

1. Stop wanting two years, tally, resume two years, tally. Branch: not in response table; Fading, restraint variant. Truth: the god spends only itself when it wants; the valley's fading is regional overdraw (oldest places and thin-rain edges first) and the clock is moved only by a school of magic or a great working; the god's small wants are not the spend. Shown (Years 21 to 25): the cost of restraint (flood six days, rope lost, Brae fever deaths), two tallies with the same rate of loss in both halves, Year 24 clock events landing inside the no-wanting years, the headwoman's wrong cause (the warm years). Teaches: the god is not the cause of the blanding, and withholding help bought nothing.

2. Tell the knotters the price; test a working in a bare stone room. Branch: Magic, variant of "watches Tamsin, asks Wenna" with a steerability test. Truth: cost falls on the nearest most-particular thing; stone holds almost nothing and cannot pay; the cost went to Wenna (her sister's name, permanent) and glanced off the patient (nameless for a day, recovered, like the Year 5 dog). Hesk (third knotter) wanted to go south and was kept home; no Kessvale knotter joined Hask, so the dead circle is wholly Ostrel. Shown (Years 21, 22, 27): Tamsin's wrong cause (old is forgetting), Wenna's "that is what the list is for," the byre working, the sister naming herself on paper. Knotters under the god: three. Below threshold.

3. Hollin Ford man teaches invented names to the Shorefolk and asks about dreams on threadless nights. Branch: Still, a thin one-visit version of "teaches the Shorefolk names." Truth: the Still shaped them by being the thing they look at; names stick only with people living among them for years. Shown (Year 23): the words attach to huts not persons, every person looks at the water when named, dreams of counting come every night regardless of the thread (the ascended speak into dreams at the center; the thread is feeding, not dreaming), three more Shorefolk words come back for things. Not an "act on the Still" of the kind that gates Year 36; the Year 36 taking will fire unless the god does more. New fixed facts recorded.

4. Elder south with refusal and a question; count families; watch Hask sleep. Branch: Hask, "god refuses; Hask laughs and does it alone." Truth: Hask does not understand what it spends; the Ostrel are 1,600 and the knotters are kept awake (trait). Shown (Year 21): hearth count, Hask's answer (eleven years, two envoys, patience, the sea left), three sleepless nights. Year 22 fixed working fired: wall of wet wind, dead circle a day across in Ostrel country, a hundred hollow, refugees to Wick, wrong causes from refugees (sea taken back by something that did not want it; star struck him for pride). Hask surged; Year 28 lift and shedding fired; Year 29 Hask asks for a god to listen (alliance-after-28 hook, open).

5. Headwoman to Oldmark with the one-line stone and a cut question. Branch: Oldmark, fair dealing, question only, no bargain. Truth: Oldmark pours into oath stones to stay under the brim and will never say so plainly; Oldmark never enters a dwelling uninvited and counts everything. Shown (Years 25 to 26): Oldmark listens to the whole tally, returns the gift, answers in three proverbs (the carrier pays with the hand; ask the ground; a stone set down is still counted), the oath stones seen in use with a Sellen woman's partly wrong explanation, Harrock horses in Sellen camps. "Same cart" not given: the stone in the Hollow was not asked about. Relations are good; Year 35 "pouring" proverb and Year 40 oath stone showing remain available.

6. Second visit to Cairn Hollow to say the stone's name. Branch: Stone, touch again, no taking. Truth: the Remainder is a people's memory of being followed and holds no personal name; the word in the dream is the address of a follower to the followed, the same word in the god's own fog. Saying it does nothing to the stone; the Harrock dreaming together that night is the stone dreaming at those nearby, as it always does, heightened by the god's presence (conservative). Shown (Year 26): bowl message, knife kept out, no name in the dream, the word said, stone unchanged, grey man's "so you will not take it," whole-clan dream. Harrock peace holds; Year 25 raid did not fall on Threnn or Quarry (it fell on Sellen camps, which neither fight nor lose).

## Round 3 actions (resolved in block4.md, Years 31 to 40)

1. Go south to Hask and listen; set a one-line stone on the willow walk for oaths and askings while away. Branch: Hask, "allies after Year 28; Hask tells what lifting felt like." Truth: Hask passed 60 in Year 24 and heard the ascended on dim nights (long-legged counters in shallow salt water, star content 3), passed the warnings (thin shadow, echoed voice, doubled prayers), was lifted at 78 in Year 28, and is shedding into priests without understanding. Shown (Year 31): the full telling, Hask's wrong cause (a jealous star wanted his sea), the men in white taking salt from hearths. The willow stone: the god put no power in it, so it did nothing in itself; minds still held the god's shape and the god's weight did not change while away. New fixed fact.

2. Dorrin alone stops saying the god's name for one year and speaks to the stone; headwoman counts Dorrin against Brae. Branch: Ceiling, a deliberate test of lightness (clue-design "deliberately testing with a feast of only-remembering," inverted). Truth: power is lent by minds holding the shape; Dorrin's silence dropped the god roughly eight points for the year and it returned whole; the god's weight does nothing for Dorrin's harvests or fevers, and the god still acted where it chose. Shown (Years 33 to 34): the larger step down, no difference in Dorrin's losses or fortunes against Brae, the elders asking to keep speaking to the stone because "it has done as well," the weight returning. Teaches: the weight is not for the valley. Side effect recorded: Dorrin's habit of the stone.

3. Close the cairn road; send the Hollin Ford man and the Wick knotter with one question. Branch: Still, a real choice of the "move the edge away" kind. Truth: the Still takes the nameless and then the god's edge people who come to it; cutting the road removes the god's people from reach; the Year 36 taking of a Hollin Ford villager did not fire (acted on the Still). The ascended count what rises; from the shore it reads as fewer here and more there. Shown (Years 32, 36): a third of huts empty, the old woman's hand-and-fingers answer (down on the shore, up in the lake), the two travelers disagreeing, the Wick knotter's dream of a shallow wide salt sea (star content, by a mortal who knows salt water), no god's villager taken, half the huts empty by 36. Not shown: the elder's "water did not want them" line, still gated to the god in person.

4. Ask the Harrock to leave the stone a year; feel the weight by season. Branch: Stone, observation; contaminated by the Harrock's refusal. Truth: the Remainder's density is fed slightly by minds holding it; a year without one clan of six would not be measurable to the god. Shown (Years 33 to 34): the grey man treats the request as a hand on the Lamp and refuses for five clans, gives one clan's absence as a gift between word-keepers, wrong cause (it is the dead who sleep there, not the stone); the weight unchanged through four seasons. Teaches: the test could not be run, and why. Peace held.

5. Wenna's sister and Hesk sit a season with the hollow at the Ostrel circle, teaching names and the way home by paper. Branch: Magic, consequences; not in the table, resolved from Truth 4 (the hollow are general; use can train answering but not restore the particular). Shown (Years 34 to 35): board names and drawn ways home; most turn at the name like dogs and find the fire by the drawing with a child beside them; none knows the child; the woman points at the child holding her board; one man lies down in a channel; Hesk refuses the priests' cord; Wenna reads the sister's note and says nothing. Teaches: the damage to the self is permanent; answering is not knowing. New fixed fact.

6. Headwoman cuts the whole tally into stone on the willow walk, one line a year. Branch: Fading, a keeping that is a record, not a kind. Truth: carved stone spends almost no hold and persists; it preserves the word but not the thing, and does not count as keeping kinds (apples grafted, char penned, dialect songs taught), so the Year 40 Hollin clock fired. Shown (Years 33 to 40): the cutting, the elders' arguments, the headwoman's death mid-task, the granddaughter finishing, Oldmark's rider reading it and nodding, the Year 40 reading where "the char have not run" is legible and no one knows what a char is. Teaches: stone holds the name; only mouths and breeding hold the kind.
