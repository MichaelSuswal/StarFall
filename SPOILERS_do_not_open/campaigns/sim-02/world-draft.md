# World draft: campaign sim-02, Night 1

Written 2026-11-19, Night 1, from the blend of eight surveys (keeper log 1.5) and the star (`S/star.md`). Vote counts are in the keeper log and nowhere else. Everything marked unplaced is placed on Night 3. Nothing here is released whole.

## Tone

Dusk held a long time. Low amber light on wet stone; smoke going straight up; a window lit far off; fog at gates and on morning rivers; weather moving across a wide sky over grass. Lamplight and small fires that do not go out. The world is lived in and older than anyone's opinion of it, indifferent and not cruel, and under it something deep and patient with its own business. Peoples are watchful, tired, proud, close in winter. Palette within the house style: umber, ochre, wet grey, olive, near-black, one warm source; grey rain and fog as the weather tendency. One country colored by a dry hollow under a hill where weather does not reach; one by a crowded warm harbor.

## Climate and seasons (Q3 at 5)

Four real seasons of a middle temper, varying by latitude, altitude and distance from the sea. Winters hard enough to shut fords and keep people indoors telling stories, snow in the hills, ice on the shallows. A flooding thaw. A true summer. Long autumns with early dark, which is when most of the gods wake. Each god's waking season is on its sheet; the player's goes to the clock in 3b.8.

## The star, as it bears on the land

- Falling phase: the star is large, low and bright; its descent will be visible to the eye within the campaign; fading is visible in many places; the fall is 100 to 300 years off. The land shows it: shallowing lakes, flattened dialects at the edges, salt where water was, old places gone general.
- Living radius 120 days' walk from the center. The sheet may lie wholly inside it; the Pale's edge is beyond the fog or at the far edges (3a.3).
- Center body: a sleeping beast, visibly a beast, swollen, at the star's center. The painting must show its ground.
- Long game: hoarder only. No carriers, no thinners. Nothing stands in for them.
- Remainder: fell in godless country between two seats, the night the gods arrived.
- Star content: cities on stilts over a shallow sea, in dreams only.
- Ascended: mostly the very old, speaking in weather; frequent and strong (falling phase), few offers.

## Magic (Q6 at 4, final; Q7 pool)

Level, final tonight: a rumor most people doubt, true in some valleys and towns, never reliable enough to build a trade on; about one in a few hundred has the grip and most do not know it. Never everywhere, never gone.

How it feels and costs, one system, consistent with Truth 4: a working is attention paid, a listening; it feels like pressure behind the eyes, cold in the hands, a taste of iron, a sense of being watched by something patient or carried by something. It is simple: warmth from cold, a bleed stopped, a thing found, weather read. It costs the worker something small and specific that the worker does not choose and notices only afterward: a name, a face, the feel of an afternoon, a season of warmth or sleep. Practitioners are quiet, vague and strange to be around. Local understandings that may survive, often wrong: that a lender remembers and the price is written down; that the sky is the creditor; that the cost is a secret spoken; that it costs years from the far end of life. Given to the player: only that it is simple and costs the worker.

## The two rules (votes in the log)

- God-sense: a god knows another god only face to face, in person, within sight and speaking distance, each seen by the other. At a distance there is nothing beyond what every god knows from arrival, that others like it came down when it did. A god that keeps out of sight is not recognized. Once face to face the knowing is mutual, immediate and sure; neither can pretend not to be a god. It tells only that the other is a god, nothing of its name, nature or power. No direction, nearness, weight or count is ever felt, because nothing is felt before the meeting.
- Bodies: one body. No god has more than one.

## How much gods stand out (Q8 at 5)

A god is plainly more than a mortal in what it endures and perceives and can still be mistaken for weather, luck or a strange old person by those who do not look. Direct force is not what marks a god. A mob in numbers is a real threat. Mortals' first reaction to a god is wariness and misreading, not awe; awe is earned.

## The wild (Q9 at 2)

Familiar animals nearly everywhere, with old legends attached. Three or four nameless kinds in the whole world, kept rare, in far, high, deep or wet places; slow and large rather than quick. Unplaced: a big old thing in the deep pools; a slow thing in the high caves; something in the water with no manners. Not a zoo.

## The terrain list and each god's placement wish (Q2)

The world must contain: river country; hill country; high folded mountains with narrow passes and high valleys with one way in; marsh in the low ground; forest on slopes; steppe and the broken hills at its edge; a coast with drowned flats, an estuary and a shallow shelf; border country where lands change; ground for the center beast. No desert, no ice.

Wishes, one per god, honored in the placement plan (3a.3):
- player: river country rising to hills then high folded mountains, valleys each a small country with their own weather and dialect, marsh low, forest on the slopes, a far seldom-visited coast.
- god-beasts: steppe and its broken hill edge, rivers to cross, not settle on.
- god-thresholds: hills and river country, narrow passes, where one land ends and another starts.
- god-rivers: river country from cold hill springs to a slow mouth, marsh at the bottom, crossings.
- god-secrets: river country with a walled town on a bend, morning fog.
- god-storms: steppe breaking into ridges under an open sky.
- god-sea: coast, drowned coast, tidal flats, an estuary, a shelf, a city with its feet in the tide.
- god-stone: mountains, high valleys with one way in, places to be under.

Four gods want river country; the land is big enough that four river countries are different rivers. Two want steppe; one steppe with two edges. The player's far coast and god-sea's coast may be one coast at two distances.

## Past the borders (Q4 at 6) and the age of the story (Q5 at 9)

Three to five godless peoples, tired and proud and rooted, hold a little over half the usable land by area, far from most seats and nearer to one or two. Real wild country lies between them and at the sources and heights, and border country where no one is quite in charge. Under everything: ruins under ruins over thousands of years, several civilizations risen and fallen, the oldest layers unreadable and disputed, the lowest under water in places and under the hills in others. The deep history goes to this depth.

## Pools: widespread features, then rare candidates (unplaced)

Fears. Widespread, standing threats for the schedule: winter and hunger; a slow sickness no one can name, a road people stop using, a village gone quiet; the quiet debt called in at the worst time. Rare, unplaced: a door that should stay shut and what was left behind following; being known too well, a thing that waits; a thing that comes up out of a river at night; the slope letting go; fog and a ship-borne sickness.

Scarcity. The world's two: salt; and light for the dark months, meaning lamp oil, wax, clear glass and dry fuel, which the long autumns and early dark make bite everywhere. Local: sweet water on the coast and in drought country; good iron on the steppe; safe passage and safe ground as conditions.

Buried things. Widespread across the land: sealed doors closed from outside on purpose; old roads that go where no one remembers, ending at rivers, at rock, or running into the water; bones of something enormous, ambiguous between skeleton and structure, a rib standing from a hillside, a hollow where something lived; and, deepest, the blue-grey glass. Rare, unplaced: a drowned town under a made lake; a sealed door beside a spring; wrecks and sealed holds on the sea floor and on land the sea left; a passage with no exit; a deep place with something in it no one wants to find; gates with no walls. Underground sheet: decided in 3a.3.

Non-human peoples (Q13 at 2). Two. Widespread wish: a quiet folk of the high places and passes, night-active, who count and trade when they choose and do not talk of themselves. Second: a wary people of the water's edge, reeds or shallows. Both unplaced. Each gets a real backstory. Nothing of this reaches the player.

Travelers (Q14 at 3). About one a generation across the land, each from a plane entry, each with one tool or idea that does not fit and is hard to use well; treated with suspicion, some hunted. Candidates for what they brought: locks or the means to open them; tools that no longer work properly; cargo no one can price; something they cannot name, carried hurt; arriving at a threshold unable to say how.

Mortals worth following (Q17). The world's peoples tend to produce the ordinary, competent and stubborn with a private contradiction: keepers of what was said and of honest counts; those at a crossing who decide who passes; traders with a late conscience; midwives and failed scribes who carry more than they should. Candidates for the small cast, none scripted.

Discoveries (Q18). Truths: what the gods are and what happened here before them; why the sky came down once; why a god left; what it failed before; an old thing with a god's name in its mouth. Places: a door no one built; a place only between; the first spring every river begins from; a floor to the sea; a place where the sky is lower. Companions: earned and unusual; one who also fled; one neither god nor mortal and older; one unafraid of the deep; one who can be near without fear. Candidates for the layered mysteries and the cast, within the lore.

Undared wishes (Q19). Eight, kept in the keeper log, each a candidate for a place, being, hidden thing or thread, never a rule, never placed where its own god is certain to find it. Unplaced.

## Left out

Nothing was left out tonight for failing the star or the lore. god-beasts' undared wish touches a truth that cannot be made a rule and is held as a thread only.
