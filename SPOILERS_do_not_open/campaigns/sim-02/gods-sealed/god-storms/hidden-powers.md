# Sealed powers of god-storms (filed under Storms)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Whole Count
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: Of those the god has counted as its own, one knot each on its cord, it knows at every dusk whether the count is whole or short, wherever they are. Whole or short, and nothing else: not who, not where, not why.
Drawback: When the count is short the god can read no sky and turn no weather until it has stood among its people and counted them again with its own eyes, and the taste of metal does not leave its mouth until it has.
Limits:
  - Only those it has knotted on the cord while they stood in its sight; a knot is one living person.
  - It tells of loss or death, never of danger to come.
  - It gives no cause and no place.
  - Weaker than Foreknowing: one word at dusk, and it silences the god's other powers when the word is bad.
What would surface it: A loss: it surfaces the first time one the god has counted as its own dies or is lost out of its sight. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Spared Ground
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: Lightning does not strike within a few paces of where the god stands in the open.
Drawback: It strikes the nearest tall thing beyond those paces instead: a tree, a tent pole, a rider.
Limits:
  - A few paces, in the open, in either shape.
  - It turns a strike aside; it calls none and stops no storm.
  - The god does not choose where the strike goes.
What would surface it: A strain: it surfaces the first time the god is caught in a breaking storm with someone standing close beside it. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
