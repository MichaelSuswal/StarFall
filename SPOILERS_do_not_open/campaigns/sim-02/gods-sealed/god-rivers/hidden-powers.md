# Sealed powers of god-rivers (the Ferryman)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Thing Set Down
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: When a mortal, unprompted, sets a small thing in running water at a crossing, meaning it for whatever is there, the god hears the words said with it, wherever on that river the god is, and may grant that giver one safe crossing of that river, anywhere along it, within the year.
Drawback: An offering taken is a thanks landed. At that crossing the Face of the Weather thins: for a season, mortals there half remember that someone old was standing by, and the god's workings at that crossing can be traced to it by anyone who looks.
Limits:
  - Only an offering freely given by one who was not told to give it.
  - One crossing granted for one offering; it makes the crossing as safe as the Gate could, never safer.
  - It gives the god the words said and nothing of the giver's mind.
  - An offering made by or for another god, unruled until it happens.
  - Weaker than the Long Water: it reaches one giver at a time, and costs the god its hiding where it is used.
What would surface it: A being met: it surfaces the first time a mortal, of their own will, sets something in the water at a crossing while the god is anywhere in that river. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Clean Cup
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: Water the god lifts in its two cupped hands runs clean, however fouled the stream it came from.
Drawback: Its hands are numb for an hour after, as from the Gate, and it cannot feel what it holds.
Limits:
  - A cupped handful at a time. It cleans no well, no cistern and no stream.
  - It makes fouled water wholesome; it makes no salt water sweet.
  - It gives no knowledge of what fouled the water.
What would surface it: A strain: it surfaces the first time the god cups fouled water to give someone a drink. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
