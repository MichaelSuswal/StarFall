# Sealed powers of god-stone (filed under Stone)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Second Breath
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: One person asleep beside the god in an enclosed place of stone is kept through the night by the god's own endurance: they do not freeze, smother or starve while they sleep and the god stays beside them, however bad the air or the cold.
Drawback: The god cannot move from their side or wake them itself while it keeps them, and it counts their breathing the whole time, with its fear loud. If it leaves before they wake of themselves, whatever the night held falls on them at once.
Limits:
  - One sleeper, in an enclosed place of stone, the god within arm's reach.
  - It keeps a sleeper from cold, bad air and hunger; not from wounds, falling rock, water or anyone's hand.
  - On a god as the sleeper, unruled until it happens.
  - It works in the Winter Stillness only if the sleeper lay down beside the god before the stillness took it; the god does not choose it then.
  - Weaker than the Held Place: one life, one night at a time, and the god pinned beside it.
What would surface it: A being met at a place: it surfaces the first time someone small or weak falls asleep within arm's reach of the god in a closed place of stone where the night would otherwise kill them. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Sound Step
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: Through a bare foot the god knows whether the ground one step ahead will bear a grown mortal's weight: ice, a floor, a ledge, a scree. Yes or no.
Drawback: Its body will not step onto ground it knows is unsound, even to reach someone on it.
Limits:
  - One step ahead, by a bare foot, yes or no.
  - It tells that ground will not bear, never why or what lies under it.
  - It is the foot's knowing and needs no listening.
What would surface it: A place: it surfaces the first time the god sets a bare foot toward ice, a floor or a ledge that will not hold. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
