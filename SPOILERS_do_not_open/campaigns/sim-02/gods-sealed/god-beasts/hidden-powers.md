# Sealed powers of god-beasts (the Hunt-Warden)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Carried
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: Whoever the god lifts onto its shoulders and carries is kept: while they ride, no beast will harm them, they do not tire or take cold, and they see as far as the god sees.
Drawback: While it carries, the god cannot use the Study, the Turning or the Tally, and cannot set the rider down in haste: it must kneel to do it. For a day after, its shoulders ache toward the side the rider sat and it cannot lift its staff arm above the shoulder.
Limits:
  - One rider at a time, no larger than a grown mortal.
  - It guards against beasts, cold and weariness, not against mortals, weapons, falls or gods.
  - On another god as rider, unruled until it happens.
  - Weaker than its main powers: it keeps one person, for as long as the god will walk bent under them.
What would surface it: A being met: it surfaces the first time the god lifts a child, or anyone small and in need, onto its shoulders and walks. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Wary Sky
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: The god knows, a breath before it happens, when something large is about to fall from above within a stone's throw of it: a rock, a bough, a roof, hail of size.
Drawback: The knowing is a flinch. The god drops whatever is in its hands and ducks, whether or not it would choose to, and cannot speak for that breath.
Limits:
  - A breath of warning and no more; what falls, and why, it does not learn.
  - It warns of things that fall, never of the star, the weather to come or anything a day away.
  - It gives no cause.
What would surface it: A strain: it surfaces the first time something large falls near the god. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
