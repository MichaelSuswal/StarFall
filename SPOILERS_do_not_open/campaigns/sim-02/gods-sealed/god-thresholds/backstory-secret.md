# Secret backstory of god-thresholds (filed under Thresholds)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.5, after `S/gods-sealed/common-past.md` and tied to it. The god's own agent never receives this file, and neither does the DM. The past reaches the god only through play, as effects, never as a statement of what it is (mystery standard, rule 1).

## The rest of the story

The outline it holds: it held a gate for something greater; the weight of standing; a night it left the gate open and walked away; the sound the open gate made; the cold; not knowing whether it was released or ran.

The rest. It is the oath of the postern warden at Hesp, the gate town of Anmere. The gate had two doors. The great door was kept under the law of hospitality, with a lamp. The low postern beside it was kept by a warden sworn to the Varrach, the regency, and to no law of welcome: it opened for the regency's own comings and goings, and after the closing of the borders it was the door by which some of those turned away at the lamp were quietly let through for a price, and by which others were taken out. The warden's office was to stand, to open when told and to ask nothing. Its generosity is what the office was at its best, a door opened for someone the law had refused. Its hunger to be thanked is what the office never once received.

On the last night the warden left the postern open and walked away from the gate. No record says whether an order released it or whether it ran, and no record says what went out or came in by the low door, because that night the star fell on Anmere. The greater thing it served was the Varrach, and the same master was served by the maker of the regency's hidden ways.

## What it lost and does not remember

The face of its master. The gate itself, and that there was a second, greater door beside its own. Whether it was released. What passed through after it left.

## How this past might surface

A made closure that will not open to Unlatching because it was closed by hands like the ones it served, if the night 3 history places such a likeness, logged. Another god met face to face who seems to have stood at the same gate, by the other door. Another who seems to have had the same master. A dream beside the warm stone of a small door standing open on a dark road. Each memory its own power takes from it is the fog doing by rule what it did by nature. Nothing is scheduled.
