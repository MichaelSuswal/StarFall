# Sealed powers of god-thresholds (filed under Thresholds)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Door Held To
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: With its mist laid on a made closure, the god can hold it shut against every hand, bar, key and ram for as long as it stays there. It is the thing the office did for its master, and the thing the god says it will never do again.
Drawback: When it lets go, the god can open nothing until the next dusk: Unlatching fails it. And the count it keeps of doors opened is set back by one, which it feels.
Limits:
  - One closure at a time, the mist on it, the god beside it.
  - It holds a made closure only; a wall beside the door is as strong as it was built.
  - Against another god's hand, or a closure made by more than ordinary craft, unruled until it happens.
  - Weaker than Unlatching: it holds one door while the god stands there and gives up the god's own gift after.
What would surface it: A strain at a place: it surfaces the first time the god, with its mist on a door, wills with its whole body that the door stay shut, to keep something from someone on the near side. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The One Behind
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: Standing at a threshold, the god knows whether a person approaching it is being followed. Yes or no, and nothing else.
Drawback: The knowing passes through the one approaching as a chill between the shoulders; they feel watched, and some turn back because of it.
Limits:
  - It tells that someone follows, never who, how many, how far or why.
  - It works only at a threshold the god stands at, on one approaching it.
  - On a god approaching, or a god following, unruled until it happens.
What would surface it: A being met: it surfaces the first time someone who is being pursued comes to a threshold the god stands at. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
