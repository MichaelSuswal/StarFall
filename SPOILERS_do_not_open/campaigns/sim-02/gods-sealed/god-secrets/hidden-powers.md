# Sealed powers of god-secrets (the Listener)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Maker's Eye
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: The god knows when a hidden door, a vault or a sealed room lies within a stone's throw of where it stands. It knows that one is there and on which side of it. It does not know what is within, who made it, or why it was shut.
Drawback: Once it knows, it cannot leave the place until it has laid a hand on the door. Its body will not walk away; it may stand there in plain sight for as long as that takes.
Limits:
  - A stone's throw, and only while standing still.
  - It finds made hiding places, never a natural cave, a buried thing or a being.
  - It shows that a door is there and never what it keeps or why; of the great old things it gives the door and nothing more.
  - A hiding place made by another god, unruled until it happens.
  - Weaker than its main powers: it finds a door and binds the god to walk up to it.
What would surface it: A place: it surfaces the first time the god stands within a stone's throw of a door someone sealed on purpose and hid. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Weight Kept
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: Looking at a mortal for the space of a few breaths, the god knows whether they are carrying a secret they wish they could tell. Yes or no.
Drawback: The looking stirs it. The mortal feels the wish sharpen, and may tell the secret to the next person they trust, who need not be the god.
Limits:
  - Yes or no only; nothing of what the secret is.
  - It works on mortals, in sight, one at a time.
  - It does not make anyone speak; it makes the wish louder.
What would surface it: A being met: it surfaces the first time the god stands long before a mortal who is holding something they ache to say. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
