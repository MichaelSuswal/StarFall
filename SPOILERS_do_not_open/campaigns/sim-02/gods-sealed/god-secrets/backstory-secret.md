# Secret backstory of god-secrets (the Listener)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.5, after `S/gods-sealed/common-past.md` and tied to it. The god's own agent never receives this file, and neither does the DM. The past reaches the god only through play, as effects, never as a statement of what it is (mystery standard, rule 1).

## The rest of the story

The outline it holds: it served something larger and was useful to it; it made doors and ways and places to put what was not to be seen; it left, or ran; something like a hand on the back of the neck; it did not finish, or it finished and it was wrong.

The rest. It is the hidden works of the Varrach, the regency of Anmere: the makers of its vaults, its inward doors and its quiet removals, for as long as the regency ruled. The work it remembers in its hands is real: rooms cut under the Keld, ways under the walls of Hesp, the season in which every ford shrine on the Issel was stripped and the stone bearing the river's name was carried away to be sunk. Its honesty is the craft's honesty: a hidden door that does not work is a lie, and the works never lied about what they built. Its despair is what the works knew and could not say.

The last thing it made was the room under the Keld where the two child heirs were put, behind a door that opened only inward. Two went in. One came out. The makers built the room to keep children through the end of the world, and built it so that no one inside could open it. Whether that was the order, or their error, is the thing it cannot tell itself. On or before the last night the works left the regency's service. No release was written. Nothing looked for them, because that night the star fell.

## What it lost and does not remember

The face and name of its master. What the last room was for and who was in it. That it stripped a river of its name. That nothing is following it, because the thing that would have is gone.

## How this past might surface

A sealed door of the old world that it finds it understands the making of, if the night 3 history places such a likeness, logged. After it has dreamed beside the warm stone: a small god with a child's face, known from the dream, before whom it cannot bear to stand near a closed door; an old one at a ford, known from the dream, that it cannot look at for long; a fog at a gate that it knows from the dream once answered to the same voice. A dream beside the warm stone of hands fitting a door into rock from the outside. Nothing is scheduled.
