# Sealed powers of god-sea (the Factor)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Swallowed Thing
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: The god can take into itself one object no larger than a fist, and it is gone: not on its body, not in its coat, not findable by any search. It can bring it up again whole, at any time, from its mouth.
Drawback: While it holds a thing so, it tastes only that thing, Keeping What Sinks tells it nothing, and the first thing it ever swallowed stirs: the god is afraid without cause for as long as the object is in it.
Limits:
  - One object at a time, no larger than a fist, nothing living.
  - A thing made by more than ordinary craft, or belonging to another god, unruled until it happens.
  - It hides a thing; it tells the god nothing about the thing.
  - It does not bring up what it swallowed before it fell here.
  - Weaker than Slack Water: it hides one small thing and costs the god its sense of the deep and its ease.
What would surface it: A strain: it surfaces the first time the god, in real fear of being found with it, must hide something small and has nowhere to put it. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Fair Pan
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: Whatever is set on the god's pan scale, the god knows its true weight and whether it is what it is sold as: salt cut with sand, a clipped coin, wet grain.
Drawback: If anyone asks the god the weight or the worth of what is on the pan, it must answer truly and aloud.
Limits:
  - Only what is on its own scale, and only weight and adulteration.
  - It tells that a thing is false, never who made it so.
  - A thing made by more than ordinary craft, unruled until it happens.
What would surface it: A being met: it surfaces the first time someone sets a cheat on the god's own scale. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
