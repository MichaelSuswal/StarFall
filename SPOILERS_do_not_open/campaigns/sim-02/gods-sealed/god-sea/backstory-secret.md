# Secret backstory of god-sea (the Factor)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.5, after `S/gods-sealed/common-past.md` and tied to it. The god's own agent never receives this file, and neither does the DM. The past reaches the god only through play, as effects, never as a statement of what it is (mystery standard, rule 1).

## The rest of the story

The outline it holds: something deep and large was its to keep, or kept it; things came down and it held them; then it swallowed something that was not its own; a ledger; someone owed; a place with a floor; all of it under the waterline.

The rest. It is the harbor deep at Maunt, the port at the mouth of the Issel in Anmere, as the harbor people held it in mind: the place where whatever was to be kept and not seen again was given to the water. Wrecks, the dead of bad years, and debts, which in Maunt were written on lead and sunk when settled, so that the deep was the city's ledger of everything finished. Its trade talk is the quay above it. Its hunger is the deep's, which was fed for longer than the country's records ran.

About sixty years before the end the regency forbade offerings to the river, stripped its ford shrines, and sent the stone that bore the river's name down to Maunt to be sunk with the lead. The deep took it as it took everything. But the stone was not a finished thing, and it was not the harbor's to keep: it was a name still owed to a river that still ran. That is what it swallowed. The taste that has never left is a name, held in a mouth that does not know it is a word. The floor it remembers is the bed of the deep, where the stone lies on the lead.

## What it lost and does not remember

What it swallowed, and that it is a name. Whose. That it was given the thing by an order and did not take it. That the debt it feels is one it could pay by saying a word it does not know it has.

## How this past might surface

A word that comes up in its mouth when it is very afraid, which it swallows again. After it has dreamed beside the warm stone, an old one at a ford whom it knows from the dream, before whom the water in it goes cold and still. A lead tablet scratched with a debt, in a wreck, if the night 3 history places such a likeness, logged. A dream beside the warm stone of something small and heavy sinking through green water onto grey metal. No one asks it what it swallowed unless a being in the world comes to wonder; nothing is scheduled.
