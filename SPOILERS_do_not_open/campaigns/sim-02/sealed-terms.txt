# Sealed terms: sim-02
#
# Read by tools/lockcheck.py --leaks as the primary sealed list (change list item 30).
# One term per line; lines starting with # are comments. Each term is matched as written,
# case and all, as a whole word or phrase, and also without a leading "The" or "the" when two
# or more words are left ("the Stray" is matched only with its article).
# Any hit in campaigns/sim-02/ or in images/requests/sim-02-*.json fails the lock.
#
# Written for build 3 as a worked example, from the existing sealed files: the NPC gods'
# power cards (Name sections), the eight hidden-powers files, and the common secrets file.
#
# Left out on purpose, with reasons:
# - The one-word filing names of three NPC gods (an ordinary capitalized word each). Matched
#   case and all they would still fire on any sentence that starts with that word (the public
#   files already have two such lines). The god folder labels are matched by the tool anyway.
# - Bynames that are ordinary phrases in lower case ("the quiet one", "the one who keeps"):
#   the public files use one of them in plain prose about a gatekeeper.
# - Map names: the tool reads them from map.json and fails every one the player has not seen
#   or been told, and stops failing it when the player learns it. A map name written here
#   would keep failing after the player learns it. Only places that are not on the map
#   (the far country of the common secrets) are listed.
# - Mystery titles, schedule titles, star values, phenomena counts and the count of gods:
#   the tool reads them from their own files.
