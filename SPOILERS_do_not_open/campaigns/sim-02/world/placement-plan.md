# Placement plan: campaign sim-02, creation Night 3a

A written geography, not a drawing, made before any painting. The painting prompts are written from it; the paint then decides, and the map data follows the paint. Nothing in any release or record is written from this plan: every description of land comes from the traced map. Working names here are plain descriptions; the traced map gives names and ids.

## The sheet

One continent on one square sheet, about 2,560 miles across at two and a half miles to the pixel, north at the top. The star's center is near the middle of the sheet. The living radius is 120 days' walk from the center, about 1,680 miles on rough ground, so the whole sheet lies under the rain except its farthest corners, which are sea or fog. Sea lies along every margin: a narrow sea under cliffs in the west, a cold sea in the north, a broad shallow sea in the south and south-east. No desert, no ice.

## The ground

- The western range: a long folded mountain range down the western third, north to south, highest and widest in its middle, snow on its heights, high closed valleys in its heart, forest on its eastern slopes. Narrow cliff coast beyond it.
- The north: the range drops to steep wooded hills. A river gathers from the north-western uplands, crosses low wet ground, and runs north through a gap in those hills to a lowland and the northern sea.
- The north-west uplands: between the range and the central lowlands, hill country cut into closed river valleys, each shut in by its ridges; their rivers run east and north-east down to low wet ground and the northern river.
- The center: a wide, shallow, pale, bare basin ringed by worn low hills, with no river leaving it. At its middle, the sleeping beast: a single dark mound.
- The north-east and east: a wide grass steppe. Along its northern edge, broken stony hills falling to the north-east coast, crossed by steppe rivers running north. On its eastern side, long parallel ridges.
- The south-center: a long river rising in the hills east of the basin, running south-west and then south across wide farmed lowlands to a great delta marsh on the southern sea.
- The south-east: a low drowned coast of islands, creeks and tidal flats, with the estuary of a second big river that comes down from the steppe's southern edge; a pale shallow shelf offshore.
- The south-west: low wooded hills below the range's southern end, and a slow river looping through them to the south-western sea, with one great bend.

Every terrain on the Night 1 list is here: river country (four different rivers), hill country, high folded mountains with passes and closed valleys, marsh on the two largest rivers, forest on slopes, steppe with its broken edge and its ridges, a coast with flats, an estuary and a shelf, border country at the gap, and the center's ground. No underground sheet: see the keeper log, step 3a.3.

## The eight seats

A loose ring around the center, none in the basin, each in the country its answer 2 and the placement draft gave it, each many weeks' walk from the next.

- The player's god: the north-west uplands, in one closed valley under the range, with a cold quick river, walled fields, forest on the slopes, low wet ground far downstream. As its Day 3 brief described it.
- god-stone: the heart of the western range, above a walled seat at the foot of a high valley.
- god-thresholds: the northern gap, at the crossing town where the road meets the river.
- god-beasts: the north-east, where the steppe meets the broken hills.
- god-storms: the east, on the ridged side of the steppe.
- god-sea: the south-east estuary, at the walled city on the tide.
- god-rivers: the south-center, on the long river's middle course, above the old kingdoms' ground.
- god-secrets: the south-west, at the walled town in the river's great bend.

## Between the seats

- The three crowns of the lower river hold the long river's lower course and the plain behind the south-east coast: nearest to god-rivers and god-sea, as those two asked, and far from the rest.
- The kingdom at the north river's mouth holds the northern lowland up to the foot of the gap: god-thresholds sits on its border.
- The people of the rim hold the inner slopes of the hills around the basin and nothing else.
- The pass folk hold the heights of the western range, between and above the player's god, god-stone and god-secrets.
- The reed folk hold the delta marsh of the long river.
- Wild and unheld: the upland valleys apart from their own small peoples; the steppe's middle; the far north-east coast; the western cliff coast; the hills at the river sources.
- The warm stone that fell with the gods: in a high hollow of the range between god-stone's seat and the player's god's, in the pass folk's country. The pair was chosen by roll [r-0143], face 8, stone and player.

## Borders

Borders lie where the land changes: the tree line, the foot of the range, the gap, the rim's crest, the edge of the grass, the marsh's edge, the flood line.
