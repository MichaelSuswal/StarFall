# Starting state: the Senn

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 1,600
stores: enough grain and cheese for the winter; lodging stores good; salt adequate (it comes through them)
treasure: the toll chest, modest; bronze and cloth
trade: the toll, lodging, guiding; salt and oil pass south through them, wool and cheese north
strength: about 250 who can hold the wall; a night watch of twelve on the two gates
mood: proud, wary of the north since the envoy came
