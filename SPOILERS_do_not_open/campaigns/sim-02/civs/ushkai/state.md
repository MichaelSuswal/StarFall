# Starting state: the Ushkai

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 1,900
stores: thin after winter, as always in spring; milk coming in with the new grass
herds: about 6,000 sheep and goats, 1,400 horses, 1,100 cattle
treasure: little; felt, horses, some bronze
trade: horses, felt and wool for bronze, salt and grain, at the grass's southern edge
strength: about 400 riders with bow and lance
mood: relieved by the spring, divided about next winter's ground
