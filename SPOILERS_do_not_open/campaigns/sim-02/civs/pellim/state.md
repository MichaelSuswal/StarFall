# Starting state: the Pellim

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 20,000
stores: the boats are home heavy; with the grain boats stopped, stores carry the city two months
treasure: large and scattered among the wards; coin of several mints, cloth, bronze, tin, amber
trade: the heaviest on this coast; fish, salt, cloth and carriage out; grain, tin, timber and oil in
strength: about 2,000 ward-watch who can man the wall, 120 hulls of size; no standing soldiery
mood: generous, loud, sure of itself; amused by the crown's letter
