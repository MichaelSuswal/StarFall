# Starting state: reedfolk

population: about 4,000
stores: smoked eel and fish, reed flour; enough
treasure: small; river pearls, things brought up from the drowned stones
trade: eels, fowl, reed goods and guiding for grain, salt and bronze
strength: about 500 who fight from the water with spear and net; nearly unreachable in their own channels
mood: wary; quietly troubled about the east reach
