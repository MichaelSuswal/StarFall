# Starting state: passfolk

population: about 2,500, slowly falling
stores: sufficient for the winter inside the mountain
treasure: cords, cut stone, old copper; things found in the deep rooms and not shown
trade: silent trade by night at their two trading stones, the Leaving Stone [f67] and the Night Table [f68]: cord, horn and copper for salt, grain and bronze
strength: about 300 who would fight in their own passes, where they are very hard to find
mood: watchful; uneasy since the night of the falling
