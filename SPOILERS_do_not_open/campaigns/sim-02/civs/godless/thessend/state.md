# Starting state: thessend

population: about 60,000 (three cities and their villages)
stores: granaries two thirds full in the two upriver crowns, half in the coastal one; salt adequate; lamp oil short
treasure: large and mostly owed to each other; bronze, silver, cloth, records
trade: heavy on the river and along the coast; grain out, salt, tin and oil in
strength: about 3,000 levied spears and 400 horse among the three, never together
mood: tired, proud, mutually suspicious; the lowest crown uneasy about its old quarter
