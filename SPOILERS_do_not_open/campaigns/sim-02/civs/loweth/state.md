# Starting state: the Loweth

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,200
stores: grain low at the end of winter, as always at the thaw; fish and river trade steady; salt bought from downriver, dear
treasure: moderate; the toll chest, boats, bronze
trade: tolls and carriage on the river; grain, hides and timber down, salt, oil and bronze up
strength: about 600 who can be called, 40 boats; a wall in fair repair
mood: law-abiding and resentful; the doubled due is the talk of every landing
