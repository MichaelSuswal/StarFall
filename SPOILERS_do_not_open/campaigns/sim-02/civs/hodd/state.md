# Starting state: the Hodd

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,100
stores: put up for a long winter; adequate; lamp fat short by midwinter every year
treasure: modest; worked stone, bronze, wool, copper from the night trade
trade: wool, cut stone and cheese down the valley in summer; salt and grain up; silent trade with the heights
strength: about 600 who can hold the wall and the one way in
mood: steady, stubborn, divided about the cracked slope
