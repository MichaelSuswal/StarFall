# Starting state: the Brannow

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 9,000
stores: the harvest is in; stores carry the town eight months; the rest must be bought before the thaw
treasure: moderate; cloth, bronze, river goods, old plate in old houses
trade: fruit, cloth and river carriage out; grain in, every year; salt and oil in, dear
strength: about 900 who can man the walls; the walls old and thick
mood: close, comfortable, a little uneasy about bread prices and the heir's talk
