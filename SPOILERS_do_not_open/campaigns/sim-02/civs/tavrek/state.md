# Starting state: the Tavrek

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 2,100 in six bands
stores: dried meat and fat for an ordinary winter; salt short
herds: about 900 cattle and 600 horses of their own; they live mostly on the wild herds
treasure: little; horn, hides, a few bronze blades, the law skins
trade: hides, horn and dried meat for salt and bronze, at the hill fords, twice a year
strength: about 450 hunters who fight on foot and from horseback with bow and spear
mood: unsettled; the bands are gathering to decide the keeper, and two factions are forming
