# Sealed powers of the player's god (Vesper)

Written on creation Night 2, step 2.6, fitted to the secret backstory beside this file. Two powers, one cool and one minor, both weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is on any card until it surfaces in play; neither is ever scheduled. When one surfaces the Keeper releases it, and it is added to the card as a dated entry with the scene it surfaced in.

### The Refusal at the Sill
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the cool one
Description: Standing on a threshold it has been asked over, with a flame of its own burning inside, the god may name one person who shall not pass. That person cannot cross that threshold, by any door of that house, for as long as the flame burns and the god does not unsay it. It is the vow turned outward: the sorting the office once did.
Drawback: The bar binds the god too. While it holds, the god cannot cross that threshold in either direction and cannot step out of that hearth by the Door; it stands outside what it has shut. And the one refused knows, with certainty and at once, who refused them.
Limits:
  - One threshold at a time; a new refusal ends the old.
  - It bars a person, never a crowd, a beast or a god; on another god, unruled until it happens.
  - It ends when the flame inside goes out, when the keeper of the house asks the barred one in by name, or when the god unsays it.
  - It gives the god no knowledge of the person: no reading of intent, no cause. It is an act, never an answer.
  - Weaker than the Window and the Door: it reaches one doorway the god is standing in.
What would surface it: A strain at a place: it surfaces the first time the god, standing on a threshold it has been asked over with its own flame inside, refuses someone entry in earnest and means it with its whole body. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Lamp Count
Status: hidden (undiscovered), the minor one
Description: The god knows, at any moment, how many flames of its own are burning in the world. It knows the number and nothing else: not where they are, not who keeps them.
Drawback: Each time one of its flames goes cold the god feels it as a small dark, and its hood-flame gutters for an hour: it sees a pace less far, and anyone watching sees the flame dip.
Limits:
  - A number only. It gives no place, no direction and no cause.
  - The lantern is counted.
  - It serves what the player said the god wants (a hearth kept, a window still lit), and is held narrow for that reason: it tells the god that a light was lost and never which or why, so it gives the next question and never an answer.
What would surface it: A loss: it surfaces the first time a flame of the god's chain, other than the lantern, goes cold while the god is elsewhere. It is never scheduled.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
