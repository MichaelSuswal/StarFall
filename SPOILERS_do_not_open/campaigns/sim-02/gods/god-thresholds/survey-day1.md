## Answer 1

## Answer 2
Hills and river country. Narrow passes. Places where one land ends and another starts.

## Answer 3
Four seasons, each with a clear turning. Long autumns. Short, hard winters. Spring that comes late and means it. I want the turn itself, the days when one season stands in the door of the next. Wake me at the edge of cold.
I wake into autumn.
