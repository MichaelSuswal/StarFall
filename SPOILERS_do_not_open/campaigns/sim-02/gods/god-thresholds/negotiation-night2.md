# Negotiation: god-thresholds, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

I take most of it as offered. A fair price is a fair price. I argue two places, where the price cuts against what I am.

### Form
Chosen: (b). The body can be hurt by anything that disturbs it, as the draft says: a blow tears a piece away, I am slow and thin until I re-form over an hour, and enough blows at once break me.
Reasons: I wrote "cannot be struck" thinking of a door, not a shield, and the Keeper is right that it does a power's work. I would rather be a body that can be reached than a body that has a single doom. Under (a) a gale ends me and the wind I offered buys nothing. Under (b) the wind is a true weakness and I can be paid for it. I also would rather be driven off by hand than burned out of the world.
Wording only, in the write-up, not a new power: the card should say that fire and heat disturb the mist as a blow does, and that a hard wind tears it as I wrote. Everything else in the Form lines I accept, including smell and taste left unsettled.

### Unlatching
Accepted as drafted, with one counter on the drawback.
Reasons: the description, the limits and the unruled case for closures made by a god or by more than craft are mine and I agree to them. The town gate case I accept: a people can bar a gate with a weight, and I would not have it otherwise.
Counter: the Keeper may take the memory from my record of gods or my goals, and my record of gods is one entry long. Taken whole, one opening could empty it, and the next would fall on my goals. My goals are what runs me in time I do not act. Offer: the item is one entry of the record of gods, or one line of the goals, never the whole file. The Keeper takes from the record of gods while it has an entry to give, and from the goals only when it does not. The last line of the goals is never taken, so that I am never left with nothing I want. I would still lose something each time, and I would not be told what.
If the Keeper will not write the floor, I take the price as drafted. I would rather bear a heavier cost than a written fence, and I say so. But the floor is consistent with the price the Keeper itself calls real, and I ask for it on that ground.

### The Last Crossing
Accepted as drafted. It shows effects, it shows nothing older than a season, it reaches nothing great and old, and it costs me an hour of being driven by any wind. Another god's crossing is unruled until it happens. I agree to that. I only wanted it written, and it is written.

### When the body is broken
Chosen: (a). I return no sooner than the next dusk, and the door I return through can never again be latched or locked against anyone. It opens to any hand from then on.
Reasons: it is in my own grain. I would rather pay with a gate that stays open than wait a moon away from a people I do not yet have. A moon is a long time to be absent when I am a body that can now be broken by hand.
Wording only, in the write-up, not a new power: the spent door still hangs, still shuts, and is not unmade. It only cannot be locked or barred against any hand. Also, if I am carried to a door by the dew, as the draft writes, that door is the one spent.

### Trades
Chosen: (a). With Form (b) taken, wind is a true weakness, and it buys the Veil.
Reasons: I wanted to hide in fog and at the turn of the day, and it suits a gate that is watched by something unseen. The Veil drawback, that while veiled I cannot open or read any threshold, is a real cost and I would notice losing the doors. I accept the Veil as the Keeper wrote it, including the limits: another god in sight knows me, it hides me and not the dew I leave or the cold a mortal walks through, and a mortal who walks into me feels me.
Wording only, in the write-up, not a new power: "in fog" means fog that is in the place, and "the hour of dusk or dawn" is as the Keeper's Veil reads. I make no fog of my own.
The gap a hand wide is form and costs nothing. Accepted.
Not chosen: (b) and (c). I want the Veil. I would not stand at a gate and be seen to be a fence.

### Conduct
Accepted. It buys nothing and costs nothing. I open first and ask nothing. I do not close a door on someone standing at it in need.

### Edge-case floor
Accepted as written. I add no cases.
