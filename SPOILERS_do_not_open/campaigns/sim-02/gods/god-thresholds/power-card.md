# Power card: Thresholds (god-thresholds)

Campaign: sim-02. Written on creation Night 2, 2026-11-19, from the final text of round 2 as the god confirmed it, with the wording corrections of its round 2 reply. Description and Drawback text below is frozen. Limits settled at creation were settled at the Night 2 table; the arguments are in the sealed ledger.

## Form

Filed under Thresholds; it holds no name. Those who do not know it say the Fog at the Gate, or the Gatekeeper.

A standing fog, grey and pale, the height of a tall mortal and about as broad, narrowing toward the top. It has a front and a back. The front is a dark vertical gap down the middle, shaped like a door left half open; there is no face, and when the god attends to someone the gap turns to them. The mist is thick at the core and thin at the edges. It holds its shape in still air, drifts in wind and tears in a hard one. It moves low along the ground without feet and leaves a faint dew on whatever it passes close. It is cold to touch, and a mortal who walks through it is chilled and wet. It speaks in a low, dry voice that seems to come from the gap.

Bodies: one. A god in this world has one body and is in one place.

What the form can and cannot do: it passes through any gap a fog can pass, a hand's width or more. It cannot lift more than a mortal. The body can be hurt by anything that disturbs it: a blow or a blade tears a piece away, and fire and heat disturb the mist as a blow does; the god is then slow and thin until it re-forms over an hour, and enough at once breaks it.

Senses: it sees and hears clearly in all light; touch and cold faintly. Smell, taste and any sense not named are unsettled and asked in play, never inferred.

Pace: a mortal's walking pace along the ground, by the pace table.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: a god knows another god only face to face, in person, within sight and speaking distance, each seen by the other; the knowing is mutual, immediate and sure, and tells only that the other is a god. At a distance there is nothing to feel. Every god knows from landing only that others like it came down when it did.

When the body is broken. The god is gone from that place. It leaves one thing: a cold dew that does not dry, a handspan across, where it fell. It returns at the first door, gate or crossing that opens within a day's walk of where it fell, no sooner than the next dusk. If the dew is carried through a door, the god comes to that door instead, as if called. The door the god returns through, the dew's door included, can never again be latched, locked or barred against anyone: it still hangs and still shuts, and it opens to any hand from then on. Another god's hand on the dew, or on the return, is unruled until it happens.

### Definitions

- A made closure: a door, gate, bar, latch or lock made by mortal hands. A wall is not one; a weight is not one; a river or a ford is not one.
- A threshold: a door, a gate, a ford or a pass, a place where one crosses from one ground to another.
- In fog: fog that is in the place. The god makes none of its own.
- The hour of dusk or dawn: the hour of each at the place where the god stands.

### Things carried

Nothing.

## Powers

### Unlatching
Status: main
Description: Any door, gate, bar, latch or lock made by mortal hands that the god touches with its mist, it can open. Nothing is broken; it opens as if by someone on the other side who meant it. It does not unmake a wall, free a door jammed by weight, ice or rot, lift a bar that is only a weight, or open anything that is not a made closure. The god lays its mist across one threshold at a time and may hold it open while it stays beside it; anyone may pass while it is held. When the god withdraws, the closure is as it was.
Drawback: Each opening takes something small and dear from the god's memory without notice. The god does not know what went; it knows only, afterward, that something did.
Limits settled at creation:
  - How the drawback is enacted: for each closure opened, the Keeper removes one item from the god's records: one entry of its record of other gods while that record has an entry to give, and one line of its goals only when it does not. The last line of the goals is never taken. The god is not told what went.
  - Light and dark: the same.
  - Distance: none; the mist must be on the closure.
  - Water: works under water.
  - Carrying: a chest or a box works.
  - A door held shut by someone pushing opens, and the god does not push them back.
  - The god's own people: their gate works and costs the same.
  - Being watched: a watcher sees a door open, and a cold dew on the wood.
  - Other gods, and things made by more than ordinary craft: a closure made by a god, or warded, is unruled until it happens.
  - A town may bar its gate with a weight; the power does not lift it.

Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Last Crossing
Status: main
Description: When the god lays its mist on a threshold, it learns who or what last crossed within the last season, in what order, and which way each went: the number and the direction, never names, loads, meanings or reasons. It does not see them. Where many have crossed it learns only the last handful.
Drawback: The cold of each crossing stays. After a use the god is slowed for an hour, its mist thin and slow to answer, so that any wind drives it. A second use within a day doubles the hour.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Distance: none. Light and dark: the same. Water: nothing is read under water.
  - Nothing older than a season; nothing of the great old things. The god asked for this limit.
  - Other gods: another god's crossing is unruled until it happens; until it is ruled the god would learn only that one thing of unknown kind crossed.
  - It shows effects and never causes.

Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Veil
Status: main (bought by a trade: the wind, under Weaknesses taken)
Description: In fog, or in the hour of dusk or dawn, the god is not seen by mortals unless it chooses to be, even in plain sight within speaking distance.
Drawback: While veiled the god cannot open or read any threshold; it is a fog among fog and does nothing.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The god's wording of the ask: "that in fog or at dusk or dawn I cannot be seen unless I choose it, even in plain sight within speaking distance of mortals."
  - The Veil is a state the god enters and leaves by choice while in fog or in the hour of dusk or dawn. The drawback binds only while it is veiled; stepping out of the Veil, it may open and read thresholds again, and may be seen.
  - Other gods: another god in sight and speaking distance knows it, as the world sets.
  - It hides the god, not the dew it leaves or the cold a mortal walks through; a mortal who walks into it feels it.
  - Fog that is in the place only; the god makes none.
Rulings:
  - (none yet)
Trades: bought by the wind (Weaknesses taken). Recorded both ways.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The wind
What it is: a steady strong wind scatters the god. In a hard wind it cannot hold a place, loses cohesion, and is carried downwind at the wind's pace until it drops. In a gale it thins to nothing and re-forms where the gale ends. It binds the body.
How it can be used against the god: a people that learns it can drive the god from a gate with bellows, fans or a fired draught, and a windy pass is closed to it on most days; weather alone can carry it away from where it is needed.
What it bought: the Veil.
