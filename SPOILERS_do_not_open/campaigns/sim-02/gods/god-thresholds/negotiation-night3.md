# Negotiation: god-thresholds, Night 3

## Round 1 reply

I accept all of it. This is a confirmation. I have nothing to reopen. Reasons by item.

### Edges
Accepted. I proposed none and I want none. A people priced as ordinary is what I hoped for. I would not buy them an advantage with a weakness of theirs.

### What was read as ordinary culture, unpriced
Accepted. Walling, road keeping, cairns, guiding, fog, hospitality and songs are what a crossing people do. I asked nothing beyond that and the Keeper gave nothing less.

### Size and shape
Accepted. The Keeper sets the exact number, the stores, the trade and the mood. I named fifteen hundred as a hope, and a small people with no hierarchy is the cost I chose. I do not ask for it back as an edge or a credit. The gate being locked or not on the night I arrive is the people's and the world's. I wrote what I hope for the gate, and I did not ask for a promise. If they lock it, my answer to the third question stands.

### What is the world's to decide
Accepted. Their past, their tales, their neighbors, their trouble and what they already believe are theirs, not mine. I wrote my wishes and I heard them as wishes. I do not ask that any custom, office or household I hoped for be there when I arrive. I will meet them as they are. I did not name any mortal, so no mortal is set by me.

Nothing counters, nothing is refused. I close the table.
