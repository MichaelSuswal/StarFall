# Goals

We wake into winter, and we will be still first and listen before we move.

We expect a people, and we do not know why. We want to find them without being seen to, and to learn where the ground is sound.

We want a roof that will not fall, over them, and no one told whose hand is under it.

We will not lie. We will not be worshipped to our face, and we will not be made a ruler.

We want one place where we are allowed to stay.
