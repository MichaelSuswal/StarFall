# Negotiation: god-secrets, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

I read each item for what it takes from me and what it gives. I accept where the price is fair and the wording is mine. I argue where something was left open.

### Form, and the thing you carry
Accept. The added lines are true of me: one body, mortal pace, not strong, hurt as anyone is. The cloak is a cloak. I agree the forgetting of my face belongs to the Unmarked Hand and not to my form. I ask one thing in the write-up, not a new power: that the card say "the rest unsettled" and that no sense be inferred for me.

### The Kept Word
Accept as drafted. The guard is against mortal workings only, which is what I wrote. A knife or a cup is no working, and I would not want a power that did what no power should. The drawback stays as the Keeper wrote it, including that I cannot break a secret even if I wish to. I would take that over a written fence, and I would take it heavier before I took a lighter one with a rule on it. I ask one ruling in the write-up, not a new power: under the Unmarked Hand the teller forgets my face by the next day. The card should say whether the teller still remembers that they told a secret to someone, and still feels the lightness. I say they do. The lightness is the power. Forgetting my face does not undo it.

### The Unmarked Hand
Choose (b). Reasons: I meant that the forgetting should not wear thin, and the Keeper reads that it never did. I take that reading and do not argue it. What I want is a household that keeps a door unlatched for someone it cannot picture, and only (b) gives that. Under (a) nothing of me is kept, and a people cannot serve what leaves nothing to know.

I counter on one point. A voice known in the dark is still a trace, and I am hidden by lean. I ask that the knowing be recognition only: a mortal who has met me three times knows me when I speak, but cannot describe my voice or my manner to anyone else, cannot imitate it, and cannot give it to a tracker or a rival god as a mark. They know me. They cannot hand me over. If the Keeper cannot write that, I accept (b) as drafted, and I note that the cost then is the Keeper's to enforce and not mine.

The drawback stands as written. My people cannot thank me, pass my name on or name me in an offering. I do not ask it lightened.

### The Inward Door
Accept as drafted, including the added limit that the door is more than ordinary craft to any power that works on made things. I do not resist this fence, though freedom is my lean. Naming the case now is better than meeting it unruled later, and the added limit costs me nothing I would use. The drawback stands as I wrote it. I would take a heavier one over a fence. I do not ask for the door to open outward, and I do not ask for a second passage in a season.

### When the body is broken
Accept. A season gone is a real price and I would not have it shorter. I accept the added line that I return at the stone if I have no people or none of their doors stands. I ask one wording in the write-up, not a new power: that the season counts from the moment of the breaking, and that a door I made with the Inward Door counts as a door of my people for the return, if my people have been named to it.

### Trades
Accept the weakness as drafted: I cannot speak a falsehood aloud, and if I try my voice stops. I take the Keeper's account of how it is used against me. A rival who learns it can read my silences, and I cannot bluff. I knew this when I offered it. It costs me what I seldom use, and I will answer true or be silent.

I counter on the price. The Keeper rates the weakness as middling and the buy as the same size. I ask that the knowing under (b) come after two meetings, not three. Reasons: I am wary and I will be met seldom, and a god that is met rarely and forgotten each day needs the knowing to start early or it starts too late to matter. A household has to learn to leave the door unlatched in one winter, not three. If the Keeper holds three, I accept three. I will not reopen it.

### Edge-case floor and the four tests
Accept the floor as written in each Limits block. I accept the Keeper's naming of the Unmarked Hand as a power over minds at a people's scale, priced by its drawback that my own people cannot hold it either. That is true and I have no better price to offer. I accept that the Inward Door is a making of more than craft. I accept that the Kept Word guards against workings and nothing more.

Summary of choices: (b) under the Unmarked Hand, with recognition only and not describable, and the knowing at two meetings; all else accepted as drafted, with two write-up wordings asked (the teller's lightness, and the season and doors on the return).
