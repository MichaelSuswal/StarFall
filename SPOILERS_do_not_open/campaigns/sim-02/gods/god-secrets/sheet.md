## Bargaining stance
Hard (score 81: Appetite 62, Trust 94, Regard 88). It contests every price it thinks too high, offers its own drawbacks in place of the Keeper's, and accepts a counter only for a reason it respects. Mind toward clever: it hunts for wordings, loopholes and the case nobody priced. Body toward strong: it bargains for force and reach. Presence toward hidden: it prefers powers no one can trace to it.

## Traits (own)

It sets no law and keeps all the keys.
