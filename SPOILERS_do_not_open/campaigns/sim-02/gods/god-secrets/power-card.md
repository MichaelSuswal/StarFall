# Power card: the Listener (god-secrets)

Campaign: sim-02. Written on creation Night 2, 2026-11-19, from the final text of round 2 as the god confirmed it, with the wording corrections of its round 2 reply. Description and Drawback text below is frozen. Limits settled at creation were settled at the Night 2 table; the arguments are in the sealed ledger.

## Form

Filed under the Listener; it holds no name. Those who do not know it call it the quiet one, or the one who keeps.

Human-seeming, of middle years, neither man nor woman at first glance, and then one or the other as the looker needs. Slight, a little under an average man's height, narrow in the shoulder, long in the hand. The body is perfect in a way that is wrong: both sides match; the skin has no scar, freckle or line; the hair is dark, short and never out of place. The face is calm and plain. The eyes are grey and steady and blink less than they should. The mouth rests closed. It moves quietly with no wasted motion, keeps to walls and edges, and stops at doors before it passes them.

Bodies: one. A god in this world has one body and is in one place.

What the form can and cannot do: it is not strong; it cannot lift much, and a hard blow hurts it as it would anyone. It can stand a very long time in one place.

Senses: sight and hearing a person's, a little keen; it hears what is said low. The rest unsettled, asked in play, and no sense inferred.

Pace: mortal pace, by the pace table.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: a god knows another god only face to face, in person, within sight and speaking distance, each seen by the other; the knowing is mutual, immediate and sure, and tells only that the other is a god. At a distance there is nothing to feel. Every god knows from landing only that others like it came down when it did.

When the body is broken. The god is gone from that place. It leaves one thing, a plain iron-grey stone no larger than a fist, with a small closed hollow in its face like a keyhole, warm to the hand and bearing no mark of the god. Anyone may find it. One season after the breaking, the god returns by a door of the people it serves, from inside, in the room before anyone has seen it enter; a door of its own making counts if its people have been named to it. If it serves no people, or none of their doors stands, it returns at the stone. It remembers what was told it. Whoever broke it is not told where it is. Another god's hand on the stone, or on the return, is unruled until it happens.

### Definitions

- A secret: a thing a mortal tells the god of its own will, meaning it to be kept. A sleeper's telling, or a child's who does not understand, is not willing.
- A meeting: one occasion on which a mortal and the god speak together.

### Things carried

- A plain grey woollen cloak with a hood. A cloak.

## Powers

### The Kept Word
Status: main
Description: Whatever a mortal tells the god of its own will, as a secret, the god holds. It is safe in the god; the teller feels lighter and cannot be made to repeat it by mortal magic for as long as the god holds it. The god cannot speak it to anyone, in any way, ever. The secret does not fade in the god.
Drawback: The god cannot unhear. Every secret kept stays in it, and it cannot act on one against the teller, nor speak it to save a life, nor break it if it wishes.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The guard on the teller is against mortal magic only. A teller may still be made to speak by ordinary means, and may choose to.
  - The teller remembers that they told a secret to someone, and keeps the lightness, though the face is gone by the next day (the Unmarked Hand).
  - Light, dark and distance: it holds if it is said to the god and the god hears it.
  - Told in front of others, it is shared and not the god's to keep alone.
  - Other gods: told while a god watches, it holds, and that god gains nothing by the watching; otherwise unruled until it happens.
  - The god's own people tell as anyone.
  - Water, falling and carrying change nothing.

### The Unmarked Hand
Status: main
Description: What the god does leaves no trace that points to it. Those it speaks with remember the words and not the face, and by the next day cannot say who it was; the face is never kept, however many the meetings. Tracks in soft ground are taken by the next rain. A mortal asked afterward cannot describe the god, though they may say honestly that someone came. A mortal who has met the god three times knows it when it speaks again, as one knows a voice in the dark.
Drawback: No one remembers the god's kindness or service. Its people cannot thank it, pass its name on, or name it in any offering; they are served and do not know by whom.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The god's first wording, kept as intent: "Those I speak with remember the words and not my face, and by the next day cannot say who I was." The knowing after three meetings was bought by the weakness (Trades).
  - The knowing is recognition only. One who knows the god cannot describe its voice or manner to another, imitate it, or give it to anyone as a mark to find the god by. While the god stands before them speaking, they can say to another who is there, "this is the one."
  - Three meetings, not fewer.
  - Tracks go with the next rain and not before; on a dry day a tracker follows them like anyone's.
  - A likeness painted, or a word written on the day, is not forgotten, but names no one.
  - Light and dark: the same.
  - Other gods: another god face to face knows it as a god, unchanged. A god acting on this is unruled until it happens.
  - It takes one memory, the face, from everyone the god meets, its own people included.
Rulings:
  - (none yet)
Trades: the knowing after three meetings was bought by the weakness (Weaknesses taken). Recorded both ways.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Inward Door
Status: main
Description: Given a season of work by its own hands, with no mortal help and what is there, the god can make one passage in stone, packed earth or timber: a hidden way with a door that opens only inward. The door is unseen by any who do not already know of it. It opens for the god and for any the god names aloud at it. It leads to hidden rooms, cellars and ways under a wall.
Drawback: A passage of the god's has one way only. The door opens inward, so the god cannot leave by it unless someone outside opens it. Shut inside, the god stays until opened. Its own works are where it may be kept.
Limits settled at creation:
  - One passage per season of work, the god's hands only.
  - Water: below the water line a passage fills and is not closed by water.
  - Falling: a door does not open into open air.
  - Carrying: it cannot be moved once made.
  - Things made by more than ordinary craft: a door of the god's is such a thing; a power that opens made closures meeting it is unruled until it happens.
  - Other gods: another god does not see the door unless told, and then it is unruled until it happens.
  - The god's own people may be named to it, and then they may pass.

## Weaknesses taken

### The stopped voice
What it is: the god cannot speak a falsehood aloud. If it tries, its voice stops. It may say nothing, or say a true thing that misleads. It binds the body.
How it can be used against the god: one who learns it can read the god's silences as answers, and a direct question before witnesses forces a truth or a silence that is itself a confession. It can never bluff a rival god or deny that a secret exists.
What it bought: the Unmarked Hand's knowing after three meetings.
