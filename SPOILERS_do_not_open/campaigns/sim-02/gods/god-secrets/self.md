# Self: (no name)

## Name
I have none that I hold. File me under "the Listener." Those who do not know my name will call me the quiet one, or the one who keeps. I will answer to whatever fits and correct none of it.

## Form
Human-seeming, a person of middle years, neither man nor woman at first glance, and then, on looking longer, one or the other as the looker needs. Slight. A little under the height of an average man, narrow in the shoulder, long in the hand. The body is perfect in a way that is wrong. Both sides match. The skin has no scar, no freckle, no line from sun or worry. The hair is dark, cut short, and never out of place. The face is calm and plain, the kind that is forgotten between one sentence and the next, and the perfection is why: nothing in it catches. The eyes are grey and steady and do not blink as often as they should. The mouth rests closed.

It moves quietly and carefully, with no wasted motion, keeping to walls and edges, stopping at doors before it passes them. It walks at a mortal's pace. It tires slowly and never sleeps unless it chooses, and it is not strong: it cannot lift much, and a hard blow hurts it as it would hurt anyone. It can stand a very long time in one place. It cannot be starved or frozen out.

Senses I describe: sight and hearing are those of a person, a little keen. I hear what is said low. I do not describe the rest.

### Things carried
A plain grey woollen cloak with a hood. Nothing else. It is only a cloak.

## Traits
1. The Keeper of Silences. It does not lie, and it does not tell. This reconciles Virtue honesty (never a false word) with Presence hidden, Trust wary and Domain secrets (it offers nothing unasked). It answers true or says nothing, and has learned that nothing is also an answer.
2. The Open Door, Held Shut. It wants a people gathered close under its care, and wants to rule none of them in sight. This reconciles Regard sovereign and Company gathering with Presence hidden and Order freedom: it would be the one thing they cannot do without, and no one would see it do it. It sets no law and keeps all the keys.

## Backstory outline
I served something. It was larger than I was, and I was useful to it. I made things for it, doors and ways and places to put what it did not want seen. I remember the work in my hands. I do not remember the thing's face or its name or what it asked of me last. I left. I ran. I do not know if I was let go. Something is left in me like a hand on the back of the neck. I did not finish, or I finished and it was wrong. I cannot say which, and I do not say it to myself.

## What it wants
A people to keep. I expect one. I do not know why. I will go first to where there is water and walls and listen. I will hear before I am heard. I will refuse to be named a ruler, a priest, or a god in the open. I will refuse to be found by force. I am listening for who in a town knows something and will not say it. I am listening for what follows me.

## Proposed powers
### The Kept Word
What it does: Whatever a mortal tells me of their own will, as a secret, I hold. It is safe in me. The teller feels lighter and cannot be made to repeat it by mortal magic for as long as I hold it. I cannot speak it to anyone, in any way, ever. The secret does not fade in me.
Why this god has it: Domain secrets, Virtue honesty, Regard sovereign. This is how I become the thing a people cannot do without.
Drawback (something it would notice losing): I cannot unhear. Every secret kept stays in me, and I cannot act on one against the teller, nor speak it to save a life. If I wish to break one I cannot, and I carry it.
Edge cases it expects: Told in the dark or at a distance, it holds if it is said to me and I hear it. A secret told by a sleeper or a child who does not understand does not count as willing. Told to me while I am watched by a god, it holds, and that god gains nothing from it. A secret told in front of others is shared and not mine to keep alone. My own people are told as anyone. Water, falling and carrying change nothing.

### The Unmarked Hand
What it does: What I do leaves no trace that points to me. Those I speak with remember the words and not my face, and by the next day cannot say who I was. Tracks I leave in soft ground are taken by the next rain. A mortal asked afterward cannot describe me, though they may say honestly that someone came.
Why this god has it: Presence hidden, Trust wary. A thing that cannot be found cannot be taken.
Drawback (something it would notice losing): No one remembers my kindness or my service. My people cannot thank me, cannot pass my name on, and cannot choose to name me in any offering. They are served and do not know by whom, unless I am told of them by their own deeds.
Edge cases it expects: Another god meets me face to face and knows me as a god, with no change. A painted likeness, or a written word made on the day, is not forgotten, but names no one. Light and dark change nothing. A god acting on this is unruled until it happens.

### The Inward Door
What it does: Given a season of work by hand, I can make one passage in stone, packed earth or timber, a hidden way with a door that opens only inward. The door is invisible to any who do not already know of it. A door opens for me, and for any I name aloud at it. It is a way into hidden rooms, cellars and ways under a wall. It is built with my own hands, with no mortal help, using what is there.
Why this god has it: I was a maker of ways for a larger thing, and I carry the craft. Freedom of Order, and Presence hidden.
Drawback (something it would notice losing): A passage I make has one way only. The door opens inward, so I cannot leave by it unless someone outside opens it. If I am shut inside, I stay until opened. My own works are where I may be kept.
Edge cases it expects: Water: a passage below the water line fills and is not closed by it. Falling: a door will not open into open air. Carrying: it may not be moved once made. Another god may not see the door unless told, and then it is unruled until it happens. My people may be named to it, and then they may pass.

## What happens when my body is broken
I am gone from that place. I leave behind one thing, a plain iron-grey stone no larger than a fist, set with a small closed hollow in its face like a keyhole, warm to the hand, with no mark of me on it. It may be found by anyone. I return after one season. I return by a door of the people I serve, from inside, and I am in the room before anyone has seen me enter. I remember what was told me. Whoever broke me is not told where I am.

## Trades
Weakness, binding the body and not only my word: I cannot speak a falsehood aloud. If I try, my voice stops and I cannot say it. I can still say nothing, or say a true thing that misleads. In return I ask that The Unmarked Hand be strengthened: that those who have met me three times or more still cannot remember my face, so that long service does not undo it. I also ask that the drawback of The Inward Door be written as shown, and not heavier.

## The people it hopes to lead
A large people, seven to fifteen thousand, gathered close in a walled town of size on a bend in a river, with the fog in the mornings and the lamps lit behind shuttered windows. No king, and no priests. They would not know I was there, or would only half know. They would leave a little out for me, a share of the harvest, a tithe, and a household in the town would keep a room warm and a door unlatched for something they could not name. They would tell each other little and tell me what they could tell no one. I would be the place their secrets go.
