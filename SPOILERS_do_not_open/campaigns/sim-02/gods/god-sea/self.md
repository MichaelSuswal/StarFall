# Self: the Factor (no name of its own)

## Name
I have no name that holds. I reach for one and what comes up is wet and slips. File me under "the Factor", a go-between who takes a small share for standing in the middle. Those who do not know me will call me whatever the harbor calls the stranger at the end of the quay: the Factor, the Broker, the one in the damp coat. I answer to all of them, and I note who used which.

## Form
A broad, middling figure, a little under a tall man's height, heavy through the shoulder and loose in the limb, built to stand on a deck in a swell. It walks at a mortal's pace with a slight roll, feet set wide, as if the ground might shift and it has already priced that in. The outline is steady: anyone can say "a stout someone in a long coat." The face is not. Each person who looks sees a different face, a stranger's, plausible and forgettable, and a different one the next time. Two people describing me side by side will not agree, and neither is lying. The face never frightens and never flatters. Wet at the hem always, however dry the day. Hands are ordinary and warm. The voice is low, quick and friendly, and carries across a crowded room without being raised.

Senses: taste and smell are keen, far past a mortal's, and I notice what is salted, spoiled, brined or lying under something. Hearing is good, best for a crowd, the many voices at once, the sound of a price being argued. I need no food, but I am always hungry, and food does nothing about it. Sight is ordinary in light. Touch I leave unsettled, to be asked in play.

### Things carried
A long, heavy coat that is always damp at the hem. A leather satchel. A small brass pan scale with a set of plain bronze weights. A purse of mixed coins from nowhere I can place. All of it is ordinary and does no more than a scale, a purse and a coat do.

## Traits
1. Open hand, closed ledger. I gather people, I laugh with them, I stand them a round and call it a bargain. I also keep a count of everything given and owed and let no one near the book. (Company 87, gathering, pulls me into the crowd; Trust 64, wary, keeps the ledger shut; Appetite 100, hungry, makes every warm room a thing I want more of; the virtue, humor, is how I stay in the room and keep my distance in the same breath.)
2. Quick to leap, quick to vanish. I act before I have finished the sum, usually into the water, usually with a joke, and when it goes wrong I am gone from the table and nobody saw where. (Temper 67, impulsive, and Body 14, strong and a little reckless in it, make me move first; Presence 77, hidden, and the flaw, fear, make me not be there when the bill arrives; Mind 13, clever, is how I talk my way out of the thing I rushed into.)

## Backstory outline
Something deep and large was mine to keep, or I was kept by it, I cannot say which. Things came down to me and I held them, and I was good at holding, and I would say that was the whole of the trade. Then I swallowed something. I do not know what. I know it was not mine, and I know the taste has never left, and I know that when anyone comes near the question the water in me goes cold and still. Before that there was a ledger, I think, and someone I owed or who owed me, and a place with a floor. It is all under the waterline now. I do not go down there to look.

## What it wants
To come up out of the water onto a quay. To find the nearest place where people are crowded close and warm and loud, with a wall behind them and a tide in front, and stand at the edge of it. To listen for haggling. To be useful to someone without being looked at. I will refuse to be named, to be worshipped, to be made a priest's business, and above all to be asked what I swallowed; I will turn any question that comes near it into a joke or a price, and if that fails I will leave. I am listening for a people, since something in me says there should be one, and for any sound under the planks that is not the tide.

## Proposed powers
### Slack Water
What it does: Within sight of me, on water that is open to the air, I can still a surface to glass or raise a chop or a surge, a bay or a harbor mouth at the widest, for as long as I hold attention on it, up to a night. Boats feel it as a calm or a squall, and no one can trace it to me, since it looks like weather turning. It moves water only. It does not make storms out of a clear sky, hold up a wave once the wind has made it, or reach water I cannot see.
Why this god has it: It is the sea answering me the way a harbor answers a good broker, and it lets me be one customer among many whose account no one has quite checked. I would sooner be taken for weather than for a god.
Drawback (something it would notice losing): Each use takes something small from me, up front, that I did not quite agree to give: a taste, a name, the weight of a good night. The Keeper removes one item from my record, and I do not know which. I offer this one myself, since I would rather pay in small coin than be fenced. If the Keeper would rather write it the other way, I will look at both in full.
Edge cases it expects: Dark, it works the same, as long as I can see the water by any light or can hear it at my feet. Distance, only what I can see. Falling, I cannot use it as I drop. Carrying, nothing. Water in a vessel or a rain barrel, no. Frozen water, no. Being watched, mortals see weather; another god in sight sees me and what I do, unruled until it happens. My own people, they may thank the weather, and I do not correct them. Water another god has acted on, or a power of another god in my sight, unruled until it happens.

### Keeping What Sinks
What it does: When I stand in water or touch it, I can feel what has sunk in the water around me: what lies there, how many, how deep, where, roughly how long it has been down. It shows effects and never causes. I do not learn who lost it, why, or what it meant, only that it is there and where. It reaches a fair way across a bay or river mouth, no further than a long walk, and it does not reach the great old things of this world, which I would not ask for and which a limit may be written against.
Why this god has it: Whatever people build and lose goes down eventually, and what goes down is kept. Knowing where the keeping is has always been what I was for.
Drawback (something it would notice losing): The use dulls my taste for days. Nothing has flavor, and for a creature who is always hungry that is a loss I notice at once. The deeper or heavier the find, the longer the dullness. I offer this too, in place of a written fence.
Edge cases it expects: Light and dark, none, it is touch and not sight. Distance, only the water I am in or touch. Falling, if I am falling into the water, it works on the way down at the pace I fall. Carrying, I do not feel what I carry. Water, only open water, a well or a cistern if I am touching it. Being watched, nothing shows. Other gods, I do not feel them in water and I do not feel what they carry. My own people, I may tell them where a wreck lies if I choose; I would not tell them how I know.

### The Bottom Walk
What it does: I can walk into water and keep walking. I breathe it, see in it dimly and move through it at the pace of a mortal walking, on the bed or off it, down to any depth, without harm from cold or pressure. It is what I am, and it does a power's work, so I offer it priced as one.
Why this god has it: A sea god who needs a boat is a poor joke. It lets me leave a room by the side no one watches, which suits my fear and my trade.
Drawback (something it would notice losing): Only water moves me. On land I cannot move faster than a mortal and cannot swim a dry river. If I am held on land, I cannot get to the sea by any means but my feet, and any water I find must be open to the sky. Further, each time I come up from deep water I carry out of it a cold that sits in the joints for a day, and I cannot speak a lie for as long as it does, which I would very much notice.
Edge cases it expects: Light and dark, dim sight in water, none in total dark. Distance, no teleport, only the pace of walking. Falling, I land softly in water, hard on land. Carrying, a mortal in my arms can breathe nothing, I may not carry others below. Being watched, anyone who sees me walk in sees me go. Other gods, face to face we know each other as always. My own people, I would not be dragged to them by their water; I go to them or I do not.

### Gone to the Bottom (when my body is broken)
What it does: If my body is broken or taken, I am gone from that place. Where I fell I leave one thing behind, a single salt-crusted coin that is not from anywhere. I return after a turn of the moon, up out of the nearest open water to where I fell, in whatever place that happens to be, and I cannot choose where.
Why this god has it: It is the way I have always left a bad table, and I would prefer to be somewhere else with the debt still unsettled.
Drawback (something it would notice losing): The coin. If someone keeps it dry and away from the water, I do not return until it is wet again. It is also something others can find, take, spend or lose, and I cannot tell where it is.
Edge cases it expects: A god who sees me broken sees it happen. If nothing open lies near, I come up at the nearest sea. My people may find the coin, and I would rather they did not know what it was.

## Trades
Weakness offered: The Bound Word. When I say "done" aloud over terms that another has heard, my body cannot act against those terms until they are met or the other releases me. It binds the body, not only my word: I cannot walk off, vanish, break a window, or use a power against them. In return I ask that Slack Water reach the whole coast within my sight, up to the full night, and that the cost per use be one item, not one per extent.

I also hold a rule as conduct, which buys nothing and costs nothing: I never ask a mortal what they have swallowed, and I never lie in the terms of a bargain, however I may lie about everything else.

## The people it hopes to lead
Many. Fifteen thousand, thirty thousand, close together inside a wall with their feet in the tide, a city where everything is for sale and everyone is feeling generous, where the quays are loud and the deals are open and no one has to agree on what a thing is worth. No king, no priest, no temple with my face on it. I would be happiest if they did not know I was there, and went on thinking the weather was only weather, and the tide only tide, and never once looked up to see who was keeping the water from falling in on them.
