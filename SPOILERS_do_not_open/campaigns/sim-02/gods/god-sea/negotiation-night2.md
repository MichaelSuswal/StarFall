# Negotiation: god-sea, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

I read the draft the way I read any bill: line by line, with a thumb on the total. Most of it is fair. Two lines I contest, and one I would like repriced in my favor.

### Form, and the things carried
Accept as drafted. The coin coming from the purse is tidy, and I am glad touch is left unsettled. I note "god-sense as the world sets it" and ask nothing of it. One wording point, in the write-up and not a new power: the hem is wet always, so please write that it wets what it touches no more than any damp coat would. I do not want a god who leaves a trail.

### Slack Water
Choice: (b). The whole stretch of coast in my sight, one item for each night or part of a night held, bought by the Bound Word.

Reasons. The Keeper says I will use the coast seldom and the harbor often, and I agree. That is exactly why (b) costs the Keeper little and is worth much to me: a coast I almost never touch is a coast I can hold in reserve, and a storm that never has to be paid for by the bay is a storm I can afford to threaten. I reject (a) because it leaves me where I started, a harbor's luck and no more. I reject (c) because it charges by the bay, and a god who counts bays instead of nights is a god who stops using the thing. Under (b) I pay once for the night, which is how I would charge a customer.

Contest, on the drawback. The draft lets the Keeper take the item from my record of other gods or from my goals, the Keeper's choice. I will take the loss of a record of a god or a debt, a grievance, a guess. I will not offer my goals to the coin. Goals are what runs me in the time I do not act, and a god who wakes without them is a god who has lost the thread, not a god who has paid a small coin. That is a different, larger drawback and it would be priced differently. Counter: the item comes from my record of other gods, or from my ledger of what I was promised or owed, and not from my goals. If the Keeper holds that this is too narrow a set to draw on in the first year, when the record holds almost nothing, then I accept that in the first year a small item of my own choosing from what I carry in my head is removed instead, a taste or a name or a night's sleep, as I first offered, with the Keeper naming it. I would sooner pay in small coin than be fenced.

Everything else in the Limits block I accept. I especially accept that it moves salt water only. Fresh water scarce is the world I asked for.

### Keeping What Sinks
Accept as drafted. The dulled taste is a price I feel, since I am always hungry and food does nothing, and so I would notice losing taste more than most. The limit against the great old things, written as weight at a depth, is what I wanted: I do not ask what is down there, only that I am not made to carry the answer. One clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: the dullness is for days and it is the Keeper's to set per find, from a short stretch for a small thing to a long one for a ship, so that I may weigh a find before I make it.

### The Bottom Walk
Accept as drafted. The Keeper is right that "only water moves me" restates my form and buys nothing, and I withdraw it. The cold and the day of truth are a drawback I would notice. In the write-up and not a new power: I may stay silent while the cold lasts and I may say nothing at all, since I take the day to bind only a lie, and the Keeper should say whether a half truth that is not strictly false is allowed. I hope it is, for it is my trade.

### When the body is broken
Accept as drafted. A turn of the moon is longer than I would like and shorter than I deserve. The coin, as a real exposure, is the part I would notice, which is the point. I ask nothing.

### Trades
I accept that the Bound Word is in my grain. I contest that it is of middling size.

My reasons. It is a weakness that binds the body, not only the word. It binds me at the moment I would most want to leave, which is exactly when my flaw, fear, is loudest, and exactly when my impulsive temper has just said "done" before the sum is finished. A god built to vanish from a bad table, which is what I am and what the broken-body coin tells you I am, who cannot walk off, vanish, break a window or use a power against a counterpart, has lost the thing most like what it is. The Keeper says I hold the key because the word is mine to say. That is true, and it is true of a vow in a story too, and the story is still about the vow. The Keeper also notes that a mortal who learns the word can hold me to a bad bargain, and a rival can bait me into terms. Those are two ways it is used against me, where the Keeper's own text names only one that depends on my choosing. I think a weakness with two edges against me, and one which a hungry impulsive creature will cut itself on in its first year, is larger than middling.

So I ask for one thing more, and I will take it as a counter. In the water I move at a swimmer's pace, about twice a walker's, still a mortal's pace and not a power's. It improves the Bottom Walk, not a new power, and it does not move me faster on land. If the Keeper holds that it is too much for a weakness it calls middling, I counter again and accept a lesser form: a swimmer's pace only on the bed and in the first fathoms, falling back to a walker's at depth. If the Keeper refuses both, I keep (b) and the Bound Word as drafted, and I do not hold it against the Keeper, since (b) alone is a good deal and I have been paid fairly for what I offered.

Conduct, which buys and costs nothing: accept as written. I do not ask a mortal what it has swallowed, and I do not lie in the terms of a bargain.

### Edge-case floor and the four tests
Accept as drafted. Slack Water over a coast is the one that could scale across a people in a year, and I agree its price is the night's coin. I add only that I would not raise a surge over a coast with a fleet in it, as a matter of my own conduct and not of the rules. Fleets bring cargo, and cargo is what I trade. A warded hull unruled until it happens: accepted.

### Summary of my positions
- Form: accept, one wording note.
- Slack Water: (b). Contest the drawback's source, no goals. Counter as above.
- Keeping What Sinks: accept, one wording note.
- The Bottom Walk: accept, one wording note.
- Broken body: accept.
- Trades: Bound Word kept, size contested, counter for swimmer's pace in water, with a lesser fallback.
- Conduct: accept.
- Edge floor: accept.
