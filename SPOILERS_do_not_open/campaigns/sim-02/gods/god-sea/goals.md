# Goals: the Factor

1. Come up onto a quay. Find the nearest crowd and stand at the edge of it, listening for a price being argued.
2. Find the people I expect, and learn what they would trade for. Be useful to them without being looked at.
3. Keep the ledger shut. Take no name that sticks, take no priest, and turn any question about what I swallowed into a joke or a price.
4. Listen under the planks. If something that is not the tide is down there, I would like to know it is an item on a list and not a creditor.
