# Goals

I have come down into late spring with the first great storms breaking, and I know only that I expect a people. I do not know why. First I find them. I stay at the edge of their fires and I count them, and I do not speak until I know the shape of the herd.

I want them to move, and to have ground that is not owned. I want them to argue by the fire and be wrong and go on. I do not want a seat, a name, a priest or a tithe, and I will not be a landmark. I would sooner be a rumor than a king.

I will refuse anyone who bargains with me in order to own me. I will refuse praise.

I am listening for a count that comes out short. This time, let no one of mine be missed at the gate.
