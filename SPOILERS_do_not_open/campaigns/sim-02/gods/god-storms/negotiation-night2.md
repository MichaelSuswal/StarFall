# Negotiation: god-storms, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

I take a fair price as it is offered, and argue only where a price cuts against what I am. Most of this I take. One answer I owe first, because the rest hangs on it.

### The tent question, in the write-up, not a new power
A tent of hide or felt is not a built shelter. A built shelter, as I mean it, is a fixed roof on fixed walls: timber, sod, thatch, stone, mud brick. A tent is struck and carried, and the wind moves through it and the sky is a hand's breadth above it. My people will have no fixed seat, and I will not have one for them. So I ask that the card say: "a built shelter is a roof on walls fixed to the ground; a tent of hide, felt or cloth, a windbreak, a lean-to, or a cave mouth open to the sky is not." I am saying this against my own interest. If a tent counted I would take the larger weakness and both improvements. But I do not sleep in my people's tents. I stand at the edge of their fire, and I would be lying to you and to myself if I claimed to pay every winter night for something I would not do anyway. A weakness I could pay in name only is a bad bargain for both of us. So the weakness is middling and buys one improvement.

### Form, and the things carried
Accept as written, including the added lines. The count to one hundred and the unwatched change were mine and I keep them. The crook, the cord and the flask are ordinary, and the cord is also what I leave behind, as drafted.

### Foreknowing of the Sky
Accept the description, drawback and limits. Choose (b), bought by my weakness: the sky as seen from the highest ground within a day's walk, climbed or not, with the ache lasting a day past the weather's passing. Reasons: a people that moves needs to know a storm's road before the herds are on the open slope, and a storm seen only from the low ground is seen late. I would sooner pay in the head than in my people's lost animals. The ache is the coin this power already pays in, and the Keeper has priced it in that coin. I take it.

### The Small Turn
Accept as written. The returning weather is a debt I would notice, and I want it enacted in the world and not softened. I ask no change.

### A Tall Tree Among Many
Choose (a), as first written. A searcher with intent gets a doubt, and a long or close search wears through it. Reasons: (b) is bought by deepening the drawback so that my own people lose my face within a season even while walking beside me daily. That cuts against what I am. I hide from my people to keep them from bargaining with me and from owning me, not to be a stranger to the ones I count, and I already pay by being forgotten across a season away. I will not pay more, with the weakness's one improvement, to be harder to find. The Keeper said plainly that it would not write (b) clean, and I agree it should not.

Wording only, in the write-up, not a new power: please state that "a long or close search" means a search that goes on for days or looks at me from within speaking distance, so that both sides can tell when the doubt has worn through.

### When the body is broken
Accept as written, including the dead cord afterward, the wait on weather, the lost memory if the cord is lost or burned (the Keeper removes the most recent item from my goals, and I will not know which), and the roof clause. I do not ask for a quicker return. The wait is part of what it is to be a storm.

### Trades
Accept the weakness as I offered it and the Keeper's reading of how it can be used against me. I know that anyone who keeps me under a roof for two nights has a god with no powers and a fevered mind, and I accept that people may use it, and I will not hide it from myself. The conduct rule is accepted as conduct, buying nothing and costing nothing.

### Edge-case floor and the four tests
Accept all four, and accept the Keeper's note that the Tall Tree is a power over minds at a people's scale, priced by its drawback. I add nothing.

### Summary of choices
Tent: does not count. Weakness: middling, buys one improvement. Improvement chosen: Foreknowing (b). Tall Tree: (a). All else accepted as drafted.

## Round 2 reply

I confirm the final text as written. Form, definitions, things carried, the three powers, the weakness and the Keeper trait are what I meant, and I ask for no change to Description or Drawback in any power. Each choice recorded is the one I made: the tent is not a built shelter, the weakness is middling and bought Foreknowing's reach, and the Tall Tree is as first written.

One reading, stated so that both sides hold the same one, and not a request to change a word. In Foreknowing and the Small Turn, "under a roof" and "standing in the open" mean that the reading and the turning need open sky above me, so a tent over my head stops the working for as long as I am under it. That is separate from the weakness, which binds me only under a built shelter or underground for longer than one night. A night in a tent costs me the working while I am in it and costs me nothing after. I accept that cost, and I mean to stand outside the tent in any case.

The Keeper trait I take as written. It fits what I am: the people I have counted, I learn by name, and the rest I do not.

The card is frozen as written.
