# Power card: the Hunt-Warden (god-beasts)

Campaign: sim-02. Written on creation Night 2, 2026-11-19, from the final text of round 2 as the god confirmed it, with the wording corrections of its round 2 reply. Description and Drawback text below is frozen. Limits settled at creation were settled at the Night 2 table; the arguments are in the sealed ledger.

## Form

Filed under the Hunt-Warden; it holds no name. Those who do not know it call it the Long Hunter, or the Tall One at the herd's edge.

A giant, about twelve feet tall, built to carry: shoulders like a yoke, long arms, a deep chest, narrow hips, legs thick as young oaks. Skin the grey-brown of weathered hide, creased at knuckles and elbows, smooth over the face. The face is round, broad in the cheek, small in the mouth, with a short flat nose and no beard; it looks young and the eyes are not: pale as river ice, set wide, slow to blink. Hair cropped close, the color of dry grass. Large hands with blunt fingers, careful with small things. It walks barefoot on a long stride with little noise for its size, stoops under boughs, sits on the ground by choice, stands still very well, and carries its weight low and its head level.

Bodies: one. A god in this world has one body and is in one place.

What the form can and cannot do: a giant's reach and strength, and no more than a very large mortal has. Mortals in numbers are a danger to it.

Senses: sight sharp at distance, best in open country; hearing keen enough to pick one hoof out of a hundred; smell good, a hound's and no better. Touch, taste and any sense not named are unsettled and asked in play, never inferred.

Pace: mortal pace, on a long stride, by the pace table.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: a god knows another god only face to face, in person, within sight and speaking distance, each seen by the other; the knowing is mutual, immediate and sure, and tells only that the other is a god. At a distance there is nothing to feel. Every god knows from landing only that others like it came down when it did.

When the body is broken. If the body is broken past mending, the god is gone from that place. Its staff stays behind, driven upright into the ground to its own height where the god fell. The god returns at the next autumn, or after a full turn of the moon, whichever is later, at the staff, weak for a day. If the staff has been moved or broken, the god returns instead at the nearest river crossing to where the staff stood, with no staff in hand and not sure of the place. Another god's hand on the staff, or on the return, is unruled until it happens. The god's own people may find the staff and know it is to come.

### Definitions

- A beast: an animal that does not think in words. Not a mortal, not a god.
- A herd or pack: wild beasts of one kind moving together under one leader.
- A season: one of the year's four.

### Things carried

- A long staff of grey thorn-wood, taller than the god, scarred along its length. A staff.
- A cloak of many pieced hides, belted at the waist. It keeps off rain and no more.
- A curved horn cut from a great beast's horn, hung at the hip on a cord. It is loud and does nothing but sound.

## Powers

### The Study of Beasts
Status: main
Description: Any one beast, or the leader of one herd or pack, that the god can see and that can hear its voice, the god understands: what it wants now and what it fears, plainly, as one reads a face. The god can say simple things back (food, danger, this way, stay) and the beast takes them as a fair word from a stranger it has no reason to hate. It is not bound to obey. It works on one beast at a time, never a whole herd at once, and not on mortals, gods or anything that thinks in words.
Drawback: While the god is in the Study it hears nothing but that beast; anyone or anything else may come up on it unknown. It comes out over the length of a breath, half-blind and a little sick for that breath.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The beast does not flee the god while the god speaks to it, and may still flee if it is hurt, hunted by another, or the god falls silent. It is a reading and a fair word, never a hold. The beast does not know it is being read.
  - Light and dark: works in darkness, by sound and scent.
  - Distance: as far as the god's eyes reach the beast and its voice reaches it, no farther.
  - Water: works through water if the beast can hear.
  - Falling: not used while falling.
  - Carrying: works while carrying a beast.
  - Being watched: a watcher sees the god still and listening.
  - Other gods: a beast of another god's keeping or making is unruled until it happens.
  - The god's own people: they are not beasts; it does nothing on them.
  - Rules no mind: it reads and speaks, and never binds.
Rulings:

Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Turning of the Herd
Status: main
Description: Once in a season, the god may turn one herd or pack of wild beasts it can see and send it toward a place it names within a day's walk, for one day. The beasts go because the god has stood in their way and called, and the path it shows looks to them like the best path; they are not bound and not made to love it. At the day's end they go where they would have gone.
Drawback: For as many days as the herd was turned, the god's pace is halved.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The god's first wording of the drawback, kept as intent: "For the same number of days I turned the herd, and for no fewer than three, my pace is halved." The floor of three was traded away for the weakness (Trades).
  - The slowing begins at the day's end, when the turned herd is released, and runs for as many days as the herd was turned. Settled on the god's request that the card say where the count starts.
  - Light and dark: in darkness the beasts follow the sound of the horn; that is the power at work, and the horn is only loud.
  - Distance: only what the god can see.
  - Water: beasts that will swim, swim.
  - Falling: no.
  - Carrying: the horn and staff may be carried.
  - Being watched: a watcher sees the god stand in the beasts' path and call.
  - Other gods: a herd of another god's making or keeping is unruled until it happens.
  - The god's own people: may be told where the herd will go, and may hunt it.
  - It touches hunger, a thing this world's peoples fear; its frequency is its price, and it stays once in a season.
Rulings:
  - (none yet)
Trades: the slowing with no floor was bought by the weakness (Weaknesses taken). Recorded both ways.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Tally of Ground
Status: main
Description: When the god kneels and lays its palm on ground, it knows how many beasts of one kind passed over that ground, which way they went, and how long ago, back a few seasons. It knows nothing of why. It does not see the beasts; it knows their number and their going.
Drawback: Each use, the god tastes the last breath of the nearest dying beast of that kind, a flash of its fear that is not its own, and carries it for a day. It does not choose which.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Ground only, not stone or standing water.
  - Light and dark: works in darkness.
  - Distance: none; the hand must be on the earth.
  - Nothing older than a few seasons of passing, and nothing of the great old bones or any ground older than that. The god wrote this limit itself: "I do not ask to read what is older."
  - It counts beasts and never mortals.
  - Falling: no. Carrying: works. Being watched: a watcher sees the god kneel.
  - Other gods: the tracks of a god are unruled until it happens.
  - It shows a count, a direction and an age, never a cause.
Rulings:
  - (none yet)
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The true mouth
What it is: the god cannot say a false thing. When it tries, its mouth will not make the shape. It binds the body, not only the god's word, and holds toward gods, mortals and beasts, in every tongue. It may be silent.
How it can be used against the god: anyone who learns it can get the truth by asking, or know from its silence that there is something it will not say. It can never bluff a rival, hide its people's numbers by a lie, tell a kind lie to the dying, or promise what it does not mean.
What it bought: the Turning of the Herd's slowing with no floor of three days.
