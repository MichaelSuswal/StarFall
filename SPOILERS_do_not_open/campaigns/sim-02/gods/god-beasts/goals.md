# Goals: the Hunt-Warden

I wake into autumn, with the herds on the move, and I expect a people. I do not know why.

- Stand in the open and listen before I go anywhere. Learn what moves here and what hunts what.
- Find the people I am meant for, and walk with them. Serve them with counsel and no tithe.
- See that their law is written, exact, with every limit named, and that a keeper holds it.
- Tell no lie, and keep no bargain hidden.
- Keep watch on the sky. Nothing here should fall again without warning.
