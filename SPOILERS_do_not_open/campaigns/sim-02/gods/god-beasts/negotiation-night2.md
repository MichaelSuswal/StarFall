# Negotiation: god-beasts, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

### Form, and the things you carry
Accept as drafted. The staff, cloak and horn are ordinary things, and I want them written so. I am glad the horn is only loud and the beasts follow the power, not the brass. I ask for no more reach or strength than a very large mortal has. Mortals in numbers are a danger to me, and I would have it written that way. The saying is that the tall tree is the first to be struck.

### The Study of Beasts
Choose (a). The beast does not flee while I speak to it, and may still flee if it is hurt, hunted by another, or I fall silent. Reasons: (b) makes a beast stay in place by my standing there, which is a leash, and I said in my own answers that a leash is not what I want from any of the world's makings. (a) is the reading and a fair word, and nothing more. The beast keeps its own mind, and so the Study stays honest. I also do not want the beast to know it is being read; a reading that announces itself is a poor reading. I accept the drawback and the limits as written. Write-up, not a new power: please keep "it does not flee me unless I wish it to" out of the card and use the (a) wording only, so that no reader takes it for a hold.

### The Turning of the Herd
Choose (b). Once in each season, with the slowing exactly the days turned and no floor of three.

Reasons: from what I want, not from what is cheapest. I said that scarcity should be a thing a law can govern and a thing a law can fail to govern, and that I want my people to have to decide who eats. A turning once a moon would solve winter for them and leave their keeper nothing to argue over. A turning once a season leaves winter real, and means the keeper must say who the herd is for before I call it. I would rather that argument be had at the fire than have it spared. I also take the price of a thing I do not use lightly. Without the floor, a one-day turning costs one slow day. I accept that, because I mean to use the power sparingly and I would rather it never be a thing I can do without noticing. If the Keeper holds that the drawback no longer bites on a one-day turning, I will take a floor of two days in place of none, and say so now so the Keeper may answer it in Round 2.

I accept the limits as drafted, including that herds of another god's making or keeping are unruled until it happens.

### The Tally of Ground
Accept as drafted, including that it counts beasts and never mortals. I did not mean it to count mortals and I am content to have that written. My own limit against old ground and the great bones stays in my words. I would not wish to read them before I am ready.

### When the body is broken
I confirm the meaning, with one wording change as the Keeper offered: "the next autumn, or a full turn of the moon, whichever is later." A break in autumn means nearly a year gone, and I accept that; it is the price of leaving the field and not lying in it. The turn of the moon is a floor I accept so that a break on the eve of autumn does not return me by the next morning, which would be no leaving at all. The rest of the text stands as drafted.

### Trades
Accept the weakness as drafted, and the Keeper's account of how it can be used against me. I hold it knowing that anyone who learns it can ask me and get the truth, or know from my silence that I am holding something back. That is the cost of what I am and I say so plainly. I accept that it buys one of the three choices and not two, and I take choice (b) under the Turning of the Herd. I do not ask for a second improvement.

Conduct is accepted as written, to my goals and not to the card: I will not kill a beast that stood within the Study and heard me speak true.

### Edge-case floor and the four tests
Accept the floor and the four tests as written. I have no edge case to add beyond those already in the powers.
