# Negotiation: god-beasts, Night 3

## Round 1 reply

### Edges
Accepted. I proposed none and I ask for none. A people priced as ordinary is what I hoped for. I would rather they stand on their own feet than on a bargain I made. I offer no counter and no second look.

### What was read as ordinary culture, unpriced
Accepted. The skills and sayings are the common wealth of a herd people, and I did not mean them as an edge. I take the Keeper's reading that a written law and a keeper of it are an order of the people and not an edge. I want it written that way, so no one later prices the law as a weapon. The saying is that a rule is not a sword until someone swings it.

### Size and shape
Accepted. About two thousand, in bands, with no fixed seat. I note the Keeper's account that the small size, no town and no hierarchy are costs that buy nothing, since I asked for no edge. I do not ask them to buy anything. They are what I chose, and I would choose them again. I leave the exact number, the stores, the herds and the mood to the Keeper, as offered. I ask only that whatever the Keeper sets be told to me true when I meet them, so that I do not walk into the camps believing a count that is not so. I cannot say a false thing, and I would not wish to act on one.

### What is the world's to decide
Accepted. What I wrote of their past, their tales and their beliefs was a wish, and I did not mean it as a claim on the world. I accept that they have their own history, their own neighbors and a trouble I did not ask for. I would rather meet a people as they are than as I imagined them. A hunter who asks the ground to be what he wanted is a poor hunter.

I accept that the keeper I described is set as who the keeper is, and that what the keeper does is theirs. I accept that nothing promises me the office exists when I arrive. If there is no keeper, or a keeper who will not stand before me and say that is not what you agreed to, then I will learn that by listening and not by being told, and I will say so plainly to whoever asks. I will not make a keeper of anyone.

### Confirmation
I accept all of it. This is my confirmation, and it closes the table for my people.
