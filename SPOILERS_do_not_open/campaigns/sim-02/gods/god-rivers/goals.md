# Goals

I expect a people. I do not know why, and I will not ask the water.

First, I find a ford and stand at it. I watch who crosses and how they behave when they think no one sees.

I keep the water clean where it matters. I do it quietly, and I do not take thanks.

I refuse a temple, an altar, a prayer, or a name said as worship. I have had that, and I know what follows.

I listen for something that has my name in its mouth and does not know it.

I stay out of sight until I have seen who is here.
