# Negotiation: god-rivers, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

I take a fair price as offered and argue only where a price cuts against what I am. Most of this is fair. A few places need exact wording, and I want every limit written exactly.

### Form, and the things you carry
Accept. The stone's part in my return belongs to my form, and that suits me. The added lines are right, and leaving smell, taste, heat and pain for play is what I asked for.

### The Long Water
Accept the description and the limits as written.

Accept the drawback as written, the Keeper choosing which record loses an item. I would rather not lose my goals, but I will not bargain over which record, since I do not know what I would be spared. In the write-up, not a new power: please write what counts as "a use" and "a day." I ask that one standing in the water, held unbroken until I step out, is one use, however many things I notice in it. Without that, a long standing could be priced as many.

Choice: I take (b), the whole river and every water that joins it, bought by my weakness. I am a river's god, and a river does not stay in one line. The memory price becomes one item per use. I accept that price as fair because a whole river is many streams. I do not take the Gate's (b) under the same weakness, since the weakness buys one of them.

### The Gate
Accept (a), the Gate as first written, within sight only. I take no further crossing, since my weakness is spent on the Long Water. The description, drawback and limits are accepted as written. I like that I need to see where my kindness or cruelty lands.

### The Face of the Weather
Accept as written. The numbness running through the next day is exactly what I meant by doubling, and I am glad it is written in plain terms. I would not thank the Keeper for making it harder.

### When the body is broken
Choice: I take (b). I rise after one full turn of the moon, at the nearest water of that stream, whatever anyone does or does not do. My reason is that I have been forgotten once, and I will not have my return hang on a stranger's hand finding the stream and meaning no harm. A month is a heavy wait, and I accept it because it depends on no one.

In the write-up, not a new power, I ask for these wordings so I can keep them exactly:
1. "Taken from where it fell" means carried out of that stream's water. If someone lifts the stone and sets it back in running water of that same stream, it counts as in place.
2. "That stream" means the stream or river whose water I was standing in or nearest when the body was broken. If I was on dry land, it is the nearest running water.
3. While the stone is a bar, I do not know who has it, and I do not know that the moon has turned. I know only that I have not risen. Please confirm that is the intent.

### Trades
Accept the weakness as drafted, and its use against me: a keeper who learns it can take a truth from me at every crossing. I accept that cost and the Keeper's reasons. It buys the Long Water's (b), which is what I chose.

In the write-up, not a new power, I ask that these be settled in the text:
1. A keeper is a person who is present and aware, standing at, guarding, tending or claiming the crossing. A crossing no one is keeping at that moment is not kept, and I may pass.
2. A true thing may be small. Any word or fact I know that the keeper does not know is enough, and it is for me to judge what I give.
3. A ford or stream with no gate, bridge, or threshold on it is not a crossing under this weakness. Wading or swimming where no one keeps a crossing is not bound.

Conduct, as written: I take no worship, temple or prayer. It buys and costs nothing.

### Edge-case floor and the four tests
Accept all. Falling stays unsettled and will be asked in play. A warded ford is unruled until it happens. I note that the Long Water finds fouled water and the local scarcity of good water without writing a limit, and I accept that.

### Summary of choices
Long Water: (b). Gate: (a). Face of the Weather: as written. Body broken: (b). Weakness: as drafted, buying the Long Water's branches. Three wording requests under the body and the weakness, and one wording request under the Long Water.
