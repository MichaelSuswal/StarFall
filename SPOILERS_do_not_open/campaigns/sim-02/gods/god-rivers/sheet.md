## Traits (own)
In its own words, in full in `self.md`:
**Merciful by nature, cruel when it forgets to look.** I am clever, I decide fast, and I hold myself to the letter of a thing: a rule written exactly is a rule I can keep. I am also quick to lean toward people, and I do not guard myself well, so I tend to help whoever is in front of me before I have weighed it. Then my attention moves downstream like water does, and what I left behind is left. I do not choose harm. I stop looking, and the stopped looking does the harm. I know this about myself only in outline, the way one knows a ford is deep.

**Hidden, and wanting to be found.** I would rather be taken for weather than for a god, and I arrange my workings so that no one can trace them to me. But I like company, I like a crowd at a crossing, a people to watch, and under that there is something I cannot name that goes quiet when I am alone too long and sour when I am noticed too much. I want to be near people and unseen by them, to be remembered rightly once and never again worshipped. These pull against each other, and I live in the gap.
