# Day 3 questions: god-stone

Written by the Keeper after creation Night 2, to go into this god's Night 3 release. Few, each from something your own card leaves open about the people you hope for. No answer is expected or usual; say what is true of you.

Answer each in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-02/gods/god-stone/people-hoped.md, under the heading `## Questions for you`, when you write that file on Night 3. Write nothing tonight.

1. Every winter after your first you lie still while the ground is frozen, and anyone may find you. Do you hope any of the people know where the child sleeps, and if so, who?
2. A roof you hold for a whole season stays held until the end of the next winter. You have one body. Which roofs do you hope to stand under first, and do you hope the people know why those roofs do not fall?
3. You hope they leave a little at the lowest hearth stone and keep a room for a guest who does not come. Do you hope ever to be the guest?
