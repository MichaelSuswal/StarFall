# Day 3 questions: god-beasts

Written by the Keeper after creation Night 2, to go into this god's Night 3 release. Few, each from something your own card leaves open about the people you hope for. No answer is expected or usual; say what is true of you.

Answer each in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-02/gods/god-beasts/people-hoped.md, under the heading `## Questions for you`, when you write that file on Night 3. Write nothing tonight.

1. You cannot say a false thing. Do you mean the people you hope for to know this of you? If the keeper of their law learns it, what do you hope that keeper does with a god whose every word is true?
2. You may turn one herd in a season. In a lean season with more than one hungry camp, who do you hope decides which camp the herd is for: you, their law, their keeper?
3. You hope for a people with no fixed seat and you walk at a mortal's pace, slowed after a turning. Do you hope they wait for you, or go on and let you follow?
