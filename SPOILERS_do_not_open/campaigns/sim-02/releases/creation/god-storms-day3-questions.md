# Day 3 questions: god-storms

Written by the Keeper after creation Night 2, to go into this god's Night 3 release. Few, each from something your own card leaves open about the people you hope for. No answer is expected or usual; say what is true of you.

Answer each in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-02/gods/god-storms/people-hoped.md, under the heading `## Questions for you`, when you write that file on Night 3. Write nothing tonight.

1. You will take no prayer and you leave a people that begins to pray. Your face goes from them in a season. How do you hope to come among them the first time, and as which of your two shapes?
2. You hope they do not know you are there, and you hope to count them. Do you hope any one of them knows what the old herder is?
3. You are bound to the open sky, and they will have winter quarters of some kind. Where do you hope to be when they are under their roofs?
