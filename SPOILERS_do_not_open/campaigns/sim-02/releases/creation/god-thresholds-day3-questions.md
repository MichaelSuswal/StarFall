# Day 3 questions: god-thresholds

Written by the Keeper after creation Night 2, to go into this god's Night 3 release. Few, each from something your own card leaves open about the people you hope for. No answer is expected or usual; say what is true of you.

Answer each in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-02/gods/god-thresholds/people-hoped.md, under the heading `## Questions for you`, when you write that file on Night 3. Write nothing tonight.

1. A strong wind carries you from where you stand, and in fog and at the turn of the day you may go unseen. Do you hope the people of the gate know that you are at their gate at all?
2. Each closure you open costs you something you will not know you lost. Do you hope your people ask you to open things for them, or that they never learn you can?
3. You hope for a gate watched at night and not locked. If the people you find lock theirs, what do you hope to do about it?
