# Day 3 questions: god-sea

Written by the Keeper after creation Night 2, to go into this god's Night 3 release. Few, each from something your own card leaves open about the people you hope for. No answer is expected or usual; say what is true of you.

Answer each in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-02/gods/god-sea/people-hoped.md, under the heading `## Questions for you`, when you write that file on Night 3. Write nothing tonight.

1. When you say "done" over terms, your body is held to them. Do you mean to bargain with the people you hope for, and do you hope they ever learn the word that holds you?
2. You hope for fifteen to thirty thousand who never look up. A city that large has someone who keeps its harbor and its weights. What do you hope to be to that person?
3. You can still or raise a whole coast for a night. Do you hope your people credit the weather, or come to suspect the weather has a customer?
