# Event schedule: sim-02

Written on creation Night 3b. Years are campaign years; Year 1 opens on the night of the arrival, in late autumn. Clocked events are natural and no one controls them. Triggered events fire on what gods and peoples do, and say which clocked rows they speed, delay or turn. Every mystery's cost if ignored is a row here, with the mystery named. Signs are in the world from the lead time given; the DM never points at them, and they reach a god only through who noticed or through its own senses. Random events are weighted down in Years 1 to 7. Nothing here draws the gods toward one place, and no path exists for the plot's sake: every road, pass and door below is in `map.json` and was in the world before the gods came.

## 3. Random events

Each year, in pass one, the Keeper rolls once for each of the thirteen peoples with `tools/roll.py --campaign sim-02`, contest mode, to see whether a random event falls on it. Base 30. Factors: Years 1 to 7, minus 15 (the quiet opening; the distance between the gods does its work); a people in open quarrel with a neighbor, plus 10; a hard winter or dry summer on the clocked rows that year, plus 10; a people of more than 10,000, plus 10; a people shut in its valley or its heights, minus 10. Clamp 5 to 95. If it falls, one `--die d20` roll picks the kind:

| d20 | Kind |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | a sickness, by ship or road, never by fog; it enters at a port or a gate |
| 3 to 4 | a murrain in the stock |
| 5 | a fire in a town or a camp |
| 6 to 7 | a bad harvest or a failed pasture from local weather |
| 8 | a wolf winter |
| 9 to 10 | a good year: a heavy harvest or a strong calving |
| 11 | a feud that comes to blood |
| 12 | a death out of season of someone the people lean on |
| 13 | a birth, a marriage or a quarrel mended that people will date things by |
| 14 | a stranger or a small band asking to stay |
| 15 | a find: a hoard, an old cellar, a thing ploughed up |
| 16 | a small flood, slide or blow-down |
| 17 | a trader's windfall or a trader's ruin |
| 18 | a wonder with no meaning: a white stag, a fall of fish, a great wheel of birds |
| 19 | a false alarm that costs a season's nerves |
| 20 | two at once: roll twice more, ignoring 20 |

The outcome's size is set by the people's own state and a further contested roll with its factors logged. No random event is a clue, and none delivers one.

## 4. Recurring phenomena

True counts per year are in `state/phenomena.json`, for Years 1 to 12, in the shape `tools/phenomena.py` reads; the night turn runs `python3 tools/phenomena.py SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-02 <year>`. A count is released only when the god or its people would notice, and then exactly.

- dim-nights: nights on which the star dulls and the thread stands over the Sleeper [f27]. Seen from anywhere the star is in view as a dulling; the thread only from within sight of the Bare Floor [r14].
- floor-walkers: rim people who walk down onto the Bare Floor [r14] in the year.
- mild-ones: Hodd who come back mild from the hollow before the Hush [f16] in the winter.
- ridge-storms: great storms that break on the Rope Ridges [r20] in the spring.
- fair-salt-trains: salt carters' trains that reach the summer fair on the Wetfoot [f05].

## 5. Checked at writing

Every mystery's cost is a row: E01 (C01, C02, C03, T01, T02, T03), L01 (C04, T04, T13), L02 (C05), L03 (T05, T06), L04 (C08, T07, T09), L05 (T01, T02), S01 (C09), S02 (C06), S03 (C07, T08), S04 (C10, T10), S05 (C08), S06 (T09), S07 (C11, T11), S08 (C12), S09 (C13), S10 (T12, C14), S11 (C15). Every row has a cause in the deep history, the land's weather or a people's record. Random events are weighted down by 15 in Years 1 to 7. Recurring phenomena are in the record for twelve years. No row gathers the gods, and no road, pass or door was made for a plot.
