# Balance: campaign sim-02, creation Night 2

Written at step 2.7, after every card was confirmed and frozen and after the sealed powers were written. A weight from 1 to 10 of each god's total power as far as the Keeper can foresee: main powers, drawbacks, trades, weaknesses, the body broken, and the two sealed powers. Roughly equal, not exactly (DESIGN 3.10, 3.15). No card was edited to reach these weights. The band tonight is 4 to 6.

| God | Weight | Reasons |
|---|---|---|
| the player's god (Vesper) | 5 | The DM's lean note: at or a little below the middle at arrival, rising toward and perhaps above it as kept hearths grow. The Keeper agrees. Nothing reaches anywhere on the first day; with a network, sight and hearing at any distance and a step a day are the widest reach of any god here, bounded to firelight, with a learnable tell. No power harms, compels, hides or feeds. The vow is the heaviest weakness of the eight after the winter stillness. Absence is quick (next dusk) but spends a hearth and leaves the lantern in whoever's hand. Sealed powers: one that bars a single doorway at the cost of barring itself, one that is a bare number. Weighted at arrival 4, in ten years 6; written as 5. |
| god-beasts | 4 | Three modest powers in open country: one beast read at a time, one herd turned once a season, a tally of what crossed ground. A giant's body. It cannot lie. Its return is the slowest of the eight, up to a year. Sealed powers lift it a little (one person kept from beasts and cold while carried; a breath's warning). Its real strength will be a moving people fed in lean seasons, four times a year. |
| god-thresholds | 4 | Opens any mortal closure at the cost of a memory each time; reads the last crossings of a threshold; unseen in fog and at dusk and dawn, but then does nothing. A body any blow or fire hurts and any strong wind carries off. The quickest return, paid with a door that never locks again. Sealed powers: holding one door shut, knowing who is followed. Slight at arrival; dangerous to a walled town only once, and costly to itself. |
| god-rivers | 5 | Feels a whole river system as weight and movement at a memory per use; eases or hinders one crossing in sight; all of it untraceable. Must pay a truth at every kept crossing. A month's return, barred if its stone is taken. Sealed powers: hearing an offering and granting a crossing, at the cost of its hiding; a clean cupful. Strong as a watcher, weak as an actor. |
| god-secrets | 5 | Holds secrets it can never use or tell; forgotten by everyone, known by voice after three meetings; makes one hidden one-way passage a season. Cannot speak a falsehood; not strong. A season's return. Sealed powers: knowing a hidden door is near, bound to walk up to it; knowing who aches to tell. Its weight will come from a large people that leans on it without knowing. In a world of sealed doors its first sealed power matters, and it shows a door and never what it keeps. |
| god-storms | 5 | Three days of weather to the hour under a wide sky; small bends to weather already there, repaid harder elsewhere; forgettable in two shapes, one of them a horse's pace. Loses everything under a built roof for two nights. A return that waits on a storm. Sealed powers: whole or short at dusk; lightning turned a few paces. A moving people that never loses a herd to weather is a strong people. |
| god-sea | 6 | A whole coast of salt water stilled or raised for a night at one memory; knows what lies sunk; walks the sea floor, a little quicker than a walker in the shallows. The Bound Word holds its body to any bargain it seals. A month's return, barred while its coin is dry. Sealed powers: one small thing hidden past finding; a true scale. The highest of the eight, because its one large power scales to a fleet or a harbor and its people will be the largest. Noticeably but not far out of line; left as a fact the world's politics will deal with (DESIGN 3.15). |
| god-stone | 4 | Holds stone from falling while it stands in pain, and what it held a full season stays held to the end of the next winter; hears weight and movement a mile through rock; leaves no trace on stone. Still and helpless through every frozen season after the first; a child's body and pace; a return of one to three winters with a season forgotten. Its weakness is worth more than it bought, by its own choice. Sealed powers lift it a little (one sleeper kept alive through a night; a sound step). The lowest in reach, the highest in what it can keep standing. |

## What was done about the spread

- Before confirmation: nothing needed saying in a round 2 counter on balance grounds alone. The one power that scales (god-sea's coast) was priced in round 1 with three ways offered, and the god bought the widest with a weakness.
- After confirmation: no card edited. The sealed powers were written with the spread in view only in this sense: the three gods at 4 received sealed powers that keep or protect one life (the carried rider, the held door, the sleeper), which are somewhat more useful than the bare yes-or-no powers the others received. No sealed power reaches the size of any main power.
- god-sea stays noticeably the strongest and god-stone, god-beasts and god-thresholds the slightest. That is logged as a fact, not corrected.
- The player's card: not changed. No concern goes to the DM; `P/player/corrections.md` is not written tonight.
