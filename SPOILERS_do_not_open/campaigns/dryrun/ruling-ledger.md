r-001 | 2026-11-18 | Year 1 | Whether a petition from the god's own people, made in person in unplayed time, is a held encounter (encounters.md 6, "a pending question reaching the god in person") or a pending question (DESIGN 12.9). Cases: Wellbrook week 5 (a law of giving at the weir), Aldford week 9 (a plea to save a wife in labor), Fallstead week 22 (a mother asks an oath). | For holding: encounters.md lists a pending question reaching the god in person as major. Against: DESIGN 12.9 says open questions to the god are tracked as pending items, put to the player during the day, and if the world cannot wait the other side meets the god's silence; holding the year at every petition would stop play at week 5 of every year a god walks among its people; the god's standing conduct (observe only) already answers the moment. | Ruled: a new question put to the god by its own people in unplayed time, which standing conduct answers in the moment by silence and inaction, is a pending question for the DM to put to the player, not a held encounter. The world goes on as it would. A question that is already pending when its asker reaches the god in person, a stranger, another god, a threat, or a chance to use a power the god has, is still held. | Binds: all campaigns, all civ agents and the Keeper, until appealed with a better argument.
