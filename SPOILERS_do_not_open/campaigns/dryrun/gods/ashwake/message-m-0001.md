# Message m-0001

Year sent: 001
Sender: Ashwake, god of the Kiln
Recipient: the god I feel as the nearer pull, a little north of west
Language: god-tongue
Carrier: two walkers of the Kiln, the potter who banked her kiln on the first night and a charcoal-burner of the largest steading
Route: from the hill of vents west, following the nearer pull, through the pass people's country as they find it
Form: spoken, with a token

## Text
I am Ashwake. I hold the burning hills, east of you. I feel you and you feel me. I do not know your name. Tell it to my people.

I have a hearth that does not go out and a people who feed it. Come and see it. Walk east until the hills turn the color of ash and the ground breathes. My people will give you coals, and I will give you a place by my fire. I do not ask you to kneel. I ask you to come, so that we know each other before the others do.

## Carried with it
A bowl of live coals in a lidded clay pot, kept alive by the carriers, as proof of the hearth. Charcoal and fired pots as gifts for any who let them pass.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
Walk the nearer pull, no more than thirty days out. If you meet the pass people, give pots, say you carry a god's word, and do not fight. If you find the god, say the words, give the coals, and ask the god's name. Do not bind me to any promise. If the god asks questions, answer plainly about the Kiln and say nothing of my power. If you find no one by thirty days, or are turned back, return and say so.
