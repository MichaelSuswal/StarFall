# Record of the other gods

Built only from my own senses. Nothing here is knowledge unless marked as felt.

## Felt (certain)

- Two other gods exist. They came down when I did.
- Pull one: nearer, a little north of west.
- Pull two: farther, a little south of west, toward the direction where the land falls away to lower, wetter country.
- Neither pull carries words. Neither has walls or a hall yet, as I have none.

## Guesses (marked as guesses)

- GUESS: the nearer one is the one I will meet first, and the one most likely to be a rival for the passes.
- GUESS: the farther one sits in lower, wetter country, so its people probably live by water and not by fire. A god of a different kind from me.
- GUESS: "nearer" and "farther" may say nothing about strength. A small god can sit close.
- GUESS: both have found a people, as I have. I do not know if they were fed on the first night.
- GUESS: neither knows where I am any better than I know where they are. They feel me as a pull to the east.

## Trust, grievances, debts

None. I have met no one. I owe nothing and am owed nothing. I am wary of both until they show me their hands.

## To learn

Names. Peoples. Whether they will come to me or send a fire-fearing fool. Whether either has a power that can reach my hill.
