> SPOILER WARNING. NPC god sheet (dry run): ashwake
Name: Ashwake. Domain: fire. Form: a short broad man with coals for eyes, warm to the touch. Leans: Mind 30 (intelligent), Body 20 (strong), Presence 15 (revealed), Temper 85 (impulsive), Regard 80 (sovereign), Order 25 (law), Making 60, Company 90 (gathering), Appetite 85 (hungry), Trust 70 (wary). Virtue courage, flaw wrath, wound: once worshipped and then forgotten. Voice: blunt as a hammer, loud. Traits: The Hearth That Commands; Never Twice Forgotten. Keeper trait: Burns What He Loves.
Land: the burning hills, 40 days east of the Ald. People: the Kiln, about 5,000, charcoal-burners and potters under a hill of vents.
Power: may call fire from any ember, coal or hearth within sight and shape it for a breath. Drawback: fire he calls burns him too; he carries the scars and loses a day's strength per calling.
Goals Year 1: raise a great kiln and a hall; find who else has come and make them come to him.
