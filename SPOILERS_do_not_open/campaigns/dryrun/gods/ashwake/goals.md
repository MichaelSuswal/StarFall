# Goals, start of Year 1

1. Raise a great kiln at the town around the largest vent, and a hall beside it. The kiln first, because it is the Kiln's pride and my seat. The hall needs timber, and timber is the thin thing here.
2. Find who else has come, and make them come to me. I do not go to them. I send word, I let them see the fire, and they walk to my hearth. Two others are out west. I want to know their faces before they know my strength.
3. Never be forgotten again. Feed the Kiln's worship now, while it is a bowl of coals, and make it a habit that does not lapse.

## Changed by what I saw at landing

- The potter who banked her kiln and left coals unasked: these people already give. I will not waste that. Keep the bowl lit.
- Timber is bought from the northern people, whom the Kiln dislike. That is a weakness, a leash held by someone else. I want my own wood or a trade that does not shame us.
- The pass people hold the western passes. Whoever goes west goes through them. I am wary of them and will not insult them yet.
- No map. I know only two pulls and what I saw from the hill of vents. Everything west is a guess until my people walk it.
