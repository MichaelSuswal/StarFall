# Message m-0002

Year sent: 001
Sender: Ashwake, god of the Kiln
Recipient: the god I feel as the farther pull, a little south of west, toward the lower wetter country
Language: god-tongue
Carrier: two walkers of the Kiln, a clay-digger and a young charcoal-burner of the town
Route: from the hill of vents west, following the farther pull, down toward lower ground as the land falls
Form: spoken, with a token

## Text
I am Ashwake. I hold the burning hills, far east of you. I feel you and you feel me. I do not know your name. Tell it to my people.

I have a hearth that does not go out and a people who feed it. Come and see it. My people will give you coals, and I will give you a place by my fire. I do not ask you to kneel. I ask you to come, so that we know each other.

## Carried with it
A bowl of live coals in a lidded clay pot. Charcoal and fired pots as gifts for any who let them pass.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
Walk the farther pull, no more than thirty days out, then turn back. If you meet the pass people, give pots and do not fight. If you find the god, say the words, give the coals, and ask its name. Promise nothing. If asked, answer plainly about the Kiln and say nothing of my power. If the ground turns to marsh and you have no guide, stop and return.
