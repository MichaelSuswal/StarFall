# Decisions, Year 1

1. **Raise the great kiln in the town around the largest vent.** The Kiln build it of clay and fitted stone from the clay pits below. Reason: it is my hearth and their pride, and it needs no timber. I work it myself, loud, so they see me labor.

2. **Buy timber for the hall, and begin the hall only when it is seasoned.** Send a trading party north with charcoal and fired pots to buy beams from the northern people. The Kiln dislike them, so the party goes in force and keeps the peace. Reason: a hall of about sixty feet is 300 worker-days and needs timber felled a season ahead. Cut and stack this year, raise next year.

3. **Send two parties west toward the two pulls.** Each is two walkers, laden, walking at group pace, carrying a bowl of coals in a lidded pot and gifts of pots and charcoal. Party one follows the nearer pull, a little north of west, up to thirty days out. Party two follows the farther pull, a little south of west, toward lower country, up to thirty days out. They carry the words in message-m-0001.md and message-m-0002.md. They may not reach the gods this year. If they meet the pass people they pay in pots and do not fight. They then return and report.

4. **Set a watch on the western hills.** Six of the Kiln's keenest men keep a fire on the western rim of my land, in sight of the hill of vents. Reason: I am wary. Whoever comes from the passes will be seen before they are heard, and I want to know before they stand at my hearth.

5. **Keep the hearth bowl lit and make it custom.** Every steading banks a coal at dusk and brings one to the hearth at the town at each new moon. I will sit at it and speak. Reason: I was forgotten once. This is the habit that stops it happening again.

6. **No power this year.** I will not call fire unless I or the Kiln are threatened. If I must, I accept that it burns me, scars me and costs a day's strength.
