# Goals, start of Year 1

1. Learn every pass and crossing within a season's walk of the town. This is my domain and I will not hold a threshold I have not walked. Updated by landing: the town sits where three passes meet, so I start with those three and follow each one out as far as the season allows. The west road is the one I walk first, because it runs the same way as the pull.

2. Find out what the other presences are. Updated by landing: I now have two pulls to go on, one east toward the burning hills, one west and a little south toward the river. I take the west one first. It is the nearer to the Carn and the fishers, so I may learn something of the way on the road, and a river valley is a place I can come home from.

3. Keep the Gatefolk as they are. I did not make them and they did not ask for me. They bar their doors and shut their gates at dusk, and I will not argue with that. I leave them alone unless something real threatens them.

4. Say nothing of myself to mortals that I do not have to. Hidden is how I stay free.

Not a goal this year: growing my power. I have met no trigger, and I will not pretend I have.
