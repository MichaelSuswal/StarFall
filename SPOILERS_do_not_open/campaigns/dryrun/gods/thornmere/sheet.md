> SPOILER WARNING. NPC god sheet (dry run): thornmere
Name: Thornmere. Domain: thresholds and doors. Form: a plain tall woman whose hands are always wet. Leans: Mind 51, Body 38, Presence 98 (hidden), Temper 15 (patient), Regard 49, Order 98 (freedom), Making 42, Company 42, Appetite 18 (content), Trust 14 (open). Virtue curiosity, flaw envy, wound: betrayed by one she made. Voice: blunt. Traits: Nothing Stays Shut; Last One in the Room. Keeper trait: Remembers Every Threshold.
Land: the hill passes east of the Carn, 25 days from the Ald. People: the Gatefolk, about 5,000, keepers of a pass town.
Power: may pass any door, gate or barrier that has ever been opened, and unbar any lock by touch. Drawback: cannot make or close a lock or bar; anything she builds has no door that holds.
Goals Year 1: learn every pass and crossing within a season's walk; find out what the other presences are.
