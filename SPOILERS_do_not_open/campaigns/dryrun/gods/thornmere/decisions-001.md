# Decisions, Year 1

1. **Walk west and a little south, alone and unseen, toward the second pull.** I go on foot, at mortal walking pace with no sleep, which is up to 1.6 times a mortal's day, the Keeper to apply terrain and season. I take the Carn's hill country toward the river valley. I stop when the pull is strongest, when I reach the river, or when the year ends, whichever comes first. I do not turn back for being tired, because I am not. I go because I want to know what the other presences are, and this one is the nearer to the people I have heard of.

2. **Map the passes on the way.** At every notch, ford and fork I walk the crossing rather than pass it by, and I keep it in my head. This is goal one, and it costs me nothing on a road I am walking anyway. I carry nothing but what a hill traveler would, and I keep my wet hands in my sleeves.

3. **Keep out of sight of the Carn.** I sleep not, so I walk at night where the ground is safe and lie close by day where it is not. I will not speak with them or trade. They hold that country and the Gatefolk do not trust them, and I have no reason yet to give them a face.

4. **If I find the other god's people, I watch first.** I stay hidden, listen, and learn what they call their god and what they want. I do not name myself or the Gatefolk. If they find me, I say I am a traveler from the passes, which is true enough.

5. **If I find the god itself, I speak plainly.** I come open-handed, say who I am in one sentence, and ask what it is and what it wants of this country. I tell it nothing of my power. I make no oath and no bargain this year. If it is hostile I leave by the way I came. If it is welcoming I do not stay long, because I do not yet know what it will cost me.

6. **Leave the Gatefolk to themselves.** I give them no orders. They keep their three gates, shut them at dusk, keep their bars and take their toll as they always have. If anyone asks where I am, they may say I walk the passes. I send no one with me and no one ahead.

7. **The east pull waits.** I do not go toward the burning hills this year. I want one road finished before I start another.

Power: I will not use it unless I am shut out of a place I need to pass. If I do, I take the drawback as it stands, that I cannot make or close a lock or bar, and I will not try to build any door on this road. No growth attempt.

No message sent this year.
