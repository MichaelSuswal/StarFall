# My record of the other gods

Built only from what I have felt myself. Nothing here is known. Guesses are marked.

## What I feel

- Two pulls, no words in either. Others like me exist and came down when I did.
- Pull one: east, toward the brown smudge on the far sky the Gatefolk call the burning hills.
- Pull two: west and a little south, where the land falls through hill country toward a river valley I have not seen. The Gatefolk say the Carn live that way, and below them a people of fishers on a river.

## Guesses (all guesses)

- Guess: there are two of them, one for each pull. I cannot tell if a third sits in the same line as either.
- Guess: the eastern one lives under or near the burning hills. A god who chose fire country may be hard, or restless. I do not know.
- Guess: the western one is with the fishers or the Carn, since the pull and the Gatefolk's talk point the same way. It may be gentler than the other. That is only the water in my head.
- Guess: they are like me, with a power and a price on it. I do not know their powers and will not assume them.
- Guess: they also feel me as a pull, and may be deciding what to do about it, as I am.

## Trust, grievances, debts

None. I have met no one. I will meet the first one open-handed, because that is my nature, and I will watch my envy, because I will want whatever they have that I lack.
