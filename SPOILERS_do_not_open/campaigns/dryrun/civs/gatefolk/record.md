# The Gatefolk
About 5,000 people in and around a walled town of stacked stone at the meeting of three passes in the high hill country, twenty-five days east of the Ald and about fifteen days west of the burning hills. They live by letting travelers through and taking a toll of what they carry. Every house has a door that bars; the town has three gates and shuts them at dusk. A spring under the town never fails. West of them the Carn hold the hill country; the Gatefolk trade with the Carn and do not trust them. They were not waiting for a god and are quiet around Thornmere. Hierarchy: a gate-warden for each gate and a council of the three. Mood: wary, watchful, a little proud of being chosen.
