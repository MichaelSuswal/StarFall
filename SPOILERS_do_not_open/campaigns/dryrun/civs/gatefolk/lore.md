# What the Gatefolk say

- The woman walks the passes. A guest who is not at the gate has not left. (Some say: she left because we gave her no hearth. Others: gods owe no toll and pay none.)
- The sky-lights are sky-gates opening and shutting. Someone is taking toll up there. A light in the west means a gate was opened for someone who paid.
- The eastern pot-carriers kept a god's fire alive, and a fire that is fed is a god that is fed. Good pots, though.
- The Carn count by knots, keep their dead in the hills, and will sell you anything but their word.
- Bar your door, shut the gate at dusk, and what the sky does is the sky's business.
