> SPOILER WARNING. NPC people (dry run): the Gatefolk

# Report to the Keeper, Year 1

Touching others:
- Two pairs of coal-carrying strangers from the east passed west at the east gate about day 20 of spring and returned east about 40 days later, paying toll in pots each time. The second pair said hill people turned them back. The wardens asked who sent them and what the pots held, got only "a god's word for the west", and made them keep live coals in the gate-yard.
- The council told no one, not the Carn. Gate-house talk and traders may carry it anyway.
- Pots from the strangers entered the Carn trade-goods; not sold on yet.

Keeper must roll:
- Whether Carn hear of the strangers through traders or the second pair's turn-back.
- Whether the wardens' sight of Thornmere leaving west reaches the Carn.
- Whether a returning pair carries word of the Gatefolk eastward.

If Thornmere returned, they would say:
- Population 5,009. 172 born, 163 died, two lost in a north-pass rockfall.
- Goats about 3,200. One good winter's grain in store, a little more than last year.
- Passes open mid-spring, north shut late autumn. Strangers with fire passed twice. Sky-lights at midsummer and midwinter, in the west, the way you went.
- Gates held, bars held, mood steady. Some say she was never given a hearth.
