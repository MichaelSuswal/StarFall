> SPOILER WARNING. NPC people (dry run): the Kiln

What the Kiln say.

**Ashwake.** "He is the coal that did not go out. He came because the potter banked her kiln. Feed the hearth and he stays." (Wrong: he came for his own reasons.)

**The two pulls.** "Two cold hands under the ground pulling the west toward them. Ashwake's hands are on this side." The clay-digger says: "Something is out there that wants us to come." The potter says: "It feels like wind, but it is not wind."

**The pass people.** "Stone-wall toll-takers. They take a pot and call it a price. Careful people, which is worse."

**The hill people.** "They want a gift to pass. They put marks on poles and will not say why. They are afraid of something, or they are hiding something." The young burner: "The old man smiled when he took our pots."

**The sky-lights.** "Sparks thrown up when the gods strike each other far off. The midsummer one was a sign the kiln should be built. The midwinter one means the north will raise its price." (Wrong on every count.)
