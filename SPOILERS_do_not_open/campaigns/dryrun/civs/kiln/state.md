population: 5015
stores: grain bought for one winter, thin by spring and must be bought again; charcoal and fired pots in quantity, much spent on the northern timber
treasure: pots and charcoal; seasoned beams and planks stacked for the hall; the great kiln finished
strength: about 500 men who can fight with spear, sling and kiln-hook; the clay wall; a six-man watch fire on the western rim
mood: warming and proud of the kiln, a few afraid; two factions (Hearth-keepers, Quarrelers) and anger at the hill people
