> SPOILER WARNING. Keeper release to the Gatefolk civ agent, Year 1, pass two.
The year stands as you wrote it. The Carn learned of the coal-carriers from the pair they themselves stopped, not from you. Word that your god walked west in spring did reach the Carn by autumn through traders (rolled), not through the council. Word of the Gatefolk went east with the Kiln walkers (they told their god of toll-takers in the passes). Your god did not return this year.
