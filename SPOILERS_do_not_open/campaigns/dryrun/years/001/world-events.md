## Weather and seasons (Keeper's setting, ordinary year)
- Early spring: the Ald high and brown with snowmelt from the wood; strong current for three weeks (upstream boats halved, pace table); water meadows under water at Aldford.
- Late spring: fair, river falling to summer level by the sixth week.
- Summer: dry and warm, river low; the Aldford ford open from midsummer.
- Autumn: ordinary rains; two gales off the Grey Sea in the week of the autumn fish run, one hard enough to keep boats ashore for two days.
- Winter: ordinary; the marsh creeks freeze at the edges for three weeks around midwinter; snow lies in the hills and on the Greywall; the Ald itself does not freeze.

## Recurring phenomena
- Sky-lights, Year 1: 4 (tools/phenomena.py, sealed record). Keeper fixes their moments (new fixed facts, logged): (1) week 1, the fourth night after the god's waking, over the Grey Sea to the west, a low light that climbs only a hand's breadth above the horizon: seen from the coast and the valley, not from the Carn hills, the passes or the burning hills; (2) week 10, late spring, over the North Wood to the north, climbing a quarter of the way up the sky: seen from the valley and the Carn hills, hidden from the passes and the burning hills by the Greywall range; (3) week 20, midsummer, over the hills to the east, climbing high: seen from everywhere in the region; (4) week 46, midwinter, over the sea to the west, climbing high: seen from everywhere. Each is a single point of steady pale light that rises slowly from below the horizon, climbs for about the time it takes to walk two hundred paces, dims and is gone. The weather on all four nights is clear across the region (fixed). The Carn in the hills notch each one.

## The player's god
- The Weir-King walks the Ald from the shingle bar upriver from the first days of spring, touching the water at every village, as decided. Standing conduct: observe only, avoid strangers, retreat if seen, defend but do not kill. The Keeper sets his pace from the decision "learn the river": several days at each village. Rate fixed by the Keeper: about a week at each of the twelve villages, walking between at 1.6 times the foot rate, so Wellbrook in week 4 to 5, Aldford in week 9 to 10, Fallstead in week 13, and he stays at the falls from week 13, his decided walk being finished and his last course being to learn the river.
