## Weather and seasons (Keeper's setting, ordinary year)
- Early spring: the Ald high and brown with snowmelt from the wood; strong current for three weeks (upstream boats halved, pace table); water meadows under water at Aldford.
- Late spring: fair, river falling to summer level by the sixth week.
- Summer: dry and warm, river low; the Aldford ford open from midsummer.
- Autumn: ordinary rains; two gales off the Grey Sea in the week of the autumn fish run, one hard enough to keep boats ashore for two days.
- Winter: ordinary; the marsh creeks freeze at the edges for three weeks around midwinter; snow lies in the hills and on the Greywall; the Ald itself does not freeze.

## Recurring phenomena
- Sky-lights, Year 1: 4 (tools/phenomena.py, sealed record). Keeper fixes their moments (new fixed facts, logged): (1) early spring, fourth night after the god's waking, over the Grey Sea to the west; (2) late spring, over the North Wood to the north; (3) midsummer, over the hills to the east; (4) midwinter, over the sea to the west. Each is a single point of steady pale light that rises slowly from below the horizon, climbs for about the time it takes to walk two hundred paces, dims and is gone high up. Seen by anyone awake and looking across the whole region. The Carn in the hills notch each one.

## Godless peoples
- The Carn (hill country east of the Ald valley and west of the Gatefolk passes): ordinary year; herds, hill steadings, their notched poles. Events touching them: see pass two (Thornmere's passage, the Kiln parties, the Riverfolk boatmen).
- Salt-road peoples to the south: no contact this year (first contact Year 5 by schedule).
- The timber people north of the burning hills (named in Ashwake's landing release; new fixed fact, keeper log): sell timber to the Kiln for charcoal and pots, dislike them, trade anyway.

## The player's god
- The Weir-King walks the Ald from the shingle bar upriver from the first days of spring, touching the water at every village, as decided. Standing conduct: observe only, avoid strangers, retreat if seen, defend but do not kill. The Keeper sets his pace from the decision "learn the river": several days at each village. He is at Wellbrook about the fifth week of spring, Aldford about the ninth week, and at Fallstead below the falls by late summer. Contact with Thornmere: pass two.
