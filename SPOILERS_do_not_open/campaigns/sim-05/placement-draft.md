# Placement draft: sim-05

Provisional. Working names only; places get names and map ids when the sheet is traced on Night 3a. Nothing here is a distance or a direction to be released: land in words comes from the traced map.

## The region

One continent, its land about 1,100 miles across from west to east and about 900 from north to south, at least 650 at its narrowest (the aim is 800 to 1,200 on average, never under 600). Sea on the east and south; a colder coast in the north-west. High mountains with ice across the north and north-west. Below them a band of steppe and high plain. Several great rivers rise in the northern and central uplands and run south and east through hill country, braided middle reaches, flood plains and marsh to the sea. Forest in the west and south-west, meeting grassland. The center of the sheet is open, godless country. The rain reaches well past every edge of the sheet.

## The seats, in a rough ring

Spaced evenly in rough terms, each in the country its answer 2 asked for. The order round the ring, provisional until 3a.3 sets it: north-west, north, north-east, east, south-east, south, south-west, west.

- North-west, `god-storms`: the foot of the ice mountains, steppe and open river country below, the cold coast within reach. Landing spot: a bare ridge under open sky above the steppe. In reach of its eyes: ice peaks behind, grass to the horizon below, the line of a river, far smoke. Wakes in spring. History the land will carry: the oldest ice, with great bones in it; a people under the ice in legend; winter fuel short.
- North, the player's god: river country opening onto high plains, flat ground, with passes in the hills beyond. Landing spot: a low rise at the plain's edge above a braided reach of a river. In reach of its eyes: flat grassland on one side; the river in gravel bars and channels below, running high; a line of hills with a gap in it; the straight line of an old paved road coming down to a ford; one thread of smoke; a worn track. Wakes in early spring: snow still on the far heights, the low ground wet, the roads beginning to dry. History the land will carry: an old road network of a fallen kingdom, usable; a sealed door in the hills that waits; a high-country people beyond the passes, hard neighbors. A harbor is not at the start.
- North-east, `god-rivers`: headwater springs in stone and the long middle reaches of a river, braided, with fords. Landing spot: a stone ledge where a spring comes out, above the first reach. In reach of its eyes: the stream going down through alder, a ford below, dry channels of an older course. Wakes in spring. History: old roads along dry riverbeds; a capped spring; a water-margin people in the reed country below.
- East, `god-sea`: a coast with a walled harbor. Landing spot: the shingle outside a harbor wall, after the boats are hauled up. In reach of its eyes: the wall, lamps, the sea. Wakes in late autumn. History: a harbor built on an older harbor; great bones once washed ashore; safe harbors few on this coast.
- South-east, `god-fate`: a wide slow river with ford towns, harbors within reach, marsh edges. Landing spot: a reed bank at a marsh edge by a ford. In reach of its eyes: the ford, a tollhouse in ruin, carts on a road. Wakes in autumn. History: ruined markets and tollhouses with unread ledgers; a door with terms cut on it in the hills behind.
- South, `god-harvest`: a black-soil flood plain on one wide river, hills behind, a ford, a little marsh. Landing spot: the edge of a reaped field above the ford. In reach of its eyes: stubble, stacks, the river, a walled town's smoke. Wakes in late summer, at the edge of the harvest. History: a granary far too large, older than the town; a half-sunk altar with its face worn off; seed that breeds true is short.
- South-west, `god-beasts`: grassland meeting forest, a river through it, hills behind. Landing spot: open grass at the forest's edge by the river. In reach of its eyes: herds at the water, the tree line, hills. Wakes in autumn. History: an old road with great bones under it; a country of uncounted beasts further in.
- West, `god-roads`: the seams where steppe, hill country and river plain meet. Landing spot: a crossroads of three old ways at a ford. In reach of its eyes: the three roads going off into three kinds of country. Wakes in autumn. History: old roads to places no longer there; a hill with a sealed door.

## What the star and the lore place

- The center: the hoarder, a sleeping beast that is visibly a beast, under the star, in godless country at the middle of the sheet. The surface sheet shows it as land with a beast's shape.
- The south: the school of workers, in godless country between the southern seats and the coast.
- The oldest, most worked ground, where the fading shows: placed on 3b from the deep history.
- The warm stone that fell with the gods: in godless country between two gods. Which two is not the Keeper's to choose: it is left to the placement roll in 3a.3, among the adjacent pairs of seats once the ring is set.
- The underground: hollows only, written into `map.json` on 3a before any painting; no sheet is approved for it.
- Godless peoples, the one or two non-human peoples, the sealed doors, the bones, the rare candidates of the world draft: placed on 3a.3. No god's home ground carries more common-past likenesses than another's.
