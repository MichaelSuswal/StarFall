# World bible: sim-05

The sealed summary of the whole world, written on Night 3b, 2026-11-23. It points to the files that hold each thing and repeats only what is needed to find one's way. No public summary is built from it.

## The name

The world as the Keeper knows it: Ellowain. Each people has its own name for it and for the star, written in that people's record under `What the old tell` (the Carrow's in `civs/carrow-sealed.md` and, as their own words, in the public `campaigns/sim-05/world/world.md`).

## The rules, final

- God-sense: a knowing only face to face. Nothing at a distance, no pull, no direction. It comes slowly in a meeting, may be held off for a while by one who works at seeming mortal, and is sure within arm's reach. It tells what stands there and nothing else. Never a tally.
- Bodies: one body, for every god. A form of many parts is one body.
- Mortal magic: level 9. A worker known by name in nearly every valley, about one to every six hundred people, and a school in the south. Simple. Nothing is made; the worker pays with what is most her own. One rule everywhere.
- How much a god stands out: 7. Plainly more than any mortal; mortals can still bargain with one, refuse it and plan round it.
- The wild: 4. Familiar beasts, many legends, a few nameless things at the margins.
- Land held and open: 5. About half of what lies outside the gods' countries is held by the four godless peoples.
- Climate: 4. Four true seasons; winters close the high passes.
- The human story: 10. Thousands of years, ruins under ruins.
- Technology: before the medieval era, no steel forges; a starting level, not a ceiling.
- Oaths bind no one by any rule of the world.

## The star

`star.md`. Mature (51). Rain density 6. Living radius 70 days' walk, so the edge of the rain lies far out to sea on every side. The center body is a sleeping beast that is visibly a beast. Hoarder and carriers; no thinners, and nothing stands in for them. The Remainder fell in godless country. Content: islands that moved. Temper of those above: mostly those who want the fall soon. Ceiling 80; the voices at 64. No descent can be measured in the campaign, and none is offered as a clue.

## The land

Every god lands with no helpers in fact.
