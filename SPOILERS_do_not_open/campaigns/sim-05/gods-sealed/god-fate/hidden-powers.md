# Sealed: god-fate

### The Session Closed
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: A door, gate or lid the god shuts with its own paws, saying that the session is closed, stays shut against every mortal hand, tool and key until the god opens it or a season has passed.
Drawback: While one is held shut the god hears knocking from the inside in every quiet moment, and The Weighing gives it no answers: the scales will not settle.
Limits: One at a time; ordinary doors of ordinary craft; it does not hold against a god, and it does not stop a wall being broken beside the door.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): shutting something on someone in haste or fear.
Fitted to: the hill door, shut on the Court.

### The Sound Sum
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Running a paw down any tally, account or count of goods, the god knows, yes or no, whether the sum is honest.
Drawback: An unsound sum sticks in it: it can write no new bill until it has found the fault or told the keeper of that tally to their face that it is false.
Limits: Yes or no only, never where the fault lies or who made it; written or knotted tallies it can touch. Smaller than The Session Closed.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): handling a ledger that has been cooked.
Fitted to: the clerk's tallies, and the count it knew to be true.

### The Hand Weight
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It knows the weight of anything it lifts, to the grain.
Limit: Things it can lift; a number, nothing more.
Shows when (never scheduled): goods sold by weight, with its scales elsewhere.
Drawn from: the fee-store.

### The Bolt-Hole
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: Entering any room, it knows the nearest way out besides the one it came in by.
Limit: Ordinary ways a small beast could take; a shade on an escape.
Shows when (never scheduled): being cornered.
Drawn from: the flight of the clerk.

Ceiling: 80.
