# Sealed: god-harvest

### The Sealed Store
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: A granary or store the god seals with its own hand keeps: the grain in it does not rot, sprout or go to vermin, for up to three years, until the god or a steward it has named breaks the seal.
Drawback: It feels the sealed store as a weight and a hunger for as long as it stands; and the seal will not open, even to the god, until someone has said the god's name to it.
Limits: One sealed store at a time. Fire, flood and thieves through the wall are not stopped. Kept small in reach: this god's card already weighs well.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): sealing a store against a year it fears.
Fitted to: the granary-hall at Brede.

### The Voices Counted
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: When a hundred or more say its name together, the god knows the number of voices exactly.
Drawback: It also knows that some who said it last time are missing, though not who, and the lack aches until the next saying.
Limits: A number only, at the moment of the saying. Smaller than The Sealed Store.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): a crowd saying its name at once.
Fitted to: the corn-name said at the turning.

### The Heap Judged
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It can tell by eye how much grain is in a heap or a bin.
Limit: Grain and pulse in plain view; a number within a few parts in a hundred.
Shows when (never scheduled): a tithe brought in short.
Drawn from: the Brede tally.

### The True Seed Picked
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: From a handful of seed it can pick the grains that will breed true.
Limit: A handful at a time, by hand; it blesses nothing and speeds nothing.
Shows when (never scheduled): seed corn being chosen for a new field.
Drawn from: the seed kept at Brede.

Ceiling: 80.
