# Sealed: god-rivers

### The Night Ferry
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: In a flood or a drowning, any boat or raft the god has set its hand to that night will not sink or overturn until it touches shore, however it is laden.
Drawback: It cannot leave the water's edge, or turn to anything else, until every craft it touched that night is in or known lost; and it keeps the count of them afterward whether it wishes to or not.
Limits: One night in a year. Craft it has touched with its own hand, on the water it is standing by. It carries no one itself and calms no water.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): people in the water at night and the god at the bank with a boat in reach.
Fitted to: the drowning of Calder Hythe: the boats taken upriver, and not all of them.

### Arle's Cup
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Water the god gives one mortal from its own bowl quenches thirst and keeps that mortal on its feet for a day with no other food or drink.
Drawback: That mortal joins those the god cannot forget (its Keeper trait), and the god must ask and learn its name before it can walk on.
Limits: One mortal at a time, one day; it heals nothing and cleans no water. Smaller than The Night Ferry.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): someone dying of thirst or foul water in front of it, and the bowl in its hand.
Fitted to: the keeper of the Spring of Arle.

### The Ferryman's Eye
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It sees at a glance whether a boat or a raft is overladen for the water it is on.
Limit: A shade on a crossing's risk; it lightens nothing.
Shows when (never scheduled): a laden boat about to put out.
Drawn from: a habit of the ferrying.

### The Bank Read
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: On first sight of a stretch of river it picks the best place to ford.
Limit: Fords only, water it can see; a shade on the crossing roll.
Shows when (never scheduled): a crossing in a hurry on water it has never seen.
Drawn from: the hunter along the bank, before the ferry.

### The Lost Trail
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: Along water it is a little harder to track than a man would be.
Limit: A shade on a tracker's roll, at a bank or in a stream only; it does not end its weakness, The Hunted Run, by itself.
Shows when (never scheduled): something patient is following it along a river.
Drawn from: the years of being followed upstream.

Ceiling: 80.
