# Sealed: god-beasts

### The Hour Unbound
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When the god is bound by chain, rope, net or bar, then for one hour no made binding of ordinary craft holds it: links part and ropes burst.
Drawback: For a day afterward it shakes and can do nothing but walk, and the power does not come again for a year.
Limits: Once in a year. Bindings of ordinary craft; one set with rite or by more than ordinary craft is unruled until it happens. It frees no one else.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): being bound.
Fitted to: the chain in the Narrows.

### The Table Laid
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: At a table the god has laid out of doors and fed its guests at, no quarrel comes to blows while the meal lasts: a calm sits on the table, as at a watering place at dusk.
Drawback: The god is under it too: it can strike no one and order no harm until the meal has ended and the last guest has gone a bowshot from the table.
Limits: A feeling and never an order: one who means violence can still rise and leave the table and do it elsewhere. Mortals and beasts; on a god, unruled until it happens. Smaller than The Hour Unbound.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): enemies fed at its table.
Fitted to: the Holm Table.

### The Lead Beast
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: In any herd it sees which beast the others follow.
Limit: Sight of the herd; a shade on driving, turning or stampeding it.
Shows when (never scheduled): a herd that must be moved in a hurry.
Drawn from: the common herds.

### The Drover's Stride
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: Beasts it drives on foot go a little further in a day without tiring.
Limit: A shade on a drive's pace, about a fifth, for herds it walks with itself.
Shows when (never scheduled): a drive against time or weather.
Drawn from: bringing the herds down to the river.

### The Knotted Tally
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: A count it has once tied into its cord is never afterward lost or misread by it.
Limit: Counts it has tied; the first counting by eye still comes one short (its Keeper trait) until it counts again.
Shows when (never scheduled): an argument over an old count.
Drawn from: the count given aloud at the Table.

Ceiling: 80.
