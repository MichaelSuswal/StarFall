# Sealed: god-sea

### The Lamp Kept
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: A lamp the god lights with its own hand and sets toward the water can be seen by anyone on the water who is seeking that harbor, through fog, storm and dark, as far as a day's sail, and a craft that steers by it does not strike in the harbor's mouth.
Drawback: While the lamp burns the god knows the count of those still out, and it cannot sleep, sit at a table or leave the town until every one of them is in or known lost.
Limits: One lamp at a time, one harbor. It guides those who seek the harbor, friend or not. It calms no sea and carries no one. The largest of this god's sealed gifts, fitted so because its card weighs least.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): boats out in weather that will not bring them home, and the god with a lamp to light.
Fitted to: the harbor custom of Calder Hythe.

### The Hand Across
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: One mortal at a time, held by the hand, walks the water with the god or goes beneath it as the god does, for as long as the hand is held.
Drawback: If the hand is let go the mortal is in the water as any mortal is; and each one brought across costs the god a further day of tasting only salt.
Limits: One at a time, by the hand, at the god's walking pace, within what Sea-Step itself reaches. Smaller than The Lamp Kept.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): someone on the wrong side of water who will die there, and no boat.
Fitted to: the far shore, where the fishing families were left.

### The Count of the Room
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It knows at a glance how many are in a room or a boat, and notices the moment the number changes.
Limit: A number only, of people in plain view in one room or one craft; no faces, nothing hidden.
Shows when (never scheduled): someone slips in or out.
Drawn from: the count of boats in.

### The Welcome Weighed
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It can usually tell whether a welcome offered to it is meant.
Limit: A shade on reading a host at the threshold, at the moment of the offer only; it reads nothing else.
Shows when (never scheduled): an invitation given through the teeth.
Drawn from: the place laid at the foot of the table.

### The Deck Foot
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It does not lose its footing on a deck, a wet stone or a moving plank.
Limit: Footing only; a shade on such a roll.
Shows when (never scheduled): a fight or a rescue on a pitching surface.
Drawn from: the harbor wall.

Ceiling: 80.
