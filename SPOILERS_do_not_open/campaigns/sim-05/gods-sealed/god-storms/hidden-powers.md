# Sealed: god-storms

### The Second Thunder
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: Once in a year, in a storm it did not call, the god may speak to the storm and be answered: it knows where that storm will go and when it will end.
Drawback: For a day afterward it can call no thunder at all.
Limits: That storm only, its course and its end; nothing of who sent it, if anyone did. Kept the smallest of any god's sealed gifts: this card weighs most.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): standing in a storm that is not its own and speaking to it.
Fitted to: the two thunders that answered each other.

### The Still Chair
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Where the god sits in judgment under open sky, the wind carries no word away: everyone within a bowshot hears every speaker plainly.
Drawback: Its own words too: while it sits so it can say nothing aside, and every word it speaks is heard by all.
Limits: Hearing only, while it sits; it compels no truth. Smaller than The Second Thunder.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): a hearing held in wind.
Fitted to: the weather bargained with at the Table.

### The Roof Read
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It sees where a wind will take a roof, a tent or a stack.
Limit: Sight of the thing; a shade on what a storm breaks.
Shows when (never scheduled): a camp pitched before weather.
Drawn from: the hard weather off the uplands.

### The Long Horn
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: Its horn carries about twice as far downwind as another's.
Limit: Downwind, in open country; sound only.
Shows when (never scheduled): a gathering called across distance.
Drawn from: the rider on the ridge.

Ceiling: 80.
