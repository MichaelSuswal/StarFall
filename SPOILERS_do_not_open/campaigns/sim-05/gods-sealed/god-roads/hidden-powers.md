# Sealed: god-roads

### The Session at the Crossing
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When thirty or more mortals gather at a crossing to settle a dispute and ask the god to hear it, and it sits as a court to the end, everyone present remembers its judgment word for word for the rest of their lives.
Drawback: It must hear the matter to the end, it cannot hold Unremarked from the first word to the last, and it is bound by its own judgment as the parties are.
Limits: A crossing or a ford only; one matter at a time; it compels no one to obey the judgment, only to remember it. Kept small: this god's card already weighs well.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): being asked, in the open, to judge between two parties where ways meet.
Fitted to: the Court of Sennet at the Holm Table.

### The Door Held
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: No door, gate or lid can be shut while the god stands in its frame, flock or figure, whatever pushes on it.
Drawback: Having held a door, it must go out by that door and cannot stay inside the room it kept open.
Limits: Ordinary doors and ordinary strength; it opens nothing already shut and holds nothing against a god.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): a door being shut on it, or on someone it has just answered.
Fitted to: the hill door, shut from outside.

### The Order of Speaking
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: In any meeting it knows who has not yet spoken.
Limit: A shade on reading a council; it tells nothing of what the silent one thinks.
Shows when (never scheduled): a council where one voice is holding back.
Drawn from: the Court's own procedure.

### The Record Voice
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It can give back word for word anything said to it formally within the year.
Limit: Formal speech addressed to it; a thing lost to a memory price is lost here too.
Shows when (never scheduled): a dispute over what exactly was said.
Drawn from: the Court's record, kept by many voices.

Ceiling: 80.
