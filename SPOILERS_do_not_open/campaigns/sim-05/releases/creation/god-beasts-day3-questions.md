# Day 3 questions: god-beasts

These go into your Night 3 release. Answer each in
SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-beasts/people-hoped.md,
under the heading "## Questions for you", when your Night 3 release asks you to write that file, and in no other file.

1. You cannot speak a false number, and you count your people's herds. Who hears the count, and what is done when it comes short?
2. Your call may not be betrayed. Write how your people's hunt is named and lawful, so that anyone watching can tell a lawful kill from another.
3. You may never eat a kind you have spoken with. Which kinds do your people keep and eat?

A git pull --rebase --autostash origin main is always allowed; it writes no file of yours.
