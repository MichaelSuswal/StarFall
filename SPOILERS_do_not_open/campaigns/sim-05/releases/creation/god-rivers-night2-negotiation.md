# Negotiation: god-rivers, Night 2

Your `self.md` was read against your sheet. Nothing is sent back. Below is each power as it would be written on your card, with the price and the Keeper's reasons. The card lines say "the god"; that is you.

## Round 1

### The Listening
Draft text:
Description: When the god kneels or stands in a spring, stream or river and gives the water its whole attention, it hears what that water has carried: sounds made at the water's edge along the reach that lies within a day's flow upstream of where it stands. From the last day and night it hears the words. Older than that, back to about a season, it hears only the kind of sound: a crossing of many feet, a quarrel, a boat, something poured in.
Drawback: (your choice, below)
Limits:
- Sounds only, never thoughts. It hears words, not whether they are true.
- Running or rising water only, and only the channel it stands in, upstream. Frozen water gives nothing until it thaws.
- While it listens it is deaf and blind to everything else and does not hear its own name. It comes out over about a long breath for each hour it listened; a touch on the shoulder rouses it sooner. These are conditions of use, written here and not as the price.
- One voice out of a crowded ford takes much time.
- It shows what was heard, never why a thing was done.
- Other gods: a god's voice at the water is heard as any voice is. The listening does not say that a god spoke.
- Being watched: an old man standing in a stream with his eyes shut.
- More than ordinary craft: water made or held by more than ordinary craft may give nothing or give wrong. Unruled until it happens.
- Use: one channel at a time, as often as it likes.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying.
Choices, for the Drawback:
 (a) Struck dumb. After a listening the god has no speech at all for as long as it listened, and never less than an hour. After reaching back past the last day and night, it has none until the next dawn. It can hear and walk and make signs. It cannot bargain.
 (b) The short reach. The god is only dull and slow of speech for the rest of that day, as you offered, and the power reaches back no further than the last day and night. The older sounds are not heard at all.
The Keeper's reasons: Being deaf and blind while you listen is how the power works; by the rule every god is priced under, a condition of use is a Limit and buys nothing. What is left of your offer, a dull afternoon, is light for a power that lets you stand in a river and hear a season of what was said along a day of its bank. So either the price rises to something a bargainer feels, or the reach comes down to what the light price covers. The upstream line is written exactly because "within a day's flow" could be read both ways.

### Clean Water
Draft text:
Description: Standing in a spring or at a river's edge with its hands in the water, the god makes that spring, or about a day's walk of that channel, run clear and free of what makes people sick, for as long as the god stays within a day's walk of the place.
Drawback: It takes the sickness into itself. Each cleansing leaves it feverish, weak and shivering for seven days, and it can cleanse no second place until it has recovered. When it goes more than a day's walk away, the water goes foul again over a season.
Limits:
- The water, not the sick: it cures no person.
- A spring or a reach, never a great river or a sea. The spring must already run; it brings up no water.
- It does not undo a fouling while someone still stands there pouring it in.
- One place at a time.
- Other gods, more than ordinary craft: a fouling that is another god's work, or made by more than ordinary craft, is unruled until it happens.
- Being watched: plain to see, and so is the fever after.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, mind.
Choices: none. The price is the one you offered, with "some days" written as seven.
The Keeper's reasons: The fever is a real cost and the one place at a time keeps it from scaling across a people in a year. Accepted as you wrote it.

### The Witness at the Ford
Draft text:
Description: A bargain sworn aloud over running water, with the god standing present as witness and having heard it in full, is marked. Whoever of the sworn breaks it finds that all running water tastes of ash and iron to them for a full year, and it greys in the cup as they drink, so that those who watch them drink can see it.
Drawback: (your choice, below)
Limits:
- It forces no one to keep a bargain. It marks the one who does not.
- In person only, over running water, the whole bargain heard. Never by messenger.
- A bargain sworn freely holds even if unfair on its face. One sworn under plain force is not marked.
- The god cannot say false what was sworn, or deny that it witnessed. Once it has agreed to witness it must be present. These are conditions, not the price.
- The mark judges the words as spoken, not what either side meant.
- Other gods: a god that swears before the witness is unruled until it happens.
- Use: as many bargains as it will stand for.
- Not applicable: light and dark, distance, falling, carrying.
Choices, for the Drawback:
 (a) The shared cup. While any bargain the god witnessed stands broken and unmended, all water tastes of ash and iron to the god as well, until that breaker's year is out. It loses its taste for a river's health and for clean water for that time.
 (b) The surety. When a witnessed bargain is broken, the wronged party may claim one fair service of the god within the year, and the god may not refuse it or leave that country until it is done.
The Keeper's reasons: "Bound by the oaths it witnesses" and "loses the power if it breaks one" are penalties only carelessness triggers; under the rule for every god those are Limits and buy nothing. A witness whose mark lasts a year on anyone who breaks faith is a real power wherever people swear, so it needs a price you would feel each time. Both of these fall on you only when someone breaks faith, which is when the power does its work.

### Back to the Spring
Draft text:
Description: When the god's body is broken, it is gone for a season and then walks whole out of the spring or river head nearest to where it broke. The walk from there to wherever it was needed is added.
Drawback: Each return costs the god one item of memory or habit, taken by a logged roll from its own record (what it holds of other gods, and its goals). The god does not choose it and is not told what went.
Limits:
- The season rung of the standing scale, fixed now and never chosen at each return.
- A body held (bound, caged, frozen in, buried) or shut out is not broken, and this does not answer it.
- No set ground; the nearest serves.
Choices: none.
The Keeper's reasons: The standing scale, the same for every god, on the rung you named.

### Trade: The Hunted Run, for still water
Draft text:
Weakness, The Hunted Run: When something the god knows or believes to be following its trail on purpose (a mortal, a god or a beast) has kept after it for a full day, the god cannot stand and parley, bargain or keep an appointment. It must keep moving away by day and night and will enter no dwelling, market, ford crossing or place where it could be cornered, until the pursuer stops, loses it for a day and a night, dies, openly gives up, or catches it. Caught, the compulsion ends and it is held as the pursuer wills. It may send word ahead as it flees and may run toward its own people. A crowd on the same road, a messenger, one who has only asked to speak with it, or a pursuit it does not know of, do not count. Anyone who learns of it can keep the god from any table by setting a patient tracker on it.
What it bought: The Listening works at still water as well: a well, a pond, a cistern, a bowl of rain. Still water gives only sounds made beside it where it lay; water carried from its place gives nothing from before it was moved.
Choices: none. You need not take the trade.
The Keeper's reasons: The weakness costs you something you do not already pay and can be used against you, so it buys one step. The line about carried water is there so that a bowl is not a thing to be left in a room and collected.
