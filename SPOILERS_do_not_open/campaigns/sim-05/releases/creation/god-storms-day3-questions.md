# Day 3 questions: god-storms

These go into your Night 3 release. Answer each in
SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-storms/people-hoped.md,
under the heading "## Questions for you", when your Night 3 release asks you to write that file, and in no other file.

1. Your powers halve if no rite is made to you for a season, counted from the day you wake. What is the rite, who may make it, and how soon can it be taught?
2. You hear every word spoken under open sky within a mile. Where do your people speak when they do not wish to be heard, and do you allow it?
3. A great working is paid back on the same country within the year. Who decides when your people ask one of you?

A git pull --rebase --autostash origin main is always allowed; it writes no file of yours.
