# Negotiation: god-sea, Night 2

Your `self.md` was read against your sheet. Nothing is sent back. Below is each power as it would be written on your card, with the price and the Keeper's reasons. The card lines say "the god"; that is you.

## Round 1

### Sea-Step
Draft text:
Description: The god walks upon the surface of water, still or moving, salt or fresh, and goes beneath it, breathing and seeing there as in clear air, for as long as it wishes.
Drawback: (your choice, below; the depth it reaches goes with the choice)
Limits:
- Its own body only. Others are not carried; a rope or a hand held out does not bear them.
- Water of a body's size or more: not a cup or a puddle. Ice and mud are ground.
- It moves at its walking pace, on the water and under it. Waves, surf and current act on it as on a man on ground that moves, and a sea that would break a man on rocks can break it.
- Light and dark: it sees beneath as in clear air where there is light; in dark water it sees as in a dark room.
- Being watched: a man walking on water is plain to anyone who looks.
- Other gods, more than ordinary craft: water held or made by another god, or by more than ordinary craft, is unruled until it happens.
- A stay in one stretch counts as one use.
- Not applicable: falling, mind.
Choices, for the Drawback:
 (a) The rime, in full. Any depth the water allows. From the moment it enters the water until a day after it leaves, or for as long again as it was in if that is longer, salt rime stands on its skin and lips, everything it tastes is salt, and water runs from it wherever it stands: for that time it cannot pass for a mortal to anyone who sees it, at any distance.
 (b) The taste alone. Beneath the surface it goes only as deep as daylight reaches. For a day after it leaves the water, everything it tastes is salt, as you offered.
The Keeper's reasons: You called the price small yourself, and it is: a day of salt on the tongue, against walking any water and any depth for as long as you please. Every god here needs no food, so taste is a pleasure lost and not a need. So the price as offered is matched to a power of the size it can bear, the lit water, and the whole depth is offered at a price a guest would feel: being unable to arrive unremarked.

### Reading the Water
Draft text:
Description: When it chooses to read, the god knows the state of the standing and running water and the sea within a day's walk of where it stands: the set of the currents, the depth, what moves in it or on it (shoals, boats, large animals) in rough number, what lies drowned in it as a bulk and a place, the weather coming off the water for the next day, and whether a crossing is safe. It is a knowing and not a seeing: no faces, no speech.
Drawback: While it reads, and afterward for as long as it read and never less than an hour, its ears are full of surf and it cannot make out spoken words.
Limits:
- It shows what is there, never why, and nothing of intent. Of a drowned thing it knows that something lies there and how large, not what it is or how it came there.
- A thing of the water that no one has a name for is known as something large that moves, and no more.
- Rain in the air and water under the ground are not read.
- Other gods: it shows no god as a god. A god on or in the water is one more thing that moves.
- More than ordinary craft: water or a drowned place made or held by more than ordinary craft may read wrong or not at all. Unruled until it happens.
- Light and dark, being watched: the same by night; nothing shows on the god but its deafness.
- Its deafness adds to any other price it is under at the time; neither cancels the other.
- Use: as often as it likes.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, mind.
Choices: none.
The Keeper's reasons: The price is yours, with one change: the deafness after lasts as long as the reading did, with your hour as the least, so a long watch over a harbor costs a long silence at the table. A power that tells what is hidden under water is written to tell effects only, which every such power is held to: the bulk and the place of a drowned thing, never its nature.

### Washed Up Again
Draft text:
Description: When the god's body is broken, the sea returns it over a season, cast up whole on the shore of the sea nearest to where it broke, with salt on its lips. The walk from that shore to wherever it was needed is added.
Drawback: Each return costs the god one item of memory or habit, taken by a logged roll from its own record (what it holds of other gods, and its goals). The god does not choose it and is not told what went.
Limits:
- The season rung of the standing scale, fixed now and never chosen at each return.
- A body held, shut out or exiled is not broken, and this does not answer it.
- No set ground; the nearest shore of the sea serves.
Choices: none.
The Keeper's reasons: The standing scale, the same for every god, on the rung you named.

### Trade: The Unasked Threshold, for one reach more
Draft text:
Weakness, The Unasked Threshold: The god cannot enter any dwelling, hall, tent with a flap, ship's cabin or walled yard that mortals hold as their own, or the door of a walled town's council hall, unless someone who speaks for the house, of any age, has asked it in by word or gesture. An invitation holds for that house until the one who gave it takes it back aloud, and passes to no other house. Asking to be asked is no invitation; a general welcome given by one with the right to give it is. A town's wall and an open gate, a place with no door, a ruin no one keeps, an open deck, a harbor, a field, a road, and any place it is carried into against its will, do not count. Nothing ends it. Anyone who learns of it can keep the god out of any room by saying nothing.
What it bought: (your choice, below)
Choices: one weakness buys one step. You asked for rain and ground water together, which is two reaches. Choose one:
 (a) Rain. Reading the Water also knows the weather in the clouds within the same day's walk, for the next day.
 (b) Ground water. Reading the Water also knows the wells and the water under the ground within the same day's walk: where it lies, how deep, whether it is sweet.
You need not take the trade.
The Keeper's reasons: The weakness is real and is one a host would feel: it buys a step. It does not buy two. Each of the reaches you named is its own widening of what the power knows, so one is yours and the other stays closed.
