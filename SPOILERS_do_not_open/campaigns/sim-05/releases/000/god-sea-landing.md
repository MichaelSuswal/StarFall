# Landing release: god-sea

For god-sea alone. This is your first release. The Year 1 setup hands it to you in place of a last year's release, and you read it with your own folder, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-sea/`, before you decide your first year.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## The moment

You have come down. It is first light, the hour before sunrise, in late autumn. No one has spoken to you yet. You know no name for any place here but the names you gave yourself, and those are your own words until a mortal uses them.

## What your body tells you

Cold shingle underfoot, wet wool, salt and weed and tar on the air. The tide is making small sounds on the stones.

## What you sense of others like you

Nothing. At a distance you feel nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. Others like you came down when you did, and that is all you know of them. You will know one only by meeting it.

## What is in view

By the eyes of a tall mortal standing where you stand, in clear air. Every distance and direction is measured from the map. You stand in grid cell E3.

- A walled harbor town, about half a mile to the north-west, with lamps still lit on its wall and boats hauled up above the tide line.
- An old paved road, about half a mile to the north-west, about a mile of it in view, coming to the town from the west.
- Sheltered water to the south, about a mile off at its nearest, calm, seen out to about 40 miles.
- A low shore across that water, to the south-east, about 34 miles off, on the skyline.
- High fells to the north, about 38 miles off, on the skyline.
- In the sky: A blue star stands well up the sky to the west, paling as the light comes.

You see nothing beyond this. What lies past the skyline, what any place is called, and who lives where are not known to you yet.

## Your Night 3 release against the land as it is

That release was written before the map. Line by line:

- The wall: there, about half a mile off.
- Lamps: there, on the wall.
- The sea: the water in view is a great sheltered bay, with a low shore across it. The open sea is not in view from where you stand.
- You were told you come down on the shingle outside the harbor wall. You stand on shingle about half a mile from the wall.

## Your helpers

At landing you have no helpers in fact. Your own account of the morning says your people do not know you, so the offices you described stand, filled, under your people's own law, and each runs on your people's custom. Each becomes your helper on the day its holder has heard you and takes your orders, in play. Until then: no one acts for you while you are away, your unplayed time runs on your own conduct alone, and your people go on under their own law. An office you hoped to hold from the first hour is a hope; what your people do when you walk in is theirs. Nothing was charged for this and nothing was bought by it. The offices, all the council's own: the Speaker, the Harbormaster, the Wall-Warden with a paid watch, the Tally-Keeper, the Hall-Keeper.

## What you know of the ground

`SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-sea/map-knowledge.json` holds what you have seen, kept by the Keeper: the country you stand in, the things in view by a plain description, and a far smoke where there is one. It holds nothing your people know. That reaches you as they tell you.
