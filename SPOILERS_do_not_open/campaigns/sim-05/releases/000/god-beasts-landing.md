# Landing release: god-beasts

For god-beasts alone. This is your first release. The Year 1 setup hands it to you in place of a last year's release, and you read it with your own folder, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-beasts/`, before you decide your first year.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## The moment

You have come down. It is first light, the hour before sunrise, in autumn. No one has spoken to you yet. You know no name for any place here but the names you gave yourself, and those are your own words until a mortal uses them.

## What your body tells you

Cool grass, heavy with dew, to the knee of a mortal. The air smells of cattle and leaf mould and cold water.

## What you sense of others like you

Nothing. At a distance you feel nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. Others like you came down when you did, and that is all you know of them. You will know one only by meeting it.

## What is in view

By the eyes of a tall mortal standing where you stand, in clear air. Every distance and direction is measured from the map. You stand in grid cell B3.

- A river, about a mile to the south-east; about 3 miles of it in view, from the east round to the south-west, with herds standing at the water.
- Smoke, about 7 miles to the north-west: a town, and nothing of it but its smoke.
- A line of hills to the south-east, about 54 miles off, low on the skyline.
- Open grass all round, level to the eye's reach of about 3 miles.
- In the sky: A blue star stands high in the sky to the north-east, paling as the light comes.

You see nothing beyond this. What lies past the skyline, what any place is called, and who lives where are not known to you yet.

## Your Night 3 release against the land as it is

That release was written before the map. Line by line:

- Herds at the water: there, about a mile off.
- The tree line: not in view. From where you stand the grass runs to the eye's reach on every side; the forest's edge is farther off than you can see from this ground.
- Hills: there, very far off, low on the skyline.
- You were told you come down by the river. The river is about a mile from where you stand.

## Your helpers

At landing you have no helpers in fact. Your own account of the morning says your people do not know you, so the offices you described stand, filled, under your people's own law, and each runs on your people's custom. Each becomes your helper on the day its holder has heard you and takes your orders, in play. Until then: no one acts for you while you are away, your unplayed time runs on your own conduct alone, and your people go on under their own law. An office you hoped to hold from the first hour is a hope; what your people do when you walk in is theirs. Nothing was charged for this and nothing was bought by it. The offices that stand under your people's own law: the twelve ward-heads; the Lawsayer; the Master Drover; the elders of the herds; the keeper of the Reckoning-house; the captain of the wall, with a paid guard; the keeper of the autumn rite. The crown is no one's until your people give it.

## What you know of the ground

`SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-beasts/map-knowledge.json` holds what you have seen, kept by the Keeper: the country you stand in, the things in view by a plain description, and a far smoke where there is one. It holds nothing your people know. That reaches you as they tell you.
