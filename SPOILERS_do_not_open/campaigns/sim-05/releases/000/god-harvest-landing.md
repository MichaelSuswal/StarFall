# Landing release: god-harvest

For god-harvest alone. This is your first release. The Year 1 setup hands it to you in place of a last year's release, and you read it with your own folder, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-harvest/`, before you decide your first year.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## The moment

You have come down. It is first light, the hour before sunrise, in late summer, at the edge of the harvest. No one has spoken to you yet. You know no name for any place here but the names you gave yourself, and those are your own words until a mortal uses them.

## What your body tells you

Warm air already, dust and cut straw, the ground dry and hard under you. Stubble all round, and stacks standing in rows.

## What you sense of others like you

Nothing. At a distance you feel nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. Others like you came down when you did, and that is all you know of them. You will know one only by meeting it.

## What is in view

By the eyes of a tall mortal standing where you stand, in clear air. Every distance and direction is measured from the map. You stand in grid cell D4.

- A wide slow river, about a mile to the east; about 4 miles of it in view, from the north-east round to the south-east.
- A ford, about a mile to the south-east.
- Smoke, about 3 miles to the west: a town, and nothing of it yet but its smoke.
- A line of hills to the north, about 29 miles off, on the skyline.
- Flat black ground all round, reaped, level to the eye's reach of about 3 miles.
- In the sky: A blue star stands high in the sky to the north-west, paling as the light comes.

You see nothing beyond this. What lies past the skyline, what any place is called, and who lives where are not known to you yet.

## Your Night 3 release against the land as it is

That release was written before the map. Line by line:

- Stubble, and stacks: there, all round you.
- The river: there, about a mile off.
- The smoke of a walled town: there, about 3 miles off. The walls themselves are not in view.
- You were told you come down above a ford. The ford is about a mile from where you stand.

## Your helpers

At landing you have no helpers in fact. Your own account of the morning says your people do not know you, so the offices you described stand, filled, under your people's own law, and each runs on your people's custom. Each becomes your helper on the day its holder has heard you and takes your orders, in play. Until then: no one acts for you while you are away, your unplayed time runs on your own conduct alone, and your people go on under their own law. An office you hoped to hold from the first hour is a hope; what your people do when you walk in is theirs. Nothing was charged for this and nothing was bought by it. The offices that stand under your people's own law: the Elders' Moot of the villages; the Ferry-Wardens of the ford; the old tally-priests and their head; the keeper of the town's bins; the warden of the gate and wall, with a paid guard; the tithe-wardens and bin-masters, who gather the old tenth for the common store. The crown, the granary and the temple court are no one's to take by saying so.

## What you know of the ground
