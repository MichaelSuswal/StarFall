# Landing release: god-rivers

For god-rivers alone. This is your first release. The Year 1 setup hands it to you in place of a last year's release, and you read it with your own folder, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-rivers/`, before you decide your first year.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## The moment

You have come down. It is first light, the hour before sunrise, in spring, in the thaw. No one has spoken to you yet. You know no name for any place here but the names you gave yourself, and those are your own words until a mortal uses them.

## What your body tells you

Cold stone under you, wet with melt. The air is thin and still and smells of snow water and alder. Your staff is in your hand.

## What you sense of others like you

Nothing. At a distance you feel nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. Others like you came down when you did, and that is all you know of them. You will know one only by meeting it.

## What is in view

By the eyes of a tall mortal standing where you stand, in clear air. Every distance and direction is measured from the map. You stand in grid cell D2.

- A river, about a quarter of a mile to the south-west, small here and loud with melt; about 2 miles of it in view, running off to the west.
- An old paved road, about a quarter of a mile to the south-west, about 2 miles of it in view, going down beside the water along a dry older bed.
- A spring coming out of a stone ledge, about a quarter of a mile to the south-west.
- A ford, about a mile to the south-west, with the smoke of a few fires at it.
- All round, bare grey upland stone. Nothing else stands out on it within the two miles a tall mortal's eye picks things out at.
- In the sky: A blue star stands well up the sky to the south-west, paling as the light comes.

You see nothing beyond this. What lies past the skyline, what any place is called, and who lives where are not known to you yet.

## Your Night 3 release against the land as it is

That release was written before the map. Line by line:

- The stream going down through alder: there, as the river above.
- A ford below: there, about a mile off.
- Dry channels of an older course: there. An old paved road runs down the dry bed.
- You were told you come down on a stone ledge where a spring comes out. The spring is in view about a quarter of a mile from where you stand, not underfoot.

## Your helpers

Your refusal of helpers stands as you gave it, recorded 2026-11-23, with your reasons in your own words. The offices your people have under their own law (the camp elders, the Ford Wardens, the Reckoners, the Gathering) stand and are filled, and are not yours. The cost, stated plainly: your unplayed time runs on your own conduct alone, no one acts for you while you are away, and your people drift and go their own way. They are never forced back on you. New loyal helpers may come later through play. The refusal bought nothing: you asked no edge, so there was no price for it to bear on.

## What you know of the ground

`SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-rivers/map-knowledge.json` holds what you have seen, kept by the Keeper: the country you stand in, the things in view by a plain description, and a far smoke where there is one. It holds nothing your people know. That reaches you as they tell you.
