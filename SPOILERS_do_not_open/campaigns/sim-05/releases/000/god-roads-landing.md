# Landing release: god-roads

For god-roads alone. This is your first release. The Year 1 setup hands it to you in place of a last year's release, and you read it with your own folder, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-roads/`, before you decide your first year.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## The moment

You have come down. It is first light, the hour before sunrise, in autumn. No one has spoken to you yet. You know no name for any place here but the names you gave yourself, and those are your own words until a mortal uses them.

## What your body tells you

Cool air, a little frost on the grass, wood smoke and horse on the wind. You are in the air and on the ground at once, as you are.

## What you sense of others like you

Nothing. At a distance you feel nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. Others like you came down when you did, and that is all you know of them. You will know one only by meeting it.

## What is in view

By the eyes of a tall mortal standing where you stand, in clear air. Every distance and direction is measured from the map. You stand in grid cell B2.

- A river, 220 yards to the north-east, low and stony after summer; about 4 miles of it in view, from the north-west round to the east.
- A town at a crossing, about a quarter of a mile to the north-west, with camp fires burning low round it and wagons standing loaded.
- An old paved road, 340 yards to the north, about 2 miles of it in view, from the north-west round to the east.
- A second old paved road, about a quarter of a mile to the north-west, running off north; about 2 miles of it in view.
- A third old paved road, about a quarter of a mile to the north-west, running off west; about 2 miles of it in view.
- Open plain all round, level to the eye's reach of about 3 miles.
- In the sky: A blue star stands high in the sky to the south-east, paling as the light comes.

You see nothing beyond this. What lies past the skyline, what any place is called, and who lives where are not known to you yet.

## Your Night 3 release against the land as it is

That release was written before the map. Line by line:

- A road going off into grass, a road going off into hills, a road going off across a river plain: all there, as above. The hills and the far grass are beyond what you can see from this ground; only the roads toward them are in view.
- You were told you come down at a crossing of old ways, at a ford. The crossing and the town are about a quarter of a mile from where you stand, and the river is 220 yards off.

## Your helpers

At landing you have no helpers in fact. Your own account of the morning says your people do not know you, so the offices you described stand, filled, under your people's own law, and each runs on your people's custom. Each becomes your helper on the day its holder has heard you and takes your orders, in play. Until then: no one acts for you while you are away, your unplayed time runs on your own conduct alone, and your people go on under their own law. An office you hoped to hold from the first hour is a hope; what your people do when you walk in is theirs. Nothing was charged for this and nothing was bought by it. The offices: the Warden of the Roads, with the Road Guard; the Captains of the Roads; the head of the Fire Keepers, called the Asker; the Provisioners; the Ford Judges.

## What you know of the ground

`SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-roads/map-knowledge.json` holds what you have seen, kept by the Keeper: the country you stand in, the things in view by a plain description, and a far smoke where there is one. It holds nothing your people know. That reaches you as they tell you.
