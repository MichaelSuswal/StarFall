# Landing release: god-storms

For god-storms alone. This is your first release. The Year 1 setup hands it to you in place of a last year's release, and you read it with your own folder, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-storms/`, before you decide your first year.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## The moment

You have come down. It is first light, the hour before sunrise, in spring. No one has spoken to you yet. You know no name for any place here but the names you gave yourself, and those are your own words until a mortal uses them.

## What your body tells you

Bare rock under you and a hard wind off the ice behind. The air is thin and very cold and smells of nothing but snow.

## What you sense of others like you

Nothing. At a distance you feel nothing of another god: no pull and no direction. Others like you came down when you did, and that is all you know of them. You will know one only by meeting it.

## What is in view

By the eyes of a tall mortal standing where you stand, in clear air. Every distance and direction is measured from the map. You stand in grid cell A1.

- Open steppe below you to the south, its near edge about a mile off, last year's grass with green coming through; seen out to about 40 miles.
- A stream, about a mile to the south, running down off the heights toward the grass; about a mile of it in view.
- Smoke, about 24 miles to the south: a camp or a village, and nothing of it but its smoke.
- Behind you and to either hand, rock and ice.
- In the sky: A blue star stands well up the sky to the south-east, paling as the light comes.

You see nothing beyond this. What lies past the skyline, what any place is called, and who lives where are not known to you yet.

## Your Night 3 release against the land as it is

That release was written before the map. Line by line:

- Behind you, peaks of ice: there.
- Below, grass to the horizon: there.
- The line of a river: what is in view is a snowmelt stream, about a mile off. No great river is in view from where you stand.
- Smoke, far off: there, about 24 miles to the south, and nothing of the place but its smoke. No town is in view.

## Your helpers

At landing you have no helpers in fact. Your own account of the morning says your people do not know you, so the offices you described stand, filled, under your people's own law, and each runs on your people's custom. Each becomes your helper on the day its holder has heard you and takes your orders, in play. Until then: no one acts for you while you are away, your unplayed time runs on your own conduct alone, and your people go on under their own law. An office you hoped to hold from the first hour is a hope; what your people do when you walk in is theirs. Nothing was charged for this and nothing was bought by it. The offices, all under your people's own law: the priest-king; the High Keeper; the Marshal of the Road; the Speaker of the Herds; the Oathwitness; the Wain-masters; the council of the herd-heads.

## What you know of the ground

`SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-05/gods/god-storms/map-knowledge.json` holds what you have seen, kept by the Keeper: the country you stand in, the things in view by a plain description, and a far smoke where there is one. It holds nothing your people know. That reaches you as they tell you.
