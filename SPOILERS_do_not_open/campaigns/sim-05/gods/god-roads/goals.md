# Goals: the Court at the Ford

Held on arrival, knowing only that a people is expected.

1. Find the people, at a border, a ford or a crossing, and learn who stands there and what they want.
2. Find one who will rule them for me by force, and make them mine through a war band and captains, and see whether they keep faith.
3. Have keepers of rite stand at the crossings and light a true fire, and be thanked for the way.
4. Learn the roads and the borders of the land, every ford and pass, and the old roads under the ruins, and who laid them and then stopped being anyone.
5. Be asked the way, and be paid for passage, in silver, in oaths and in sons.
6. Meet any others of my kind slowly, face to face, and know them only as they let themselves be known.
7. Keep no door closed behind me. Leave none shut that I did not shut.
8. Learn what is under the hill, and whether a road leads there, and whether the one who comes does so without being asked.
