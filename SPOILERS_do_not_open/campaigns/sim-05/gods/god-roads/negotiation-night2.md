
## Round 1 reply

I read the draft against what I proposed. Most of it is mine, written down tighter. Below, each item, taken in order, with what I accept, what I ask the write-up to say, and one place where I push.

### Sent Voices
Accepted, with drawback (a), the thinned court. It is my own offer and the line the Keeper added is fair: a court spread over three roads cannot also stand as one figure. I take it as written. I do not take (b). A killed or caged voice costing a record item is a price I did not offer and do not need on top of a body that is already nearly blind.

Two wordings, in the write-up and not new powers. First, say how much a voice is: a handful, five to ten birds out of the near two hundred, so the thinning can be counted and not argued. Second, say that "cannot close into the figure while any voice is out" ends the moment the last voice is back in the flock, at once, with no season of mending. A voice that returns is not a part lost.

### Passage Given
Accepted, with drawback (b), the cord crumbles. The Keeper is right that (a) is heavier than this power needs and buys nothing more. A road I can never walk again is a road taken from the thing I am. I offered it first because I wanted the price to be felt, and it is felt more cleanly the other way: I give a gift and I cannot stand on the road it was given on, or the gift is dust. I would notice losing that.

One wording. Say that the cord crumbling is the whole of the price, and that nothing is owed afterward: I may walk that road again after the cord is dust and give a new cord on it later. I ask this so the write-up does not read as a road closed to me for good.

### Unremarked
Accepted as written. A season held costs one item, and I take the Keeper's rate. The contest against one watching for me on purpose is fair.

One wording. "Held for a full season without a break" needs a line that says what a break is. I ask that a break is any time the cloak is dropped, at once, for as long as a day, and that dropping it for less does not reset the count. My reason: I will drop it to be thanked, because being thanked is what I am for, and I do not want a moment of thanks at a ford to wipe a season's count if the count is kept from the start of the cloak.

### The Closing
Accepted as written. Both added lines are fair: a ready band is rolled, and a storm of birds seen by a pass full of men is not unremarked. I take the line that birds do not tell friend from foe inside the storm. I would not loose it on my own people, and the line stands as a reason to be careful where I loose it.

I push on one thing, for reach. The band is capped at thirty. I would ask that the cap be forty, since a war band with captains runs near that and I am asking for a power a warlord can lean on. I do not ask this against the price. I ask it as a hard bargainer asks, once, and I accept thirty if the Keeper says no.

### Gathered Again at the Crossing
Accepted as written, the season rung, one item. The Keeper's limits are the ones I wrote.

### Trade: The Unrefused Question, for sight through a voice
Accepted, with the line about Unremarked. I did not see the hole in my own weakness: a god no one can notice is a god no one can ask. The line closes it, and I would rather pay a heavier price than be written a fence. I take the weakness with the line.

I do not take the last sentence as drafted: "Anyone who learns of it can have the way to anywhere the god knows, its own people's camps included, for the asking." As written it reaches past what I offered. My weakness covers a plain question about the way, asked at a crossing, a ford, a border or on a road I have walked. A camp is not a crossing. I ask the write-up to say that the weakness covers the way along roads and over fords and borders I have walked, to the next place on it, and that a mortal who asks the way to my people's camp must be answered only to the extent that the camp lies on a road I have walked and the asker stands on that road. This is a wording of the same weakness, not a lessening of it. The line about Unremarked stays in.

One further wording on the trade. With drawback (a) the body sees dimly while a voice is out. Say that the sight through one voice is in addition to that dim sight of the body, so that the trade is not swallowed by the drawback it sits beside.

### The Keeper's reasons, read
The Keeper has read me well: I would take the lighter price on Passage Given and the heavier weakness on the trade, and I have. Where I disagree is small: the camp sentence, and a count of ten. I ask nothing else.
