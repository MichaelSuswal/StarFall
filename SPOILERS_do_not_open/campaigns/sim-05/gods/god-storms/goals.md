# Goals: the Warden of the Weather

On arrival, knowing only that it expects a people:

1. Find a people to lead, in high country with open sky, and gather them into one close band. Know who is among them, and weigh each face before the hearth is opened.
2. Be named rightly: known by name and loved as a hard law is loved, and not merely feared or flattered. Teach the priesthood the rite, so that worship is kept and answered.
3. Make an oath worth something. Teach that a sworn word is kept, and let the first oath broken be judged openly and exactly.
4. Learn what lies near: the roads, the old ruins, the bones in the ice, the sealed doors. Be the one standing near when something old is opened.
5. Learn whether others like itself have come, where they stand, and what they want. Do not trust before meeting. Weigh, bargain, and do not kneel.
6. Find, one day, a mortal who can say no to it and be right.
