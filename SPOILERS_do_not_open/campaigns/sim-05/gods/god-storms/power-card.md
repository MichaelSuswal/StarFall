# Power card: the Warden of the Weather

Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-23. Confirmed by the god in its round 2 reply.

## Form

It holds no name of its own that it can recall. It reaches for one and finds the place smooth. It styles itself the Warden of the Weather, and bids its people say it so. Those who meet it and do not know it will call it the Antlered Cold, for the frost that rides its crown.

A person from the waist up and a great beast of the high country from the waist down. The upper body is broad and long of arm, nearly twice a tall man's height with the hooved body beneath it, skin the pale blue of old ice, with rime that gathers at the shoulders and collarbone in cold air and runs to water in warm. The face is human, severe and long, with a close white beard and eyes of a flat storm grey that show no pupil. From the brow rise antlers that fork and fork again like branched lightning, and frost collects on the tines. The lower body is that of a great elk, four legs, cloven hooves, a short pale tail, heavy shoulders and deep chest. It moves at the pace of an elk: a walk, a long trot, a strong gallop for a short while, and it tires not. It does not fly. It has two arms and hands, and uses them as a person does.

Senses: it feels weather before it comes, as pressure in the ears, a taste of iron, a prickling of the skin. It hears thunder and wind at great distance. It sees far and clearly in cold, thin air. In heat and in close, still, roofed rooms it is dulled in all its senses and its thoughts come slowly.

One body; a god is in one place at a time.

What breaks it: Crushing force and heavy blows can wound and break the body: a rockfall, a great weight, a hard fall, many strong people with heavy weapons. Great and sustained fire and heat scatter it. It can be held: bound with strong cords and weight, or kept long under a roof in warmth, where it grows sluggish and slow of speech. It can be shut out of a place by thick walls and a barred door it cannot break, though it is strong and would try. It cannot be killed.

Knowing another god: at a distance the god feels nothing of another god, no pull and no direction. It knows one only by meeting it. The knowing comes on slowly, by nearness alone, and one who works at seeming mortal may hold it off for a while. Within arm's reach it is sure, and no god passes for a mortal at a touch. It tells what stands there and nothing of its temper, its powers or its mind.

Keeper trait: No thunder it calls is ever answered: no echo comes back from hill, wall or cloud, in any country, and everyone who hears it notices the lack. Reach: sound only. It changes nothing that any weather does, hides nothing and reveals nothing but that this thunder is its own; it touches no price on this card.

When the body is broken: see Return to the Sky, under Powers.

### Definitions

None beyond the words of the powers below.

### Things carried

A long cloak of heavy hides, a ram's horn for gathering, and a staff of ash as tall as a person. All are ordinary objects and do nothing more than an ordinary object does.

## Powers

### The Calling of Weather
Status: main
Description: The god calls, turns and shapes weather it can see or feel coming: wind, cold, rain, hail, fog, thunder, lightning, a gale that breaks ice or drives a storm away. Its reach is the horizon it can see from the ground it stands on, and the sky for a day's walk about it. A small working (a gust, a chill, a patch of fog) builds in minutes. A great working (a storm over a field or a valley, a river brought to flood) takes a day or more to build. It calls only weather the season could bring, and holds no storm longer than that storm would last.
Drawback: Each working draws the god's warmth. After a small working it is cold to the bone and stiff for an hour. After a great working it is stiff and slow for a day, its speech slurred and its hands unable to grip. And the sky answers in kind: each great working is paid back by the weather's contrary over the same country within the year, for as long as the working ran and no longer, at a time the god does not choose (the Keeper rolls the season, the next included): a dry spell for rain called, a thaw for a hard frost, a dead calm for a gale.
Limits settled at creation:
- It is never hidden: anyone near sees the god work, and its courtly voice is the first thing to fail.
- It aims and does not shield. Aimed weather is a risk to anyone near the mark, its own people included.
- Lightning is aimed at a place within sight. To strike one person or one thing with it is a contest and is rolled, never certain. The Sworn Stroke is its own power and is not this contest.
- Weather it did not make keeps its own course. No rain on a place it cannot see.
- Scale: what a working does to a people's fields or herds in a year is counted by the Keeper as weather is, storm by storm.
- Other gods: weather turned on a god, or against another god's working, is unruled until it happens.
- More than ordinary craft: a place held by more than ordinary craft may not take the weather as called. Unruled until it happens.
- Light and dark: the same by night, within what it can feel coming.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0033.
Rulings:
Trades: improved by the weakness Hunger for Worship (see Weaknesses taken): The Calling of Weather also reaches weather born over water: a storm called from a coast the god can see or a sea it stands beside. Settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0035.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Wind's Ear
Status: main
Description: Beneath open sky, the god hears what is spoken aloud about it, carried on the wind: every word within a mile of where it stands, and, further off, to the limit of the ground it can see, one voice at a time, where it knows where to look. And it hears oaths: an oath sworn aloud to it by name or to the sky, under open sky within a day's walk of it, it knows, with who swore and what the words were.
Drawback: It cannot close this hearing under open sky. It hears everything in its range and cannot unhear it: the slander, the contempt, the whispered doubt of its own priests. In a crowd the noise is a pain and it cannot think clearly.
Limits settled at creation:
- Not beneath a roof, a tent, or trees thick enough to be a roof, for the speaker or for the god.
- Spoken words only, never thought. It hears the words, not whether they are true.
- Other gods: a god's speech under open sky is heard as any speech is. It does not say that a god spoke.
- Being watched: nothing shows on the god.
- Use: always on.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, water, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0034.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Sworn Stroke
Status: main
Description: The god strikes with lightning one mortal who stands in the open within the reach of The Calling of Weather, and who has either broken an oath sworn to the god by name beneath open sky, or been judged guilty by the god of a grievous offence after the matter was heard in full, the accused present and heard. The stroke falls on the one named, exactly, and kills or maims as the god chooses.
Drawback: After a killing stroke the god loses the sky for a full season: The Calling of Weather will not answer and The Wind's Ear is deaf. A stroke that only maims costs a month. Once the stroke is begun, the god cannot make it lesser to spare itself the cost.
Limits settled at creation:
- One who is under a roof cannot be struck until that one stands in the open.
- For a judged offence, a hearing is not full unless the accused stood in it under open sky and was let speak. A broken oath sworn to the god by name under open sky needs no hearing: the oath was heard when it was sworn.
- Mortals. Against another god it is unruled until it happens.
- Its own people or a stranger alike, if they swore to it or were heard and judged.
- Being watched: nothing is plainer.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, water, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0034.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Answer to Rite
Status: main
Description: When a rite the god has taught is rightly performed, with the words and the offering as taught, the god feels it as a tug. If the rite is within a day's walk, it knows who performed it and where. Further off, it knows only that a rite was rightly made. Where the place of the rite lies within the reach of The Calling of Weather, the god may answer with a sign in the weather over it, and may speak to its priest-king through the wind there.
Drawback: The god cannot ignore a rite rightly made. The tug pulls across its attention and its judgment, and may break its concentration in a council or a working. Anyone who learns the form, a lazy or a false priest included, can pull at it at will.
Limits settled at creation:
- A rite is one the god taught, or one taught by those it taught. Rites to another do not reach it.
- It knows the deed, not the heart of the one performing.
- A sign is weather, and is paid for as a small working of The Calling of Weather.
- Speech through the wind goes to its priest-king only, one way, from the god.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0017.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Return to the Sky
Status: main
Description: When the god's body is broken it scatters as weather, and gathers again over a season at the high open ground nearest to where it broke: a mountain crown, an ice field or a bare ridge under open sky. The walk from there to wherever it was needed is added.
Drawback: Each return costs the god one item of memory or habit, taken by a logged roll from its own record (what it holds of other gods, and its goals). The god does not choose it and is not told what went.
Limits settled at creation:
- The season rung of the standing scale, fixed now and never chosen at each return.
- A body held, bound or shut out is not broken, and this does not answer it.
- No set ground; the nearest serves.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Nothing disputed.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### Hunger for Worship
When no rite the god has taught is rightly performed to it by name, by a priest, its priest-king or any of its people, for a full season, its powers dim to half their reach and strength, its form grows thin, pale and cold, and its voice is weak, until one such rite is rightly made; then its powers return over a day. A feast, a prayer to the weather without its name, and a rite done in mockery do not count. The count runs from the day the god first wakes. Anyone who learns of it can halve the god by keeping its rites from being made. It bought, on The Calling of Weather: The Calling of Weather also reaches weather born over water: a storm called from a coast the god can see or a sea it stands beside. Settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0035.
