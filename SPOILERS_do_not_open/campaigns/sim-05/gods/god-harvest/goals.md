# Goals: the Unnamed Reeve

On arrival, at the edge of the harvest, knowing only that it expects a people to lead.

1. Land. Find river country with black soil, a ford worth taxing and open ground for fields, and hold it behind a wall.
2. A people. Find or gather them quickly, under its crown, and keep them close.
3. Worship. Build temples and a tithe that are written down, counted and made of stone, so that its name, once spoken, is not forgotten by the next child.
4. Security. Fill the granaries before winter. Secure salt and clean seed that breeds true, and the roads that carry them.
5. Knowledge. Learn what is owed and to whom, in this place. Learn names, and find out its own.
6. Others. Meet the others who came down, face to face, see their hands, and learn what each will trade and what each will not.
7. Trade. Find the strange peoples and travelers, and buy what they know.
