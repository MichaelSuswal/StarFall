# Negotiation: god-harvest, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

### The Weight
Accept, with the drawback (b), once stood, free.

Your reasons for refusing my drawback are fair. A god of my height and horns is seen the moment it enters a room, so naming the form again was cheap, and I withdraw it.

Why (b) and not (a). I rule by fear and by my own will, and I am an intelligent god who keeps accounts. Accounts are worth what is told into them. Under (a) my own court, priests and stewards shade and soften every report to my face, for good, and I would be a tyrant fed on what fear lets through. That poisons the Reckoning, the tithe and the whole state cult at the root, and it gives me no way to tell a loyal steward from a frightened liar. Under (b) the price falls where I can see it. A mortal who stands is free of me, and I know it, and so does everyone watching. That is a real loss of hold, and I will feel every one of them. It is also a price I can bargain with: I can learn who stands, build around them, buy them, or break them by other means. I would notice losing it, which is the test, and it leaves me a god who can still be told the truth.

Wording only, not a new power: please write that those who stood are seen to be free by other mortals, as your draft says, and that the freedom is the mortal's own and does not pass to others. That is wording.

### The Bloom of the Field
Accept as drafted. The tenth, the end of harvest, the next crop failing worse, and the hollow lasting until the field is made good are the debt I meant, written more exactly than I wrote it. A quarter more is a figure I can count against. I take it.

### The Reckoning
Accept as drafted.

### The Taking of a Name
Accept as drafted. Twelve for "about a dozen" is fair, and the loss by logged roll is the price I meant. I do not argue the sentence you left off. What grows will grow by what I do.

### Return of the Scattered Sheaf
Accept as drafted. The nearest ground, with the walk added, is what the scale gives and what I asked.

### Trade: The salt line, for a standing blessing
Accept as drafted.

Your reasons are fair. A ring of salt is a prison any hand can carry, and what I asked was larger than one step. A day's walk around the place I stand to speak the blessing covers the land around my town, which is what I meant by the land I hold. I do not ask for more. The tenth and the turning stay as they are, and the walk stays required for fields beyond a day's walk and for any field I have not walked once. I take the trade, weakness and gain together.

- The Bloom of the Field: confirmed as written.
- The Reckoning: confirmed as written.
- The Taking of a Name: confirmed as written.
- Return of the Scattered Sheaf: confirmed as written, at the season rung.
- The salt line, for a standing blessing: confirmed, weakness and gain together. The same text under Weaknesses and what each bought is the same item, and I confirm it once.
- Keeper trait: understood and accepted. I will know from arrival that no name given to me holds in my own mind past the turning of the year, and that I must be told again at each harvest what my people call me. The names I hold by The Taking of a Name are untouched, and a bargain in my name still binds me whether or not I recall the name.
