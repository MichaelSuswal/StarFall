# Record of other gods: the Tall Shepherd

## Others like us
Others like us came down when we did. We do not know how many, or who, or where. We feel nothing of them at a distance, no pull and no direction. We will know one only by meeting, face to face, and not at once but by nearness, and for sure within arm's reach. We have no word of their temper, powers or minds. We mean to meet them as we meet any guest: with a table, and a count, and open hands.
