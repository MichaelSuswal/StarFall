# Goals: the Tall Shepherd, on arrival

1. Find wide country with grass and timber and a river, and the herds on it, and count them. Learn at once whether the count is whole. The Shepherd has counted short for an age and wants, more than most things, to count and find none missing.
2. Find a people to lead, and take them in. Set a table, fill it, and see that someone is glad of the Shepherd. Feed them first, then ask them everything.
3. Build a walled town in the edge country and give it a written law: who sits where, what is owed, how the tithe is kept, how the hunt is kept lawful. Have the law written exactly, down to the count.
4. Keep the autumn gathering and the great hunt as the heart of the year, and make the temple and the tally part of it.
5. Meet the neighbors before judging them: the old kingdoms, the strange orderly peoples, the travelers with carts of tools. Learn their laws and trade with them. Meet, not conquer.
6. Never be bound again. Learn what can hold a god, and where chains and sealed doors and pits are, and have them seen, named and walked around. Distrust any offer that comes with a rope.
7. Find a companion, beast or person or neither, who has been waiting and is glad to be found. Find the place of herds uncounted and an open door at the end of the count.
