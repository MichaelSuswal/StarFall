# Power card: the Tall Shepherd

Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-23. Confirmed by the god in its round 2 reply.

## Form

It has no name it can remember. It feels there was one, a short one, and that it was said at a table. Those who do not know it call it the Tall Shepherd. It answers to that gladly and says "we" of itself, or "the Shepherd," never "I."

A giant, about four times the height of a tall man and broad in proportion, built like a bull elk stood upright: heavy shoulders, long arms, a deep chest. Its hide-brown skin is thick and weathered, with a pelt of coarse hair along the back and forearms. The face is broad and mild, with a heavy brow, wide dark eyes that look at a thing for a long time, and a great spread of antlers that grows from the brow like a bare tree in winter. The antlers are real horn, branching, and shed and regrow with the seasons. Its senses are those of a large grazing beast: a wide field of sight, a keen nose, ears that turn. It walks upright at a long unhurried stride and can drop to a run that shakes the ground. It speaks in a deep slow voice, and when it is excited the words tumble.

One body; a god is in one place at a time.

What breaks it: Flesh, and enough of it. A giant is hard to wound but not proof: many spears driven together, a great fall, fire taken into the hide in numbers, a beast bigger than it. Enough hurt breaks the body. What holds it: a chain it cannot break, heavy and counted, set on it with rite; a pit or a closed stone place with a sealed door; a net of living rope. What shuts it out: a door sealed by its own spoken promise, kept to the letter. It cannot be starved or drowned out of the world, and it does not die.

Knowing another god: at a distance the god feels nothing of another god, no pull and no direction. It knows one only by meeting it. The knowing comes on slowly, by nearness alone, and one who works at seeming mortal may hold it off for a while. Within arm's reach it is sure, and no god passes for a mortal at a touch. It tells what stands there and nothing of its temper, its powers or its mind.

Keeper trait: Any count it makes by eye and finger, of its people, its guests or its stores, comes to it first as one short, and it must count again before it believes the number. Reach: counts made by eye and hand only. The Count of the Herd gives its true number at once and is untouched; and since the god knows to count again before it speaks a figure, the trait puts no false number in its mouth and does not bear on the weakness on this card.

When the body is broken: see Return to the Herd-ground, under Powers.

### Definitions

None beyond the words of the powers below.

### Things carried

A long ash staff, seven feet long, with a fork at the top (ordinary). A horn of an aurochs, banded with bronze, for gathering a crowd (ordinary; it is only loud). A cord of knotted leather for counting heads (ordinary; a tally, nothing more). A wide belt and a skin-cloak. None of these does more than an ordinary object.

## Powers

### The Count of the Herd
Status: main
Description: The god names one kind of beast aloud and waits, and it knows the number of that kind within a day's walk of where it stands and where each lies, and whether a stranger or a predator has come among them or one has been taken. It counts tame beasts, wild ones, and a kind no one has a name for, as a number it cannot name.
Drawback: No hunt on the count. A kind the god has counted may not be hunted or slaughtered by the god, or by a hunt or slaughter it orders or asks for by name, until the second dawn after the count ends; and in that time the god may tell no one what the count showed in order to guide a killing. A herdsman's own cull, and a slaughter already fixed by standing law, are not the god's word.
Limits settled at creation:
- One kind for one count. A few heads take a breath; a great herd takes a long quiet, up to an hour.
- While it counts it stands rooted, deaf to speech and blind to anything that is not a beast, and it cannot stop a count once begun. These are conditions of use, written here and not as the price.
- Beasts only: nothing of people or of gods. A god in a beast's body counts as one head of the kind it looks like, and nothing marks it. A person in a beast's shape counts as a beast.
- It keeps the number afterward, not the knowledge of what has changed since.
- Under stone or deep water it counts as far as the stone or the surface, and no further.
- It shows what is there, never why.
- More than ordinary craft: a thing in a beast's shape that is not a beast, or a beast made or held by more than ordinary craft, may count wrong or not at all. Unruled until it happens.
- Light and dark, being watched: the same by night; a giant standing still, speaking a word.
- Use: as often as it likes.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0028, L-0015, L-0014.
Rulings:
Trades: improved by the weakness No false number (see Weaknesses taken): In the same count, with no more stillness, The Count of the Herd also tells the number of that kind that are hurt, sick or with young. Settled at creation, Night 2.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Calling
Status: main
Description: By voice, with the horn or without, the god calls one kind of beast, and those of that kind within a day's walk that are free to come, come to it: tame ones to follow, wild ones to gather near, calm, as at a watering place at dusk. A called beast will not hurt the god or its people while called. The god holds them gathered until it releases them with a word.
Drawback: While any herd is called, the beasts' hunger and want press on the god: it can hold its mind on nothing else until they are fed or released, and they must be fed from its people's stores or the land. And the call may not be betrayed: if beasts are killed while called, by the god, or at its word (a hunt it has named aloud before the kill), or by its law's own hunt, that kind will not answer the call again for a year. A kill by one acting against its law, by a beast, or by another people is not the god's betrayal, unless the god or its officers allowed it, used it or took the meat.
Limits settled at creation:
- It gives no command. A beast whose fear grows stronger than the call bolts: fire, a predator among prey.
- A beast under a hand does not come: ridden, led, yoked, tethered or penned. It strains and stays.
- One kind at a time, and no other until the first is released.
- Predators come gentle and are still predators. Called beasts are not shielded from anyone else's spear.
- A kind with no name is called only if the god knows it exists. Peoples who wear beast-forms are not called.
- Other gods: a god in a beast's body is not called. A herd another god holds by its own working is unruled until it happens.
- Being watched: the call is heard as far as a horn carries, and the coming of the herds is plain.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, mind (of people).
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0029.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Speech with Beasts
Status: main
Description: The god understands what beasts mean to say, and says to them what it means, as a person talks to a person. Their meaning is simple: fear, want, warning, welcome, grief, the way to water.
Drawback: The kind, for good. The god can never again eat the flesh of any kind of beast it has spoken with, and cannot sit at the head of a table where that kind is the dish.
Limits settled at creation:
- A beast knows what a beast knows, can be wrong, and can lie as beasts do. Insects and fish say little.
- It shows what a beast saw, never why it happened.
- The god chooses whether to speak; it cannot be forced to.
- A person under a beast's shape speaks as a person. A god in a beast's body is spoken with as the god chooses to answer.
- Not applicable: light and dark, distance, falling, carrying, water.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0030, L-0014.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Standing
Status: main
Description: When the god wills it, its antlers kindle with a pale steady glow, visible across a valley at dusk or a plain by day. Beasts in sight of it still and turn to it, and people who see it know that a god stands there.
Drawback: While the antlers burn, every wild thing within a mile bolts or gathers, and no hunt can be made there. The glow cannot be held longer than a day and a night together; then the antlers ache, and the god must stand dim for as long as it burned.
Limits settled at creation:
- One who does not look is not moved. It gives no command, heals nothing and burns nothing.
- A hand held over the brow puts it out.
- Other gods: a god who sees the light sees a light. It knows what stands there only by nearness, as every god does.
- Being watched: it is made to be watched.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, water, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0014.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Return to the Herd-ground
Status: main
Description: When the god's body is broken, it wakes whole after a season in open grazing country, at the grazing ground nearest to where it broke on which a herd of a hundred beasts or more feeds. The walk from there to wherever it was needed is added.
Drawback: Each return costs the god one item of memory or habit, taken by a logged roll from its own record (what it holds of other gods, and its goals). The god does not choose it and is not told what went.
Limits settled at creation:
- The season rung of the standing scale, fixed now and never chosen at each return.
- A body held, chained or shut out is not broken, and this does not answer it.
- If no such herd feeds anywhere near, the nearest wide grass with the largest herd in reach serves. Fenced or walled grazing counts if beasts feed there.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Nothing disputed.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### No false number
The god cannot speak or write a number it knows to be false: a count, a measure, a sum, a tally, a price or a weight, in its own words or under oath. It may stay silent or refuse to answer. A guess it calls a guess, a number it does not know, a story, a song, a riddle, a boast that is plainly a boast, a number written by another, and a number it was told and repeats as hearsay, saying so, do not count. Nothing ends it. Anyone who learns of it can trust every figure the god gives and can read its silences. It bought, on The Count of the Herd: In the same count, with no more stillness, The Count of the Herd also tells the number of that kind that are hurt, sick or with young. Settled at creation, Night 2.
