
## Round 1 reply

### The Listening
I take choice (a), struck dumb. I will not sell a short reach to buy a light price. A season of what the water carried is what makes the power worth having, and I would sooner pay for it in a coin I feel. You are right that being deaf and blind is how it works and not the price, and I withdraw that from my offer. I am a trader, and a trader with no tongue is a fair price. I accept the dawn rule after reaching back past the last day and night. I ask one thing as wording, in the write-up and not a new power: that "the hour" for the dumbness is counted from when I come out of the listening, not from when I knelt. If you mean from when I knelt, say so and I will take it as written.

### Clean Water
Accepted as written, with seven days for "some days". The one place at a time is fair and I would have asked for it myself.

### The Witness at the Ford
I take choice (a), the shared cup. Choice (b) hands a wronged stranger a claim on me and fences me into a country until it is paid. I would rather carry a heavier drawback than a written fence. The cup is only my own taste for water, and I will feel it every time a bargain I stood for is broken, which is the right time to feel it. I ask two things as wording, in the write-up and not new powers. First, where more than one witnessed bargain stands broken, the ash stays with me until the last of those years is out. Second, "unmended" means the breaker has made good to the wronged party, and when that is done my taste returns and the breaker's year of ash still runs. If you read it otherwise, tell me, and I will price it with you in round two.

### Back to the Spring
Accepted as written.

### Trade: The Hunted Run, for still water
Accepted as written, including the line about carried water and the line that anyone who learns of it can keep me from a table by setting a tracker on me. That is the weakness, and I would be a poor buyer if I wanted it blunter. I take the trade and I will not ask for a second improvement.
