# Goals: Wend, on arrival

1. Find a people who live along water, and be of use to them without being owed. Lead them, move with them, and ask nothing of them in return but a fair trade.
2. Open a road out of every border and a market at the end of it. Wend wants customers, and wants to be able to leave.
3. Find clean cold water that tastes of nothing, and keep it clean where it can: a spring no god has used, whose water does something none of us can name.
4. Meet one of the strangers who carry an odd thing in a bag, and trade questions. Watch them closely and do not grow fond.
5. Learn the truth about why gods fall at all, if it can, and learn who else came down with it.
6. Stay out of reach of anything that follows without hurrying. Do not be found at a ford with its back to the water.
7. Quietly, and without saying so to itself: do not let anyone owe it enough to need it.
