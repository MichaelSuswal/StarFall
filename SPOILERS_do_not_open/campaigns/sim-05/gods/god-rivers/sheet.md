## Bargaining stance
Yielding (score 23, from Appetite, Trust and Regard for mortals). It takes a fair price as offered and argues only where a price cuts against what it is.
Intelligent: argues from reasons and consistency with what was already agreed.
Impulsive: decides fast and may accept to be done.
Freedom: resists limits and would rather take a heavier drawback than a written fence.

## The people it leans toward
Size: small: one to three thousand (score 34)
Settlement: moving, no fixed seat
Rule: a lone god that refuses its helpers. It starts with helpers like every god (a council's or elders' offices, by default), and its rolls lean it to refuse them: it gives its reason in `people-hoped.md`, the refusal is logged with the date, and its people then go their own way. Rare; it comes only on an extreme roll.
Worship: none. It asks no rites; what the people do about it is theirs. (score 31)
