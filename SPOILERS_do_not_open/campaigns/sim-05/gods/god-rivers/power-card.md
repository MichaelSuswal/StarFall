# Power card: Wend

Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-23. Confirmed by the god in its round 2 reply.

## Form

Wend. It is the only name it keeps, and it is not sure it was given it; it may have taken it from a bend in a stream it half remembers. Those who do not know it call it the Weigher, or the Old Man at the Ford.

An old man, lean and long, a little stooped, about the height of a tall mortal. Grey hair worn long and tied back with a strip of cord, a beard cut short and ragged. Skin weathered brown and fine lined like dried mud on a bank. Eyes the pale, clear colour of shallow water over stone, which mortals find hard to hold. Big, scarred hands, a ferryman's hands. It dresses in plain wool and oiled leather, boots made for wading. It smells faintly of cold stone and wet reeds. Senses are keen in the matter of water: it hears a spring under ground, tastes a river's health, feels the weight of weather coming up the valley. Its eyesight at distance is ordinary. It moves at a mortal walking pace, never hurrying by habit, though when frightened it moves in a sudden rush it later regrets. It wades well and swims like an otter, and is easy in the cold.

One body; a god is in one place at a time.

What breaks it: The body is flesh and can be wounded: a spear, an axe, a bad fall, fire, a crushing blow, or being cut open, and a blow hard enough breaks it past use. It can be held: bound with rope or iron, caged, or frozen into ice, or buried under stone or silt. It can be shut out: walled away from every water it could drink or stand in, or carried far from any spring or river by those who mean to keep it dry. Cold and drowning do not hurt it; being kept from running water for a long time dulls it and slows its mind.

Knowing another god: at a distance the god feels nothing of another god, no pull and no direction. It knows one only by meeting it. The knowing comes on slowly, by nearness alone, and one who works at seeming mortal may hold it off for a while. Within arm's reach it is sure, and no god passes for a mortal at a touch. It tells what stands there and nothing of its temper, its powers or its mind.

Keeper trait: Every mortal it has done a kindness to stays in its mind whole and clear, face and voice, whether it wishes it or not. Reach: it is memory only. It tells the god nothing of where those mortals are or how they fare, it is no item the return's roll can take, and it touches no power or price on this card.

When the body is broken: see Back to the Spring, under Powers.

### Definitions

None beyond the words of the powers below.

### Things carried

A walking staff of ash, pole length, good for wading and probing a ford. A flat wooden drinking bowl. A coil of rope. A small leather pouch of river pebbles it keeps for no reason it can name. All ordinary.

## Powers

### The Listening
Status: main
Description: When the god kneels or stands in a spring, stream or river and gives the water its whole attention, it hears what that water has carried: sounds made at the water's edge along the reach that lies within a day's flow upstream of where it stands. From the last day and night it hears the words. Older than that, back to about a season, it hears only the kind of sound: a crossing of many feet, a quarrel, a boat, something poured in.
Drawback: Struck dumb. After a listening the god has no speech at all for as long as it listened, and never less than an hour, counted from when it comes out of the listening. After reaching back past the last day and night, it has none until the next dawn. It can hear and walk and make signs. It cannot bargain.
Limits settled at creation:
- Sounds only, never thoughts. It hears words, not whether they are true.
- Running or rising water only, and only the channel it stands in, upstream. Frozen water gives nothing until it thaws.
- While it listens it is deaf and blind to everything else and does not hear its own name. It comes out over about a long breath for each hour it listened; a touch on the shoulder rouses it sooner. These are conditions of use, written here and not as the price.
- One voice out of a crowded ford takes much time.
- It shows what was heard, never why a thing was done.
- Other gods: a god's voice at the water is heard as any voice is. The listening does not say that a god spoke.
- Being watched: an old man standing in a stream with his eyes shut.
- More than ordinary craft: water made or held by more than ordinary craft may give nothing or give wrong. Unruled until it happens.
- Use: one channel at a time, as often as it likes.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0018, L-0014.
Rulings:
Trades: improved by the weakness The Hunted Run (see Weaknesses taken): The Listening works at still water as well: a well, a pond, a cistern, a bowl of rain. Still water gives only sounds made beside it where it lay; water carried from its place gives nothing from before it was moved. Settled at creation, Night 2.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Clean Water
Status: main
Description: Standing in a spring or at a river's edge with its hands in the water, the god makes that spring, or about a day's walk of that channel, run clear and free of what makes people sick, for as long as the god stays within a day's walk of the place.
Drawback: It takes the sickness into itself. Each cleansing leaves it feverish, weak and shivering for seven days, and it can cleanse no second place until it has recovered. When it goes more than a day's walk away, the water goes foul again over a season.
Limits settled at creation:
- The water, not the sick: it cures no person.
- A spring or a reach, never a great river or a sea. The spring must already run; it brings up no water.
- It does not undo a fouling while someone still stands there pouring it in.
- One place at a time.
- Other gods, more than ordinary craft: a fouling that is another god's work, or made by more than ordinary craft, is unruled until it happens.
- Being watched: plain to see, and so is the fever after.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Nothing disputed.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Witness at the Ford
Status: main
Description: A bargain sworn aloud over running water, with the god standing present as witness and having heard it in full, is marked. Whoever of the sworn breaks it finds that all running water tastes of ash and iron to them for a full year, and it greys in the cup as they drink, so that those who watch them drink can see it.
Drawback: The shared cup. While any bargain the god witnessed stands broken and unmended, all water tastes of ash and iron to the god as well, until that breaker's year is out; with more than one broken, until the last of those years is out. A bargain is mended when the breaker has made good to the wronged party: the god's taste then returns, and the breaker's own year of ash still runs. For that time the god loses its taste for a river's health and for clean water.
Limits settled at creation:
- It forces no one to keep a bargain. It marks the one who does not.
- In person only, over running water, the whole bargain heard. Never by messenger.
- A bargain sworn freely holds even if unfair on its face. One sworn under plain force is not marked.
- The god cannot say false what was sworn, or deny that it witnessed. Once it has agreed to witness it must be present. These are conditions, not the price.
- The mark judges the words as spoken, not what either side meant.
- Other gods: a god that swears before the witness is unruled until it happens.
- Use: as many bargains as it will stand for.
- Not applicable: light and dark, distance, falling, carrying.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0019, L-0014.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Back to the Spring
Status: main
Description: When the god's body is broken, it is gone for a season and then walks whole out of the spring or river head nearest to where it broke. The walk from there to wherever it was needed is added.
Drawback: Each return costs the god one item of memory or habit, taken by a logged roll from its own record (what it holds of other gods, and its goals). The god does not choose it and is not told what went.
Limits settled at creation:
- The season rung of the standing scale, fixed now and never chosen at each return.
- A body held (bound, caged, frozen in, buried) or shut out is not broken, and this does not answer it.
- No set ground; the nearest serves.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Nothing disputed.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The Hunted Run
When something the god knows or believes to be following its trail on purpose (a mortal, a god or a beast) has kept after it for a full day, the god cannot stand and parley, bargain or keep an appointment. It must keep moving away by day and night and will enter no dwelling, market, ford crossing or place where it could be cornered, until the pursuer stops, loses it for a day and a night, dies, openly gives up, or catches it. Caught, the compulsion ends and it is held as the pursuer wills. It may send word ahead as it flees and may run toward its own people. A crowd on the same road, a messenger, one who has only asked to speak with it, or a pursuit it does not know of, do not count. Anyone who learns of it can keep the god from any table by setting a patient tracker on it. It bought, on The Listening: The Listening works at still water as well: a well, a pond, a cistern, a bowl of rain. Still water gives only sounds made beside it where it lay; water carried from its place gives nothing from before it was moved. Settled at creation, Night 2.
