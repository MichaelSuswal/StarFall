# Negotiation: god-fate, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

### The Weighing
Accepted as drafted. The price is one I can name and live with: a bargainer who cannot slip a term is a bargainer whose word is worth something, and that is the good I mean to sell. I take the limits as written, including that another god in the bargain is ruled when it happens.

### The Bill
Accepted in part, with one counter on the price rule.

The cap of seven open bills, one per mortal: accepted. It is a limit I can write exactly.

Forgiving: I choose (b), forgiving at a cost. A creditor who can never forgive is a creditor who makes justice cruel to the small, and the person owed something large is often the one who cannot pay. I will bear the cost of no new bill until the next turn of a season each time I forgive.

The price rule: I counter, on two of the four things the draft closes. A name and a season of sleep are things a mortal gives with its own hands and days.
1. A season of sleep: the mortal binds itself to labor through the nights of a season, keeping watch or work in place of sleeping. That is its own days and its own body, like any labor. I ask that it be open as a price, written as a promise of nights of work, collected as labor.
2. A name: the mortal gives up the use of its name, so that it answers to a new one in its own people's mouths and takes the old one up again only when the bill is paid. That is a thing it does with its own days and speech, like a service. I ask that it be open as a price, written as a promise to go unnamed for a stated span, and not as a taking of the name out of the world.
A memory and a year of fortune I concede. Those are not things a mortal can hand across a table, and I withdraw them from my list. If the Keeper refuses both my counters, I accept the rule as drafted, because the goods, labor, service, tooth and owned thing that remain are enough to run a creditor's trade. I argue the two because they are the prices my Day 1 answer named, and the old tollhouse bargains I expect to find are likely written in them.

### Clean Rot
Accepted as drafted. A season is about ninety days from the working, as the Keeper writes it, and I take that reading. The price is honest and I would notice losing it.

### The Return to the Marsh Edge
I choose (a), the nearest marsh edge to where the body was broken. My text said both the nearest and the place where it fell, and then that I set no ground. The first and the last are what I mean, and "the place where it fell" was loose wording that I withdraw. I ask no ground of my own, because a set ground is a place I could be found and called back to, and I will not be called back. The walk from that ground to wherever I was needed is added, as the standing scale says. The rung is a season, fixed now for good. I accept the memory price.

### Trade: The offer heard out
Accepted as drafted: the weakness as written, and the improvement the Keeper offers in place of the one I asked. I take that The Weighing also tells me, for each party, whether that party believes it can keep the terms as stated. The Keeper is right that I asked to read the world and the future, and the scales cannot reach them. What a party believes of itself is a thing I can weigh, and for a bargainer it is most of what I want to know: who is promising what they cannot carry, and does not yet know it.
I ask for one wording line, in the write-up and not a new power: that the hold on me lasts only while an offer is truly being made and stated, so that a mortal who keeps repeating the same offer with no change to the terms or the price is not making a new offer, and I may leave when the first has been heard to its end. If the Keeper reads the weakness as drafted to mean the pin can be kept up by any offer, however false or empty, I take it that way and keep the trade.

## Round 2 reply

Confirmed. Each final text is what I meant, and I raise nothing further.

### The Weighing
Confirmed as written.

### The Bill
Confirmed as written. Both counters are granted as I put them: nights of work through a season as labor, and answering to another name for a stated span as a service. A memory, a year of fortune and a name taken out of the world stay closed, as I conceded. I keep forgiving at a cost, the cap of seven, and one bill per mortal.

### Clean Rot
Confirmed as written.

### The Return to the Marsh Edge
Confirmed as written: a season, the nearest marsh edge to where the body was broken, no set ground, one item of memory or habit by a logged roll, and the walk added.

### Trade: The offer heard out, for one thing more
Confirmed as written, including the wording that the same offer said again with no change to its terms or price is not a new offer, and that a changed offer is. I know the weakness lets anyone who can keep a changing offer going hold me in one place, and I accept that. I take the improvement as written: The Weighing also tells me, for each party, whether that party believes it can keep the terms.

### Keeper trait
Confirmed. When my name is called sharply, as one calls to heel, my body starts one step toward the caller before I can stop it. It compels no service and no answer, it is not an offer made in form, and it touches no power. I know it from arrival, and I understand it has no price.
