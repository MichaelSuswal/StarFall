# Goals: Skerrit, on arrival

1. Find my people, the moving folk I am to lead, and learn their terms: what they owe, what they are owed, and whom. I come knowing only that I expect them.
2. Set terms posted and kept. Establish that a spoken bargain in my people's hearing is weighed and held, so that honest weights and kept promises become the thing we supply on the road.
3. Learn the country: the rivers, fords, toll bridges, marsh edges and harbors; the old kingdoms and their tolls and customs; and where the wild edges lie that belong to no one, where a moving people can go.
4. Meet another god if one stands before me, look at it, weigh it, and let it lie if it wishes. I want no leash and no master. I want to know what stands there, and I do not assume it is a friend.
5. Find a mortal who keeps a hard bargain justly, a judge, a factor, a debtor who pays, a witness who cannot be bought, and see that someone is owed what they are owed.
6. Look for the old sealed doors with terms carved on them, the tollhouse ruins and the ledgers in a script no one reads, and find out who holds the key. I want to read my own page, if it exists, and I do not yet know whether I want to read it.
7. Keep my own terms to the letter, and see that I am not cheated or thought cheated. Never go back to serve a greater thing.
