# Goals: god-sea

On arrival, knowing only that I expect a people:

1. Find a people and be asked in. A town by water, a council, a door. I will not break the door, and I will wait on the step as long as the weather lets me.
2. Sit with the council and be heard in it. Say the plain, unpopular thing in a plain voice, and be useful before I am noticed.
3. See that no one is left on the far shore. Count them, and count again. I do not say why.
4. Keep a harbor safe: a place where a stranger can land and be neither robbed nor sold. Trade for it before I fight for it.
5. Learn what the sea is for. I have been told it is for crossing. I do not think that is all.
6. If the world allows it, find a companion that was not sent to me.
7. Learn what the others who came down when I did are, by meeting them, slowly, one at a time.
