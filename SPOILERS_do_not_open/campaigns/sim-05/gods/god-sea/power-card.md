# Power card: Ysmer

Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-23. Confirmed by the god in its round 2 reply.

## Form

Ysmer, which it holds the way one holds a coin found in an old coat: it may be its own. Those who do not know it will call it the Late Guest, or the Tall Stranger, or whatever a harbor makes of a man who arrives after the boats are hauled up.

A man of middle years and a little more than a man's height, lean, weathered, grey in the beard, with large slow hands and the patient face of someone who has waited at many doors. Eyes the grey-green of water over sand. Dressed as a traveler in plain wool and a salt-stiffened coat. Hearing and sight as a keen mortal's. He walks evenly, as on a deck, even on dry ground, and stands with his feet a little apart. The wrongness is this: his shadow does not follow the light. It falls toward the sea, wherever the sea lies, at every hour, and at night, away from any water, it lies still and points the way home. Few will see it unless they are looking at the ground.

One body; a god is in one place at a time.

What breaks it: A blow of a mortal's weapon wounds him as it would a man; enough of them, or a drowning that holds him down, or a fall, breaks the body. Fire, crushing and long cold break it. Being held in a place (chains, a locked room, a pit) holds him without breaking him. Being barred from the shore for long hurts nothing, but he is poorer for it.

Knowing another god: at a distance the god feels nothing of another god, no pull and no direction. It knows one only by meeting it. The knowing comes on slowly, by nearness alone, and one who works at seeming mortal may hold it off for a while. Within arm's reach it is sure, and no god passes for a mortal at a touch. It tells what stands there and nothing of its temper, its powers or its mind.

Keeper trait: A debt owed to him he cannot simply let lie: to leave it uncollected he must say aloud, to the debtor's face, that he lets it go. Reach: his own claims only. It binds no one else, forces no collecting, and touches no power or price on this card.

When the body is broken: see Washed Up Again, under Powers.

### Definitions

None beyond the words of the powers below.

### Things carried

A coil of rope, a flask of ordinary water, a knife for bread, a small iron lamp. All ordinary and none of them does more than its kind.

## Powers

### Sea-Step
Status: main
Description: The god walks upon the surface of water, still or moving, salt or fresh, and goes beneath it, breathing and seeing there as in clear air, for as long as it wishes. Beneath the surface it goes only as deep as daylight reaches.
Drawback: The taste alone. For a day after it leaves the water, counted from the last moment any part of it is in the water, everything it tastes is salt.
Limits settled at creation:
- Its own body only. Others are not carried; a rope or a hand held out does not bear them.
- Water of a body's size or more: not a cup or a puddle. Ice and mud are ground.
- It moves at its walking pace, on the water and under it. Waves, surf and current act on it as on a man on ground that moves, and a sea that would break a man on rocks can break it.
- Light and dark: it sees beneath as in clear air where there is light; in dark water it sees as in a dark room.
- Being watched: a man walking on water is plain to anyone who looks.
- Other gods, more than ordinary craft: water held or made by another god, or by more than ordinary craft, is unruled until it happens.
- A stay in one stretch counts as one use.
- Not applicable: falling, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0023, L-0014.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Reading the Water
Status: main
Description: When it chooses to read, the god knows the state of the standing and running water and the sea within a day's walk of where it stands: the set of the currents, the depth, what moves in it or on it (shoals, boats, large animals) in rough number, what lies drowned in it as a bulk and a place, the weather coming off the water for the next day, and whether a crossing is safe. It is a knowing and not a seeing: no faces, no speech.
Drawback: While it reads, and afterward for as long as it read and never less than an hour, its ears are full of surf and it cannot make out spoken words.
Limits settled at creation:
- It shows what is there, never why, and nothing of intent. Of a drowned thing it knows that something lies there and how large, not what it is or how it came there.
- A thing of the water that no one has a name for is known as something large that moves, and no more.
- Rain in the air is not read.
- Other gods: it shows no god as a god. A god on or in the water is one more thing that moves.
- More than ordinary craft: water or a drowned place made or held by more than ordinary craft may read wrong or not at all. Unruled until it happens.
- Light and dark, being watched: the same by night; nothing shows on the god but its deafness.
- Its deafness adds to any other price it is under at the time; neither cancels the other.
- Use: as often as it likes. A reading is one act of attention, begun and ended at will; nothing is owed for water it merely stands beside.
- Not applicable: falling, carrying, mind.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Nothing disputed.
Rulings:
Trades: improved by the weakness The Unasked Threshold (see Weaknesses taken): Reading the Water also knows the wells and the water under the ground within the same day's walk: where it lies, how deep, whether it is sweet. Settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0024.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Washed Up Again
Status: main
Description: When the god's body is broken, the sea returns it over a season, cast up whole on the shore of the sea nearest to where it broke, with salt on its lips. The walk from that shore to wherever it was needed is added.
Drawback: Each return costs the god one item of memory or habit, taken by a logged roll from its own record (what it holds of other gods, and its goals). The god does not choose it and is not told what went.
Limits settled at creation:
- The season rung of the standing scale, fixed now and never chosen at each return.
- A body held, shut out or exiled is not broken, and this does not answer it.
- No set ground; the nearest shore of the sea serves.
- Text, limits and price settled at creation, Night 2. Nothing disputed.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The Unasked Threshold
The god cannot enter any dwelling, hall, tent with a flap, ship's cabin or walled yard that mortals hold as their own, or the door of a walled town's council hall, unless someone who speaks for the house, of any age, has asked it in by word or gesture. An invitation holds for that house until the one who gave it takes it back aloud, and passes to no other house. Asking to be asked is no invitation; a general welcome given by one with the right to give it is. A town's wall and an open gate, a place with no door, a ruin no one keeps, an open deck, a harbor, a field, a road, and any place it is carried into against its will, do not count. Nothing ends it. Anyone who learns of it can keep the god out of any room by saying nothing. It bought, on Reading the Water: Reading the Water also knows the wells and the water under the ground within the same day's walk: where it lies, how deep, whether it is sweet. Settled at creation, Night 2. Ledger: L-0024.
