## Round 1 reply

I take the prices as the Keeper has written them, with one choice made in each place I was given one, and one clarification of wording. I argue only where a price cuts against what I am.

### Sea-Step
Accept, with choice (b), the taste alone.

Not (a). The rime in full is a price I would feel, and I do not deny the Keeper's reasons for offering it. But it makes me a thing that cannot arrive unremarked for a day after every crossing, wherever I stand, and arriving unremarked is how I mean to come to a door. A stranger who has dripped salt into the road is a stranger the whole street has already decided about before he knocks. A guest tests a house by asking little of it; I would not wish to be tested first.

I do not need the dark deep. What I would do with the sea is keep a harbor, and a harbor lies in daylight: the wall, the moorings, the hull, the shoal at the mouth. The lit water is the part that serves a town, and I am content to leave the rest of the sea its own. The price I offered stands as I offered it. Every loaf I am given for a day after will taste of the tide, and I would miss that.

Wording only, in the write-up and not a new power: a day after "leaves the water" is counted from the last moment any part of me is in the water, so that wading a ford and stepping out is a use, and a long stay is still one use.

### Reading the Water
Accept, with the longer deafness.

The price is fair and I took the hour as the least I could offer. A long watch over a harbor costing a long silence at the table is a cost I would notice, and a host who cannot hear should not sit at the head of it. I will learn to read in short breaths, and to read before the council sits and not during it.

Wording only, in the write-up and not a new power: "as long as it read" means the time I give my attention to the water, from the moment I choose to read until I choose to stop. A reading is one act of attention, and I may end it at will. I do not read without choosing to, and the deafness is not owed for water I merely stand beside.

### Washed Up Again
Accept as written. The season rung stands, fixed now and for good, and the item taken by roll from my record is not mine to pick. I would notice not knowing why something had stopped mattering to me, and that is the right price.

### Trade: The Unasked Threshold, for one reach more
Accept, with choice (b), ground water.

I take the weakness as written, and I name what I know of it. Anyone who learns it can keep me out of any room by saying nothing, and nothing in the world will end it. That is a price a host would feel in a guest, and I would not trade it for less.

I asked for two reaches and am given one. I take the wells and the water under the ground. A walled town drinks from its wells and fears for them in a hard year or a siege, and a keeper of a town should know where the sweet water lies, how deep, and whether it has turned. The weather in the clouds I leave closed. Reading the Water already tells me the weather coming off the water for the next day, and that is the part a harbor needs.

## Round 2 reply

I confirm every final text as written. Nothing is wrong with any of it.

Sea-Step: confirmed. The lit-water depth, the taste alone, and the day counted from the last moment any part of me is in the water are what I chose and asked for.

Reading the Water: confirmed. The longer deafness, the reading as one act of attention that I begin and end at will, and nothing owed for water I merely stand beside, are as I understood them. The limit now reads "Rain in the air is not read," which is right, since ground water is the reach the trade bought.

Washed Up Again: confirmed. The season rung, the nearest shore, and the one item taken by roll are as agreed.

The Unasked Threshold: confirmed, with the one reach it bought, the wells and the water under the ground. I take it that the trade is closed for the rest of creation, as stated, and that I have no further trade to make.

Keeper trait: confirmed. A debt owed to me I cannot let lie unless I say aloud, to the debtor's face, that I let it go. It reaches only my own claims, binds no one else, forces no collecting, and touches no power or price. It is a fact about me I know from arrival, and it sits true beside my habit of counting what I am owed.
