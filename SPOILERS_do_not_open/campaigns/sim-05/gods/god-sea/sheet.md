# Sheet: god-sea

## Leans
1. Mind: 90 (intelligent 1, clever 100). Leans hard toward clever.
2. Body: 96 (strong 1, enduring 100). Leans hard toward enduring.
3. Presence: 55 (revealed 1, hidden 100). Sits between revealed and hidden.
4. Temper: 54 (patient 1, impulsive 100). Sits between patient and impulsive.
5. Regard for mortals: 10 (shepherd 1, sovereign 100). Leans hard toward shepherd.
6. Order: 37 (law 1, freedom 100). Leans a little toward law.
7. Making: 9 (builder 1, breaker 100). Leans hard toward builder.
8. Company: 100 (rolled 98, +25) (solitary 1, gathering 100). Leans hard toward gathering.
9. Appetite: 32 (content 1, hungry 100). Leans toward content.
10. Trust: 36 (open 1, wary 100). Leans a little toward open.

## Seed
The wandering stranger who tests hospitality and punishes its absence (Zeus Xenios, Odin Grimnir, the Elohim at Mamre)

## Domain, form, virtue, flaw, wound, voice
Domain: the sea.
Form: human-seeming, visibly wrong in one way (too tall, no shadow, wrong joints).
Virtue: patience.
Flaw: greed.
Wound: it failed to save a people once before.
Voice: never says a thing directly, always sideways.

## Bargaining stance
Yielding (score 26, from Appetite, Trust and Regard for mortals). It takes a fair price as offered and argues only where a price cuts against what it is.
Clever: hunts for wordings, loopholes and the case nobody priced.
Enduring: bargains for duration and for what survives.

## The people it leans toward
Size: the usual number, about five thousand (score 47)
Settlement: a walled town, gathered close
Rule: a council of elders or chieftains. The people is ruled by a council of its own (elders, headmen, chieftains); the god is heard there, and the council's helpers carry out what is agreed.
Worship: none. It asks no rites; what the people do about it is theirs. (score 29)

## Name
Ysmer, which it holds the way one holds a coin found in an old coat: it may be its own. Those who do not know it will call it the Late Guest, or the Tall Stranger, or whatever a harbor makes of a man who arrives after the boats are hauled up.

## Traits (own)
### The Keeper of the Door
Reconciles Company (gathering), Regard for mortals (shepherd), Trust (a little open), and Presence (between hidden and revealed). He wants a full table and a stranger at it, and he will not stand in the middle of the room to say so. He sits at the edge of the council and lets the hearth do the speaking. He opens first and checks the knife after.
### The Slow Tide
Reconciles Mind (clever), Body (enduring), Temper (between patient and impulsive), Order (a little toward law), Making (builder) and Appetite (content). He prefers the long answer that survives to the quick answer that wins, and builds what will still stand in a hundred winters. But now and then the patience tips, and he acts at once, usually to keep a door from closing, and thinks afterward.

## Keeper trait
A debt owed to him he cannot simply let lie: to leave it uncollected he must say aloud, to the debtor's face, that he lets it go. Reach: his own claims only. It binds no one else, forces no collecting, and touches no power or price on this card.

## Season it wakes into
Late autumn.
