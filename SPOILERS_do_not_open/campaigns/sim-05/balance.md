# Balance: sim-05

A rough weight from 1 to 10 for each god's total power. Roughly equal, not exactly. The band aimed for is 5 to 6.

## First weights: main powers, drawbacks and trades (2.6)

- The player's god: 6. One large power (The whole map, with round counts bought by a weakness) at a real price, blind beyond; three small to middling ones; a light weakness. The DM's lean note: about even at landing, below even in its first seasons (it lands holding nothing), leaning up as its holdings grow, since nothing but its legs limits what it claims. Read here as 5 at landing and 6 over a long game; written 6.
- `god-rivers`: 5. A listening that reaches a season back along a river, at the price of its speech; a clean spring at the price of a week's fever; a witness's mark; a weakness that lets any patient tracker keep it from a table.
- `god-roads`: 6. Live voices at a day's walk, sight through one by its trade; a force power against a band of thirty; a passage gift; going unremarked. Each paid for in its own body or its worship; a weakness that gives the way to any asker.
- `god-sea`: 3. Walking the lit water; a knowing of water for a day's walk, with ground water by its trade; a heavy weakness (no threshold unasked). It took the lighter price and the narrower power each time. The lowest card.
- `god-fate`: 5. A yes or no on a party's intent and belief; small turns of chance sold on bills, capped; drawing rot at a season's sickness. A light weakness.
- `god-beasts`: 5. A count and a call of beasts for a day's walk, both hedged against feeding a people without work; speech with beasts; a beacon. A weakness that binds its numbers to truth.
- `god-harvest`: 6. A blessing worth a quarter more grain, standing for a day's walk by its trade, which is the largest economic power in the campaign; a dread; a knowing of bargains; twelve held names. A large weakness (the salt line).
- `god-storms`: 8. The largest power in the campaign (weather over all it can see, with flood and gale), a sure killing stroke, hearing for a mile, an answer to rite. Real prices on each, and still above the band. Told so in its round 2 counter, before confirmation; it confirmed. Its card is frozen.

## Second weights: with hidden powers and knacks (2.7)

The sealed gifts are fitted toward the band: largest where a card is lowest, smallest where it is highest. No card was edited.

- The player's god: 6. Hidden powers modest (a taking-apart that loses no piece; one small thing kept alive), three knacks. Drawn from `mirror.md`. They add reach in craft and nothing in war or knowing.
- `god-rivers`: 6. A night in a year when no boat it touched sinks; a cup of water; three knacks.
- `god-roads`: 6. Kept small: a judgment remembered; a door that will not shut; two knacks.
- `god-sea`: 5. Lifted most: a lamp that brings in everything on the water for a day's sail; a hand that takes one mortal across; three knacks.
- `god-fate`: 6. A door that stays shut a season; a yes or no on any sum; two knacks.
- `god-beasts`: 6. An hour in a year when no ordinary binding holds it; a table where no blow falls; three knacks.
- `god-harvest`: 6. Kept small: one sealed store that keeps three years; a count of voices; two knacks.
- `god-storms`: 8. Kept smallest: one question to a storm not its own; a hearing no wind spoils; two knacks.

## What stands

`god-storms` stays noticeably stronger than the rest. It is a fact the world's politics will deal with (DESIGN 3.15): its people are not yet gathered, its hunger for rite halves it within a season of waking until a rite is taught, every great working is paid back on its own country, and each killing stroke costs it a season of sky. `god-sea` stays a little below the band and is the one god no door opens to unasked. No concern about the player's card goes to the DM.
