# World draft: sim-05

The first draft, from Night 1. Nothing here is placed. Placement, names and map ids come on Night 3. Vote counts and placements are in the keeper log only.

## Tone
Ordered, watchful, expectant. The light that suits it is the edge of the day: dawn and dusk, lamps, low sun. Its peoples count, weigh and keep tallies, hold bargains before witnesses, and keep a place for whoever arrives. Weather keeps its seasons and can be read.

## Climate and seasons
Four true seasons. Winters are hard enough to close the high passes and ice the edges of rivers; growing seasons are of fair length. Harsher with height and with distance from the sea, milder on the coasts. (Scale result 4.)

## The star's results that bear on the land
- A mature star: medium, strong rain; fading in the oldest places only; no descent that can be measured.
- The rain reaches 70 days' walk from the center, well past the edges of the sheet.
- At the center, under the star, the hoarder: a sleeping beast, visibly a beast.
- Hoarder and carriers are here. There are no thinners.
- The Remainder fell in godless country.
- Content: islands that moved. Temper of those above: mostly those who want the fall soon.

## Magic
Level, final: 9. A worker known by name in nearly every valley and district, never in all, and a school in the south. Simple at every level.
Feel and cost, one system: quick in the working, and it feels like borrowing. A craft, taught. Nothing is made; something near goes duller; the worker pays with what is most her own: names, faces, memories. Signs: warmth in the hands, a taste of iron or salt, prickling skin.
Local understandings, partly wrong, unplaced: the price can be fixed beforehand; the debt is owed to the teacher; it is a tide; it is only listening.

## The two rules
God-sense: a knowing only face to face. Nothing at a distance. In a meeting it comes slowly; one who works at it may pass for mortal for a while; within arm's reach it is sure. It tells what the other is and nothing more.
Bodies: one body.

## How much gods stand out
Scale result 7. Plainly more than any mortal worker, and known once met. Mortals look, and many kneel; they can still bargain with a god, refuse it and plan around it.

## The wild
Scale result 4. Familiar animals and many legends; a few nameless things, kept to the margins: deep water, reed beds and fogs, borderlands, high cold country.

## Land held and open
Scale result 5. About half the land outside the gods' countries is held by godless peoples, about half open, by area and by count of regions. Kingdoms rooted along rivers and coasts; wild between and at the edges.

## Age of the human story
Scale result 10. Thousands of years, ruins under ruins, records poor, stories that differ from place to place.

## Terrain the world must contain
Several great rivers with springs, braided reaches, fords and flood plains; high plains; steppe; hill country; mountains with ice and passes; grassland with forest edge; marsh; coast with harbors; sea.

## Placement wishes
- The player's god: river country opening onto high plains, flat ground with passes. A coast with a harbor is a second wish, not at the start.
- `god-rivers`: headwater springs in stone and the middle reaches of a river, with braids and fords.
- `god-roads`: the seams where steppe, hill country and river plain meet.
- `god-sea`: a coast with a walled harbor.
- `god-fate`: a wide slow river with ford towns, harbors within reach, marsh edges.
- `god-beasts`: grassland meeting forest, a river through it, hills behind.
- `god-harvest`: a black-soil flood plain on one wide river, hills behind, a ford, a little marsh.
- `god-storms`: mountains with ice, steppe and open river country below, a coast within reach.

## The underground
Underground: yes, mostly solid rock with hollows only, shallow (cellar depth to a few hundred feet): the door halls behind the sealed doors, and, as Night 3 places them, the great granary, the lower harbor and the capped spring.
No cave country, no people living below, no deep roads. The image file does not approve an underground sheet, so the layer is data only and its request is held.

## Pools

### Widespread features
- Fears: the lean year and the winter that outlasts its stores; sickness in grain, herd and camp; the broken word between peoples; high water.
- Scarcities: salt, first; good iron, second. Trust is a temper of the peoples: a kept word is rare and dear, bargains are witnessed, guest-custom is strict.
- Buried: old roads everywhere, paved, some along dry riverbeds, some leading to places no longer there; usable from the first day.
- Mortals the peoples tend to produce: keepers of true accounts who cannot be bought; those who say the plain unwelcome thing and are right; those who live between, guides and border captains.
- Travelers: few but known, a handful in a generation, each with one odd thing.
- Non-human peoples: one or two at most, few.
