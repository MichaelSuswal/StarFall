# Sealed past: the player's god

The rest of the story behind the outline. Tied to the common past (`gods-sealed/common-past.md`).

What it was. The Lineward of the Liberty of Asken, in Sarn Veth: the office that walked the boundary of that district every year with chain and stakes, and stood its stones back up, from one written record, the roll. Asken had no walls and no lord; its law was its line. Its people held that what lay inside the line stayed itself because the line was walked. The country remembered the Lineward as a tall grey walker who was never late and never wrong about a distance.

Why it stopped walking. The year after the drowning of Calder Hythe, the harbor's survivors came up the Marrow and asked to be taken inside the line. The roll said where the line ran, and the Lineward kept them outside it. On that same walk it found a stretch of the line, as the roll gave it, running through ground that had gone plain: the stones stood, and there was nothing particular left to bound. To walk that stretch was to put its name to a measure of nothing. It struck the stretch from the roll and walked a shorter line. That left the hamlet of Tharrow Green outside; its people went down to Brede and were entered there under another name. The next year there was another stretch, and the year after. Each cut was true to the ground and false to the roll. When the next cut would have gone through the middle of Asken, it destroyed the roll, so that no one could read how far the line had come in, and it did not walk again.

The stake. Before it destroyed the roll it cut the cuts into the first stake of the old line: one notch for each year it shortened the line, spaced by the length struck out. That is the worn stake in the coat. It is the only record left of where the line ran, and the god cannot read it.

What it lost and does not remember: what was inside the line (a people who trusted it to keep them themselves); that it kept the harbor's people out; that the roll was not lost but destroyed, and by whom; Tharrow Green.

How it may surface, by the god's own action only, and never told to it (ledger L-0013): the spacing of the notches, if it ever lays the stake against its own chain; a harbor god to whom it feels a debt it cannot place; a harvest god whose people, in another country, were once its own; the dream at the warm stone of a line of stones in empty ground; a carrier's song that holds the words "Tharrow Green".
