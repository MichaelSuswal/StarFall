# Unsaid wants: Ostrane (sim-05)

Written by the DM. Read by the DM, the Keeper and the checker.

## 2026-11-23, creation Day 2

Given by the player privately, unasked, with the first reply of the day. In the player's words:

> I want to hold this land long enough that someone else has to learn to hold it after me. I don't want to be the only one who can.

Confirmations: none yet. Given today.

Given by the player privately with the second reply of the day, beside the backstory outline. In the player's words:

> I'm afraid the lost roll isn't a hole in the past. I think it's a hole I made. I've said I don't remember why I stopped walking the line, and I'd rather not find out here. I would still like the Keeper to know it is there, and to decide whether it is ever found.

DM note: this holds a request not to be told something at the creation table (why the god stopped walking the line). Honored: the DM asks nothing further about it. Whether it is ever found is left to the Keeper, as asked.

Confirmations: none yet. Given today.

Given by the player privately with the third reply of the day, beside the list of things carried. In the player's words:

> The worn stake is the one thing I would not trade away, even for a power. Don't let the roll take it unless the Keeper has to.

DM note: "the roll" here is the logged roll that takes one item of memory or habit when the return is paid (power card, The return). The worn stake is on the card under Things carried as an ordinary possession.

Confirmations: none yet. Given today.

## 2026-11-23, creation Day 3

Given by the player privately, unasked, with the first reply of the day, beside the list of the five offices (the Reeve, the Marshal, the Warden of Marks, the Clerk of the Tally, the Judge of the Ford). In the player's words:

> I'd like at least one of the five offices to go to someone who disagrees with me, and who'll say so to my face. I don't think I can see my own flaws in the roll, and I'd rather someone else could.

DM note: this is a wish about who a helper is, left to the Keeper's Night 3 fitting of the cast. The player did not say which office. The player sets who a mortal is, never what that mortal will do. Nothing of it was said at the open table and the DM asks nothing further about it there.

Confirmations: none yet. Given today.

Given by the player privately, unasked, with the second reply of the day, beside the names of the people and the town. In the player's words:

> I'd like to learn the name of the place I lost, and I'd rather it never be told to me by anyone I'm trying to rule.

DM note: "the place I lost" is the place of the backstory outline, whose name the god has lost. The DM does not know that name and asks nothing further about it. Whether and how it is ever learned is the Keeper's.

Confirmations: none yet. Given today.

Given by the player privately, unasked, with the third reply of the day, beside the yes to the read-back. In the player's words:

> I'm leaving one thing deliberately open. I wrote the first line as a first action because I want the first thing I do to be a fair, plain, small act. I want the Carrow to see me keep a measure before they see me take any ground.

DM note: this qualifies an open conduct line. The open profile has the god start its first line (from the gap in the hills, as a wish) among its first actions; pacing a line closed is taking ground. That open line now carries the plain mark "check with the player before acting", with no reason given, and no agent runs unplayed time on it until the DM has checked with the player. The acts that keep a measure (asking to see the oldest disputed fence, setting the foot and the chain in iron beside the Carrow's own) carry no mark.

Confirmations: none yet. Given today.
