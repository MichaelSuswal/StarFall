# Sealed: the player's god

### The Laid-Out Work
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When the god takes a made thing apart with its own hands (a bridge, a wall, a mill, a boat, an engine of war), every piece comes away whole and is laid out in order, none is lost or mistaken, and the god, or any hands working under its eye, can put the thing back exactly, there or on other ground, in the time the taking down took.
Drawback: Once it has begun, it cannot leave a thing half apart: until the last piece is laid out it can turn to nothing else, not the chain, not a read of its claimed ground, not a parley, and it whistles the whole time.
Limits: Made things of ordinary craft, no larger than one crew could take down in a season. Never a living thing. A thing made or held by more than ordinary craft is unruled until it happens. It gives no knowledge of how to make such a thing new, only of how this one goes together. Weaker than The whole map; counted in the weight.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): the first time the god takes a made thing apart by hand under real need, with something riding on the pieces.
Fitted to: mirror line 1 (the broken thing taken fully apart, every piece in order, no part lost), made into a gift of this god; fitted to the sealed past, where the same hand took a line apart cut by cut. It may also serve what the god openly says it wants (roads, forts, an empire's works): allowed, narrow, and flagged in the neutrality check. It gives the next step, never an end.

### The Kept Leaven
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: One small living or burning thing that the god tends with its own hands once in each season (a hearth fire, a leaven, a graft, a hive, a seed line) does not fail between tendings from neglect, blight or a bad season.
Drawback: It is a tie: the season the god does not come back to tend it in person, the kept thing dies within the month, and the god can keep no other for a year.
Limits: One thing at a time, small enough to tend with two hands in an hour. A blow, a flood or a deliberate hand still ends it. It does not spread to the field or the herd around it.
Surfaces by (never scheduled): a season in which something small the god has tended by hand ought to have died and did not.
Fitted to: mirror line 4 (one starter kept alive for years by weekly tending); fitted to the sealed past, a thing kept long enough that it is not lost.

### The One-Handed Knot
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: A knot the god ties holds until a hand unties it or a blade cuts it, and it can tie one with either hand alone.
Limit: Rope and cord of ordinary make; it shades a result (a load that does not slip, a prisoner's bonds, a boat's mooring) and does nothing a good knot could not do.
Shows when (never scheduled): something rides on a knot the god tied.
Drawn from: mirror line 5.

### Rain by the Smell
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: Where it stands, the god is usually right about whether rain comes within the day, from the look and smell of the air.
Limit: A day ahead, where it stands, rain only; a shade on a guess, never a knowing, and nothing against weather a god has called.
Shows when (never scheduled): the god says aloud what the weather will do and something is planned on it.
Drawn from: mirror line 6.

### Early to the Ground
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: Setting out in fair time for a place and an hour it has named, the god arrives ahead of the hour.
Limit: A shade on a journey's timing roll, to an appointed place only; it shortens no road and outruns no pursuit.
Shows when (never scheduled): a meeting where who stood on the ground first matters.
Drawn from: mirror line 7.
