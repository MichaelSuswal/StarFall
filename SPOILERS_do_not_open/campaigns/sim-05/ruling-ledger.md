- L-0006. 2026-11-23, creation Day 2. Card: the player's god, The return. Matter: its text, rung and price. ## The player's god: the Keeper's Night 2 review

- L-0007. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Card: the player's god, The paced claim and The slow chain. Matter: the chaining rate, set provisionally at the table at a third of the god's walking pace. Arguments: none against; set by the DM, provisionally, for the Keeper. Ruled: confirmed. The plain chaining rate is a third of the pace table's foot rate for the ground the line crosses; with The slow chain the god chains at a quarter of it. Reasons: a lone chainman who lays a hundred feet, sets it true, marks and moves on covers about a third of what the same legs would walk; the table's figure matches the pace table's real rates, and the weakness was written against whatever rate was confirmed. No change to the card. Binds: the card.
- L-0008. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Card: the player's god, The return. Matter: where the god gathers if it holds no claimed ground on the day (provisional at the table: the nearest standing boundary mark of anyone's setting). Arguments: none against; the player had offered to be scattered wherever the world sends it. Ruled: confirmed as written. Reasons: the standing rule gives the nearest ground of the god's kind when a set ground is gone; a boundary mark is this god's kind of ground. The set ground (its own claimed line's nearest standing mark) gives no advantage the nearest would not that the added walk does not pay for. No change to the card. Binds: the card.
- L-0009. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Card: the player's god, True ground and The whole map. Matter: the shape of the two information powers against the mystery standard (provisional at the table, priced by shape). Ruled: the shape stands, with one Limit added to True ground, which The whole map takes with the same sense: it reads the ground and what stands on it, and gives no distance, height or bearing for anything in the sky. Reasons: both powers show effects on a surface and no cause, and the god's own instrument is allowed to earn what it measures on the ground. A sense of true bearing and distance that reached upward would hand over, at a glance and from any spot, a measurement the mystery standard requires to be earned (rule 14). The player's own words set the sky outside this god ("weak at the things that need no ground, such as sea, sky and the inside of a mind"), so the Limit is fair to intent and fits the power as first written. Not written on the card by the Keeper: sent to the DM in `campaigns/sim-05/player/corrections.md` to put to the player before anything is played. Binds: the card, once the DM has entered it.
- L-0010. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Card: the player's god, What is held. Matter: how much more a mortal working aimed at moving, hiding or erasing a held line costs its worker (left to the Keeper at the table). Ruled: twice what the same working would otherwise cost its worker. Reasons: "a good deal more" needs a figure that can be applied the same way every time; double is the plainest. Sent to the DM in `corrections.md` as a figure for the Limit. Binds: the card.
- L-0011. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Card: the player's god, The return. Matter: the player's ask that the roll be logged where the Keeper can show it, so that it knows what was taken once it is gone. Arguments: for, the player's (fairness to the one playing; the player reads its own conduct profile and chronicle and would see a line struck in any case); against, the Keeper's own (every other god loses its item unknown, and the drawback's text says the god does not choose it). Ruled: granted to the player, not to the god. The roll is logged in the sealed roll log as every roll is. After the return has happened, the Keeper's release to the DM names the item taken; the DM tells the player in (oog) which item went and strikes it by a dated entry; in the fiction the god no longer holds it and is not told. Nothing is shown before the return, and the choice stays the roll's. Reasons, by the judgment order: fairness to the player's intent, and no power on the card changes. Sent to the DM in `corrections.md` as an answer. Binds: the card, and the DM's handling of every return.
- L-0012. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Sealed matter, from `player/unsaid.md` (third sealed answer). Card: the player's god, The return. Matter: the player asks that the return's roll not take the old worn stake unless the Keeper has to. Ruled: the roll takes an item of memory or habit from the god's record; the stake is a thing carried and is never on the roll's list. A line of the record that is about the stake (keeping it, where it is kept) is left off the list the roll chooses among for as long as any other item stands on it, and is taken only if it is the last. Reasons: fairness to the player's plain intent; one line held back from a list of many changes no odds worth pricing. This is a request about how a price is taken, made at the creation table, and not a want to be acted on in the god's conduct, so the three-year confirmation rule for unsaid wants is not what governs it; it stands from tonight. Nothing of it is written in any public file. Binds: the Keeper, at every return.
- L-0013. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Sealed matter, from `player/unsaid.md` (second sealed answer) and the Day 2 line in `rejected-premises.md` (one request not to be told something). Matter: why the god stopped walking its line; the player would rather not find out at the creation table and leaves to the Keeper whether it is ever found. Ruled: it is written tonight in the god's secret past and is findable in play, by the god's own action only. It is not told at creation, not asked about again, never delivered by a visitor, a letter or a morning story unasked, and the DM is told nothing of it. Whether it is found is left to what the god does. Reasons: the request is honored at the table as made (DESIGN 3.4); the mystery standard forbids both holding a placed thing back and delivering it (rules 4, 8 and 11). Binds: the Keeper.

## NPC gods: Night 2, entered at round 2

Rules that bind every card (entered once; no god is told that another argued them):
- L-0014. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Every card. Matter: what is not a price. Ruling, from `engine/rules/powers.md`: a condition of a power's use, a penalty only carelessness triggers, and a drawback that says the god's form or voice again, are Limits and buy nothing; a price a god can end at will is no price; a price that rests on a god needing food is light, since no god needs food. Applied to `god-rivers` (The Listening, The Witness at the Ford), `god-beasts` (The Count of the Herd, The Standing, Speech with Beasts), `god-harvest` (The Weight), `god-fate` (The Bill), `god-sea` (Sea-Step). Arguments: each god's offer as written in its `self.md`; the Keeper's as in each round 1 file. Each god accepted the rule in its reply.
- L-0015. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Every card. Matter: the campaign's god-sense and one-body rules against a power. Ruling: no power marks or finds a god at a distance (`god-beasts`, a count does not mark a god's beast body; refused, accepted without argument); a part sent out of a body of many parts is a part of the one body, with no will or power of its own (`god-roads`, Sent Voices). Binds every card.
- L-0016. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Every card. Matter: a memory price. Ruling: one item removed from the god's own record by logged roll, at the rate the card states (`god-roads`: a full season of Unremarked, one item; a second closing in a day, one item; `god-harvest`: a name let go takes that person from its record, chosen by roll). Arguments: none against; set by the Keeper, by `powers.md`.
- L-0017. 2026-11-23, Night 2. Every card. Matter: word between far places. Ruling: no power carries who, where or what at any distance as a messenger would; `god-storms`'s Answer to Rite tells who and where only within a day's walk, and beyond that only that a rite was made. Arguments: the god's text gave no distance; accepted in its reply.
