# The Reedwain: starting state

Set by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Year 0, at the god's landing.

population: about 1,800
stores: grain, salt fish and fodder for about four months, in the wains
treasure: modest but exact: the common account, coin and weights in the Account Wain, debts owed them at every ford
trade: carrying for a fee: salt, grain, cloth and hides between the bay, the fords and Meldry [f27]; small trade of their own in rope, mats and salted fish
strength: a paid guard of about thirty; some four hundred who would stand at the ring
mood: settling accounts; troubled by the forged tally
workers of magic known by name: about 3
helpers serving the god: none at landing
