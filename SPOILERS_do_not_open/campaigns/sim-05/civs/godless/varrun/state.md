# The Varrun: starting state

Set by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Year 0.

population: about 2,400
stores: barley, cheese and dried meat for eight months; they store against two winters
treasure: large for their numbers: copper, tin, worked stone, old bargains owed
trade: copper, a little tin and stone work for grain, salt and cloth, at the Varrun Gate [f50], twice a year
strength: about four hundred who fight, in holds no army has taken
mood: closed, patient, unalarmed
