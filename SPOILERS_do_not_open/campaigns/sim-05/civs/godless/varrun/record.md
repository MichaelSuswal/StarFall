# People: the Varrun

A godless people. Written by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Run by the Keeper in the night turn. Land from the traced map; every travel time is the tool's, by the route and means named, at the pace table's rates for a fair summer day.

## Who they are
A high-country people, not human: shorter and broader than men, grey-skinned, slow to grow and long-lived by about half again, with their own speech and their own law. Few. Hard neighbors: they keep their borders and their bargains exactly and forgive nothing by forgetting.
## How many
About two thousand four hundred, in Varrunhold [f31] and five lesser holds.
## Where
The Varrun Heights [r02], above the Varrun Gate [f50]. From Varrunhold [f31] down to the gate on foot over open ground: 53.7 miles, 6.7 days.
## How they live
In holds cut into the mountain sides: goats and hill sheep, terraced barley, copper and a little tin from their own workings, stone work no lowland people can match in patience. They come down to the gate twice a year to trade metal for grain, salt and cloth.
## Their code
A boundary is cut in stone and is never moved. A bargain is kept to its letter and to the day. No lowlander sleeps above the gate without a hold's leave. A debt outlives the debtor.
## Their history in the land
They say they were in the heights before any road was paved, and that the kings who paved the Old North Road [f40] made a bargain with them at the gate: the road for peace, and the door for silence. The kingdom is gone and the Varrun still keep their side. That bargain is why the ford below has not been attacked from the north in living memory. How old it is, and what the door part meant, is 3b's.
## What they believe about gods
That the mountain keeps what is put into it. They make no rites and expect no god; they would deal with one as with any lord of the plain, by bargain at the gate.
## What they want
To be left alone above the gate, to be paid in full, and to keep the Stane Door [f51] shut, as they hold they are bound to.
## Whether and how they touch the great events
They hold the mouth of one sealed door [f51] and the hall behind it [u01], and know more of the old kingdom's bargains than anyone living. They have good copper and a little tin, in a world short of iron. Their numbers do not grow. They are the high-country people of the Night 1 pool.
## Their magic
About four workers known by name, at the world's level, fitted as for every people. They call the price "the mountain's share".

## What the old tell
Added by the Keeper on Night 3b, 2026-11-23. This is what the people say of the past. It is their telling, not the world's word, and parts of it are wrong. Nothing agreed on Night 3a is changed by it.
- They came over the ice before any road.
- They made a bargain at the gate, the road for peace and the door for silence, with one king of the plain.
- The Stane Door [f51] holds the dead of the kings of the plain.
- They call everything under the gate the Below, and the star the Nail.
