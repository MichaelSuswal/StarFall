# People: the Vessari

A godless people. Written by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Run by the Keeper in the night turn. Land from the traced map; every travel time is the tool's, by the route and means named, at the pace table's rates for a fair summer day.

## Who they are
A human people of the south coast: harbor towns, fishers, salt boilers, vine and lemon growers, and the hosts of the school of workers at Ombrel [f66]. A league of towns with a yearly-chosen head, not a kingdom.
## How many
About twenty-eight thousand along the coast, some four thousand of them in Vessa [f30]. The school holds about sixty workers and learners, more than any one people has by its own share.
## Where
The Vessar Coast [r22], with herders and thin farms on the Sable Plain [r20]. From Vessa [f30] to the School at Ombrel [f66] by the School Road [f47]: 23.6 miles, 1.2 days.
## How they live
By the sea and by salt: pans along the coves, fish, coasting trade by small sail, wine and oil. The school takes learners from every people and is paid in kind; the towns feed it and are proud and uneasy.
## Their code
Salt is weighed in the open. A harbor is never closed to a boat in weather. The school's teachers are not to be struck or lied to. A debt to a teacher is paid before any other.
## Their history in the land
The league is some generations old, made from towns that outlived an older realm of the coast. The school is older than the league and no town claims to have founded it. The ground about Ombrel has been worked for a very long time. The depth and the dates are 3b's.
## What they believe about gods
That the working is a craft and the gods, if any, are older craftsmen. They hold that the worker's debt is owed to her teacher, which is partly wrong. They would send a god a factor, then a teacher.
## What they want
The salt trade, safe coasts, true seed to sell north, and the school kept theirs.
## Whether and how they touch the great events
They hold the school of workers, the largest gathering of mortal magic under this star, and the oldest worked ground of the south. They hold most of the world's salt. They are the nearest human neighbors of `god-harvest`'s people and sell them seed and salt.
## Their magic
About forty-five workers known by name among the towns, at the world's level, fitted as for every people, and the school besides.
