# People: the Meldrin

A godless people. Written by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Run by the Keeper in the night turn. Land from the traced map; every travel time is the tool's, by the route and means named, at the pace table's rates for a fair summer day.

## Who they are
The old human kingdom of the middle river, rooted and tired: farmers, river towns, a king in a seat too large for him, lords who answer late. They hold themselves the heirs of the kings who paved the roads.
## How many
About forty-five thousand: some six thousand in Meldry [f27], the rest in Estbridge [f28], Tollford [f29] and the farms and villages of the vale.
## Where
The Meldrin Vale [r14] and the Eastmeld Plain [r17], with a claim on the Tollwater Reach [r24] that Tollford [f29] upholds and the lower fords ignore. From Meldry [f27] to Estbridge [f28] on foot over open ground: 81.5 miles, 5.8 days. From Meldry [f27] to Tollford [f29] by the Meld Road [f42]: 205.8 miles, 10.3 days. From Meldry [f27] to Threeways [f23] by the Meld Road [f42]: 241.9 miles, 12.1 days.
## How they live
Grain, orchards and cattle on old fields inside older walls; river trade; tolls wherever a toll can still be taken. A king, a council of lords, a clerkly court that keeps rolls no one reads. More empty houses in Meldry [f27] each generation.
## Their code
The king's road is free and the king's ford is tolled. A lord's word binds his heirs. Hospitality for three nights. No one ploughs the downs.
## Their history in the land
They count their kings back a long way and are sure the count is right; it is not. The roads, the tollhouses and the Dry Market [f61] are their ancestors' work or older. The kingdom was far wider once. The depth and the dates are 3b's.
## What they believe about gods
That the old kings were favored and the favor was withdrawn. Shrines to the river and the road, kept by custom. They would treat a god as a foreign king: with ceremony, an envoy and delay.
## What they want
Their tolls, their old borders in name at least, salt at a fair price, and not to be asked to decide anything this year.
## Whether and how they touch the great events
They live in sight of the Sleeper's Downs [r15] and do not go up; the villages nearest the downs are the ones emptying fastest, and people there are careless of names. From Meldry [f27] to the Sleeper's Brow [f65] on foot over open ground: 134.9 miles, 9.6 days. They sit on the Meld [f04] between the peoples of `god-rivers`, `god-roads`, `god-fate` and `god-sea`, and each of those peoples' troubles has a Meldrin toll in it.
## Their magic
About seventy-five workers known by name, at the world's level, fitted as for every people; most villages have one.
