# People: the Ilwe

A godless people. Written by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Run by the Keeper in the night turn. Land from the traced map; every travel time is the tool's, by the route and means named, at the pace table's rates for a fair summer day.

## Who they are
A water-margin people, not human: slight, cold-skinned, web-fingered, at home in water for an hour at a time. Few. Strict in their own custom and fair in dealing: they will not cheat a stranger and will not let one stay.
## How many
About fifteen hundred, in Ilwenstead [f32] and some twenty reed steadings.
## Where
The Ilwemere [r23], the reed delta of the Meld [f04]. From Ilwenstead [f32] to Tollford [f29] on foot over open ground: 168.4 miles, 21.7 days through the reeds; they go by punt and are quicker.
## How they live
On piled houses and floating reed islands: fish, eels, waterfowl, reed and salt-grass, a little salt boiled from the tidal pans. They guide strangers through the channels for a fee and take them out again.
## Their code
No stranger sleeps in the reeds. What is traded is traded on dry ground at the marsh edge, before a witness of each side. The dead are given to the tide. A channel once marked is not unmarked.
## Their history in the land
They say the delta is theirs because no one else can live in it, and that it was smaller once, when there was a harbor at the old mouth. Kingdoms upriver have claimed them and none has collected. What truth is in the harbor story is 3b's.
## What they believe about gods
That the water is one creature with many mouths and wants nothing. They have kept away from every power that came into the reeds and expect to keep away from the next.
## What they want
The reeds left uncut and the channels left unstaked; salt-grass and fish enough; to be owed nothing by anyone upriver.
## Whether and how they touch the great events
They are the only guides through the delta and the only people who know its channels. Wanderers who sing lists of names have come to the marsh edge in their grandmothers' time and were guided through; the Ilwe remember the tune and not the words. They are the water-margin people of the Night 1 pool.
## Their magic
About three workers known by name, at the world's level, fitted as for every people.
