# The Orvanni: starting state

Set by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Year 0, at the god's landing.

population: about 11,000
stores: thin after a hard winter: dried meat, curd and fat for about two months, until the herds are in milk
treasure: horses, cattle and sheep; felt, hides; a little bronze
trade: horses, hides and felt to Threeways [f23] for grain, bronze and salt
strength: no standing guard; about two thousand riders who can be called by the horn
mood: lean and proud; glad of the thaw; divided over the oath quarrel
workers of magic known by name: about 18
helpers serving the god: none at landing
