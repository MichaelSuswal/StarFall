# The Fordfolk: starting state

Set by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Year 0, at the god's landing.

population: about 18,000
stores: grain, dried meat and fodder gathered at Threeways [f23] for about five months for those who winter there; the bands carry two months
treasure: moderate: salt, iron bars, good horses and cord-promises held in passage
trade: the carrying trade of the three roads: salt and iron up from the Meld, horses and hides down from the steppe, tolls and guiding at every ford
strength: the Road Guard, about three hundred riders under five captains, paid from tolls; some two thousand more could be raised from the bands
mood: busy and confident at the gathering; uneasy under an old Warden
workers of magic known by name: about 30
helpers serving the god: none at landing
