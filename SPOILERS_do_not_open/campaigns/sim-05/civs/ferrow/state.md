# The Ferrow: starting state

Set by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Year 0, at the god's landing.

population: about 1,800
stores: dried fish, eels and traded grain for about four months; thin after winter
treasure: little: cord-debts owed them along the river, a few bronze weights
trade: ferrying, fish, rope and reed work for grain and salt, at every ford from the springs to Tollford [f29]
strength: no guard; about three hundred who would stand with poles and fish spears
mood: steady and watchful; sore about the king's toll
workers of magic known by name: about 3
helpers serving the god: none at landing
