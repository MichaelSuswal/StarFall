# The Quayfolk: starting state

Set by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Year 0, at the god's landing.

population: about 5,000
stores: salted and smoked fish, grain and oil counted in for the winter: about five months
treasure: modest: the hall's chest, salt in store, and debts named aloud
trade: salt and salt fish up the Toll Road [f43] for grain, timber and iron; coasting trade by small sail
strength: a paid wall watch of about forty; some six hundred who would man the wall and the boats
mood: drawn in for winter; anxious for the missing boat and the salt
workers of magic known by name: about 8
helpers serving the god: none at landing
