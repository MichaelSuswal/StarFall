# The Askeld: starting state

Set by the Keeper on Night 3a, 2026-11-23. Year 0, at the god's landing.

population: about 9,000
stores: barley, oats, cheese, salted and smoked meat for about six months; the tithe-house of the hill's rite holds a tenth more
treasure: moderate: herds are the wealth; wool, hides, bronze, tally-debts
trade: wool, hides, cheese and timber for salt from the south coast and iron from the roads
strength: a paid wall guard of about a hundred; some fifteen hundred who can be mustered from the wards and the drovers
mood: well fed and loud at table; uneasy about the short counts
workers of magic known by name: about 15
helpers serving the god: none at landing
