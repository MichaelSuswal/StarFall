# Event schedule: sim-05

Written on Night 3b, 2026-11-23. Years are years of play. Every rolled year and every rolled choice names its roll. Months are counted from the waking season of the country the row names: month 1 is the first month of the season its god wakes into. A row for a country with no god counts from the clock's `season_opens` (early spring) and says so. A season named in a row is the season in that country in that numbered year. Nothing here is released. Signs reach a god only through who noticed, or through its own senses, and no agent points at them.

Waking seasons, for the month counts: the Carrow, early spring; the Orvanni, spring; the Ferrow, spring; the Fordfolk, autumn; the Reedwain, autumn; the Askeld, autumn; the Quayfolk, late autumn; the Orrenfolk, late summer.

## Random events

One cast for each country in each year, by `tools/roll.py --campaign sim-05`, with the factors named: `--base 30`, and in Years 1 to 7 `--factor "the quiet opening=-20"`, so one chance in ten in the opening years and three in ten after. The yearly cast is not an act of any god or people and takes no sealed factor. Where the kind drawn is a harm that must choose among several peoples, the choice is weighted as `sealed-factors.md` gives, logged before the cast.

If the cast succeeds, the kind is drawn with `--die d20`:

| Face, Years 1 to 7 | Face, Year 8 on | Kind |
|---|---|---|

| 7 to 10 | 5 to 7 | Beasts: wolves in a hard month, a rogue boar, a swarm, a great run of fish or flight of geese. |

| none | 16 to 17 | Sickness in a camp, a herd or a store, small. |
