# Hidden powers: the Cairn (the player's god)

Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18), in card format. Both are weaker than the god's main powers. The DM's lean note put this god below the middle at arrival, so both sit at the fuller end of what a hidden power may be. Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper releases its Description, Drawback and Limits to the DM, who adds it to `P/player/power-card.md` the same day with the scene it surfaced in.

### Where the Road Meant to Go
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: With a hand laid for about an hour on a marker of an old road that other hands built (a waystone, a milestone, an ancient cairn, a paved edge), the Cairn feels the next marker of that road in each direction: a bearing and a rough distance, out to about a day's walk, and whether that marker still stands, lies fallen, or is buried or drowned. One marker at a time. Marker by marker, at walking pace, a lost road can be followed.
Drawback: An old line pulls against the new. From the moment it reads an old marker, its own line is silent to it: it can neither feel nor speak at any hand-laid cairn until it has walked to one of its own and laid a hand on it again.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Roads and lines that were marked on purpose by their makers. Not game trails, not rivers.
  - It gives the next marker, never the road's end, and nothing of who built it or why.
  - A road whose next marker is more than a day's walk off gives nothing in that direction.
  - None while it is in pieces.
  - What it finds is not an honest cairn of its own and carries none of its powers.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A place: the first time it lays a hand, attending, on a marker of one of this world's old roads.
Fitted to: What it was (one stone on a long line, knowing where the line went), what it lost (the end of the road), and what it said it wants to find out.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### The Count of Hands
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: At any hand-laid cairn it is touching or listening at, the Cairn knows how many stones have been added by other hands since it last laid its own hand there.
Drawback: A stone taken away spoils the count. It then feels only that the number is wrong, as an ache in the seam nearest its eyes, and not by how many, until it has rebuilt that cairn by hand.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Hand-laid cairns only, and only ones that have not gone out.
  - A number. Not who, not when.
  - None while it is in pieces.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A place: the first time it returns to a hand-laid cairn that others have added to.
Fitted to: Every hand that added a rock was a small prayer, and it used to know each one.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
