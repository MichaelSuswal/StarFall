# Secret backstory: the Cairn (the player's god)

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). Consistent with `deep-lore.md` Truth 5, which wins wherever this file and it could be read apart. Nothing here is placed in the world; what of it can surface is fixed on Night 3.

## The country that fell

Under the deep lore's Truth 5, the eight gods of this campaign are what one far starfall threw up: the most-held patterns of one country that died. None of them was a person. Each is a people's long memory of something they followed, leaned on or prayed at. The names below are names that country's people used; the "events" are that people's own stories, which is the only form in which a god carries them. The full account is in `S/gods-sealed/the-country-that-fell.md`.

The country was called Vessary by the people who lived in it. One long river, the Anwy, ran through it from a mountain called the Orrun down to a fog coast at Anwy Strand. In its last age it was old and tired, with harvests failing year on year. Four things happened close together near its end, and every god of this campaign carries a piece of them:

1. The Judgment at Tessaly Ford. The twin courts split, the matter went to the lot, and the court of the sworn word put down the court of leave.
2. The Calling-In. The granary kingdom of Marrowmead called in the seed it had lent the hill people of Orrun's Dale, by the letter of an old oath between the two.
3. The Closing Winter. The Orrun pass shut. The hall's servants drove the herds down the road the night before. The Dale people wintered in the mountain's rock-cut halls with no seed and no herds, and none came down in spring.
4. The Roll finished. At the Low House the song of the country's names, sworn never to be closed, was sung to its last cadence.

Then the star came down. Nothing in the gods' memories reaches that night.

## What this god is

Keld Anwy: one named cairn on the Line, the marked road that ran the length of Vessary. It stood below the Orrun pass on the low side, where everyone who came down out of the mountain added a stone for having got through. It is the people's memory of that act: a hand, a stone, someone has been here and got through. The player said it was never a person, and that is exactly so.

## The rest of the story behind the outline

The player's outline says: a marker, one stone on a long line across another country; every added rock a small prayer; it knew where the line went and every place it had been laid; the line and the country gone, it does not know how; its name lost, and the end of the road, which ran to a place where something was waiting. The rest:

- Its name was Keld Anwy, which in the speech of Vessary meant something near "go on, the river is ahead".
- The line was the Line, from the Orrun pass to the fog coast at Anwy Strand, cairn to cairn, broken into stages by lamp-houses.
- How it ended, for this cairn: the night before the pass shut in the Closing Winter, the hall's stewards drove the herds down past it, and each added a stone. Those were the last hands. The people of Orrun's Dale wintered in the mountain and none came down in spring. Keld Anwy stood waiting for travelers out of the pass, and no hand ever added a stone to it again. Then the road below it emptied too.
- Where the road ran: to the last lamp-house, at Anwy Strand. What was waiting there was a lamp, hung on a bone hook in a window every night by its keeper for whoever was still coming down the road.

## What it lost and does not remember

Its name. The end of the road. That the last people it was built to welcome never arrived. It does not carry a wound as the rolled gods do, since the player gave none, but this is the shape of one: a marker that kept faith with a road no one was left to walk. Its honesty, the thing it is and not a thing it prefers, is all that is left of what a cairn on the Line was for.

## Its belief about the old roads here

It suspects some of this world's old roads were its own, or something like it. That is its belief. The Line was in Vessary, and Vessary is gone; no road of this world is the Line. Whether anything of the Line could have been carried here, as a name or a habit of marking, depends on what the star holds and is decided on Night 3 under the deep lore. Nothing is promised either way, nothing will be placed to reward the belief or to refute it, and no one will ever tell it.

## How this past might surface

- Its hidden power Where the Road Meant to Go: an old line pulling against the new.
- Its hidden power The Count of Hands: it used to know every hand.
- A lamp left burning in a window at the end of a marked way.
- A huge old god with a notched ear who adds a stone to one of its cairns without being asked.
- Its name, if anything in this world still holds the Roll of Vessary and ever sings that verse in its hearing.
- A granite god who counts one more than are present.
Each admits other readings: travelers add stones everywhere, lamps are lit everywhere, and a name in a song is only a sound until something answers to it.
