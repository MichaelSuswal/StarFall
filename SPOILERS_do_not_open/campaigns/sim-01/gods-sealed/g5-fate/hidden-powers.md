# Hidden powers: Ivrenna (`g5-fate`)

Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). This is the `Hidden powers` section of `S/gods/g5-fate/power-card.md`, kept apart from the god's folder so its agent cannot read it. Both powers are weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper writes its Description, Drawback and Limits onto the card and tells the agent in its release.

### The Lot Held
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: Ivrenna can take one chance out of the world before it falls and hold it in her left hand: a fever's turning, a lot about to be drawn, a river's rise at one ford, the fall of one roof beam. While she holds it, that chance does not fall. Nothing else stops. When she opens the hand, it falls as it would have, unleaned. She can hold one, for up to a season.
Drawback: The hand holds one thing only. To take a chance she must open it, and whatever was in it is let go and does not come back. While she holds a chance she cannot Tilt the Odds, and when it falls the Debt is opened for it twice over.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Only a chance she could have leaned: in reach, sensed, not yet fallen, not a mind's choice.
  - It defers; it never decides. What was going to be likely is still likely.
  - If she is struck senseless the hand opens.
  - Not a chance another god is steering.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A strain and a choice: the first time she opens her left hand on purpose with a chance in reach that she cannot bear to see fall. What has been in the hand since before her name is spent in learning this.
Fitted to: The closed hand, the wound (a mortal loved and lost), the greed that lets no entry close, and the secret: a lot once palmed.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### The Crossing
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Standing in the water of a ford, on a ferry landing, or at the meeting of two roads, Ivrenna knows who crossed there last and which way they went: one traveler or one party, as a shape, a number and a direction.
Drawback: The crossing takes its toll. Each asking, her dew stops for an hour, her breath does not show, and anyone who knows her would see that something had been paid.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The last crossing only. Not the one before.
  - A shape and a number, not a name or a face.
  - She must stand in the ford's water or on the meeting itself.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A place: standing at a ford or a crossroads with her mind on someone who has passed.
Fitted to: Her one clear memory: a place where roads met and a thing could go one way or the other.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
