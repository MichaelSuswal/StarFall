# Hidden powers: Amarreth (`g3-song`)

Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). This is the `Hidden powers` section of `S/gods/g3-song/power-card.md`, kept apart from the god's folder so its agent cannot read it. Both powers are weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper writes its Description, Drawback and Limits onto the card and tells the agent in its release.

### The Roll
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When a mortal freely gives Amarreth their name to keep, and sings it to her once, she takes it into a song she carries. A name on the Roll is never taken by her forgetting, and its owner does not lose that name to anything: not to age, not to a working's cost, not to her own Gathering Song. She may carry about three dozen names.
Drawback: Every name on the Roll must be sung aloud by her once in each year, in its place, or it falls off, and with it goes one thing of her own, chosen as her forgetting chooses. The more names she carries, the longer the song, and she cannot sing it and anything else in the same night.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Freely given and sung by its owner; a name told by another does not take.
  - It keeps the name, not the person. It does not heal, and does not stop death.
  - A name stays after its owner dies, for as long as she sings it.
  - She cannot add her own name. It is already kept another way.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A being met: a mortal who is losing themselves (the very old, a worker of magic far gone, the dying) asks her to remember them and sings her their name.
Fitted to: The wound (an oath broken, a world broken with it), her domain, and the secret: what she kept before was a roll of names.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Whole Once Heard
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: A song Amarreth has heard whole, first note to last, is hers for good. The forgetting never takes it, and she can give it back exactly as it was sung, in that singer's manner.
Drawback: She cannot alter a song she holds whole. She cannot soften a verse, drop a name, or sing the flattering version, even to spare someone, and if asked to sing it she sings all of it.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Whole songs only. A fragment is as losable as anything.
  - It keeps the song, not what she knew about it.
  - The one song she must not finish is held this way already.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A strain: the first time her forgetting would have taken a song she has heard whole, and does not.
Fitted to: The Keeper trait (one song known whole and never sung) and the honesty that will not cushion.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
