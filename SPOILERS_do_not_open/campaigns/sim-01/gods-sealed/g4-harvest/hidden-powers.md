# Hidden powers: Garrow (`g4-harvest`)

Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). This is the `Hidden powers` section of `S/gods/g4-harvest/power-card.md`, kept apart from the god's folder so its agent cannot read it. Both powers are weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper writes its Description, Drawback and Limits onto the card and tells the agent in its release.

### The Long Sleep
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: Garrow can lie down in open ground, be covered by its own people's hands, and sleep. While it sleeps, the fields within sight of where it lies do not blight, and stores laid by within that sight keep as under a Keeping with nothing borrowed from the next harvest. It sleeps until the season it named before lying down, or until one of its people comes to the place and calls it by name in real need.
Drawback: Asleep, it knows nothing and can do nothing: no Reckoning, no word, no defense of itself or anyone. It cannot wake itself early. It wakes knowing nothing of what passed, and whoever dug it up had the choosing of the hour.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It must be buried by mortal hands that mean it well. It cannot bury itself.
  - Within sight of the place only: about as far as a low hill shows.
  - A full season at the least.
  - Weather, war and human hunger are not held off. Only blight and spoilage.
  - If it is dug up by anyone else it wakes dazed for a day.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A strain: Garrow brought to the ground by stacked Reckonings in a failing year, or by a grave wound, in a field of its own people, with the feeling of the sleep coming on.
Fitted to: The Keeper trait (it knows the sleep), the fear, and the secret: its kings were laid in a low hill and promised to wake.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Seed Knows Seed
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: With a pinch of seed or grain in its bare palm, Garrow knows where it was grown, as a direction and a rough distance, and how many winters it has been kept.
Drawback: Grain that was taken by force or by a levy from those who grew it burns in its palm like a coal, and its hands shake for the rest of the day. It cannot tell that burn from its own guilt, and does not try.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Seed and grain only.
  - Where grown, not who grew it or who holds it now.
  - Mixed grain gives the largest part only.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A thing met: the first seed it handles that was not grown where it is standing.
Fitted to: The scarred hands that remember, and the secret: grain taken back.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
