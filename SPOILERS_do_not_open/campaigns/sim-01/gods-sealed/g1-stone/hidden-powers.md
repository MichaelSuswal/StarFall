# Hidden powers: Orrun (`g1-stone`)

Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). This is the `Hidden powers` section of `S/gods/g1-stone/power-card.md`, kept apart from the god's folder so its agent cannot read it. Both powers are weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper writes its Description, Drawback and Limits onto the card and tells the agent in its release.

### One More
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When a mortal Orrun has let into one of its shelters is dying there of cold or hunger, Orrun, present and with a hand on that mortal, can give of its own body to carry that one through the night. The mortal lives to the next dawn whatever the cold, and wakes warm. One life in a night.
Drawback: A piece of Orrun the size of a fist flakes away and is gone, and it is lessened, a little weaker and a little smaller, until the next thaw. If it does this more than three times before a thaw, it cannot open a new shelter until the thaw.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Cold and hunger only. Not wounds, sickness or age.
  - Only inside a standing shelter of its own, only for one it has looked in the eye.
  - It buys one night. The next night is the mortal's own.
  - Not in the dead of its winter dimness unless it has stood still by that mortal a full day.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A strain: a death from cold or hunger about to happen under its own roof while it is there with its hand free. It is the count in its head, one more than are there, coming right for one night.
Fitted to: The wound (a people it failed to save), the mercy, and the secret: in the last winter the count under the mountain went down one by one.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### The Weight of Snow
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Standing under a slope, a cliff, a roof or a wall, Orrun knows whether it will give way within the next day: a loaded snow slope, a hanging rock, a beam past bearing, a pass about to close.
Drawback: The knowing comes as the old weight behind its ribs. For the rest of that day it is slowed as if one more shelter stood.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Only what stands over the place where it stands, about a bowshot around.
  - It says that it will give, not when within the day and not why.
  - Not the work of a god or a mortal working, which it does not feel coming.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A place: the first time it stands still under a slope or roof that is about to fail.
Fitted to: Its own stated fear (the mountain itself, falling, walls that give way) and the night the pass closed.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
