# Hidden powers: Orrun Tallow-Hand (`g7-beasts`)

Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). This is the `Hidden powers` section of `S/gods/g7-beasts/power-card.md`, kept apart from the god's folder so its agent cannot read it. Both powers are weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper writes its Description, Drawback and Limits onto the card and tells the agent in its release.

### The Drive
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When Orrun Tallow-Hand walks behind a herd, the herd goes where it means it to go, at its pace, without straying, bolting or balking: up to several hundred head, over any ground a beast can cross, by day and night, for as long as it walks. The beasts do not tire while it drives them.
Drawback: While it drives it is the drover again. It walks behind and cannot ride, sit, eat, or take the head of anything. At the drive's end the road is paid: about one beast in ten lies down and does not get up, and that falls on it through the Count as any death does.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Herd beasts and pack beasts only. Not predators, not mortals.
  - It must walk behind them itself.
  - The beasts feel what they were spared when the drive ends: a day of driving is a day of rest owed.
  - No bargain is made, so no beast is bound and none is betrayed. This is not parley.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A strain: a herd that must be moved at once, before snow, fire or raiders, and no one else to do it. It gets behind them and the body knows what to do.
Fitted to: The wound (a servant who fled), the Keeper trait (the seat by the door), and the secret: herds driven down a road on the night it ran.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### The Earmark
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: A beast that Orrun Tallow-Hand notches in the left ear with its own hand, it can always find: direction and rough distance, anywhere within the reach of the Count. A marked beast will not be bargained or driven away from it by anyone else. A few dozen at most.
Drawback: A marked beast's death lands on it twice as hard as any other, wherever in reach it happens. And the mark is a mark: anyone can read whose beast it is.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Its own hand, with a blade, on a living beast that stands for it.
  - Beasts only.
  - The mark does not make a beast obey.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): An act: the first time it marks a beast as its own.
Fitted to: The notch in its own left ear, which it does not remember getting.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
