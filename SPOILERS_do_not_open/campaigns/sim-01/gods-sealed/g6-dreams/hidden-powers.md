# Hidden powers: Orvane (`g6-dreams`)

Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). This is the `Hidden powers` section of `S/gods/g6-dreams/power-card.md`, kept apart from the god's folder so its agent cannot read it. Both powers are weaker than the god's main powers. Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper writes its Description, Drawback and Limits onto the card and tells the agent in its release.

### The Last Lamp
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: A lamp that Orvane lights with its own hand and hangs from its bone hook does not go out until dawn: not by wind, rain or snow. Anyone lost in the dark within sight of it knows it for the way to safety and can come to it without straying. One lamp.
Drawback: While that lamp burns Orvane cannot go more than a few paces from it. It cannot use the Quiet Door, and the houses it holds give no rest that night, since it is tending a lamp itself and not sitting in theirs.
Limits settled at creation:
  - A mortal's hand can still put it out, and Orvane feels that as it does a snuffed house.
  - It draws the lost. It does not choose who comes, and it hides Orvane from no one.
  - It gives a direction and a sure foot toward it, nothing against cold or pursuit.
  - The hook must be its own. If the hook is taken the power sleeps.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A strain: someone it cares for is lost out in the night, and it lights a lamp itself, which it never does, and hangs it on the hook.
Fitted to: The bone lamp-hook it will not part with, the wound (worshipped, then forgotten), and the secret: the last house on a road, where a lamp was kept for whoever came.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Who Sleeps
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Standing wholly still at night, Orvane knows of every mortal within about a bowshot whether they sleep or lie awake: how many, and where.
Drawback: The stillness takes it. It does not feel time pass while it stands so, and it stays until something moves it or the night ends.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Asleep or awake, a number and a place. Not who, not why.
  - Night only. Mortals only.
  - It cannot be done while it holds its presence in, since that is a power used.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A place: standing outside a dark house at night in its full stillness.
Fitted to: What it was good at: being asked for a gentle night, and knowing who needed one.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
