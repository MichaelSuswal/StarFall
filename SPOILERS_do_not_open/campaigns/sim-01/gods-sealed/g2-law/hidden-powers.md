Campaign: sim-01. Assigned by the Keeper on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18). Neither is scheduled. When one surfaces, the Keeper writes its Description, Drawback and Limits onto the card and tells the agent in its release.

### The Hearth's Leave
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When a thread Kest holds has snapped, and breaker and wronged both stand before it, and the wronged says aloud that the breach is forgiven, Kest may lay its left hand on the breaker and the thread is whole again, as if it had never snapped. Everyone present feels that it is so. The oath stands from that moment as freshly sworn.
Drawback: Kest takes the blow of the snap a second time, twice as hard, and a crack opens across the left palm that does not close for a season. While it is open the left hand can do nothing else.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Mortal oaths only. An oath between gods is not Kest's to mend.
  - The wronged must be present and must say it freely; Kest cannot forgive on anyone's behalf, its own oaths included.
  - Once per snapped thread.
  - It mends the oath, not what the breach destroyed.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A strain: Kest reaches with the left hand, the one it never uses, toward a mortal whose broken oath it holds, while the wronged party is forgiving them aloud. Most likely when the breaker is someone it has come to rely on and the breach was a mercy.
Fitted to: The wound (a sibling killed) and the Keeper trait (left-handed, doing everything with the right). The left hand's office is the one its sibling held.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### True Level
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Kest sees at a glance whether a made thing is true: whether a wall is plumb, a floor level, a measure full, a balance honest, a weight what it is marked.
Drawback: A thing that is not true itches at it. In a room with a false scale or a short measure it cannot rest, cannot keep its eyes off it, and sooner or later says so aloud, welcome or not.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Made things of measure and building only. Not words, not people.
  - Sight only; it needs light and a clear look.
  - It says that a thing is false, not who made it so.
Trigger that would surface it (never scheduled): A thing met: the first rigged balance or short measure it looks straight at.
Fitted to: The level eyes, the equal footprints, and a world where honest weights are scarce.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open
