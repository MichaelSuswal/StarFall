# Event schedule: sim-01

Fixed at the lock. Years are campaign years; the player's year opens in late autumn, so "Year N winter" is the winter that opens Year N. Each event has a cause. Signs are in the world and are never pointed at; they reach a god through who noticed or through its own senses. Version 0.1 plays Years 1 to 5; the schedule runs past that so nothing has to be added.

## 3. Random events

One roll per year for each of the eight seats' countries and one for each godless people, by `tools/roll.py`. An event happens on a success.

Odds: base 30. Factors: Year 1, -25. Year 2, -20. Year 3, -15. Year 4, -12. Year 5, -9. Year 6, -6. Year 7, -3. From Year 8, no step. A year in which a clocked event already falls on that country, -10. The tool clamps at 5, so Year 1 is one chance in twenty.

If an event happens, a second roll picks it (d20 by the standing mapping):

1. A good year: one harvest or herd a fifth over.
2. A lean year: a fifth under.
3. A murrain among one kind of stock.
4. A house fire that takes a store.
5. A bridge, ford marker, causeway or pass track washed out.
6. A marriage that binds two quarrelling houses, or breaks them further.
7. A stranger with a skill settles.
8. A feud death.
9. A late frost.
10. A wolf winter.
11. A find turned up by the plough or the spade, ordinary and old: a bronze, a quern, a post-hole ring.
12. A trader's caravan or boat a season early, with news from two regions off.
13. A band of twenty to sixty landless people asks to settle.
14. A local sickness, ordinary, costing one in fifty.
15. A notable birth: twins, a caul, a child with a marked gift for something ordinary.
16. A notable death among the elders.
17. A theft that is laid at the wrong door.
18. An unusually good run of fish, fowl or game.
19. A raid by outlaws, twenty or fewer.
20. A quarrel over a boundary with the nearest neighbors.

No random event creates ground, a ruin, a being or a secret. A find (11) is always ordinary.

## 4. Recurring phenomena

True counts are in `S/state/phenomena.json`, read by `tools/phenomena.py SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-01 YEAR`.

- `dim-nights`: nights in the year on which the star visibly dims for an hour or more. Seen from everywhere on the sheet when the sky is clear. On each, the thread stands over Lake Enner and is seen from the rim. Cause: the feeding faction of the risen.
- `carrow-pass-open-weeks`: weeks in the year the Carrow Pass can be crossed. Cause: snow.
- `sallow-trains-carrow`: Sallow salt trains that cross the Carrow Pass into Carrow that summer. Cause: the Wardens' spring allotment and the weeks open.
- `geese-days`: days in autumn on which the great skeins pass south over the Westermoor. Cause: the season. No mystery attached.

## 5. Checks at writing

- Every event above has a cause. Every mystery's cost is here as a clock or a trigger.
- Random events are weighted down in Years 1 to 7 by the stated steps.
- No event herds the gods toward one place. The lake's reach touches different seats in different decades and compels no one to go anywhere. The Longwalkers' road passes one seat's country and is far from six. No road, pass or secret way exists for the plot: the High Roads are six thousand years old, are in `map.json`, and go where the Abiders wanted to go.
- The first five years are quiet by design: a levy, a salt price, a channel shift, an early winter, a death, a slide, a flood, a fever, a drought, and distance.
