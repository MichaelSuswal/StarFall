## What each result means here

- Phase. The star hangs steady to any instrument a god could build in a century. Two sightlines a thousand years apart do differ (see the deep history: the Sighting Stones on the Ennerwold), by less than a finger at arm's length. Fading is found in the oldest and most spent ground only: the bowl of the Ennerwold round Lake Enner, and one patch on the Black Plain. The fall is tens of thousands of years off and is no clock in this campaign.
- Ceiling. 74, on the deep lore's scale where a newly arrived god sits near 20. The Q8 blend was 6, in the middle band, so the roll was taken unshaded (keeper log, step 3.4). Written to every NPC card's sealed file and to the player's sealed file. Never released.
- Rain density 1. Mortal magic is at 2 on the survey's scale after the clamp from 5: commonly one known worker in a whole country. Each god-led people has one or none. The feel and cost are as blended on Night 1.
- Living radius. 110 days' walk from the point under the star, which is Lake Enner. At the pace table's rough-ground rate that is about 1,500 miles. The mapped sheet reaches at most 1,160 miles from the lake (its far corners), so every mapped place is under the rain. The Pale is rumor only: the Tarsk tell of grass with no kinds in it, forty days beyond their farthest wells.
- Center body. Lake Enner is the hoarder. It is not a lake. See the deep history and the epic.
- Long-game presence. The hoarder is Lake Enner. The carriers are the Longwalkers, who cross this country about once a generation. The thinners are the pale herds, which keep to the Pale and its near edge and are met on this sheet only as rumor and, rarely, as a stray.
- Remainder. None. Nothing of the gods' origin lies on the ground in this world. Truth 5 has no body here but the gods themselves and what the Longwalkers carry.
- Star content. One enormous forest. The dreams the ascended send are of standing under a canopy with no edge, of counting trunks, of leaf-light. The deepest ruins in the Ennerwold carry leaf and trunk patterns no living people uses.
- Ascended temper. Mixed. One faction feeds Lake Enner by the thread and wants the fall. One calls strong gods upward for company. The old ones are weather. The risen god Enna (deep history) is of the second faction.

## The oath rule's hidden form, fixed here

From Night 1: an oath between gods binds with real force, and a broken one is collected on later and sideways. Fixed inside the deep lore's one mechanism: a god swears with something of its own named behind the word, and by naming it the god lends that thing a part of its own hold. If the god breaks the oath, that part is spent: within one to three years the named thing goes general. A named herd loses its markings and breeds plain. A named hall stops being told apart from other halls and is let fall. A named name is mispronounced, then forgotten. The god itself is lighter by a little (about 2 on the density scale for an ordinary oath), for good. No sign announces it and no god is told. The first place it can be seen is the named thing. Reputation is separate and works as reputation does.
