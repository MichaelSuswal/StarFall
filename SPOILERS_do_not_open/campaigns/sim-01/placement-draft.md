# Placement draft: sim-01 (Night 2)

Provisional. Night 3 lays out the land in full, places the star's center, and may move any of this. The region is taken as roughly continental, about 1,600 miles east to west and 1,300 north to south, so that eight gods on a rough ring stand 400 to 550 miles from their nearest neighbors: five to eight weeks on foot over mixed ground by the pace table. The middle of the ring is left open on purpose; what stands there is Night 3's and the deep lore's.

Climate is the harsh four-season climate of the world draft: high ground and the north hardest, low rivers and coasts less hard, nowhere mild.

Five river gods sit on four river systems and one shared headwater country: g2's gorge river and g3's marsh river both drain west and south-west; g4's plain river and g6's coastal river drain south and south-east; g5's folded river drains east. Night 3 decides which rivers join. Every terrain on the Night 1 list has a seat or a gap between seats: the steppe and the deep forest lie east and north-east; the long coast runs south-east; the fog coast lies south-west; marsh lies at both river mouths.

Nearest neighbors, for first contact (provisional): the player's are g1-stone to the east along the high ground and g2-law to the south down the western rivers. Both hold or lack presence-hiding differently: g1 can hold in, g2 cannot and will not.

## What each land will carry (a paragraph each, to be written in full on Night 3)

All of this is draft. Names are not given; Night 3 names things. Nothing here is placed in `map.json` yet.

The Cairn's uplands. Thin soil over old stone, long valleys running down from broken tops, a pass at the valley head. The human story here is as deep as anywhere (the Q5 blend is 10): the oldest of the vanished peoples built in stone on high ground and ran roads along the ridges, with another idea of distance, and their waystones outlast everything built since. Later peoples used the valleys and left the tops alone. The player's old-road candidates from Night 1 (a road built by people with another idea of distance; a sealed door or two) fit this country best of any. Its own undared wish is kept as the world's temper, not placed here.

g1's range. Peopled valleys under passes that close. A long history of small hill peoples who wintered hard, and at least one that did not come through a winter. Candidates from the pools: ground or walls that give way; a pass that closes; a door in the rock (its own Q18 wish, so not on its certain path); the slow non-human kind that keeps to the stone, if Night 3 places them here.

g7's forest edge and steppe. Herds on the grass, deep cover behind. A history of movement more than of building: peoples who came through with animals and left little but tracks, mounds and names of fords. Candidates: a beast that hunts hunters; the forest path-keepers and the grass breeders among the rare non-human candidates; the custom of a place set for the stranger, held by some people here who are not its own.

g5's folded river. Old settled country, tally-keeping, flood-wise. Layer on layer of river towns moved uphill and down as the river moved. Candidates: a debt that outlived its makers; unfinished promises of the dead; a sealed door someone shut out of fear or to keep a word.

g6's coast river. Settled low country with hills behind and marsh ahead. The longest unbroken habitation of any seat: hearths on hearths. Candidates: buried halls with carved walls (its own Q12 wish, so not on its certain path); something abroad in the lampless hours; the night-keeping people among the rare candidates; a traveler from afar with tools no one can explain.

g4's plain. The granary of whoever held it, many times over. The deepest plow soil in the region, and under it the footings of earlier peoples' storehouses. Candidates: the great empty granaries (its own Q12 wish, so not on its certain path); runs of bad harvests; seed grain as the local scarcity; something grown and kept through a long sleep (its own undared wish, likewise kept off its path).

g3's marsh river. Low wet ground, slow water, roads that go under it. A country that has been drowned and drained more than once, with a singing tradition older than its present people. Candidates: roads under water; a door with a song for a lock; the one set of enormous bones taken for hills; living rememberers as the local scarcity; a song still sung of something long broken (its own undared wish, kept off its certain path).

g2's broken river ground. Fords worth fighting over, boundary stones the river has swallowed and given back. The most contested country in the region's history: every kingdom that held the fords wrote its law on stone and every flood moved the stones. Candidates: a door with an oath on its frame (its own Q12 wish, so not on its certain path); tablets no one can read; a first oath still kept (its own undared wish, likewise); broken faith between peoples as the standing threat.

The godless peoples (Q4 blend 6: four or five, old and rooted) and the large pieces of wild country go in the gaps between seats and in the open middle. Night 3 places them.
