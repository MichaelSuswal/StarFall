# World draft: sim-01 (Night 1)

Built from eight surveys, one voice each. Every number and every choice is traced in `keeper-log.md`, Night 1, step 1.4, with its roll ids. Vote counts are in the log and nowhere else. The deep lore wins over everything here. Nothing in this file is placed: placement is Night 3.

Two lines are still open tonight and are marked PENDING. They close when two gods answer what was put back to them.

## Tone

Small warm lights in a large, old, indifferent country. The country is not hostile and not kind. Light is low and comes from one source: dusk, the blue hour after sunset, a fire, a lamp, overcast. Weather turns: mist in the low ground, cold at the door, clear cold dusks on the heights. Palette within the house style: umber, ochre, gold going to brown, near-black, the blue hour as the cool accent. The world's peoples gather close indoors and are talkers, swearers and bargainers; outside the door there is a great deal of dark country and something old under it.

## Climate and seasons

Harshness 2 on a scale where 1 is harshest and 10 mildest. Four real seasons everywhere. Long hard winters, short bright summers, sharp turns, an autumn with an edge. It varies as real climate does, by latitude, altitude and distance from the sea. The low river country and the coasts are less hard than the high ground. Nowhere is mild.

Waking seasons are personal and are kept per god on each sheet. The player wakes into late autumn.

## Magic

Level: PENDING (one god's follow-up on question 6). Provisional in any case until the star's range is rolled on Night 3; if the blend falls outside the range it clamps to the nearest end.

Spread: one rule, unevenly spread. Most people in most places doubt it; in some places everyone knows someone. The unevenness follows the deep lore, not any wish.

Feel and cost, one system for the whole world, inside the deep lore's Truth 4: a working feels like listening hard and being answered. A held breath, a reach, the place leaning in. It is small, specific and tied to where the worker stands. It makes nothing. It costs the worker something particular of her own, slowly and never countably: the rest in her sleep, the taste of food, a face, a morning, a name she answered to. She often cannot say afterward what went. There is no meter. What else pays is the deep lore's and is not common knowledge.

Local understandings, each held somewhere, each wrong about something that matters (unplaced):
- the price is named before the work, and it is fair;
- the cost falls only on the one who spends, never on a stranger;
- it costs years of life;
- it is paid in a sworn word;
- it is a debt, and someone keeps the ledger.

## The three rules

One rule each, for the whole world. No local exceptions.

God-sense. Every god knows from the fall that others like it came down when it did, and nothing more. On the ground a god senses another only in its presence, about as near as speech carries. There it feels plainly that this is one like itself. The sense tells that, and not what: nothing of nature, power, name or intent. A god may try to hold its presence in; whether it can is a matter for its own card. Nothing reaches a god at a distance unasked. The deep lore's pull exists and is among the things investigation can reveal; how is set on Nights 2 and 3 and is never stated.

Oaths. An oath sworn between gods binds with real force. It must be sworn freely and on purpose, with something of the god's own named behind it. Nothing stops a god from breaking one. A broken oath is collected on, later and sideways, at the breaker's cost. The form of the cost is fixed on Night 3 inside the deep lore and is never stated to any god. Reputation applies besides.

Bodies. One body. A default, since no survey spoke to it.

## How much gods stand out

Level: PENDING (one god's answer 8, given again). Feeds the ceiling roll on Night 3 and mortals' first reactions.

Fixed whatever the level: a god can be wrong about things, and mortals can oppose a god and damage its work.

## The wild

Strangeness 3 on a scale where 1 is familiar animals and a few legends and 10 is creatures no one has a name for. Mostly ordinary beasts. A handful of true legends in the deep places. A very few things with no name, at the edges, on the heights and on old ground. One rule for creatures everywhere; where strangeness gathers, it gathers for the deep lore's reasons.

## Past the borders

6 on a scale where 1 is wild and unwalked and 10 is old kingdoms everywhere. Somewhat more land is held than open. The godless peoples are old, rooted and several, with borders, customs and long memories. Large pieces of real wild country lie between and beyond them. Number and claims are set on Night 3.

## Age of the human story

10 of 10. The deepest the scale allows: several risen and fallen peoples, layer under layer, the oldest older than any tongue now spoken, no one sure who built what. Night 3 writes the deep history to this depth.

## Non-human peoples

2 on a scale where 1 is none and 10 is many kinds. They exist and are very few: one kind for certain, at most a second and rarer one. What they are is set on Night 3 and is never told to the player.

Widespread shape (the main candidate): old, slow, long in memory, keeping to old places, poor at lying or unwilling to, with their own law and their own reasons, hard to talk with.

Rare candidates, all unplaced:
- a people who keep the night hours, courteous and hard to read;
- keepers of the old forest paths, who guide for a fee;
- breeders of fine animals on the high grass;
- a small watchful people who keep records of everyone;
- a people who lie as a courtesy and take plain speech as an insult;
- a people who keep their own count of the seasons.

## Travelers from afar

2 on a scale where 1 is none and 10 is many. One, or two, within reach of the campaign's history. From under another star, per the deep lore.

What they brought, candidates, all unplaced:
- a tool no one understands, and a language only half of which works;
- a song no one here has heard, and a trade no one can place;
- plain knowledge: how to store grain, how to read weather;
- tools no one can explain, and no way home;
- a seed; a map no one can read; a trick of smithing.

## Terrain the world must contain

Mountains with peopled valleys. Upland moor and broken hills with long valleys, thin soil and much stone. At least four separate river systems: one of gorges, fords and shifting channels; one of broad flood plain with deep soil and a low hill in sight; one folded round by low hills; one running down to marsh and delta. Marsh, and river-mouth marsh. A fogbound low coast. A long coast. Deep forest. Forest edge. High steppe and open grass.

## Placement wishes (one per god; nothing placed)

- The player: high country, uplands, moors and broken hills with long valleys; a coast within a few weeks' walk, not at the landing.
- g1-stone: mountains, stone that stands, valleys that hold people.
- g2-law: river country and broken ground where rivers change course; flood plains, fords that matter, water-cut cliffs.
- g3-song: river country going down to marsh and a fogbound coast; low wet ground and slow water.
- g4-harvest: flat river flood land with deep soil, open on both banks, a low hill in sight.
- g5-fate: river country with low hills folded round it; a fog coast as second choice; not the high bare places.
- g6-dreams: river country with hills at its edges, a long coast in reach, marsh at the river mouths, a wide night sky.
- g7-beasts: forest edge and high steppe, game country, deep cover behind and open grass in front.

Five gods ask for river country. They are spaced evenly on Night 3, so the rivers are different rivers.

## Pools

### Fears (candidates for the event schedule)

Widespread:
- runs of bad harvests and hard winters, hunger that comes slowly;
- broken faith between peoples, and the debts it leaves;
- the slow loss of what a place knows of itself (the deep lore's Truth 3).

Rare candidates, all unplaced:
- a pass that closes;
- a sickness that moves along a trade road;
- a village turned on itself by a rumor;
- ground or walls that give way in the mountains;
- a beast that hunts hunters;
- something abroad in the hours when the lamps run out.

### Scarcity

World scarcities:
- salt, the material one, shaping roads, marriages and wars;
- reliable faith, the social one: a promise that will still be good in ten years is rare and dear, so witnesses, sureties, hostages and true scales are paid for and fought over. A temper of the peoples and their history, not a rule.

Local shortages, candidates, all unplaced: iron (short in many places); kept seed grain; living rememberers, the old singers; safe passage and winter shelter; restful sleep.

### Buried things (candidate sites for the deep history and the short mysteries)

Widespread:
- old roads, of more than one vanished people, some running where no road should, some to places that are gone or under water;
- ruins under ruins.

Present but few: sealed doors, each a single site.

Rare candidates, all unplaced:
- a door with an oath written on its frame, the swearer forgotten;
- a door with a song for a lock;
- one set of enormous bones, plowed around and taken for hills;
- great stone granaries no one remembers building, empty;
- one or two buried halls with carved walls;
- tablets no one can read;
- a road built by people with another idea of distance.

The deep lore's own buried body lies under the deepest ruins regardless of any wish.

### Mortals worth following (candidates for the small cast; no one is scripted)

Widespread kind: ordinary, stubborn, honest people. No conquerors, no chosen ones. People who keep faith, tell the truth when it costs, and can say no to a god.

Rare candidates, all unplaced:
- a wandering recorder: mapmaker, surveyor, trader or healer, who ends with more knowledge than power;
- a midwife who also lays out the dead;
- a public witness or judge who changes their mind aloud and means it;
- a storekeeper who tells the bad news;
- a keeper of faith who refuses an easy betrayal and is not rewarded;
- a host with a full hall and true scales;
- a keeper of a house, a ledger and a promise who does one impossible thing late in life.

### Discoveries (candidates for the layered mysteries and the discoverable powers)

Widespread: beings able to become a companion to a god. Several in the world, each with its own mind, none a servant, none assigned to any god.

Rare candidates, all unplaced:
- a place that turns out to be listening back;
- a door in the rock;
- a place that takes a price to enter;
- a being long here, neither god nor mortal, that tells the truth when asked;
- a thing out of the wild that follows and chooses to stay;
- a strength that comes of being many (a candidate discoverable power, near the deep lore's Truth 8).

Truths about a god's own past are served by the secret backstories on Night 2.

- The player. Kept as the world's standing temper: it stays indifferent and keeps secrets never solved. Candidate thread: something in the world that keeps what was done.
- g1-stone. Candidate: a people that did come through, somewhere.
- g2-law. Candidate: a first oath still being kept under everything, its terms known to no one. May never be found.
- g3-song. Candidate: a song still sung of something long broken, and a listener for it.
- g4-harvest. Candidate: something grown and kept through a long sleep; a place or a breed that came through.
- g5-fate. No wish given.
- g6-dreams, g7-beasts. Fame cannot be placed; it is earned in play. Candidate custom for some people, not their own: a place set at table for the stranger.
