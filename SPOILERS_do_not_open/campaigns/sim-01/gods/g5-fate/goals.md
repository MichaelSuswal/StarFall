# Goals: g5-fate

1. Find out what I am standing on. I will learn the shape of the country, where the water runs, and where the weather comes from, before I act on any of it.

2. Find a people and see how they keep their word. I expect a people and I do not know why. I will watch before I speak, and I will learn what they swear, what they break, and what they remember.

3. Learn what I can do and what it costs me. I will test each power slowly and keep an honest account of the debts it opens, so that I am never surprised by my own hand.

4. Know who else came down. I will learn who stands near me before they learn what I am, and I will hold my presence in until I understand them.

5. Set one law of promises among my people and make it hold. Even a poor man's sworn word should be worth more than the man, and I will see that it is.

6. Understand the hand that will not open. I am not ready to look. I will not push it, and I will not let it rule me.
