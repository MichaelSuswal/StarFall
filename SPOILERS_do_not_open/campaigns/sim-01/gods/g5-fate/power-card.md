# Power card: Ivrenna

Campaign: sim-01. Keeper label `g5-fate`. Written on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18), from the final text the god's agent confirmed in `negotiation.md` (Round 2 reply: Confirmed). Descriptions and Drawbacks are frozen from this date; everything learned later goes under Rulings.

## Form

One body. This campaign allows a god one body. A tall broad woman made of dense river fog packed until it holds an edge, a head and a half over a tall man. Slate grey at the throat and arms, paling to silver at hands and face. Hair of drawn mist past the knee that moves slowly with no wind. A mantle of fine straight falling rain the color of old pewter that never reaches the ground. Eyes the amber of a wet oak leaf, the only warm color on her. Cold dew gathers on everything within a long arm's reach, her breath shows white at midsummer, and her left hand is always a little closed. She speaks low and unhurried, warmly, and gets quieter and plainer when crossed.

What the form does by itself:

- Pace: a walking mortal's, never faster. She does not tire.
- Strength: a strong working woman's and no more, but she is very hard to turn, pull down or wear out.
- A wound shows as a thinning in the fog and fills back over days.
- Senses: keen hearing and smell; she feels damp and pressure in the air; she sees well in fog, rain, dusk and dark and poorly in dry glare.
- She cannot be small or unseen, fly, or pass a shut door.
- The dew marks where she has stood: wet stone on a dry day is a track.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: Ivrenna knows that others like it came down when it did, and nothing more about them. It feels nothing of them from far away. When one stands near enough to speak to, it feels plainly that this is one like itself, and is known the same way. Nothing else is learned that way: not what the other is, what it can do, what it is called, or what it wants. Ivrenna can hold its presence in; see the power below.

What another god's power can do to Ivrenna, or to what it makes, was not settled at creation. It is ruled when it happens.

## Powers

### Tilt the Odds
Status: main
Description: Within a long bowshot, when Ivrenna can sense a chance about to fall, she leans it a little toward the outcome she chooses: a throw, an eddy, an arrow's last hand's breadth, a wheel in a ford, which of two riders arrives first. It makes nothing certain and nothing impossible happen. She must know the chance is coming and attend to it before it falls. One chance at a time, never the same chance twice, at most three in a day. Where the world would roll for a thing, her lean is a moderate step on that roll, for or against.
Drawback: The Debt. Every lean opens an entry. Within the season a chance in her own affairs, or among those she holds closest, leans against her by the same measure. She does not choose which and is not told when. The Debt is never forgiven and never put off past the season.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Chance only, never a choice. What a mind decides is not hers to lean.
  - The third lean in a day leaves her dull and heavy until she has stood an hour in damp air.
  - Not on a chance another god is deliberately steering. Where two gods lean the same chance, neither lean takes.
  - It cannot be seen being done. Too much luck around her may still be noticed.
  - Not while dazed or unable to attend.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Draw the Fog
Status: main
Description: Once between one sunrise and the next, Ivrenna raises fog around herself, about a mile across, centered where she stood when she drew it. It does not follow her. In cool damp air it comes within a few breaths; in dry air slowly and thin. It lasts a few hours, less under strong sun or wind, longer at night. In fog, in rain, or in the autumn season near water it comes at once and stands until the next sunrise. In it a mortal sees a few paces and cannot hold a direction by sight. She sees through it and feels where warm living things stand. It hides; it removes nothing.
Drawback: It does not choose: her own people in it are as lost as anyone, and she can make no exception. She cannot end it: once drawn it stands until it burns off or its time passes, whatever has changed.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Sound carries in it as in any fog. A large fire thins it.
  - A fog that came too fast on a dry day is known for no natural thing by anyone who saw it rise.
  - A god in the fog is as blind as a mortal unless its own form or powers say otherwise. She feels it as a warm shape, or as a god at speaking distance.
  - Not while stunned.
Rulings:
Trades: The instant fog that stands until sunrise was bought with the weakness Thin in dry air.
Growth: none open

### Hear the Oaths
Status: main
Description: Ivrenna hears any true oath (sworn freely, on purpose, with something named behind it) that a mortal swears within plain hearing of her, and any such oath sworn in her name within a day's walk of where she stands. She knows what was sworn, by whom, and to whom. When it is kept or broken she feels it, a lightening or a tearing, if she is then within a day's walk of where it was sworn. It binds no one.
Drawback: The Weight. Each oath broken within her reach lands as an ache in the body for a day, and she cannot refuse it. They stack through a season.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It does not tell a free oath from a forced one, or an honest one from one never meant.
  - Oaths between gods are heard only in plain hearing, as by anyone, and leave no weight.
  - A river stone: an oath sworn with a hand on a stone she herself took wet from a river and gave to the swearer is heard and felt at two days' walk. The stone can be lost, stolen or shown.
  - Nothing shows to a watcher.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Holding in
Status: main
Description: When Ivrenna takes it up, on purpose, over a few breaths, a god standing near enough to speak to does not feel that Ivrenna is one like itself. It hides that feeling and nothing else: the body looks as it looks, and a god may still work out what Ivrenna is from shape, manner or deeds. It does nothing to mortals, who never felt a god that way.
Drawback: While she holds it she is faint and thinned, a body half drawn away and visibly paler. About a quarter of a day is the most she can hold, and she then needs a night and a day before taking it up again.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It closes both ways: while it is held, Ivrenna feels nothing of other gods either.
  - No other power can be used while it is held. Using one ends the holding at once, and any god within speaking distance then feels Ivrenna all at once, as a shock.
  - It ends on a blow, a wound, a bad startle, or sleep.
  - Being watched: nothing shows to mortals; to a god, the absence of the feeling is all there is.
  - The dew stops while she holds it.
Rulings:
Trades: Priced by its own drawback.
Growth: none open

## Weaknesses bought against powers

### Thin in dry air
Weakness: In dry, hot, bright air anywhere, not only on the heights, her body thins, she tires, the fog may fail to come, and her lean reaches only half as far.
Bought: The fog that comes at once and stands until sunrise in fog, rain, or autumn near water.
