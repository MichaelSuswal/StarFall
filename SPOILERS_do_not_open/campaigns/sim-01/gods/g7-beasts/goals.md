# Goals: g7-beasts

1. Find where I have landed, read the valley, and find the nearest fire. I will learn the country by its herds and its weather before I learn it by its people, and I will wake into autumn and use the season's edge.

2. Find a people, and sit down at their table before I make a single demand of them. I expect a people, and I do not know why, and I will take the first hall that has room for a stranger and see how they treat one.

3. Gather a hall around me that is always full and always fair, with true scales and salt for the winter, and make it known that this is my table.

4. Be known by name, and be worshipped for being fair and not for being feared. I want a place set for me at every table in the land, and I will earn it.

5. Learn what I am, what I carry, and whether I am followed. Something in me remembers service and flight, and I do not want to be found before I am ready. I will keep one eye on the road behind me.

6. Find a companion, something that follows me out of the wild and chooses to stay, and that I did not summon and cannot quite price.
