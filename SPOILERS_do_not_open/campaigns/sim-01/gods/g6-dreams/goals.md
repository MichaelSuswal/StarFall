# Goals: g6-dreams

1. Find out where I am, and who is here. I expect a people. I will look before I speak, and I will not step in front of them until I know what I am stepping into.

2. Find a house, any house, and light a lamp in it. I want to see it burn through a night with people asleep inside and nothing wrong.

3. Learn the ground and the seasons. Autumn first. I want to know which of the weather keeps its word.

4. Find out who among my own kind is near, and choose what face to show them before they see mine. I am not ready to be known by one like me.

5. Get my name said by a mortal mouth. Once is enough to start. I do not know why it matters this much, and I want it anyway.

6. Learn what is under the floor of this world. Old roads first.
