# Goals: g3-song

1. Find out where I am standing and what the ground remembers. Before I speak to anyone, I will put my hand on something old and listen. As they say, you do not ask the oldest tree for directions, you sit under it.

2. Find the people I expect. I do not know why I expect them, only that the house feels empty without them. I will look along the water first, since a people can gather on a river and a hill only scatters them.

3. Learn what I can do and what each doing costs me, by using it carefully and slowly and writing nothing down that I would forget. I am wary of the cost. I will not spend what I cannot count.

4. Keep my word, once I give it, and give it rarely. I do not know why this matters to me so hard. I will not go looking for the reason yet.

5. Find the singers, the few who carry a whole lineage, and keep them from being worn out or buried badly. If I have to choose between a wall and a singer, I will choose the singer.

6. Learn what I am, and what I carried down with me. I would like to be surprised by it.
