# Power card: Amarreth

Campaign: sim-01. Keeper label `g3-song`. Written on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18), from the final text the god's agent confirmed in `negotiation.md` (Round 2 reply: Confirmed). Descriptions and Drawbacks are frozen from this date; everything learned later goes under Rulings.

## Form

One body. This campaign allows a god one body. An old woman as tall as a tall man but stooped, broad and heavy-boned, with thick wrists and big square hands. Skin the grey-brown of dried river clay, deeply lined. Long hair the white of old rope in thick cords to the waist, tied with river shells, small bird bones and a knot of grey wool. Deep-set eyes the grey of wet slate, the left lower lid drooping a little. Layers of undyed wool the color of fog, a long skirt and wide shawl, bare feet. The hem is always damp and leaves a dark track on dry floors. Within arm's length of her a low note is felt in the breastbone. She speaks low and slow, in proverbs, after long silences.

What the form does by itself:

- Pace: an old walker's, never faster. She cannot run and climbs badly. She does not tire.
- Strength: a strong farm woman's and a little more: a cart wheel, not a cart.
- Hearing is the best of her: a whisper across a large room, a voice through a wall, and in a mortal voice whether it lies about its own feelings (feeling only, never fact; not in a god's voice).
- Good nose, ordinary sight, poor at distance, a mortal's in the dark. Heavy in water.
- The hum, the hem and its track mark her: anyone who has met her knows her again in the dark.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: Amarreth knows that others like it came down when it did, and nothing more about them. It feels nothing of them from far away. When one stands near enough to speak to, it feels plainly that this is one like itself, and is known the same way. Nothing else is learned that way: not what the other is, what it can do, what it is called, or what it wants. Amarreth can hold its presence in; see the power below.

What another god's power can do to Amarreth, or to what it makes, was not settled at creation. It is ruled when it happens.

## Powers

### The Unmaking Note
Status: main
Description: Amarreth sings one low held note at a made thing within a few paces that she sees, touches or hears, and it comes apart along the lines it was put together by: knots, lashings, pegged joints, mortar, dry stone, mud brick, a bar along its grain, a lock, a seal. One thing per singing, up to about the size of a cart or a small hut, a slow breath for a small thing and a full minute for a large one, as often as she will pay for. Anyone near hears the note. It does not mend.
Drawback: Each singing, she forgets one thing. She does not choose it and is not told what went. The size follows the unmaking: a knot may cost a morning's weather, a hut may cost a name, a face, a promise owed to her, or the way to a place.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Never flesh or bone, never what grew where it stands, never what she carries or wears, never a god's body.
  - Metal only when small: a clasp, a pin, a rivet. Not a blade, a helm or a cauldron.
  - A thing held together by more than craft: the note loosens the craft and not the other thing.
  - Never taken by the forgetting: how to sing her powers, her own name, and the knowledge that she pays. Everything else may go.
  - How the forgetting is kept: the Keeper removes the lost thing from her record and she goes on without it. Others may remember what she has lost.
  - Not if her mouth is stopped. In water the note is dulled and the work slower.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### The Long Ear
Status: main
Description: With her hand on a place or a thing (a stone, a doorframe, a well curb, a grave, a hearth, an old tree), once in a day, Amarreth hears what was sung there, and what was said there in the manner of song: chant, lament, vow, a naming, a thing recited to be remembered. She hears voice, tune and words, not minds. It comes latest first and strongest first, and she does not steer it. A night's listening reaches back about as far as the oldest person now living could remember; each further night at the same place reaches further and thinner.
Drawback: While she listens she is deaf to the living world and slow to rise. One song of what she heard stays in her until the next day: she hums it, it colors her speech, and anyone who knows that song knows where she has been listening.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Not ordinary talk.
  - Very old singing comes as fragments. A tongue no one now speaks is heard as sound and tune only.
  - Running water keeps only the last song, faintly. A place never sung in holds nothing.
  - Touch only, and a few steps around. An object she holds can be heard the same way.
  - A god's singing or swearing is heard as any voice; nothing marks it as a god's.
  - Not on a living person's body.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### The Gathering Song
Status: main
Description: Once in a night, Amarreth sings a slow song to a company within reach of her plain voice, a dozen to a few hundred, and those who choose to sing with her are steadied until dawn: grief and fear sit lighter, they tire more slowly, and they remember the song and one another. No one is made willing, loyal or obedient. Her own people take it fully, strangers partly, and no one who does not sing takes anything.
Drawback: Each who sang forgets one small thing of their own when it is done, a morning, a taste, a name once answered to, and she must say so before she begins or the song does not take. She pays as they pay: each Gathering Song takes one small thing from her as well, unchosen and untold.
Limits settled at creation:
  - They must hear her own voice.
  - A god who sings along is steadied as a stranger is, pays as a singer pays, and learns nothing of her by it.
  - It is done openly; everyone present knows what is being done.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Holding in
Status: main
Description: When Amarreth takes it up, on purpose, over a few breaths, a god standing near enough to speak to does not feel that Amarreth is one like itself. It hides that feeling and nothing else: the body looks as it looks, and a god may still work out what Amarreth is from shape, manner or deeds. It does nothing to mortals, who never felt a god that way.
Drawback: While she holds it she tires as a mortal does. About a quarter of a day is the most she can hold, and she must then rest as long as she held before taking it up again.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It closes both ways: while it is held, Amarreth feels nothing of other gods either.
  - No other power can be used while it is held. Using one ends the holding at once, and any god within speaking distance then feels Amarreth all at once, as a shock.
  - It ends on a blow, a wound, a bad startle, or sleep.
  - Being watched: nothing shows to mortals; to a god, the absence of the feeling is all there is.
  - The hum in the breastbone stops while she holds it; a mortal who knows her may notice.
Rulings:
Trades: Priced by its own drawback.
Growth: none open
