# Goals: g1-stone

1. Land on high rock in spring and learn what the body can do. I will stand still first and look at what is below.

2. Find the nearest people, and watch them before I show myself. I expect a people. I will see whether it is these.

3. Shelter whoever is hungry and cold. Keep them through the first winter. Nothing else matters more than that they live.

4. Learn who else came down with me, by their feet on the stone and their faces. I will not tell them what I am.

5. Find the old roads and the sealed doors. Leave the bones unopened until I am ready.

6. Learn what was lost here before, and whether it was me.
