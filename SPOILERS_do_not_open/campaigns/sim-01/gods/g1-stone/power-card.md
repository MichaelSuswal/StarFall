# Power card: Orrun

Campaign: sim-01. Keeper label `g1-stone`. Written on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18), from the final text the god's agent confirmed in `negotiation.md` (Round 2 reply: Confirmed). Descriptions and Drawbacks are frozen from this date; everything learned later goes under Rulings.

## Form

One body. This campaign allows a god one body. A figure of grey granite near nine feet tall, a head and a half over a tall man, as broad as two men, long thick arms and short heavy legs. White quartz seams run through the stone; one crosses the face from the left temple to the jaw. Frost lies in the creases of elbow, neck and knee in every season, and the breath shows as cold fog. In spring moss and lichen come on the shoulders and the backs of the hands and fall away by autumn. Eyes pale as meltwater with no dark center, hair a coarse mat the grey of wet slate, a long mantle of undyed felt pinned with a shard of black stone, bare feet. It speaks in very few words, low and slow.

What the form does by itself:

- Pace: a quarter slower than a walking mortal, always (see Weaknesses). It does not tire. It cannot run.
- Strength: a loaded cart's weight, or a boulder four men could not shift; it can hold up a wall or a roof beam for a long while.
- It sinks, cannot swim, and cannot cross water deeper than its chest. Cold does not trouble it. Heat dulls and slows it.
- Hard to cut. Bronze and many hands over time can chip and crack it; a great fall breaks pieces off that heal slowly.
- Senses: a strong mortal's sight and hearing, a poor nose. Through bare feet on stone it feels heavy footfalls and rockfall within about a stone's throw.
- It cannot enter a small room or be quiet on gravel.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: Orrun knows that others like it came down when it did, and nothing more about them. It feels nothing of them from far away. When one stands near enough to speak to, it feels plainly that this is one like itself, and is known the same way. Nothing else is learned that way: not what the other is, what it can do, what it is called, or what it wants. Orrun can hold its presence in; see the power below.

What another god's power can do to Orrun, or to what it makes, was not settled at creation. It is ruled when it happens.

## Powers

### Hearth of Stone
Status: main
Description: With both hands on natural rock that has never been worked (a cliff, an outcrop, a great boulder), over about an hour, Orrun opens a dry room with one doorway, large enough for about twenty mortals to sleep. It keeps out wind, snow and rain and holds the warmth of those inside. It makes nothing else. The doorway admits only those Orrun has looked in the eye, one by one, before or after it opens; to all others it is rock. The room stands until the next full thaw, then closes over several days, slowly, and never on a living thing. At most three stand at once. Orrun knows where each is and nothing of what happens in it.
Drawback: Each standing shelter holds a part of Orrun and slows it by a quarter of a walking mortal's pace, on top of its standing slowness, until that shelter closes. Once it has let someone into a shelter it cannot shut them out of that shelter while it stands, and it can be wrong about them.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Unworked natural rock only: not a wall, a tower, a quarried face, a sealed door or anything hands have shaped. Not wet, soft or crumbling stone, nor rock under flowing water.
  - Always a blind room with one mouth. It never joins a cave, a passage or another room, and is never a way through.
  - Mortals with picks and days can cut into a shelter as into any rock.
  - Orrun may close a shelter early by going to it and laying hands on it for an hour.
  - Floor on the slowing: outside winter, shelters never bring Orrun below a quarter of a walking mortal's pace. In winter there is no floor; with shelters standing it may be unable to move at all.
  - It must touch the stone and have the use of both hands. It cannot be done unseen.
  - A god may enter only if Orrun has met its eyes. What a god's power can do to a shelter is ruled when it happens.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Stone Hearing
Status: main
Description: Standing still with both bare feet and one hand on bedrock, Orrun feels what moves on the same unbroken rock. Footfalls, hooves, rockfall and digging reach it from as far as about a day's walk in that country, with rough number, heavy or light, two legs or four, and direction. Voices and breathing against rock reach it only from within about a bowshot, as sound without words. Nearby things come at once; the far edge comes after about an hour of standing.
Drawback: While it listens Orrun is deaf to the air and its eyes see only dimly, as through water. It cannot move its feet without losing the hearing, and coming back to itself takes a slow count of a hundred.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Soil, sand, deep snow, scree, and any river or lake break the link.
  - Works the same in light and dark, and lying down with a hand on rock.
  - A god on the same rock is a footfall like any other, as heavy as its body. Nothing tells Orrun it is a god.
  - It hears no words and no thoughts; its own people are footfalls like anyone.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Return At Thaw
Status: main
Description: If Orrun's body is broken, scattered or buried so that it cannot act, it is whole again at the next spring thaw, standing on the high stone nearest where it fell, bare, with moss on the shoulders. It remembers what happened up to the breaking and nothing of the time between.
Drawback: From the first hard frost to the thaw, every year, whether or not the power is ever used, Orrun moves at half its pace, its senses are dulled, and its powers are weaker: a shelter takes three hours to open, no more than one new shelter can be opened in a winter, and Stone Hearing reaches half as far.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Not while pieces lie under deep water or in a river, until they are moved to stone and a thaw comes.
  - A piece carried more than a few days' walk off does not return with it; Orrun comes back lessened until it recovers the piece.
  - Not from being bound or held by a living thing or a god.
  - What it carried stays where it fell. It works on Orrun alone.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Holding in
Status: main
Description: When Orrun takes it up, on purpose, over a few breaths, a god standing near enough to speak to does not feel that Orrun is one like itself. It hides that feeling and nothing else: the body looks as it looks, and a god may still work out what Orrun is from shape, manner or deeds. It does nothing to mortals, who never felt a god that way.
Drawback: The price is the standing slowness under Weaknesses. For that price there is no limit on how long it may be held while awake.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It closes both ways: while it is held, Orrun feels nothing of other gods either.
  - No other power can be used while it is held. Using one ends the holding at once, and any god within speaking distance then feels Orrun all at once, as a shock.
  - It ends on a blow, a wound, a bad startle, or sleep.
  - Being watched: nothing shows to mortals; to a god, the absence of the feeling is all there is.
Rulings:
Trades: Bought with the weakness A heavier body.
Growth: none open
