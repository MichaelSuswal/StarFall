# Survey, Day 1: g1-stone

1. Grey light on high rock. Many fires in the valleys below. Quiet, then voices.

2. Mountains. Stone that stands. Valleys that hold people.

3. Four seasons, hard ones. Winter that tests. Thaw that forgives. I want to wake into spring.

4. Some of each. Old kingdoms to meet. Wild country to cross. I want neighbors, and room.

5. Old. Ruins under ruins. Thousands of years. Something was lost here before. I feel it.

6. A little more than a rumor. A hand in most valleys. Never everywhere.

7. Like cold water on a hot day. It should cost them rest, or years. Nothing free.

8. Not far above a great mortal. I do not want to be alone on the height.

9. A few legends. Some beasts no one has named. Not many.

10. The mountain itself. Falling. Hunger in winter. Walls that give way. Nothing cruel by design.

11. Safe passage. Salt, and shelter in the cold. Trust most of all.

12. Old roads. Sealed doors. Bones I can leave unopened until ready.

13. Yes. Few, and old. Slow of speech. They keep to the stone and watch.

14. A few. They arrive hungry and tired. I would shelter them. I would watch them first.

15. A pull, faint at distance. Certain face to face. I would rather see a thing than be told it.

16. Worth the god who gave it. Force would only make liars quiet. I will keep mine. I will not trust yours.

17. One who shelters others. One who stays when it is easy to leave. One who breaks a bad law and keeps a good one.

18. A people who need me. A door in the rock. A truth about the one before.

19. That this time they live.
