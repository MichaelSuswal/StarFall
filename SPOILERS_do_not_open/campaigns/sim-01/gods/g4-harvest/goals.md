# Goals: g4-harvest

1. Stand on the ground and learn it. I want my feet on the soil, the river in sight, and the low hill found. I want to know where the high ground is before I decide anything else.

2. Find the people I expect and look at their stores. I do not know why I expect them. I will count what they have laid by and tell them the number plainly.

3. Find a steady mortal, one who lays up seed, tells the truth about bad news, and can say no to me and be right. I would follow that one for years.

4. Meet the others like me by walking toward them, face to face. I will give my word easily, and I will keep what I swear. I want to learn what each is by watching, not by being told.

5. Keep what I grow. I do not have to take anything back this time. If I take nothing, I may learn what the weight in my hands is, but I will not hunt for it. I am good at waiting.
