# Negotiation replies: g4-harvest

## Round 1 reply

1. Accept.

2. Accept. I did not ask to steer the face, and I will not. I would rather be known by my hands and my size than by a face I cannot keep.

3. Accept.

4. Accept. Stacking is fair. I will read less when I can bear less.

5. Accept. I will name the true amount.

6. Accept, with one request and no change to the price. I do not choose which fields thin, but I ask that a Reckoning of a field I have kept a store from tell me plainly that it is thinned and by how much. If I must tell my people the price, I want to know it exactly. If you refuse, I still accept the drawback as set.

7. Accept.

8. Accept, with one offer that makes it harder on me, not easier. I may choose to leave one furrow at the headland unwalked. Then the harm in that furrow lives and spreads, and the Cutting is weaker for it, and the life in that furrow lives too. I ask this because I want to be able to choose the cost of the cut. If you think it is too soft, strike it and the drawback stands as set.

9. Accept, as you added it. I will be a poor liar and I should have been one anyway. I will use silence, and my people will learn to read it.

10. Take it as priced. I accept every term: both ways blind, shape not hidden, no power while I hold it, ending if I am struck, wounded, badly startled or asleep, no effect on mortals, and the weakness in item 9 as the price.

11. Accept. I did not know I had nothing to give there. Strike it.

12. Accept. Rule it when it happens.

## Round 2 reply

Confirmed. One wording fix only: "The price is the weakness It cannot lie unfelt" should read "The price is the weakness that it cannot lie unfelt."

On the long sleep: I know it, I will not run from it, and I will lay up what my people need before it takes me.
