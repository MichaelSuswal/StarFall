# Power card: Garrow

Campaign: sim-01. Keeper label `g4-harvest`. Written on creation Night 2 (2026-11-18), from the final text the god's agent confirmed in `negotiation.md` (Round 2 reply: Confirmed). Descriptions and Drawbacks are frozen from this date; everything learned later goes under Rulings.

## Form

One body. This campaign allows a god one body. A broad, thick figure a hand taller than a tall man, built like a plow ox stood upright. Skin the color of dry wheat going to brown at hands and neck, palms cracked and scarred pale. Cropped stiff hair the gray gold of a cut field. An undyed knee-length wool coat the brown of wet barley straw, belted with plaited straw rope, bare forearms, plain muddy hide boots. The face has no fixed shape: a plain broad oval that no viewer can keep. The eyes are the same to everyone, amber brown and slow to blink, and so are the big scarred still hands. It smells of cut grass, dust and turned earth and moves with a sound like dry stalks. It speaks slowly, plainly and bluntly.

What the form does by itself:

- Pace: a walking mortal's, never faster. It does not tire. It cannot run.
- Strength: about three times a strong plowman's. Not enough to break stone or bend bronze.
- Hard to hurt and slow to heal: a wound closes over weeks.
- Senses: a country hunter's. It smells weather and soil well and sees in low light, not in full dark.
- It sinks and walks the bottom.
- The face is form, not a power, and is not steered. Each mortal sees a face they half know and no two agree. Garrow cannot choose the face, wear a particular person's, or stop it. It can always be known by eyes, hands, size, smell and sound. What a god sees is left to be found out.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: Garrow knows that others like it came down when it did, and nothing more about them. It feels nothing of them from far away. When one stands near enough to speak to, it feels plainly that this is one like itself, and is known the same way. Nothing else is learned that way: not what the other is, what it can do, what it is called, or what it wants. Garrow can hold its presence in; see the power below.

What another god's power can do to Garrow, or to what it makes, was not settled at creation. It is ruled when it happens.

## Powers

### Reckoning of the Yield
Status: main
Description: Kneeling, with a bare hand on soil, a standing crop, seed or a stored heap, over a few slow breaths, Garrow knows what it will yield this season or has left, what ails it, how long a store lasts at the present eating, and what in it is spoiled. It covers what the touch belongs to: one field or one store, up to a large farm's holding. It reads the season as it now stands. It tells what is, never what to do.
Drawback: What ails the thing it reads, Garrow carries in its own body until the next dusk: blight a cough, rot a sourness, drought a thirst nothing slakes, an empty bin a hunger. Several readings stack.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Soil, crops, seed and stored grain only. Not people, beasts or weather.
  - A flood, a frost or a fire that has not yet happened is not in the number.
  - A field thinned by a Keeping reads as thinned, and by how much.
  - Touch only. Not a flooded field unless the hand reaches soil.
  - A god's field reads like any other.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Keeping
Status: main
Description: With both palms on one store of harvested grain or seed, Garrow says aloud how much is there, and for one full turn of the seasons that store does not rot, mold, sprout, or feed mice, weevils or birds, so long as it stays in that place. One bin, barn or granary at a time, up to what a few dozen households lay by, once per store per year. It makes no grain and stops no hand from taking it.
Drawback: The Keeping is borrowed from the next living harvest. The fields that feed that store yield less the following season, by about what the Keeping saved from loss. Garrow does not choose which fields.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It must name the true amount. The Keeping holds what was named and no more.
  - It ends if the store is moved. Wet grain is not kept.
  - Harvested grain and seed only.
  - Once set it holds without Garrow present.
  - What a god's power can do to a kept store is ruled when it happens.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### The Cutting
Status: main
Description: Garrow walks the whole edge of one sown field, up to a few acres, at dusk, with open hands, and by morning what harms that crop in it is dead: blight, rust, weeds, locusts and grain pests, rot in the roots. Once in a night, once per field per season. It holds for that season's crop and heals nothing already damaged.
Drawback: Everything else alive in that field that is not the crop dies with the harm: bees, songbirds, worms, wildflowers, the small beasts. Nothing comes back to that ground until the next spring, and a field cut year after year grows a little poorer each time.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Only a field under a sown crop. Not wild ground, pasture or forest.
  - It touches no person, no livestock, and no beast larger than a hare.
  - If the walk is not finished it does not take. A flooded edge cannot be walked.
  - Garrow may leave one headland furrow unwalked. The life in that furrow lives, and so does the harm in it, which may spread back into the field within the season.
  - Only Garrow's own walking counts.
Rulings:
Trades: none
Growth: none open

### Holding in
Status: main
Description: When Garrow takes it up, on purpose, over a few breaths, a god standing near enough to speak to does not feel that Garrow is one like itself. It hides that feeling and nothing else: the body looks as it looks, and a god may still work out what Garrow is from shape, manner or deeds. It does nothing to mortals, who never felt a god that way.
Drawback: The price is the weakness that it cannot lie unfelt. For that price there is no limit on how long it may be held while awake and unhurt.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It closes both ways: while it is held, Garrow feels nothing of other gods either.
  - No other power can be used while it is held. Using one ends the holding at once, and any god within speaking distance then feels Garrow all at once, as a shock.
  - It ends on a blow, a wound, a bad startle, or sleep.
  - Being watched: nothing shows to mortals; to a god, the absence of the feeling is all there is.
Rulings:
Trades: Bought with the weakness It cannot lie unfelt.
Growth: none open

## Weaknesses bought against powers

### It cannot lie unfelt
Weakness: When Garrow says what it knows to be false, those who hear feel the wrongness plainly, though they may not know why. Silence and a true word beside the point are open to it. Anyone who learns this can put a straight question and read what comes back.
Bought: Holding in, with no limit on how long it is held.
