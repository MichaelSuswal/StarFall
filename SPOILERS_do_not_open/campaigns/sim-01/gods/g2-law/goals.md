# Goals: g2-law

1. Land, stand up, and be seen. I want to walk into the first place with people in it and let them look at me. A door that opens slowly is no door, so I will open it fast.

2. Learn what I can do by doing it. I will try my hand on a barrier, my voice on a crowd, and my ear on the first oath I hear, and I will learn what each costs me before anyone leans on me for it.

3. Find a people to lead, and find out why I expect one. I will look first for those who swear to things and keep them, in river country with old walls, and I will judge them by their promises before I judge them by their strength.

4. Meet another one of my kind face to face, and see them. I want the first look to be mine to remember. I will not swear anything at that table until I have learned what the other wants, because a quick oath is a bad one.

5. Find a place I cannot enter without paying. I would like to learn what it feels like to be bound, and I will not break the bond to find out.

6. If there was a first oath sworn in this land, see that it holds, and do not ask what it was. Something in me goes quiet when I say it, and I will leave it quiet.
