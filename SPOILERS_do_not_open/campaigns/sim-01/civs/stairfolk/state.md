population: about 800, in six steadings down the valley of Stair; no town
stores: low, as every spring; about five weeks of barley and beans across the valley, Topsteading and Gullet nearer three; cheese and dried goat besides; seed held back
treasure: little; felt, wool and cheese are the trade goods; about 300 pounds of salt left in the valley
trade: two or three Sallow pack trains along the Underskarn track each summer, salt and a little iron in, wool and felt out; a few walkers down the Enn each autumn
strength: about 130 who can fight with spear, sling and axe; no walls but the houses; no standing fighters
mood: tired at winter's end, watchful, not unhappy; they have no god and do not know one has arrived
