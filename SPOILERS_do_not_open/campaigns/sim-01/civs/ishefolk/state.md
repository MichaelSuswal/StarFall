population: about 3,000, in some thirty long-house households on the middle Ishe
stores: good; orchards and terraces in, hay made; the common store at Postlanding half full
treasure: modest; bronze, cloth and boats; debts owed and owing counted to the token
trade: boats down the Ishe to the east coast twice a year; salt and horses from the Tarsk; apples, cloth and timber out
strength: about 480 who can fight; no walls; old hill banks that could be refitted
mood: steady and busy with harvest; six households in open dispute over inherited debts; no god known
