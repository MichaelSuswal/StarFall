# Ennermere: record

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3. Run by the Keeper in the night turn.

Who: human. The old kingdom of the bowl of the Ennerwold, round Lake Enner. About 40,000: 9,000 in Ennerhold in the Gap, 3,000 each at Fallwater and Hithe-under-Hill, the rest in shore villages and hill farms.

How they live: farming the bowl's rich inner slopes, orchards, fine weaving, bronze-casting, stone-cutting. They do not fish the lake, sail it or swim in it. The law against it is older than the kingdom.

Code: the water is not touched. A house has one name and all its people carry it. The king keeps the Gap. A guest is asked his house, not his name.

Rule: a king at Ennerhold. At the lock, King Enner the Younger, thirty-four, the ninth of the line since the god went. The priests of the Soft House, about sixty, keep the god's memory and say it will return.

History: see the deep history, Layer 5. Great for six hundred years under the god Enna, shrinking for three hundred since. They hold the bowl and the Maundhead and claim, without enforcing it, everything they once held.

What they believe about gods: that they had the only one, that it went up to the star for their sake, and that any new thing calling itself a god is either Enna's herald or a liar.

What they want: the god back. Failing that, their old marches. They look at the lake a great deal.

Great events: they sit on the epic and on two long mysteries. The names custom is theirs. Their king's chest holds Queen Annis's account.
