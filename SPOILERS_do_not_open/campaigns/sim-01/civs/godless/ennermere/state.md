population: about 40,000; 9,000 in Ennerhold
stores: good; the bowl has not had a failed harvest in living memory
treasure: large and old; bronze, silver, carved stone, cloth
trade: cloth and bronze work out through the Gap and down the Maund; salt in from Maundsea and the Sallow; little interest in anything else outside
strength: about 3,500 who can be raised, 400 of them the King's Gapwards under arms; walls across the Gap; the rim hills
mood: quiet, inward, a little dreamy; proud; fewer children each generation
