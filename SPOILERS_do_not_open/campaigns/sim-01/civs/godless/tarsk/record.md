# The Tarsk clans: record

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3. Run by the Keeper in the night turn.

Who: human. Horse and cattle herders of the High Grass, in eleven clans. About 20,000, never in one place; they winter round Lake Sere at Serewell and scatter over the grass from thaw to first frost.

How they live: herds, mare's milk, felt tents, bows from horseback. They rake salt from Lake Sere's rim at summer's end and sell it to the Edgefolk and the River Households.

Code: a place is set at every meal for the one still on the road, and a stranger who comes takes it without asking. A guest is safe for three days. Horse theft is answered with blood. The pale herds are not hunted.

Rule: clan heads, and a salt-chief chosen at Serewell each autumn to set the salt price and speak for all. At the lock the salt-chief is Ogrim of the Red Mare clan.

History: out of the east about 600 years ago. They pushed the Edgefolk's forebears north off the grass. They have raided the Folds twice in sixty years, in drought.

What they believe about gods: the sky is one great tent and the star is its smoke-hole. They have no god and want none. They tell of grass far to the east that has no kinds in it.

What they want: grass, water, a good price for salt, and to be left alone. The Red Mare clan wants the Folds' orchards.

Great events: drought on the steppe sends them south and north (event schedule). The traveler Tuale lives among them. The pale herds stray onto their farthest grass. The Longwalkers enter the sheet through them.
