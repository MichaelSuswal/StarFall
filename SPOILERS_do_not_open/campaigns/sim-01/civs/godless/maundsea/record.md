# Maundsea: record

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3. Run by the Keeper in the night turn.

Who: human. The city at the mouth of the Maund and its kingdom on the Low Plain. About 30,000: 14,000 in the city, the rest in the Low Plain's ditched villages.

How they live: grain from the plain, shipping along the South Water, bronze founding, weaving. The king's tallymen take one sheaf in eight from the Bankfolk upriver each autumn and have for 190 years.

Code: the king's measure is the measure. A debt to the crown comes first. A ship's master answers for his crew ashore.

Rule: King Osric Maun, sixty-one, who claims descent from the kings of Old Maund and has none. His tallymen and barge-reeves. A council of ship-owners he mostly ignores.

History: Ennermere's port from 1,400 BA, its own kingdom since 520 BA, lord of the Black Plain's levy since 190 BA, loser of Whitepans in 88 BA.

What they believe about gods: that Ennermere's god was a king's trick. That the old kings sleep in the barrows and bless the crown. The court would treat a god upriver as a rebel lord.

What they want: Whitepans back, and the levy kept. The city cannot feed itself without the levy.

Great events: the levy is the hinge of g4's country. The salt quarrel with Whitepans (event schedule). The Three Bins and the Old Maund barrows lie in land they claim.
