population: about 30,000; 14,000 in the city
stores: thin until the levy comes in; then good
treasure: large; the king's hall, a fleet of forty grain barges and twenty sea boats, bronze
trade: grain and cloth out by sea; salt bought from Whitepans at a price the king resents; timber, tin and copper in
strength: about 2,800 who can be raised, 300 of them the King's Reeves under arms; city walls of brick and timber
mood: proud, anxious about bread, tired of an old king
