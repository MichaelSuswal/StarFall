# The Sallowmen: record

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3. Run by the Keeper in the night turn.

Who: human. Brine-boilers and salt-carriers of the Sallow, the high pale basin over the Carrow Pass. About 9,000: some 2,500 in Brinehall on the Brine Mere, the rest in pan-steads round the shore and herding hamlets on Sallowdown.

How they live: they boil brine in lead and clay pans over peat and dung fires, and carry salt by pack pony west over the Carrow Pass, east along the Underskarn to Stair and beyond, and south down the Wend to Corrford. They smelt a little bog iron from the Wend Heads and sell it dear.

Code: a measure of salt is a measure, never shorted. A carrier's road-right is sacred: no one robs a salt train and trades with the Sallow again. Children are kept from strangers in fever summers.

Rule: seven Pan-Wardens, heads of the oldest pan families, who fix the price each spring. At the lock the eldest is Warden Ebba Thrale, sixty-six.

History: two thousand years on the mere. They were the Hollin's patrons and know, as a thing not said to outsiders, that their fever emptied Carrow. They count the Carrowfolk as cousins of a sort and give them a fair price.

What they believe about gods: that the lake people's god was real and left, and that a god is a kind of king. They would deal with one as with any buyer.

What they want: to keep the price up and the roads open. To find more fuel; peat on the Sallow is running thin.

Great events: the pan fever (event schedule). The Longwalkers cross their northern edge. They are the nearest neighbors of the player's people and the second-nearest of g1's.
