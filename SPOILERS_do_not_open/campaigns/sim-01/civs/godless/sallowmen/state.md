population: about 9,000; 2,500 in Brinehall
stores: good; salt beyond counting, grain bought
treasure: large for the north; bronze, cloth, some silver in the Wardens' house
trade: salt out by three roads, about 400 pony-loads a year; grain, wool, cheese, timber and fuel in
strength: about 900 who can fight, 120 of them road-guards who ride with the trains; a palisade at Brinehall
mood: comfortable, close-fisted, worried about fuel
