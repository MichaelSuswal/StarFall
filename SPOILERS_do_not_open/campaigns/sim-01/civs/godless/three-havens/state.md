population: about 15,000; 6,000 at Sarnmouth
stores: fair; fish, bought grain
treasure: good; ships, bronze, salt in store
trade: Whitepans salt and Maundsea grain west; smoked eel, timber, stone and wool back east; about sixty coasting boats
strength: about 1,200 who can fight, most of them boatmen; timber stockades; the fog and the bars
mood: sharp, cheerful, sure of themselves
