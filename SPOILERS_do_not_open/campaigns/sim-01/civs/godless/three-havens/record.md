# The Three Havens: record

Campaign: sim-01. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3. Run by the Keeper in the night turn.

Who: human. Three free towns on the Murk and the Sarn mouth: Sarnmouth, the chief, at the river mouth; Murkhaven on the bay's east beach; Westhaven on its north shore. About 15,000 between them and their fishing hamlets.

How they live: fishing, coasting trade in fog, pilotage, boat building. They carry Whitepans salt west along the South Water and sell it up the Sarn as far as Corrford, and they hold the only way salt reaches the Cutfolk.

Code: a pilot's word in fog is law aboard. A wreck's cargo belongs to the finder, its crew to no one: they are fed and sent home. A bargain made on the quay is witnessed by the harbor bell.

Rule: each town has a Harbor Reeve chosen by its boat-owners; the three meet at Sarnmouth each spring. At the lock Sarnmouth's reeve is Tam Greaves, a hard bargainer.

History: founded by the survivors of drowned Halmer's coast villages, 2,400 years ago. Free ever since, because no one else can find the channels.

What they believe about gods: sailors' beliefs. Something in the fog that takes boats and is owed the first fish. No god has ever been theirs.

What they want: the salt carrying trade kept in their hands, and Corrford's tolls lowered.

Great events: they are the pinch on two god-led peoples' salt. They hear news from the whole south coast first.
