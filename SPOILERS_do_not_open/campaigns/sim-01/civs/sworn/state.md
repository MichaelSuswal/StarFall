population: about 3,000; some 1,800 in Corrford town, the rest in about thirty holdings
stores: fair; the town granary a third full after winter; spring fishing begun
treasure: modest; toll silver and bronze in the Hall chest, worth about a year's tolls
trade: ford tolls on the west road; salt bought from Sarnmouth boats and Sallow pack trains at a hard price; stone, flax and wool out
strength: about 450 who can fight, 60 of them the ford wardens who drill; old walls in fair repair on the bluff
mood: busy, argumentative, proud of the Ford Peace; sore at Sarnmouth; no god, and no notion one has come
