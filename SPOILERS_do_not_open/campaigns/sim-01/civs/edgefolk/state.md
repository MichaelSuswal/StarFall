population: about 2,000, in seven steadings on the forest edge; gathering at Antlerhall at autumn's end
stores: good; meat drying, herds fat; salt for about half the winter's curing in hand
treasure: herds; hides, timber, some bronze
trade: salt from the Tarsk for hides and timber; furs west along the edge track
strength: about 400 who can fight, most mounted or good with a bow; no walls; halls of heavy timber
mood: high at the end of a good season; uneasy about the price the Tarsk will ask for winter salt; no god known
