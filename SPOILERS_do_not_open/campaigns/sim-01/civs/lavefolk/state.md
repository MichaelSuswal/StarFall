population: about 2,000, in hamlets and steadings on the lower Lave; some 300 at Piers
stores: good; hay and grain in, stock fat
treasure: modest; bronze, cloth, a little silver at Piers
trade: Whitepans salt by pack and boat; reed, wool, cheese and cloth out; a market at Piers each new moon
strength: about 300 who can fight; no walls anywhere
mood: easy, house-proud, incurious about the country beyond the hills; no god known
