population: about 3,000, in ten villages along the middle Maund; Stonesteading the largest at about 420
stores: a fair harvest in; bins about two thirds full before the levy; seed set aside in every village
treasure: little; grain and flax are the wealth; some bronze
trade: grain down the Maund to Maundsea, by levy and by sale; salt, bronze and fish up from the city at the city's price
strength: about 500 who can fight with spear and bill; no walls; dykes
mood: tired and satisfied after harvest, sullen about the levy due in eleven days; no god, and no notion one has come
