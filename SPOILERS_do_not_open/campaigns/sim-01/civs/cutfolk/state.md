population: about 1,500, in some forty steadings on the cuts; gathered at the Long Roof on Eelholm from first frost
stores: good for the season; smoked eel and goose, peat stacked; grain thin, as always
treasure: almost none; smoked eel and reed work are the trade goods
trade: Sarnmouth boats twice a year, salt and bronze in, smoked eel, down and reed mats out
strength: about 220 who can fight, with fowling bows and eel spears; no walls; the marsh itself, which strangers cannot cross
mood: settled, dry-humored, turning inward for winter; one singer, Old Wenna Marl, is failing and they know it
