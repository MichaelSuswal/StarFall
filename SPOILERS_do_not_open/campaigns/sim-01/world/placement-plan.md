# Placement plan: sim-01 (Night 3, written before any painting)

A written geography. The paintings are made from this, and the map data is then traced onto the paintings; where the paint differs, the data follows the paint and this plan is not corrected.

## Scale and frame

One square sheet, about 1,640 miles on a side (1,024 painting pixels at 1.6 miles to the pixel). North is up. Positions below are given as parts of the sheet (west, center, east; north, middle, south) and as rough pixel aims, x east and y south. The star's living radius is 110 days' walk, so the whole sheet lies inside the rain; the Pale is far beyond every edge.

## The frame of sea and land

Sea on three sides. A narrow cold sea, the Grey Reach, runs down the whole west edge. It widens in the south-west corner into a shallow, fogbound bay, the Murk. The sea continues along the whole south edge as a strip, the South Water, widens again in the south-east corner and runs up the east edge to about three fifths of the way down the sheet, where the land takes the edge back. The north edge and the north-east are land to the edge of the sheet and beyond.

## High ground

- The Skarn, the great range: a wall of snow mountains along the north, from the north-west quarter across to the north-east quarter, highest in the middle, its valleys opening south. Aim x 300 to 720, y 60 to 160.
- Vorn: one bare, dry, grey mountain standing a little apart at the east end of the Skarn. Aim (700, 150).
- The Westermoor: upland moor and broken hills in the north-west, the Skarn's western shoulder, long valleys running south and west, a strip of lower coast country between it and the Grey Reach. Aim x 120 to 330, y 130 to 400.
- The Sallow: a high closed basin between the Westermoor and the Skarn, pale with salt flats and brine springs. Aim (300, 170).
- The Ennerwold: a broad ring of old worn hills in the very middle of the sheet, enclosing a bowl. Aim center (535, 480), ring radius about 110.
- The Folds: low rounded hills in the east, folded close around a river. Aim x 760 to 930, y 460 to 580.
- The Lave hills: gentle hills standing back on both sides of a river in the south-east. Aim x 660 to 820, y 660 to 830.
- The Corr breaks: broken, water-cut hill country in the west, rust-colored cliffs, where a river comes through a gorge. Aim x 180 to 330, y 440 to 560.

## The center

Lake Enner: one large, dark, still, roughly round lake in the bowl of the Ennerwold, with no river leaving it. Aim (535, 480), about 60 pixels across. This is the point under the star.

## Rivers (each runs downhill to a coast, except the Enn)

- The Enn: from the middle of the Skarn south to Lake Enner, where it ends. Aim (510, 180) to (530, 450).
- The Wend, with the Carrow Water at its head: from the Westermoor south to join the Corr. Aim (232, 240) to (265, 495).
- The Corr: from the western slopes of the Ennerwold west through the Corr gorge, out across a stony plain in shifting channels to the Grey Reach. Aim (400, 480) to (62, 545).
- The Sarn: from the south-west of the Ennerwold, slow and brown, south-west into a great marsh and the Murk. Aim (380, 620) to (130, 860).
- The Maund: from the southern slopes of the Ennerwold due south across a wide black plain to the South Water. Aim (480, 610) to (505, 965).
- The Lave: from the south-east of the Ennerwold south-east between its hills to a delta of marsh and many mouths on the south-east coast. Aim (650, 620) to (815, 905).
- The Ishe: from the eastern slopes of the Ennerwold east through the Folds to the east sea. Aim (650, 470) to (975, 600).
- The Tarrow: from the forest in the north-east, east across the steppe and off the sheet. Aim (760, 200) to (1024, 330).

## Low ground, forest, grass, marsh, coast

- The Dimmet: deep dark conifer forest across the north-east, from the east end of the Skarn to the sheet's north and east edges. Aim x 700 to 1024, y 0 to 210.
- The forest edge: where the Dimmet gives out south and east onto the grass. Aim a line from (730, 230) to (1000, 180).
- The High Grass: pale gold steppe in the east and north-east, south of the Dimmet and north of the Folds, running off the east edge. Aim x 800 to 1024, y 220 to 460.
- Lake Sere: a white-rimmed salt lake on the steppe. Aim (960, 400).
- The Holt: broadleaf forest between the Westermoor and the Ennerwold. Aim x 330 to 440, y 280 to 440.
- The Black Plain: flat dark farmland on both banks of the Maund, with one low hill standing alone. Aim x 400 to 600, y 700 to 940; the hill at (507, 838).
- The Sarnmoss: reed marsh, alder and standing water around the lower Sarn. Aim x 110 to 260, y 760 to 900.
- The fog coast: the low shore of the Murk, flat and indistinct. Aim x 90 to 170, y 700 to 960.
- The Lavemouths: delta marsh on the south-east coast. Aim (815, 905).
- The long coast: the south-east and east shore, long beaches and low cliffs, with pale salt pans at one place. Aim from (620, 960) round the corner to (990, 620); the pans at (900, 800).
- The Corr plain: stony flood plain between the Corr breaks and the west coast. Aim x 70 to 180, y 480 to 600.
- Wild country, unheld: the Holt; the southern Westermoor; the scrub and wood between the Black Plain and the Sarn (aim x 280 to 400, y 700 to 900); the hill country between the Lave and the Maund; the north-east Dimmet; the middle steppe.

Neighbor distances by the aims: 450, 456, 459, 470, 406, 478, 463, 483 miles round the ring. On rough ground at 14 miles a day that is 29 to 35 days' walking with no rest and no obstacle, in practice six to eight weeks. The lake at the center is 480 to 600 miles from every seat.

## The godless peoples, between the seats

- The Sallowmen: the Sallow, over the pass from Carrow. Between the player and g1.
- Ennermere: the bowl of the Ennerwold round Lake Enner. The middle.
- The Three Havens: three towns on the west coast, Greyhaven north of the Corr's mouth, Corrmouth at it, Lowhaven toward the Murk. Between the player, g2 and g3.
- The Tarsk clans: the High Grass, with Lake Sere. Between g7 and g5.
- Maundsea: the city at the mouth of the Maund and the lowest plain. Between g4, g3 and g6.
- Whitepans, a free salt town on the long coast, is counted with the peoples of the south-east, not as a sixth people (it is described under Maundsea's rivals and the Lave).

## Underground (data only; no painting was approved for these in this test)

- Layer 1, the Low Ways: the halls inside Vorn; four outlying sealed halls on the old High Roads (one in the Westermoor, one in the Corr breaks, one under the Sarnmoss, one in the Folds); the carved halls under the north Ennerwold.
- Layer 2, the Glass: a sheet of blue-grey glass under the deepest ruins in the bowl of the Ennerwold, reached at one place only.

## Climate across the sheet

Harsh everywhere (blend 2 of 10). Hardest in the Skarn, the Sallow, the Westermoor and the steppe: winters of five months or more, passes shut. Less hard on the Black Plain, the Lave, the Ishe and the coasts: winters of three to four months, rivers iced at the edges. The west coast is wet and windy, the Murk fogbound most mornings, the steppe dry and cold in the lee of the Ennerwold and the Skarn. Rain shadow: the Sallow and the High Grass are dry; the Westermoor, the Corr breaks and the Sarn country are wet.
