### the Low Ways (`under-1`, depth 1)

- the Halls of Vorn (`vorn-halls`). About 600 Abiders. The Count is cut on the walls of the lowest hall.

- the hall behind the Shut House (`shut-hall`). One Abider, Ninth-of-the-Door.

- the Sunk Hall (`sunk-hall`). Flooded to the knee. Empty since the drowning of Halmer.

- the hall behind the Debt Door (`debt-hall`). Holds the pledge of the Vane and Orrel houses: a bronze cauldron of split tallies.

- the Leaf Halls (`leaf-hall-ways`). Five halls in a line. High Ennermere's, 900 years old. Anyone who spends a night inside comes out missing one small particular thing.

- the hall behind the Sworn Door (`sworn-hall`). Empty. A stone bench facing the wall away from the lake.

- the Dry Loft (`dry-loft-store`). Forty sealed jars; nine hold live seed.

### the Glass (`under-2`, depth 2)

- the Glass (`the-glass`). Blue-grey glass that rings when struck, under everything in the bowl. Reached only by the Ringing Stair.

- the foot of the Ringing Stair (`ringing-stair-foot`).

No painting exists for either underground layer. Michael approved two paintings for this test (surface and cloud). The layers are data only; `map.json` names painting files that are not there, and the map tool will need them before any underground sheet can be drawn.
