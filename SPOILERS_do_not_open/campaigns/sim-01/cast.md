# The small cast: sim-01

World-level figures placed at creation, each with nature and history fixed. Everyone else rises from play. Places are `map.json` ids.

1. **Lake Enner** [enner]. A being, not a place. Nature and wants: the epic in `mysteries.md`. It does not speak. It keeps bargains.
2. **The Stayer** [vorn-halls]. The bound god in Vorn, from the country before the last fall. Neither mortal nor a god as the eight are. Answers one question truly per asker per year, in few words, and volunteers nothing. Wants nothing it will say. From the Q18 pool: a being long here that tells the truth when asked.
3. **The Abiders** [vorn-halls], about 590 in Vorn. A people as a cast member. Old, slow, exact, unable to lie with any ease, keeping one law. Their speaker to outsiders, when they need one, is Third-of-the-Count, a woman of about 300. They keep the Count: a record cut in stone of what has been done in the land, village by village, century by century. From the Q13 pool (the one non-human kind this world holds) and the Q19 pool.
4. **Ninth-of-the-Door** [shut-hall]. The last Abider outside Vorn, 480 years old, alone 250 years in the hall under Windlow Moor. Sweeps his sill. Walks the High Road to the Old Men once a year, at night, in the ninth month, and looks down into Carrow. Speaks human speech of 300 years ago, very slowly. Knew the Hollin. Has watched the Carrowfolk arrive and has never spoken to one. Curious, lonely, and not aware that he is either. Dies in the winter of Year 14.
5. **The Longwalkers** (on their road; off the sheet until Year 6). About 140. Their list-keeper at the lock is a woman called Odalys, about sixty. Nature: `mysteries.md`, Long 4.
6. **The Grey Hound** [holt]. The last of a line of great hounds the Hollin bred for forty generations and kept pure: grey, rough-coated, tall as a pony at the shoulder, about ninety years old and not failing. It has run alone in the Holt since its people died. It is a beast, with a beast's mind and an old beast's judgment. It follows no one. It has, twice, walked beside a traveler for a season and then left. From the Q18 pool: a thing out of the wild that may choose to stay.
7. **Pell Harrow** (at the lock, in [corrford-town], lodging by the ford). A Sarnmouth-born trader's son of twenty-six who walks the roads pacing distances and writing them on birch bark, for no one. Has walked from Maundsea to the Sallow. Carries eleven rolls. Honest, talkative, bad with money. From the Q17 pool: the wandering recorder.
8. **Hesper Vane** [postlanding]. Reckoner of the River Households, sixty. Keeps the common count and one secret (the Debt Door's true terms). Has refused two bribes and one marriage to keep the office clean. From the Q17 pool: the keeper of faith.
9. **Sefa Vey** [whitepans]. Granddaughter of the traveler Issan Vey, thirty-eight, a brine-grader. Keeps his case of instruments and uses two of them. From the Q14 pool.
10. **King Enner the Younger** [ennerhold]. Thirty-four, ninth king since the god went. Reads the old slats. Has begun to wonder why his own children answer to one name. Able, proud, and afraid of the answer.
11. **Marra Voss** [antlerhall]. Headwoman of the Edgefolk, fifty-one. A full hall and true scales. From the Q17 pool: the host.
12. **Enna** (in the star). The risen god of Ennermere. Reaches down only in dreams, near the lake and to gods near the ceiling. Not lonely, it says.

Companions (Q18 pool). Beings able to become a companion to a god, none assigned to any: the Stayer, Ninth-of-the-Door, the Grey Hound, and any mortal above. None is a servant. Each can refuse.
