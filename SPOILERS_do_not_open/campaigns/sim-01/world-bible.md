# World bible: sim-01

Written at the lock, creation Night 3. A summary with pointers; the named files hold the detail and win where this file is shorter. The deep lore wins over all of them.

## Rules, final (one rule each, the whole world)

- God-sense: face to face only, about as far as speech carries; a plain knowing that the other is one like oneself, and nothing more. Nothing at a distance unasked. The deep lore's pull exists as something investigation can reveal: at the shore of Lake Enner, and at the Door of Vorn, a god feels faintly which way each other god lies. Nowhere else. This is the Night 1 ruling made concrete; it is a fact of two places, not a second rule.
- Oaths between gods: bind with real force; collected on later and sideways. The hidden form is in `star.md`.
- Bodies: one.
- Mortal magic: level 2 of 10 after the clamp. One known worker in a country. Feel and cost as blended on Night 1 (`world-draft.md`), inside Truth 4. Every god-led people has exactly one worker; each is named in its record with what she has spent. The local wrong understandings from Night 1 are held as follows: "the price is named first and is fair" by the Edgefolk; "only the spender pays" by the River Households; "it costs years of life" by the Stairfolk and the Bankfolk; "it is paid in a sworn word" by the Sworn; "someone keeps the ledger" by the River Households.
- How much gods stand out: 6 of 10. Awe and wariness, not helplessness. Gods can be wrong; mortals can oppose them.
- Climate: 2 of 10, harsh. The wild: 3 of 10, mostly ordinary. Past the borders: 6 of 10, five godless peoples. Age: 10 of 10. Non-human peoples: 2 of 10, one kind, the Abiders, and no second. Travelers from afar: 2 of 10, two: Issan Vey (dead) and Tuale (living).
- Scarcities: salt, from three sources only (the Brine Mere, Lake Sere, Whitepans); reliable faith. Iron is short everywhere and comes mostly from the Wend Heads bogs through the Sallow.

## The star

`star.md`. Mature. Ceiling 74. Rain density 1. Living radius 110 days' walk. Center body a lake. All three long-game kinds. No Remainder. Content one enormous forest. Ascended mixed.

## The land

`map.json` (traced on `map/surface.png`), `world/gazetteer.md`, `world/placement-plan.md`. One sheet, 1,638 miles on a side. 91 regions. Sea to the south and south-east and a bay in the south-west; land to every other edge. The Skarn along the north. Lake Enner in the middle, about 400 miles across. Two underground layers as data only.

Where the painting overruled the plan: there is no western sea; the west edge is land. The lake is far larger than planned. One river, not two, drains the west (Wend, Corr, Sarn). The Maund rises under the lake's south rim. The forest between the moors and the bowl lies further west. The seats were re-set on the painted ground.

## Where each Night 1 candidate went

Placed: old roads (the High Roads); sealed doors (four Abider halls, the Debt Door being one reused); the oath-frame door (the Sworn Door); the song-lock door (the Singing Door); enormous bones (the Ribs); empty granaries (the Three Bins); buried carved halls (the Leaf Halls); unreadable tablets (the Law Stones); a road with another idea of distance (the High Roads' forty-mile stages); a pass that closes (Carrow Pass, the High Stair); a sickness on a trade road (the pan fever); a village turned on itself by rumor (S8); ground that gives way (S4); a beast that hunts hunters (S6); something abroad in the lampless hours (S8); a place that listens back and a place that takes a price to enter (Lake Enner; the Leaf Halls); a door in the rock (the Door of Vorn); a being long here that tells the truth (the Stayer); a thing out of the wild that may stay (the Grey Hound); the stranger's place at table (the Tarsk); the non-human main shape (the Abiders), who also keep records; all seven kinds of mortal worth following appear among the cast and the named people of the records; traveler's tool and half-working speech, a seed, plain knowledge (Issan Vey, Tuale).
Left out, since the blend held one non-human kind and no more: the night-keeping people, the forest path-keepers, the grass breeders, a people who lie as courtesy. A map no one can read and a trick of smithing were left out as one traveler's gift too many. A strength that comes of being many is not a place; it is the deep lore's shedding and is found by doing it.
Undared wishes: `mysteries.md`, last check.

## Limits of this test, for whoever reads the world later

- Two paintings only. The two underground layers have no paintings.
- The sheet's scale (1.6 miles to a pixel) is far from the map rules' 0.25, and regions are 40 to 250 miles across, not 20 to 60. Regions near the seats are smallest.
- Region polygons were traced by raster from seed points and simplified to about a pixel; neighbors may show hairline gaps or overlaps of under two pixels.
