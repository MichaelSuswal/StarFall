# The event schedule: campaign sim-04

Written on creation night 3b, 2026-11-22. Years are years from the arrival. A season named in a row is the season in the country the row names, in that numbered year, since the gods woke into different seasons of one arrival. Every range and method was written in the keeper log before its roll. Nothing here is released; signs reach a god only through who noticed, or through its own senses. Counts of recurring things are never taken from this file: the night turn runs `tools/phenomena.py SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-04 <year>`.

## Random events

Each country, each year, in pass one: one roll with `tools/roll.py --campaign sim-04`, base 30, the factor `opening=-20` in Years 1 to 7, so one chance in ten a year in the opening and three in ten from Year 8 (`clue-design.md`, the pacing spine). For a god-led country the sealed factors are applied after the stated odds, since most kinds below are troubles: 5 points off for each step of that god's luck, the chance never under 5, named in `--what` before the roll. On a success, the kind by `--die d10`:

| Face | Years 1 to 7 | Year 8 onward |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | weather: a late frost | weather: a late frost or a wet harvest |
| 2 | weather: a wet harvest | weather: a dry summer |
| 3 | weather: a dry summer | beasts: wolves, a lost herd, a swarm year |
| 4 | beasts: wolves on the high ground | births: twins, a strong lambing |
| 5 | beasts: a lost herd, a swarm year | a quarrel over a boundary or a debt |
| 6 | births: twins | a quarrel over a marriage or an inheritance |
| 7 | births: a strong lambing or calving | strangers on the road |
| 8 | a quarrel over a boundary | a local sickness, of people or of stock |
| 9 | a quarrel over a debt | a fire |
| 10 | a quarrel over a marriage | a find: something old turned up by plough or flood, from the nearest site the gazetteer holds for that country |

No random event places anything the world did not hold at the lock.

## Checks

- Every mystery's cost if ignored is a row above: E01 in C07, C09 and T11; L01 in C04, T09 and T12; L02 in T04, T05 and T06; L03 in T01 and T02; L04 in C03, C11 and T08; S01 in C12; S02 in T10; S03 in C13 and T03; S04 in C14; S05 in C15; S06 in C16; S07 in C17; S08 in C18.
- Weather and other natural harms the Keeper set itself (C01, C02, C05, C06): one falls in Years 1 to 7 (C01), on one people, so no people takes more than one in the opening. C02 and C05 both fell on one people by their rolls, in Years 8 and 12, outside the opening; neither was moved.
- Where a roll chose which people a natural harm falls on (C01, C02, C05, C08), the choice was weighted away from a lucky god's people by its steps, named in the roll before it was made.
- No event herds the gods toward one place, and no path or secret way exists for the plot (DESIGN 8.5). The carriers' round is theirs and passes where it passes. The stone, the lake and every site lie where the map already had them; nothing summons anyone to any of them.
- No row names, or has any mortal give, a thing the player left unnamed for play.
- Headings are kept to two levels and events to table rows.
