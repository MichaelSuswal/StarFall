# The world bible: campaign sim-04

Written on creation night 3b, 2026-11-22. The sealed summary of the whole world. No codex or other public summary is built; the player's files and the morning stories carry the history. Where this file and a file it points to differ, the file it points to stands.

## The name
The Keeper's name for the world is Ulverdan. No people uses it. What each people calls the world and its star is in `cast.md`.

## The rules made final
One rule each for the whole world; no wish made a rule read differently in any place.
- God-sense: nothing at any distance. Within speaking distance a god feels another as a weight and a stillness in the air about it; slow, fallible, and able to be hidden from for a while by a god that takes pains. A weight, never a count. Every god knows that others like it came down when it did, and nothing more.
- Bodies: one.
- Mortal magic: level 3 (low and real), inside the star's 2 to 4. It is simple and it costs the worker herself: sleep first, then memory, last her name. About one worker to five hundred people in every people (ledger L-0027). A school is more than twelve workers under one patron (schedule row T03).
- How gods stand out: 7. A god stands well above any mortal worker; one that keeps quiet can pass for a strange lord or a great sorcerer for a time.
- Climate 4, the hard side of the middle: four true seasons everywhere. Held and open land about half and half. The wild 3: familiar beasts nearly everywhere. Non-human peoples 2: one. Travelers 2: two.
- Technology: before the medieval era, no steel forges; a starting level and not a ceiling (DESIGN 4.7).
- Oaths are not a rule of the world. Gods cannot die.
- The hour of the arrival: first light, the same for all eight.

## The star

## Where each Night 1 candidate went

- Q7 minority understandings (a working as a debt to a lender; a working that holds while an oath holds; a working as a thirst): kept only as what some workers believe, each wrong about something that matters. None is a second system.
