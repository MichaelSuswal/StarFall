## E01 The Long Lowering
Kind: epic. Span: 60 years and past them. Related to: L01, L02, L03, L04, S01, S02.
Answers questions: yes

### Truth
The star is the gathered particularity of a vast spent country, hanging over the place where the land breathes out hardest, and it is coming down. It stood about 52 miles over the lake's middle when the oldest sightline was laid and stands 40 at the arrival. It loses height by the square of the years and falls in Year 146 [r-0165]. The fall ends what stands under it and pours new country over the ground: both, always. What is kept densely enough comes through as itself: a name sung yearly by a thousand mouths, a breed kept pure, a thing given to the carriers and planted under another star, a god bound to something that can bear it. The lake wants the fall and will lie under it. Most of those who rose into the star want it soon. The answer, once found, still has to be done, across decades, and no one god can do it alone: names need many mouths in more than one country, the carriers must agree to carry, and the lake and the voices pull the other way.

The central measurements are two: how fast the star is lowering and that the lowering quickens, which together give when; and what comes through. Each is earned only by a god's own instrument, a counter it sent, an alliance it made or a place it went. The ways, all of them: its own marks set in stone at two places and read at two intervals; a sighting arc built from what Isk Vennar can teach, and two readings with it; entries of the Oum's Count bought one at a time and reckoned by the god itself; the old sightline at the Turnings [f31] set against its own present sighting, which gives the whole lowering and no rate; and, for what comes through, a thing the god has itself stored or given to be carried, and its own watching of what the ticks then take and leave. None is a scheduled visitor: the carriers pass in Year 10 and Year 40 and carry no measure of the star, and both travelers stay where they are.

Limit: The Oum know only what is entered in the Count: arrivals, risings, deaths, debts, terms, and where the great light stood against the peaks at each hundredth year, in fists and fingers. They do not know causes, rates, the lake's nature, the star's nature, what a risen god becomes or anything the Count does not hold, and they say so. They do not lie and do not volunteer. They answer only a question put in person at Koldry [f81] by one who first gives an entry of equal weight for the Count, one question for one entry, no more than one in a season from any one asker, and a question that asks them to reckon, judge or guess is answered "It is not entered."

Limit: Isk Vennar knows how her arc is held and read and about half of how one is made. She does not know why her line walked, what the star here is, its height or its rate, and she has taken no reading here, so she has no figure to give. She answers anyone who comes to her at Skarhaven [f83] and asks; teaching the making takes a year beside her, and the half she lacks must be worked out by the learner.

### Body
The star, in every sky. Lake Hesh [r28] under it. The Turnings [f31]. The blue-grey glass under the round vault behind Gathol Door [f24] and under Ammeth Hall [f82]. The Oum's hundred-year notes at Koldry [f81]. Isk Vennar's arc at Skarhaven [f83]. The old stores of the road-builders: the Comb Barrows [f62], Carn Vault [f40], Wether Door [f41].

### Cost if ignored
The fall itself lies past sixty years. Inside them: the lowering is plain to a careful eye by Year 20 and to everyone by Year 40, and when it is common talk the peoples look for a cause and find the gods, who came late. A god that never measured is then surprised by everything that already knew, and has stored nothing: at each tick its country loses kinds it could have kept, and nothing of its people is set to come through.
Cost check: no agreed weakness or ruling bears on it. Checked: every card's Weaknesses taken (the Hollow Season; Guest-law; the plea for water; the Oath at the Threshold; the Barred Door; none on three cards), the people's edges L-0019, L-0020 and L-0023 to L-0026, and the world rulings L-0009 and L-0027; none speaks of the star, of kinds kept or lost, or of blame.

### Clock or trigger
Clock: rows C09 (the lowering by year) and C07 (the ticks). Trigger: row T11 (the lowering becomes common talk).

## L01 The Unrippled Water
Kind: long. Span: 30 years. Related to: E01, L03.
Answers questions: yes

### Truth
Lake Hesh [r28] is not a lake with a thing in it. It is one living thing, older than the last fall, that eats what makes things particular and stores it. It lay under the last impact and came through, and it means to lie under the next and take a great share of what pours out. It is fed from above: on nights the star dims, a thread of blue light comes down to its middle, sent by those in the star who want the fall soon. It hastens the fall by making the country round it plain. Over thousands of years it has taken a city and the names of the people on its shore, by being what they look at. Under this star it is swollen. From Year 11 [r-0172] it draws sleepers into the water: first the nameless of the shore, then, as its reach widens, people within three days' walk, a week's, nine days'. Force used against it feeds it.

Limit: The voices know what they were, what they want and the powers they offer. They do not know why the plane keeps accounts, what they will become after a fall, whether they are one or many, or the year of the fall, and asked these they say they do not know. They cannot lie as mortals lie and have no view but their own. They speak only in dreams, only to one sleeping on the shore of Lake Hesh [r28] on a night the star dims or to a god at or past four fifths of the ceiling, one exchange in a night, and they answer no question about the star's height or its rate.

### Body
Lake Hesh [r28]. The thread. The shore people at Lowater [f67], Nenna [f66] and Ammot [f68]. The drowned city under the water off Nenna [f66].

Limit: The shore people know what they have seen at the water and what their grandparents told them. They do not know what the lake is or why names go, and most do not answer at all. One of the oldest will speak, once, to a stranger who has stayed a night among them and asked nothing the first day.

### Cost if ignored
From Year 11 the shore people walk into the water in their sleep and are not found, in the numbers the record holds. From Year 14 it takes from farms within three days' walk of the water; from Year 17 within a week's walk, which reaches Pellam [f36] and the upper landings of the Maud [f07] above Maudry [f64]; from Year 20 within nine days' walk, which reaches Maudry [f64] itself and the western steadings of Vestfen [r25]. Those are the first god-led peoples it reaches, by distance alone.
Cost check: checked against god-dreams' card (the Sleeper's Door, the Hollow Season, L-0014), the Merrowen's edge (L-0024), god-rivers' card (Reading the water, Clean water, the plea for water, L-0011, L-0012) and the Maudrins' edge (L-0025, which speaks of flux and water fever only): a sleeper taken is not a sickness, not foul water and not a dream entered, and none is contradicted.

Clock: row C04 (the takings and their reach), with the counts in `state/phenomena.json`. Triggers: rows T09 (force against the lake) and T12 (the shore people given names).

## L02 The Warm Stone
Kind: long. Span: 30 years. Related to: E01, L03.
Answers questions: yes

### Truth
The eight gods are what the fall of a far country threw: not persons, but the patterns that country held hardest, each a people's long memory of being led or kept. That country is 190 years' walking to the east. One piece of the same throw did not come together as a god. It fell as stone in Ammerholt [r24] in the same hour (the unnamed fallen stone [f69]): warm, blue-grey, heavy for its size. It holds the same memories thinly and dreams them at anyone who sleeps near: a crowd kneeling, and a river none of them has seen, which is the river in every one of the eight gods' own fog. A god that touches it knows the river. One mortal in the land carries words from that same country without knowing what they are.

Limit: Caddo Reth knows his chant, his warrant's words, his craft with horse and saddle, and what his father told him: that the line rode out of a river country rather than be hunted. He does not know what the names in the chant are, what the warrant bound, how far the country lies or that it has ended, and he says so. He answers anyone who asks him civilly at Varn [f78], as often as they like, and recites the chant only to one he has come to trust, which takes a season of dealing.

### Body
The unnamed fallen stone [f69] in Ammerholt [r24]. The gods' shared hour of arrival. Caddo Reth at Varn [f78], his saddle, his warrant and his chant.

### Cost if ignored
If no god has reached the stone by Year 19 [r-0173], the charcoal burners and hunters of the forest's edge and the Harrow Roughs [r27] have found it, sleep beside it, and have a priesthood of about thirty that is no god's and that chants the gods' own wound. If one god reaches it before the others, that god learns the shape of every other god's past, which is a lever.
Cost check: no agreed weakness or ruling bears on it. Checked: every card's Weaknesses taken and L-0001 to L-0027; none speaks of the stone, of a god's past, or of worship given to a thing that is no god.

### Clock or trigger

### Clues

#### The stone itself

Readings: a piece of the light; a thing that failed to wake; a piece of wherever the gods came from; a wonder and no more.

What is shown: a framed saddle with iron loops for the feet. SEEN, by anyone at Varn [f78].

What the world does: it dreams the crowd and the river and knows the river for its own. Row T05: it now has what the stone holds of the others' pasts, in the same thin form.

#### The god carries the stone into its town as a relic
What the world does: row T06. The town dreams the wound. Within four years there is a cult of that grief, which is not worship of the god and gives it nothing.

## L03 The Lifted Kings
Kind: long. Span: 30 years. Related to: E01, L01.
Answers questions: yes

### Truth
A god grows as more minds hold its shape. Under this star there is a weight past which the star's pull is stronger than the ground's, and a god heavier than that lightens and rises into the star for good. Here the mark is 80 on the lore's scale, where a god just landed stands near 20; from 64 those already risen begin to speak to it in dreams, each offer a power that costs weight toward the mark. Three gods came down here 2,614 years ago [r-0164] and all three rose: Marrec by ordering more worship to root himself; Ammeth after binding itself to a hill that could not bear it; Hollin last, after giving itself away for four centuries. Each left its mark on the land. There are ways to stand past the mark: to bind to a thing great enough to carry it, to give power away into people and places, or not to grow. No one living knows any of this in these terms.

Limit: The voices know what they were, what they want and the powers they offer. They do not know why the plane keeps accounts, what they will become after a fall, whether they are one or many, or the year of the fall, and asked these they say they do not know. They cannot lie as mortals lie and have no view but their own. They speak only in dreams, only to one sleeping on the shore of Lake Hesh [r28] on a night the star dims or to a god at or past four fifths of the ceiling, one exchange in a night, and they answer no question about the star's height or its rate.

Limit: The Oum know only what is entered in the Count: arrivals, risings, deaths, debts, terms, and where the great light stood against the peaks at each hundredth year, in fists and fingers. They do not know causes, rates, the lake's nature, the star's nature, what a risen god becomes or anything the Count does not hold, and they say so. They do not lie and do not volunteer. They answer only a question put in person at Koldry [f81] by one who first gives an entry of equal weight for the Count, one question for one entry, no more than one in a season from any one asker, and a question that asks them to reckon, judge or guess is answered "It is not entered."

### Body
The god's own shadow. Ammeth Hall [f82] and its dry hill. Hollin Spring [f74]. The bones and the bronze in the old way behind Gathol Door [f24]. The torn court roll at Hallisk [f35]. The Count at Koldry [f81].

### Cost if ignored
A god that reaches 64 hears the voices. A god that takes their offers without understanding passes 80, and after three years above it rises on the next night the star dims, and is gone from the ground. The world warns three times: its shadow pales, its worked stone softens at the edges, the voices speak. It does not warn a fourth time.
Cost check: checked against every card's Ceiling line (`Set by the world. Not shown.`) and the ceiling line at the foot of each sealed hidden-powers file (80; the voices at 64): the same figures. No agreed weakness or ruling (L-0001 to L-0027) speaks of a god's weight or of rising; none is contradicted.

## L04 The List-Singers
Kind: long. Span: 30 years. Related to: E01.
Answers questions: yes

### Truth
The carriers are a people, few and human-seeming, who have come through at least two falls by singing names. A name sung by enough mouths for enough years is dense enough to cross bare country and to come through an impact, and the carriers bear names from dying countries to living ones and plant them. A band of about forty walks one round through this land every thirty years: ashore at Varn [f78], up the Skein [f08], across to the Maud [f07], round the lake's south and west, down the Carn [f05] to Carnmouth [f42]. Their last passing was twenty years before the arrival; the next are Year 10 [r-0174] and Year 40 [r-0175]. They taught the road-builders, who built the stores. They will carry a country's names if asked and taught them, and unasked if they judge it worth it. They want nothing else.

Limit: The carriers know the names in their lists, the order of their round, and that a name carried long enough comes back. They do not know why, what the star is, when anything will happen, or the name of the country the gods came from, and they say so. They answer only when spoken to directly by the one who asks, in the nights of a passing, once to any asker in a passing; they sing nothing under compulsion, and no one of them holds a whole song.

### Body
The band on its round. Their songs. The Comb Barrows [f62], where a people once stored its names in wax. The name-stones on the glass in the round vault behind Gathol Door [f24].

### Cost if ignored
A god that does not speak with them in Year 10 has no one to ask in Year 40, and nothing of its country is carried: whatever it later learns about what comes through, it has no way to send anything out before the fall. A god that seizes them loses the Year 40 passing for every country on the round.
Cost check: no agreed weakness or ruling bears on it. Checked: every card's Weaknesses taken and L-0001 to L-0027. The round passes Carnford [f39], whose people keep open gates (L-0026): the band is a peaceful comer and the weakness is not touched.

### Clock or trigger
