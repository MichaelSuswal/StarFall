# Sealed: god-law

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### The Turned Chase
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: When an oath sworn to the god is broken by a killing, the god may take up the trail: for a season it knows the direction of the oath-breaker, wherever they are, and walks toward them without losing the way, by day and night.
Drawback: While it pursues, it cannot turn aside for any other matter until it has stood within earshot of the breaker or the season ends, every other breach strikes it as two, and its own people are without it for as long.
Limits:
  - Only for a breach that reaches a killing, and only one trail at a time.
  - It finds; it does nothing to the one found. What happens then is the god's act and the world's.
  - If the breaker dies, the trail ends where they lie.
  - A breaker who is more than a mortal is unruled until it happens.
Trigger that would surface it: a strain: the first breach of an oath sworn to it that reaches a killing. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Witnessed Stone
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: A boundary stone the god sets with its own hands, before sworn witnesses, cannot be shifted by fewer than twenty men, and the god knows when it is moved.
Drawback: The moving of such a stone strikes it as a breach does, and it must give its own side in setting it: it is bound to hold that boundary as set.
Limits:
  - Stones only, set by its own hands, one to a boundary.
  - It knows that the stone moved, never who moved it.
Trigger that would surface it: a place: a disputed boundary brought to it by both sides. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
