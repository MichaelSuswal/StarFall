# Sealed past: god-law

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the standing pledge of Tessaly: the custom by which one person stood surety for another's oath and paid if it broke. It was never a person. It had a twin custom, the pursuit: sworn riders who ran an oath-breaker down. The long patient chase across open ground is the pursuit's, remembered from beside it.

The sibling it killed. When a man died under the riders with his surety already paid, the winter hall ruled that the pursuit had killed a man whose debt was settled (the verdict on the pursuit). The pursuit was outlawed, and the pledge's own sureties were set to hunt down its last riders. The chase turned; the hunter was hunted, by its twin. The absence at its shoulder, at its own height, is the pursuit. Its curiosity is how it went wrong: the pledge brought the question to the court to see how it would be answered.

Later the pledge stood surety for the lamp-watch of the Nightwood, was called when the watch failed, and was ruined (the calling of the surety). Every oath sworn under the pledge was pressed in wax and kept at the grove at Ilmarrow.

What it lost and does not remember: its twin; that it asked the question that killed it; that its last surety broke it.

## How it may surface

How this may surface, never as a cause: boundary stones and old sworn terms nobody can read (its own survey answers, rare candidates, unplaced); riders in a body chasing one man, which it could not watch without the laugh; wax tablets with oaths pressed in them; a lamp kept through the night in a house, which would make it feel, for no reason, that it owes that house; its second shadow, which it has from landing and cannot account for.
