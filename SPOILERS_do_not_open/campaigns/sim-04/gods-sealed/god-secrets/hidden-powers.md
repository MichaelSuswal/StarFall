# Sealed: god-secrets

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### The Last Fare
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: When a mortal dies within a spoken voice of the god, having given it a message for a named living person, the god carries it: when it next stands within a spoken voice of that person, they hear the dead one's own voice say the message, once.
Drawback: Until the message is delivered the god carries a fare: it cannot seal a new confidence and cannot use Unlatching.
Limits:
  - One fare at a time; a second dying message while one is carried is heard as any words are and is not carried.
  - The message is as it was given: the god cannot change it, and it proves nothing but the voice.
  - It carries words, not the dead, and tells the god nothing it did not hear.
  - If the named person dies first, the fare is lifted at the god's next crossing of running water.
Trigger that would surface it: a being met: the first mortal to die speaking to it. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Dry Crossing
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: With its pole, the god can take itself and one passenger across any running water in the time a ferry would take, on any thing that floats.
Drawback: It must name a fare before putting out and take it on the far bank; if the fare is refused or forgiven, it cannot cross that water again for a season.
Limits:
  - One passenger, and what that one can carry.
  - Not across the sea, and not through ice.
Trigger that would surface it: a place: a river in flood, and someone on the bank who must cross. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
