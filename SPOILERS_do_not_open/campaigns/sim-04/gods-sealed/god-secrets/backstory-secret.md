# Sealed past: god-secrets

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the ferrymen of the Aunmere's last reach: a guild, generation after generation, that poled every one of the country's dead over to Orrin Holm, heard what the dying could not carry, and was sworn never to tell it. It was never one ferryman. The long line of people who did not speak is real. So is the weight of the boat.

The oath it broke. In the last season the keepers of the wax grove at Ilmarrow brought the rolls of names down the river to be carried to the island (the rolls at the landing). The ferrymen had sworn to carry them. On the night the keepers stood at the landing with the rolls, the ferrymen would not put out; they said the water was wrong, and it was. The star came down that night. The sound like a country of ice giving way was the fall. The far landing was gone because the island was gone. The oath did not break the world; the world ended in the hour the oath was being broken, and the pattern cannot tell the two apart.

What it lost and does not remember: who stood at the landing (the grove's keepers, with no one bearing their order's name that year); what it swore (to carry names, not the dead); that the confidences it kept were never told, to anyone, and went down with it.

## How it may surface

How this may surface, never as a cause: a ferry landing with no river beside it, which its own survey answer asked for (a rare candidate, unplaced); a swarm of bees gathered and waiting at a waterside, which would trouble it without its knowing why; the word "Orrin" or "Ilmarrow" in a sung list; the weight of another god that makes it want, for no reason, to be asked for passage.
