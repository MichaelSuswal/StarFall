# Sealed past: god-trade

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the counting-house of Tessaly, whose own name was Varrendy: the treasury and tally-hall the tally-masters built room by room, each count checked against the one before, where every weight and measure of the country was kept true. It was never one tally-master. The many rooms, the care and the pride are exact.

How it was shut in. When the pledge's sureties were ruined and the city's debts could not be settled, the council shut the house with the tally-masters inside to finish a full reckoning, and wardens brought down from Keshgate barred the door from outside (the barring of the counting-house). The name Varrendy was struck from the city's rolls until the count should balance. It never balanced, the door was never opened, and the counting went on inside until the end. The sound of a door is that bar. The one on the other side is the wardens. The smooth place where a name should be is the struck name.

What it lost and does not remember: its name, which was taken by an act and not by time; that it was not shut in for a fault; that those who barred it were only keepers of doors, sent.

## How it may surface

How this may surface, never as a cause: a small labyrinth above ground with a marked exit, a door that opens from the inside (its own survey answers, rare candidates, unplaced); the word "Varrendy" in a sung list, which is the only way this world could give it its name back, and nothing brings such singers to it by schedule; a figure of stone standing at a door, which it would not turn its back on; a ledger that will not balance.
