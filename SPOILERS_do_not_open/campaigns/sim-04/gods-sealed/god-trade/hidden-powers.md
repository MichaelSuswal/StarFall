# Sealed: god-trade

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### The Standing Measure
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: A weight, measure or scale the god has trued and then marked with its thumb stays true, whoever handles it. A hand that tries to falsify it finds the palm darkened as in the Reckoning until the measure is restored.
Drawback: Each standing measure counts as a standing bargain toward the twelve and the forty of the Reckoning.
Limits:
  - Ordinary weights, measures and scales only, and nothing too large for it to lift.
  - The mark shows; anyone can see which measures are the god's.
  - It makes no one trust anyone; it rules no mind.
Trigger that would surface it: a strain: finding the same weight falsified a second time after truing it. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Unbarred Way
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: Inside any building, cave or walled place, the god knows the direction of the nearest way out that is not fastened against it.
Drawback: It cannot rest in a place where every door is shut, fastened or not: it is restless and slow of thought until one stands open.
Limits:
  - A direction, never a map, and nothing of what lies along the way.
  - If every way is fastened against it, it knows that and no more.
Trigger that would surface it: a place: finding itself underground or in a house of many turnings with no door in sight. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
