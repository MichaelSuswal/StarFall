# Sealed past: god-dreams

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the lamp-watch of the Nightwood: the custom of the forest towns that every house keep a lamp through the night and every street one waker, against a sleep that some did not wake from. It was never a watchman. The hall of lamps is every street of those towns at once. The people whose breathing went quiet are the streets found asleep in the watch's last winters.

How it failed. The watch asked the winter hall for relief from its dues so it could keep more wakers, and the appeal was never judged (the unheard appeal). The watch was kept under oath with the standing pledge of Tessaly as its surety; when streets were found asleep the watch was held forsworn, the pledge was called and ruined (the calling of the surety), and after that no one would stand for the watch. Whole streets went unwatched. The one who stood beside it in the dark, taller in the lamplight, is the pledge: its surety, which it brought down with it.

What it lost and does not remember: that it asked for help and was not answered; that it ruined the one that stood for it; what the sleep was. The country never knew what the sleep was either.

## How it may surface

How this may surface, never as a cause: lamps kept on a sill by people who no longer know why (its own undared wish; a rare candidate, unplaced, and never placed where it is certain to find it); sleepers who do not wake, wherever this world has them; a very tall figure seen by lamplight, which would stop it where it stands; the weight of another god that makes it feel it is owed an answer.
