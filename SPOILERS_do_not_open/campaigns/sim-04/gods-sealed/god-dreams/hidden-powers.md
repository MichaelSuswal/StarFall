# Sealed: god-dreams

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### The Waking Hand
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: A sleeper the god touches, or whose dream it is in, it can wake, from any ordinary sleep however deep: drink, drug, sickness, exhaustion.
Drawback: It takes the sleep on itself: it sleeps, helpless, for as long as the sleeper would have, up to seven days.
Limits:
  - It wakes; it does not keep awake, heal the cause, or turn back one who is walking.
  - A sleep that is more than ordinary is unruled until met.
  - One sleeper at a time.
Trigger that would surface it: a strain: a sleeper among its own people who does not wake when called. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Wick Answering
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: A lamp, candle or laid fire the god can see, it can light with a look.
Drawback: Its own lantern goes out at the same moment and cannot be relit until the next dusk.
Limits:
  - A flame so lit is not a lamp lit for it by a mortal, and serves none of its powers.
  - It lights what is laid to burn; it sets nothing else afire.
Trigger that would surface it: a place: a dark house of its people where the lamp has gone out and no one can light it. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
