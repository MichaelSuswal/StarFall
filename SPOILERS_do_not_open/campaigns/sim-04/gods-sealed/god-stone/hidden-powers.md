# Sealed: god-stone

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### The Door's Side
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: With its hand on a shut door, gate or sealed way of stone, the god knows which side it was shut from, and whether anything has passed it since it was shut.
Drawback: Having learned it, the god cannot leave that door as it is: within a season it must open it, ward it or see it opened, or Reading the Stone goes dark until it does.
Limits:
  - Which side and whether, never who, what or when.
  - Doors of stone or set in stone only.
  - A door made or kept by more than ordinary craft is unruled until met.
Trigger that would surface it: a place: the first sealed door of stone it lays a hand on. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Stair-Sure Foot
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: On rock the god cannot be made to fall, and a mortal holding its hand on rock does not slip.
Drawback: Timber and water will not bear it: a plank bridge or a boat fails under it.
Limits:
  - Rock and laid stone only.
  - It holds a mortal up; it does not carry one.
Trigger that would surface it: a being met: a mortal about to fall on a mountain path within its reach. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
