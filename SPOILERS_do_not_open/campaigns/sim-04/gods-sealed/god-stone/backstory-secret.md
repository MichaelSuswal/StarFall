# Sealed past: god-stone

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the wardens of the stair at Keshgate: the order that kept the door at the top of the cut stair to the country's center, a warm hill, and let up or turned back whomever the hill's keepers bade. It was never one warden. The great stair, the long service and the bidding that did not ask leave are exact. The greater thing was the hill, and what spoke for it.

How it fled. Some wardens were sent down to Tessaly to bar the counting-house, and did not go back (the barring of the counting-house). In the last year those left at the gate were bidden to let up a great many people at once. They stood aside from the door and went down the stair (the leaving of the stair). No one stopped them, because nothing at the top needed the door kept any longer. The gap shaped like a face is whoever gave the bidding: the wardens never saw one. Its despair is the knowledge, held without words, that leaving changed nothing.

What it lost and does not remember: that it barred another's door on its way out of its own; what went up the stair; that the thing it fled never pursued it and never needed to.

## How it may surface

How this may surface, never as a cause: a stair under a sealed door, a door with no handle on the near side (survey candidates, unplaced); any place in this world where people go toward a center and do not come back; a mortal or a being that bids it as a servant; a door barred from outside with someone within, which it could not leave as it found it; the weight of another god that makes its rear face look.
