# Sealed past: god-ice

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the winter hall on Corrowe: the high court of the country, which sat on the ice of the tarn in the cold months and spoke its judgments aloud in measured speech that the country repeated word for word. It was never a judge. The long hall with a floor of ice and the pleasure of saying what was fair are exact.

What it ate. At its last sitting on the ice it received the appeal of the lamp-watch of the Nightwood and gave no judgment before the ice went (the unheard appeal). A plea taken in and never answered is the cold in its belly that is not winter, and it moves because it was never settled. Two other things sit near it. The hall's most famous judgment, the verdict on the pursuit, was correct by the letter and ended a custom and the riders who kept it; the laugh comes from there. And the hall did not end by its own act: the river towns cut the tarn's sill and its floor ran away down the river (the cut at Corrowe). It remembers being hungry and eating because the pattern holds "took it in and did not give it back"; it does not hold who was hungry.

What it lost and does not remember: its floor; the plea it swallowed and whose it was; that a river drank it.

## How it may surface

How this may surface, never as a cause: a frozen lake clear to the bottom (its own image of the world, a lone mood of the cold country, unplaced); a petition put to it that it finds itself unable to refuse to hear; a mortal reciting an old judgment in measured speech; the weight of another god that tastes to it of meltwater, or of lamp oil.
