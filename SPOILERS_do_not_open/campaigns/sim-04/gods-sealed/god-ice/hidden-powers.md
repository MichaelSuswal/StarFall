# Sealed: god-ice

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### The Long Keeping
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: What is laid in ice of the god's making does not rot or change for as long as that ice holds: food, a body, a written thing.
Drawback: One keeping at a time, and while it holds, Winter's Reach reaches half as far. The seven days' slowness of the working that made the ice runs as written.
Limits:
  - The ice holds as long as the god renews it once in a season by a working; unrenewed, it melts as ice does.
  - Nothing living is kept alive in it.
  - What is taken out is as it went in, and from then on it ages.
  - Things of more than ordinary craft are unruled until met.
Trigger that would surface it: a strain or a place: a winter store of its people beginning to spoil, or a body brought before it for judgment that must not be buried yet. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Unspoken Plea
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: The god knows when someone within earshot has come to ask its judgment and has not dared to speak: that there is such a one, and which, never what the matter is.
Drawback: Until that one speaks or goes out of earshot, the god cannot leave the place.
Limits:
  - Mortal persons only.
  - It compels no one to speak.
Trigger that would surface it: a being met: a mortal who comes before it with a grievance and turns away without speaking. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
