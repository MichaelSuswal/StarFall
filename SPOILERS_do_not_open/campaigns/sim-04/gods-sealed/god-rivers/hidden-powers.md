# Sealed: god-rivers

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### What the River Gave Back
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: Once in a season the god can give back, laid on the bank, one thing that water it is standing in has swallowed within the last year: a drowned body, a lost tool, a sunk boat's cargo. A body is given back dead.
Drawback: For a month after, it tastes that thing in all water: Reading the water gives it nothing.
Limits:
  - Only from the channel it stands in, within a day's drift.
  - It restores no life and mends nothing.
  - It must know or be told that the thing went in.
  - Things of more than ordinary craft are unruled until met.
Trigger that would surface it: a strain: a plea for water made to it on behalf of someone already drowned. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Firm Bed
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: Where the god stands in a channel a full day and night, the bed firms under it into a ford a laden mortal can cross, for a season.
Drawback: It must stand the whole day and night without moving, the ford is plain to all and serves anyone, and for seven days after it moves at half its pace.
Limits:
  - One ford standing at a time.
  - Not in a channel wider than a bowshot, and not in flood.
Trigger that would surface it: a place: the first time it stands a full day and night at a crossing its people need. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
