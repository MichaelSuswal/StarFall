# Sealed past: god-rivers

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the Aunmere's habit of being followed: the long memory a river country had of one river that every road, town and ford kept to. It was never a person. The "many small things moving, and then one thing" it remembers is its own headwaters gathering. The high cold clear place is Corrowe, the tarn at its head, and the stillness there that it did not mind was the winter hall sitting on the ice: for most of the country's life the river began under a court.

What it ate. In the dry years the river towns cut the sill of the tarn to keep it running (the cut at Corrowe). The tarn drained in three summers and the court's ice went down it as meltwater. The river kept running and the towns lived. The taste that has not left is the tarn. What it should not have done was done for it and by it at once: it was the thing kept alive, and it took the place where it was not alone. The voice that said its name once is a whole country saying "Aunmere"; it does not have the word.

What it lost and does not remember: its own name; the tarn; that the thing it swallowed was another's seat.

## How it may surface

How this may surface in the world, never as a cause: a spring at a river's head that feels known to it (its own answer to the survey asked for one; nothing is placed where it is certain to find it); meltwater in spring, which is when it wakes; the weight of another god near it that for no reason it can find tastes of cold; the word "Aunmere" in a list of names sung by wanderers, if such people ever sing in its hearing.
