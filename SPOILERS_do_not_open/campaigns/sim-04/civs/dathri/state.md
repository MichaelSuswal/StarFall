# the Dathri: starting state

Creation night 3a, 2026-11-22. Year 0.

population: about 5,000
stores: barley, beans and cheese to the end of winter and about three weeks past it; the lean season is now
treasure: little: wool, cut stone, a few bronze tools per house
trade: stone, wool and cheese out by the notches; salt and bronze in, dear. Rating 2 of 5
strength: about 500 who would hold a notch; no standing band
mood: proud, curious, short of temper about the price of bronze
