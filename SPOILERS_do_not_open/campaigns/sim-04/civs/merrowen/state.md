# the Merrowen: starting state

Creation night 3a, 2026-11-22. Year 0.

population: about 5,000
stores: grain, smoked eel and roots to the end of winter; oil and tallow short by midwinter in a bad year
treasure: modest: salt, oil, a town chest the Steward keeps
trade: eels, charcoal, reed and wool out; salt and oil in by the Salt Way [f80]. Rating 2 of 5
strength: about 400 under the Marshal at muster; the wall in fair repair
mood: quiet and uneasy; the succession is on every tongue
