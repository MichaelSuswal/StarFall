# Godless people: the shore people of Lake Hesh

## Who they are
Human. Fishers and reed-cutters of the lake shore, in a few villages: Lowater [f67] on the east shore in Heshshore [r29], Nenna [f66] on the north, Ammot [f68] where the Carn [f05] leaves the lake. Their neighbors call them the Heshmen. What they call themselves is less each year.
## How many
About 6,000.
## Where
Heshshore [r29] and the villages round Lake Hesh [r28].
## How they live
They fish the shallows and the streams and never the deep water. Reed, fowl, small plots. They trade little and ask little. They are gentle, slow to answer, and hard to tell apart in memory.
## Their code
Do not fish the deep. Do not swim at night. Do not point at the water. A person is called by what they do.
## Their history in the land
They have been on the shore longer than any kingdom's record. Old Dunmarrow [r07] tax rolls list them by name, household by household; the newest rolls list them by trade only, because that is what they gave. The lake has grown in the telling and, the oldest say, in fact.
## What they believe about gods
They look at the lake. They do not say what they believe.
## What they want
To be left as they are.
## Whether and how they touch the great events
At the heart of them. The lake is the star's center and the hoarder; these are its shore people (deep lore, Truth 7). Their names are thinning. What follows, and when, is 3b's.
## What they know of the land
The shore, seen. Pellam [f36] and Carnford [f39]: reported. Maudry [f64]: rumored. Pellam [f36] to Nenna [f66], on foot, over open ground, no route: 64.0 miles, 4.6 days.
