# Godless people: the Oum

## Who they are
Not human. The one such people of the land (Q13 at 2, the most shared kind): tall, grey, slow, long-lived, patient past any human measure. They keep the Count: a record of terms, debts and deaths, theirs and others', on baked clay.
## How many
About 800.
## Where
The Koldry Hills [r26]; their hold is Koldry [f81].
## How they live
Terraced gardens, goats, clay. They rarely leave the hills. Once in some years a few come to the edge of Vestfen [r25] or to the coast to trade and to ask questions for the Count. A child among them is an event of decades.
## Their code
A thing entered in the Count is so. A maker is held to the letter of what it said it made, and they call that justice. They do not lie, and they do not volunteer.
## Their history in the land
They were here before the forest was this size. Ammeth Hall [f82] in their hills is not theirs and they do not use it; it has a stair under a sealed door. They remember more than they will say.
## What they believe about gods
That gods are a kind of entry: they arrive, they are counted, they leave or rise. They are unimpressed.
## What they want
The Count kept whole and themselves let alone.
## Whether and how they touch the great events
They hold a long record of what the land used to have. Limits on what any of them knows and how they may be asked are written in 3b with the cast (mystery standard 16).
## What they know of the land
Their hills, seen. Vestfen [r25], Vesthallow [f71], Ammerholt [r24] at its eastern edge: seen over long years. Everything else: old reports, some centuries stale. Koldry [f81] to Vesthallow [f71], on foot, over open ground, no route: 243.3 miles, 25.7 days.
