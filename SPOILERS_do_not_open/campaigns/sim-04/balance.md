# Balance: campaign sim-04

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21. A rough weight from 1 to 10 for each god's total power: main powers, drawbacks, trades. Roughly equal, not exactly (DESIGN 3.15). The band the eight fall round is 5. Cards are frozen; nothing here changes one.

## Weights before the hidden powers

Set from the confirmed cards, before any hidden power was fitted.

| God | Weight | Reasons |
|---|---|---|
| the player's god | 6 | Six blocks. The Listening gives knowledge of a whole valley with no travel; the Splitting puts it in five places. Held down by season, night, rain and cold on everything, no voice and no hands, a showing each time it listens, a memory lost with each part. The DM's lean note says up, mostly by the Listening, kept at the player's choice; the Keeper's reading agrees. |
| god-rivers | 5 | Six powers after it shrank the largest itself. Reads a whole channel for the present, cleans one water, hides a village, sends one passive part, holds one flood a year. Every use is paid in its own body and time; one real weakness that can be used to draw it out. |
| god-secrets | 4 | Four quiet powers: a seal on a told thing that it can never use, the opening of ordinary locks at the cost of its face, a knowing of the dead that stops it an hour a body. Nothing that moves a people or a field. No trade. |
| god-dreams | 5 | A word in one dream a night out to three days' walk, sight through any lamp lit for it at any distance. Held down hard by the weakness: with no lamp lit for it, it has nothing, and it lands so. |
| god-ice | 5 | A killing frost or a frozen river within a mile, a week's slowness after each; binding terms spoken one mortal at a time under a ceiling of its own. No trade. |
| god-law | 3 | One power, a knowing of broken oaths, that enforces nothing and binds it to a side of every oath; a blow for each breach. One weakness for a modest reach. It refused the second power it was offered. |
| god-stone | 6 | Three wards that close passes to all comers, building at twenty masons' rate, a mile's reading of stone. The wards cost it its legs and at three its freedom to leave; the widened weakness binds it in most places it will bargain. Told in round 1 that it sat a little above; it chose to carry the lean. |
| god-trade | 4 | True weights by touch, four kept roads with a feeling of what passes, sworn bargains that mark a breaker. Useful to a people more than to the god; its weakness is heavy. |

No god is far out of line. The widest gap is between god-law at 3 and the player's god and god-stone at 6.

## How the hidden powers were fitted

Each god has one cool and one minor, both weaker than its main powers, each from its sealed past. Toward the band: god-law's cool one is the strongest of the eight (a trail to an oath-breaker for a season), since it sits lowest; god-secrets' and god-trade's add real use (a dead voice carried; measures that stay true); the player's and god-stone's are kept narrow (a thing kept unaltered; which side a door was shut from), since they sit highest. The others are fitted level.
