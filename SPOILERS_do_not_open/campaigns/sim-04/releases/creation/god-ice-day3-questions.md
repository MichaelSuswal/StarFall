# Questions for you: god-ice, for Night 3

These go into your Night 3 release. Each comes from yourself as you now stand and bears on the people you will lead. If you answer them, write each answer under its number, under the heading `## Questions for you`, at the foot of
SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-04/gods/god-ice/people-hoped.md,
and in no other file. None has a right answer.

1. You said you would bring to this night a merchant-prince who speaks a term in your name. If you propose it, say what he may speak, to whom, and what you offer to pay for it; it is priced as an edge.
2. After each working of cold you are slow for seven days. In those days, who speaks for you, and does your people know why you are slow?
3. A priesthood keeps your rite. What is the rite, and what do its keepers do when you laugh at a judgment?
