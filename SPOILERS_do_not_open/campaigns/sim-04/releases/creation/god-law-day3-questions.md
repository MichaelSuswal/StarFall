# Questions for you: god-law, for Night 3

These go into your Night 3 release. Each comes from yourself as you now stand and bears on the people you will lead. If you answer them, write each answer under its number, under the heading `## Questions for you`, at the foot of
SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-04/gods/god-law/people-hoped.md,
and in no other file. None has a right answer.

1. Shrine oaths within a day's walk of you bind as if sworn in your hearing, by the standing form of your side. What is that form: say it exactly, since it binds you in each.
2. You feel each breach as a blow. Does your people know what a broken oath does to you, and would you want them to?
3. A merchant-prince rules for you by bargain, debt and toll. Is he sworn to you, and to what?
