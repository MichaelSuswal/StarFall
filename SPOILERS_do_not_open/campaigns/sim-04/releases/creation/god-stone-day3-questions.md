# Questions for you: god-stone, for Night 3

These go into your Night 3 release. Each comes from yourself as you now stand and bears on the people you will lead. If you answer them, write each answer under its number, under the heading `## Questions for you`, at the foot of
SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-04/gods/god-stone/people-hoped.md,
and in no other file. None has a right answer.

1. With three wards standing you cannot leave your valley. Which thresholds do you mean to ward first, and who among your people is named to pass them?
2. Any word you give under a roof of stone binds you in body. Where does your council sit, and does it know?
3. You shape stone at the rate of twenty masons and are stiff the day after each day worked. What do you build first, and what do your people build without you?
