# Questions for you: god-rivers, for Night 3

These go into your Night 3 release. Each comes from yourself as you now stand and bears on the people you will lead. If you answer them, write each answer under its number, under the heading `## Questions for you`, at the foot of

and in no other file. None has a right answer.

1. Your Cupful may be carried by a mortal. Is there anyone among your people who would carry it for you, and what do they think they are carrying?
2. You cannot refuse a plea for water made in your hearing at water you have touched. Do your people know this of you, and if they do, who is allowed to ask?
3. You mean a warlord to hold the wall while you stay in the mist at the ford. How do your orders reach him, and how does he know they are yours?
