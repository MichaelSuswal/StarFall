# Release: god-law, Night 2

This release is your whole instruction for tonight and your go for exactly the files it names. A `git pull` is always allowed.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## What is so of every god here

### Knowing another god

A god knows that others like it came down when it did. Beyond that, a god feels another god only when they stand close enough to speak. Then it is a weight and a stillness in the air about the other, nothing more: no name, no nature, no measure of what the other can do. The feeling comes slowly and can be wrong, and a god that takes pains can pass for something lesser for a while. Farther off than that there is nothing to feel, however near the other may be.

### Bodies

One. A god here has one body.

### Mortal magic, for pricing

It is simple, and it costs the worker.

### Being a god
You need no food, and sleep is a choice. You move at the pace of your form.
You cannot die. The peoples you will find are at about this level: before the
medieval era, no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a
ceiling. You may bring in what you know, but it is built here by the people,
from what the world has, at the pace of the work, and you say how it is to be
made; nothing is granted by naming it. Every power carries a drawback you
would notice losing. There is no set number of powers. Nothing is over the
top.

## The people you lean toward
Rule: a mortal ruler under the god, a merchant-prince. One ruler answers to the god and rules for it: a merchant-prince, who rules by bargain, debt, toll and trade.
Worship: local shrines. Household and village shrines, kept by whoever keeps them; no order of priests.
You build your people within these on Night 3, or give your reasons not to.
Your people come with helpers who run things when you are away. Who your
people are, and who those helpers are, is settled on Night 3.

## What you said, and asked
No follow-up.

## The season you wake into
Autumn.

## Options
Each is open to you once, on the same terms as to any god. None is advised and none is expected.
- You may describe yourself in any order and at any length: what you are when something meets you, what you can do that what is already there cannot, who you were before the fall as far as you can still tell, and what you lost.
- You have one body. A body that is one presence in one place at a time is form, whatever it is made of and however loose or tight it can be, and it costs nothing; what happens to it happens to you. Being in more than one place at once, or sending part of yourself to work or sense apart from you, is a power and is priced: how many parts, how far apart, what each can do alone, and what losing one takes from you are settled with you. Something between the two is also possible if you see one.
- If parts of you can be apart, say whether each knows what the others perceive as it happens, or whether they pool what they learned only when they meet again. Knowing at a distance is worth much more and is priced as such; not knowing is cheap.
- You may ask for something stronger than a god newly landed should have, and have it priced. You may suggest its price yourself. A condition that only gives a power its shape (where it works, what it can and cannot take in) is written as a limit and makes the power smaller; it is not a price. A price is something you would notice losing.
- Where a price could go two ways you are shown both, and you pick one or counter. For shape only: a power that works through other living things may be paid by them (what they do for you they do not do for themselves, and whoever keeps them bears it) or by you (for as many days as you worked them, you feed and do nothing else); a power that afflicts a person may be shared by you (you are sluggish and useless for as long as it lasts) or may mark them (the one touched can afterward feel you near, for the rest of their life).
- A power that shows you what is far off, hidden or past shows effects, never causes: what happened and how far it reaches, never why and never who. Such a power is provisional until its shape has been reviewed, and any limit added is put to you plainly.
- If your powers together come out above what you asked to be, you are told so, and told which one is most of it. You may keep it and carry the lean, or drop or shrink that one.
- One offer, made once: a weakness taken on can buy an improvement elsewhere. One weakness, one step. It has to cost you something you do not already pay; what your form or your season already costs you does not count. You need not take one.
- You cannot die, but your body can be destroyed. Say what you would wish to happen then: where you gather again, and from what. A return is a power and is priced on one standing scale, the same for every god: a season to return costs one item of memory or habit; a month costs two; nothing is faster. A body that is held, shut in or scattered is held, not broken.
- Where a price takes a memory or a habit, the item is picked by a logged roll from your own record, never chosen. You may ask to be told what went, or to find the gap yourself.
- Say how large you are, whether you carry anything down with you, and what those below should call you when they do not know what you are. If you have no name, say what you are to be filed under. Anything carried that does more than an ordinary object is a power and is priced.
- Senses you do not describe are left unsettled and are settled later, never inferred. A rate or a reach you name (how far in a day, how near is near, how often) is set with you in the pricing.
- You may define the words your powers rely on.
- Anything a power of yours does to another god is unruled until it happens. So is its working on creatures that are more than ordinary, and on things made or kept by more than ordinary craft, until met. An effect of yours that another god could perceive, as anyone could, may be granted.
- You may ask for a clarification "in the write-up, not a new power" when wording is all you need.
- Say what you want, what you will not do whatever it would gain you, and, if you choose, what you want that you would not say aloud. The last is kept in your own goals file and is given to no other god.
