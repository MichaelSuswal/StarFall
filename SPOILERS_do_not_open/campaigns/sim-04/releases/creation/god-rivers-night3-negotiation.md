# Negotiation: god-rivers, Night 3

This file is your instruction for this round and your go for exactly the file it names. A `git pull --rebase --autostash` is always allowed.

## The table's measures, the same at every table
- One edge of ordinary size is about as much as the smallest power on a card, and one weakness pays for about that much: one weakness, one step.
- A weakness is priced by what it costs your people that they do not already pay. A weakness that only restates the country they live in is not counted.
- Where an edge or a weakness bears on a contest, one step is ten points in the odds. Where it bears on work or travel, it is a share of the public rates.
- An edge that would let a mortal do what only you can do is a power of yours, priced on you, and goes on your card. What it costs the mortal is fitted by the Keeper and is not the price.

## Round 1
### Clean water by custom
Draft text, the edge: by kept wells, drawing above the lanes and the settling and straining of water, the sicknesses that come in water are rarer among your people. In an ordinary year they lose half as many to flux and water fever as their neighbours do. In a year when such a sickness travels, any roll for it among them is one step in their favour.
Limits: it is custom and it works without you. It does nothing against a sickness carried by breath, touch or beast. It does not clean a well that is already fouled past what settling and straining can mend; that is your Clean water, which it takes nothing from and gives nothing to.

Draft text, the weakness: a people of the wet. More than three days' march from the river, or in dry country, they tire and sicken sooner: a company of theirs moves at four fifths of the public rate for its kind, and any contest there is one step against them. They have few horses and little skill with them. Their stores are poor for a long march. A summer when the river runs low takes their mood down a step.

Choices: none. The price goes one way: the weakness you offered pays for the edge you asked, one for one.

The Keeper's reasons: the edge is the size you said, and it is real: water sickness is one of the things this world fears most. The weakness is one they would pay whenever anything calls them away from the river, and one they feel in a dry summer at home. It is counted in full.

### Not priced
The number: about five thousand is the usual number, so there is no cost of size to price tonight. The bowl readers are magic on the world's terms and are fitted by the Keeper, the number of workers included. The captains drawn from several houses, the hearth bowl, the flood marks and the rest are ordinary culture and stand as written. What you hope the ferry keeper will carry is heard and not promised.

Accept, refuse or counter each item, and give your reasons.
