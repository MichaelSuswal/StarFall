# Ruling ledger: campaign sim-04

Every ruling the Keeper or the DM makes. Check here before ruling on anything. Ids are `L-NNNN`, in the order entered. Judgment order: fairness, above all to the god's original intent; then fit with the power as first written; then balance.

## Rulings settled at the player's Day 2 table

Entered on Night 2, 2026-11-21, from `campaigns/sim-04/player/power-card.md`, `power-lean.md`, `rejected-premises.md` and the Day 2 transcript. Each id is written back beside its ruling on the card. Year 0. God: the player's god (filed under "the Thrum").

### L-0001
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: whether a swarm is one body.
Arguments: the player wished on Day 1 for many bodies and no main one; the world's rule is one body. The DM offered a swarm as the single body at no price, with dividing as a priced power; the player took it.
Ruling: a swarm that is one presence in one place is form and costs nothing; dividing it is the power the Splitting.
Binds: the player's card. The shape of the ruling (a body of many parts that is one presence in one place is form) is how this table reads the bodies rule for any god.

### L-0002
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: what "near" is.
Arguments: none against; set by the DM, by "sight and sound of your body, or of a part", and agreed at the read-back as a new term.
Ruling: within about 200 yards of a part and about 450 yards of the whole of the god. Marked provisional for the Keeper; confirmed by the Keeper on Night 2 as set (keeper log, 2.3).
Binds: the player's card: the Working's reach and the Sting's mark.

### L-0003
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: a day's flight, and the god's pace.
Arguments: none against; set by the DM from the player's "a day's range", agreed.
Ruling: about 20 miles in a fair day; no flight at night, in rain or in cold. Marked provisional for the Keeper; confirmed by the Keeper on Night 2 as set (keeper log, 2.3). The rate is the card's and is not on the pace table.
Binds: the player's card: the Form's pace, the range of parts in the Splitting, the going-wild limit.

### L-0004
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: choosing where a loss falls.
Arguments: the player asked to "choose which part gets the loss, if I see it coming". The DM held that a memory price is taken by logged roll for every god and is never chosen, and recast the ask: the god chooses which part stands in harm's way. The player agreed.
Ruling: the god chooses which part stands in harm's way, never which memory goes.
Binds: the player's card. Settled against the god in part.

### L-0005
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: the price of a lost part.
Arguments: none against; set by the DM by analogy with the first rung of the standing return scale, the rules not covering a divided body; the player had asked that a lost part cost "a real memory or a habit".
Ruling: a part killed costs one item of memory or habit for good, by logged roll from the god's record, not told to the god.
Binds: the player's card. For the Keeper: a part of a god that thinks and acts on its own is priced at one item when killed; a thing sent that cannot act is not this case and is priced on its own terms.

### L-0006
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: how parts are played and how unplayed parts run.
Arguments: none against; set by the DM as new wording, agreed, with the player's condition that it be told afterward what a part did and not what it knew.
Ruling: in scenes the player plays any part, one at a time; each knows only what it has itself seen since it last touched another; a part not played carries on with the purpose it left with, by standing conduct, cautious and in character; the player is told what it did, not what it knew.
Binds: the player's card; the DM's releases and the night turn's handling of unplayed parts.

### L-0007
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: the ground a broken god returns at.
Arguments: the standing scale returns a god at the nearest ground of its kind. The player wished to return from the hive it has tended longest, wherever it then stands, "a place I could have been reached by something I did"; the DM wrote it so, with two fallbacks, at the first rung.
Ruling: the return ground is the hive tended longest; with none tended, the oldest wild hive in the chosen valley; with no valley chosen, the wild hive nearest where it fell. A season, one item.
Binds: the player's card. As a world rule, the same for every god (entered once, no god told that another argued it): a god may name the ground it returns at in place of the nearest ground of its kind, at no price either way, so long as the ground is of its own kind and is fixed by a rule written on its card, never picked at the time of the return; the rung is chosen at creation and written on the card; the way from that ground is travelled at the god's pace.

### L-0008
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: parts that die on the day the whole god dies.
Arguments: none against; set by the DM, agreed at the read-back as a new term.
Ruling: parts that died in the same day as the last of the god are covered by the Regathering's one item; parts lost earlier have already been paid for.
Binds: the player's card.

## Rulings of the Night 2 table (the NPC gods)

Entered on Night 2, 2026-11-21, at round 2, when the final text was written. Year 0. Each is noted as confirmed when the god confirms. Gods are named by label.

### L-0009
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: every card (a world rule; no god is told another's table used it). Matter: the measures and standing prices of the creation table.
Arguments: none against; set by the Keeper, once, the plainest way, where no file sets a value (runbook principle, run 02, 2.3).
Ruling: a day's walk is 20 miles on any ground; earshot, a spoken voice and speaking distance are about 50 paces in open air and not through walls; where a mortal is left able to feel a god near, near is about 200 yards (the number the DM set for the same price at the player's table); a memory price removes one item from the god's own record by logged roll (an NPC's record of gods and its goals; the player's chronicle canon and conduct lines, through the DM's release); the rung of the return scale is chosen at creation and written on the card; while a god is gone, whatever of its needs it to keep it up lapses.
Binds: every card.

### L-0010
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Cards: god-ice, god-law, god-trade (binds more than one card; each argued it alone and none is told of the others). Matter: "my own word binds me, or the power fails" offered as a drawback.
Arguments: each god offered being held to its own side of its sworn terms, with a loss if it breaks them, as the price of a power that makes a sworn word tell. The Keeper: `powers.md` names a penalty only carelessness triggers ("lose it if you break your word") as a Limit that buys nothing; a price falls in the ordinary use.
Ruling: the self-breach penalty is written as a limit on all three cards. Each power carries a price that falls in ordinary use: god-ice, the standing terms weigh (twelve, forty); god-law, a side given to every oath and a blow for every breach; god-trade, the standing bargains weigh (twelve, forty). A term or bargain counts as standing only while unfulfilled, unreleased and unended.
Binds: the three cards; any later power of any god priced the same way.

### L-0011
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-rivers. Matter: the Mist veil's price.
Arguments: the god offered "whole attention, no speech, a crawl while it holds the veil". The Keeper: true only while the veil is up, so a condition of use. Two prices were put; the god took the one it pays itself, refusing to have its people pay.
Ruling: the conditions are limits; the price is that for as many days as it held the veil it cannot read or clean water.
Binds: god-rivers' card.

### L-0012
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-rivers. Matter: what the plea for water buys; whether the Cupful is a second body; the shrinking of Turn the channel.
Arguments: the god asked that its Reading reach the whole length of any river it had touched, past and present, for one weakness. The Keeper: one weakness, one step; that is several. The god asked a ruling on the Cupful. The god was told its card sat above a newly landed god's by Turn the channel, and chose to drop the bending of a channel and keep the holding of a flood.
Ruling: the weakness buys the whole length of the channel it stands in for what the water carries now only. The Cupful is a part of its one body and no second body (the reading of L-0001). Turn the channel is the holding only, shrunk by the god's own choice, and the card says so.
Binds: god-rivers' card.

### L-0013
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-secrets. Matter: Unlatching's price; the return's bound.
Arguments: the god's drawback named two prices (a day and a night after each opening; a cost that runs with the door). Both were put; it took the scaled one, with the Keeper's floor that a latch costs until the next dawn. Its return named running water "within a day's walk"; the Keeper: the card must say where it gathers on any ground.
Ruling: the scaled price (next dawn; a day and a night; seven days). The return is at the nearest running water, however far.
Binds: god-secrets' card.

### L-0014
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-dreams. Matter: the Lamp-Sight's reach and price; the return with no lamp.
Arguments: the god proposed sight and hearing through any lamp lit for it, with no reach stated, past and present, at the price of dulled senses while looking and a blow if the lamp is broken. The Keeper: sight and hearing at any distance is more than a newly landed god carries; the present only; the price offered is a condition of use and a blow a careful god seldom pays. The god wrote that with no lamp it cannot return; the Keeper: the card must say where it gathers on any day.
Ruling: any distance, sight only, the present only; the god is dazzled for as long as it looked and as long again. With no lamp kept, it gathers at the nearest hearth kept through the night. The Hollow Season does not stop the return and holds from the rising if no flame lit for it burns.
Binds: god-dreams' card.

### L-0015
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-ice. Matter: Winter's Reach's price; a term spoken under the god's seal by a mortal.
Arguments: the god offered the lingering cold as the drawback. The Keeper: turned on another's ground that is the power's effect, so a price must fall whichever way it is turned. The god asked whether a merchant-prince may speak a term in its name under a seal. The Keeper: a mortal acting for the god changes what the god can do and is a power of the god's, priced on the god.
Ruling: the lingering is a limit; the god pays seven days of slowness and no cold after each working. The seal is refused on these terms; open as an edge of its people on Night 3, priced there, which the god says it will bring.
Binds: god-ice's card; the Keeper's pricing of that edge on Night 3a.

### L-0016
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-law. Matter: Unmarked Hand; what Guest-law buys.
Arguments: Unmarked Hand hid a trail the god's powers do not leave; by `powers.md` an ask that describes no act only a god can do is offered both ways. The god took it as manner at no price and refused the power. The god asked that shrine oaths anywhere bind as if in earshot, for one weakness. The Keeper: every shrine at any distance is more than one step.
Ruling: Unmarked Hand is manner under Form, not a power. Guest-law, leaving out the god's own people, buys shrine oaths within a day's walk of where the god then is. The god's side of a shrine oath may be fixed ahead in a standing form that binds it in each.
Binds: god-law's card.

### L-0017
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-stone. Matter: the ward's price; the Oath at the Threshold.
Arguments: the god offered being "uneasy" far from its oldest ward. The Keeper: unease is nothing the Keeper can apply. The god refused a tether as a written fence and took the weight. The god offered a weakness that binds only when it swears in a doorway, for a valley's reach of Reading. The Keeper: a thing only carelessness triggers does not buy; a valley is several steps.
Ruling: each standing ward stiffens the god (half pace; no shaping; cannot leave the valley or mountain). The weakness is widened to every roof or walls of stone and every pass, caves and cut hollows included, and buys a mile's reach. The card sits a little above the band by the god's choice.
Binds: god-stone's card.

### L-0018
Date: 2026-11-21, Night 2. Year 0. Card: god-trade. Matter: what the Barred Door buys.
Arguments: the god asked two steps (three days' reach and twice the roads) for one weakness. The Keeper: one weakness, one step, however heavy.
Ruling: four roads kept at once. The reach stays a day's walk.
Binds: god-trade's card.

## Rulings settled at the player's Day 3 table

Entered on Night 3a, 2026-11-21, first, before any pricing of the night, from `campaigns/sim-04/civs/sallowfolk/record.md`, `campaigns/sim-04/player/power-card.md` (the dated Day 3 entries), `rejected-premises.md` and the Day 3 transcript. Year 0. God: the player's god (filed under "the Thrum"). People: the Sallowfolk.

### L-0019
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 3 (entered Night 3a). Year 0. Record: the Sallowfolk's. Matter: the price of the first edge (unusually good keepers of bees, with a trade in honey, wax and tapers to match), and how two refused defaults count in it.
Arguments: the player asked that its people's bees be "the best-kept in the region", with a candle trade. The DM: a real edge, so priced; how the neighbors keep bees is the world's, so "best in the region" is recorded as the people's belief. Full price asked: two weaknesses (they live by the hive; their goods say where they come from and their small wealth has no walls). The smaller number and the lack of a town and seat each bear on this edge, one step each in the god's favor (DESIGN 3.11), so the second weakness was dropped. The player accepted the first weakness, and accepted that the Working's cost on hives lands on this people's table.
Ruling: the edge stands at one weakness: they live by the hive (little grain to spare, salt and iron bought with honey and wax, a poor hive year a hungry winter for the whole valley, little else to sell). "Best in the region" is belief, not fact. Two refused defaults (the number; the town and seat) are spent here and bear on no other price.
Binds: the Sallowfolk's record; the Keeper's fitting of their stores and trade; the pricing of any NPC people's edge where a default refused bears on it (one step each, in the one price it bears on).

### L-0020
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 3 (entered Night 3a). Year 0. Record: the Sallowfolk's. Card: the player's, the Tasting (dated entry 1, Day 3 close). One matter marked in two places, one id. Matter: the price and the limit of the second edge (they notice early).
Arguments: the player asked that its people notice weather, blossom and blight before others do. The DM: real, and it must take nothing from the god's Tasting; a limit was put (by ordinary signs, days ahead of other farmers, effects and not causes, never before the god's own tasting of the same ground would). Full price asked: two weaknesses (slow to act on what they see; an eye for hive, weather and blossom and not for people). Having no helpers bears on this edge, one step, so the first was dropped and the second charged. The player accepted the limit and the weakness.
Ruling: the edge stands with its limit, at one weakness: they read strangers and bargains poorly. The refused helpers are spent here and bear on no other price.
Binds: the Sallowfolk's record; the player's card (the Tasting keeps its lead on the same ground).

### L-0021
Date: 2026-11-21, Day 3 (entered Night 3a). Year 0. Card: the player's, the Working (dated entry 2, Day 3 close). Matter: whether moving stores from a surplus hive to a short one is a use of the Working.
Arguments: the player gave as standing conduct that it would move stores to a hive gone short by its doing. The DM: moving stores is the Working by the card's own Description, so it is a day of work on both hives and counts toward the three days each can bear in a season. Said with the read-back; the player agreed.
Ruling: as the DM put it. No term of the power changes.
Binds: the player's card; the Keeper's count of days worked on each hive in the night turn.
