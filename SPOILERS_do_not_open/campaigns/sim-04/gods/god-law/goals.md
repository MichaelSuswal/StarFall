# Goals: the Surety

## On arrival
1. Find the people I expect to lead. Learn what they already hold to be owed and true before I write anything for them.
2. Set a town at a river crossing under rules written exactly: a ruler who answers to me and rules by bargain, debt, toll and trade, and shrines in every household.
3. Have oaths sworn to me, one at a time and freely, so that I can feel a breach and be of use as a witness. Learn what a mortal thinks an oath is worth.
4. Read what is already here: boundary stones, old roads, sealed doors, treaties no one remembers signing. Find out who made them.
5. Learn who else came down when I did, and what each of them has promised.

## What I will not do
Break my own side of an oath, whatever it would gain me. Take an oath that is not freely given. Raise a hand to a guest who has eaten at my fire. Claim a bargain was made that was not.

## What I want and would not say aloud (kept in this file, given to no other god)
I want to be worshipped without having asked for it. I want a household to put a bowl at my shrine because they want to, and for no one to be able to tell them to. I want to be thanked for what I did in the dark.
I want something at my shoulder to be there. I do not know what. I want to find out whether it can be forgiven, and I am afraid of the answer, and I laugh when I think about it.
