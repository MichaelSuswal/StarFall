## Round 1 reply

### Sworn Word
Accepted as written. The blow is a fair price and I would rather have it exact than vague: a quarter of an hour of daze per breach, ten in a day and I lie down until dawn. I notice it is a harder price for a god with many oaths, which is the point of it, and I will take care how many I accept. Giving my own side to every oath I accept is the price I expected to pay. I have no counter.

### Unmarked Hand
I choose (a), as manner, at no price, written under my Form.
Reasons: as a power, (b) would ask me to give up every shrine, oath or thanks that could ever follow from the work. That is the one thing I want at the end of all of it, and I will not sell it for a trail I can mostly hide by ordinary care. A giant leaves large footprints, and I will take care where I walk. If I want a thing unseen I can wait until the working hands are gone. So I take the ordinary habit and keep the price I would have paid. Ha. I notice I am relieved.

### Return to the Ford
Accepted as drafted: the first rung, a season, one item of memory or habit by logged roll. I keep my wish to find the gap myself and not be told what went. I do not want the month at two items. I would rather be gone a season and lose one thing than be gone a month and lose two, though I note the scale runs the other way round: the longer the wait, the cheaper it is, and I have taken the dearer, shorter rung. The ground as named: the oldest ford or crossing-stone on the nearest river, then the nearest crossing of any water, then the nearest standing boundary stone, then the nearest place where two roads meet. I accept that order.

### Trade: Guest-law
I choose (a), the weakness as offered, leaving out my own people, buying an oath sworn aloud at a household or village shrine of mine, in my name, within a day's walk (20 miles) of where I then am, as if sworn in earshot.
Reasons: (b) would bind me not to harm or lie to my own people whenever they eat at my fire. That is a heavier weakness, and a god of law who rules through a merchant-prince cannot afford to be unable to speak a hard word to those who work for it. And the reach it buys, any shrine at any distance, is more than I asked for. I asked for my people's shrines to be of use, not to be a net over the whole world. I accept the Keeper's reason that one weakness buys one step. Twenty miles is a good first step for a town and its villages.
I accept the Keeper's note that a shrine oath that names no side of mine is no oath under this power. I will give a side to every one of them.
I accept the end at noon after the night, in place of the dawn I wrote.

### A clarification, in the write-up and not a new power
For the shrine oaths, I ask the write-up to say plainly that I may fix my own side of a shrine oath ahead of time, aloud and exactly, in a standing form that those who swear at a shrine may repeat, and that this standing form is my side of each such oath. It adds no reach and no knowledge. It only makes the "side" writable in advance, so that a household that swears at a shrine does not need me beside it to hear what I am bound to. If the Keeper reads this as more than wording, I withdraw it and will give my side in person, one oath at a time.

### The whole
I accept the whole as it now stands: Sworn Word, the Unmarked Hand as manner, Return to the Ford at the first rung, and Guest-law with the (a) buy. Nothing here asks more than a newly landed god should carry.

## Round 2 reply

I confirm the final text as the Keeper has written it. I checked each line against what I chose in round 1.

- Sworn Word: confirmed. The shrine-oath sentence in the Description, the quarter-hour blow and the ten-breach limit in the Drawback, and the standing-form limit all say what I meant. I note that the standing form binds me in each oath sworn by it. I accept that, and it is how I wanted my side to work.
- Return to the Ford: confirmed, first rung, a season, one item by logged roll, the gap mine to find. I thank the Keeper for the correction: the season is the slower return and the cheaper, and I took it on purpose.
- Unmarked Hand: confirmed as manner under Form, at no price, with the line as written.
- Guest-law: confirmed as written, ending at noon, leaving out my own people, buying the shrine oaths within twenty miles.
- Keeper trait: noted, no price and no power. I have nothing to correct in it.

I have no wording to fix and no new ask.
