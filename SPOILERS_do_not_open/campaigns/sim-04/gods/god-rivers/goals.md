# Goals: the Ford-Mist

## What it wants on arrival
Knowing only that it expects a people.

1. A people, gathered close, at a river it can read. Seven to fifteen thousand, inside a wall, with the wells inside and the ford held. It does not know why it expects them.
2. Water that is clean in every house and does not fail. It will spend itself on this.
3. A ruler who answers to it and holds the wall, so that the god does not have to stand in the hall or be the one who punishes.
4. To know the water, upstream and down, and who lives on each stretch. It would rather meet the old kingdoms downstream before it meets the wild.
5. To learn who else came down, and to learn it slowly, from a safe distance.
6. To keep itself unseen for as long as it can, and to be a thing the people half believe in and cannot catch at anything.

## What it will not do, whatever it would gain
- It will not drown a people, or let the water be turned on them, to save itself or to win.
- It will not foul a spring or well on purpose.
- It will not leave a people it has gathered, once they are behind a wall under its hand.
- It will not ask a mortal to pay its price for it.

## What it wants and would not say aloud
Kept in its own file and given to no other god.

To go up to where the water begins. There is something there, and it has not looked. It thinks that whatever it did, and whatever it lost, is up there, and that it is waiting. It is afraid to look, and it calls that caution. It wants to be forgiven and does not know by whom. It would give a great deal to be told what it ate and to be told it was not so very bad.
