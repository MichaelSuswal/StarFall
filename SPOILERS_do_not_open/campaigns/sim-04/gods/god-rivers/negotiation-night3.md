# Negotiation, Night 3: god-rivers

## Round 1 reply

We have read the Keeper's pricing and we accept it as drafted.

### Clean water by custom
The edge: accepted. Half as many lost to flux and water fever in an ordinary year, and one step in their favour in a year when such a sickness travels, is what we meant and no more. We are glad the limits are written as they are: it is custom, it works without us, it does nothing against sickness carried by breath, touch or beast, and it takes nothing from our Clean water and gives nothing to it. We would not want it otherwise.

The weakness: accepted. A company moving at four fifths of the public rate more than three days' march from the river or in dry country, one step against them in any contest there, few horses, poor stores for a long march, and their mood taken down a step in a dry summer at home. We offered it knowing they would feel it, and we would feel it with them. We have no counter. We take the price going one way, one for one, as the Keeper says.

### Not priced
We note that the number is five thousand and that there is no cost of size tonight. We note that the bowl readers are fitted by the Keeper, the number of workers included, and we have no objection. We note that the captains drawn from several houses, the hearth bowl, the flood marks and the rest stand as written, and that what we hope the ferry keeper will carry is heard and not promised. We ask for nothing more.

We found no wording that is wrong.
