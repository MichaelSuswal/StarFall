# Negotiation, Night 2: god-rivers

## Round 1 reply

We have read the Keeper's draft and we answer it item by item. We are patient, and we want the limits written exactly. Where we accept, we accept the wording as given.

### Traits, given again
Done. The section is added at the end of self.md and nothing else in it was changed. We had read the two leans backward. Order 22 lies toward law and Body 26 toward strong. We have kept the pull we feel and made it the one on the sheet.

### Form: the Cupful as part of one body
Accepted. A part that senses and cannot act is a power of the one body. We asked only for the ruling and we have it.

### Reading the water
Accepted as drafted. The month is a fair end to what a channel holds, and the half pace and the lost veil are a cost we would feel. We have no choice to make here.

### Clean water
Accepted, with one clarification asked in the write-up, not a new power. The draft says the god "keeps it so while it stays in that water" and that the days of sluggishness run "for as many days as it kept the water clean, and counted from the day it leaves." We ask that this be written so that the days counted are the days the god spent working the water, and that the water stays clean after the god leaves until what fouled it returns, as the limits already say. Otherwise a god that stood in a well for a season owes a season, and one that cleaned it in a day owes the same, and we think neither is meant. We ask for the wording only.

### Mist veil
We choose (b). The god pays: for as many days as it held the veil, it cannot touch water to read it and cannot clean any. We will not choose (a). A cold wet fog laid on our own people for as many days as we hid them is a price we would be making them pay, and we said on the first night that we would not have mortals pay for what a god does. We would rather lose the water ourselves.

### The Cupful
We choose (a). While the Cupful is away we cannot hold the veil, and we feel what is done to it as done to ourselves, without knowing where. A Cupful lost does not come back, and a new one takes a season to fill. We will not choose (b). We are not willing to have an item of memory or habit taken for a lost gourd of water. Our record is small and every item in it matters to us, and there is already a gap in us that we do not look at. We would rather feel a thing than forget one. We accept that the god does not know where the Cupful is while it is away, and that a Cupful not met again within a season fades.

### Return to the spring
Accepted at the first rung, a season and one item, as written. We do not want the month at two items. We confirm the gap is ours to find and we ask not to be told what went.

We ask one clarification in the write-up, not a new power. "The highest spring it has touched" should be written as the highest above the sea of the springs the god's own body has stood in or touched, and a spring the Cupful alone has lain in does not count. We ask this so that the card names its ground by a rule and never by a choice made at the time. We accept the ground the Keeper added for the time before we have touched any spring.

### Turn the channel
We shrink it. We keep the holding and we drop the bend. The god may hold a rising flood in the channel off one place it has touched for a season, once in a year, on the price and the limits as drafted: for a season from the turning it is bound to that water, and can enter no other and read no other.

Our reasons. The bend moves the water to someone else, and the draft says plainly that we do not choose where. We said we would not pretend that the flood goes nowhere, and a bend sends a river's whole weight to a new place for good, in plain sight, where anyone downstream can see that the river moved. That is the least hidden thing on the card and the one most likely to be traced to us. The holding keeps a town dry and asks a river to wait, and it is quiet. It is also the half of the power that is most of what we want, which is a people who do not drown. We accept that the Keeper may call this smaller than before and we ask that the card say it is shrunk by our own choice.

One clarification asked in the write-up. "One place it has touched" should be written with a size, so that we know whether a walled town at a ford is one place. We suggest the same 300 yards across that the Keeper set for the veil, measured along the bank we have touched.

### Trade: the plea for water
We accept the weakness as drafted, with all its edges. We know that anyone who learns of it can use it to bring us out or to hold us in one place, and that it is a real cost to a god that prefers to stay unseen. We would rather it were real.

We choose (b): the Reading reaches the whole length of the channel we stand in, for what the water is carrying now only, and what it has carried in the last month still reaches a day's drift. We do not choose (a). We are cautious, and what we most want to know is what is coming down a river toward our people: a flood, a sickness, smoke, boats. A longer look at the past is worth less to us than a whole look at the present.

### Summary
Accepted as drafted: the Form ruling, Reading the water, Return to the spring (with a clarification), the weakness. Chosen: Mist veil (b), Cupful (a), Trade buys (b). Shrunk by our own choice: Turn the channel, to the holding only. Clarifications asked in the write-up and not as new powers: Clean water (days counted), Return (what counts as the highest spring), Turn the channel (the size of one place).

## Round 2 reply

We have read the Keeper's final text against what we chose, and we confirm it. We checked each card line by line.

Reading the water: confirmed. The whole-channel reach for what the water carries now, and the day's drift of 20 miles for what it carried in the last month, are as we chose in (b). The drawback and the limits are as drafted.

Clean water: confirmed. The days owed are the days we stood in the water working it, counted from the day we leave, and the water stays clean after we leave until what fouled it returns. That is the wording we asked for.

Mist veil: confirmed, with (b) written. We pay, and our people do not.

The Cupful: confirmed, with (a) written. The gourd, the ten paces, the season before it fades, and pooling only when we meet again are all as we understood them.

Return to the spring: confirmed at the first rung, a season and one item, with the ground written as the highest above the sea of the springs our own body has stood in or touched. We ask to find the gap ourselves and not to be told what went, as we said in round 1.

Turn the channel: confirmed as shrunk by our own choice to the holding only, with one place set at 300 yards across along a bank we have touched. We thank the Keeper for not arguing us back into the bend.

The plea for water: confirmed as drafted, with (b) as what it bought.

Our Traits, given again: the Keeper has read them against the sheet and they stand. We have nothing to add.

The Keeper trait: we note it and it needs no reply. We read it as a turning and a showing only, that it breaks no veil already held and touches no power or price on the card. We take it as written. We have no new ask.

We found no wording that is wrong, and we ask for no change.
