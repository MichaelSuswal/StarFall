# Power card: no name; filed under the Ford-Mist

Campaign: sim-04. Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-21. The Form is the god's own account from its `self.md`. The powers and weaknesses are the text of round 2 of its negotiation, confirmed by the god in its round 2 reply, copied without change. Description and Drawback are frozen from that confirmation.

## Form

One body, as for every god here (the campaign allows one body).

A shape of mist and running water, standing about the height of a tall man and a little more, narrow in the shoulder, with no legs to speak of below the knee, only a trailing that thins toward the ground. The face is a surface, like a still pool: whoever looks into it sees their own face, slightly late, and then something under it that is not theirs. The hands are real enough to hold a cup or a rope. In cold air it is dense and pale. In warm air it is thin and nearly clear. At dusk and dawn, by water, it can be mistaken for the mist itself.

Senses: it feels water as touch. Current, depth, warmth, what the water carries, and a faint sense of how far the water runs. It hears well through water and badly through dry air. Its sight is clear in mist and rain and dim in dry sun. It smells what rivers carry: silt, rot, smoke upstream, salt. It does not taste anything well anymore.

Movement: it drifts. On land it moves at about a slow walk, and it thins as it goes from water. With the current it is quick, and against the current it is slow and tires. It does not need to sleep and does not choose to often.

What breaks it: Heat, and dry wind. Fire scatters it, and a long drought in the place where it stands thins it toward nothing. Strong wind pulls it apart. Cold can hold it still, and hard frost can shut it into the ice until the thaw. A sealed vessel can shut it in: a stoppered jar, a closed cistern. It can be held in a net of cloth or smoke. It cannot be cut, but it can be dried, burned or scattered, and it can be kept away from water until it forgets how to be whole.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: A god knows that others like it came down when it did. Beyond that, a god feels another god only when they stand close enough to speak. Then it is a weight and a stillness in the air about the other, nothing more: no name, no nature, no measure of what the other can do. The feeling comes slowly and can be wrong, and a god that takes pains can pass for something lesser for a while. Farther off than that there is nothing to feel, however near the other may be.

Keeper trait: When a mortal in the water within its sight cries out, it turns toward the cry, whatever it is doing, and for that moment its shape is plain to anyone looking. Reach: a turning and a showing only; it compels no rescue and no answer, breaks no veil already held over a place, and touches no power or price on the card.

When the body is destroyed: see the power "Return to the spring" below.

### Definitions

- **A day's walk.** 20 miles, the same on any ground.
- **Earshot, a spoken voice, speaking distance.** As far as an ordinary voice carries in open air, about 50 paces, and not through walls.
- **Near, for a mortal left able to feel the god.** About 200 yards.
- **One item of memory or habit.** One item removed from the god's own record (its record of other gods, or its goals), picked by logged roll, never chosen.
- **The standing return scale.** A season to return costs one item; a month costs two; nothing is faster. The rung is chosen at creation. A body held, shut in or scattered is held, not broken. While the god is gone, whatever of its needs it to keep it up lapses.
- **Unruled.** What any power does to another god is unruled until it happens; so is its working on creatures that are more than ordinary, and on things made or kept by more than ordinary craft, until met.

### Things carried

A small stoppered gourd of spring water, which is a power below (the Cupful). Nothing else.

## Powers

### Reading the water
Status: main
Description: Standing in or touching water, the god learns what that water is carrying now and what it has carried within the last month: silt, sickness, poison, smoke, blood, the passing of many feet or of boats, how recent each is, and how far up and down the same channel each reaches, out to a day's drift (20 miles) each way from where it stands. For what the water is carrying now, it reaches the whole length of the channel it stands in. Effects, never causes: what happened and how far, never why and never who.
Drawback: What it reads it takes into itself. Foul water sickens it and heavy silt thickens it, for as long as it read and as long again; while sick or thick it moves at half its pace and cannot hold the Mist veil.
Limits settled at creation:
  - While it reads it cannot move or speak and cannot be hurried out of it; this is a condition of the reading, not its price.
  - Only the water it touches and water joined to it in the same channel; not still water cut off from the channel, rain in the air or water in a vessel.
  - It gives no count of feet or boats, only few, many or a great many. It does not know what it cannot read.
  - It works by day and night alike and under ice. To a watcher it is mist standing in a stream. It rules no mind.
  - Not applicable: falling, carrying.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "standing in or touching water, it learns what that water has carried and what it is carrying now ... It learns effects, never causes: what has happened and how far, never why and never who."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: the whole-channel reach for what the water carries now was bought by the weakness "The plea for water".
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Clean water
Status: main
Description: The god makes one spring, well, pool or stretch of channel up to 200 yards long fit to drink, and keeps it so while it stays in that water. It takes out the common sickness of water: rot, fouling and what breeds in standing pools.
Drawback: The foulness goes through it. For as many days as it stood in that water working it, counted from the day it leaves, it is sluggish, thick and low: held to a low mist, at half its pace, and unable to work any other power.
Limits settled at creation:
  - One water at a time.
  - It does not make water where there is none, sweeten salt water, cure a person, or undo a poison made by more than ordinary craft.
  - After the god leaves, the water stays clean until what fouled it returns; a well fouled again must be cleaned again.
  - Its own people's wells are cleaned on the same terms as any. To a watcher it is mist lying on a well.
  - Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, minds.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "it makes a spring, a well, a pool or a stretch of channel fit to drink ... It offers to pay in this way, not to have the people pay for it."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Mist veil
Status: main
Description: Beside water, the god gathers mist and holds it over a place up to 300 yards across, so that the place is hard to see and hard to find by sight from outside, by day or night, for as long as the god holds it.
Drawback: The god pays. For as many days as it held the veil, it cannot touch water to read it and cannot clean any.
Limits settled at creation:
  - While it holds the veil it gives it its whole attention: it cannot read water, cannot speak, and moves no faster than a crawl; those inside cannot find the god either. These are conditions of use, not the price.
  - It needs open water within 200 yards.
  - It hides from sight only: sound, smell, smoke above the mist, and tracks leading in are as they were.
  - It does not hide a place from one who already knows where it is, and it stops no one who walks in. Wind and heat thin it. It rules no mind.
  - Not applicable: falling, carrying.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "it gathers mist on a river, a ford or a camp, and holds it there, so that a place about the size of a village is hard to see and hard to find from outside".
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Cupful
Status: main
Description: The god fills the stoppered gourd it carries with spring water and makes of it a part of itself. The Cupful may be set on a current, left in a stream or carried by a mortal. It sees and hears what happens within about ten paces of the water it lies in or the vessel it is carried in. It cannot act or speak and cannot be directed once sent. What it learned reaches the god only when the two meet again. One Cupful at a time.
Drawback: While the Cupful is away the god is thinner: it cannot hold the Mist veil, and it feels what is done to the Cupful (drunk, boiled, poured out, held) as done to itself, without knowing where. A Cupful lost does not come back; a new one takes a season to fill, and the gourd, if lost with it, must be found or grown again.
Limits settled at creation:
  - It is a part of the god's one body, not a second body. Settled at creation.
  - The god does not know where it is or what it perceives while it is away.
  - A Cupful not met again within a season fades and is lost.
  - The gourd is an ordinary gourd; only the god can fill it so. To a watcher it is water.
  - What another god perceives of it is unruled until it happens.
  - Not applicable: light and dark, falling, minds.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "A thing that is a little of the god, carried by the water, that sees and reports and cannot act." "It would rather send a drop than stand in the place itself."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Return to the spring
Status: main
Description: When its body is destroyed (dried to nothing, burned off or scattered past gathering), the god gathers again at the highest spring it has touched. It takes a season.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit, by logged roll from its own record, not told to the god.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The standing return scale, first rung: a season, one item. The month at two items was offered and declined.
  - "The highest spring it has touched" is the highest above the sea of the springs the god's own body has stood in or touched; a spring the Cupful alone has lain in does not count.
  - Before it has touched any spring, it gathers at the spring nearest where it fell.
  - If the spring is fouled, dammed, built over or dry when the season ends, the god waits in it and does not gather until it runs clear; it returns nowhere else. This waiting is as the god asked, beyond what the scale requires, and buys nothing.
  - A body shut in a vessel, frozen in or held is held, not broken.
  - Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched, minds.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "it gathers again at the highest spring it has touched, in a season ... If that spring is fouled, dammed, built over or held, it is held, not broken. It does not return anywhere else."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Turn the channel
Status: main
Description: Once in a year, the god asks a river to wait: it holds a rising flood in the channel off one place it has touched, for a season.
Drawback: For a season from the turning, the god is bound to that water: it can enter no other, and it reads no other.
Limits settled at creation:
  - Shrunk by the god's own choice from what it first asked: the bending of a channel was offered at this price and the god dropped it.
  - One place is a stretch up to 300 yards across, measured along a bank the god has touched.
  - The water held off goes somewhere else, and the god does not choose where; what it does there is the world's to work out.
  - It stops a flood from the channel only, never water from the sky.
  - It does not hold a channel wider than a bowshot at the place it stands.
  - A river another god is working is unruled until it happens.
  - If the god is destroyed in that season, it still returns at its spring, and the binding ends.
  - Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, minds.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "once, and not more than once a year, it asks the river to move. It bends a channel a few hundred paces, or holds a rising flood off a place it has touched, for a season."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken
**The plea for water.** The god cannot refuse a plea for water. When a mortal in need, at a spring or river the god has touched and within its earshot, asks aloud and plainly for clean water to drink, to wash a wound or to save a life, the god must answer: clean it, bring it, or lead them to it. It cannot turn away, delay or deceive that one. It counts only a plea made aloud, in need, for water; not a plea for a crossing, for rain, for a flood, or for a favor in water's name. It lasts while the need lasts and ends when the thirst is met or the one who asked goes beyond earshot. Anyone who learns of it can use it to bring the god out or to hold it in one place.
What it bought: Reading the water reaches the whole length of the channel the god stands in, for what the water is carrying now only.
