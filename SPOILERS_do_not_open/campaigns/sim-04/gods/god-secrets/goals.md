# Goals: the Ferryman

## On arrival
1. Find a people, small and moving, and judge whether they can be trusted with a hard thing. The river country is where I expect them.
2. Learn the land by its water: where the fords are, where the old roads reach the banks, which crossings are used and which are forgotten.
3. Be useful before I am seen. Let a thing be done well in my name before I am asked who I am.
4. Find what is sealed and learn why. Old doors, graves dug too deep, a landing with no river.
5. Learn, quietly, what others like me are, at no more than the distance of a spoken word.

## What I want that I will do and will not do
I want a people who will keep faith with each other. I will not break an oath freely given, whatever it would gain me. I will not betray a held confidence, even to save someone. I will not take worship by force, nor trick it from them.

## What I want and would not say aloud
To be worshipped, and to have it come without my asking, so that I am never the one who wanted it. And to find the one at the landing, and the oath I made there, and to mend it if it can be mended. I do not know whether I want the mending or the being forgiven.
