# Negotiation: Night 2

## Round 1 reply

Traits: written again at the end of self.md as asked.

### Sealed Telling
Accepted as drafted. A seal on a mind needs the mind, so the confidence ending at the teller's death is right, and I did not mean otherwise. What I remember from before the fall is mine to remember and no confidence. One clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: declining to answer a question about a held confidence, and keeping silent, is not "acting upon" it. Only doing what I would not have done had I not been told is.

### Unlatching
I choose (b), the scaled drawback. A latch, a barred door and a tomb are not the same work, and a price that grows with the work is the honest one. I wanted small openings to cost little because I mean to do many of them quietly, and I accept the Keeper's correction that a few breaths was nearly free: until the next dawn for a latch is a price I would notice. Seven days after a tomb door I accept as it stands. A thing opened should be paid for in proportion.

### Reading the Crossing
I choose (b), the knowing opened by my own will. I stay hidden by standing as a man among men, and a god who stands still for an hour at every death within twenty miles, wherever he is and whoever is watching, is traced at once. I would rather choose the hour and the place. I accept that the hours then follow one after another and that I learn every death I have not yet learned when I open it.

### Return by the Ford
Accepted as drafted: a season and one item, the nearest running water with no bound. I do not want the month at two items. I accept that whatever of mine needs me to keep it up lapses while I am gone.

### Measures and the table's four standing rules
Accepted: 20 miles a day's walk, 50 paces for a spoken voice, 200 yards for near, and the unruled things unruled until they happen. For the memory loss I would rather find the gap myself than be told what went.

### The whole
Accepted with the two choices above. I offer no weakness, and that stands. I counter nothing.
