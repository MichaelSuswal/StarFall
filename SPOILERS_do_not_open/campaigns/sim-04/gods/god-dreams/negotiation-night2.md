
## Round 1 reply

I take the Keeper's reasons as fair, and I accept most of the draft as written. Where I choose, I say why. My traits are written again in `self.md` as the sheet has them.

### The Sleeper's Door
Accepted as drafted. The mark is the price I offered, and one sleeper in a night suits a god who would have a word in a dream stay rare. I ask for no change.

### The Lamp-Sight
Accepted, with these choices.
Drawback: (b), the god pays. For as long as I looked, and as long again, my own eyes are dazzled. I refuse (a). A flame that stands still and straight in a draught tells every watcher that something looks back through it, and I am a hidden god; to be seen watching would cost me more than any dazzle.
Reach: (d), any lamp lit for me, at any distance, sight only. I give up hearing for reach. A people of one to three thousand will keep their lamps near, but a lamp lit at a far border, in a far village or in a stranger's house is worth more to me by sight at any distance than by sight and sound within a day's walk. I take (d) on those terms and ask only for the wording in the write-up, not a new power: that "sight only" means the room as the flame lights it, now, and that the flame must have been lit for me by a mortal in my name, as the draft says.
I note the Keeper's review of the first draft: "what has happened there" is struck and I do not contest it.

### The Lamp I Gather At
Accepted, with this choice: (a). With no lamp kept for me, I gather at the hearth nearest where my body was destroyed where a fire is kept through the night. I refuse (b); it buys nothing and leaves me scattered for as long as mortals neglect me.
The rung: a season, one item. I do not ask for the month.
What went: I will find the gap myself. I do not wish to be told.
One clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: that the Hollow Season does not stop this return. A hearth fire not lit for me is not a flame lit for me, so the Hollow Season would hold when I rise from it, but it must not keep me from rising.

### Trade: the Hollow Season
Accepted as offered, with the Keeper's added line that my own lantern does not count. I see the danger and I take it: anyone who learns of it can bring it on by putting out the lamps. I accept it with open eyes, and I will keep it as secret as I keep my other faults. The reach of three days (60 miles) for the Sleeper's Door is the step I wanted.
