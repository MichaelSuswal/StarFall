
## Round 1 reply

### The dark eye (the edge)
Accepted as drafted. It is the size I said, it needs some light, and it touches neither the Sleeper's Door nor the Lamp-Sight. I ask no change.

### The glare (the weakness)
Accepted as drafted. Three quarters of the public rates in the open from late spring to early autumn, mid-morning to mid-afternoon on a clear day, one step against them in sun contests, and eye sickness for some each summer. My people would notice it every year, and I do not wish it softened. It pays for the edge one for one, and I leave the price as it stands.

### Not priced
Accepted. I take the number as given, the household lamp as custom with its practical cost in oil and tallow, and wick-singing and its number of workers as the Keeper fits them. I make no new ask.
