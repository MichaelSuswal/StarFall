# Goals: Ennevar

## On arrival
1. Find or gather a people to keep. Small, settled, able to endure a hard winter.
2. See a lamp lit for me in every house of that people, and a shrine in every village. Be kept, not merely obeyed.
3. Raise a court between me and the world: a mortal line on the throne in my name, counsellors and stewards who stand before me, so that I am reached only through courtesy.
4. Keep the people safe from the unseen. Learn what comes in the night, and be awake when it does.
5. Learn, quietly and from a distance, who the others who came down are, and what they are, before they learn what I am. Pass for lesser while I look.
6. Learn why the dreaming mind opens as it does, and what lies beyond it.

## What I will not do, whatever it would gain me
- I will not abandon a people of mine in a crisis I could have met.
- I will not let a lamp lit for me go unanswered when a mortal calls.
- I will not trade my people's safety for my own, or for worship.
- I will not feign a harm to a sleeper that I have not done, nor harm a sleeper in a dream to make them serve.

## What I want and will not say aloud (kept in this file, given to no other god)
I want a companion: one of my own kind, or a mortal who is not afraid of me and cannot be bought, who will stay awake with me in the dark. I do not believe I will find one. I want to be forgiven something I cannot name.
