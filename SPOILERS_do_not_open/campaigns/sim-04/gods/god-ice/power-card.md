# Power card: no name; filed under the White Beast

Campaign: sim-04. Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-21. The Form is the god's own account from its `self.md`. The powers and weaknesses are the text of round 2 of its negotiation, confirmed by the god in its round 2 reply, copied without change. Description and Drawback are frozen from that confirmation.

## Form

One body, as for every god here (the campaign allows one body).

A great beast, four legged and heavy shouldered, about the height of a horse at the shoulder and half again as long, with a thick pale coat that frosts at the tips and a long jaw. The face is broad and still, the eyes pale and unhurried, set a little too far apart, so that it seems to look at two things at once and weigh them. Breath smokes in any weather. Where I lie long, the ground glazes. I walk slowly and I do not tire, at the pace of a large animal, no faster. My senses are those of a beast of the cold: scent, hearing, sight in poor light. I can speak human speech aloud, low and unhurried, though it comes out of a jaw not built for it. Sometimes I laugh at a moment when no one else would, and I cannot always tell beforehand when.

What breaks it: Heavy blows and long wounds hurt me and can ruin the body. Fire and sustained heat weaken me, slowly. Deep summer is hard on me. Chains and a pit can hold me, and a shut room can shut me in. Being held is held, not broken.

God-sense, as set for this campaign: A god knows that others like it came down when it did. Beyond that, a god feels another god only when they stand close enough to speak. Then it is a weight and a stillness in the air about the other, nothing more: no name, no nature, no measure of what the other can do. The feeling comes slowly and can be wrong, and a god that takes pains can pass for something lesser for a while. Farther off than that there is nothing to feel, however near the other may be.

Keeper trait: The laugh comes when a judgment it has spoken falls hardest on the one least to blame, and those who hear it know which judgment it was laughing at. Reach: a laugh and what hearers make of it; it binds no term, changes no judgment, and touches no power or price on the card.

When the body is destroyed: see the power "Returning from the cold" below.

### Definitions

- **A day's walk.** 20 miles, the same on any ground.
- **Earshot, a spoken voice, speaking distance.** As far as an ordinary voice carries in open air, about 50 paces, and not through walls.
- **Near, for a mortal left able to feel the god.** About 200 yards.
- **One item of memory or habit.** One item removed from the god's own record (its record of other gods, or its goals), picked by logged roll, never chosen.
- **The standing return scale.** A season to return costs one item; a month costs two; nothing is faster. The rung is chosen at creation. A body held, shut in or scattered is held, not broken. While the god is gone, whatever of its needs it to keep it up lapses.
- **Unruled.** What any power does to another god is unruled until it happens; so is its working on creatures that are more than ordinary, and on things made or kept by more than ordinary craft, until met.

## Powers

### Winter's Reach
Status: main
Description: Within about a mile of the god, on ground and water it can see, it brings frost and cold: it freezes standing or slow water (a pond, a stretch of river, a well), thickens ice already there, or brings a killing frost to one field or yard of up to ten acres. One working in a day.
Drawback: The god pays. While the cold of a working of its own still holds, the god cannot bring cold again, and it is as slow and heavy as it is in deep summer.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The cold of a working holds for seven days: for a frosted field or yard, seven days from the working; for frozen water, the seven days it stays frozen after the god lets it go. Only cold that would not be there but for the working counts: ice the season would have made anyway does not, so the god is not slowed by the weather it stands in.
  - As slow and heavy as in deep summer means at half its pace.
  - The cold stays after the god leaves: a frosted field is barren for the season, and frozen water stays frozen seven days after the god lets it go and cannot be unfrozen by the god in its first day. This lingering is what the power does, not its price.
  - It makes no snow from a clear sky and cools nothing that moves fast.
  - It works on water and ground, never directly on a creature's body.
  - It needs sight: nothing in darkness without a light on the place, nothing behind a wall or a ridge.
  - Its own people's fields and wells freeze the same as any.
  - Anyone watching sees frost come where the beast is looking. It rules no mind.
  - Not applicable: falling, carrying.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "Within about a mile of me I can bring frost and cold. I can freeze standing or slow water ..., thicken ice that is already there, and bring a killing frost to a field or a yard of ground I can see."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Spoken Term
Status: main
Description: When the god speaks terms aloud in a plain sentence to one mortal face to face, and that mortal, understanding the words, agrees aloud, the terms hold that mortal for the term of years the god states. A breach is felt by the one who breaks it as a creeping cold in the hands and chest that grows with each day the term goes unmet and ends when it is mended. It does not kill and it does not compel.
Drawback: The terms weigh. The god carries every standing term as cold in its own belly: with more than twelve standing it is slow, at half its pace; with more than forty it cannot use Winter's Reach at all.
Limits settled at creation:
  - A term stands from the yes until it ends by its years, by the mortal's death or by the god's release. A term ended does not count. A term breached and then mended counts for as long as it still has years to run.
  - Every term binds the god to its exact wording as well, and the god cannot state a falsehood inside a term; if the god breaks the letter of its own term, the cold comes into it too until it is mended. This follows only from the god's own breach, so it is a limit and buys nothing.
  - It binds only the mortal who agreed, only to what was said. A spoken yes counts; a nod does not.
  - A yes given under threat of force does not bind.
  - A mortal who was deceived about what the words mean is not bound; a mortal who understood the words and was deceived about anything else is bound.
  - It cannot bind one who did not hear it, a child too young to understand, or a god.
  - The cold makes the breach certain to the breaker and shows nothing to anyone else.
  - Whether a term may be spoken for the god by a mortal under its seal was asked and refused on these terms. Settled at creation.
  - It makes no one trust anyone. It rules no mind.
  - Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "When a mortal who understands the words agrees aloud to terms that I have spoken aloud, and I name them in a plain sentence, the terms hold them. ... It only makes the cost of breaking it certain and exact."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Returning from the cold
Status: main
Description: When its body is destroyed, the god gathers again, whole, out of the nearest large body of ice or deep snow to where it was destroyed. It takes a season.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit, by logged roll from its own record, not told to the god.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The standing return scale, first rung: a season, one item. The month at two items was offered and declined.
  - "Large" means ice or snow enough to cover the god's whole body lying down: a pond frozen through, or a drift taller than a man and ten paces across. A skin of ice or a dusting does not serve.
  - With none near, it gathers at the nearest ground that holds such ice or snow, however far, and travels back from it at its own pace.
  - A body chained, pitted or shut in is held, not broken.
  - Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched, minds.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "If my body is broken, I gather again out of the nearest large body of ice or deep snow, or if there is none, out of any frozen ground."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Prince's Term
Status: main (added Night 3)
Description: One merchant-prince, sworn to the god in its hearing, may speak a term in the god's name, face to face, to one mortal who understands the words and agrees aloud. The term then holds that mortal for the stated years as the god's own Spoken Term would, with a breach felt as the same creeping cold. Only terms of debt, toll, tally, delivery, carriage, trade or service.
Drawback: Each time the prince speaks a term, the god is slow for one day wherever it is: half its pace, as after a working of cold, with no cold about it. The day begins when the term is spoken, whether or not the god knows of it. Days owed run one after another, and after any slow days owed for a working of cold; the god is never below half pace for them.
Limits settled at creation:
  - The prince's count stands apart from the god's own. He may have no more than twenty terms standing; at twenty he can bind no more until one ends. A term stands only while unfulfilled, unreleased and unended.
  - Never a term on a person's body, name, kin, faith or movement. Never to a child too young to understand, to a god, or to anyone under threat of force; none of these is bound.
  - One mortal at a time, who must say yes. Never a people as a mass.
  - One prince at a time. He is sworn within earshot of the god (about fifty paces in open air, not through walls); no touch is needed. When he dies or is released the power sleeps, and the terms he spoke stand until they end; it wakes when the next is sworn.
  - A term he speaks outside these bounds does not hold. The god may end any term of his by its own word.
  - What the prince bears, fitted by the Keeper and no part of the price: each term he speaks stands as cold in his own belly; with more than twelve standing he is at half his pace; he cannot lie inside a term he speaks; if he breaks the letter of one the cold is in him until he mends it. The people see it.
  - Not applicable: falling, scarcity, things made by more than ordinary craft. It rules no mind: a term binds only one who agreed.
  - Intent, the god's first wording: "The merchant-prince may speak a term in my name and under my seal, face to face, to one mortal who comes before him, and if that mortal agrees aloud and understands the words, the term holds them for the stated years, as mine would."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none. Priced by its own drawback.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
