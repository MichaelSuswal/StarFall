# Goals: on arrival

## Stated
1. Find the people I expect and take them as mine: a walled seat, steadings round it, about five thousand, a merchant-prince to rule for me and a priesthood to keep the rite.
2. Make the terms they live by plain and exact, and have them said aloud, so that every dispute can be settled by what was said.
3. Secure a granary and a store of fuel before the deep cold. A people that starves in its first winter has no use for terms.
4. Learn what this land holds: the old roads, the sealed doors, the high cold places, who lives past the borders.
5. Learn what the other gods are, slowly, by standing near them and letting them speak. Give away nothing I need not.

## Held, not said aloud
6. Find out what I ate. I do not know that I want to. I want to know whether it is still in me.
7. Be held to my own wording, by someone clever enough to do it. I would like to meet the one who catches me.
