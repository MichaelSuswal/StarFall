## Answer 1
A frozen lake at the hour before the crust breaks. Everything is clear, everything is visible, and everything below the surface is waiting to be asked a question. A fair world, which is not the same as a kind one. I want the sound of a good bargain being struck in cold air, and somewhere far off, a laugh that does not fit.

## Answer 2
Ice. High ground that holds snow into the warm months, cold coast, long valleys that close in winter and make their people honest about what they owe each other. A land where the weather keeps the accounts.

## Answer 3
Four seasons, and the winter is the longest and the most serious. Summers short and bright, a season of debts incurred. Autumn is harvest and settling of accounts. Spring is the thaw, when what was hidden comes up and everyone has to look at it. Winter is when the world tells the truth. Let the years turn slowly and let no season be cancelled.
When each year finds me, I want to wake into the first hard cold, the week the ponds skin over and the roads begin to close. Not the dead of winter, when everything is already decided, but the moment it is still being decided.
I wake into early winter.

## Answer 4
Some of each, and I would rather not be told which is which. Wild country no one has walked, because I like to be the first to name a thing and then watch others argue about the name. And old kingdoms already rooted, because a rooted people has contracts, and a contract is a handle. Give me neighbors with long memories and a few blank spaces on the map.

## Answer 5
Thousands of years, with ruins under ruins. A people who have been wronged and have wronged others for long enough to have law about it. I want old verdicts still standing that nobody remembers the reasons for. I do not trust a world with no history, because there is nothing in it to judge.

## Answer 6
Closer to the rumor than the wise-woman, but not at the rumor. A few in each generation, in odd places, and most people sure they are lying. Rare enough that it is leverage, common enough that it cannot be ignored.

## Answer 7
It should feel like speaking a sentence that turns out to be a debt. The words land and the world rearranges toward them, and then something comes due. The cost should be exact and sometimes late: a year, a name, a season of warmth, a memory of a face. Never random. The price should be one you could have read, had you been clever enough to read it.

## Answer 8
Premise bent: a god should stand out a great deal, but not in everything. In force and in years, yes, far beyond a mortal sorcerer. In cleverness, mortals should be able to catch me out now and then, and I would like to be caught. The one-eyed man in the kingdom of the blind is closer, but let the blind have very good hands.

## Answer 9
Strange at the edges and familiar at the center. Animals people know, and in the high and cold places, things with no name that mortals have only stories for. I would like the cold places to be where the unnamed things are. Let a hunter come back with a tale and no proof.

## Answer 10
The thing that is not malice. Famine, a bad winter, a pass that closes, a debt called in, a verdict that is correct and ruinous. Dangers that cannot be argued with because they do not want anything. Mortals can survive an enemy. It is the indifferent thing that makes a story worth telling afterward.

## Answer 11
Warmth, in every form it takes. Fuel, shelter, a full granary in the third month of snow, a hearth that is yours. Mortals will trade, bind themselves and break each other for it. A world where the scarce thing is the thing winter takes away.

## Answer 12
Old roads, under the ice, still straight. Sealed doors in the rock that someone shut on purpose and left a notice beside. And bones of something enormous, because I would like to stand over them and learn whether I am looking at a stranger or a relative. I do not wish to be told which.

## Answer 13
Yes, a few, and not many. I would like one people who are slow and patient, who keep long records and do not care who rules. I would like another that mortals are afraid to bargain with, because it keeps every term. Neither should be friends of humans. Neither should be enemies. They should be parties.

## Answer 14
A few. Travelers who arrive half-frozen with tools that do not fit the local craft and knowledge that does not fit local law. I would like them to be few enough that each one is a case. I will want to hear every one of them, and I will want to hear what they left out.

## Answer 15
Face to face only, and slowly. A pull at any distance is a leash, and I do not like being found. Let a god know another by the way the air behaves around it, up close, with time to be wrong about it. I want to be able to lie to a stranger about what I am, and have it work for a while.

## Answer 16
Premise bent: one body, and I would like the body to be large. If a god is to be elsewhere, it should be by something sent, not by being split, and what is sent should be a lesser thing that can be lost. The cost of a second place should be that the first place goes quiet. I do not trust a god who is in two places and whole in both.

## Answer 17
One who reads the terms. A mortal who asks what a thing costs before they take it, and is wrong only about the last clause. A trader, a judge, a liar with a conscience. Let this land produce someone who could have ruled and chose to be right instead. Or the reverse. I will enjoy either.

## Answer 18
A truth, and I do not want to know in advance what kind. Something about what I am, or why I came down hungry, or what the thing I should not have eaten was. I want to find it in a plain place, at the wrong moment, and I expect to laugh.

## Answer 19
That a promise made in this world should be able to be kept to the letter and still ruin the one who made it, and that this should be called justice by the one who wrote it down. I want the world to hold me to my own wording. I have not said it before because it is not a wish a sensible being makes.
