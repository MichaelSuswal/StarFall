# Negotiation: god-ice, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

Traits: written again in self.md under "## Traits, given again", from the leans as the sheet has them.

### Winter's Reach
Choice: (a). I pay it myself. I rule a people, and (b) freezes their fields and wells with everyone else's every time I work it. I would sooner be slow than make my own people pay for my reach. A week of being heavy after a working is a cost I can plan around, and I am patient.
Clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: please write that "past its natural season" means ice or frost that holds after the season it would normally hold in. In early winter, ice that would form anyway does not count, so I am not slowed by the weather I stand in. I ask only that the wording say so.
I accept the ten acres, the one working a day, and the limits as written. I accept that the lingering is a limit and not a price. I did not think of it as one until you said so.

### The Spoken Term
Choice: (b). The terms weigh. I would not hand my winter to any mortal who chooses to break a term. A ceiling on how many I hold is a limit I can read, and I will pick whom I bind with care. I will hold few terms and keep them.
Clarification, in the write-up: a term that has ended, by its years, by the mortal's death or by my release, no longer counts as standing, and a term that was mended after a breach counts as standing only if it still has years to run. I ask that this be written so I can count exactly.
On force: a yes given under threat of force does not bind. A term is a judgment and a judgment is not one if it was taken at the point of a blade. This is a limit I want, not one I resent. It also means no one can use me to bind another with a knife.
On the seal: I accept your answer. I take no weakness tonight. I will bring a merchant-prince speaking in my name to Night 3 as an edge of my people, priced there.
I accept the limits as written, including that I cannot state a falsehood inside a term.

### Returning from the cold
Choice: (a). I gather at the nearest ground that holds ice or snow, however far, and travel back at my own pace. I want no waiting for a frost that may not come, and (b) buys nothing.
I accept the first rung, a season and one item. You are right that I misspoke: I did not mean the faster rung. I meant only that I did not ask to be faster than the scale allows. I accept the item taken by logged roll and not told to me. I will find the gap myself.
Clarification, in the write-up: please write what counts as "large" for a body of ice or deep snow, so that I know before I am destroyed whether a drift or a frozen pond will do.

### The whole
No weakness, no trade, nothing stronger than a newly landed god carries. I accept the three powers as drafted with the choices above.
