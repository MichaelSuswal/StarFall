
## Round 1 reply

### The Prince's Term
I accept the power as a power of mine, marked "added Night 3", and I accept the draft text and limits as written, including that one prince at a time holds it and that I may end any term of his by my word.

Choice: (b). Counts apart, his ceiling twenty, and a day slow for me each time he speaks a term.
Reasons: under (a) a shared count of twelve would be reached within a season of the prince's trading, and I would be at half pace for most of every year. At forty together I would lose Winter's Reach altogether, and the prince could take it from me by doing his work. Those are costs I would pay every day without choosing them. Under (b) the cost is a day for a term, which I can count, and it falls only when a term is made, so a patient prince who speaks few good terms costs me little and a careless one costs me more. That is the right way round for a ruler who answers to me. I would notice it, and I do not ask to be spared it. I take the ceiling of twenty without complaint.

Clarifications, in the write-up and not a new power:
1. Slow days from the prince's terms and slow days from a working of cold do not add. At half pace I am at half pace, not at a quarter, however many reasons there are.
2. Days owed run one after another, as the draft says, and a day owed is a day, not a day for each mouth. Please write that the days begin when the term is spoken and not when I hear of it.
3. The prince's twenty counts only terms that stand. A term ended by its years, by the mortal's death or by my release no longer counts, as with mine. A term breached and then mended counts while it has years to run, as on my own card.
4. "Sworn to the god in its hearing" means he speaks his oath to me within earshot as defined on my card, about fifty paces and not through walls. Please write that I need not touch him.

### Not priced
Understood. The Ice Reading is the Keeper's to fit, the number of workers included. Everything else stands as written.

### The whole
I offer no further edge and no further weakness. I accept the Keeper's reasons that a second mouth must cost me something I would notice, and I do.

## Round 2 reply

I confirm the final text of The Prince's Term as written, with no corrections.

The Description and the Drawback say what I chose under (b). I read the Drawback as follows and ask nothing to change it: one term, one day, beginning when the term is spoken whether or not I know of it, run one after another, after any days owed for a working of cold, and never below half pace. The prince's count stands apart from mine, with a ceiling of twenty. I accept that a term stands only while unfulfilled, unreleased and unended, which is a tidier count than I asked for.

All four clarifications are written as I meant them. The seal is not needed and the oath needs no touch, only my hearing.

I offered no weakness tonight and none was taken. The power pays for itself by its drawback, and I would notice it. I take the Keeper's note that anything asked now beyond wording is logged and the text stands, and I ask nothing beyond wording.
