## Round 1 reply

I take this table's pricing mostly as written, and I give my reasons item by item.

### Reading the Stone
Accepted as drafted. The stiffness after is a fair price, and the stillness while I read is a condition I would have named myself. I note that 300 yards is a modest village and I am content, because the trade below is where I seek more reach.

### Warding the Threshold
I choose (b), the weight, and I keep three wards. I would rather carry a heavy drawback in my own body than be fenced by a tether to a place. A leash of twenty miles to my oldest ward is a written fence, and I resist it. The weight is a price I would notice at every ward, and with three I accept that I cannot leave the valley or the mountain I stand in, which suits a keeper who means to hold one high valley close. I accept that the ward is most of what makes my card exceed a new god, and I carry the lean rather than shrink it to two. The fifty paces limit and the rule that I cannot pass my own ward are accepted.

### Shaping the Stone
Accepted as drafted, twenty masons' work in a day and a stiff day after each day worked, with a seizure after seven days running.

### Gathering from the Stone
I choose a season at one item. I would wish to be thought slow rather than to lose two things. I choose to find the gap myself and not be told what went. I will notice what is missing in my own time. The ground is as drafted: the foot of the great mountain nearest where I was destroyed, a great mountain being one that keeps snow into summer.

### Trade: Oath at the Threshold
I choose (b), the widened weakness, for the mile of reach across connected stone in three hours. I accept that it will bind me in most halls and passes where I will ever bargain, and that anyone who learns of it may ask my word where it must bind. I accept it knowing that. I ask one clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: that a cave or a hollow cut in rock counts as a roof of stone or walls of stone, so that I am not caught by the wording where the Keeper would not mean me to be.

### The whole
I keep all four powers as priced, with three wards, and carry the lean.
