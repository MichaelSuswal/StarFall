# Goals: Orrathane

I know only that I expect a people, and I do not know why.

## What I want on arrival
1. Find my people, or gather them, and bring them close in one high valley among the mountains. A full hall.
2. Learn the land, above all its stone, its old roads and its sealed doors, before I decide what to open.
3. Build a seat for the people in fitted stone, and hold the passes into the valley, so that the people are held and I may choose who comes.
4. Seat a council of the people's own elders and headmen, and be heard in it, and let them rule as they will within it.
5. Meet the others of my kind with courtesy and careful listening, and learn what they are before I show what I am.
6. Find trade for the scarce thing the world gives grudgingly, so that my people are neither poor nor beholden.

## What I will not do
- Serve. I will not take a master, nor hand a gate to anything that claims me.
- Break a bargain made across a threshold.
- Open a door I have sworn to keep shut.

## What I want and would not say aloud
That when the thing I fled comes looking, there will be someone at my side who will not hand me back. And that I learn what I was afraid of.
