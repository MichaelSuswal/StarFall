## Bargaining stance
Measured (score 42: Appetite 66, Trust 57, Regard 4). It argues for what it cares about most, gives way on the rest, and wants reasons for any price it thinks high. Temper toward impulsive: it decides fast and may accept to be done. Order toward freedom: it resists limits and would rather take a heavier drawback than a written fence. Presence toward revealed: it wants powers others can see work.
