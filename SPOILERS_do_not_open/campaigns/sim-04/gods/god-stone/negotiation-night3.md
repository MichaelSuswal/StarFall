## Round 1 reply

### The fitting of stone
I choose (b), both steps, and I accept the second weakness as the Keeper wrote it. I would rather my people carry a heavy weakness than a thin edge. Walls that stand and work done in half the labour are what I want them known for, and I want them seen to work, and a people that depends on the ways out for its bronze is a people that will keep those ways in good order. I accept the thin harvest as counted in full, and I accept the tool weakness: whenever the ways out are shut to their traders for more than a season, by snow, war or any other cause, their stonework falls to an ordinary crew's pace, and after a year it stops as the tools wear out.

I ask one clarification, in the write-up and not a new power. My own ward on a notch is a way I may shut, and I mean to name the traders by name to pass it. I ask that a notch warded by me, with named traders passing it, is not counted as a way shut to their traders, and that it counts as shut only if no trader is named to pass any notch for more than a season. If I shut every way to them by my own choice, let it count against them in full, and I will know that I did it.

### Not priced
I accept that the stone-feelers are fitted by the Keeper, the number of workers included, and that the moot house, the cairns, guest-right and the rest stand as written.

## Round 2 reply

I have read the final text against what I chose and confirm it as written, with no correction of wording.

- The edge: standing dry stone, and half the labour at 3 worker-days a yard of built wall and 1 for dry stone to head height, at a mortal crew's pace and with mortal tools, taking nothing from Shaping the Stone. This is what I meant.
- The first weakness, the thin harvests, is as I offered it and bought the standing walls.
- The second weakness, the bought-in bronze, is as the Keeper wrote it. My clarification is taken: a notch I have warded, with traders of theirs named to pass it, is not shut to them. I note and accept that if by my wards and my naming no trader of theirs can pass any way out for more than a season, it counts in full.
- The poor riders, poor sailors and slowness outside the range stand as ordinary fact of the people and are not counted.

Nothing is wrong with the Keeper's copy.
