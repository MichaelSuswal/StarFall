# Negotiation: the Weigher, Night 3

## Round 1 reply

The Ford's [f39] Scales: accepted as drafted. One step above a town of its size and place is the size I meant, and I do not ask for more. I agree it is custom and works by hand, and that it gives nothing to the Fair Weight and takes nothing from it. I take the limit on "a day's walk and more" as written: it is what they believe, and how far the name has truly travelled is the world's.

Open gates: accepted as drafted, one for one. A first day against them and then the wards standing as any town's would is a fair count, and I would not shrink it. It is the weakness I offered and I mean it. A gate that is never shut against a traveler is worth a risk to the people who keep it, and they will feel the risk the first season a stranger takes advantage, as I wrote. I ask no change.

Not priced: heard and agreed. The number stands at about five thousand, the tongue for salt is the Keeper's to fit including how many workers, and the wicket in the river gate is the town's to keep or refuse. I did not ask for it to be promised.

I have no counter and no clarification. The price goes one way and I take it.
