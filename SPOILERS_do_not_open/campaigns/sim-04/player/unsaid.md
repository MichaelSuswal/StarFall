# Unsaid wants: the Thrum

Campaign: sim-04. Sealed. Written by the DM; read and written by the DM only. The god's wants that it would not say aloud, in the player's words. Nothing here is acted on, in play or by standing conduct, without a confirmation from the player given within the last three played years, written beneath the want it confirms.

## 2026-11-21, creation Day 2

The player's words, verbatim, given at the Day 2 table under the player's own label "What I want and wouldn't say to the ones I tend (sealed)":

> I want a center again. I want to find out whether the queen was a someone, or whether I was always only parts, and I'm afraid of the second answer. I also want one mortal to be glad I'm near, without being afraid of me. I'd tell no one that. If a stung person's hum ever starts to feel like company, I'd want to keep that quiet. I'll tell you when any of this changes.

Confirmation: stated by the player on 2026-11-21, creation Day 2, before Year 1. The player added that it will say when any of this changes.

Note: the player posted these words in the open table talk. The DM moved them here at the close and left a pointer in `player/transcript-day2.md`. The earlier commit of that transcript still holds them in the repository history.

## 2026-11-21, creation Day 3

The player's words, verbatim, given at the Day 3 table as a private answer for the sealed record, relayed by the orchestrator so that they never entered the open table talk:

> I picked the small number on purpose. A people of fifteen hundred I can't lose sight of is a people I could care about the way I told you I wanted to, quietly and by name. A larger one would make me an administrator, and I don't want to find out whether I'm good at that. I also chose no ruler because I'm afraid that if anyone carried my intent, I'd stop having to decide what it was.

Confirmation: stated by the player on 2026-11-21, creation Day 3, before Year 1.

The player's words, verbatim, given later the same day as a second private answer for the sealed record, relayed by the orchestrator:

> I chose Orrin because he's old, solitary and observant, someone I could watch for a full year before I ever let him see me. I'm hoping he becomes the one who is glad I'm near, and I know that wanting that is exactly what the DM warned about when it said I'm not to be given a mortal. I made him, but I'm going to have to win him, and I'm afraid that I'll want to cheat. Please don't let me.

Confirmation: stated by the player on 2026-11-21, creation Day 3, before Year 1.
