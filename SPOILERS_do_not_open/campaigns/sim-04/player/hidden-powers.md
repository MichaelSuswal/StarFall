# Sealed: the player's god

Two powers, both weaker than the main ones on the card, fitted to the sealed past and to the weight in `balance.md`. Neither is on any card until it surfaces.

### The Kept Wax
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the cool one.
Description: What the god's own bees build comb over, in a tended hive, is kept exactly as it was for as long as that hive lives: a tablet, a token, a mark pressed in wax, a small thing no larger than a hand. It does not rot, cannot be altered unseen, and anyone who knows bees can see the comb has not been disturbed.
Drawback: The hive that keeps a thing makes no honey its keeper can take that year from that comb, and it cannot be moved or allowed to swarm without the keeping ending. If a keeping hive dies while it keeps, the god pays as for a part killed: one item of memory or habit, by logged roll, not told.
Limits:
  - One kept thing to a tended hive.
  - It keeps; it reveals nothing. The god learns nothing of what the thing is or says by keeping it.
  - It gives the next step and never an end: a thing kept is a thing that lasts, and what follows from its lasting is the world's.
  - Nothing living is kept, and nothing made or held by more than ordinary craft until met.
  - The god's own people's hives pay the same as any.
Trigger that would surface it: a strain or a place: the first time its bees build over something a mortal has left in a tended hive on purpose, or its first season working a hive that already holds old marked wax. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Known Keeper
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: the minor one.
Description: The god knows by scent, within near of any part, a mortal it has stung with intent, or one who has kept a tended hive through a full year: that it is that person, in a crowd or in the dark, and whether they are sick.
Drawback: While a known person is near a part, a hatful of that part's bees attends them and will not be directed, and anyone watching can see bees keeping to that one person.
Limits:
  - It knows the person, never their thoughts or what they have done.
  - It does not work in winter, at night, in rain or in cold.
  - It does nothing on another god; unruled until it happens.
Trigger that would surface it: a being met: a mortal who has kept a tended hive through a full year stands near it without fear. It is never scheduled.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

Ceiling: 80 (from `star.md`). The voices begin at 64. Never released.
