# Sealed past: the player's god

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21, under `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. The god's own outline is in its `self.md` (the player's in `campaigns/sim-04/player/backstory.md`); this is the rest. It reaches the god only through play.

## What it was

It is the wax grove at Ilmarrow: a walled grove of hives where the country's sworn records were kept pressed in wax, built into comb and kept by the bees themselves. It was never a person and never one hive. "A place where something was kept, a grove or a comb" and "patient work with no audience" are exact.

What it kept, and why. For most of its life, the wax copy of every oath sworn under the standing pledge of Tessaly: the country trusted wax that living bees kept over any written word, because no hand could alter it unseen. At the end, something else. Wanderers who sang lists of names taught the keepers that a name kept by many, long enough, is not lost when its place is. In the last season the keepers pressed into wax the names of every village and household they could gather, sealed the grove's door, and carried the rolls down the river to be taken to Orrin Holm (the rolls at the landing). The ferrymen had sworn to carry them and on the last night did not put out.

The center it lost. The grove was kept by a woman who held the office and the name of Sennamel, one after another, each taking the name when the last died. So the queen was a someone, many times over, and was also only an office: both of the answers the god fears and hopes for are true. The last Sennamel died in the spring of the last year and no one took the name. The grove went to the landing with no center. That is the queen it lost, and Sennamel is the name it lost; the grove's own name, Ilmarrow, is gone from it too.

What it lost and does not remember: both names; that its keeping failed at the water's edge, by another's failure; that what it kept at the last was names.
