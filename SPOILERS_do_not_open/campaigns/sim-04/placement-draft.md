# Placement draft: campaign sim-04

Written on creation night 2, 2026-11-21. Provisional: the placement plan is written in 3a.3, the sheets are painted from it, and the data follows the paint. Working names only; places get names and ids when traced. Nothing here is a distance or a direction any release may use.

## The region

One continent on one sheet. Its land is planned at about 1,100 miles across from west to east and about 900 from north to south, nowhere under 600 across in its overall shape, about 1,000 on average. A land of rivers: several separate river systems, each its own country, with wild country between. A cold coast along the north. A high range in the north-east. A great lake at the center, under the star, from the star's roll. Whether the sheet's edges reach the thinning country (70 days' walk from the center) is settled in 3a.3 from the scale; at this size they do not on a road and may at the far corners over rough ground.

## The ring

Eight seats, spaced evenly in rough terms round the central lake, none within sight of the lake or of another seat. Round the ring, by working name:

1. The cold coast and its closing valleys (north): god-ice.
2. The high range and the cupped valley (north-east): god-stone.
3. The forest and fen under the hills (east): god-dreams.
4. The braided mist rivers (south-east): god-secrets.
5. The long river, from high springs to marsh (south): god-rivers.
6. The flowering valley (south-west): the player's god.
7. The ford where a road meets wide water (west): god-trade.
8. The slow rivers with crossing towns (north-west): god-law.

Held and open land about half and half. Three to five godless civilizations and the non-human people are placed in 3a.3, in the gaps and at the center.

Left to the roll in 3a.3, not to the Keeper's preference: the remainder that fell in godless country lies between two adjacent seats of this ring; which pair is rolled among the eight adjacent pairs once the ring is set. Every rare candidate of the pools stays unplaced until then, and none is placed where its own god is certain to find it.

## Each god's landing country and landing spot

Each from its own second answer and the terrain list. The landing spot says what ground the god stands on and what lies in reach of its eyes by the sight rule (`engine/rules/map.md` 1.4), for the Day 3 brief and the Night 3 releases; exact views come from the traced map.

- **The player's god.** Country: a broad river valley with many edges: water meadows, hedged fields and orchards on the lower slopes, old woods in the side valleys, heather uplands above. The mildest of the eight countries. Landing spot: a low rise at a meadow's edge on the valley floor, an old hedge beside it, in early spring. In reach of its eyes: the meadows and the river's line of willows, hedges and bare orchards on the nearer slope, woods higher, the brown heather of the uplands closing the view on both sides, and smoke from more than one place down the valley. The land shows of people: laid hedges, pruned orchard trees, a rutted cart track along the foot of the slope, grazed turf. Deep history the land carries: orchard country for a very long time, on top of older fields; one of the old straight roads under its meadows.
- **god-ice.** Country: high ground that holds snow into summer, a cold coast, long valleys that close in winter. Landing spot: the head of a long valley above the tree line, in early winter, first snow down. In reach: the valley running down to a grey sea, a walled seat's smoke far down it, steadings on the valley sides, ice already on a small lake below. Deep history: a people who came by the coast after an older people of the valleys failed; straight roads under the ice.
- **god-stone.** Country: a high range with passes, and one broad valley cupped in it. Landing spot: a ledge on the valley's rim in spring, snow above and meltwater below. In reach: the whole cupped valley with terraces and a gathered settlement, two notches in the far wall, peaks beyond. Deep history: the deepest stone in the region, with sealed doors in the mountain roots and work older than any living people.
- **god-dreams.** Country: forest and fen where they meet, hills behind, a river between. Landing spot: the wood's edge above the fen in late autumn, leaves down. In reach: mist on the fen, a rise with a walled seat on it, lights of scattered steadings, the hills as a dark line. Deep history: the fen was open water once; an old hall of forgotten purpose in the wood.
- **god-secrets.** Country: braided rivers in mist, wooded banks, few fords. Landing spot: a gravel bar at a ford in autumn. In reach: two channels, the far bank's trees, the smoke of a camp that is not a town. Deep history: a moving people on ground that settled kingdoms once held; landings where the water has since moved away.
- **god-rivers.** Country: one long river from springs in high ground through braided reaches to slow brown water and marsh. Landing spot: the bank at a bend in the middle reach, in the spring flood. In reach: a walled town at the bend, wet meadows, the high ground the river comes from as a far line. Deep history: the most-worked river in the region, with weirs on weirs and a drowned town somewhere under a made lake.
- **god-trade.** Country: wide water crossed by a road, low hills. Landing spot: the road itself on a low hill above a ford, in autumn. In reach: the ford, a walled town at it, a second road along the bank, carts. Deep history: a crossing as old as the old roads; one of them runs under the living road.
- **god-law.** Country: wide slow rivers with towns at the crossings, hills at the edges. Landing spot: a river terrace above a crossing in autumn. In reach: a toll-post and market town, boundary stones along the terrace, hills behind. Deep history: a land divided and redivided, with carved stones no one can now read.

Seasons: each god wakes into its own (the sheets); the player's is early spring, set to the clock in 3b.8.
