# Placement draft: campaign sim-03, Night 2

Provisional. Working names only; ids come when the map is traced on Night 3a. Nothing here is a description of land for release: no distance, direction or line of sight leaves this file. The placement plan of 3a.3 is written from this and must honor `campaigns/sim-03/player/day3-brief.md`.

## The region

One continent on one sheet, at the scale of a map of Middle-earth: roughly 1,400 miles wide and 1,000 miles tall, sea on one long side and part of another, the rest running off into fog at the edges. The star's center lies inland, near the middle of the sheet. With a living radius of 110 days' walk the Pale lies well beyond the sheet on every side; 3a.3 confirms this against the pace table.

Terrain the sheet must hold (world draft): three old forests or one great one in three parts; limestone karst with sinkholes; marsh and reed marsh; a high mountain range with many passes; a high river basin behind narrow passes; broken hills with hidden valleys; a great river system with high springs, fords, one great crossing and slow lower water; steppe with a long dry road; a coast with a harbor; ground for the center beast. An underground layer exists (data only unless the sheet is approved).

## The eight seats, in rough terms

Spaced evenly in a rough ring around the center, each in country that suits its wish, with wide godless country between. The order round the ring is not fixed here: it follows the terrain once the painting exists.

- The player's god: old forest on broken limestone, sinkholes, marsh at the low edges, the mountains far off and seldom seen. Deep history it will carry: the oldest burying ground in the region lies under this forest, and the forest and the ruins line up because both followed the same water under the rock.
- god-beasts: old deep forest with river country at its edge and marsh between; a walled place with the wild close round it. Deep history: a hunting kingdom's game preserve, kept wild by law for centuries after the kingdom was gone.
- god-stone: high broken mountains, scree, cliff, many passes. Deep history: pass-forts of three ages, each built on the last, and one pass closed by an old fall no one living saw.
- god-trade: lower river country running to a coast and a harbor, fords and long roads. Deep history: a league of ford towns that struck a common weight and fell apart over it.
- god-dreams: river country beside a long dry road, steppe, reed marsh where the river braids. Deep history: a caravan road older than any town on it, with way-stations whose builders are not remembered.
- god-war: high river country, narrow passes, a wide basin. Deep history: a basin kingdom that held its passes for a thousand years and was ended from inside.
- god-secrets: deep forest and broken hills, valleys hidden one from the next, stone already laid. Deep history: walled seats of a people who built to be overlooked, emptied without a siege.
- god-rivers: river country from high springs to wide slow water, one good crossing. Deep history: a crossing fought over by every power the region has had, with a toll-stone older than its bridge footings.

## Things the star or the lore places by choice among the seats

- The Remainder, which the lore puts in godless country between two gods: not placed here. It goes to the placement roll in 3a.3, among the adjacent pairs of seats once the ring is set.
- The center beast and its ground: under the star's center, which is fixed by the star and not by any seat.
- The carriers' road and the thinners' range: set in 3b.4 from the map.
- Every rare candidate in the world draft stays unplaced until Night 3. No god's undared wish is put where that god is certain to find it, and no home ground is given more of the common past's likenesses than another.
