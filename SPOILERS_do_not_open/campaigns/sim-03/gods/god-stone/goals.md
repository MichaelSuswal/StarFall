# Goals: god-stone, on arrival

We know only that we expect a people. We do not know why.

## What we want, and will say aloud
1. Find the people we expect. Gather them by walking, not by speaking. Get them moving before the thaw ends and over the high passes before the weather turns.
2. Ground that holds. A place to stop that nobody can take, a roof, a wall, a year of grain in a cellar. We want it scarce, so that those who keep it keep it by strength.
3. Find the old things in the rock: a sealed door, an old road that climbs and does not come down, something enormous folded into a mountain. We want to find a door and not be told what is behind it yet.
4. Learn what moves in the high places and the deep places before it learns us.
5. Meet any other like us face to face, at a pass, and decide with our own eyes whether to feed it or throw it down. We would like their tools.

## What we want and would not say aloud
Kept here and given to no other god.
1. A people we did not lose. We cannot picture it, and we are not sure we could look at it if it were shown to us.
2. The truth about the ones before. What fell, who fell with it, and which of us was holding it.
3. That this time the mountain holds.
4. To not be looked at closely. We want to be a rumor to our people, and a hand on the slope. The one who looks closely is the one who learns we are small.
5. To learn what the other half of us was, and to find out whether it is a god that came down when we did, and if so to be the one that decides.

## What we fear and will not say
That the cold weight in the chest is not the weight of the ground.
