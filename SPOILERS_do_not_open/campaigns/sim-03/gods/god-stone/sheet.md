## Bargaining stance
Measured (score 55: Appetite 81, Trust 72, Regard 11). It argues for what it cares about most, gives way on the rest, and wants reasons for any price it thinks high. Mind toward intelligent: it argues from reasons and consistency with what was already agreed. Body toward strong: it bargains for force and reach. Temper toward impulsive: it decides fast and may accept to be done. Order toward law: it wants every limit written exactly.

## The people it leans toward
Size: the usual number, about five thousand (score 51: Company 61, Appetite 81, Regard 11)
Settlement: moving, no fixed seat
Rule: a god-king with a court and priesthood, a warlord in manner. The god holds the crown itself, with a court and officers; its word is law, carried by them. A priesthood stands in it only as the worship lean gives one. As a warlord, it rules by force, through its war band and captains.
Worship: local shrines (score 43: 101 minus Presence 36, Regard 11, Appetite 81). Household and village shrines, kept by whoever keeps them; no order of priests.

## Name

## Traits (own)
1. A breaker that leans hard to law. Reconciles Making (breaker, 98), Order (law, 8), Temper (impulsive, 99) and the virtue of courage. We knock a thing down before we have decided to, and then we want the ruin set out exactly: who stood where, what gave, what the limit was, what was said and what was owed. We break the world and then insist on its rules. We cannot abide a wall without a reason or a bargain without its edges written, and we are the first to put a hammer to both. When a thing holds under our blow we respect it, and we hold to it in turn.
2. A shepherd that is cruel by not noticing. Reconciles Regard for mortals (shepherd, 11), the flaw of cruelty from indifference, Company (gathering, 61), Trust (wary, 72) and Presence (hidden, 65). We want to keep a people, feed them, count them, and get them over the pass alive, and we are the one who walks on when one of them falls behind, because we did not see it and did not feel it as we think we should. We gather close and look at them from far off. We watch the herd, not the animal, and we stay behind our captains because the ones who look closely are the ones who learn how small we are. The kindness is real and the cruelty is real, and neither one is aimed.

## Keeper trait
You always know, without counting, exactly how many of your people are on the march, and you feel the number drop.
- It gives how many of your people are on the march and that the number has dropped. It does not give who, where or why. It is no power.

## Season it wakes into
Spring. In its own words: "the season of the thaw, when the ice cracks and the passes open."
