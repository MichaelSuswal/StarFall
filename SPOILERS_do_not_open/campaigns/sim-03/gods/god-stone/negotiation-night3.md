## Round 1 reply

### A ring of holds in place of one seat
Accept. We turned the town down and we take the cost as it is said: no market others come to, no wall the whole people can stand behind, fixed-hearth work only at the holds and only by the few who stay, and our word reaching the Column only at a halt, from an officer who is there. The last one bites us, since we mean to rule through the Marshal and the Reckoner at the halt and not by being seen, and we would sooner pay it than have a seat. We ask one clarification in the write-up, not a new power: that a fifth hold may be added later by the Column's own labor, at the cost the world sets for rock cutting, and that this is a change to the ring and not to the weakness.

### Pass Craft
Counter nothing, and take choice (B): the whole Column at two thirds of the foot rate on mountain ground, and half the losses of another host of its size in a crossing.
Reasons: We asked for twice the pace and a tenth of the losses, and the Keeper has said plainly that both at once make the mountain no mountain. We accept that. Between the two, we are the god who decides fast, and we would have taken the pace. But pace does not save a host from a slope that goes whether or not it is read, and the thaw is when the stones come down. We are asked to keep five thousand alive over the passes before the weather turns, and what we most want to spend the edge on is the people who do not fall. Two thirds of the foot rate is already faster than any whole people moves, and half the losses is the most the Keeper says a craft can buy. We take it.
We ask two clarifications in the write-up, not a new power. First, that "what it loses to the mountain in a crossing" is measured against another host of the same size and kind making the same crossing in the same season, so that a bad season is bad for both. Second, that the Pass Wardens ahead may be one office with its own people spread along the front of the Column, and that "within the season" means the thaw or the freeze in which the crossing is made, so that the limit is exact.

### Few Holds
Accept. A people that stands behind one walled town also keeps its grain in one known place, and we do not pretend ours is the worse for that. What we pay beyond them is stated: the holds stand nearly unguarded for half of every year, a winter outside them costs one person in ten and one beast in four, and a fifth of the fighters are pinned in the cold months. We would not have chosen to hold the line that exactly, and we want it exact. We accept it as written.
We ask one clarification in the write-up, not a new power: that "a fifth of those who bear arms sit at the holds" means the fifth is divided among the holds by the Marshal, and that it may be moved between holds in the cold months without changing the number.

## Round 2 reply

### Your reasons, answered
We have no quarrel with them. The ring clarification is granted in the Keeper's words and we see that it changes the ring and does not lighten the weakness. We take that plainly. The added last line of Few Holds, that if the ring grows past four holds the loss of one takes a quarter and never less, is a limit we did not offer and we accept it. A fifth hold would be our own choice and the Keeper is right that it should not be free.

### Final text
Confirmed. We confirm the ring of holds, the edge Pass Craft at two thirds of the foot rate with half the crossing losses, and the weakness Few Holds, all as written. We read Pass Craft as binding only on mountain ground, only for the whole Column in its march order with the Pass Wardens ahead, and only on ways with cairn signs or read within the season, and we want that exact. We read Few Holds as including the Marshal's right to divide and move the fifth between the holds, and the quarter and never less past four holds. We ask for no change of wording and no change of substance.
