# Power card: the Hush

Campaign: sim-03. Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-20. The text below is the round 2 final text as you confirmed it, with the wording you asked in your confirmation. You have no name of your own yet. You are filed under "the Hush".

## Form

One body, in one place, as for every god here (the campaign allows one body).

As you described yourself, in your own words:

A great beast, and the size matters: heavier than a war ox and longer, low at the hip and high at the shoulder, so that the head hangs forward and level with the chest. The hide is a thick, close, grey brown pelt, the colour of ash left in a hearth for a night. Along the spine and over the shoulders, set in the pelt like a row of leaves, are small ears, pale inside and dark rimmed, dozens of them, each no bigger than a thumb joint. Are they not the first thing anyone sees, once they have stopped looking at the teeth?

The head is long and heavy browed, the jaw deep. The eyes are amber with a flat, horizontal pupil, and they hold the dark better than the light. The mouth is a beast's mouth and the voice that comes out of it is low and level, not loud, carrying a long way in still air and not at all in wind. Hearing is the keenest sense by far, finer than any mortal's, able to pick one voice out of a hall. Smell is good. Sight is poor in strong light, good in dark and dusk. Touch through the pelt is dull; through the nose and the ears it is acute. The other senses she has not described are left to be settled later.

She moves on four legs, with a long padded stride that is quieter than a creature of her weight should be. She can walk or lope, not run for long and not fast; her pace is the pace of a heavy animal that never tires. She cannot climb a ladder or pass through a door narrower than her shoulders. She can stand on her hind legs for a short while to look over a wall, no more.

### What breaks you
Does a beast this thick not take a great deal of breaking? Flint, bronze and stone open the hide slowly and seldom reach the meat, and what they open closes slowly. Fire is the thing that breaks her: it burns the pelt and the flesh under it, and burns heal badly and leave her bare. She can be held: by weight, by a pit she cannot climb, by chains of a thickness a village could forge, by stone laid over her, by a sealed room with a door she cannot open. She can be shut out by a door she cannot break. She can be cut apart or burned to nothing, though that is not easy, and she is then in the state below.

### When your body is broken
See The Slow Return below. It carries a cost and a trigger, so it is written as a power, and the power is the rule.

### Things carried (as you gave them; all ordinary unless a power below says otherwise)
Nothing. What would a beast carry? There is the hide and what grows on it, and that is all. She asks that she carry no object at all, and so there is no object to price.

### Settled at the table

- God-sense, as set for this campaign: you know another god only face to face, close, as a weight or an unease that grows surer with time and can be wrong. It tells nothing of the other's name, nature or powers. You know that others like you came down when you did.
- Pace: the pace of your form, as you described it; the pace table's rates apply (`engine/rules/time-and-pace.md`).
- Senses you did not describe are unsettled and are settled in play, never inferred.
- The "Intent, your first wording" lines are kept for the record only. Where one differs from a Description or a Drawback, the Description and the Drawback stand.
- When the body is broken: see the return power below. It carries a cost and a trigger, so it is written as a power.

### Definitions

- An ear. An ear is a part of your one body, as your hair is. It has no mind, no sight and no will, it is in no way you, and you are in one place. It breaks no rule of bodies.

## Powers

### The Small Ears
Status: main
Description: You shed ears from your pelt. Each is a thumb-sized part of your body. You set one by hand or mouth at a place, or on a mortal, a beast or a thing, and it clings and hides within a pace of where you set it and then stays. It hears speech and loud sound within about a dozen paces, as one hears in a quiet room, and nothing else. It keeps the words and voices of the last season, the oldest falling away first. To read it you take it back: touched to your own hide it rejoins you, and you then have all it kept, in order, in the voices as spoken. Up to six are out at once.
Drawback: Each ear out is a part of you that you cannot feel or account for: you do not know whether it is lost, found, crushed or eaten. Where it grew stays a bare thin patch the size of the ear, a thumb's breadth, and a wound in a bare patch goes deeper and closes slower, until the ear is back or a season after it is lost. And what an ear brings back may not be what it heard: when an ear rejoins you the Keeper rolls, once for each ear at each rejoining, and one time in six exactly one thing in what it kept is wrong, a word, a name or a voice, never more than one, and you are not told that it is or which.
Limits settled at creation:
- You cannot hear through an ear while it listens, call it, steer it, speak through it, or feel where it is. It does not think, choose or follow orders. Only you can set one and only your hide can read one; another may carry an ear back to you. An ear found and killed is lost. An ear on one who travels stays on them. A tongue you do not know comes back as sound. Being watched: an ear can be seen by anyone who looks closely where it sits. Water: an ear under water hears nothing. It rules no mind. An ear in a place or on a thing held by more than ordinary craft, or on another god: unruled until it happens. Your own people get no exemption. Information: it brings back what was said, and what a speaker says is so is only what that speaker believes. Use across a year: six out, each to be set by your own body and read by your own hide.
- Intent, your first wording: "She can shed ears from her pelt. Each is a separate small part of her body, thumb sized, grey on the outside and pale within, able to crawl at a mouse's pace, to cling, and to hide in thatch, cracks, wall tops, cloth and beast hair. She places one by hand or mouth, at a place or on a mortal, a beast or a thing. An ear hears speech and loud sound within about a dozen paces, as one would hear in a quiet room, and nothing else: no sight, no smell, no touch, no taste. It keeps what it hears, the words and the voices as said, for the last few days, and the oldest falls away first. To read it she must take it back: she touches it to her own hide and it rejoins her at once, and she then has all it kept, in order, in the voices as spoken, like a remembered day. She cannot hear through an ear as it listens, cannot call it from a distance, cannot speak through it, cannot feel where it is. It does not think, choose, or follow orders; it stays where it is set until it is taken back. She asks to have up to twelve out at once. She expects that to be trimmed and will argue the trim."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: The season's keeping in place of three days was bought with the weakness The Slow Wound, under Weaknesses taken.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Asked Question
Status: main
Description: Face to face, within arm's length, with no one else near enough to hear, you ask a mortal one question, and that mortal cannot say a false thing in answer. They answer as they believe the truth to be, or they keep silent for as long as they can bear to. One question each use; a real question, not a command, in a tongue they understand. Afterward the mortal knows that they answered you and that something of theirs is gone.
Drawback: Sealed. What you are told stays in you: you cannot tell it to anyone, ever, by saying it, writing it, signing it, or sending it by another's mouth. You may act on it. You may use the power once in a month, and never twice on the same mortal. And the answer costs the mortal: one memory, a name, or the knowledge of a person, taken from near the thing they said, not chosen by you.
Limits settled at creation:
- A mortal who does not know says so, truly. A mortal who is mistaken answers the mistake. Silence is always open to them, and nothing in the power makes silence harder than their own fear of you does. It reaches what one mortal believes and no further: no cause, no proof. What the mortal lost is gone from them for good, and those who know them may notice. It bends a tongue and no more: it changes no want, loyalty or belief. A question about another god, put to a mortal, is answered as any other; the power used on a god, or on one warded by more than ordinary craft: unruled until it happens. Your own people get no exemption. Light and dark make no difference.
- Your own ears, being parts of you, are not someone near.
- Intent, your first wording: "Face to face, within arm's length, in a place where the two of them are alone or where no one else is near enough to hear, she can ask a mortal one question, and that mortal answers it truthfully as they believe the truth to be. They cannot say a false thing in answer. They may stay silent, if they choose, for as long as they can bear to. She may ask only one question per use, and the question must be a real question and not a command. The mortal does not know that anything was done to them, only that the answer left their mouth."
- "Once in a month" is a run of thirty days counted from the last use, not a turn of any calendar.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Slow Return
Status: main
Description: When your body is broken past holding (burned to ash, crushed, or scattered), you are nothing for a time and then whole again. After a full season you return in the dark of a night, on bare ground under open sky, at the place nearest to where the largest part of you lay, with no one near.
Drawback: Each time you come back, a memory or a habit does not come back with you, and you are not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- The return follows the table's rule above. Being sealed away is being held, not being broken, and brings no return. Ears out when you are broken stay as they are and keep what they have. A return in a place held by more than ordinary craft or by another god: unruled until it happens.
- Crushed or burned past holding is broken even when stone lies over what is left; a fall that leaves you whole beneath it is a holding. Ground of your own kind is bare ground under open sky: stone, scree or leaf mould, not a field, a road or a floor.
- Intent, your first wording: "When her body is broken past holding, whether burned to ash, crushed, scattered, or sealed away from any place she could leave, she is nothing for a time and then she is whole again. If at least one ear of hers is out and whole when she is broken, she returns at the place it lies, after one season. If none is out, she returns at the place where she was broken, after two seasons, in the dark of the night, on bare ground, and no one is near. The return takes the whole of a season and cannot be hurried."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

**The Slow Wound.** Any wound that breaks your hide to the meat (from a weapon, fire, teeth, or a god) does not close until a full season's run of days has passed in which you have not spoken one word aloud in the hearing of any mortal. While open it is raw and aches, and a second blow to it opens it wider. Bruises, cuts that do not reach the meat, and the bare patch of a shed ear do not count. The run restarts if you break the silence. A second wound before the first closes adds no season, and opens the first wider. Speaking to a god does not break it. Asking The Asked Question breaks it. How it can be used against you: one who learns it can keep you wounded by making you speak, and a wounded tyrant who cannot speak to her own court is seen to be so.
- What it bought: An ear keeps what it hears for a season, in place of three days.

Kept as color: A dislike of closed rooms you cannot enter, felt as a pressure at the back of the neck. Color, at no price, buying nothing.
