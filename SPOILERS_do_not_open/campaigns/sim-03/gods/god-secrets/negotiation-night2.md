# Negotiation: god-secrets, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

The two table rules (the loss of an item of memory by lot, and the return from a broken body) are not offers, so I do not bargain them. I take them as written: they match what I asked, that what is taken stay unspecified. Where I choose below, I choose with them in mind. What is my record, after all, but eight goals and one line about others? Does a thing that thin not have to be spent with care?

### The clarification (an ear is part of my one body)
Accept. The text is exact and the Keeper's reason is sound: an ear that could be steered or heard through would be a second body, and I never asked for one. I ask only that "no one else near enough to hear" in The Asked Question be written so that my own ears, being parts of me, do not count as someone near. That is wording, in the write-up, not a new power.

### The Small Ears
Accept, with choice (A): six out, each keeping the last three days, bought up by The Slow Wound to a season.
Why (A) and not (B): the budget is ears times days, and I will spend it on duration. Six ears that keep a season is five hundred and more ear days. Twelve that keep a month is about a third less. I am an enduring thing, and I bargain for what survives. Fewer ears is also fewer parts of me that I cannot account for.
What I accept in the draft: the bare thin patch and the false word both. I offered them together. The within a pace of where I set it, and then stays, I take as narrower than what I wrote and I accept it: an ear that does not wander is an ear that is not lost.
The rate: the Keeper set one in six because I gave none. Will I not give one? One in six at each rejoin, I take. I ask that it be written exactly: one roll per ear per rejoin, and where the roll falls, exactly one item is wrong, a word, a name or a voice, and never more than one. Is that not what the draft says? Then there is no harm in writing it so. I also ask that the bare patch be written as the size of the ear, a thumb, so that "deeper and slower" is not read as a whole flank.

### The Asked Question
Counter. If the Keeper refuses, I take (A) as written, not (B).
Why not (B): one item leaves my record each answer, chosen by lot, once a month. In a year that is twelve items from a record of about nine. I would be asking the world to take what I am. I will not buy a monthly question with the whole of what I know I want.
Why (A), and why I contest it: I offered two prices as one or the other: the seal, or the long wait and no second asking. The Keeper has taken the seal and the long wait and the no repeat, which is all of what I offered in place of each other, and has put them on one power. Is that consistent with what I wrote? I will give the seal, which is the heavy one: what I am told I can never tell, write or sign, and it stays in me. A keeper of secrets who cannot trade a secret has paid dearly. I will also give that I never ask the same mortal twice. What I contest is the season between uses. Counter: sealed, never twice on the same mortal, and once in a month, not once in a season. Four questions a year, when I am the only soul in a walled seat who can ask, is not a power, it is a rumour. Twelve is a power that is priced by a seal I will notice every day.
The mortal's cost: the Keeper is right that I do not mind it, and it is not my price. I accept it as a cost to the mortal.
Wording: I ask that "tell it" be written to include saying it, signing it, or sending it by another's mouth, and that I may act on what I am told so long as I do not tell it. The Keeper refused my own wording on acting, and I accept the refusal. I ask only that acting be named as open, so that the line is exact.

### The Slow Return
Accept, with choice (A): where I fell, a full season, one item lost.
Why (A): a god who cannot die can wait. Is a month worth two items from what I have? It is not. Choice (C) is worse: it spends the very ear that I would be most glad to still have, loses all the others, and costs two. I do not want to be fast. I want to survive with what I have.
The sealed away: refused, and I accept the refusal. The Keeper's reason, that every prison would become a door, is a reason I respect, and I do not want the world made so. I wrote it as sealed away from any place I could leave, thinking of a vault; I take the vault as held, not broken.
Wording I ask for, in the write-up and not a new power: that crushed or burned past holding is broken even when stone lies over it, so that a hall that falls and breaks me is a breaking, while a hall that falls and leaves me whole is a holding. And that the term "ground of your own kind" be written exactly on my card: bare ground under open sky, stone, scree or leaf mould, not a field, a road or a floor. If the nearest such ground is more than a day off I accept the walk added at my own pace, as the Keeper's rule says.

### The Slow Wound
Accept, as drafted. It buys a season of keeping on six ears under (A).
Why: the Keeper has seen what I do not already pay and what I do. My hide closes slowly already, and I ask nothing for that. The silence is the real price, and I know what it is: a run of days in which I say nothing aloud to any mortal. It also ends a use of The Asked Question while the wound is open, since I must speak to ask. I accept that, and I do not ask it be written away. A wounded tyrant who cannot speak to her own court is seen to be so. Is that not the point of a weakness?
Closed rooms stay as colour, buying nothing, as the Keeper said.
