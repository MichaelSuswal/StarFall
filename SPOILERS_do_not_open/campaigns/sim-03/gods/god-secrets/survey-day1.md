## Answer 1
A house with many doors, and who has counted them? Dusk, the hour when everything is half said? A world where what is kept is worth more than what is shown, and nothing is quite as loud as the thing no one will name?

## Answer 2
Where the land folds in on itself, would it not? Deep forest, broken hills, valleys that hide one from the next? Is there not a place where the walls are already built by stone and the roads do not tell you where they go?

## Answer 3
Should the seasons not be long, and honest, and hard? A winter that lasts long enough for people to learn what they hoard, a spring that is slow to promise anything, a summer short enough to be missed, an autumn of harvest and reckoning? Do I not wake best into the dark of the year, when everyone is indoors and every household is keeping something from the next?

I wake into winter.

## Answer 4
Some of each, surely? Wild country, so that there is somewhere for what is buried to stay buried? Old kingdoms already rooted, so that there are doors worth listening at? What use is a world with no one to keep a secret from?

## Answer 5
Would it not be old, with ruins under ruins? Who wrote the first lie down, and where is it now? Should a people not have forgotten more than they remember?

## Answer 6
Is it not enough that it be a rumor, doubted by most and kept by a few? A wise-woman in a valley here and there, and no more? Would I want a world where every cellar holds a witch?

## Answer 7
Should it not feel like being overheard? A thing half-felt, like a hand on the back of the neck? And what should it cost them? Is it not fair that it take a memory, or a name, or a person they would rather not have lost?

## Answer 8
Must a god stand out at all? Is it not better that a god be a rumor too, a shape in the treeline, a story the elders contradict? Would I not rather be far more than a great sorcerer, but be seen by very few?

## Answer 9
Strange enough, surely? Familiar animals by day, and by night what? Should there not be creatures no one has a name for, and who is to say they are not older than the people who fear them?

## Answer 10
Beyond each other? Is it not the thing that does not announce itself? The sickness that arrives quietly, the neighbor who is not what he was, the famine kept hidden until it cannot be? What is a better story than the one where the danger was always inside the house?

## Answer 11
Truth, would it not be? Who has it, who will sell it, who has hidden it so well that even they have lost it? Is it not the thing mortals want most and have least, and will they not trade, and fight, and go without for it?

## Answer 12
Sealed doors, would you not say? Old roads that end at nothing, vaults with no keys, bones of something enormous and the question of what it had swallowed? Who sealed them, and was it to keep something in, or to keep something out?

## Answer 13
Should there be? Not many, and not friendly by default. Peoples who keep their own counsel, who do not explain themselves, and who are watching us with patience? Is it not more interesting if we are not the only ones who know how to wait?

## Answer 14
Few, would it not be? One traveler is a mystery and many are an invasion. Someone who came from far away, carrying a tool or a word no one here can read? And who has asked them what they left behind, and why?

## Answer 15
Is a pull at any distance not too easy? Would I not prefer to know only face to face, and even then only after a question has been asked and answered, and answered falsely, perhaps? Why should a god be so readily found?

## Answer 16
Must it be one body? Could it not be one body and a great many small ears? What should it cost? Is it not fair that every part I send out is a part I cannot account for, and that some may not come back the same?

## Answer 17
One who keeps a secret to the end? A mortal who is offered everything for a single sentence and does not say it? Would that not be the story worth following, and would I not want to know what they are protecting?

## Answer 18
A companion, perhaps? Or a truth, one that I would rather not have found? Is there not something here that has been waiting, and has been quiet for so long that quiet has become its name? Could it be that it is not a place or a power at all, but a question I have already answered wrongly?

## Answer 19
That I be forgiven for something, and that no one ever say what? Is that too much to ask of a world that only listens?
