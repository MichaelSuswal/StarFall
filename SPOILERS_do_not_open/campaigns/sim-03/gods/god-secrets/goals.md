# Goals: the Hush, on arrival

She knows only that she expects a people, and does not know why.

## Stated goals
1. Find her people and the walled seat where they live, among broken hills or deep forest, in the dark of the year. Take the crown. Does a people not need someone to keep them?
2. Know who else came down, before they know her. What are they, and what can they do? She knows only that others came when she did.
3. Hear what the people say when they think no one is listening. Learn what each household is keeping from the next.
4. Find the one among them who will not give up a secret for any price, and learn what they are protecting.
5. Find the sealed doors, the old roads that end at nothing, the vaults without keys. Learn who sealed them and whether it was to keep something in or something out.
6. Learn what has been waiting here, quiet so long that quiet is its name. Is it a companion, a truth, or a question she has already answered wrongly?
7. Keep herself a rumour. Be seen by very few, and be believed by fewer, but be obeyed by all.

## What she wants and would not say aloud
That she be forgiven for something, and that no one ever say what. She does not know what it is. She does not ask herself.
