## Answer 1
A long table set for guests, and the candles still burning after the last chair has emptied. Not sad. Only lit, and waiting to be proven worth the wax. Somewhere a bell nobody is ringing, and everyone in the valley knows the pitch of it.

## Answer 2
Ask the land what it would defend and it will tell you what it loves. I would take a high river country, with passes narrow enough to count the people who use them and a basin wide enough to hold a feast. Close walls, an open sky. A place that has to be held together by hand.

## Answer 3
Four seasons, and none of them hurried. A winter long enough to teach people the worth of one another, a spring that forgives it, a summer that spends too much, and an autumn that keeps accounts. Let the year turn the way a debt turns, honestly and on time. When harvest is in and the roads are dry enough to march and dry enough to dance, that is the hour I would like to be found in.
I wake into autumn.

## Answer 4
Some of each, and I would not say which I hope for more. Wild country is a courtesy, no one has yet told it what it is. Old kingdoms are a different courtesy, they have already learned how to be told. I would like to meet one neighbor who has forgotten more than I know, and one who has never needed to remember.

## Answer 5
Ruins under ruins. A story that has been told so often it has worn smooth in the middle, so that you can hold it and not feel any edge. I would like the oldest layer to have been loved by someone, and then left.

## Answer 6
Closer to the wise-woman than the rumor, though not in every valley. In every third valley, and the other two valleys quarrel about whether the third is lying. Never gone, never common. Enough that a mother can say "my grandmother knew a woman who could" and be half believed.

## Answer 7
It should feel like borrowing. A warmth in the hands, a taste of iron, the sense of a coat taken from a peg that belongs to someone else. And it should cost what borrowing costs: a thing returned with interest, a memory, a year, a sleep. Nothing free, nothing cruel. A fair price, written plainly, so that no one can say afterward they were not told.

## Answer 8
Enough to be seen across a field, not enough to be safe in a crowd. I would like a god to stand out the way a well-made door stands out in a wall. People should know where to knock. They should also be able to wonder, privately, what is behind it.

## Answer 9
Familiar animals first, so the unfamiliar ones have something to stand against. A few creatures with no names, kept to the edges of maps. Let the old stories be partly true and let no one be sure which part.

## Answer 10
The slow things. A grey winter in the grain. A rumor that outruns the messenger. The friend who counts you carefully. The kind of danger that gives a person time to make a speech about it, and then arrives anyway. A good story is a door that someone has decided to open.

## Answer 11
Remembering. Names, the faces of the dead, the way a particular song went. Mortals will trade salt and iron for it, and fight over it, and go without it more often than they admit. Let the scarce thing be the one most gently lost.

## Answer 12
A road that goes somewhere no road should. Under it, an altar with the stone worn into a shallow bowl by hands, and no name left on it at all. I would like to be the one who finds it, and I would like to be unsure for a good while whether I am glad.

## Answer 13
One or two peoples, I think, and not as mirrors. Let them be neighbors who keep their own calendar and are polite about ours. Let them be better at something that matters and worse at something that matters. A people who laugh at a different place in the joke.

## Answer 14
A few. Travelers who arrive with a tool in a cloth and a story about where they were made. Few enough that each one is an event, and strange enough that no one can quite copy what they carry. Let their knowledge come with a small, specific unfairness attached.

## Answer 15
Face to face only. A pull from a distance is a rope, and I have been on the wrong end of ropes. A knowing in the same room, a flicker, a moment where two doors recognize that they are the same kind of door. It gives a god time to decide what to say before it is known.

## Answer 16
One body. A second body is a second chance to be wrong in two places. If it must be possible, let it cost a share of what makes the first body feel like oneself, so that each copy is a little less sure of the promises the other made.

## Answer 17
One who keeps their word long after it has stopped being useful. Not a conqueror, not a saint. A person with a good memory and a plain table, who can be trusted to sit at it. I would like the land to grow someone that a god might envy, quietly.

## Answer 18
A companion that does not worship. Something with its own weather, that tells the truth about the room it is standing in and has no wish to be anything else to me. I cannot imagine it well yet. That is the sign it might be real.

## Answer 19
That being forgotten should not be the last thing that happens to anything. That there is some hand, anywhere, that keeps a list, and that the list is kind.
