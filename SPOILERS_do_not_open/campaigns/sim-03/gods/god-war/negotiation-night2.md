## Round 1 reply

### The two table prices (loss of memory or habit, return from a broken body)
Accept both. I asked that what is taken stay unspecified, and a lot among the items on my own record keeps it so: I will find the gap by noticing it. I take the table's rule on returns as written, including the walk added when the nearest laid seat is more than a day off.

### Your own strength (under Form)
Accept. Three strong men's strength and wind is what I meant by strong, and it is the stock Loaned Strength lends from, so the count belongs on the card. A cut closing in a day, and burns, bones and deep wounds mending at a mortal's pace, is the plain way to write it. One clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: that "a cut" means a wound a blade or flint can make in the skin and flesh, so that it is not later read as covering anything deeper.

### Loaned Strength
Accept, no choice to make. I asked for three borrowers knowing the price would bite, and it bites in the right place: at three I am a mortal man in plain sight, and that is a fair sum to pay for lending a people a day of being more than they are. I accept the added line that my own cuts close no faster than a mortal's while a loan stands. I accept that my own people get no exemption, and that the use is as often as I choose to be weak.

### The Counted Ground
Accept, no choice to make. I wanted every limit written exactly, and "earth only", "scored and walked in plain sight" and the arrow loosed from outside all do that. It stays a hand and not a will, and anyone can end it with a foot. I would not want it to be more than that.

### The Bell Nobody Rings
Accept as written, no choice to make. I take the day of deafness as a fair price for a sound no one has to be asked to hear.

### The Set Place
Choice (A): a full season, one item lost each time.

Reasons: I am patient and would sooner wait a season than lose two items to be back in a month. My record is thin tonight, one entry in the record of gods and a short list of goals, and two items gone each time would hollow it fast. The table sells nothing faster than a month, and a month costs double, so the season is the better bargain for a god that has nowhere to be before the people are found.

I accept the refusal of the next dusk, and the table's rule in place of my thirty mile bound, and clothes returned with no gear. One clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: that a seat laid for a guest and left empty is "ground of my own kind" for the table's rule, so that the card and the rule name the same place.

### The Refused Door
Accept, as offered, and it buys what I asked: the Bell reaches a day's walk, about twenty miles, in place of a morning's. I know who may use it, those I have fed, and I would give the bread anyway.
