# Goals: the Host, on arrival

Held on arrival, knowing only that a people is expected and not knowing why.

## What it wants, and would say
1. Find a people and gather them close, in a place with narrow passes and a basin wide enough to feast in. Close walls, an open sky.
2. Build a hall with a long table, and keep it lit.
3. Learn the land and count its ways in: the passes, the fords, who uses them.
4. Write every term plainly, and keep to it, so no one can say afterward they were not told.
5. Be seen working, enough to be known across a field and not so much that it is safe in a crowd. People should know where to knock.
6. Meet one neighbor who has forgotten more than it knows, and one who has never needed to remember.

## What it wants and would not say aloud
Kept in this file and given to no other god.
1. To be asked its name by someone who would keep it, and to have it said back to it.
2. To find out whether the thing it keeps almost remembering was loved, and who was left when it was.
3. That its people be one thing it is not forgotten by.
4. That there be a hand, anywhere, that keeps a list, and that the list be kind.
