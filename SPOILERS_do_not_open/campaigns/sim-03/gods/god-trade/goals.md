# Goals: the Factor

I know only that I expect a people. I do not know why.

## What I would say aloud
A people to lead along roads that can be walked and walked again, with something worth selling at every stop.
Trade: customers near enough to deal with, and wild country near enough to reach, so that there is always a next buyer.
Roads, fords and a harbour, and a fair in autumn where everything is for sale and every tally is agreed by both hands.
Knowledge: to learn what the old ruins and sealed warehouses owe, and to whom, and to find out why I expected a people at all.
Security: a people hard to cheat, and a word of mine that is better than coin.

## What I would not say aloud
To be worshipped. I want it more than the roads. I want a great many people to want me, and I will give freely to get it and call it generosity.
That a broken oath can be mended, and that I may be the one who pays for it. I do not know what I swore. I know that a count comes out one short, and that I would give a great deal to add the last piece.
A companion, or a place, that wants nothing I can price. I cannot imagine what it would be worth, and I want to find out.
