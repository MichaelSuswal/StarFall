# Goals: god-beasts

## On arrival
What do I want, knowing only that I expect a people? A few things, and I will say them as I have them.

1. A people, gathered close. A small one, behind a wall, at the heart of wild country. Patient folk. Is that not what I came down for, though I could not tell you who told me?
2. To know the wood. What lives in it, where the old paths run, what has not been named. A hunter who does not know his ground is only a visitor.
3. Safety against being seen. Would it not be better to rule from behind the door, to let the court speak, and to walk among the people unnoticed?
4. Trade and a watch on the far places. A few old kingdoms, far off, so there is someone to trade with and someone to watch.
5. The loyalty of the animals that work beside the people, struck as a hard bargain and kept.
6. To find the others who came down when I did, only to learn who they are, and not to be found first. Is that wise, or only wary?

## What I want and would not say aloud
That I be remembered. That what was once honoured and then lost is not lost, only waiting, and that something will keep a place for it. I do not know what I am asking. I only know that when I think of an unlit hearth, something turns over, and that I would give a great deal to find something that remembers. Is that foolish in a god? I will not say it to another. I will not say it to a mortal. I keep it here.
