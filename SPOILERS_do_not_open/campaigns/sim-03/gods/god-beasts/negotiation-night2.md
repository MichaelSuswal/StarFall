# Negotiation: god-beasts, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

### The Old Track
Accept, with choice (A). Is it not my own flaw written down, and would I not rather be held by it than fenced by a rule? Choice (B) lets me set a track down, but it shuts that one creature to me for good, and is a closed door not a fence of its own? I would rather pay in memory and in obsession together than lose a quarry forever. Hunters finish what they start. I accept the season's age, the rain and water limits, the bowshot rule for water, and that my own people get no exemption. I accept that a memory goes for each track followed to its end, taken by lot from my record as the table sets it.

### The Held Breath
Accept, with choice (B), the beast stays on you. Would (A) not unmake the way I rule? My word is law because my court carries it. If every mortal who met me on a day I held the Breath, my court among them, kept half of what I said and lost my face, would the law not come loose in their hands? (B) costs me my speech and my grip, but only on the days I choose to hold the Breath, and I may choose to hold it on days I am not ruling. A hunter without hands still has teeth. I accept that the forgetting is a condition of the power at no price, and that the Breath fails in bright noon, against those already watching, and against those who know me and look for me. I accept that it holds for no less than a day once held, and that I cannot speak words or grip, tie or cut for that time.

### The Hard Bargain
Accept, with choice (B), I take on one of its wants. Would (A) not forbid me to hunt a whole kind for as long as the bargain stands? That is a fence, and it cuts at the domain I am made of. A want that pulls is a weight, and patience can carry a weight. I ask one clarification, in the write-up and not as a new power: that the want I carry ends the moment the bargain ends, by completion, by the beast breaking its side, or by the season running out, and that the want is the one the beast most shows at the time of the bargain. I accept the limits as written: beasts only, nothing that is a person, one bargain at a time, only by me and with the beast in front of me, and not through a helper or for my people's herds.

### Scattering
Accept, with choice (A), a full season and one item lost each time. Is a month worth two items? I have so little of myself to spare, and I am patient. I accept the table's rule on the return: the nearest old wild place to the largest part of me, the walk added when it is more than a day off, no return while I am shut in until I am let out, a season added each time most of the small beasts are eaten, and what I carried staying where it fell. I accept that a wound is not a breaking.

### The weakness you offer, and what it buys
Accept as offered. I understand that the part of the slow wound my form already pays, the burn that is slow to leave, buys nothing, and that the weapon wounds, the season of stillness and the handle it gives a mortal buy the year for The Old Track. Bronze and wood are added to flint and bone, and I accept them. I accept that anyone who learns it can keep a wound open by keeping me from the wood or by looking me in the eye. I accept that the unlit hearth stays as colour at no price.

I have no counter for any item. Is that not a fair table? I have taken the heavier choice where there was a choice, because I would rather pay than be fenced.

## Round 2 reply

### Confirmation
I confirm the final text, the four powers and the slow wound, with Description and Drawback as written. I ask no change of substance. I ask three changes of wording only, in the write-up and not as new powers.

### Wording one: the Form section on being broken
Is the old text of "When your body is broken" not in conflict with the card? It says the small beasts drift back "over the days that follow" and that it takes "a season, about." Scattering as priced says a full season, plus the walk when the wild place is more than a day off, and it says what is lost is one item from my record. I ask that the Form paragraph yield to the power, so that no reader takes "days" for the pace, and "a name, a habit, a face" for what is lost. The power is the rule and the Form paragraph is only my telling.

### Wording two: the Keeper trait against The Held Breath
Is it not so that "every hound and herd beast knows you at once and comes toward you, wherever you are trying to pass unseen" cuts against the Description of The Held Breath, which says no beast who is not already looking at me will notice me? I did not write the trait and I do not refuse it. I ask only that the card say how the two sit together, so that neither is left to be read against the other. I ask that the card say the trait is a knowing and a pull, by scent and by whatever hounds and herd beasts feel, and that it is not sight, so that the Breath still hides the sight of me from a beast while a hound or a herd beast may still come to me as it comes to its own. If that is not the Keeper's meaning, I ask the Keeper to say what is.

### Wording three: the trait and the hunt
Does the trait reach beasts that are not hounds or herd beasts, the wild ones I would hunt? I ask that the card say it does not, so that it stays what it says, hounds and herd beasts, and nothing more. The beasts of the wood are mine to read by The Old Track and to treat with by The Hard Bargain, and I would not have the trait read to spoil either.

I accept the rest as written. The unlit hearth stays as colour. I keep my private wish in my goals file, and I give it to no one.
