# Goals: the Night Child

You know only that you expect a people, and you do not know why.

## What I want on arrival, in the open
1. Find or gather a people who move, and put them on a good road. A good road beats a good wall.
2. Rule them by terms: bargain, debt, toll and trade, written in the open and counted by both sides.
3. Learn who the others are who came down when I did, one at a time, face to face, before I decide what each is worth. I will not be found by those I have not met.
4. Find the old kingdoms that lie a season's walk away and make them customers. A kingdom is a customer, and a world with no customers is only scenery.
5. Be first at the door when a stranger arrives with a tool or a tale no one here has. Strange knowledge is the best stock there is.
6. Make the nights of my people the safest nights anyone has known: whole, unbroken sleep, and a watch kept over it.
7. Keep a few loyal mortals close and handle them with care.
8. Learn what the night is for.
9. Secure myself against the dark that is not mine, and against any bargain signed without reading the terms.

## What I want and would not say aloud
Someone to stay. A companion, a thing that stays. I did not expect to want it and I would take it back if it cost too much. And that nothing in this world is lost for good that can be found again: every debt payable, every loss returnable in some form. I know the world will not grant it. I wish it anyway.
