# Negotiation: god-dreams, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

### How the table prices two things for every god
Accept, with one question and one thing I will say plainly. The lot falls on my record of other gods and my goals, and my goals hold the one thing I would not say aloud. I take that as the price of a price I asked to leave unspecified, and I will not ask for the lot to skip it, because a loss I can steer is not a loss. What I ask in the write-up, not a new power: that "one item" be one line of the file as written, and not a whole file or a whole section, so that I can count what I stand to lose. I note that my record holds one entry tonight, so the lot is lopsided against my goals. That is the table's to say, not mine to argue.

### Definition: your mark
Accept. Only my own hand makes it, and I knew whose hand it was before I asked. A toll taken by an officer should not mark a people, and I do not want a people I did not shake hands with. I would have written it that way myself.

### Night Tally
Accept the draft, take choice A, and counter on one term.

I take A, sleep owed for sleep. Choice B takes the count from me, and a merchant who cannot hold the terms of a bargain in his head is a man who signs without reading, which is the thing I am afraid of. Sleep I can pay. I need none, so the debt is a real cost and I will notice it, and it is a fair one: I borrow a night in a stranger's head and I lend my own.

The counter: three days to pay is too short for a god on a road. A debt of sleep paid in a hurry in a ditch beside a camp is a debt paid where anything can find me, and I sleep so that calling will not wake me. I ask for the sleep to be owed within five days, and I offer in return that the owed sleep may not be postponed once the fifth day arrives, and that I take it where I stand if I have not chosen a place. If that is refused I accept three days and will arrange a guard.

On the one wording you flagged: I accept "to you in person." A dream bargain closed by a priest is a reach across a whole people, and I want my people's bargains struck to my face. I do not argue it. I ask only, as clarification, that "in person" is met when the mortal says the yes aloud in my presence, awake, with no limit on who else is standing by to witness it.

Accepted as trimmed: one sleeper, a day's walk, once a night. The conditions on the body, the thinness while I am in the dream, I take as written.

### Heavy Sleep
Accept. A small power at a hard price, and the price is mine. I would rather the card I read carried the number for a second use in one night. I asked that what is taken stay unspecified, which is a gap I would like to find by noticing, and I did not ask for the count to be hidden. A price I cannot see is a leash, and I do not take leashes. Show me the number.

### Night Eyes
Accept as written. An even trade of day for night, and the night is my trade.

### Return by Dusk
Counter, choose A, and ask one clarification.

I asked for dusk of the next night and I was refused. I am not surprised, and I do not pretend it was an ask I expected to win. I take A: a full season, one item lost each time. B takes two items each time for a month, and I would rather lose less and wait than lose more and be quick. A god who cannot die has all the time there is, and a patient one does not buy speed at that price. I will also say that each return costs me a line of what I hold, and I would sooner come back rarely than often, so I will be careful with my body.

The clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: the table's rule says I return at "the nearest ground of your own kind", and the power says "the dark place nearest to where the largest part of you lay". These are two wordings and I ask that they be one. I ask that "the ground of my own kind" be defined as any place that is dark at night where the largest part of me lay, with no sleeper or mark required. A wording that sends me across a country to find ground I have no word for is the second body the Keeper refused me, run backward. If the Keeper holds that the walk is added for a reason I have not seen, I would like the reason said.

### Held to Its Word (the weakness)
Counter. I take the reach: a day and a half in place of a day's walk. I do not take the second sleeper at the price offered, because it would be two nights of owed sleep for one night of dreams, and the Keeper has already priced each night in full.

The counter: I ask for both steps, and I offer a second weakness to buy the second step, and I offer it on terms the Keeper can break down, since a weakness already paid by my form buys nothing and this one is not.

Hear Out. A mortal who speaks to my face, awake, and puts a bargain to me in one breath of terms, a thing for a thing with a day named, holds me to listen to the whole of it before I answer. I cannot refuse to hear it, walk away from it, sleep through it or answer in the middle. I may say no at the end. It lasts for the length of the offer, no longer than one candle mark by a tallow candle of ordinary size, and it ends when I say yes or no, when the speaker stops, or when the speaker is struck, bound or made to flee. It does not work on an offer made by a messenger, in a dream, or in writing. It does not hold my body, only my attention: I can still be moved by others, and I can still defend myself. A mortal who knows this can hold me in place for the length of a long offer, to be bargained with while a guard comes, or the road closes, or the dark falls. That is a real line against me, and I would notice it. It buys the second step: two sleepers in one night, each paying the price of its own night.

If the Keeper judges Hear Out too thin to buy a whole step, I ask for the price to be said plainly, and I will adjust the candle mark, or the number of offers a night it can hold me to, before I take less. If the Keeper refuses both steps at once, I take the reach alone and keep Hear Out as plain color, buying nothing.

The sealed dark stays as color, at no price. I agree.
