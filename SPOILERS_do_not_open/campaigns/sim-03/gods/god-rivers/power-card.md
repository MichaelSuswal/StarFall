# Power card: the Ford Warden

Campaign: sim-03. Written: creation, Night 2, 2026-11-20. The text below is the round 2 final text as you confirmed it, with the wording you asked in your confirmation. You have no name of your own yet. You are filed under "the Ford Warden".

## Form

One body, in one place, as for every god here (the campaign allows one body).

As you described yourself, in your own words:

One body with two shapes in it, as a gate has two faces, one turned toward the people and one toward the road. The shapes are not two bodies. Wounds taken in one are in the other.

The Crown shape. Tall, a head and a half above a tall man, broad through the chest and shoulders, long in the arm. Skin the grey brown of wet river stone. Dark hair cropped close, with one braid of grey that hangs like water weed. A square, hard face, heavy brow, a pale seam down the left side of the jaw like a flow mark in sand. It looks like what mortals call a war chief, and it carries itself so. It moves slowly and with weight, like a man wading a ford who means to arrive.

The Ford shape. Slight, a little under the height of an ordinary man, stooped at the shoulder. A plain, lined, pleasant face with a dry look to it. Grey white hair, big knuckled hands. The kind of face a ferryman has, which people see and do not remember. It moves quietly and sure of foot on wet stone and gravel.

Both shapes have the same eyes, the color of deep water, and it cannot change them. Both walk at a man's pace.

Senses: ordinary, and on the keen side of ordinary. It sees well at dusk and in low light. It hears the pitch of moving water quickly and knows a loud river from a quiet one by ear. It has no other sense beyond a mortal's kind. Anything it has not described here is left unsettled and is settled later, never inferred.

### What breaks you
Bronze, flint, fire and heavy blows wound it as they would a mortal, and wounds close at a mortal's pace and do not scar. It can be burned and crushed. It can be held: bound with rope, cord, chain, shackle, bolt or bar, shut in a pit or a sealed room, closed in any loop of ordinary make. It can be shut out from running water (see Trades). It cannot drown. It cannot die.

### When your body is broken
See Return by Water below. It carries a cost and a trigger, so it is written as a power, and the power is the rule.

### Things carried (as you gave them; all ordinary unless a power below says otherwise)
All ordinary, no power attached, and it asks that they be written as ordinary things.
- A pole of ash wood, about as tall as a man, worn smooth by hands.
- A long cloak of oiled wool.
- A linen shirt and wide trousers, loose cut and cord belted, so they hang right on either shape. Nothing in the cloth is more than cloth.
- A bronze knife, short, in a leather sheath on the belt.
- A hide bag holding a flint, a striker stone and a handful of dry tinder.
- A plain skin for water, empty.

### Settled at the table

- God-sense, as set for this campaign: you know another god only face to face, close, as a weight or an unease that grows surer with time and can be wrong. It tells nothing of the other's name, nature or powers. You know that others like you came down when you did.
- Pace: the pace of your form, as you described it; the pace table's rates apply (`engine/rules/time-and-pace.md`).
- Senses you did not describe are unsettled and are settled in play, never inferred.
- The "Intent, your first wording" lines are kept for the record only. Where one differs from a Description or a Drawback, the Description and the Drawback stand.
- When the body is broken: see the return power below. It carries a cost and a trigger, so it is written as a power.

### Definitions

- The water you stand in. The one river or stream you are standing in, from its spring to where it meets another water or the sea. A feeder is another water.
- Running water. Fresh water moving in a bed: a spring, a stream or a river, under ice if it still runs. Wells, cisterns, ponds, rain, snow and still lake water are not, except where a stream runs out of a lake.

## Powers

### The Two Faces
Status: main
Description: You change between the Crown shape and the Ford shape. The change takes about a hundred breaths, in which you are between: soft, slow, dull in every sense, unable to act, and as easily hurt as a mortal. Anyone watching sees it.
Drawback: The between leaves you unable to defend yourself or flee. You may change once between one dusk and the next, and not at all while held or bound.
Limits settled at creation:
- One body: wounds carry across both shapes. No third shape, and neither shape copies any mortal. The Ford shape's forgettable face is an ordinary plain face and no power: people may fail to remember it as they fail to remember any ferryman, and one who has reason to look will remember it. Both shapes keep the same eyes. Being watched: one who sees the change knows what they saw. Against a god's sight: unruled until it happens.
- Intent, your first wording: "It changes between the Crown shape and the Ford shape. The change takes about a hundred breaths, in which it is between: soft, slow, its senses dull, unable to act, and as easily hurt as a mortal. Anyone watching sees the shape change."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Water's Ear
Status: main
Description: Standing still in running water to the knee or deeper, eyes shut, you hear what happens in and on the water you stand in, below you. You hear the whole run below you to its mouth: wading, crossing, drinking, washing, boats and their loads, numbers, and things dropped in; voices only as voices (how many, raised or low), with no words; no sight. You learn what is done, never who anyone is or why.
Drawback: The water keeps your hearing. For as long as you listened, and never less than a day, you are deaf on dry land.
Limits settled at creation:
- While listening you are deaf and blind to where you stand and cannot move; anyone may come up behind you. This is a condition, not the price. Only what touches or sits on the water, and sound made within a few paces of the bank. Nothing of dry land beyond that. Not on still water, a well, a pond, a lake except where a stream runs out of it, nor through ice thicker than a hand. Only the water you stand in, not its feeders or branches. It never tells a god from a mortal. Light and dark make no difference. It rules no mind. Water held or turned by more than ordinary craft, and water another god holds: unruled until it happens. Your own people get no exemption. Use across a year: limited by the price below.
- What you learned stays with you seven days and then fades, unless you have spoken it aloud, set it down in marks, or told another. This is how the power works, not its price.
- Intent, your first wording: "Standing in running water to the knee or deeper, still, with its eyes shut, it hears and sees what happens in and on that same water below where it stands: people wading, crossing, drinking, washing, things dropped in, voices spoken over it within a few paces of the bank. It asks for the whole run of the water below it, to its mouth."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Bar the Ford
Status: main
Description: Standing in the water, you raise the river against one crossing on the water you stand in, a ford, a channel or a gap: one within your sight, or, seen or not, one within a morning's walk (about eight miles) where you have stood before. Over about a hundred breaths the river rises in its own bed there, quick and cold, to a man's chest and a little over the banks, and it holds until the next dusk. It is plainly seen and heard.
Drawback: The water is taken, not made. Below the crossing the river runs thin for the same span, and it starves those below as it bars those above, your own people among them. You cannot end it early, even if you were wrong. And you cannot change shape for a day after.
Limits settled at creation:
- One rising at a time. Only moving water in a bed you could stand in: not a lake, a pond, a well, or frozen water. The rise does not travel past the crossing's own width and is not a wave. It bars those who wade; a boat can still be poled across, slowly. One caught in the ford as it rises has the hundred breaths to get out. It rules no mind. Water held by a dam or other work of more than ordinary craft, and any water another god holds: unruled until it happens. Your own people get no exemption.
- Intent, your first wording: "It raises the river against a crossing. Within its sight, at a ford, a channel or a gap in the water it stands in, the river rises in its own bed, quick and cold, up to a man's chest, and a little over the banks. It holds until the next dusk. The rising is plainly seen and heard. It asks to do this at any crossing on that same water within a day's walk, and not only within sight."
- The water you stand in and the water of the crossing you bar are the same one river or stream, near or far.
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: The reach beyond sight was bought with the weakness Dry Ground, under Weaknesses taken.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Slip the Knot
Status: main
Description: With a hand still wet from running water, you touch a bond of ordinary craft and it gives: a knot, a cord, a shackle, a bolt, a bar, a latch, a lock, a door fastening, however strong. It may be a bond that holds you or one that holds another.
Drawback: After each use you are stricken for about an hour: your hands shake, your mind will not settle, and you can neither decide nor act, though you can be moved. You cannot bind again what you loosed.
Limits settled at creation:
- The hand must be wet from running water, and stays wet enough for about a hundred breaths after it leaves the water; rain, sweat, a cup or a skin of carried water will not serve. A bond is a thing made to hold shut or tied. A wall, a pit, the fixed bars of a cage and a hand holding on are not bonds. Only the bond gives, not what it is fixed to. It rules no mind. A bond made by more than ordinary craft, or by a god: unruled until it happens.
- Intent, your first wording: "With a hand wet from running water, it touches a bond of ordinary craft and the bond gives: a knot, a cord, a shackle, a bolt, a bar, a latch, a lock. It may do this to a bond that holds itself or that holds another. It asks for any such bond, however strong."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Return by Water
Status: main
Description: When your body is cut apart, burned to the bone or crushed past holding, what is left runs as water and silt to the nearest running water, and you are nothing for a time. After a full season you re-form at a crossing on that same water below where you entered it, in the shape you last wore, wet and with nothing on you.
Drawback: Each time you come back, a memory or a habit does not come back with you, and you are not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- The return follows the table's rule above. You do not choose the crossing. What you carried does not come back. If you are broken where no running water can take what is left (shut in, or on ground with none in reach), what is left lies where it is and does not return until running water reaches it or it is carried to running water. Held and not broken, you are held.
- Ground of your own kind is running water, and a crossing on it.
- Intent, your first wording: "When its body is broken (see 'When your body is broken' above), it goes to the nearest running water and re-forms at a crossing below, within a season, in the shape it last wore."
Rulings: none yet.
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

**Dry Ground.** Out of sight and sound of running water you weaken. The first two days cost nothing. From the third day you weaken by the day, and by the sixth day you can use no power and can do nothing but go toward water. It ends a day after you are again within sight or sound of running water. How it can be used against you: a mortal who learns it can pen you a few hundred paces from a stream and wait.
- The measure is whether running water is near enough to be seen or heard, not whether you can hear it: a day of deafness beside a stream does not start the count.
- What it bought: Bar the Ford reaches any crossing within a morning's walk (about eight miles) where you have stood before, seen or not.

Kept as color: You will not sleep in a room with a door you cannot open from the inside, and you keep your back to a wall in a room with one door. Color, at no price, buying nothing.
