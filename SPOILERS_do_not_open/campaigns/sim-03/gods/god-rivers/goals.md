# Goals: the Ford Warden

On arrival it knows only that it expects a people. It does not know why.

## What it wants, and would say
1. A people gathered close at one crossing that the whole land comes to, with itself at the gate, watching both roads.
2. The high spring at the head of the water, taken first, before anyone else drinks at the source.
3. Salt for the cold months, and a granary that outlasts winter. The thaw tells you who prepared.
4. Knowledge of this country: where the roads run, where the magic runs, what neighbors stand near, and what lies old and shut under the mud. A neighbor you can watch is safer than one you cannot.
5. A mortal it can turn its back on: a gate-warden, a ferryman or a river pilot, clever, with a good memory, who keeps an oath when it costs and knows when not to swear.
6. To meet any other god face to face and weigh it before any bargain. It will not be weighed from afar.
7. Trade, where the other party keeps its own laws and a bargain means something.

## What it wants and would not say aloud
Kept in this file only. Given to no other god.
1. To never be held again. That no god, its own or another's, could be bound against its will, and that no open gate could be made into a cage. It does not know why this sits so deep. It feels it in the hands when a rope is coiled or a latch drops.
2. A companion who does not need to be watched. Failing that, a spring no one has found, at the very top, where clean cold water comes out of the dark and belongs to no one yet.
3. To be found and not caught: to be tall enough that mortals know what crossed the water, and still able to be missed by those who do not know to look.
4. Never to be found in pieces.
