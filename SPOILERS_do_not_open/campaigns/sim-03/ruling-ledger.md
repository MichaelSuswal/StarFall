# Ruling ledger: campaign sim-03

Every ruling the Keeper or the DM makes. Check here before ruling on anything. Ids are `L-NNNN`, in the order entered. Judgment order: fairness, above all to the god's original intent; then fit with the power as first written; then balance.

## Rulings settled at the player's Day 2 table

Entered on Night 2, 2026-11-20, from `campaigns/sim-03/player/power-card.md` and the Day 2 transcript. Each id is written back beside its ruling on the card. Year 0. God: the player's god (filed under "the Understory").

### L-0001
Date: 2026-11-20, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: what tended ground is and what it takes to make.
Arguments: the player asked for hearing "within my forest" with no limit on range, and offered "only on ground I have personally touched and tended first" as a drawback. The DM could not promise a forest, and took the tending as a condition and not a price.
Ruling: tended ground is living soil the god has worked with its own bare hands until its threads have taken; about three days of the god's work for an acre (five worker-days by hand, at a god's work rate).
Binds: the player's card; every power on it that names tended ground. The rate is fixed and is not on the pace table.

### L-0002
Date: 2026-11-20, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: what a tended line is and its rate.
Arguments: none against; set by the DM by analogy to walking and hand work.
Ruling: a strip a pace wide, laid by walking it barefoot and slowly, about a mile a day.
Binds: the player's card. The rate is fixed.

### L-0003
Date: 2026-11-20, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: what a tended line crosses.
Arguments: the player argued that a line over a sinkhole, a cave floor or leaf litter over rock should carry where living soil is at the surface, since the country it asked for is broken rock. The DM agreed on soil and rock and would not write it on any named country.
Ruling: a line carries over any living surface soil, however thin, whatever lies under it; down into a hollow or sinkhole and across its floor if living soil runs unbroken; a sheer drop of bare rock breaks it; a cave floor carries only as far in as its soil is alive; it breaks at bare rock, paving, floors, open water, and running water wider than a stride that no roots cross; marsh carries if alive.
Binds: the player's card.

### L-0004
Date: 2026-11-20, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: what dead or sterile ground is, for the weakness.
Arguments: made exact by the DM after the player accepted the weakness, and put back for a second yes, which was given.
Ruling: ground where nothing grows within a bowshot of the god: salted, burnt to mineral, poisoned, bare rock, deep cave, and open water out of reach of soil. A town with gardens and weeds is living ground. Frozen ground is asleep, not dead.
Binds: the player's card, the weakness and every power that names lifeless ground.

### L-0005
Date: 2026-11-20, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: whether the day of lost speech scales below a day.
Arguments: the player read "never less than a day" as a floor and asked for it to be confirmed.
Ruling: a floor. Listen for the minimum and a day is lost; listen longer than a day and as long as was listened is lost.
Binds: the player's card, the ground-ear.

### L-0006
Date: 2026-11-20, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: where a slow word is sent from, and whether a touched plot needs a line.
Arguments: the player bought reach to touched plots with its weakness and asked that "touched" mean bare hands on the soil of the plot. The DM added a term it had not stated and the player accepted it.
Ruling: a slow word is always sent from tended ground, whatever the plot. A touched plot needs no tended line to it, and a word to a plot not joined by a line takes ten days to a month.
Binds: the player's card, the slow word.

### L-0007
Date: 2026-11-20, Day 2 (entered Night 2). Year 0. Card: the player's. Matter: the drawback of the slow return.
Arguments: the player gave a wish for how its body breaks and asked for the rule. The DM wrote it as a power and proposed the drawback from the player's own Day 1 answer 16; the player accepted and asked that what is lost stay unspecified.
Ruling: each return from nothing leaves behind a memory or a habit, and the god is not told which. What is lost stays unspecified to the player.
Binds: the player's card, the slow return. How the loss is enacted is L-0008.

## World rules set at the Night 2 table

Each binds every card, the player's included, by its nature. No god is told that another's table raised it.

### L-0008
Date: 2026-11-20, Night 2. Year 0. Cards: every card. Matter: how a drawback that takes "a memory or a habit, not told which" is enacted.
Arguments: `powers.md` section 6 gives the mechanism for an NPC god (the Keeper removes an item from the god's record) and none for the player, and `power-lean.md` asks the Keeper to set it. Five NPC gods and the player asked that what is taken stay unspecified.
Ruling: for an NPC god, each time the price falls due the Keeper removes one item from that god's `record-of-gods.md` or `goals.md`, chosen by a logged roll among the items then in both files, and logs it; the god is not told. For the player's god, each time the price falls due the Keeper chooses by a logged roll one item among the entries of the god's chronicle canon (`people.md`, `threads.md`) and the lines of its conduct profile under "How it goes about things", records it in `S/player/growth.md`, and releases to the DM only that the god no longer has that item; the DM plays the god without it and does not announce it, and the player finds it by the gap. Where a card names a rate, the rate is the card's; else one item each time.
Binds: every card with such a drawback. Marked for Michael, since the player's half is a procedure no file gives.

### L-0009
Date: 2026-11-20, Night 2. Year 0. Cards: every card. Matter: how the table prices a return from a broken body.
Arguments: the player's slow return was priced by the DM at no less than a full season, at the nearest living soil to the largest part of what is left, no escape from being held, for one memory or habit lost. Seven NPC gods proposed returns from a day to two seasons, some at a place far from where they fell.
Ruling: a full season at the nearest ground of the god's own kind to where the largest part of it lies is paid by one item lost; a return in a month is paid by two items each time; nothing faster is sold. If that ground is more than a day off, the walk at the god's pace is added. Held, bound, buried or shut in is not broken. Remains kept from the ground of the god's kind do not return until it reaches them or they are let out. What another god does to remains is unruled until it happens.
Binds: every card. The player's slow return already sits inside it and is unchanged.

### L-0026

Arguments: `powers.md` refuses a drawback that restates the god's form or voice. Four gods offered as drawbacks things that are how the power works (helpless or dulled while it is used), a mood or wound the god already carries, a thing the god wants anyway, or a penalty that falls only if the god is careless.

No weakness buys a change of order of scale.
