# Event schedule: campaign sim-03

Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-21. Clocked events run on fixed years no one controls. Triggered events fire on a condition. Every mystery's cost if ignored is a row here, with the mystery named. Years are years of play; Year 1 opens the morning after the arrival. Each year opens in the season its god woke into; a season named in a row is the season of that year in the country named. Rows are cited by their letter and number. Nothing in this file is released; the signs reach a god only through who noticed, or through its own senses, and no one points at them.

Numbers that were rolled carry their roll ids. Numbers that were set carry their reason. Years the deep lore gives for its own test world were treated as shapes: each range was written into the roll's name before the roll.

## Random events

Each year, for each of the twelve peoples' countries, the Keeper makes one roll with `tools/roll.py --campaign sim-03 --who keeper --what "random event, Year N, <people>" --base 30`, adding `--factor opening-years=-20` in Years 1 to 7 (the stated step: the chance is 10 in a hundred in the opening and 30 after) and any factor the year's record gives (a country already under a clocked weather year: `--factor already-struck=-20`). On a success, one `--die d20` roll on the table below. The event falls on that country and reaches whoever would notice.

| d20 | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 | A hard winter: passes or fords shut a month longer, stores a fifth short. |
| 4 to 5 | A wet autumn: harvest a fifth short, roads deep, rot in store. |
| 6 to 7 | A dry summer: low rivers, thin grass, fire in the dry wood. |
| 8 | A great storm: roofs, boats and old trees down. |
| 9 to 10 | Beasts: wolves, boar or a rock cat on the herds for a season. |
| 11 | A sickness in one herd or one crop, for a year. |
| 12 to 13 | A good year: mast, grass or fish in plenty. |
| 14 to 15 | A quarrel between two houses comes into the open. |
| 16 | A birth out of the ordinary: twins, or a child in the ruling house. |
| 17 | A death out of season in an office or a great house. |
| 18 | A stranger: one trader, tinker or wanderer from the next country but one. |
| 19 | A find: something small and old turned up by a plough, a flood or a fall of rock, from that country's own page of the gazetteer. |
| 20 | A rumor from two countries away, half right. |

## What this schedule does not do

No event brings the gods toward one place. The Canters' round passes each settled country in turn by the roads that exist and asks nothing of any god. The stone, the hill, the door and the springs stay where they are. No road, pass or way under the ground was made for any event: every way named here is in the map as traced on Night 3a or as added tonight for the deep history, with its id.
