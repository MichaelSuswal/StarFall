## The rules, final

- God-sense: face to face only; a weight or unease that grows surer with time and can be wrong; never who, what or how many. One rule for the whole world.
- Bodies: one body, one place.
- Mortal magic: level 4 of 10, final on Night 1. Three to five workers in a people of five thousand, each with one small thing, paying late and in the self. One rule everywhere; no country is an exception.
- How gods stand out: 4 of 10. More than a great sorcerer, not by a mile.
- Climate 4, the wild 5, past the borders 4, the age of the story 10, non-human peoples 4, travelers 3.
- Technology: before the medieval era, no steel forges. The one piece of worked iron on the sheet hangs at Bell Pass [f16].
