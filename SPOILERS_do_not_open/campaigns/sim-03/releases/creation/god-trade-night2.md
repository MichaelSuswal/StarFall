# Release: god-trade, Night 2

This release is your whole instruction for tonight and your go for exactly the files it names.

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## What is so of every god here

### Knowing another god

A god knows another god only face to face. At a distance there is nothing to feel, however near the other may be over the next hill. Close, in the same room or across the same fire, a god feels another as a weight or an unease, and the longer the two stand near, the surer it grows. It is a feeling and not a proof, and a god can be slow to trust it or wrong about it. It tells only that another like it is there, in front of it: nothing of its name, its nature or what it can do.

What every god knows from the moment of landing is only this: that others like it came down when it did.

### Bodies

One body. A god here has one body and is in one place.

### Mortal magic, for pricing

It is simple, and it costs the worker.

### Being a god
You need no food, and sleep is a choice. You move at the pace of your form.
You cannot die. The peoples you will find are at about this level: before the
medieval era, no steel forges. Every power carries a drawback you would notice
losing. There is no set number of powers. Nothing is over the top.

## The people you lean toward
Rule: a god-king with a court and priesthood, a warlord in manner. The god holds the crown itself, with a court and officers; its word is law, carried by them. A priesthood stands in it only as the worship lean gives one. As a warlord, it rules by force, through its war band and captains.
Worship: a priesthood. Keepers of rite with standing among the people, offerings brought, no tithe by law.
You build your people within these on Night 3, or give your reasons not to.

## What you said, and asked
No follow-up.

## The season you wake into
Autumn.

## Options
Each is open to you once, on the same terms as to any god. None is advised and none is expected.
- You may describe yourself in any order and at any length. You may come down with a name or with none; with none, say what you are to be filed under until someone names you.
- Anything that would let you reach, sense or act beyond your one body is a power: described by you, priced with a drawback, tested against edge cases, and written on your card.
- You may ask for something stronger than it should be and have it priced. An ask may be trimmed, and a trim is said to you plainly. You may argue any line.
- Where a price could go two ways, you are shown both and you pick one.
- You may propose drawbacks of your own. Some of what you propose may be taken as conditions of the power and not as its price.
- A weakness taken on can buy an improvement elsewhere. You do not have to take one. A weakness your form already pays buys nothing; to buy, it needs a price you do not already carry. For size only: a weakness that buys might be a kind of wound that does not close until a season is spent a certain way, a kind of ground that weakens you by the day until you can do nothing but leave, or a line that a mortal who knows it can hold against you; it might buy a power's memory lengthened from days to a season, a power's reach taken one step further, or two uses standing at once where there was one. A weakness may also be kept as plain color under your form, at no price, buying nothing.
- A god cannot die, but its body can be hurt, held, burned, cut apart, shut out. How your body breaks and how it returns is yours to set. If what you describe has a trigger and a cost, it is written as a power and needs a drawback you would notice losing. One price that may be put to you for it: each time you come back from nothing, a memory or a habit does not come back with you, and you are not told which. You may say no and be shown another.
- Where a drawback takes something from you, you may ask that what is taken stay unspecified, so that you find it by noticing a gap.
- Say what is on you when you land: clothes, a bag, a tool, anything in your hands. You may ask that they be ordinary things with no power attached. Anything carried that does more than an ordinary object is a power and is priced.
- Senses you do not describe are left unsettled and are settled later, never inferred.
- You may define terms your powers rely on (what counts as your mark, what makes a place your own). Staying in a place and working it builds what your card defines and what the time spent produces, and nothing else.
- Anything a power of yours does to another god, or to a thing made or held by more than ordinary craft, is written "unruled until it happens".
- You may ask for a clarification "in the write-up, not a new power" when wording is all you need.
- You may say what you want that you would not say aloud. It is kept in your own goals file and is given to no other god.

Your people come with helpers who run things when you are away. Who your people are, and who those helpers are, is settled on Night 3; tonight you write only the paragraph the form below asks for.
