# Starting state: the Marrans

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,000
stores: the harvest is in and middling; grain bought as well as grown; stores for the winter in the town granary
treasure: moderate, and much of it owed: boats, carts, goods in carriage, tallies
trade: carriage of grain, hides, wool, reed and dried fish; salt carried for others, dear
strength: a war band of about 150 under captains who guard roads and fairs; 300 more could be called
mood: busy and sharp at the fair; one house in five quiet about what it owes
