# Starting state: the Column

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,000, in about a thousand households
stores: short: a fifth of the winter grain spoiled at one hold; march stores for the crossing and little over; the herds thin after winter
treasure: little: beasts, felt, rope, worked stone; a little bronze
trade: felt, cheese, rope and carriage out; salt and bronze in
strength: about 700 who bear arms in several bands; a fifth of them tied to the holds in the cold months
mood: lean and impatient for the thaw; the Marshal and the Warden of the Holds at odds
