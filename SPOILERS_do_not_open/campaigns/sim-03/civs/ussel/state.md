# Starting state: the Ussel

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,000
stores: stores laid in close, house by house; enough for the winter in most folds, not in one
treasure: small and hidden: wool, hides, charcoal, old worked stone
trade: almost none; a little charcoal and wool out, salt in when anyone brings it
strength: about 300 who can hold the wall and the roads through the folds
mood: shut in for the winter, each house with its own quiet
