# Starting state: the Weirfolk

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,000
stores: the winter stores are nearly eaten, as every spring; grain and salt meat for six weeks; the meadows under flood
treasure: moderate: toll goods in store, boats, salt, a little bronze
trade: tolls and ferriage; salt, hides, grain, bronze and cloth pass through
strength: a sworn band of about 200 who keep the roads, the stakes and the wall
mood: hard-worked and short-tempered in the flood; uneasy about Averley's [f39] message
