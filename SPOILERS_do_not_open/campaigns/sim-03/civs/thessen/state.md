# Starting state: the Thessen

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,000
stores: the mast is down and the pigs are out; cured meat, grain and roots to carry an ordinary winter, thin if the sickness takes the herds a third time
treasure: moderate: hounds, hides, cured pork, worked horn; little metal
trade: hounds, hides and cured meat out; wool, salt (dear) and bronze in
strength: about 400 who hunt with bow and spear and can hold the wall; the Captain of the Wall has 40 on the gates
mood: close and watchful; two houses at odds; the old uneasy about the herds
