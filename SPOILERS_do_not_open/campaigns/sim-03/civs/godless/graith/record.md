# People: the Graith

Campaign: sim-03. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3a, 2026-11-20, from the placement plan and the traced map. Godless.

## Who they are
Not human. A slow people of deep and high rock, heavy, grey, long-lived, who live at a pace of years and remember further back than any human people here.

## How many
About 800, in halls under the eastern heights of The Scree Marches [r03], entered at Graithdeep [f46].

## How they live
They cut and shape rock, tend pale growths in the dark, and come to the surface seldom. A conversation with one may take a season. They keep The Sealed Door [f49] shut and do not say what it is. The old way that goes under the range at Deepway Mouth [f56] is known to them.

## Their code
A thing said is said once. A thing shut stays shut. They are not friendly and not hostile: a stranger who waits long enough at the mouth is answered, and a stranger who enters is put out.

## Their history in the land
Older in the range than the forts of three ages. The Column has gone round them for as long as the Column remembers.

## What they believe about gods
They do not use the word. They speak of "the loud brief ones", and it is not clear whether they mean kings, peoples or something else.

## What they want
Quiet, and the Door left shut.

## Whether and how they touch the great events
They may, through what they remember. What they remember, and the limit on how they may be asked, is in `mysteries.md`, L03, and in `cast.md`.
