# Starting state: the Sallow

The evening the gods come down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 2,500
stores: enough; dried fish and fowl
treasure: small: cords of debts owed them, worked bone, amber
trade: dried fish, eels, reed and down out; bronze, salt and cloth in, by night
strength: about 300 who can fight from punts with dart and net; no one fights them in the reeds
mood: withdrawn; the night market shut to the road people this winter
