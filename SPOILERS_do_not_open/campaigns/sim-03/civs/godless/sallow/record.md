# People: the Sallow

Campaign: sim-03. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3a, 2026-11-20, from the placement plan and the traced map. Godless.

## Who they are
Not human. A small, long-fingered, large-eyed folk of reeds and river edges, who work and trade by night and lie up by day. Mortals are not sure they count as persons, and the Sallow do not argue it.

## How many
About 2,500, in stilt places through The Rattlereed [r12], the largest Nightstake [f45], and a few boats' worth down The Sarn [f03].

## How they live
Fish, eels, fowl, reed and what the river brings. They build on stakes over water and move by punt. They keep a night market at the edge of the reeds for those who come with something to sell.

## Their code
Nothing is bought or sold by daylight. A debt is a knot in a reed cord, and a cut cord is a feud. They keep their own counsel and their own law, and they do not enter a house with a hearth.

## Their history in the land
They were in the reeds before the road was a road. They have dealt with the road people for longer than the Lowlamp have had that name.

## What they believe about gods
That gods are day-things, loud and soon over.

## What they want
Their reeds uncut by others, their nights their own, and the tally of three winters' reed-cutting at the ford met.

## Whether and how they touch the great events
Not by design. They see a great deal of what moves on the river at night. What they will say of it is theirs.
