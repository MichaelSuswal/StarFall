# People: the kingdom of Averley [f39]

Campaign: sim-03. Written by the Keeper on creation Night 3a, 2026-11-20, from the placement plan and the traced map. Godless.

## Who they are
Human. An old rooted kingdom of farmers, salt-makers and clerks in The Old Low Country [r15], between The Sarn [f03] and The Aver [f05].

## How many
About 40,000: about 9,000 in the walled city of Averley [f39], about 1,200 at Saltsteps [f40], the rest in villages (Nettlebridge [f41] among them) and farms.

## How they live
Grain, orchards, cattle, and salt boiled and dried in pans cut in the headland rock. The city is laid out in rings round an empty paved court, and no one remembers why it was built so. A written law. A queen's seat held for the last eleven years by a regent, Osmer, for an heir not yet of age, with a council of the salt houses.

## Their code
A debt is written and is paid, and an unpaid one is inherited. Salt is the crown's and is sold, never given. A boundary once walked is never walked again. They are courteous, slow, and certain that they were here first.

## Their history in the land
About six hundred years under the present line, on ground that has held a city far longer. They outlasted the league of ford towns and bought up its debts. They hold old half-sticks against many houses of the Marrans, and by an old treaty take a tenth of the toll at the crossing held by the Weirfolk. The deep history fixes their dates in 3b.

## What they believe about gods
That the first kings became something more and watch the court. A court rite with no priesthood of weight. They do not expect gods to walk in, and a regent has reasons not to want one.

## What they want
To keep the salt, collect what is owed, see the heir crowned, and be left as they are.

## Whether and how they touch the great events
They do. The Warm Stone [f42] fell in their upland sheep pasture the night the gods came down, about 55 miles north of the city. On the evening of the arrival no one has found it. It lies in godless country between two seats, placed by roll [r-0147, r-0148].
