# Starting state: the Arroway

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,000
stores: the harvest is in and good; barley, oats, beans and roots in the granaries to carry the winter with some over
treasure: moderate: flocks, bronze, a little worked iron, stamped bread boards, old plate
trade: wool, cheese and grain out through the passes while they are open; salt, tin and iron in, dear
strength: a watch at each of four passes, about 200 in all; 500 more could be mustered
mood: content after harvest; two houses at odds over a pass
