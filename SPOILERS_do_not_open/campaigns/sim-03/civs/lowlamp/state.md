# Starting state: the Lowlamp

The evening their god comes down. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,000 at the winter ground
stores: winter stores of grain, dried fish and cheese bought and laid in; fodder short if the freeze is long
treasure: good: herds, wagons, goods in store, debts owed to them
trade: tolls, carriage, hides, wool, felt, reed mats and dried fish; salt bought and sold
strength: a night watch of some hundreds in turn; about 200 who ride with the trains armed
mood: settled for the winter; uneasy about the reed folk's silence
