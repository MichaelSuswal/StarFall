# Balance: campaign sim-03

A weight from 1 to 10 for each god's total power: main powers, drawbacks, trades. Roughly equal, not exactly (DESIGN 3.15). The band aimed for is 4 to 6. Written on Night 2 before the hidden powers; written again below with them.

## Before the hidden powers

| God | Weight | Reasons |
|---|---|---|
| the player's god | 4 | The DM's note: low at landing, since every active power runs on tended ground and it lands with none; a slow body and a heavy weakness (fire, salt, dead ground, a line that holds it both ways) larger than what it bought. Rising with years: the ground-ear has no limit along a tended line, so its reach is the labor it has spent. That is craft, not growth, and is watched, not corrected. |
| god-beasts | 4 | A tracker to a year's age, a hiding that leaves it wordless for a day, one beast's bargain at a time. No force, no reach across distance. Heavy memory prices. A weakness that takes a season. |
| god-stone | 6 | The largest single stroke at the table: a bowshot of stone at a word. Held down by the second fall, the season's rest on each stone, the day of numb hands, and a standing-water line any mortal can dig. A child's body. |
| god-trade | 5 | Two seals of voice and weight, knowledge of every debt and debtor, a war band hardened by hand at the price of carrying its wounds. Hollowed while a seal stands; bound by its own careless word; wounds that wait on a true gift. |
| god-dreams | 5 | Two dreams a night at a day and a half, paid in real sleep; a touch that puts one mortal to sleep at a heavy price; night sight for day sight. Two weaknesses, both lines a mortal can hold. A child's body. |
| god-war | 5 | Three men's strength lent out to three; a ring where no hand is raised; a bell across a day's walk. Each is paid in its own body or hearing. A door any gifted mortal can close on it. |
| god-secrets | 6 | Six ears keeping a season, one in six false; a monthly question no mortal can answer falsely, no longer secret and sealed in it; a great beast's hide. Slow to move, shut out by any narrow door, silenced by a wound. |
| god-rivers | 5 | Two shapes; the whole river heard below it, without words; a crossing barred within a morning's walk; any ordinary bond opened with a wet hand. Every power needs running water; six days from it and it is powerless. |

No god is far out of line. Two sit at the top of the band. For god-stone this was said in its round 2 counter, with reasons, before it confirmed. The player's god is at the bottom at landing by its own choices, noted by the DM, and no question is put to the DM about it: nothing on the card needs changing and the lean is one the player chose with its eyes open.

## With the hidden powers

Fitted in 2.7 to bring each toward the band: the two lowest have the fuller hidden powers, the two highest the narrowest. A hidden power counts for little until it surfaces.

| God | Weight | With hidden powers | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| the player's god | 4 | 5 | Rootmeet is the fullest of the eight and serves what the player says it wants (the dead); flagged in the neutrality check. Narrow limits: recent mortal graves in tended ground, one a season, effects only. |
| god-beasts | 4 | 5 | Lastwalk answers its forgetting in one narrow place. |
| god-stone | 6 | 6 | Heldslope is narrow (thirty paces, paid in days of stillness). |
| god-trade | 5 | 5.5 | Mendterm once a year. |
| god-dreams | 5 | 5.5 | Keptplace cannot be chosen. |
| god-war | 5 | 5.5 | Keptlight, three lights. |
| god-secrets | 6 | 6 | Swallowword costs it pace and weight each time. |
| god-rivers | 5 | 5.5 | Opengate binds it to the gate it opens. |

Spread after: 5 to 6. No god stays noticeably stronger.
