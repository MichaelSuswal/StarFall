# Sealed past: the player's god

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. No agent reads this. It reaches the god only through play, and never as a cause.

The outline it gave: the quiet part of something large, the connective tissue between other lives; it lost the connection itself.

The rest. It is the under-weave of the Marrowood, the forest of Veshkarn that was also that country's burial ground. For longer than Veshkarn had writing its dead were walked across the ford at Cennac and laid under trees, and the wood's threads ran grave to grave and grove to grove, so that the people held the wood, the dead and the joining of them as one thing they never named. It was never worshipped and never one thing. It was what was between.

What it lost and does not remember: the felling. Near the end the wood was cut, on the flanks of Corrow and along the Ondre, for palisades and pyres. The tree-graves were opened. The joining was severed everywhere at once, and the slope the roots had held came down in the next thaw. What it carries as "a single body that feels each of its edges" is a people's memory of a wood cut into stumps. Its dread of fire and bare ground is that memory too. Its fear that its patience is "a nicer way of taking" has a root it cannot see: the wood did take its people, every one, gently, in the end.

It wonders whether what it lost is down in the old layers here. It is not. What it lost is under another star's fall, across the plane. What is in the old layers here is this land's own, and it will have to find that out by looking.

How this might surface, never as a cause: a blue-grey stone that dreams a wood with graves under every tree; a walking people's song with a gap where a forest's name should be; the way it knows, with its hands, how deep to lay the dead.
