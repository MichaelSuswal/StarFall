# Sealed: the player's god

### Rootmeet
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: Lying a whole night with your bare skin on a mortal's grave that lies in tended ground, you live one hour of that person's life as they lived it: what they saw, heard and did in that hour, and nothing of why.
Drawback: For a season after, you carry that person's strongest habit as your own, and you do not know which of your habits it is.
Limits settled at creation: A mortal's grave only, in ground you have tended, of one buried within about sixty years. One grave in a season, and never the same grave twice. You do not choose the hour. It shows what one person lived, which is only what they knew and believed; it shows no cause and nothing of what lies under the grave. The graves of other kinds of people, and anything older, are unruled until it happens. It gives a next step, never an end.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain or a place: a night spent at rest or in grief on a grave it has tended.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Cleanbind
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: A wound on a mortal or a beast that you dress with your own bare hands does not go bad: no rot, no fever from the wound. It heals at its own pace.
Drawback: The rot goes somewhere. Each time, something stored within a bowshot spoils: a crock, a sack, a side of meat, about a day's food for one household.
Limits settled at creation: The wound must be dressed within a day of its making. It closes nothing, sets no bone and brings no one back. Wounds from more than ordinary craft, and wounds on a god, are unruled until it happens.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain: dressing a wound by hand when no one else can.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
