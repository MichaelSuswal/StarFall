# Sealed past: god-trade

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. No agent reads this. It reaches the god only through play, and never as a cause.

The outline it gave: a tent, a table with a cloth, a hand struck on a hand, terms fair on both sides; then a great scale set down and tipped; a basin going cold, a cloth wrung out; being wanted by a great many; a count that comes out one short.

The rest. It is the struck hand of Veshkarn: the oath of the scale, sworn at the long table of Tal Sedder between the river towns and the hill clans, close to war over salt and seed. Fair weight, open fords, no arming of the passes. With it came the older custom the river towns kept, of washing the gear of those about to fight, done properly, once.

What it lost and does not remember: the oath was broken. One party armed the passes within a generation, and the last war followed, and the felling for it, and the slide. The pattern that had been a peace became the memory of a peace that failed, which is why it is hungry to be wanted and wary of every offer. It does not know which side broke it, and no one can tell it: the roll that held the terms was swallowed at Illun, one name short of whole. That is the count that comes out one short.

How this might surface: it cannot hear terms recited without listening for the missing one; a blue-grey stone that dreams a long table and a scale; a walking people's list of names that stops one short.
