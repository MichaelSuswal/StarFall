### Mendterm
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When two mortals who broke a bargain with each other stand before you, both willing, and strike hands again on terms you restate, the grievance of the old breach leaves them: they keep the facts and lose the grudge.
Drawback: You take the grudge. For a year you carry it as your own toward whichever of the two broke faith first, and you are not told which.
Limits settled at creation: Once in a year. Both must be present, willing, and understand. It changes no fact, pays no debt and binds no one to the new terms beyond their own word. Between gods, or on a bargain held by more than ordinary craft, unruled until it happens.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a being met: two parties who try, in front of it, to mend a broken oath and fail.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Trueweight
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Any balance or measure in your hand reads true, and you feel a false weight or a shaved measure as soon as you hold it.
Drawback: Your own hand will not use a false measure: it shakes until you set it down.
Limits settled at creation: Weights, measures and tallies you hold. It tells you that a thing is false, not who made it so.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain: handling a cheat's scale.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
