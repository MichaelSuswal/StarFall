# Sealed past: god-dreams

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. No agent reads this. It reaches the god only through play, and never as a cause.

The outline it gave: a fire and a herd it counted, a voice across the fire that counted it back; drink and generosity and being a little dangerous; a place set beside it, a hand's height off the ground, with no one in it; a number it cannot count past.

The rest. It is the herd-feast of the autumn crossing at Ondremouth in Veshkarn. Every autumn the herds were driven across the Ondre under the gate, counted in on one bank and counted out on the other, and the night after was the country's one loose night. The herd-god was always shown as a child, so that is the shape it kept.

What it lost and does not remember: the voice across the fire was a herd-boy, Dovrin, the one mortal the pattern held as its own, who played the counting game with it each year of his short life. On the night of the two closings the gate was shut with the herds half over. Dovrin was on the far side with the last of them and did not come in. The number it cannot count past is the count of the herd that night, at the head where he stood. Its fear of a sealed dark and of a contract signed unread is the gate.

How this might surface: a low seat or a child's stool set beside it without its noticing; a herd counted aloud near it; a blue-grey stone that dreams a river gate closing.
