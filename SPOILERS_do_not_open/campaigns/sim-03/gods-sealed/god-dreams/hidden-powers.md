### Keptplace
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: One mortal who, of their own will and with no bargain, has stayed beside you through a danger becomes the one for whom you keep the place. While that mortal lives you know that they live, and roughly which way they are.
Drawback: If that mortal dies while the place is kept, Night Tally is closed to you for a year, and the place cannot be given again for a year.
Limits settled at creation: One mortal at a time. It cannot be bargained, bought or ordered, and you cannot choose it; it happens or it does not. It tells you nothing of what they do or think.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a being met: a mortal who stays through a danger when it would have paid them to leave.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Herdcount
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Having walked once through a herd or flock, you know its count without counting, and you know at each dusk whether one is missing.
Drawback: While one is missing you cannot rest or sleep by choice, and a sleep you owe is still owed.
Limits settled at creation: One herd at a time, of beasts. It tells you the number and nothing of where the missing one went.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain: counting a herd aloud at dusk.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
