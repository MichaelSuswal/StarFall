# Sealed: god-stone

### Heldslope
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: With both hands braced on rock that has begun to fall (a slide starting, a wall going, a roof cracking), you hold it. It does not come down while you stand braced and do not move.
Drawback: The weight is real. You can do nothing else while you hold, and for each hundred breaths you held, you lie still as stone for a day after you let go. When you let go, it falls.
Limits settled at creation: A span of thirty paces at most, for no longer than from one dawn to the next. It holds a fall and mends nothing. It does not hold what Fault-Word broke. A fall made by more than ordinary craft or by a god is unruled until it happens.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain: a fall beginning above its own people while its hands are on the rock.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Dustsign
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: The grey dust you leave on stone you have touched stays a season through rain and wind, and you know your own dust by touch in the dark.
Drawback: Anyone who learns the look of it can follow where you have been.
Limits settled at creation: Stone only. It tells you that you were there and nothing else.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain: finding its way back along rock in the dark.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
