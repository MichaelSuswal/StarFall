# Sealed past: god-stone

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. No agent reads this. It reaches the god only through play, and never as a cause.

The outline it gave: one of two, the one that walked behind and held the weight; a people it was set to keep; a mountain that did not hold.

The rest. It is the shadow of Corrow, the mountain at the head of the Ondre in Veshkarn. That country held its mountain as a pair: the bright face that was looked at, prayed to and painted, and the dark weight behind that did the holding. The hill clans wintered under its south flank for longer than they had writing.

What it lost and does not remember: when the Marrowood was felled on the mountain's flanks there was nothing under the slope, and in the next thaw the south flank came down on the winter town. It was the one that held, and it did not hold. Its hands are scarred from that night, a people's memory of digging. The other of the pair is not here and did not come down: the bright face was under the fall and was never thrown. It will look for its other half among those who came down when it did, and none of them is it.

How this might surface: it wakes with its hands open when rock moves; a blue-grey stone that dreams a great many feet stopping at once; a child's certainty, with no reason, about which slope is going to go.
