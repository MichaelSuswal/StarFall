# Sealed past: god-rivers

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. No agent reads this. It reaches the god only through play, and never as a cause.

The outline it gave: a threshold of worn stone with a face cut on each side; water going by and people crossing; a gate that shut behind it and not before it; a long stillness under even weight; a dripping it counted.

The rest. It is the keeper of the river gate at Ondremouth, where the Ondre left Veshkarn. One face looked up the river at the country and one looked out. It weighed what came and went, and it took the toll.

What it lost and does not remember: on the night of the two closings the bell-wardens shut the gate to keep the country from ending by letting nothing more leave it, and they shut the keeper inside its own gatehouse so that it could not open it again. The herds were half across. It stood in the dark under the weight of its own gate for the rest of that country's time, which was the length of an age to it. It does not know that it did nothing to deserve it and nothing to stop it; it was only in the way. Its caution is the gatehouse.

How this might surface: its hands at a dropped latch; a carved post with two faces; a blue-grey stone that dreams a river no one here has seen; the quiet of water near it when it is afraid.
