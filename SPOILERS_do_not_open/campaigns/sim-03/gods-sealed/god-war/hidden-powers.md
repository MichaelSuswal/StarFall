### Keptlight
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: A light you kindle with your own hand before a laid seat does not go out for wind, rain or want of fuel for a season, and no one who shelters within its glow through a night dies of cold that night.
Drawback: While any such light burns you feel cold as a mortal does and can be slowed and numbed by it.

Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain: lighting a light for someone lost in a hard winter.

### Namehold
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: A mortal who gives you a name and says it to your face, meaning it, keeps your face in mind from then on, as no one else can.
Drawback: That mortal may refuse you their door as if you had given them a gift, whether or not you have.
Limits settled at creation: Only the mortal who gave the name, or one to whom that mortal gave it face to face. It makes no one remember anything else.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a being met: someone who names it and asks for it by that name.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
