# Sealed: god-beasts

### Lastwalk
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When you walk beside a dying mortal or beast through its last hour, it dies without fear and without pain, and speaks or looks clearly once at the end. Every mortal present for that hour remembers your face afterward, whatever else would take it from them.
Drawback: You take up the dead one's last unfinished errand as a track, and it holds you as The Old Track holds you until it is done or lost.
Limits settled at creation: One death at a time; you must be there for the whole hour and unhidden. It does not delay or prevent the death. The death of a god's servant by a god's hand, and anything not mortal, is unruled until it happens.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a being met: a death it attends of one it has kept faith with.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Hearthwake
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: A cold hearth you lay your hand on takes fire once, without flint or tinder, if there is fuel in it.
Drawback: You flinch from it: you cannot hold The Held Breath until the next dusk.
Limits settled at creation: A hearth, a fire pit or a shrine lamp, with fuel laid. Not a weapon; it lights nothing else.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a place: cold ash at an untended shrine or an empty house.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.
