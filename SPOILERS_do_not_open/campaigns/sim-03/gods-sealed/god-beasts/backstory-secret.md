# Sealed past: god-beasts

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. No agent reads this. It reaches the god only through play, and never as a cause.

The outline it gave: something people came to; a threshold worn smooth by feet, smoke, cold ash; they stopped coming.

The rest. It is the walker of the dead at Cennac, the ford in the Marrowood of Veshkarn where every body was walked across the water before it was laid under the trees. The people held it as half of the wood and half of the house: the thing that met the dead at the door and saw them over. A hearth burned beside the threshold stone for as long as the rite was kept.

What it lost and does not remember: it was not struck down or betrayed. It was left off. The villages thinned, the rite fell to one house and then to none, and the hearth at Cennac went out three generations before the end. It was already half forgotten when the star fell, which is why it came through thin, hidden and content. It does not remember that its whole work was the dead. It remembers only the threshold and the ash.

How this might surface: its hands know how to carry a body; the dying are calm near it and it does not know why; a blue-grey stone that dreams a ford in a wood with a cold hearth beside it.
