### Swallowword
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When a mortal, willing and asking, tells you a thing they cannot bear to know, you can take it in: it is gone from them, wholly, and kept in you.
Drawback: It is sealed in you as an asked answer is, you dream it, and for each one you carry you move at half pace for a day after taking it. The weight under your ribs grows and does not lessen.
Limits settled at creation: The mortal must ask and must be willing at the moment of telling. One thing each time: one deed, one sight, one name. It takes knowledge, not consequence: what was done stays done, and others who know it still know. On a god, unruled until it happens.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a being met: a mortal who begs it to take something from them.
Rulings: none yet.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Hushfall
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Within arm's reach of you, when you will it, no sound made carries further than a dozen paces.
Drawback: You are hoarse for a day after and your voice does not carry at all.
Limits settled at creation: Sound only, and only what is made within your reach. It lasts while you stand still.
Trigger that would surface it (a kind, never a date): a strain: needing quiet while hunted or held.
