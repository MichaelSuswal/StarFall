# Sealed past: god-secrets

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to `gods-sealed/common-past.md`. No agent reads this. It reaches the god only through play, and never as a cause.

The outline it gave: a long dim keeping place where everything said was kept and it was the keeper; it knew how every story would go but its own; a weight under the ribs, a taste that is not hunger.

The rest. It is the keeper of Illun, the long low house of clay rolls in Veshkarn where everything sworn was kept. The country held it as the one who knew how each kept thing would end, and who could not read its own roll.

What it lost and does not remember: near the end a walking, singing people came to Illun to ask for the country's names, to carry them out before the fall. The last roll held those names, and the terms of the oath of the scale, and it was the only roll whose ending the keeper could not see. It did not hand it over. It took it in. Nothing of Veshkarn was carried out by anyone, and the weight under its ribs is the country's own name-list, one name short. It wants to be forgiven and never told for what. This is what.

How this might surface: a walking people who will not sing near it, or who sing and stop; names it knows that belong to no place here; a blue-grey stone that dreams a long dim house; the taste at the back of its throat when a list is read aloud.
