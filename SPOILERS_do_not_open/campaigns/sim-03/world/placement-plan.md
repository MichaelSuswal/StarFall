# Placement plan: campaign sim-03, Night 3a

A written geography, made before any painting. Working names only; ids come when the map is traced, and the data follows the paint. Nothing here is a description of land for release. North is the top of the sheet.

## The sheet

One continent on one square sheet, about 1,600 miles across. Sea along the whole west edge and the whole south edge, meeting at the south-west corner. The land runs off the north and east edges into fog. The star's center lies inland at the middle of the sheet. With a living radius of 110 days' walk (about 1,500 miles at the rough-ground rate of 14 miles a day) the edge of the rain lies beyond every edge of the sheet: the Pale is not on it.

## The land

- The north: a high broken mountain range runs west to east across the northern fifth of the land, widest and highest in its middle, snow on its peaks, with many passes. Its western end breaks down into wooded hills. Its eastern end opens round a wide high basin ringed by mountains, with a river through the floor of the basin leaving southward by a narrow gap.
- The north-west: deep dark forest over broken, folded hills, many small steep valleys hidden one from the next, the foothills of the range's western end.
- The west: a great old forest, lowland, reaching nearly to the west coast. A river comes out of the middle uplands and runs west along the forest's southern edge to the western sea, with a wide belt of marsh and reed between the forest and the river country.
- The south-west: old forest on pale broken limestone ground, pocked with sinkholes, streams that vanish; where the land drops toward the south-west coast the forest thins to marsh. The northern mountains are very far from here.
- The south: low open river country. A wide slow river comes from the middle uplands and runs south through meadows and worked land to the southern sea, with a sheltered bay at its mouth.
- The south-east: river country. Streams gather out of hill ground south-east of the middle and become one broad river that runs south-east through low flood meadows, with one hard-bedded shallow where it can be crossed, and joins the eastern river before the southern sea.
- The east: wide pale steppe. The river from the high basin runs south through it, and in the middle of the steppe it divides and joins through a long reed marsh. A band of hard pale ground runs north to south beside it, where a road could go.
- The middle: open upland heath and worn low hills, a wide shallow bowl. In the bowl lies one long dark hill with the plain shape of a great animal lying asleep, curled, a mile or two long: the center beast's ground, under the star's center.

Rain shadow: the range takes the northern weather; the east is dry (steppe) and the west and south-west wet (forest, marsh). Rivers run from the range and the middle uplands down to the west and south seas.

## The eight seats

In a ring round the middle, each about 550 miles from it and 400 to 450 miles from the next (four to five weeks' walk on rough ground), none crowded, none cut off. Order round the ring, clockwise from the north: god-stone, god-war, god-dreams, god-rivers, god-trade, the player's god, god-beasts, god-secrets.

- god-stone, north: the middle of the range, among the passes. Its people's ring of winter holds on the lee (south) side.
- god-war, north-east: the high basin, the town on the basin floor by the river.
- god-dreams, east: the steppe road beside the reed marsh, the winter ground at a ford of the braided river.
- god-rivers, south-east: the one good crossing of the south-east river.
- god-trade, south: the southern river, a town at a ford a little above the bay.
- The player's god, south-west: the limestone forest, as its Day 3 brief described it; marsh at the low edge. Whether and where the unnamed town stands is set here: it stands in the forest, in a shallow valley of deeper soil between limestone ribs, and the god comes down in the forest outside it, on the worn track.
- god-beasts, west: the great forest's southern edge, where it opens on the marsh belt and the river country beyond.
- god-secrets, north-west: the folded forest hills.

## The godless peoples

Four (the Q4 scale at 4, read as the middle of three to five: decision in the keeper log), holding somewhat less ground than lies open, between the seats:
- an old rooted human kingdom in the low country of the middle south, between the southern river and the south-east river;
- a human herding people of the middle uplands, round the bowl;
- a folk of reeds and river edges, not human, in the eastern river's marshes and down its lower course;
- a slow people of deep and high rock, not human, in the eastern half of the range.

## The underground

The world has a country below (Night 1, Q12): old roads under newer ground, galleries in the limestone of the south-west, deep ways in the northern rock. Its sheet is not approved; it is described for a held request and is traced as data only on the surface grid.

## Placed by roll

The Remainder lies in godless country between two seats: by the placement roll among the eight adjacent pairs of the ring above, between god-rivers and god-trade [r-0147, replacing the void draw r-0146], 46 hundredths of the way from the first to the second [r-0148: 25 plus half of 42]. That ground is the old kingdom's low country.
