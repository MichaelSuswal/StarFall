# World draft: campaign sim-03, Night 1

Written 2026-11-20, Night 1, from the blend of eight surveys (keeper log 1.5) and the star (`S/star.md`). Vote counts are in the keeper log and nowhere else. Everything marked unplaced is placed on Night 3. Nothing here is released whole.

## Tone

The half-lit hour held long: dusk at a ford, lamps going low over a counted market, the edge of a wood when the animals have gone quiet. A held breath. Things kept, counted, watched and waited for. The world is very old and layered, so that whatever is dug into has something older beneath it; it is beautiful and not kind, and indifferent to who is asking. Peoples are guarded, slow to trust, and prize a kept word and a kept memory. Palette within the house style: umber, ochre, olive, wet near-black, one low warm source; long dusks and a wet, rich autumn as the weather tendency. Countries that may carry a lone mood, unplaced: a wet forest floor where nothing is wasted; a counted market; a long table still lit after the guests have gone.

## Climate and seasons (Q3 at 4)

Four real seasons on the hard side of the middle, varying by latitude, altitude and distance from the sea. Winter long and hard enough to close passes, test granaries and kill the unprepared. Spring late, and loud when it comes: ice breaking, rivers rising. A real summer, shorter in the high country. Autumn wet and rich, the season of harvest, fairs and reckoning. Each god's waking season is on its sheet; the player's (autumn) goes to the clock in 3b.8.

## The star, as it bears on the land

- Young phase: a small, high, pale star. Thin rain. Fading rare, in at most a place or two. No descent that anyone could measure within the campaign; the fall tens of thousands of years off. Voices from above rare and faint.
- Living radius 110 days' walk from the center. Whether the sheet reaches the edge is settled in 3a.3.
- Center body: a sleeping beast, visibly a beast, small for its kind, under the star's center. The surface painting must show it or its ground.
- Long game: all three. The hoarder (the beast), carriers (a walking people who sing names) and thinners (pale herds) each need a body in 3b.4.
- Remainder: fell in godless country, the night the gods arrived; placed by roll in 3a.3.
- Star content: a fall's new ground that remembered the one before, in dreams only.
- Ascended: mixed temper, few and far under a young star.
- For 3b.4: the human story is as old as the scale goes (below) under a star that is young. Both stand.

## Magic (Q6 at 4, final; Q7 pool)

Level, final tonight, at the top of what this star allows: a rumor most people doubt that turns out true in some valleys; a worker here and there, unevenly spread, some places plainly stranger than others. Never everywhere, never gone.

How it feels and costs, one system, consistent with Truth 4: a working feels like borrowing, a coat taken from someone else's peg, a pull under the skin, a taste of iron or copper. It is simple. It costs the worker, slowly and in the body and the self: warmth, sleep, a year, a memory, a name; the price is noticed late and easily misjudged. Local understandings that may survive, often wrong: that it is tending and coaxing what grows and rots; that it is a hunt; that it is weight; that it is being overheard; that it is a current that must be given back to water; that a ledger somewhere balances. Given to the player: only that it is simple and costs the worker.

## The two rules

- God-sense: face to face only. Nothing at any distance. At close quarters a god feels another as weight or unease, surer the longer they stand near; a feeling, not a proof; it says that another is there, never who, what, or how many.
- Bodies: one body, one place.

## How much gods stand out (Q8 at 4)

A god is more than a great mortal sorcerer, and not by a mile. Mortals who meet one look twice, argue, and can name a price; awe is not the first reaction, and a god that wishes to pass for a strange mortal may manage it for a time. Shaded the ceiling (in `S/star.md`).

## The wild (Q9 at 5)

Familiar beasts along roads and in settled valleys; a good many legends, some true; strangeness with its own logic, gathered where the ground is high, deep, wet or old; nameless things rare and wary.

## Past the borders (Q4 at 4) and the age of the story (Q5 at 10)

Somewhat more open country than held: old rooted kingdoms exist, some having forgotten why they were built as they were, with wide wild between and a few stretches no one has walked. The human story is as old as it can be: ruins under ruins over thousands of years, many peoples risen and fallen, the oldest layers thin and told wrong; some peoples are recent arrivals.

## Terrain the world must hold, and each god's wish

Old forest (three wishes), limestone karst with sinkholes, marsh and reed marsh, high mountains with many passes, a high river basin behind narrow passes, broken hills with hidden valleys, river country (five wishes: several rivers or one great system of many reaches, high springs to slow water, fords, one great crossing), steppe with a long dry road, a coast with a harbor, and ground for the center beast.
- The player's god: old forest on broken limestone, sinkholes, marsh at the low edges, mountains far off and rarely seen.
- god-beasts: old deep forest, river country at its edge, marsh between; a walled center with wild pressing close.
- god-stone: high broken mountains, scree, cliff, many passes.
- god-trade: river country running down to a coast and a harbor; fords and long roads.
- god-dreams: river country beside a long dry road; steppe; reed marsh where the river braids.
- god-war: high river country; narrow passes; a wide basin.
- god-secrets: deep forest, broken hills, valleys hidden one from the next.
- god-rivers: river country from high springs to wide slow water; one good crossing.

## Pools

### Fears (Q10)
Widespread: the slow dangers (hard winter, hunger, failed harvest year on year, sickness in herd and grain); the danger from inside (the broken word, the neighbor who is not what he was).
Rare candidates, all unplaced: a thing in the woods that is wrong and not hostile; avalanche and collapse on the march; a night that takes and a road walked twice; a hunting thing that learns; a thing that wears a friend's face; a people forgotten.

### Scarcity (Q11)
World scarcities: salt; sound seed and safe stores (grain that breeds true, a year's grain kept).
Temper, not a good: trust and remembering are short everywhere.
Local candidates, unplaced: iron; whole sleep; the loyalty of working beasts; a fine thing no one needs.

### Buried (Q12)
Widespread: old roads that go nowhere now, some under newer ground or river mud; sealed doors in rock. The world has an underground layer (the underground sheet is not approved; data only unless Michael says otherwise).
Rare candidates, all unplaced: something enormous and dead, too large to be sure it is bones, in more than one place; untended shrines; a nameless altar worn to a bowl by hands; locked storehouses with a ledger no one reads; a door that opens only when something is paid; doors locked from the inside; a broken seal; a thing that was meant to hold and wants to stay buried; a small wrongness in the land where a god has stood (one voice's image of god-sense, kept only as a candidate thing to be found).

### Non-human peoples (Q13 at 4)
A few kinds, each few; wary, keeping their own counsel and law. Mortals unsure whether they count as persons.
Candidates, all unplaced: a slow people of deep and high rock, living at a pace of years, long in memory; a folk of reeds and river edges who trade by night; one unreadable kind; a people who keep the night watches and do not sleep as humans do; speakers on four legs.

### Travelers (Q14 at 3)
Few, one or two at a time, from under other stars. Each carries a tool no one understands or knowledge half right (a measure, a way of counting, a crop, word of other beasts and other dead), with a cost attached; a problem for mortals before a gift to anyone. All unplaced.

### Mortals worth following (Q17)
Widespread: keepers without crowns, who hold a word, a crossing, a grave or a town past the point of profit.
Candidates for the small cast, all unplaced: a ferry or gate keeper who knows every debt on the river; a keeper of the dead who knows each name; one who walked out of a ruin and gathers by walking; a clever poor bargainer; a child who befriends what others fear; a cook or midwife who holds a town together.

### Discoveries (Q18)
Widespread wish: a companion that does not worship and wants nothing a god can give.
Candidates, all unplaced: a truth about what lies underneath and who came before, and about the dead; why old forest and old ruins line up; a place where a god's word means nothing; a spring no one has found; something that remembers.

### Undared wishes (Q19)
Handled with care; none is placed where its own god is certain to find it; many may be kept and never found. All unplaced: that what was honored and lost is waiting; that this time the mountain holds; that a broken oath can be mended; that nothing lost is lost for good; that some hand keeps a list, and the list is kind; forgiveness without the telling; a gate that cannot be made a cage (as a place or thread only, never a rule). The player's wish, that nothing exist only for its god, is kept by building nothing for it.
