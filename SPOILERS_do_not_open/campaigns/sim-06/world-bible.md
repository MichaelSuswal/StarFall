## The rules, final
- God-sense: nothing at a distance. At close quarters a god feels another as a weight that settles into knowing over a moment. It tells that the other is a god and nothing more. Never a tally.
- Bodies: one body.
- Mortal magic: level 7, common, simple, and it costs the worker: small and countable each time, large over a life, toward her name. One system everywhere. No more than about a dozen workers in any one place now; more than twenty under one patron is a school (`event-schedule.md`, L05-n).
- How much gods stand out: 5. Plainly more than any mortal worker, and still troubled, answered and hurt by a gathered people.
- The wild: 4. Familiar beasts and many legends; a few nameless things at the edges.
- Climate: 4. Four real seasons, hard winters.
- Held and open land: 5, about half and half.
- Age of the human story: 9. `deep-history.md`.
- No underground. Buried things are sites on the surface sheet.
- No rule of the world is different in any one place.
