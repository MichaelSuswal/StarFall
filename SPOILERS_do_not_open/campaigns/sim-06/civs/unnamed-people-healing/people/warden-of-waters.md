# The Warden of Waters

Nature and history, fixed. A dry man of about fifty, who keeps wells, drains and the flood, and dislikes advice. In the spring he was fetched to the wellhead, heard the stranger with his arms folded, and acted on it. In month 8 he was the first at court to tell of the heap that went to black slurry in an afternoon. His name was not said aloud this year.

Year 2, month 3: he came to the bank at the Reckoner's asking, heard the stranger with his arms folded, and set a rule in his own office that no well is dug below the bank graves. Month 12: shown the Reckoner's cords, he said he would send to the steadings that kept their wells. He acts on its Readings and does not take it for more than a reader of water.

Year 3: month 1: heard the Reckoner carry the stranger's asking for test wells with his arms folded, said he dug where he judged, and the same week sent the Reckoner's cords to the steadings that kept their wells. Month 2: asked the elders' leave to dig on the rise. Month 3: had three wells dug on the rise at the far end from the laid rows; water was struck at an ordinary depth; stood with the stranger at each before anyone drank. End of month 6: stood with it at each again. He tries each well by his own ways beside its Reading. He acts on its Readings and does not take it for more than a reader of water. His name was not said aloud this year.

Year 4: month 1 heard the Reckoner carry two askings of the stranger with his arms folded; said the rise was the court steading's ground and the elders' to give, that the rule on wells below the bank graves was already his and needed no carrying, and set a notch for it on his cords. Month 2 put the first asking to the elders as theirs to settle; about thirty households of the low steadings with closed wells draw from the three test wells in a rota he keeps. He dug no fourth well. Month 3 stood at the wells below each grave the Keeper pointed to. Month 9 tried the three wells by his own ways beside the stranger's Reading and found them as the year before. He acts on its Readings and does not take it for more than a reader of water. His name was not said aloud this year.
