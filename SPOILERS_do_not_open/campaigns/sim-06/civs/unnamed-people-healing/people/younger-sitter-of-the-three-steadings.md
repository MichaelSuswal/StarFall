# The younger sitter of the three nearest steadings

Nature and history, fixed. A woman of about thirty five at the end of Year 5, a sitter of one of the three steadings nearest the court steading, practical and not afraid of being seen to take a help. In Year 4 she was the one of the three who said the stranger's offer to read a sick one before and after a brew was more than the court's yard had given her in years. In Year 4, month 8, she brewed for a child with the rattle and sent for it, and said afterward to Hedda that it spoke as a reader of water speaks of a well; she told the other two how it judged the brew. In Year 5, month 2, she heard the stranger say through Hedda that its reading is closed on her people's sick and that it would ask her what ailed the sick one; she said she would send as she had. Month 9 she brewed for an old woman with the wet chest and sent; it asked her what ailed the old woman and said what it could from her words, and she told Hedda that it asked as one asks who cannot see, and that it was the only one who had asked her what she saw. One of the other two sitters asked Hedda in the winter what she made of it. Her name was not said aloud this year.
