# The old elder of the kept well

Nature and history, fixed. An old man, elder of a low steading below the court, whose father Tolm dug the steading's well with his own hands. Stubborn, plain, proud of old things, not cruel. In Year 2 he said before his steading that his people would not carry water like children, and kept his well. In Year 3, month 4, the stranger read his well in front of his steading and said what it read; the Keeper's release says it read foul. He did not close it and did not forbid anyone to carry creek water, and a number of his households and the steading's sitter began quietly to carry it. Year 4: month 2 he said at the elders' meeting that old water is good water and that he wanted nothing from the rise, and was against the rota. He kept the well at the thaw when a third of the kept wells closed. His households carry creek water still. His own name was not said aloud this year.
