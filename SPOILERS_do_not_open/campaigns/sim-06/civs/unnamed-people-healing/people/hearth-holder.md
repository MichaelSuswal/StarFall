# The Hearth-Holder

Nature and history, fixed. A man of the line, about sixty at the end of Year 1, who holds the throne in the Tender's name. He is not a man who asks, and in month 11 he sent to Hedda when his youngest grandchild, a girl of four, took the wet chest. His name was not said aloud this year. He has not met the stranger. In the last month he sent a runner to say he would be glad to have the stranger sit at the seat's hearth when it was well. He has said nothing in the open about who the stranger might be.
