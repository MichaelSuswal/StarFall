# What the people now say, end of Year 1

Where they explain a cause, they are wrong about something that matters.

## About the woman at the water gate
- That she is the guest the cup has waited for since Nessa Ferry first set it. This is said by the children, the Seat Warden's neighbors and a few old women. The Warden herself says only that the cup has not been empty of her. Wrong, or at least unproven, in taking her for what the cup is for.
- That she is a namer who forgot to sing: she asked the names of the hamlets and the channels, and the namers also gather names. Wrong: the namers pay the ferry with a song and go on, and she asked no one to sing.
- That she is Nessa Ferry's kin come back from the water's side. Said by the old women, who count a grandmother by her floods. Wrong in the cause of her coming.
- That she is a salt carriers' counter, sent to count the town for a master upriver. Said by the Wary. Wrong in the master, and half right that she counts.
- That her cold hands are a woman giving her warmth to the sick and shrinking for it, a larger measure of the midwives' quieting touch. Said by the midwives and the hamlets. Wrong in the cause: they think it is the same craft as the midwives', only more of it.
- That the river did not take a child this winter because she sat the long half day at the gate. Said by the ferry keepers, who had no drowned child in the ice. Wrong in the cause: it is the cold and the care of the ferry keepers.

## About the water and the ford
- That the deep water leaves the ford because the ford is crossed too little, so it forgets the place. Said at the weirs. Wrong: the cause is the channel's own moving.
- That the water listens a little at the edge of a field and not in a market, as before; now added, from the autumn: and a long time at the water gate, for a guest who asks.
- That a flood mark on the gate stone will tell a bad flood early. Said by the ferry keepers. Wrong, since a mark records only what has come.

## About the star
- The old women say the star is an eye that closes for an hour when the river sleeps. The salt carriers say a sky god blinks. This year the weirs say it came twice in the month she came. The Ferry Elder says it came last year too and nobody came. All wrong, and none know a cause.

## About their rulers and neighbors
- That the council is loud, quarrelsome, long and fair, and that the Speaker this year speaks well for the town to strangers.
- That the salt carriers cross less because the upriver gods are quarrelling and the roads are closed. Wrong in the cause, by their own stories.
- That the boat bands go at the lowest tides to hold their own council in the deep water. Wrong in what they do, no one having followed.

## About the well
- That the well turned because the stranger fouled it. Said for some days by the Wary, then withdrawn when the keepers drew the lining and showed the slip and the midden wash. Told afterward as a story against hasty blame.

## What they say about gods
They still hold no god. Some now say, softly, that if the river ever sent one, it would come on foot and ask for nothing.
