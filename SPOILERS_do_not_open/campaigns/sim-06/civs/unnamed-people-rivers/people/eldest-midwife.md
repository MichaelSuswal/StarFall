# The eldest midwife (unnamed)

Office: eldest of the midwives, with a seat on the council. A woman of about seventy in Year 1, bent, sharp, with a quieting touch that costs her the feeling in the hand for some days, as the record of her kind gives.

Nature: unsentimental, kind with her hands and not with her words. Watched the guest's hands on the first day and was the first to say, at the council, that the guest has a gift like her own and a worse cost. Took the guest into the circle of the midwives' talk.

Year 1: knows of the hamlets' fevers, and called the visitor to the hamlet where the child lay. Holds that the guest's cold hands are the midwives' craft in a larger measure. Is wrong in the cause. Does not take her for a god.
