# The ferry child (unnamed)

A girl of nine in Year 1, a ferry keeper's daughter, who works the landing at the water gate with a pole. She was the first of the town to see the stranger, and called the Seat Warden. Quick, bold, bad at keeping still.

Nature: curious, unembarrassed. She gave the guest a name in front of the other children (see report) and it stuck. She follows the guest about and asks her anything. She is the town's first child to be told a story of the water by her.

Year 1: first sight of the stranger; asked her a hundred questions; carried messages. Takes her for the cup's guest. Will be ten in the spring.

Year 2: ten in the spring. Sat at the front when the guest told the children where she woke, and said "then you are a spring's," which went round the town. Asked to go with her to the ford and was refused by the Ferry Elder. Carried messages and sulked. Still takes her for the cup's guest.

Year 3: eleven in the spring. Asked again at the fire whether she could go to the ford, and was answered that the Ferry Elder had said no and that the guest would tell her what she saw. Asked whether she was a spring's, and was answered that the guest did not know. Hid under the reed mat in the stern of the barge before it cast off, with a day's bread, and was walked up the bank by the ear by the Ferry Elder. Carried the Reckoner's cord messages all summer. In winter asked the Elder again and was told when she is twelve and has a pole of her own. Still takes the guest for the cup's guest.
Year 4: twelve in the spring. On the first day the ferry opened in the second month the Ferry Elder gave her a pole cut to her height, as he had said, and told her before the landing that she would go to the ford and no farther. She went down the brook by barge with the party, was taken on at the ford landing by the ford keepers, poled the ford ferry under the oldest of them while the party was below, and heard at the ford fire the guest answer the ford keeper. Did not hide on the barge. Asked the guest at the fire "Then what are you?" and was told. Still takes the guest for the cup's guest.
Year 5: thirteen in the spring. Asked the Ferry Elder to let her come on the carriers' road and was told it was a road and not a ferry. Went to the ford by barge with the party, was taken on again at the landing by the oldest ford keeper and poled the ford ferry while the party was on the road, and came home by the Brook Path with them, if the party came back. Was among the first to the weirs for the fish. Still takes the guest for the cup's guest.
