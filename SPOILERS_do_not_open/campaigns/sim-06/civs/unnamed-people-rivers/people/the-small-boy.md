# The small boy (unnamed)

A mill hand's youngest, about six in Year 3 and about seven in Year 4. Small, stubborn, not shy at a fire full of bigger children.

Nature: says the true thing flatly and waits. Not afraid of the guest, who is a visitor with a sit at the fire to him.

Year 3: at the children's fire in spring said "A spring cannot walk," when the guest told the children she woke at a spring and did not know whether she was any spring's. The others laughed; the ferry child did not. It went unanswered that spring.
Year 4: sat nearer the front, told all winter by the older children that he was the one who had made a guest say she did not know. The guest told him before the others that he was right, that a spring cannot walk, that she is not a spring, that she woke at one and walks and does not know why the one came with the other. He said "I said." The laugh that followed was on the grown people, and he took it. Takes the guest for a guest who keeps her word to a small boy.
