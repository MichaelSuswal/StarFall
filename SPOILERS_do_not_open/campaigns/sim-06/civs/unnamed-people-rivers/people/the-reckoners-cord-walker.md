# The Reckoner's cord walker (unnamed)

A young man of about 22 in Year 2, who has walked behind the Reckoner four years learning the cords. Careful, quiet, proud of his knots and a little afraid of the guest.

Nature: exact. Does not round a count. Likes to be asked what a mark means and can say.

Year 2: named by the Reckoner to walk with the guest to the ford and keep the low-water cord. Kept the hamlet counts, stood by while the ford keepers cut the summer mark, watched her stand in the larger river and said afterward only that it was a cold river for standing in. Walked home with her by the Brook Path. One of the Counters.
