# The Reckoner's cord walker (unnamed)

A young man of about 22 in Year 2, who has walked behind the Reckoner four years learning the cords. Careful, quiet, proud of his knots and a little afraid of the guest.

Nature: exact. Does not round a count. Likes to be asked what a mark means and can say.

Year 2: named by the Reckoner to walk with the guest to the ford and keep the low-water cord. Kept the hamlet counts, stood by while the ford keepers cut the summer mark, watched her stand in the larger river and said afterward only that it was a cold river for standing in. Walked home with her by the Brook Path. One of the Counters.

Year 3: about 23. Named again to walk with the guest, this time down the larger river three days and back, knotting a count of every landing and hearth they passed, to be brought to the Reckoner before it was told. What his knots hold is as the Keeper gives it. One of the Counters.
Year 4: about 24. Named again to walk with the guest, this time five days down the larger river and back, knotting a count of every landing and hearth passed and every gift given, to be brought to the Reckoner before it was told. Knotted the weir figures at the low water. What his knots hold below the ford is as the Keeper gives it. One of the Counters.
