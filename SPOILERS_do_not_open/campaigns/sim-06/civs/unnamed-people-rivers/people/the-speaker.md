# The Speaker, Year 2 (unnamed)

Office: Speaker of the council, chosen at the spring flood, one of the council, who opens and closes the sitting and speaks for the town to strangers. In Year 2 the council chose the head of a middle kin house, a woman of about 52, not the Speaker of Year 1.

Nature: plain, dry, patient, asks the one question nobody wanted to be the one to ask. Heard the guest to the end at the hearing about the ford and asked why a guest cared for it, and called the answer honest and odd. Does not take the guest for more than a guest with a good eye for water.

Year 2: chosen on the sixth day after the flood went down. Opened and closed the hearing about the ford and the one sitting in autumn. Her term ends at the choosing in the first days of Year 3.

Year 3: the council chose a new Speaker on the sixth day after the flood went down: the head of a younger kin house, a man of about 46, loud, quick to laugh, slow to decide, who hears a thing three times before he speaks on it. He is not named in this record. The Speaker of Year 2 went back to her seat. He answered the guest's question plainly, that the council had not settled the ford, and the council set a second small weir. He opened and closed the hearing for her leave and the one sitting in autumn. His term ends at the choosing in the first days of Year 4.
