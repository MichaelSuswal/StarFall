# The head of the oldest kin house (unnamed)

Office: head of the oldest kin house and a council seat. A man of about sixty eight in Year 1, grey, proud of his line, who counts his house back further than the low seat.

Nature: conservative and suspicious of strangers who count. Stood against the guest's counting at the first hearing and stood down only when the Reckoner's second cord was set. Leads the Wary.

Year 1: spoke against her at the hearing, put the first whisper about the well, and was publicly shown wrong by the well keepers. He bore it, grudgingly, and has since said little. He does not take her for more than a guest, and asks what she wants.

Year 2: asked at the hearing about the ford "The water told you? Which water, and what did it say?" and said a cord of what the town already knew was a cord of nothing, and did not stand against it. Stood apart at the short listening. In autumn said the cords were the Reckoner's now and he would say nothing against a cord that was the town's. Quieter. Leads the Wary. Does not take her for more than a guest, and still asks what she wants.
