# The mark cutter, a younger ferry keeper (unnamed)

A man of about 26 in Year 3 who cuts the flood marks and the low-water marks cleanest of the ferry keepers. Proud of a straight cut, easy to tease, quick on a pole.

Nature: eager, a little in awe of the guest and of the larger river, and does not say which more. Does not take her for more than a guest with a gift.

Year 2: walked with the guest to the ford, cut the summer mark with the cord walker standing by.
Year 3: named again. Went down the larger river three days with her and back, cut the marks at the ford, sat the watch by the sleeper. What he saw below the ford is as the Keeper gives it. Not a helper by the Keeper's rule unless the Keeper so rules.
Year 4: about 27. Named again. Went down the larger river five days with her and back, if the party came back, cut the summer mark at the ford and the autumn mark at the gate stone with the others. What he saw below the ford is as the Keeper gives it. Not a helper by the Keeper's rule unless the Keeper so rules.
