# The older ferry keeper of the ford landing (unnamed)

A woman of about 41 in Year 3, with hands knotted from the pole. She has worked the ford landing most summers of her life. Dry, exact, counts days by the pole and by her back, and does not like to be hurried or flattered.

Nature: practical, wary of big water, fond of the guest's questions about it. Does not take her for more than a woman with a good eye for water.

Year 2: named by the Ferry Elder to walk with the guest to the ford. Kept the landing for the six days there.
Year 3: named again. Said three days down and not four at the council, and held to it. Poled the barge down the larger river and back and sat the watch at the ford by the sleeper. What she saw below the ford is as the Keeper gives it. Not a helper by the Keeper's rule unless the Keeper so rules.
Year 4: about 42. Named again. Said she had said three last year because she knew three, and would go five if the water stayed low and no storm showed, and counted the days herself. Told the guest at the council that no runner could be had below the ford. Poled the barge down the larger river and back and sat the watch at the farthest reach, if the party was back. What she saw below the ford is as the Keeper gives it. Not a helper by the Keeper's rule unless the Keeper so rules.
Year 5: about 43. Not named to go. Kept the ford landing with the ford keepers while the party was on the road, said she knew the carriers from the ford and not from the road, took the town's side of the telling to the carrier family at the ford, and named the younger ferry keeper the better walker. Not a helper by the Keeper's rule unless the Keeper so rules.
