# Unnamed people of god-secrets: state at the end of Year 1

Campaign: sim-06. Written by the civ agent at the close of Year 1 (the year runs autumn to autumn). Last year's state was the Keeper's starting state of creation Night 3a.

population: about 5,040, in the walled town [f35] and on the marsh edges and the boats. Up about 40 from 5,000. About 205 born and about 170 died, the deaths weighted to the old and very young in the winter fever of the fifth month; a few dozen drovers' and boat families' kin settled in, a few left.

stores: the autumn harvest was ordinary and the granary was full at the start of the year. The count cried in month 2 showed a little under a twentieth short of the Warden's old book (rats, damp in the low bins, and grain gone on a carter's word without a stick), now written down with a cause. Salted fish and beer ordinary. Drawn down through the winter as usual. The new harvest is coming in at the year's end and looks ordinary.

treasure: middling and unchanged: the granary, the council's weights, scrap silver and bronze in many private chests. A little scrap silver came in from the stranger's food, bed and room, a few weights' worth.

trade: grain, reed work, salt fish and leather out; stock, wool and metal in, by river, track and coasting boat. Ordinary. The river gate bar grounded loaded boats twice in the spring, so some carriage was lost or slowed. The Boat Master took on one cargo weighing at the stranger's hand for the south.

strength: a standing watch of about 100 and a call-up of about 500; earth and timber walls. Unchanged. The Gate Warden had a quiet watch put on the stranger's room in the first month and relaxed it in the winter.

mood: busy and talkative, still a little sour over the dredging, which is sharper and not settled. A stir about the stranger who counts: half amused, half wary, and a joke that the books come right behind him. The council is divided five to four over his stool and has not moved from it.

## Keeper, Year 1, pass two
Nothing was found on the open ground in month 8: nothing lies hidden there. The stranger was back from the south-west inside the fortnight, before the year's end. No one entered his room on the dog's night.
