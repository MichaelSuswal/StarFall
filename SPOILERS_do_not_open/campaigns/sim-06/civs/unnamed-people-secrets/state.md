# Unnamed people of god-secrets: state at the end of Year 2

Campaign: sim-06. Written by the civ agent at the close of Year 2 (the year runs autumn to autumn). Last year's state was the civ agent's at the close of Year 1.

population: about 5,085, in the walled town [f35] and on the marsh edges and the boats. Up about 45 from 5,040. About 210 born and about 175 died; the winter fever of the fifth month was lighter than last winter's but took the old and the very young again. A few dozen kin of drovers and boat families settled in, a few left. Net of those, about 10 added.

stores: the autumn harvest was ordinary. The sacks counted in at the granary door over twenty-three days came to 31,400. The closing count of the whole store, old stock and new, was cried as the Warden's count; what the whole store showed is the Keeper's (see the report). Drawn down through the winter as usual. Salted fish and beer ordinary. The lads' sticks at the damp corner, to the end of the year: about 1,640 rat tails taken, 44 sacks pulled spoilt from the low bins. These sticks cover only the damp corner and the tails; the Warden holds that the rats take more than the tails show. The new harvest is coming in at the year's end and looks ordinary.

treasure: middling and unchanged: the granary, the council's weights, scrap silver and bronze in many private chests. A little scrap silver came in from the stranger's passage down the river (two weights) and his lodging. One league weight was turned up by a mason in the tanners' end and given to the Clerk. The boat families' debts to two large houses are larger than the council knows.

trade: grain, reed work, salt fish and leather out; stock, wool and metal in, by river, track and coasting boat. Ordinary. One loaded boat grounded at the river gate bar in spring, three boat-days lost and about a fifth of a load wetted. The Boat Master's string of three boats went down to the mouth in summer on the ordinary run and came back on it.

strength: a standing watch of about 100 and a call-up of about 500; earth and timber walls. Unchanged. The Gate Warden relaxed the quiet watch on the stranger's room in the fifth month, after the ration weighing showed the watch eats a thirty-eighth of the store's issue, where the council had reckoned a twenty-fifth.

mood: busier and a little warmer than last year, easier at the granary, which has been credited to the Warden, and less sour over the dredging, which is now argued from a count and still not settled. The council renewed the stranger's stool six to three. The town is uneasy about the sky: the star dimmed five times, eight since the stranger came, and the cups set on the river steps after such nights are more than any year before.

## Keeper, Year 2, pass two
- The closing count did not reach the far wall and was not squared [r-0297]; the cry was held.
- The stranger came back with the string on the ordinary run and was in the town for the month 11 night.
- On the walk through the store the far bins were found hollow [r-0299]; the Clerk was sent for. What the store holds against the roll is now known to the Warden, the Clerk and the stranger.
