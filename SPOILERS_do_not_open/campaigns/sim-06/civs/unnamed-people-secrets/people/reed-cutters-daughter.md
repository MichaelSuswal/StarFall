# The reed cutter's daughter (unnamed)

Filed by office of her own making; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 5.

## Nature
A woman of the marsh edge, grown, who keeps her father's house since he died in the winter of Year 3. Quiet and stubborn, and slow to give a thing she has not weighed.

## History
Her father, a reed cutter of about seventy, began a telling to the stranger on the granary bench in the winter of Year 2, about the marsh at night and where he stood once on the edge of it, and was stopped before it was done. He told her the Counter will not take a telling you do not mean to give. She came to him in month 6 of Year 3 and the stranger asked whether it was hers to give and whether she means someone to carry it.

## Year 5
- Month 12, eighth day: came to the granary yard bench and asked whether it was time she told what her father began. Was told the same as before, and that she is to keep it until she knows. Said she does not know yet. Told no one.
