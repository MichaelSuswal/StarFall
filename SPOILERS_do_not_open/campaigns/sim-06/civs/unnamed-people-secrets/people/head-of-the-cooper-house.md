# The head of the cooper house (unnamed)

Filed by office; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 3.

## Nature
About fifty-five years old in Year 3. Smooth, careful and fluent, a man who coopers and brews and has kept his house's name clean by never being first to speak. Keeps the far run's first bins for his barley by his house's old right. Sits at the council's long table.

## History
Set the widow of his second son's family to keep his books, both the open tally and the second. Keeps, with the tanners' house, a second tally against the boat families. Voted for the stranger's stool in Year 2 after being against it in Year 1.

## Year 3
- Month 1, third day: told by the Granary Warden at his door that the far run was hollow. Said the roll is wrong, that his house put its barley in that run every autumn before witnesses, and that he would send a keeper with pleasure. Sent the widow, his second tally keeper, since refusing would be remarked. Did not look at her when he told her.
- Month 2, fourth day: at the council said his house counts, that the council should look to the old Warden's time, and that he regretted the Warden had been served so by his uncle. When the east fields head moved a search of the Warden's house, did not second it, and looked at his hands.
- Does not take the stranger as a god.

## Standing
Smooth and not charged. Not trusted by the east fields house.
