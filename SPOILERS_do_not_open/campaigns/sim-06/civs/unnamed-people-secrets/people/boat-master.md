# The Boat Master (unnamed)

Filed by office; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 2.

## Nature
About forty-six years old in Year 2. Easy, curious and a trader to the bone, who asks of anyone what they have seen on the river. Sees what a count can do for his people and is not afraid of one.

## History
Holds word and goods along the river and the Boat Master's crews. Took the stranger's first weighing of a cargo for the south in Year 1. His sister's son steers the first boat of the summer string. A cousin's family's boat was the one that grounded at the river gate in the eighth month of Year 2.

## Year 2
- Month 7: gave the stranger, from his crews' sticks and memory, what last spring's two groundings cost, and let his crews sound the channel at the gate with notched poles at the spring high water and the summer low. Judged that a count of the bar might serve the boat families better than a quarrel does.
- Month 10: gave the stranger a berth as a weigher on the first boat of the string that went down at the end of the first market week, for two weights of scrap silver. Asked why; was told the stranger wanted to see who trades on the water below the town.
- Does not take the stranger as a god; he does not know of one. Takes his counsel in the course of trade.

## Standing
Liked by his crews and the Speaker, and thought too easy with strangers by some of the boat families. Sits at the council's long table by custom.

## Year 3 (added by the civ agent)
- Month 7: gave the stranger the first loaded boat at the gate, a boat of the cousin's family, and let his crews sound the bar at the spring high water and the dry-spell low water.
- Month 8: his cousin's family's boat and a farming house's hired boat grounded on the bar in the dry spell. Gave the Clerk the crews' figures for the two springs.
- Month 10: gave the stranger a berth on the upriver crew of the cousin's family for three weights of scrap silver. Asked why; was told he could take it or leave it. Told only the Clerk.
- Still takes the stranger's counsel in the course of trade and does not take him as a god.

## Year 4 (added by the civ agent)
- Month 7: gave the stranger the first loaded boat at the gate and had his crews sound the bar a third spring, high water and low.
- Month 8: three boats grounded in the dry spell. Gave the Clerk the crews' figures.
- Month 10: gave the stranger a berth on the upriver crew of the cousin's family for three weights of scrap silver, and told the Clerk the stranger means to be back before the harvest. Had charge of the dredging from the eighteenth day.
- Said at the council that the marsh was loud on the night of the star in month 8.
- Does not take the stranger as a god.
