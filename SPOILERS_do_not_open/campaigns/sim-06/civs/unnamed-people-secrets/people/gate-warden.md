# The Gate Warden (unnamed)

Filed by office; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 2.

## Nature
About forty-nine years old in Year 2. Cool, short and fair, a soldier of the town and no talker. Holds that a man with no name is a man to be watched. Does not owe favors and does not like to be seen to owe them.

## History
Keeps the standing watch of about a hundred and the call-up of about five hundred, fed from the common store. Put the quiet watch on the stranger's room in Year 1 and relaxed it in the winter of Year 1 and again in the fifth month of Year 2. Voted against the stool in Year 1 and Year 2.

## Year 2
- Month 4: took the stranger's offer of a cup in the market street, after four days' thought, and put his questions. Said afterward that the man answers like a man who has been asked before. Did not say he believed it.
- Month 5: let the stranger weigh the watch's rations. The watch draws a thirty-eighth of the store's issue, where the council had reckoned a twenty-fifth. Took the figure to the council as the store's own sticks, not as the stranger's, and said he owed no man a favor for it. Did not change his mind about the stranger.
- Month 6: asked at the council table whether anyone had been told what the nights of the star are.
- Said, in the tenth month, that the stranger's going down the river was the second sensible thing he had done.

## Standing
Hard, and proved fair. Sits at the council's long table by custom.

## Year 3 (added by the civ agent)
- Month 4: asked the stranger at the watch's fire, with the watch captain by, where the grain went. Was told the stranger did not ask and would not guess. Said it is the answer of a man who knows or of one who truly does not, and that the two sound alike.
- Month 7: said at the council that the nights of the star were coming faster.
- Month 9: asked what the stranger meant to do in the summer; said that his going up the river was the third sensible thing he had done and that he did not like to count it.
- Still does not take the stranger's word as a god's. Keeps the watch's rations at the figure the stranger gave.

## Year 4 (added by the civ agent)
- Month 4: said at the council that the nights of the star were three in the autumn and winter alone.
- Month 9: asked the stranger what he meant to do in the summer. Said he has lost count of the man's sensible acts and will not start again.
- Still does not take the stranger's word as a god's. Keeps the watch's rations at the stranger's figure.
