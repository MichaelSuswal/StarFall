# The Speaker, head of the tanners' house (unnamed)

Filed by office; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 4.

## Nature
About fifty-eight years old in Year 4. Dry, measured and plain-spoken, a leather dealer who keeps a house's business by never being owed what he cannot collect. Keeps, with the cooper house, a second tally against the boat families, and knows his own part of it and not the cooper house's. Holds the table to its rules and says so.

## Year 4
- Month 7: took the chair with the opening of the river, and said the table would hold to the dredging rule it made, because it made it.
- Month 9: put the rule, carried six to three. Said at the table that his house, with the cooper house, would lend the boat families their levy shares against next season's carriage.
- Month 10: his house lent first. The Clerk wrote it in his private book.
- Does not take the stranger as a god.

## Standing
Respected at the table, not loved. Not charged with anything.
