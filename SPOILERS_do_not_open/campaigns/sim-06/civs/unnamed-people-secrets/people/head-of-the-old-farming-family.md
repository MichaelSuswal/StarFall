# The head of the old farming family of the east fields (unnamed)

Filed by office; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 3.

## Nature
About seventy years old in Year 3. Old, loud and proud, the head of the oldest of the farming families, who have kept the first bins at the far wall since before the Warden's uncle's time. Quick to say he has been robbed. Believed more than he is liked. Sits at the council's long table.

## Year 3
- Month 1, third day: told by the Granary Warden at his door that the far run was hollow. Said the roll is wrong and the Warden's uncle's carters know why, that his house has been robbed or its grain misentered in his father's day or the uncle's, and that which of the two is the Warden's to find. Sent his brother's grandson, about nineteen, as his keeper.
- Month 1, sixth day: his keeper's stick ran two sacks short at one bin and was found wrong on recount. Said at home that the boy is no counter.
- Month 2, fourth day: at the council said the roll is wrong and his house has been robbed, and moved that the council search the Warden's house, the Warden's uncle's old house and the carters' yards. The motion had no second.
- Does not take the stranger as a god.

## Standing
Sore, not charged. Thought to be loud and not the whole of it.

## Year 4
- Month 1, third day: sent the grandson to the far run and a second grandson to the near bins. Said again his house was robbed.
- Month 2, fourth day: said it at the council again and was cut off by the Speaker. Said it is not finished.
- Month 6: said the Clerk's winter proves the carters left no stick.
