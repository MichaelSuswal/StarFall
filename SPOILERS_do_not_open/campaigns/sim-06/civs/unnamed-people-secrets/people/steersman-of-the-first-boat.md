# The steersman of the first boat of the summer string (unnamed)

Filed by office of his own making; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 2.

## Nature
About twenty-eight years old. The Boat Master's sister's son, a river man who has made the run to the mouth six times. Steady, quick with the poles, and not talkative with strangers.

## Year 2
- Month 10, the ninth day: took the stranger aboard the first of three planked boats as a weigher and counter with a crew of five, and said the old words of the boat families to the water, "Ivo Strand's water, Ivo Strand's fortune". The string is his uncle's and the run is the ordinary run: down to the mouth in two days, a fortnight at the families' landing, up in three.
- What he sees of the stranger on the run is the Keeper's.

## Standing
Not on the council. Will be asked about the mouth by the boat families when he is back.
