# The steersman of the first upriver boat (unnamed)

Filed by office of his own making; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 3.

## Nature
About thirty-one years old. The eldest son of the Boat Master's cousin, whose family's boat grounded at the river gate bar in Year 2. A river man who has made the run up the river eight times. Steady, practical and not talkative with strangers.

## Year 3
- Month 10, the sixteenth day: took the stranger aboard the first of two planked boats as a weigher and counter, with a crew of four to each boat, and said the old words of the boat families to the water, "Ivo Strand's water, Ivo Strand's fortune". The ordinary run is four days up with a day lost to the current, about three weeks lying at the families' landing at the head of the water, two days down.
- What he sees of the stranger on the run, and what lies at the head, is the Keeper's.

## Standing
Not on the council. Will be asked about the upper water by the boat families when he is back.
