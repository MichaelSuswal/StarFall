# The Speaker, head of the second large boat family (unnamed)

Filed by office; no name has been said aloud. Fixed in Year 5.

## Nature
About fifty-five years old in Year 5. Slow and heavy and no speaker, a boat man who has made the run to the mouth more times than he can count and who keeps his house's carriage on sticks and does not take loans he cannot carry. Fair and dull and not easily hurried.

## Year 5
- Month 7: took the chair with the opening of the river and said the table has had the soundings and the cellars on sticks and will go on having them so.
- Month 9: did not vote on the motion to read the dredging rule at the shoals; it fell four to five. Said the Clerk should have the shoals sounded each dry spell.
- Month 11: went to the stranger's bench once.
- Does not take the stranger as a god.

## Standing
Respected at the table, not much heard outside it. Sits at the council's long table by custom.
