# The Longwalk people of god-beasts: state at the end of Year 2

Rough figures from the people's own cords, not a census. Where a number turns on a ruling the Keeper holds (the spring wolves, the dry month, see years/002-report.md), it is given for the plain case, and the other branch is given after it.

population: about 5,070, one band on the move, now coming down from the winter woods toward spring. Births about 200, deaths about 175 (the old in winter, a few infants, and a flux at the fouled pools in the dry month that took about sixteen, twelve of them children). The band grew by about 25, less than last year, because of the flux and the lean year.

stores: dried and salted meat low again at the year's end, about as last spring; salt about three fifths of the year's wanting came in from the stone towns and is nearly spent, less than last year because fewer sound beasts went to pay for it; iron a few tools fewer than last year at the same price. The larders were filled enough from the autumn hunt, not well, and the fat laid on the herds in the fattening season was thin because of the dry month.

herds: about 4,330 cattle, 5,480 goats, 905 sheep, about 715 dogs (rough cords). Cattle thin but alive. Calves held about even; the dry month took about eighty cattle, thirty goats and twenty sheep; the cough took thirteen beasts in all (eight in spring, five in autumn) and did not go through the herds. Other branch (the dry month not held): about 4,050 cattle, 5,350 goats, 860 sheep.

treasure: the herds and the dogs; little metal; salt and iron bought at the stone towns' price, no cheaper than last year.

trade: out, sound sheep and goats, hides and felt and fewer cattle by one trade party to the stone towns in the fifth month; in, salt and iron at last year's higher price. No other trade. No stranger came to the fires.

strength: about 900 who can carry a spear, about 300 of them hunters by trade; no walls. The hunter gravely wounded last year walks with a stick and no longer hunts, and is counted out. No new wounds of note. No blow was struck in the winter camp.

mood: more watchful than last year, and the surface less unhurried. The dry month, the flux and the lean autumn have the band counting what the next dry month would do. The cutters farther in and the price of salt unchanged keep the worry about the thinning woods. The star has dimmed five times this year, more than last year, and this is talked of at every fire. The herders and the dog handlers speak of the keeper with something near trust, and say the herds slept better at the water. The council speaks of it with attention and no more. The Speaker is wary of leaning on it. The three clans that camp apart have come nearer this winter than last, after the dawn water and the month of the keeper at their margin, but still winter apart and are not reconciled. The Speaker's chest fever has the clans talking quietly of who follows him.
