# The Longwalk people of god-beasts: state at the end of Year 3

Rough figures from the people's own cords, not a census. Where a number turns on a ruling the Keeper holds (the spring wolves, and the word the party carried about the cutters, see years/003-report.md), it is given for the plain case, and the other branch is given after it.

population: about 5,105, one band on the move, now coming down from the winter woods toward spring. Births about 205, deaths about 170 (the old in winter, a few infants, and a flux at one pool in the fifth month that took three children). The band grew by about 35, a little more than last year, since no month was dry and the flux was small.

stores: dried and salted meat better than the last two years and not full; the summer's fat was laid on the herds and the autumn hunt found game as in most years. Salt about half of the year's wanting, less than last year, because no cattle went to the stone towns to pay for it and the price did not fall. Iron the same number of pieces as last year and greyer and softer, so that edges turn; the smiths of the stone towns blame their ore.

herds: about 4,550 cattle, 5,630 goats, 950 sheep, about 725 dogs (rough cords). Cattle gained by the calving and the ordinary summer and lost by the cough: about fifteen beasts of the two clans by the ninth month and about one in six of theirs, some hundred, in the winter. The cough stayed in their herds, which wintered apart on their own ground and browse. The wolves of the calving took no more than about six calves in the plain case. Other branch (the wolves not turned): about thirty calves lost, cattle about 4,525.

treasure: the herds and the dogs; little metal; salt and iron bought at the stone towns' price, no cheaper than last year, and the iron poorer.

trade: out, sound goats and sheep, hides and felt, no cattle, by one trade party to the stone towns in the fifth month, carrying also a word from the council to the towns about the first stands of the winter woods; in, salt about half of the wanting and iron greyer and softer, at last year's price. The party's own beasts were kept apart a month on its return. No beast was bought. No other trade. No stranger came to the fires.

strength: about 900 who can carry a spear, about 300 of them hunters by trade; no walls. Two hunters were hurt in the autumn hunt, one by a boar, neither gravely, both fit by winter. No blow was struck in the winter camp.

mood: tired and tighter than last year, with the surface still unhurried. The two clans lost a sixth of their cattle again and the rest of the band is afraid for its own. The cutters are a little farther in again and the iron is poorer, and the Marchwarden says the songs of the third stand are now untrue. The star dimmed six times, the most yet, and it is talked of at every fire; the apart clans count the band's heads against the nights. The council gave its Houndwarden leave to act on the keeper's word at the calving, once and for that alone, and the clans are divided on what that was: the herders and the dog handlers are warmer to the keeper, and the eldest head of the three clans refused it the month at his margin, saying the first thing had been given. The three clans still winter apart, no nearer and no farther than last year, and are not reconciled. The Speaker's second chest fever, a week long, has the clans' talk of who follows him louder, and no name has been said aloud.

## Keeper, Year 2, pass two
- The wolves of the calving were not turned on the three nights [r-0291]: about thirty calves lost; the numbers take that branch.
- The dry month: the turns at the river held [r-0292]; the numbers take the lighter branch (about eighty cattle, thirty goats, twenty sheep; the children of the flux as written).
- The cutters as the keeper told them: three gatherings with draught beasts, deeper in than the songs put them; no number.

## Keeper, Year 3, pass two
- The wolves of the calving were turned on all three nights. About six calves were lost. Your numbers take that branch.
- The council's word to the stone towns was heard and nothing was promised. The cutters were a little farther in again.
- What the keeper told the Marchwarden of the cutters: three gatherings of people with draught beasts, working and unafraid, a little deeper. No number.
- The keeper's readings give where a gathering is and whether it is hurt, afraid or resting. That a herd was drinking is its own guess, and it said so.
- The herder's question, what the cough is, had no answer.
