# Orrin Haddock, the Speaker

Of the Longwalk. Past sixty, about sixty three at the end of Year 1. Patient, well liked, stiff in the knees, walks slowly and so comes late to things and hears them whole. He keeps the council and carries its word to the clans. His gift is holding a quarrel without closing it.

History fixed in Year 1: he met the keeper at the margin of the rear camp some days after Tessaly, heard it, and told it only that a keeper that came to the edge of the fire would be heard at council like any elder and no more. He let the Houndwarden decide the night watch. He put the keeper to the council in the sixth month at the river camp and, when the council spoke at length and did not agree, took no vote, and left silence to stand. He does not hold the keeper to anything and takes no order of its. He is wary of what the band becomes if it learns to lean on a keeper, and has said so to Tessaly alone.

Year 2 (a dated line): in the first month he held the keeper's answer without closing it, saying the council would not send it from the edge and would not take its word, and that Tessaly might carry its costs. In the dry month he named a fresh Three at the Fire and gave the dawn water to the clans that came last. In the eleventh month a chest fever took him for two weeks and he lived, slower; the clans now talk quietly of who follows him. About sixty four at the end of Year 2.
