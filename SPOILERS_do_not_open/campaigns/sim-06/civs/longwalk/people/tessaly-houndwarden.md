# Tessaly, the Houndwarden

Of the Longwalk. In middle years, about forty five at the end of Year 1. Lean, watchful, slow to speak, close to the dogs; she reads a camp by what the dogs do. Born to a clan of dog breeders of the band, married into no other, mother of two grown children. She holds the dogs, the herds and the signs, and the cord count of bold beasts.

History fixed in Year 1: she was the first to meet the keeper, at the rear camp at the dusk of the fourth day of the year. She put down a hunter's raised spear with one low word. She let the keeper keep the night watch at the margin on her own word as Houndwarden, and carried its word to the Speaker. She keeps the cord of bold beasts at the keeper's asking, and reads it at council. She has not taken the keeper's word as an order of any kind and does not act in its name; she has agreed to a watch and a count, as between a Houndwarden and any who stands at the margin. Her view of it is a dog handler's: it is quiet, it does not hurry, it is not afraid of her dogs and her dogs are not afraid of it, and that is more than she can say of many people.
