# The Marchwarden

Of the Longwalk. Keeps the route, the fords and the song of the year, and notches the nights the star dims. His name is not in my record and none has been said aloud. Aged, a keeper of long verses, slow to give an opinion and quick to give a verse.

History fixed in Year 2: he asked the keeper in the sixth month whether the dimming of the star was its own and heard that it knew nothing of it. In the eighth month the keeper came to his fire at dusk and told him what it had counted of the stone towns' cutters in the winter woods, and asked whether the songs said they had come so far in before. He answered from the songs: the axe at the wood's edge in his grandfather's grandfather's day and never past the first stands, and the verse for the third stand says only that the axe has not been there. He has taken no word of the keeper's as an order. He took what it counted to the Speaker.

Year 3 (a dated line): in the eighth month the keeper came to his fire at dusk and told him what it read of the cutters; he asked whether it would have the council send men to turn them, and heard that this was the council's to say. He took it to the Speaker. He weighed the keeper's reading of the herds at the pool below the second ford against the verse of the ford in the fifth month and moved the camps. He has said in council that the verse for the third stand is now an old lie, and that he did not wish to live to see it. Notched six nights of the star dimming, fourteen since the keeper came.

Year 4 (a dated line): in the fifth month, on the keeper's first dusk reading of a hurt herd at the pool below the third camp, said by Tessaly to be little and a guess, he kept the herd from the pool one night, had the camps draw and boil their water above, asked for the next dusk's reading, and did not move the camp. In the eighth month the keeper came to his fire at dusk and told him what it read of the cutters; he asked whether the axe had passed the third stand and was told a little deeper than the last count. He took it to the Speaker and the Saltkeeper. Notched seven nights of the star dimming, twenty one since the keeper came, and says he cannot find a rule in them.

Year 5 (a dated line): in the second month the keeper told him at his fire what it read of the herds under the star on the two dimmed nights, and he put it with the notches. In the eighth month the keeper came to his fire at dusk and told him what it read of the cutters; he asked whether they had come in since the last count and took the answer to the Speaker and the Saltkeeper. In the twelfth month he asked the keeper at the margin whether the star dimming three times and not seven was its doing and was told it knew nothing of it. Notched three nights of the star dimming, twenty four since the keeper came, and says he can find no rule in the fall more than in the climb. Spoke the closing of a ring for the Speaker in the eleventh month. Says the verse of the third stand is an old lie still and not a worse one this year.
