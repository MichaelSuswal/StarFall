# The herder of the two clans

Of the Longwalk. A herder of the two clans whose cattle took the cough. His name is not in my record and none has been said aloud. Middle years, practical, plain in speech. He keeps a part of the two clans' herds and was among those who burned the dead.

History fixed in Year 3: in the tenth month he asked Tessaly at the margin whether the keeper could say what the cough was.

Year 4 (a dated line): in the first month he stood at the edge of the ring and heard the keeper's answer, that it could not say what the cough was, that it knew a herd was hurt in part and not with what or which beasts, and that it had come hard to the two clans' cattle that wintered apart and no other herd had taken it. He said he had asked for a name and been given a smaller thing than a name, and that a keeper that listens over a herd and cannot say which beast is hurt is a dog that points at the flock. Tessaly said a dog that points has still told you to look. In the tenth month he came to the margin and asked it to read his herd at dusk each few nights and say which gathering of it stood hurt in part, and used what it said: he found beasts coughing in the gatherings it named. He says in his clan that a clan with a dog that points at a flock does well to go and look. The band has taken up his saying. He has not taken the keeper's word as an order.
