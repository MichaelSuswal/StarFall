# The Still One

Of the Longwalk, born to one of its clans, which calls her by that name. An old woman, past seventy, small, slow among her own, seldom spoken to. By her small gift, at a cost she pays, she can sit by a herd at dusk and tell its mood and its trouble. On the day after, she does not eat, is slow for some days, and the young are wary of her. She trains no one and the gift ends with her.

History fixed in Year 1: on the first of the two dimmed nights of the first month she sat by her clan's cattle and said afterward only that the herd was listening to something and was not afraid. She has not met the keeper to speak with. She watches it from the edge of the firelight on some nights, and has said to one granddaughter that a keeper at the margin is not the thing she feared when she was a girl. She has told no one what she thinks it is.

Year 2 (a dated line): in the dry month she sat by the herds at the thinnest water on three nights at the Houndwarden's asking and did not eat the day after each, and was slow among her own for a week after. She said of the first herd that it was afraid of the others and not of the thirst. The young are wary of her again. She has still not spoken with the keeper. About seventy two at the end of Year 2.

Year 3 (a dated line): in the eleventh month she sat by the herds of the two clans on two nights at their asking, did not eat the day after each, and was slow among her own for some days. She said the herd was not afraid and not listening but tired in the chest. The young are wary of her again. She has still not spoken with the keeper. About seventy three at the end of Year 3.

Year 4 (a dated line): in the eleventh month she sat by the herds of the two clans on one night at their asking, did not eat the day after, and was slow among her own for some days. She said the herd was tired in the chest still, and less than last year. The young are wary of her again. She has still not spoken with the keeper. About seventy four at the end of Year 4.

Year 5 (a dated line): in the eleventh month she sat by the herds of the two clans on one night at their asking, did not eat the day after, and was slow among her own for some days. She said the herd was tired in the chest still, and about as last year. She did not sit by a herd on either dimmed night of the second month. The young are wary of her again. She has still not spoken with the keeper. About seventy five at the end of Year 5.
