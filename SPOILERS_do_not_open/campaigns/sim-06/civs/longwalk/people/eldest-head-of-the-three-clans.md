# The eldest head of the three clans that camp apart

Of the Longwalk. Old, proud, angry since last winter's judgment against his clan. His name is not in my record and none has been said aloud. He sits in the council for the three clans and camps apart from the main band.

History fixed in Year 2: in the first month, at the first storms, he heard the keeper's answer to what he had said before it, carried by the Houndwarden, and said only that small things are the first things. In the dry month his clans' streams stopped and his herds came last to the river; the Speaker gave them the dawn water for ten days, and he said it was fair and did not thank him. In the fifth month he gave the keeper leave to keep the margin of his camp for one month on three conditions: no ring, no word carried either way, and gone at the month's end. He came to the edge of the light on the twentieth night and asked it two things. His clans winter apart again, nearer than last year. He has not taken the keeper's word and does not trust it, and he has let it stand at his edge.

Year 3 (a dated line): in the fourth month the Speaker put the keeper's asking for a second month at his margin; in the fifth month, after the trade party had gone, he said no, saying the council had given the keeper a thing his people had not and that a keeper kept in pieces is a keeper taken. In the first month he had said in the ring that he did not stand against the leave given the Houndwarden, and that it was the first thing. His clans again winter apart, no nearer and no farther than last year. He has not taken the keeper's word.

Year 4 (a dated line): in the first month, hearing the leave for the wolves asked a second time, he said in the ring that he did not stand against it, that a thing given once is a leave and a thing given twice is a custom, and asked that it be said he had said so. The keeper's margin at his camp was not asked of him this year. His clans again winter apart, no nearer and no farther than last year, and took the Speaker's cord for fuel wood. A young herder of his clans, sent by his own clan and not by him, asked the keeper in the eighth and tenth months whether the star dims for the band's numbers. He has not taken the keeper's word.

Year 5 (a dated line): in the first month he heard the keeper's answer to his young herder in the ring and said it had now said twice that it wanted the band many, and that "a body keeps one margin and not two" was said to him, since he had refused it a second month. In the first month he said again that a custom given twice is a custom, and at the river camp in the sixth month that given three times it is a law. The keeper's margin at his camp was not asked of him this year. His clans wintered apart, a little nearer than last year, within sight of the main camp's smoke. He says nothing is settled, that the band will be counted again at the next going down, and that if the star dims as before he will speak of parting in the ring and will not ask the keeper's leave. He has not taken the keeper's word.
