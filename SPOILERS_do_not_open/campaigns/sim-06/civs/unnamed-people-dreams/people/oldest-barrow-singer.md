# The oldest of the barrow singers

Unnamed. No mortal has said her name aloud in play.

A very old woman of the lower town, the last who had the third song whole. (Earlier files of mine wrote "he"; the Keeper's line in the Year 4 release says "her", and that is what happened. This file follows it.) Said again at the winter tables of Year 2 and again of Year 3 that the young cannot keep it. In Year 2 two young households sat with her through the winter and had half of it by the thaw; in Year 3 they sat again and had a little more, and in month 12 she sang it through to them at her own hearth and said it was as far as she could give it. They had it by two parts in three. She was failing. The songs stood at three of five. No one had asked the guest about them.

Year 4: died on the eleventh day of month 3, at dawn, of her age, in her own house of the lower town, which had not kept the guest's flame. The two young households sat the night with her and she tried to give them the last part of the third song and her voice would not hold it. No one asked the guest to walk her last sleep. She was laid in the burying ground at the foot of the barrows, and the first and second songs were sung over her whole and the third in its two parts, stopping at the gap. The last third of the third song is in no living mouth.
