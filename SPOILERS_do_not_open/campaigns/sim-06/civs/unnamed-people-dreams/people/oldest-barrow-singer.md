# The oldest of the barrow singers

Unnamed. No mortal has said his name aloud in play.

A very old man of the lower town, the last who has the third song whole. Said again at the winter tables of Year 2 and again of Year 3 that the young cannot keep it. In Year 2 two young households sat with him through the winter and had half of it by the thaw; in Year 3 they sat again and had a little more, and in month 12 he sang it through to them at his own hearth and said it was as far as he could give it. They have it by two parts in three. He is failing. The songs stand at three of five. No one has asked the guest about them.
