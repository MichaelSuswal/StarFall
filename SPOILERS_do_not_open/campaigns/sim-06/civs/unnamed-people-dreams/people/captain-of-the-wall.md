# The captain of the wall

Unnamed. No mortal has said his name aloud in play.

A man of the watch, the Warden of the Wall's captain, about forty-five winters at the end of Year 4. Told every man who sits the guest's door, before his first night, in her words, what he sits against. In month 1 of Year 3, in the yard of the watch, asked the guest how a man is to know the standing body from the sleeping one, and in month 1 of Year 4 heard her answer: that her body stands and does not lie down when she walks a dream, and that a hind lying down is not her in a dream. He said to her that the wall has seen her lie still on lot nights for two winters and written it asleep, and asked what the wall has been sitting; he has no answer of hers. Had the way from the lean-to to the south-east gate staked and kept clear, and had the four walk it by day with a cord. He said to the Warden of the Wall on the way back from the yard in Year 3 that he did not think that was the whole of it, and he has not changed his mind.
