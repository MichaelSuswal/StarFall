# The Warden of the Door

Unnamed. No mortal has said his name aloud in play.

An old man, about seventy winters at the end of Year 1 (a year older than at its start). Keeper of the empty place at the table and its lamp, an office he has held for most of his grown life. He has named no successor and told the seat in the last month of summer that he will name one before the first frost of Year 2. Patient, courteous, a little deaf, and quicker to act than he looks. He kept the lamp lit at his own hearth for the awaited guest through every year of his office, and when she came he met her at the gate on foot and carried the table out to her himself rather than carry her in.

Year 1: met the guest at the gate, gave her the custom's words, took her for the awaited one on the third night and said so to the throne alone, spoke for her to the court, asked that the court's hearth be lit, took her to the Night Sitter. He is the guest's first helper among the offices. He does not repeat what she says about herself.
