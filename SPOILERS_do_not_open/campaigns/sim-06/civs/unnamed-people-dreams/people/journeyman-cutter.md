# The journeyman cutter of the harbor party

Unnamed. No mortal has said his name aloud in play.

A man of about thirty-four winters, the old cutter's best hand at dressing. Chosen by the old cutter in month 2 of Year 3 to go with the court's party to the harbor and read what the house wants of the scar's stone, since the cutters cut to what their hands know and not to any cut they have not seen. Carries the old cutter's two dressed pieces as samples. Goes by the leader's word on the way and means to speak for the stone and for nothing else. What came of the attempt is the Keeper's.
