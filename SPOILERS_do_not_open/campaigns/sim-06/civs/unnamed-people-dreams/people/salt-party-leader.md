# The salt party's leader

Unnamed. No mortal has said his name aloud in play.

A man of about forty winters. The Steward of the Store's chief cord-keeper of the trade goods: counts the carriers' loads at the gate each season and knows to the pot what they ask. Careful, exact, not at ease with a boat or with gods. Chosen by the Steward in month 2 to lead six to the harbor town west along the coast where the salt is boiled. Has the guest's message by heart. Means to take the court's boat down the river and go on by the coasting ship if one is at the mouth, or on foot with the linen and oil if not, and will not put the river boat to sea. Set out in the third week of month 2. What came of the attempt is the Keeper's, in the report.

Year 3 (end): about forty-one winters. Asked the Steward to send another and was told the factor had seen his face; went. Led eight in two planked boats with stone, linen, pots and oil to the harbor town west along the coast, set out on the twenty-fifth day of month 2, with the guest's words by heart from three evenings in the Warden of the Door's house. Means to take the boats down to the mouth and the coasting ship on, or leave the stone with the boatmen and go on foot with the samples. Binds the court to nothing. What came of the attempt is the Keeper's.

Year 4 (end): about forty-two winters. Chosen again by the Steward; did not ask to be replaced; the Steward said a man who brings a thing home whole from the quays is a man who listens. Asked for a better boat for the bars and was given a third boat. Led nine in three planked boats with sixty blocks of stone, linen, pots and oil, and the guest's words by heart from three evenings in the Warden of the Door's house, set out on the twenty-fourth day of month 2. Noted on the first evening that the words put the town four days down the river from the mouth when the town is four days up, and will say four days up if asked where the bend lies. The seat allowed him to go on with two runners by the coast if the factor offers a road he judges safe. What came of the attempt is the Keeper's.
