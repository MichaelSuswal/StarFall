# The salt party's leader

Unnamed. No mortal has said his name aloud in play.

A man of about forty winters. The Steward of the Store's chief cord-keeper of the trade goods: counts the carriers' loads at the gate each season and knows to the pot what they ask. Careful, exact, not at ease with a boat or with gods. Chosen by the Steward in month 2 to lead six to the harbor town west along the coast where the salt is boiled. Has the guest's message by heart. Means to take the court's boat down the river and go on by the coasting ship if one is at the mouth, or on foot with the linen and oil if not, and will not put the river boat to sea. Set out in the third week of month 2. What came of the attempt is the Keeper's, in the report.
