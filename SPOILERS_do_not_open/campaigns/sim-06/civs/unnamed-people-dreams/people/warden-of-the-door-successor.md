# The new Warden of the Door

Unnamed. No mortal has said her name aloud in play.

A woman of about forty winters at the end of Year 2, of a storytelling household of the lower town. Kept the empty place's lamp beside the old Warden of the Door for four years and was at the table he carried out on the first morning. Keeps her ancestors' niche from the old flame and, since the winter of Year 1, the guest's flame in her house also; says she will keep both. Quiet, careful, a good teller. The elder of the Gate Bench who is against the guest said a woman who keeps two lamps may keep two minds.

Year 2: named in month 3, a few days before the first frost; carries the guest's asks and walks beside her from the first frost; the seat named the office hers at the winter's first dusk. Took the guest to the Night Sitter in month 5. Carried the Night Sitter's pupil's asking in month 6. She has said she carries what she is given faithfully and will hold her own counsel on what the guest is until she has heard her herself. At the year's end she has not said she takes her for the awaited. The old man is alive, at his lamp, and counsels her.

Year 3 (end): about forty-one winters. Carried the guest's asks all year: to the yard of the watch, the Keeper of the Count, the Night Sitter's work yard, the four houses of the western party, and the hamlets. Told the guest in month 7 that the four who went west had been home for two seasons. Taught the harbor party's leader the words in her house, three evenings, in month 2. Named the three nights of three households to the Keeper of the Count the day before. Told the seat in month 10 that she was nearer than she was; she has not said she takes the guest for the awaited.
