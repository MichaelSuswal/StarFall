# The seat

Unnamed. No mortal has said her name aloud in play.

A widowed woman, about forty-six winters at the end of Year 1, of the seated family, twelve winters on the throne at the year's end (eleven at its start). Patient, well liked, slow to speak and not slow to decide. She holds the throne in the god's name by the line's own belief and does not claim the guest's voice or her word. She speaks to the whole people from the wall at dusk.

Year 1: heard the offices one at a time, let the guest stay on the court's bread after three nights, spoke the guest's custom from the wall (asks only where asked, none shamed), lit the court's hearth from the guest's lamp in late autumn and then said she took the guest for the awaited one. She is the people's face to the guest and the court's brake on any hurry.

Year 2 (end): about forty-seven winters, thirteen on the throne. Spoke the guest's answer about the star from the wall in month 1 and added that the Count's door would be open on the first of each month. Heard the salt asked in month 2 and sent six, refusing the Warden of the Wall's two men on the Steward's word. Gave leave for the wall to hold the guest's door. Named the new Warden of the Door's office hers at the winter's first dusk. Held to her line: does not claim the guest's voice.

Year 3 (end): about forty-eight winters, fourteen on the throne. Spoke from the wall in month 1 that a flame a household lights from its own kept flame is a shrine. Heard the harbor's offer in month 2 and accepted it on the twelfth day, holding the bargain as the town's and not the guest's. Refused the Warden of the Wall's two men again. Heard the new Warden of the Door say in month 10 that she was nearer.
