# The two young households of barrow singers

Unnamed. No mortal has said a name aloud in play.

Two households of the lower town, one of a kept house and one not, who sat with the oldest of the barrow singers through two winters and hold two parts in three of the third song. Year 5, month 1, day 9: heard the guest at the foot of the barrows say she could not have walked the old singer's sleep and would not have taken a song from it, and sang her the two parts, and she listened to the end. Heard in month 3 that no singer in any hamlet holds the third part; old people remember the first words of its second turn, and an old woman at the farthest hamlet wept and said her mother sang it at the harvest. In the summer sent to ask the guest to hear the song again with a verse from that old woman; the question waits on the Warden of the Door's cord. The last third is in no living mouth.
