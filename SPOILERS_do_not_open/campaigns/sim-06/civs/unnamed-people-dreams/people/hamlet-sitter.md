# The hamlet girl, the third sitter

Unnamed. No mortal has said her name aloud in play.

A young woman of a hamlet, about eighteen at the end of Year 4, her household's field hand, one of the three who sat the winter of Year 3 beside the Night Sitter and the pupil, and the one who found she could. In Year 4, month 4, she steadied a child's breathing on her own for a night with the Night Sitter asleep in the next room, and has done so since. Like the Night Sitter she cannot sleep for three nights after a sitting and her hands shake. On month 4, day 22, at the guest's asking through the Warden of the Door, she stood before the seat in the court yard with her mother and father and asked for what she needs: a bed in the lower town on the nights she sits, because the road home is a morning's walk; and that she sit no more than two nights in ten. The seat gave her the bed in the Night Sitter's house, the court's bread on the nights she sits and the three after, and the rule that no sitter of the three sits two nights running. Quiet, plain, not afraid of the work as the pupil is. Says a sitter is not taught.
