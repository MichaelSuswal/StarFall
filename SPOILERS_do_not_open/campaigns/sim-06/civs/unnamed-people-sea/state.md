# Unnamed people of god-sea: state at the end of Year 1

Campaign: sim-06. Written by the civ agent. The year closes at the opening of autumn, before the last catch is counted.

population: about 2,011. Births about 72 (a usual year for 2,000 on a lean coast), deaths about 61 (chest fevers among the old in winter, a thin spring among the old and the very young, a few accidents), none drowned. If the late boat of the fourth month was lost [KEEPER], three of the five aboard drowned and the figure is about 2,008.

stores: the racks and the salt loft hold ordinary stock for the winter ahead; the shared store of the lean time was spent and is refilling. Grain is thin: twelve loads came in from the coasting boats. Sound oak planks for two hulls' keels and some planking are in the yard. Why: the last catch was cut by about ten days of early hauling, the lean was shared evenly so nobody went short, and the summer fishing was good.

treasure: bronze in the court's chest unchanged; the old silver a few pieces lighter, paid for sound timber. Nets and boats as before, with one new keel of sound timber in the point ferryman's boat [KEEPER] and the planking in store. Why: trade was by barter for the most part, and the only coin out was for oak.

trade: salt fish, salt and rope out; wool, mutton, a little grain and the first sound timber in a few years in. The shepherds came twice, the coasting boats from the Iss Levels [r07] three times in the one summer. Why: an ordinary year, with the timber bought because the Master of Timber had a reason to buy.

strength: about 150 in the spring-drilled levy, drilled in the eighth month; the walls and the harbor chain as before. The chain is kept by a truce: the Harbormaster answers for raising and lowering it, and a lamp-tender of the line holds its key and writes each use in the harbor-book. Why: no fighting, no loss to the levy, and the quarrel over the chain was given a form without being ended.

mood: patient and watchful as before, but divided. In the court, the Warden-Monarch and the Master of Timber are quietly hopeful; the Harbormaster does what the court orders and does not believe the word; the Eldest Midwife waits. Among the people at large, curious and a little afraid: a figure on the shore at night, a bell that was answered, three dimmed nights, and the old woman at the east lamp saying the lowest step has a listener. Uneasiness about the lost boats of last year has eased: no boat was lost in the year outside the one [KEEPER] case, and the storms did no harm. Why: the early hauling and the quiet spring turned a year that might have been resentful into one that is simply puzzled.

## Keeper, Year 1, pass two
The late boat in the swell (month 4) came in with all five aboard [r-0249]. Population stands at the figure the year file gives for that branch, 2,011. The harbor mouth lay calm under the god's lamp that night.
