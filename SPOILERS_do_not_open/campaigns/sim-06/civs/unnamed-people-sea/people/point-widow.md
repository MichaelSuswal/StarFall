# The widow of the point

Unnamed. The widow of the master of the decked boat of the point that broke at the harbor mouth on the twentieth day of the eighth month of Year 4. About forty-four in Year 5. Her husband and her elder son drowned and have stones on the empty cairn. The younger son lives with her.

## History (fixed)
- Year 4, month 8: the boat broke; she left no bread on the lowest step and said so.
- Year 5, month 1: stood apart from the court at the cairn and did not look toward the lamp-house side.
- Year 5, month 3: told the Master of Timber she would not have a hand put to a keel that was the lamp's; was told the oak and keel were the yard's; did not ask whether any other hand would lie on it. Said her younger son would build the boat with the yard's men, that she would set no hand to it and forbid none.
- Year 5, month 4: heard the court's rule name the point. Said they named the point because it had lost one.
- Year 5, month 10: did not come to the slip when the new boat was launched. Left no bread.
- Year 5, month 11: stood behind the ferryman on the quay head through the swell. See state.md for the two ways.

## Nature
Hard, plain, silent in company. Holds that a lamp is a lamp and a rule is a rule and that neither brings a man home.
