# The widow of the east cove

Unnamed. A woman of about seventy-six in Year 4. Lived in the second house above the east cove landing, a short drag from the water, with her door on the shingle. Her husband was lost to the sea thirty years before and has a stone on the empty cairn. Two daughters work the fish-houses.

## History (fixed)
- Year 4, month 10: past knowing four days with a wasting in the chest. The Eldest Midwife named her bed for the keeping's visit, once, at the lowest tide at dusk; the daughters were sent to their aunt's across the town. She died in the grey before dawn with the Midwife's hand in hers. She did not know the god was there. Dead.

## Nature
Plain, unspeaking in company, one who put a stone on the cairn each year.
