# The Warden-Monarch

Unnamed. A woman of fifty-two in Year 1. Of the line of lamp-wardens, the family that has kept the harbor lamp and the harbor-book; sacred for the old promise and the lamp.

## History (fixed)
- Daughter of the Warden-Monarch before her. Her mother died of lung-rot when she was thirty, and she took the lamp and the throne that year. Twenty-two years on the throne.
- Every autumn of her rule she has hung the sealed jar on the lowest step and struck the bell once, as her house's custom requires, and has had no answer in twenty-two years. She came to the custom as a form of manners, the thing one does.
- Has one grown daughter, twenty-seven, her heir, who tends the lamp on storm nights.
- Her husband is dead, of a fever in his fortieth year, some twelve years ago.

## Nature
Quiet, exact, stubborn and plain, a keeper of books and of habits. Dislikes show and is not easily moved by rumor. She keeps still when frightened. She has kept the lamp lit for twenty-two years and holds that to be her whole office. She is aware that the line's standing against the Harbormaster over the chain is thin, and does not enjoy that her new word makes it easier.

## Year 1
- Met the god at the tide-door in the second month, kept still, was shown its shape, took its word on the stick it answered with. Told the inner court, giving the name Skerr. Has told no one all she saw.
- Brought the Master of Timber to the quay for the god's first work on timber and for the second.
- Settled the chain's truce with the Harbormaster in spring.
- A helper of the god by taking its word, by the Keeper's record.

## Aging
One year older each year. Her heir is the line's next.

## Year 2
- Month 1: set the jar and struck the bell; was told by the god at the tide-door that it does not know the name Brennis, wants the eldest's telling, and will not take the bed. Kept the Brennis answer from the court and told only her heir (month 4).
- Months 1 to 3: asked the eldest, one by one, for the telling of the promise, and told it to the water at the tide-door in month 3.
- Month 4: chose the old woman at the east lamp and the boy as tenders of the keeping's lamp.
- Month 5: stood at the water's edge in the swell. [Standing outside the court depends on the boat: see state.md.]
- Fifty-three at the year's end.
