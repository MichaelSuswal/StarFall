# The senior Captain

Unnamed. The elder of the two Captains, who hold the boats and the muster of about one hundred and fifty.

## History (fixed)
- Year 2: saw the figure at the harbor mouth in the swell and said nothing more.
- Year 3, month 5: saw it again at the mouth, closer, with the Captains' boat standing by. Went to the Warden-Monarch in the lamp-house the next morning and asked whose lamp it was. Was told: the keeping's, that it asks nothing of the levy, and that the Captains were left out for no reason but that nobody asked. Said he would tell the levy nothing. Did not say he took its word. Knows the keeping is real from the Warden-Monarch's mouth.
- Month 8: took the Captains' boat and the Harbormaster to the chain's line for the lead and grapnel and asked nothing of why.

## Nature
Steady, sparing of words, a man who obeys a plain order.

## Year 4
- Month 7: told by the Warden-Monarch that the keeping would be gone from the water for some weeks and the lamp would stay in her keeping. Asked whether the lamp would be lit if a boat were caught; was told she did not know.
- Month 8: stood by at the mouth in the Captains' boat in the swell; looked along the strand and the water's edge for the figure and saw none; told the Warden-Monarch so in the morning. Kept it from the levy. See state.md for the two ways.
