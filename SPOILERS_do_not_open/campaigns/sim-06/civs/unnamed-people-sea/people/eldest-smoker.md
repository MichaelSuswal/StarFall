# The eldest of the smokers

Unnamed. A man of about eighty-eight, a smoker of fish, of the smokers' lane on the east side of the lowest step.

## History (fixed)
- Gave the Warden-Monarch a telling of the promise in Year 2. Lived in the third door up the smokers' lane, the last house before the racks, with a granddaughter.
- Year 3, month 2: past knowing three days with a wasting in the chest. The Eldest Midwife named his bedside for the keeping's visit, once, at the lowest tide at dusk. He died in the grey before dawn with her hand in his. He did not know the god was there. Dead.

## Nature
Slow, wry, a smoker's cough.
