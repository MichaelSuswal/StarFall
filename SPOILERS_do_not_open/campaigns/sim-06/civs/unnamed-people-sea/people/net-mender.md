# The oldest net-mender

Unnamed. A woman of about eighty in Year 3, the oldest of the town's net-menders. Not the net-mender who calls fouled lines free, who is a younger woman and was ashore in the swells.

## History (fixed)
- Mends nets in the loft above the racks. Her mother and her mother's mother told her the telling of Low Garrow, and they did not agree whether the promise is older or younger than it.
- Year 2: gave the Warden-Monarch the telling of Low Garrow, and her mother's saying about the promise.

## Nature
Dry, unhurried, mends while she talks. Does not claim to know what she was told.

## Year 3
- Month 7: asked by the Warden-Monarch, for the keeping, where the old tellers say Low Garrow lay. Said it lay at the end of the causeway where the cut stones go under at the lowest tides; that her mother and her mother's mother do not agree whether past the last stones or along the road's near end; that the stones lie five days on foot round the shore by the old count; that the stones were square; and that those the sea spared walked the other way and founded the harbor town. Said she did not know it was so.

## Aging
One year older each year.
