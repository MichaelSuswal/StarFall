# The boy at the west end of the quay

Unnamed. A boy of about fourteen in Year 2. A lamp-tender at the west end of the quay.

## History (fixed)
- A fisher's son of the town. Quick, quiet, a runner of oil and errands.

## Nature
Watches more than he says. Asks the one question and then keeps it.

## Year 2
- Month 4: chosen by the Warden-Monarch as one of two tenders of the keeping's lamp. Asked whose it was, was told, and said nothing more. Was seen after to look at the water at dusk.
- Month 5: in the swell, ran oil from the lamp-house to the water's edge, fell on the quay stones, bruised his knee, did not stop.

## Aging
One year older each year.

## Year 3
- Month 5: ran oil from the lamp-house to the water's edge through the swell and did not fall. Kept the lamp with the old woman.
- Fifteen at the year's end.
