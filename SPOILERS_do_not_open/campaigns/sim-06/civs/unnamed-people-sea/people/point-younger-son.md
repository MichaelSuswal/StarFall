# The younger son of the point

Unnamed. Sixteen when the decked boat of the point broke in Year 4, hauled out by the Captains' boat; seventeen in Year 5. Son of the widow of the point. His father and his elder brother drowned.

## History (fixed)
- Year 4, month 8: hauled out of the water by the Captains' boat.
- Year 5, month 5 on: at the point's keel every day from the first light, with the yard's men and the hired hand, building the new boat.
- Year 5, month 10: left a heel of bread on the lowest step the morning the new boat first went out, with no word said. His mother has not.
- Year 5, month 11: stood on the quay head with the ferryman and did not put out in the swell.

## Nature
Quiet and quick. Does things before he says why.
