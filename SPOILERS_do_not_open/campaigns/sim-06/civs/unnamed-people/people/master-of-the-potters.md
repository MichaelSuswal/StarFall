# The master of the potters (Wynne Daub): what the god could not witness

Name from the Keeper's list. Not said in the Weightward's hearing.

**Year 3, month 8.** She had the two sticks read to her by the Tally-keeper pair after pair and sat with them four nights. She had worked out for herself what her clay loses in the fire: between one part in twelve and one part in fourteen of a piece's dry weight, piece by piece, about one part in thirteen over the load, and that pieces weighed alike before the fire do not come out alike. She said it to nobody. She kept to herself the margin she shaped with.

**Year 3, month 9, the shut sitting.** She did not vote on the cost of the spares, which were hers to shape. She voted for cutting the second stick for the carrier, in the five.

**Year 4, month 3.** The downs clay cracked a load in three at the kilns that fire it, and she told her boys to say nothing. Orrin came to her door with a tile and his own stick and asked plainly whether pieces weighed alike come out alike. She said no. He asked about the downs clay. She said it was worse and that the cracking worries her more each year. She said she had not the weight for it. He said he had a thirteenth and she had the same one. She said she supposed so. She told nobody afterward.
