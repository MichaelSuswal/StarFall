# The old net-maker of the door-stone: what the Weightward could not see

The public file is `campaigns/sim-06/civs/unnamed-people/people/net-maker-of-the-door-stone.md`.

Her stone has lain at her door since before she married into the house, and at her husband's mother's door before that. She believes it came up from the old quay in her husband's grandfather's time, for the cost of the carrying. She does not know and does not mind. It is a door-stone. It holds the door against the Alder when the water comes over the step. Nothing was ever cut on it.

She refused four offers for it because the door would blow in without it. She put a grandson on it at night because a boy had chipped it with a nail, not because she believes it is anything. The neighbor who caught the boy is her sister's husband. Her son-in-law is the captain who told her of the Weightward's asking. He thought it might be the seventh and said so to his wife and not to the landing.

She knows nothing of the old Weightward's seventh, of the clerk or of the punt.
