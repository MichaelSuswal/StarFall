# The head of the raking household: what the Weightward could not see

The public file is `campaigns/sim-06/civs/unnamed-people/people/head-of-the-raking-household.md`.

The household borrowed the clean salt to make good the sixth in the sixth month of Year 2 from the head of salt's own store. That is why he took their part and said a short measure and a cheat are different things. They repaid it by the last raking of the autumn of Year 3. He told nobody, and neither did she.

The night before the Weightward came she did not sleep. Her husband's brother said it would find clean salt and it would make no difference, since the town had made up its mind a year ago. She said then let the town's mind be made up with the baskets in front of it. After it went she sat by the shed with her hands in her lap and then sent her younger son to the head of salt with word that it was done. He said, "Good."

She has nothing hidden and nothing to boast of. She does not forgive being looked at and she does not regret it.
