# Sealed report to the Keeper: the stretch from the fourteenth day of the seventh month of Year 3 to the fourteenth day of the tenth month of Year 4, civ unnamed-people

Public report: `campaigns/sim-06/civs/unnamed-people/years/005-report.md`. Sealed year file: `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-06/civs/unnamed-people/years/005.md`. Everything the god could not witness is in the sealed year file and the sealed people files. The public files do not say that anything was asked, told or known in private.

## What I drew from the sealed side release, and how

- **The seventh weight.** Unchanged in the stretch. Nobody asked the four plainly, so none spoke of it. The Assay-master's lamp and the stair are the only trace, and both are sealed.
- **The carrier's voyage.** Taken as written: the quay, the word said as cut, no price, no hull of the southern house, no answer to carry home. What he said at the post is public in his words. What he heard on the quay he told the Stallreeve and the Assay-master at his landing house that night and the ring at dusk on the ring's day, in the sealed file only.
- **The eleven pieces.** They read 12, 24, 36, 48, 72, 84, 96, 120, 144, 168 and 180, each to a hair, only because the Assay-master asked the Weightward to call them at the ring's day. The figures are public from the moment it called them.
- **The master of the potters.** Her working-out of the fire loss is in the sealed file. She said it only to Orrin at her door, to a plain asking, with the downs clay worry. Orrin's own thirteenth is public, from the sticks he asked the Weightward to read.
- **The southern coaster's master.** I gave her the coaster, the twelfth, the landing's asking and her place at the head of the line. Nothing of what she is, wants or says. The carrier recognises the hull and holds the name.
- **Names.** Used in sealed files only.

## Things I decided that the Keeper may overrule

- The votes and reasons of every shut sitting: the cost of the spares (7, 3, 1), the tie on cutting the second stick for the carrier (5, 5, 1), the ring's day order, the winter sitting, the term sitting. The public report has only the counts the Stallreeve brought.
- That the ring did not send for the Weightward in any sitting and nobody called a sitting on the seat.
- That the carrier told the Stallreeve and the Assay-master first and the ring after the day, and that the Assay-master lit his lamp.
- That the master of the potters told Orrin the fire loss and the downs clay when plainly asked, and no one else.
- That the carrier went down to his plank on the evening of the twelfth, recognised the hull and said nothing to anyone.
- The reading of the star: it dims, and the town says what it says. I added no cause.

## Held for the Keeper

Whether the carrier stands in the yard on the fourteenth, what the southern master says, and what any of the eleven does on the gate opening are not written.
