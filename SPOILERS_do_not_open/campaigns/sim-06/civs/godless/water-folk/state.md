# The water folk: starting state
population: about 3,000, in boat bands on the lower rivers and sheltered coasts of the south and south-west
stores: what the week's fishing gives; little kept
treasure: none they would call so; fine reed work, some traded metal
trade: reed work, eels and pearls out, by barter at dusk; metal and salt in
strength: none as a body; they vanish into the channels
mood: wary and quiet
