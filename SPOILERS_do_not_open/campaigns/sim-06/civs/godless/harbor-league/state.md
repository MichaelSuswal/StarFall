# The harbor league: starting state
population: about 25,000, in Corrith [f74], Port Sennet [f75] and havens of Sutter Strand [r28]
stores: fish, salt and bought grain; enough
treasure: large: ships, cargoes, silver
trade: salt, fish and carried goods everywhere a hull can reach in daylight
strength: about 60 coasting ships; harbor watches of about 500 in all; crews who fight when cornered
mood: confident, sharp and quarrelsome among themselves
