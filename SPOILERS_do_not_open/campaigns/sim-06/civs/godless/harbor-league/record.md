# Godless people: the harbor league

## Who they are
Mortal folk of the common kind: a league of harbor towns, sea traders, shipwrights, salt boilers and fishers, ruled by their ship-owning houses.

## How many
About 25,000: about 8,000 in Corrith [f74], about 4,000 in Port Sennet [f75], the rest in small havens along the coast.

## Where
Sutter Strand [r28] on the south coast, between the Dunmar Horn [r17] and the mouth of the River Ellow [f07]. The Night 3a plan set them on the east coast; the painted east coast runs off the sheet and its water joins no other on it, so they stand on the south coast, where the sea the sheet shows can carry their ships. Their coasters work the Lome Sea [r32] and enter the Gulf of Maun [r31] in summer. From Port Sennet [f75] the unnamed bend town [f50] is 152.0 miles on foot over open ground, 11 days.

## How they live
Coasting ships of planked build, sailed in daylight and beached at night; salt pans; fish; carrying trade. A council of ship-owners in Corrith [f74] with a yearly master.

## Their code
A cargo is sacred once it is tallied aboard. A wrecked crew is fed and sent home by any haven. A house that will not pay a lost ship's widows loses its berth.

## Their history in the land
They say their towns were founded by captains of the old trading power when its sea-doors were shut. Some quays are older than the towns. The true account is written on Night 3b.

## What they believe about gods
That the sea has moods and no master, and that a god ashore is a landlord. They pour the first cup of a voyage over the side.

## What they want
Salt, timber and safe harbors; the gulf's trade without paying anyone for it; no one else's ships on their coast.

## Whether and how they touch the great events
Set in full on Night 3b.

## Trouble
Sound timber is running short; two houses are close to a feud over a lost cargo.

## Night 3b: what the old tell, and their part in the great events
Added on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-24. The telling may be wrong; the truth is in `deep-history.md`.
- They tell that Captain Sabeth Corr shut the old sea-door at Corrith [f74], kept the fleet from the stewards and founded the league. That is true. They add that she sank the last steward's ship, which she did not.
- At Port Sennet [f75] crews say one word as they pass in under the old sea-door, and call it the door's name. They say it keeps a hull from wreck.
- The Wreck House at Port Sennet [f75] enters every crew pulled from the water since the founding; the town says it has never lost a crew it took in.
- Josse Tarn, wrecked on Sutter Strand [r28] in 12 BA, lives at Port Sennet [f75]; three coasters carry his hung steering board.
- House Corran of Port Sennet holds the debts of the family that carries salt to the bend town (mystery S06). House Sennet and House Corran are the two near a feud over a lost cargo.
