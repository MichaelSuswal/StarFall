# The Aldermark kingdom: starting state
population: about 40,000, in Aldermoot [f70], Wenlow [f71] and river villages of the Aldermark [r10]
stores: full granaries; two good harvests
treasure: large and old: bronze, silver, written dues
trade: grain, cattle, linen and written credit out; iron, salt and timber in
strength: a royal guard of about 400; a levy of about 6,000 by the landed houses
mood: proud, slow, litigious
