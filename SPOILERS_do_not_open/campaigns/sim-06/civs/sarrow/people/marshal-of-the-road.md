# The Marshal of the Road

The office holder is unnamed; no one said the name aloud in Years 1 or 2. Middle aged, careful, does not like orders that restate the custom, since they sound like the custom being doubted. Keeps the scouts, guides, riders and toll houses at fords and passes. Rides less than he used to, and has riders to do what he did.

Fixed this year: he told the hall in month 2 that the riders had seen the thin night watches on the road and had not thought to bring it up. His riders carried the order against boys alone on the watch. He walked Far Notch and the road to the northeast with the Guest at the thaw, named the near houses as the thin ones (the ford and the two nearest it) and the far houses as the honest and the rich, with some pride. At the two farthest he saw new roofs, good horses and full smokehouses, said he had not walked those houses himself in some years, and went quiet. He has not spoken of the roofs. He confirmed the columns' coming by rider as ordered. Ages one year.
