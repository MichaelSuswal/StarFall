# The younger Doorwarden of the lower columns

Unnamed; no one said the name aloud. A Doorwarden of one of the lower columns, of an age to have children, the one who asked the Guest to judge a matter of grass in the last month of Year 2 while his senior did not stop him.

Fixed this year: in month 2 he sat with the Guest at the sill table of his column's standing house in the winter city, with the two households, and the captain of the column confirmed the line she set. He took the matter home closed and said so at his column's rite fire. The senior Doorwarden of the lower columns, hearing it, said a hillside argued four years was settled in a morning and then said it was settled as it should be. The younger man now has some standing in his columns as the one who asked and was answered. Ages one year.
