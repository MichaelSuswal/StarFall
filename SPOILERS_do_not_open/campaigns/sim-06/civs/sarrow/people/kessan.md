# Kessan, called the Lintel

The Warlord of the Sarrow. Fifty winters at the opening of Year 1, fifty one at its close. Heavy, scarred, careful with his word, short in temper, eleven years over the war band. Respected, not much loved; afraid he will not be ready.

Fixed this year: he is not afraid of the Guest, only of being found unready. He gave her his counts truly and quickly, and was relieved, which he told his captains, who took it badly. He was read at the table and disliked it, and did not say so in the hall. He sat at the foot of his own table the first night of snow, from awe and not from weakness. His captains murmur that the Guest holds the sill court and his men answer at it. He judged the sill case of the toll watcher with her and thought it just, and wished his man had had a day to answer. He led the hunt in the sixth month, and led the host up in spring. He believes the host is the one thing the Guest has not asked for, and does not know what he will do if she does.

Ages one year. Acts from his own will.
