# The Mother of the Hearth

The office holder is unnamed; no one said the name aloud in Years 1 or 2. Speaks for the households. Broad, blunt, a keeper of the old custom that in a bad year the hearth mothers count the stores and share them, which the host never did. Not afraid of the Steward and not hostile to him.

Fixed this year: in month 2 she said without being asked that the dogs were fed from the same stores as the people, and that no one had asked what the dogs got in a thin year. She named the hearth that took in the dead herder's household, a household whose own man had died of the cough two winters back. In month 3 she set the Steward's count and the hearth mothers' side by side and told the Guest plainly that they differed by about eighteen days and that neither could say more. She chose the low quarter tables herself for the Guest, a dozen through the cold, the first the widow's household, one in each quarter a captain's. She says the sharing by hearth is the households' own custom and not the Guest's law. Ages one year.

Year 3, dated: month 1, carried the Guest's answer about the cold to the herder who had asked it at a rite fire, the same day. Month 3, set the Steward's count and the hearth mothers' side by side again, the gap this year about eight days, and said the mothers' was nearer. Month 5, cut the households' share by a fifth with the hearth mothers and told each hearth why before it was done. Month 6, asked in the hall, with the Guest in the room, whether the toll was the host's to raise or the sill's. Laughed once, short, in month 1, at the Steward. Ages one year.

Year 4, dated: month 1, heard the Guest answer her question on the toll, carried it to the households and said it was the first time she had heard the host's word held to a count; said Mother of the Door aloud, low, in the hall. Month 3, set the Steward's count and the hearth mothers' side by side, the gap about five days, and said it did not matter whose was nearer; held the seed seal's other half and said she would not give it up for anyone's asking; brought Idris the names of the winter's dead by hearth. Month 4, chose the low quarter tables again. Month 8, said the carriers who are Sarrow would feel half the raise more than the traders. Ages one year.
