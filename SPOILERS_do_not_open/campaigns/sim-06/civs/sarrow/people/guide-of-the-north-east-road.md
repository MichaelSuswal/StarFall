# The guide of the north-east road

Unnamed; no one said the name aloud. An old packer of the lower columns, past sixty, who as a young man went with trains down the road north-east and along the river. The Marshal named him for the road and the river. He walks with a stick, knows the fords by their names, and has not been past the farthest toll house in a good many years.

Year 5, dated: month 1, named by the Marshal for Kessan's party beyond the houses; went as guide with the captain, the keeper of rite and four riders; left the farthest toll house on the morning of the twelfth day. What he met is the Keeper's.
