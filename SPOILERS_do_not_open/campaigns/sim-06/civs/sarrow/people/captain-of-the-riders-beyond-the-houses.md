# The captain of the riders beyond the houses

Unnamed; no one said the name aloud. A captain of the middle of the eight, about forty winters, who holds a column on the road, who was not at the far houses with the first captain, and who is hard to anger. Kessan chose him for that and said so before the hall. He has sat at the sill courts twice and carried goods with trains as a young man and knows how a master reads a guest.

Year 5, dated: month 1, was named by Kessan the day after the Guest said yes; heard the Guest's terms for the party given to Kessan before Idris; was given the Guest's word for the king aloud, to give into the hand of the first officer or clerk of the chancery who would take it and wait as long as a guest may be asked to wait. Took four laden beasts of felt, iron and toll cloth from the Steward, a keeper of rite, a guide, four of the Marshal's riders. Said at the farthest toll house, on the evening before the twelfth day, whose riders they were and by whose leave, and left it on the morning of the twelfth day of the month. What he met beyond is the Keeper's.
