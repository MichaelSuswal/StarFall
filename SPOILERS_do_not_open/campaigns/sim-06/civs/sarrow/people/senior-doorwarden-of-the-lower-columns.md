# The senior Doorwarden of the lower columns

The old Doorwarden passed over in Year 1. Unnamed; no one said the name aloud. Old, hard, the keeper of the old forms in the lower columns, who holds that rank is how a rite is spread across a people so that it does not die with one woman's sense.

Fixed this year: in month 5 he came to the hall door, was heard in person with Idris Veld at the Guest's side, and said what he meant: that a rite held by four is a rite held by four, and that the lower columns, where the oldest rite fires are, have no one who has the oath from the Guest's mouth. He said Idris was the best keeper of the sill in the high rite and had chosen well for a woman choosing by sense. He asked that the oath be taught to him or to one of his, and was given the answer the Guest had decided, that she chooses no one new this year. He said the lower columns would have the Guest at their fires in the summer. In the summer he was courteous and no more, did not speak of the oath, and did not stop a younger Doorwarden of his columns from asking the Guest to judge a matter of grass in the last month. He is not Idris's enemy so much as a man heard and not given what he asked. Ages one year.
