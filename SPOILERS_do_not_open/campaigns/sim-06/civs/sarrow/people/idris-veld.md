# Idris Veld, the First Doorwarden

An old woman, patient, shrewd, well liked, thirty years in the rite. Ages one year.

Fixed this year: she bade the Guest in at the gate by word in the old form, after a short wait while the gate guard held. She was read at the table and called it courteous. She was named a guest by the Guest, the only one named, and was taught the oath in person. She chose three Doorwardens by sense and not by rank, and passed over the senior of the lower columns, who has said the sense will run out; this is her first enemy of the year. She chose the door of the salt store for the third lesser mark, to learn how the stores stand, and the Steward knows it. She told the Guest what the recital says of a name over a sill: there is none, the sill bears the name of Marda Sill, first toll taker at Marle Ford for the Sarrow, and before her the Sarrow guided for the wardens of a league in the lowlands. She did not ask why, and did not ask again. She sits at the Guest's right hand at the sill court. Her standing is up, and she knows it will cost her.
