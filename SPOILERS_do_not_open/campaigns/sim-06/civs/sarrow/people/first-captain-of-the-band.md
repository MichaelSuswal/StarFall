# The first captain of the band

The eldest of the Warlord's eight captains. Unnamed; no one said the name aloud. Old, plain, exact, who had never walked to the far toll houses and had never liked the Marshal's riders.

Fixed this year: Kessan named him to walk with the Marshal to the two farthest toll houses and the two near ones in the first weeks after the thaw, and said he chose him for the dislike. He counted the beasts, the sacks, the racks and the roofs, set the cord beside the riders' tally and asked each watchman where the new horses were bought and with what. He reported at the hall flat and plain. Ages one year.
