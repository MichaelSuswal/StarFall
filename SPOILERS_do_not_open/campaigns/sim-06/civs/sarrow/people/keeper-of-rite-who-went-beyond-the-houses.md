# The keeper of rite who went beyond the houses

Unnamed; no one said the name aloud. A keeper of rite with the river speech from carrying, the one who read the river king's letter aloud to the lower column in the river speech in Year 3 and the question of whether it should be answered. Idris Veld named him to go with Kessan's riders to keep the guest law at every table they sit at.

Year 5, dated: month 1, named by Idris; went with the captain, the guide and four riders, with the Guest's word for the king; left the farthest toll house on the morning of the twelfth day of the month. What he met is the Keeper's.
