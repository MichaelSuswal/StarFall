# The Sarrow people of god-thresholds: starting state

Campaign: sim-06. Set by the Keeper on creation Night 3a, 2026-11-24.

population: about 18,000, a host on the move between the high grass and its winter ground in Marle Gorge [r03], with a winter city [f60] of about 4,000
stores: barley, curd, dried meat and fodder for the winter: enough for an ordinary winter, not for a long one
treasure: herds, horses, bloomery iron, and tolls in kind laid up at the winter hall
trade: carrying, guiding and tolls; wool, felt and iron out; grain, salt and cloth in
strength: about 360 sworn spears under eight captains; about 3,500 more when the host calls; the passes themselves
mood: expectant at first snow; hard, hospitable, and tight-lipped about the stores
