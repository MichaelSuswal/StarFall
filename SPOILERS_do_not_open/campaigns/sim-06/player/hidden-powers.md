# Sealed: the player's god

### The Standard's Pocket
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When a mortal freely puts a pledge into the god's hand as surety for a debt owed to the god or its house, and the god carries it in its empty pocket, the god knows from anywhere, at any hour it turns its mind to it, one thing, yes or no: does the pledger still hold the debt as owed. One pledge at a time.
Drawback: While a pledge is in the pocket the scales hang uneven by its weight, and The True Measure is dark until the pocket is empty again.
Limits: A pledge freely given, never taken. It tells nothing of where the pledger is, what they mean to do, or why the answer changed. Not on another god; unruled until it happens. It gives the next step (ask, or go and see), never an end. It compels no one and binds no one.
Surfaces when: the god pockets, without thinking, a token a debtor presses on it as surety; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the even stone that pocket was for (secret past). It may also serve a thing the player has said its god wants; kept narrow for that, and flagged in the night's neutrality check.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Worth Under Weight
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Of food set in the pans of the god's scales, the god knows one thing more than its weight: whether it will feed, yes or no.
Drawback: Having so weighed a food and found it wanting, the god cannot keep silent about it if asked: on that one matter silence is closed to it.
Limits: Food only, in the pans only. It tells that a food will not feed, never why, never what was done to it or to its ground. Spoiled, blighted and wrong in any other way all read the same.
Surfaces when: food that weighs true and does not feed is set in its pans; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the last tally (secret past): a thing true by weight that did not feed.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Cordwise
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: The god ties and reads knotted counting-cord quickly and without error, its own or a stranger's of a kind it has been shown.
Limit: A craft of the hands. It does not read a private cipher or a cord whose system it has not been shown, and it counts nothing a clerk could not count with time.
Surfaces when: it keeps or checks a long count on cord in front of others.
Drawn from: the card: the coil of knotted counting-cord (Things carried).

### Bargain-Ear
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: The god recalls the exact words of a bargain it heard struck, for about a year.
Limit: Only words it heard itself, only the terms as spoken, and no better than a very good clerk's written note would be. It tells nothing of what was meant.
Surfaces when: the terms of an old spoken bargain are disputed in front of it.
Drawn from: the world: its settled peoples are talkers who count what is owed.

### Dusk Reckoning
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: The god reads small marks and does sums in lamplight or dusk as easily as at noon.
Limit: Eyes and figures only; it sees no further and no more than is there.
Surfaces when: it works a ledger or reads a seal's small marks in poor light.
Drawn from: the world: dusk and lamplight are its hours.
