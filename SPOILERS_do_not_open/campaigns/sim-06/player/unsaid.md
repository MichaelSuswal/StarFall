# Unsaid: the Weigher

Written by the DM. Read by the DM, the Keeper and the checker.

Campaign: sim-06. Every sealed answer the player gives, in the player's words, dated, by kind. No want here is acted on without a confirmation from the player given within the last three played years, written beneath it.

## Want

- 2026-11-24, creation Day 2 (asked last, as the unsaid want): "The Weigher wants to be owed. Not paid, owed. I want the whole world to stand in my ledger, and I want to be the one measure nobody can check. I sealed myself with honesty because it is the only thing that lets me be the standard without anyone daring to weigh me. I would call it fairness, but half of it is a wish never to be audited."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 2 (same reply): "I do not want to find the house again, and I do not want anyone else to either."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (a sealed part of the first reply): "I want the Clerk of the Seals to be someone I can trust, and I will be watching to see whether they are. I have a feeling about that office. Seals are how I feel broken promises, so whoever keeps the register will know before anyone else who is lying."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (a sealed part of the second reply): "I would like to find out how they died, and whether the dent in my scales is older than the fall. I do not want to say that aloud."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (a sealed part of the third reply, the yes): "I would like the old keeper's death last spring to turn out to be ordinary. A fever, an old man, a hall that fell quiet. I do not trust that I want it to be ordinary, and I do not trust that I want to be right about it. I will watch for who benefits from the vacancy."
  Confirmations: none yet.

- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the second reply, standing in the gate of the town's house): "I am less calm than I look in that gate. I am watching the person at the balance more than the basket. If the weights on the wall are mismatched and cracked, I want to see whether anyone in this yard hears that as a lie."
  Confirmations: none yet.

- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the fourth reply, after answering "Who are you?" at the bench): "I'm uneasy about "who are you" and how close I came to saying the other word. I did not lie, and I would have been right not to. But I noticed I wanted the yard to know. I want to be told I'm the standard, and a town that stops quarrelling about weights because of me is a town that has made me the standard."
  Confirmations: none yet.

- 2026-11-24, Year 2 played day (a sealed part of the first reply, after remitting the thief's price at the gatepost): "Remitting the price is half generosity and half tactics. A man who is not forced to pay me will talk to me, and a bonded man who tried my pocket is a man whose master I want to know. I'd rather the town think I was soft than think I was hunting."
  Confirmations: none yet.

## Fear

- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (same reply): "I am afraid of being the one that register catches."
- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the fifth reply, before the dusk meeting with the Assay-master; a decision and a fear in one reply, kept whole): "I've decided to tell the Assay-master about the True Measure, and not the other two, because a man who has weighed all his life will find out what I can do within a month, and I would rather he hear it from me. The Reading frightens me more. If he knew I could tell whether a man meant his terms when he struck them, the first thing he would do is ask me to read him. I do not want to learn what I would read."

## Request to the Keeper

- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (same reply): "I want the vacancy to be a hint. A keeper of the standards who died last spring should not be only a cause of the quarrels."

## Other

- 2026-11-24, creation Day 2 (same reply, the god's belief about its own past): "I think the debt I forgot is mine. I think I was the one who shorted a house that trusted me, and I became a god of true measure because I could not bear to know what I had weighed false. When I say I am owed something and owe something, I believe the second sum is larger. I do not want to find the house again, and I do not want anyone else to either."
- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the third reply, after speaking to the weighing at the bench): "I did not enjoy that it was easy. The True Measure made me right in front of a whole yard in the first hour, and the only thing I felt was how much I wanted to be right again. I'm going to watch myself on that."
