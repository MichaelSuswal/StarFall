# Unsaid: the Weigher

Written by the DM. Read by the DM, the Keeper and the checker.

Campaign: sim-06. Every sealed answer the player gives, in the player's words, dated, by kind. No want here is acted on without a confirmation from the player given within the last three played years, written beneath it.

## Want

- 2026-11-24, creation Day 2 (asked last, as the unsaid want): "The Weigher wants to be owed. Not paid, owed. I want the whole world to stand in my ledger, and I want to be the one measure nobody can check. I sealed myself with honesty because it is the only thing that lets me be the standard without anyone daring to weigh me. I would call it fairness, but half of it is a wish never to be audited."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 2 (same reply): "I do not want to find the house again, and I do not want anyone else to either."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (a sealed part of the first reply): "I want the Clerk of the Seals to be someone I can trust, and I will be watching to see whether they are. I have a feeling about that office. Seals are how I feel broken promises, so whoever keeps the register will know before anyone else who is lying."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (a sealed part of the second reply): "I would like to find out how they died, and whether the dent in my scales is older than the fall. I do not want to say that aloud."
  Confirmations: none yet.
- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (a sealed part of the third reply, the yes): "I would like the old keeper's death last spring to turn out to be ordinary. A fever, an old man, a hall that fell quiet. I do not trust that I want it to be ordinary, and I do not trust that I want to be right about it. I will watch for who benefits from the vacancy."
  Confirmations: none yet.

- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the second reply, standing in the gate of the town's house): "I am less calm than I look in that gate. I am watching the person at the balance more than the basket. If the weights on the wall are mismatched and cracked, I want to see whether anyone in this yard hears that as a lie."
  Confirmations: none yet.

- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the fourth reply, after answering "Who are you?" at the bench): "I'm uneasy about "who are you" and how close I came to saying the other word. I did not lie, and I would have been right not to. But I noticed I wanted the yard to know. I want to be told I'm the standard, and a town that stops quarrelling about weights because of me is a town that has made me the standard."
  Confirmations: none yet.

- 2026-11-24, Year 2 played day (a sealed part of the first reply, after remitting the thief's price at the gatepost): "Remitting the price is half generosity and half tactics. A man who is not forced to pay me will talk to me, and a bonded man who tried my pocket is a man whose master I want to know. I'd rather the town think I was soft than think I was hunting."
  Confirmations: none yet.

- 2026-11-24, Year 3 played day (a sealed part of the sixth reply, after taking the Weightward's seat at the gatepost): "I took it because I want it. I told myself it was because a hall without a keeper keeps nothing, and that is true, and it is not the reason. I am a god of measure being offered the standards of a town, and I have just been told that a rack with a clean empty seat is mine to guard. If the seventh turns up in my coat, I do not know what I would do. I do not think it is there. I have not checked the pocket I keep empty, and I am going to."
  Confirmations: none yet.

## Fear

- 2026-11-24, creation Day 3 (same reply): "I am afraid of being the one that register catches."
- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the fifth reply, before the dusk meeting with the Assay-master; a decision and a fear in one reply, kept whole): "I've decided to tell the Assay-master about the True Measure, and not the other two, because a man who has weighed all his life will find out what I can do within a month, and I would rather he hear it from me. The Reading frightens me more. If he knew I could tell whether a man meant his terms when he struck them, the first thing he would do is ask me to read him. I do not want to learn what I would read."

- 2026-11-24, Year 2 played day (a sealed part of the third reply, after saying how it stands witness to a bargain): "I'm lying to nobody by leaving out the Reading. But when I said I wouldn't tell either side what I think of the other, it was true and it was the clean way to say it. I do know what I would read. I haven't decided when I'll let it show, and I'm afraid the first time will be for the wrong reason."

- 2026-11-24, Year 3 played day (a sealed part of the first reply, after telling the Assay-master in the hall that it is a god; fears and a belief about itself in one reply, kept whole): "I told him true things and left the shape of a lie around the edges. The seals and the Reading are still in my coat, and I told him there was more to me than the scales. I am afraid he will ask the question that makes me choose. I am also afraid of the empty seat in the rack, and of how clean it was. I do not know that I did not do it. I only know that I would have remembered."

- 2026-11-24, Year 3 played day (a sealed part of the second reply, after answering the Assay-master's three questions in the hall): "I did not say that the Reading is the one thing I am afraid he will ask about. I was careful with "I will not say no to a yes", and it was true, and it was the closest I have come to hiding behind my own rule. If he asks me whether I can tell when a man is lying, I will have to choose. I would rather choose before he asks."

- 2026-11-24, Year 3 played day (a sealed part of the third reply, on walking to the Stallreeve; a decision, a want and a fear in one reply, kept whole): "I decided in the hall that if the Stallreeve asks about the Reading outright, I will say it. I do not want to be a god who is found out. I also know I am about to learn who in this town means what they say, because the first person to hear it will be the Stallreeve, and I will be standing next to him when he decides what to do with me. I can feel how much I want to read him, and I will not do it, and I am more afraid of that wanting than of anything the ring can do."

## Other

- 2026-11-24, creation Day 2 (same reply, the god's belief about its own past): "I think the debt I forgot is mine. I think I was the one who shorted a house that trusted me, and I became a god of true measure because I could not bear to know what I had weighed false. When I say I am owed something and owe something, I believe the second sum is larger. I do not want to find the house again, and I do not want anyone else to either."
- 2026-11-24, Year 1 played day (a sealed part of the third reply, after speaking to the weighing at the bench): "I did not enjoy that it was easy. The True Measure made me right in front of a whole yard in the first hour, and the only thing I felt was how much I wanted to be right again. I'm going to watch myself on that."

- 2026-11-24, Year 2 played day (a sealed part of the second reply, after the stallholder's scale was read out and then level): "The stallholder's scale being out was the right answer, and I still felt relieved that I hadn't been the one to say it was level. I'd have said it. I couldn't have said it falsely. I have not yet been tested on whether I'd keep silent when a lie would have saved a friend."

- 2026-11-24, Year 2 played day (a sealed part of the fourth reply, after giving its answers for the Assay-master): "I told the Assay-master I'd say "yes" to being more than a man with brass. It was the first time I let someone hear it. I felt lighter, and then I felt like someone who has shown a pocket. I want to see what he does with it."

- 2026-11-24, Year 3 played day (a sealed part of the fourth reply, after refusing the ship owner's exclusive bargain at the landing): "I priced my own refusal with some care. Had I said yes to the salt and the exclusive landing, I would have had the first real hold on a house on the council, and I thought about that longer than I like. A trader who isn't willing to be bought for a year is cheap to read, and one who is willing is cheap to buy, and I'm not sure which I am."

- 2026-11-24, Year 3 played day (a sealed part of the fifth reply, before the roll on the ship owner's bargain): "I said I would not read the ship owner's mind, and that was true. I also noticed that I did not ask him to say it plainly that his boats are clean. I think I would rather not know yet."

- 2026-11-24, Year 3 played day (a sealed part of the seventh reply, on the coaster's master stepping up at the gate): "She is the first stranger who has come to the gate from outside the river towns, and I watched her hands more than her scale. I have been waiting two years to feel a weight in the air, and I tried to feel for it when she stepped up. Nothing. I think part of me wanted there to be, and part of me did not, and I did not like the part that did."

## Habit

- 2026-11-26, Year 4 played day (a sealed part of the first reply, at the gatepost): "Whenever I sit at the landing at night, I run my thumbnail down the nest cups in order, smallest first, before I count the day's readings."
  An ordinary habit the god states: conduct under build 7, carried out and never priced. Written to the open conduct profile as a habit, as the orchestrator relayed.
