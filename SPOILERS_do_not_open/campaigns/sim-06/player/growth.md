## Bargain-Ear (knack)
Related skills: hearing spoken bargains struck and recalling their terms; standing witness
Use: Year 1: a related skill used in part. It heard small bargains struck daily at the gate and in the market; no terms were disputed in front of it, it recalled none, and it gave no answer when asked to stand witness (month 6). Not surfaced.
Use: Year 2: a related skill used. It stood witness to a boat sale between two households of boatwrights (month 6) and said what it saw and heard; it heard bargains struck daily at the gate. No old bargain's terms were disputed in front of it. Not surfaced.
Use: Year 3: a related skill used. It struck a bargain at the landing and stood witness to a boat sale (Year 2 month 6 as told, Year 3 month 4); the Tally-keeper said its terms for the seat back and no one disputed them. No old bargain was disputed in front of it. Not surfaced.
Grown (year, how far):

## Dusk Reckoning (knack)
Related skills: reading small marks and doing sums in dusk or lamplight
Use: Year 1: no use. It read no marks and did no sums in poor light; the meeting by lamp was of weights in pans.
Use: Year 2: a related skill used in part. It counted the day's readings over in its head each night by the channel folk's lamp (decision 16); it read no marks in poor light. Not surfaced.
Use: Year 3: a related skill used in part. It read loads at dawn in half light, by weight in the pans; it read no marks and did no sums in poor light. Not surfaced.

## Year notes
- Year 1: no attempt to grow a power was stated or made. The True Measure was used daily as the card gives it; use without an attempt is not practice and nothing is counted. The Seal, the Reading and Seals by factors were not used. No hidden power's moment came: no pledge was pressed on it, and the food it took in fees fed as food does. No trigger, failure or near miss.
- Year 2 (with the tail of Year 1): no attempt to grow a power was stated or made. The True Measure was used daily as the card gives it, and in the hall and at the pits under two rulings (L-0037, L-0038); a ruling on what the card already covers is not growth, and use without an attempt is not practice. The Seal, the Reading and Seals by factors were not used. No hidden power's moment came: no pledge was pressed on it; the fees it weighed fed as food does; no food that does not feed was set in its pans. No trigger, failure or near miss.
- Year 3: (with Year 2 again from the first held moment): no attempt to grow a power was stated or made. The True Measure was used daily as the card gives it, at the gate, at the landings (L-0037 covers salt cut with sand), in the hall and at the pits. The Good-Faith Reading was named aloud to the town and not used. The Seal and Seals by factors were not used. No hidden power's moment came: no pledge was pressed on it; the half-sticks it took up are the council's askings and its own yes, not surety for a debt; no food that weighs true and does not feed was set in its pans. The pocket was not checked in unplayed time (the keeper log). No trigger, failure or near miss. Three years in, nothing is counted against any power: use as the card gives is not practice.
