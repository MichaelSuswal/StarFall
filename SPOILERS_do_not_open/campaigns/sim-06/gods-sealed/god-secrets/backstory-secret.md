# Sealed past: god-secrets

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to the common past (`gods-sealed/common-past.md`). No agent reads this, the DM included.

## The rest of the story
It is the pattern of the inward keeper of the Lamp Gate: the one who faced the city, kept the gate's book of who had passed, and each dusk checked it against the door tally of the House of the Even Pan across the square. The city called both keepers of the gate by the gate's name, Ashevar. When Tolcarrath walled the gate up against the coast people, the name went out of use with the gate; no one took it and no one gave it away. It was simply no longer said. The voice that called it by name was the outward keeper's, across the arch, at the change of the lamps.
The tally stick it carries and cannot read is the counting-house's door tally for the last season, the one behind the last tally. It had it in hand for the dusk check on the night the door was broken, and the check was never made.

## What was lost and is not remembered
The name Ashevar, and that it shared it. That it voted, as keeper of the book, for the shutting of the gate, out of caution.
