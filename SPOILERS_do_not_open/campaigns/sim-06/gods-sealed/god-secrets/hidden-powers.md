# Sealed: god-secrets

### The Passing Book
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: Of anyone who has said their own name aloud to the god at a gate of the town it lives in, the god knows, wherever it is, each time that person passes that gate again, and which way.
Drawback: Everyone so written remembers the god's face, and where they met it, for good.
Limits: One gate, of its own town. Fifty names at most; a new one past that drops the oldest. It tells the passing and the direction, nothing of errand or company.
Surfaces when: a traveler gives it their name at the gate; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the gate's book (secret past). Fitted up: its card is the lightest.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Tally-Reader
Status: hidden (discovered Year 3, month 5: the Clerk put the old league's tallies in its hand)
Kind: minor
Description: Any tally stick, knotted cord or private merchant's mark the god holds in its hand, it can read as its maker would.
Drawback: For a day after, it cannot speak a false figure about that account.
Limits: Counts and marks only, not letters or ciphers of words. The one tally stick it carries stays shut to it.
Surfaces when: it is handed a stranger's tally; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the dusk check of tally against book (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Figure-Memory
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It keeps figures it has read: sums, dates, quantities.
Limit: As a very good clerk does; it reckons no faster.
Surfaces when: it recalls an old account without the book.
Drawn from: its secret past: the keeper of the book.

### Even Shares
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It divides goods among parties so that none feels cheated.
Limit: Only what is there to divide, and only among those present.
Surfaces when: it settles a division at council or market.
Drawn from: its secret past: the gate's dues were split each dusk.

### Stray's Welcome
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: As the dog, it is fed scraps and let lie by the fire where a strange dog would be driven off.
Limit: A dog's welcome, no more; it opens no barred door.
Surfaces when: it comes as the dog to a hearth that does not know it.
Drawn from: its secret past: the gate dog every town has.
