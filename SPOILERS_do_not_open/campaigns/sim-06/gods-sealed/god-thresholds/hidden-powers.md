# Sealed: god-thresholds

### The Road Lamp
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When she stands in a doorway with a lit lamp and names aloud one person she is waiting for, that person, if within a night's walk, sees a light in the right direction, however the land lies between.
Drawback: She cannot leave the doorway until that person comes or dawn does.
Limits: One person, named, who must be looking. It shows a direction, compels no one and shows the light to no one else.
Surfaces when: she holds a lamp in a door for someone lost in the dark; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the lamp held for those coming up the road (secret past). Fitted small: her card is the heaviest.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Knock-Knowing
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: From a knock on a door she stands behind, she knows whether one stands outside or more than one.
Drawback: Having heard the knock, she must answer it: open, or speak through the door.
Limits: One or more than one, never who, how many or what they carry.
Surfaces when: someone knocks at a door she is behind; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the outward keeper's work (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Table-Seating
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: She seats a table so that those who would quarrel are not side by side.
Limit: Only among people she has watched a little while.
Surfaces when: she places guests at a meal.
Drawn from: her secret past: the gate fed those it let in.

### Sleeper's Carry
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: She lifts and carries a sleeping or hurt person without waking or jarring them.
Limit: One person, as far as a strong back goes.
Surfaces when: she carries someone in over a sill.
Drawn from: her secret past: the ones brought in out of the dark.

Ceiling: 73. From `star.md`. Never released.
