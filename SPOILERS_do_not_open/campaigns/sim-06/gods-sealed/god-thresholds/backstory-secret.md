# Sealed past: god-thresholds

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to the common past (`gods-sealed/common-past.md`). No agent reads this, the DM included.

## The rest of the story
It is the pattern of the outward keeper of the Lamp Gate: the one who faced the road, held the lamp for those coming up out of the dark, and decided who was let in. The city called both keepers by the gate's name, Ashevar. When the city walled the gate up, the outward keeper was on the outside of it: the people's memory left her standing on the road with the coast people she would have admitted, the latch gone from under her thumb. The name was last said over that threshold by the inward keeper, calling the change of the lamps, the evening before the masons came. The key that fits no lock is the Lamp Gate's.
Those the gate turned away were in the crowd that broke the door of the House of the Even Pan that winter. She was remembered among them and against them both.

## What was lost and is not remembered
The name Ashevar, and that another shared it. That she had argued for opening and lost. That she was shut out, and by whom.
