# Sealed: god-sea

### The Chain Drawn
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: With its burned left hand closed on a chain, rope or boom that it has itself made fast across a harbor mouth or a channel no wider than a bowshot, nothing afloat passes that line while it holds.
Drawback: The hand burns as it did the first night for as long as it holds, and it cannot let go before the tide next turns. For a day after, the hand will not close.
Limits: Only a line it made fast with its own hands. It stops hulls and floating things, not swimmers, not what moves under the water, not the sea itself. One line at a time.
Surfaces when: it takes hold of a harbor chain in a moment of need; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the harbor chain it did not close (secret past). Fitted up: its card is the lightest of the NPC gods' but one.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Surge Flinch
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Some hours before the sea rises out of its ordinary course along the coast where it stands, the god's body flinches, and it knows a rise is coming.
Drawback: It flinches at every spring tide too, and cannot tell which kind until about an hour before.
Limits: A warning only: no height, no place beyond the coast it stands on, no cause.
Surfaces when: a surge or an unnatural rise is coming to the shore it is on; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the night the water came up (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Banked Coal
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It keeps a fire, a forge or a lamp alive through wind and wet when another hand would lose it.
Limit: Ordinary fuel, ordinary flame; it lights nothing that could not be lit.
Surfaces when: it tends a fire or a lamp in foul weather.
Drawn from: its secret past: the forge by the shore.

### Crew Count
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: At a glance at a boat coming in, it knows whether every one who went out is aboard.
Limit: Only a boat it saw leave, and only whether all are there, not who is missing.
Surfaces when: boats it watched go out come home.
Drawn from: its secret past: the keeper of a harbor town.

### One-Hand Forging
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It works hot metal well with one hand, and judges a heat by its color.
Limit: A smith's skill; it makes nothing past the world's craft and no faster than the work goes.
Surfaces when: it takes up a hammer at a forge.
Drawn from: its secret past: the harbor-smith.
