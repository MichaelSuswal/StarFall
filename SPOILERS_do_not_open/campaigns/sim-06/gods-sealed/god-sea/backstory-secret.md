# Sealed past: god-sea

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to the common past (`gods-sealed/common-past.md`). No agent reads this, the DM included.

## The rest of the story
It is the pattern Marrask held of its harbor-smith: the one at the forge by the shore who made and kept the chain across the harbor mouth, the boat-nails and the lamp irons. The office ran in one family and the town remembered it as one broad, silent figure with a burned hand. The hand was burned on the night the water came up: the smith went to draw the chain's great pin white-hot to close the harbor against the surge, alone, having sent the apprentices up the hill, and did not get it closed. Skerr is the name the town's children used for the smith, from the sound of the chain on the stone.
The sea came over the bar because the Ovane no longer built it. The smith did not know that and the pattern does not either.

## What was lost and is not remembered
Marrask. That it sent the apprentices away and so had no second pair of hands at the pin. That the failure began upriver, a generation before, and was no one's at the harbor.
