# Sealed past: god-rivers

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to the common past (`gods-sealed/common-past.md`). No agent reads this, the DM included.

## The rest of the story
It is the Ovane: the habit of a river of being come to. For an age the people of the upper country came down each morning, put their hands in the water and said its name together. The word it cannot hear is its own name, Ovane. Then the upriver towns cut the feeders into their fields, and the mornings stopped. It ran thin for three generations with no one at the bank, and the flat stone it carries is the stone at the old washing-place where the crowd had stood.
Thin, it stopped building the bar at its mouth, and the sea came over Marrask. At its ford in Varrow Chase a king and a stag died in its water. It took both and said nothing, being a river.

## What was lost and is not remembered
Its name. That the forgetting was a matter of ditches and no malice. That its thinning drowned a town.

## How it might surface

- The warm stone that fell with them dreams a kneeling crowd and an unseen river: the river is this one.
- god-sea and god-beasts, each at close quarters.
