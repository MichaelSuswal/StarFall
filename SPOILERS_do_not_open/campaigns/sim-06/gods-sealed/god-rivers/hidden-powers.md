# Sealed: god-rivers

### The Turned Bed
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: Standing in a dry riverbed, it knows which way that bed's water went: toward which side it was turned or drawn, as a direction.
Drawback: It goes dry and thirsty for a day, as after a small working, and cannot shift for that day.
Limits: A direction only: not who turned it, when, how far, or why. A bed dry by drought alone tells nothing.
Surfaces when: it stands in a dry bed that once ran; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the turning of the channels (secret past). Fitted small: its card stands above the band.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Wader's Line
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Wading into any water, it knows the safe line across.
Drawback: Those who follow its line once cannot find it again without it.
Limits: Only where a safe line exists; it makes none. Only while it wades.
Surfaces when: it leads others across a strange ford; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the ford (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Birth-Hands
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It delivers a child well, with the plain skill of an old midwife.
Limit: Skill, not healing: what skill cannot save, this does not.
Surfaces when: it attends a birth without using Warm hands.
Drawn from: its secret past: the hands that dipped in it each morning were mostly women's.

### Missing One
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: Among a group it has counted before, it knows at a glance that one is missing.
Limit: That one is missing, never who or where.
Surfaces when: it looks over its people gathered.
Drawn from: its secret past: the mornings it counted the crowd.
