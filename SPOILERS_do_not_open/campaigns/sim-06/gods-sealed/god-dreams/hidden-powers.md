# Sealed: god-dreams

### The Kept Chair
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: At a table of her own people where one place is laid and left empty for someone of that table who died within the year, everyone who ate there dreams that night, once, of the dead one sitting down, and hears one thing the dead had said in life to someone at that table.
Drawback: She carries that table's grief as her own for a season, and for that season Keeping Sleep will not hold for that household.
Limits: It gives nothing no living person at the table already knew: the words are remembered words. It raises no one and answers no question. Once for each of the dead.
Surfaces when: she sits at a table of her people where a place is kept for the newly dead; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the table with one more chair than there are people (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Sleeper Count
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Standing under a roof, she knows how many under it are asleep, and whether one of them is near death.
Drawback: While one under that roof is dying she cannot bring herself to leave it.
Limits: A count and one yes or no; never who, and never why.
Surfaces when: she enters a house at night where someone is failing; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the night watch (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Watch-Wakeful
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: She stays attentive through a long watch and notices a change in a sleeper's breathing.
Limit: A nurse's skill; it mends nothing and foresees nothing.
Surfaces when: she sits a night at a sickbed.
Drawn from: her secret past: the vigil at the beds.

### Fair Loaf
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: She portions food so that it goes round and none is wasted.
Limit: It makes no food; a share is still only a share.
Surfaces when: she divides a short supply among many.
Drawn from: her secret past: the house's table.

### Once-Told Names
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: She keeps a name told to her once.
Limit: Names only, and only those said to her face.
Surfaces when: she greets by name someone met once long before.
Drawn from: her secret past: the watch knew every bed by name.
