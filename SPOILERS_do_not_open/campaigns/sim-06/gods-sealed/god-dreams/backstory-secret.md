# Sealed past: god-dreams

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to the common past (`gods-sealed/common-past.md`). No agent reads this, the DM included.

## The rest of the story
It is the pattern of the night watch at the Low Field: the vigil kept at the beds of the sick and beside the dead. The watch was begun by one woman, the first keeper, and she died in the house the winter the menders shut their teacher out, with the watch sitting round her and no one to mend her. The watch did not end with her; that night it became a thing the people held apart from any one keeper. That is the death it was born of, and the hand going slack was the first keeper's hand in the hand of the next. Vael is what the watch was called: the word the keepers said to each other at the change of the lamp.
The long table with one more chair than there are people is the house's table. The empty chair was kept for the mender who had been shut out.

## What was lost and is not remembered
The first keeper's name and face. That the one for whom the chair was kept was the mender. That the watch could not have saved her and was never meant to mend.
