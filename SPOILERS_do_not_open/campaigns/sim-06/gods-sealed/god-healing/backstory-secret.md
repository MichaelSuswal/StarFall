# Sealed past: god-healing

The rest of the story behind the outline, tied to the common past (`gods-sealed/common-past.md`). No agent reads this, the DM included.

## The rest of the story
It is the pattern of the mender of the Low Field: the one who tended the sick at the marsh edge, laid out the dead, and taught others to do both. What it made was the Calder Hands, the company it taught, the best work of the office's long life. In the country's last generations the Hands began to take payment, mending and the laying-out both, and when the teacher would not, they shut it out of the house it had built, by a door barred from inside by a hand it had trained. That winter the first keeper of the night watch died in that house, unmended. The thing that came to carry the dead from the tall grass was the mender itself, in the years before, and also the Hands' own bearers after; the pattern cannot tell them apart.
Ilvane is no name from that country. It chose it here.

## What was lost and is not remembered
The name of the Calder Hands and of the one who barred the door. That it had refused, on the night before, to mend a paying house's child ahead of a pauper, and that this was the Hands' reason.
