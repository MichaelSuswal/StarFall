# Sealed: god-healing

### Clean Laying-Out
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: A body the god lays out with its own hands carries its sickness no further: no one takes that sickness from that body or from what was buried with it.
Drawback: The hands that laid it out cannot mend for a day and a night for each body, and smell of the laying-out as they do after rot.
Limits: Only the dead it has itself washed and laid out, one at a time. It does nothing for the living sick, the house, the water or the air.
Surfaces when: it lays out the dead of a sickness that travels; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the laying-out at the marsh edge (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Cupped Water
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: Water the god lifts in its two cupped hands is clean to drink.
Drawback: Its hands crack and weep for a day after, and Mending comes clumsily through them.
Limits: One drink at a time, drunk from the hands. It cleans no well, spring or vessel.
Surfaces when: it gives a drink from fouled water with its own hands; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the slow water at the tending ground (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Sure Blade
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It cuts cleanly with a small knife: cloth, cord, root, and flesh when a mender must.
Limit: A hand's skill with an ordinary knife.
Surfaces when: it cuts something fine in front of a watcher.
Drawn from: its secret past: the mender's work.

### Dose by Eye
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It judges by eye how much of a known herb a given body should have.
Limit: Only herbs and bodies it knows; it discovers no remedy.
Surfaces when: it helps a mortal healer measure a dose.
Drawn from: its secret past: it taught others to tend.

Ceiling: 73. From `star.md`. Never released.
