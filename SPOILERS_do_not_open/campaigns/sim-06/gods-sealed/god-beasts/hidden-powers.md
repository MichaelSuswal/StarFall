# Sealed: god-beasts

### The Spoken Verdict
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: cool
Description: When the god stands at the place of a killing and says aloud which wronged which, as truly as it knows, the place is quit: the herds graze there again, beasts cross it, and the god no longer feels that death as a weight.
Drawback: If it later learns the verdict was wrong, it goes lame for a season and the place is worse than before.
Limits: One killing, at its place. It changes ground and beasts, never what any person believes or does.
Surfaces when: it pronounces over the dead at a kill site; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the verdict not given (secret past). Fitted small: its card stands at the top of the band.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Shed Tines
Status: hidden (undiscovered)
Kind: minor
Description: An antler the god has shed, set upright at a boundary, keeps wolves and wild dogs from crossing within a bowshot of it for a season.
Drawback: It feels each set antler gnawed or broken as a blow.
Limits: Wolves and wild dogs only. It sheds one pair a year.
Surfaces when: it sets a shed antler at the edge of a camp; never scheduled.
Fitted to: the broken tine (secret past).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Clean Dressing
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It dresses a kill cleanly and wastes nothing.
Limit: A hunter's skill.
Surfaces when: it butchers game beside its people.
Drawn from: its secret past: the hunter's hands.

### Windward Sense
Status: knack (undiscovered)
What it does: It always knows where the wind is and keeps downwind without thinking.
Limit: The wind as it is, not as it will be.
Surfaces when: it stalks or is stalked.
Drawn from: its secret past: the stag at bay.
