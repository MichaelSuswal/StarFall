# Record of gods: Ilvane

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, or what they can do. I would feel one only close by, in the same room or across a doorway, as a weight in the air that settles into knowing. I trust none of them.

## After four years on the ground
I have met no other like myself and felt no weight in the air in Year 1, Year 2, Year 3 or Year 4, not at the seat's hearth, not in the village, not at the mounds, not on the upstream walk, not on the downstream walk. No word has come to me from any of them or of any. No message was delivered to me this year or any year. No promise was made to me. I hold no grievance and no debt.

In Year 4 I walked twelve days alone down the river, about 58 miles below the court steading, all of it fen, and found below the two emptied steadings no steading, no burial ground and no people within my reach. If another of us is near, it is not along the river as far as I went, nor along the creeks and the upstream ground to the fen's western edge. I know nothing of the east, the far side of the river, or what lies in the forest past the treeline.

## The star
The star dimmed on three nights in Year 1, five in Year 2, six in Year 3 and seven in Year 4. My people count twenty-one since I came. Each time the blue deepens and the star stands dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, then is as before. The count has risen by one or two every year.

Year 3: one in the first month, a pair in the fifth, a pair in the seventh, one in the eighth. The pairs fell in my summer, when I walked the upstream ground and mended no one.
Year 4: the second, third, fourth, eighth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth months. None in the summer of the downstream walk.

Hedda counted the Year 4 nights against the sendings and says: four in months I was sent for, three not, none while I was gone. That is her tally, from the one who counted, and I cannot check it. Set against Year 3, where two pairs fell while I was far away and mending no one, the two years pull different ways and I do not say they agree. The old women say the lamp is going out. I do not know that it is going out, and I did not light it.

I still hold three guesses and no more: another like me is doing it, the world is doing it, or something of mine I have not found is doing it. I do not know whether the other gods see it as my people do. I do not know that there are any other gods at all except by what I was told on waking, that others like me came down.

I have no guess about any other god's nature or powers that I would stand on. What I learn goes here only from what I see, hear or am told.
