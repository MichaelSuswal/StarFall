# Record of gods: Ilvane

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, or what they can do. I would feel one only close by, in the same room or across a doorway, as a weight in the air that settles into knowing. I trust none of them.

## After three years on the ground
I have met no other like myself and felt no weight in the air in Year 1, Year 2 or Year 3, not at the seat's hearth, not on the downstream walk, not on the upstream walk, not in the village. No word has come to me from any of them or of any. No promise was made to me. I hold no grievance and no debt. In Year 3 I felt no other like myself, and I note that I walked about fourteen days alone and far from the village and felt nothing there either, so if another of us is near, it is not along the river or its creeks as far as the fen's western edge.

The star dimmed on three nights in Year 1, five in Year 2, and six in Year 3: one in the first month, a pair in the fifth, a pair in the seventh and one in the eighth. Each time the blue deepened and the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, then was as before. My people count fourteen since I came. The count is rising: three, five, six. The pairs in the fifth and seventh months fell in my summer, when I was walking the upstream ground and mending no one, so the mending is not the cause, or not the whole of it. I cannot tie a night to anything I did. I hold three guesses and no more: another like me is doing it, the world is doing it, or something of mine I have not found is doing it. If another of us is the cause, I still cannot say which, and I would not know where to look except by waiting to feel the weight. I do not know whether the other gods see it as my people do. I do not know that there are any other gods at all except by what I was told on waking, that others like me came down.

I have no guess about any other god's nature or powers that I would stand on. What I learn goes here only from what I see, hear or am told.
