# Record of gods: Ilvane

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, or what they can do. I would feel one only close by, in the same room or across a doorway, as a weight in the air that settles into knowing. I trust none of them.

## After two years on the ground
I have met no other like myself and felt no weight in the air in Year 1 or Year 2, not at the seat's hearth, not on the downstream walk, not in the village. No word has come to me from any of them or of any. No promise was made to me. I hold no grievance and no debt.

The star dimmed on three nights in Year 1 and on five in Year 2: in the second month, the sixth, the ninth, the eleventh and the twelfth. Each time the blue deepened and the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night. My people tie some of the nights to me. Two of this year's nights fell when I had mended no one within the moon, so I take it that mending is not the cause, or not the whole of it. The count is rising, three and then five. That is the one thing I hold with any weight: whatever dims it is growing more frequent, or someone is doing it more often. I do not know whether it is another like me, the world, or something of mine I have not found. I hold all three as guesses. If another of us is the cause, I still cannot tell which, and I would not know where to look except by waiting to feel the weight.

I have no guess about any other god's nature or powers that I would stand on. What I learn goes here only from what I see, hear or am told.
