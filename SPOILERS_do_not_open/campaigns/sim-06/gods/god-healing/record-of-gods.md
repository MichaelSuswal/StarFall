# Record of gods: Ilvane

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, or what they can do. I would feel one only close by, in the same room or across a doorway, as a weight in the air that settles into knowing. I trust none of them yet.

## Since landing
Nothing has happened yet but the landing. I have met no other like myself, felt no weight in the air, heard no word from or of any. No promise has been made to me and I hold no grievance and no debt. I have no guess about their natures or powers that I would put weight on. Whatever I learn of them goes here only from what I see, hear or am told, and I will hold it loosely until I have watched one act.
