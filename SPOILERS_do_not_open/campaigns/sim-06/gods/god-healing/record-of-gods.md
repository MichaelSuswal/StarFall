# Record of gods: Ilvane

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, or what they can do. I would feel one only close by, in the same room or across a doorway, as a weight in the air that settles into knowing. I trust none of them.

## After a year on the ground
I have met no other like myself and felt no weight in the air in all of Year 1. No word has come to me from any of them or of any. No promise was made to me. I hold no grievance and no debt.

The star dimmed on three nights, two in the first month and one in the fifth, each time for about an hour in the middle of the night, and my people say such nights have come in other years. This year many of them tied the nights to me. I did nothing I know of on those nights, and I do not know what dims it. It may be another like me, or the world, or something of mine I have not found. I hold all three as guesses and put weight on none. If another of us is the cause, I cannot tell which, and I would not know where to look.

I have no guess about any other god's nature or powers that I would stand on. What I learn goes here only from what I see, hear or am told.
