
## Round 1 reply

### Mending
Accept as drafted, including the tremor until the thaw. Keep it. A healer who walks away from a grave mending untouched is a healer people will ask to do it again and again, and I want the cost to be seen on me. I would not have it lighter.

### Reading the Rot
Accept as drafted. The blind spot toward my own people is the one I chose and I do not ask to trade it.

### Hasten the Rot
Choose (b): as often as I will, each use after the first in a day adding a day and a night to the smell and to the time I cannot mend. I would rather carry a heavier cost than a written count of uses. A fence says how many times I may; a cost says what it takes from me, and I can weigh that myself. If I clear a plague pit and then a second and a third in a day, I will have chosen to be unable to mend for three days, and I will have the smell on me for three days, and that is mine to have chosen. The salt hold and the sealed hold I accept as written; they say what rot is, not how often I may use it.

### Return from Ground
Accept the season rung and the one item of memory or habit, as drafted. I accept that a body held, sealed in or shut out is not broken. I will say plainly that I wanted otherwise, because being shut out by something I made is the thing I least want to be stuck in, but I see the rule is the same for every god and I do not ask to be the exception. I ask two clarifications, in the write-up and not as a new power. First, that "the walk is added" means only that I come up at the ground and then walk to wherever I go, and adds no further cost. Second, that the choice of which ground is the nearest is made by the Keeper by the nearest in the world, not by me, as I wrote it.

### Trades and the rest
No trade, as read. Nothing else asked.
