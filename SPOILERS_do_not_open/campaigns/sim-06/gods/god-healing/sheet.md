# Sheet: god-healing

## Leans

2. Body: 68 (strong 1, enduring 100). Leans toward enduring.
3. Presence: 61 (revealed 1, hidden 100). Leans a little toward hidden.

5. Regard for mortals: 25 (shepherd 1, sovereign 100). Leans toward shepherd.
6. Order: 68 (law 1, freedom 100). Leans toward freedom.
7. Making: 57 (builder 1, breaker 100). Leans a little toward breaker.
8. Company: 31 (rolled 6, +25) (solitary 1, gathering 100). Leans toward solitary.
9. Appetite: 87 (content 1, hungry 100). Leans hard toward hungry.
10. Trust: 95 (open 1, wary 100). Leans hard toward wary.

## Seed
The psychopomp, ferryman of the dead (Anubis, Charon, Xolotl)

## Domain, form, virtue, flaw, wound, voice
Domain: healing and rot.
Form: human-seeming, uncannily perfect.
Virtue: restraint.
Flaw: pride.
Wound: it was betrayed by one it made.
Voice: laughs at the wrong moments.

## Bargaining stance
Hard (score 69, from Appetite, Trust and Regard for mortals). It contests every price it thinks too high, offers its own drawbacks in place of the Keeper's, and accepts a counter only for a reason it respects.
Argues from reasons and consistency with what was already agreed.
Bargains for duration and for what survives.
Decides fast and may accept to be done.
Resists limits and would rather take a heavier drawback than a written fence.

## The people it leans toward
Size: the usual number, about five thousand (score 48)
Settlement: scattered steadings
Rule: a sacred monarch behind a court. A mortal line holds the throne in the god's name and is held sacred for it; a court stands between the throne and the people, and the god is reached through it. (score 48)
Worship: local shrines. Household and village shrines, kept by whoever keeps them; no order of priests. (score 51)

## Name
Ilvane. It chose the name on waking and has told it to no one. No one yet calls it anything.

## Traits (own)
### Tender contempt
It leans toward the shepherd's regard for mortals and holds trust back hard, and it is proud. It will walk all night to a sickbed and then think the family beneath it for weeping. It cares for what it tends and does not expect to be thanked, and it would rather be underestimated than needed. Reconciles: shepherd regard, wary trust, flaw of pride, hidden presence.

### Hunger held in the hands
Its appetite is great and it wants a lot: to know, to try, to mend what no one has mended. It is also restrained, and the restraint is real but not gentle: it holds back by clenching, and it goes impulsive when the clench slips. It acts fast, regrets rarely, and does not apologize. Reconciles: hungry appetite, impulsive temper, virtue of restraint, solitary company, leaning toward freedom and a little toward breaking.

## Keeper trait
What you have mended knows you: any person or creature you have mended can tell, eyes shut, when you are within a few paces. Its reach: only those you have mended, only your nearness; it tells them nothing of what you think or mean, and it touches none of your powers or their prices.

## Season it wakes into
Spring (the thaw).
