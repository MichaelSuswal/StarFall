# Goals: Ilvane (Year 1, standing on the ground)

1. Walk the land near me alone first, the mounds and the river edge, and see what is there before I decide who to be to anyone. Smoke to the south-east says a village; I will see it from the edge before I enter it.
2. Learn what ails the land and its people. Read the water, the wells and the ground before I mend a single body. Know before acting.
3. Be useful quietly. Mend only what no one else there can mend, and stay a stranger in undyed wool to anyone who does not look closely. Mending costs me a tremor until the next thaw, so I spend it where it counts and keep count.
4. Find who keeps the dead here, and why they do it so carefully. Look for the truth of where the dead go, or do not.
5. Meet the court's offices one at a time, starting with whoever counts the sick and the dead. Let an office hear me and take my word before I rely on it. Give no office or line a hold on me that can be turned against me.
6. Make the people need me less each year: learn what the sitters, midwives and bonesetters know, and leave the village healthier in its own hands.
7. Hold the hunger and the pride in check. Do not let a mending turn into a crowd at my door, and do not let myself think the families beneath me for their weeping.
8. Learn who the others like me are, only from close quarters, and trust none until I have seen them act.
