# Goals: Ilvane (on arrival)

1. Walk the edge of the land alone first, and learn what is there before deciding who to be to the people.
2. Find clean, slow water and ground where things are seen to end and begin, and settle near it.
3. Learn what ails the land and its people: sickness, fouled water, hunger, old burials. Know before acting.
4. Be useful quietly. Mend where it is needed, and stay a stranger to anyone who does not look closely.
5. Find a truth about where the dead go, or do not, and learn who buried what with such care, and why.
6. Lead a people who tend each other well enough that they need it less each year.
7. Be safe from its own making: do not give any helper, line or court a hold on it that can be turned against it.
8. Learn who the others like it are, from close quarters, and trust none of them until it has seen them act.
