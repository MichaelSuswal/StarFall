# Goals: Ilvane (Year 2)

1. Keep faith with the Reckoner. She took my word, and she asked that I take no step toward the court without coming to her first. I come to her first, every time, and I say so to her plainly. She is the one office that has heard me, and I do not spend that on a shortcut.
2. Stop the dead from fouling the living. The ground of the bank burials and the wells of the low steadings read foul alike, and two steadings stand empty. Take that to the Warden of Waters and the Keeper of the Mounds, who hold the wells and the ground, and let them move the new graves and the wells themselves. I advise, I do not command, and I do not rot a grave that is not mine to unmake.
3. Be seen on my own terms before I am watched on theirs. The Judge-Mother wants me watched, and the Hearth-Holder has asked me to the hearth. Go to both openly, once, and let them look. A god who hides from a court that has already noticed him is the one they fear.
4. Learn the moss of the mounds and what the sitters know. The old women say it no longer bites the tongue. I want to know what has changed, and I want the sitters to need me less each year.
5. Mend less and later. Thirty was enough for a year. Keep the tremor to the thaw and no longer, spend mendings only where no sitter can reach, and keep count. Do not let the child's mending become the rule I am asked to keep.
6. Hold the pride and the clench. Do not think the families beneath me for their weeping. Do not let the Keeper's trait turn their knowing of me into a crowd at a door.
7. Do not bind myself to any office or to the line. Take no seat I have not chosen, and give no hold that can be turned on me.
8. Learn who the others like me are, only close up, and trust none until I have seen them act. Watch the sky, and do not name its cause until I know it.
