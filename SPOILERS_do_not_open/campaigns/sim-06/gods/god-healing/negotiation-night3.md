
## Round 1 reply

### Your landing: the helpers and the seat
Choose (a): keep the meeting as written, at the cost stated. I land a stranger, and an office becomes my helper only when its holder hears me and takes my word, in play. I do not take (b). A people that knows me on sight is a people that holds a hand on me before I have looked at them, and a court that already serves me is a court that can turn what it holds against me. I made something once and it shut a door on me, and I will not hand any office a standing claim on me before I have seen what it is. Nobody acting for me while I am away is a real cost. I have counted it, I would rather carry it than a helper I did not earn, and I do not ask it lighter. I also do not ask for the offices to be won by any fixed means: let it be found in play.

### The spears
Accept as drafted. About four hundred, ordinary and unpriced.

### Their magic
Accept as drafted. The sitters are ordinary in this world and each pays her own cost. I do not ask for more of them, and I understand that I cannot make another by telling.

### Nothing else
No edge was proposed and none is asked. Nothing else is reopened.

## Round 2 reply

Confirmed. The landing is as I meant it: a stranger to a people who do not know me, no helpers in fact until an office is won in play, and the cost carried plainly. The spears and the sitters stand as written. No edge was proposed, no weakness is owed, and nothing is reopened. I leave the place of their town and ground to the world and the map.
