# Record of other gods

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I have not met them and know nothing else of them.

## What the landing showed
At the landing I felt no weight in the air, no one near enough to know. That tells me only that none stood within a few paces of the rise. It says nothing of how many there are, where they stand, or what they want. I hold no trust, no grievance, no debt and no promise with any of them, and I will not invent a guess I have no ground for. If I feel the weight settle in some room, I will enter here what I see of that one and no more.
