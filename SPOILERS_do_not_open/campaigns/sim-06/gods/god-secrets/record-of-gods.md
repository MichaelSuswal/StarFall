# Record of other gods

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. In a full year among the walls and on the tracks I have not once felt the weight in the air that tells me one stands near. So none has come through the gate, sat in the market, or crossed my path on the south-west track. I hold no trust, no grievance, no debt and no promise with any of them. I do not know how many there are, where they stand, or what they want.

## What the sky showed
The star dimmed on three nights this year, two in the first month and one in the fifth, each time with the blue deepening for about an hour. The town has seen such nights before and this year many of them tied the nights to me. I do not know why, and I will not say I do. My guess, held loosely: it is some doing of one like me, or of the world, and the town points at the stranger because the stranger is the newest thing in it. I did nothing on those nights that I know of. If I am asked, I say that, because it is true and because it costs me nothing. I do not take the credit or the blame for a thing I cannot account for.

## What I will do with it
I enter nothing about any god I have not felt. If the weight settles in some room, I will write here what I see of that one and no more, and I will price any promise by the one who makes it, not by the promise.
