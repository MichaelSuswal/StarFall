# Goals

Held now that I stand on the rise, in order of weight.

1. See the people, whoever they are, through the first winter with a full granary. I have not seen the granary and I do not yet know the harvest. A full store is still the best argument I know for a town staying together, so I begin by learning the count.
2. Be received at the town as a trader and a guest, entered under no name, and win a hearing at the council one office holder at a time. Nobody acts for me until a holder has heard me and taken my word, and I would have it so.
3. Stay one large merchant among many. Be useful in the books, say true things about them, and be asked last. Do not be the only light in the room.
4. Find one mortal who can say a true thing to me and have it stay said, and who is not hurt by being trusted. I do not go looking by pressing. I let long talk and the winter do it.
5. Learn the ground: the worn track both ways, the river, who keeps books, who trades, who watches, where the old roads and sealed doors are, and what the marsh folk and the hedge-witches are. Keep my own counsel while I learn.
6. Ask quietly, and only in passing, whether any place here holds a lost name. Claim nothing until I know whether it is mine.
7. Be missed a little, by someone, when I am away. I will know in the second autumn whether I was.
