# Goals

On arrival, in order of weight.

1. Find the people I am to lead and see them to a walled town on a river bend before the first winter, with the granary full. A full granary is the best argument I know for staying together.
2. Take a seat at their council as a clever stranger, be heard, and not be mistaken for the only light in the room.
3. Find one person, mortal, who can say a true thing to me and have it stay said, and who will not be hurt by being trusted.
4. Learn quietly whether any place in this land holds a lost name, and not claim it until I know whether it is mine.
5. Learn what sort of world this is: who keeps books, who trades, who watches, and where the old roads and sealed doors are. Keep my own counsel while I learn.
6. Be missed a little, by someone, when I am not there.
