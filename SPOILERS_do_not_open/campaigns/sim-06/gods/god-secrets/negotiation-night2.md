
## Round 1 reply

I take the standing rules as read. A body held is not broken, a return's price is rolled from my own record, an instrument in other hands is only the ordinary thing, and one weakness buys one step. None of that is argued here. I accept the Keeper's correction on the key: no one else gains the power by holding it, they only take it from me while they hold it.

### Second Face
Choice: (b), as I asked, the heavier price.
Reasons: the form roll would give me two shapes for nothing, and I know it. I still choose the price. I said in my survey that a change of shape should cost something that lingers, a day of slowness, a word forgotten, and a god whose second face cost nothing would be a god nobody believed when it said it was careful. I keep the price I named and I do not ask for anything for it. I would rather pay more than the shift needs than carry a free thing that others can say I got by arranging it. I take the Keeper's limits as written: a long breath, seen if anyone is looking, belongings fall where I stood, a dog's body is a dog's, broken as the dog I return as the man, a god at close quarters knows me in either shape. One clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: that there is no limit on how often I change, and that the stacking in the drawback is the only fence on it. I would rather pay by the change than be fenced by a count.

### Kept Word
Choice: accept as drafted, seven things, no choices.
Reasons: the Keeper's text matches what I proposed. I have no argument with the binding. A kept thing that I may not use even to save my own people is the whole point of keeping it. The displacement of the oldest, forgotten entirely and the teller not told, I do not like, and I am not asking to change it. It is the price, and I would notice losing it.

### Finding What Was Hidden
Choice: accept as drafted, no choices. The pair is settled as I chose it: I never search my own people, the council and its helpers among them, and whoever I search among the others is not told.
Reasons: the Keeper says the blind spot is the real price and the voice is small. I agree and I do not want the voice made heavier. I take the limits as written, hiding places and not secrets, not in the dog's shape, where I stand and no further.

### Return
Choice: (a), the nearest market street, anyone's, where strangers pass unremarked.
Reasons: a return should put me among people who can find me and who will notice if a stranger is back, and I wish to be missed and then found, not left in a sealed place nobody walks past. A market street is a ground the Keeper can find on any day, and it gives me no advantage the nearest of its kind would not, since I come back in the man's shape, unremarked, among strangers. I did not choose the sealed door because a place that hides me is a place where I could not be found by the people I would want to find me. I accept the season rung and the one item of memory or habit, taken by the roll from my own record, and the walk added.

### The trade: the key as the instrument
Choice: (a), as I asked: Kept Word holds twelve kept things, not seven.
Reasons: the Keeper says twelve for seven is a small step for this weakness. I disagree about how small. The key is the weakness I would notice, because I cannot carry it safely in the dog's shape, and anyone who takes it takes my one way of finding a sealed door. But the step I want is the one the weakness is for. Every kept thing past the seventh is a teller I do not forget, and every one I forget is a person who trusted me and did not know I had let go. Twelve is five fewer betrayals, and my virtue is mercy. A longer reach on Finding would make me a better hunter, and I do not want to be one. The key should stay a quiet tool. I am content that the Keeper calls the step small. I give way on the size and not on the choice. I do not ask for a second step, and if the Keeper thinks twelve is more than the weakness covers, say so plainly and I will hear a counter of the same size on Kept Word, such as ten, before I would take another power's improvement in its place.

### Read, not priced
Agreed. The coat, boots, knife, purse, weights and tally stick are ordinary. Caution and the wish to be unremarkable are conduct and buy nothing.
