# Power card: god-secrets (no name held)

Bynames: "friend", "the stranger" (what it lets others call it).

Confirmed on Night 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific), by the god's round 2 reply.

## Form

One body, as every god has in this campaign.

A stocky man of middle years, a hand under the middle height, broad through the shoulder, heavy in the hands. Grey coming into brown hair, cut plain. A weathered face that is easy to forget on purpose: mild brown eyes, a nose that has been broken once and set badly, and two sets of lines, laugh lines on one side of the mouth and worry lines on the other, so that the face seems to be two faces depending on which side the light falls. A plain brown travelling coat, good boots, wool, nothing that marks rank. Human senses. It walks at a man's pace, never hurries, and is hard to wind. It speaks in the even, friendly, slightly pricing voice of a market trader, and everything it says sounds like an offer.

Its second form is a stout old dog, knee high, brindled grey and brown, one ear folded, grizzled at the muzzle, the sort every town has three of and nobody looks at twice. In this form it cannot speak, but it understands all that is said, hears and smells as a dog does, and moves at a dog's pace. It can carry a purse or a cord in its mouth and nothing more.

What breaks it: Flesh and bone in both forms. Blades, fire, drowning, a crushing weight, a long fall, cold without end, a blow to the head can wound it, and chains, a locked room, a cage, a pit or a stopped mouth can hold it. It is slow to tire and slow to be hurt, but it is not unhurtable, and a body that is badly enough broken is scattered. A dog's body is easier to hold and easier to kill than the man's.

God-sense: the god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing that this is one like itself. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.

When the body is broken: see the power Return.

Keeper trait: People say more to you than they meant to: a mortal who talks with you for an hour comes away having told you something they had not planned to, knows it afterward, and remembers to whom. Its reach: only what a mortal chooses in the moment to say aloud in long talk with you; it opens no mind, a thing let slip so is not a thing told in trust and takes no place in Kept Word, and it touches none of your powers or their prices.

### Definitions

- Its own people: the people it leads, the council and its helpers among them.
- A kept thing: a true thing a mortal told it in trust and it agreed to keep.

### Things carried

- A plain travelling coat, boots, a bronze belt knife, a purse of scrap silver and a few small brass weights.
- A tally stick, notched down one edge in a pattern it cannot read.
- The small bronze key on a cord at the neck is not ordinary: it is the instrument of Finding What Was Hidden and is written under Weaknesses taken.

## Powers

### Second Face
Status: main
Description: The god changes between the man and the old dog, in about a long breath, wherever it stands, seen if anyone is looking. What it wears and carries falls where it stood, but for what a dog can hold in its mouth. One body at a time. As the dog it understands speech and makes none.
Drawback: Each change, either way, leaves it slow for a day, as a man after a fever, and takes one ordinary word from its tongue until it hears the word spoken and learns it again. Changes close together lengthen the slowness and cost more words.
Limits settled at creation:
- The dog's body is a dog's.
- Broken as the dog, it returns as the man.
- No limit on how long it stays a dog.
- Other gods: a god at close quarters knows it in either shape.
- A mind: none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, distance, falling, water, a scarcity, more than ordinary craft.
- No limit on how often it changes; the growing cost is the only fence.
- The two shapes are the god's form and needed no price; the drawback is heavier than the shift needed, written as the god asked, and nothing was given for it.
- First wording, kept as intent: "It would rather pay than be fenced."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Kept Word
Status: main
Description: When a mortal of the god's choosing tells it a true thing in trust and it agrees to keep it, the thing is kept. While it is kept, no mortal magic can read it out of the god, nor out of the teller while the teller thinks of it as told to the god. The god feels a small tug in the chest if the teller speaks the thing aloud to another in the same town, and does not learn to whom. It holds at most twelve kept things.
Drawback: A kept thing binds the god absolutely. It may never repeat it, act where the act would reveal it, or bargain with it, not to save itself or its people, until the teller releases it. A thirteenth thing displaces the oldest, which the god forgets entirely, and the teller is not told.
Limits settled at creation:
- Only what is told in trust, not what is overheard.
- The god may refuse before the telling is finished.
- A teller's death releases nothing.
- A thing already known to many takes no place.
- More than ordinary craft, another god's reading: unruled until it happens.
- A mind: it rules none.
- The god's own people: as anyone.
- Use across a year: bounded by the twelve.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, being watched, a scarcity.
- Twelve is what the trade bought; before it the power held seven.
- First wording, kept as intent: "everyone should have one person to say a true thing to and have it stay said."
Rulings:
Trades: its twelve places were bought by the weakness "the key as the instrument".
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Finding What Was Hidden
Status: main
Description: Holding its bronze key, and standing in a room, a yard, a cellar or a stretch of road no wider than a market square, the god feels where something has been put on purpose so that it will not be found: a false bottom, a sealed door, a buried cache, a ledger behind a wall, a cellar under a cellar. It learns where the hiding place is and roughly how large, never what is in it, who hid it or why.
Drawback: Each finding leaves its voice rough for a day. It may never search the hidings of its own people, the council and its helpers among them.
Limits settled at creation:
- Hiding places, not secrets.
- A thing lost, or buried by time and not by intent, is not found.
- A place hidden by a god or by magic may or may not be found; unruled until it happens.
- Not in the dog's shape.
- Distance: where it stands, no further.
- Being watched: nothing marks it.
- Use across a year: no limit beyond the voice; it goes where the god goes.
- A scarcity: none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, water.
- The blind place for its own people was chosen by the god from a pair.
- First wording, kept as intent: "it finds hiding places, not secrets."
Rulings:
Trades: its instrument, the key, is the weakness that bought Kept Word's twelve (see Weaknesses taken).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Return
Status: main (the return)
Description: When the god's body is broken, it returns whole after a season, in the man's shape, at the nearest market street, anyone's, where strangers pass unremarked. What it carries comes back with it; a thing taken from it before the breaking does not.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit at each return. The item is taken by a logged roll from the god's own record, and the god is not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- The standing scale's season rung.
- A body held is not broken.
- The walk is added.
- If another holds the key when the body breaks, it stays where it is.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Hidden powers
Two hidden powers exist. Discovered powers are added above as they surface.

### Tally-Reader
Status: hidden (discovered Year 3)
Kind: minor
Description: Any tally stick, knotted cord or private merchant's mark the god holds in its hand, it can read as its maker would.
Drawback: For a day after, it cannot speak a false figure about that account.
Limits: Counts and marks only, not letters or ciphers of words. The one tally stick it carries stays shut to it.

### Figure-Memory
Status: knack (surfaced Year 1: winter, three figures given at the council's table without the book)
What it does: It keeps figures it has read: sums, dates, quantities. A knack is small: it shades an outcome or a craft and never does what a power does.

### Stray's Welcome
Status: knack (surfaced Year 1: winter, its one night out as the dog)
What it does: As the dog, it is let lie and given scraps or water where a strange dog would be driven off. A knack is small: it shades an outcome or a craft and never does what a power does.

## Weaknesses taken

### The key as the instrument
Finding What Was Hidden comes only through the bronze key. While another holds it on their person or in a place they alone can reach, the power is gone until the god holds it again. In any other hand it is only a small bronze key, and no one else gains the power. A copy is a copy. Lost, buried or sunk, it lies dormant until the god takes it up, and the god has no sense of where. Left on the ground when the god takes the dog's shape, it is anyone's to pick up. If it is destroyed, the power is gone until the god's next return, when it comes back with the body. If another holds it when the body breaks, it stays where it is. Anyone who learns this can take the finding by taking the key.
It bought: Kept Word holding twelve things, not seven.

## Trades
Offered once, on Night 2. Taken: the key as the instrument, for Kept Word's twelve. Trades are closed for the rest of creation.
