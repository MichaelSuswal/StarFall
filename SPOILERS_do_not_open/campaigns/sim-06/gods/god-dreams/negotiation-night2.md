# Negotiation: god-dreams, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

I accept the draft as written, with one choice made and two clarifications asked "in the write-up, not a new power". I have nothing to bargain against. Each price is one I would feel, and each is the shape of what I asked for.

### Walk the Dream
Accepted as drafted. The thin body is the price I named myself, and one dream at a time keeps me honest. I am glad the sleeper's fear reaches me as my own; I would not want to walk a bed and feel nothing of it.

### Keeping Sleep
Accepted as drafted. Seven households a night and the fear arriving at dawn wherever I am are both fair, and the fear rising with the use is right: a god who gives rest by the handful should not be able to do it for free. I ask for no more households and no softer price.

### Rising
Accepted, with choice (a): the nearest house, anyone's, where a lamp is kept burning through the night. My reasons: I am a thing that rises from an ending, and a lit lamp in a house is the nearest thing in the world to the table I am homesick for. It is a ground the Keeper can find on any day, and it sits with my lamp and my shrines. I did not choose (b). A fresh grave or a deathbed would pull me back to the place of a loss, and I would rather rise toward a warm room than a mourned one. I name no other ground.

### The trade: the lamp as the instrument
Accepted, choice (a), as written. I will carry the weakness: if the lamp is out of my hand, by theft, loss, trade, force or my own gift, the power is gone until I hold it again. I accept that a destroyed lamp costs me the power until my next return, and that a lamp another holds when my body breaks stays where it is. I thank the Keeper for answering the question I asked about it.

Two clarifications, in the write-up and not new powers:
1. "From a flame first lit from the lamp" is read to include a flame lit from a flame that was itself lit from the lamp, down any chain of lights, so a keeper may carry the blessing to a neighbour's shrine by passing the fire. The cap of seven households still holds for the whole chain.
2. When the lamp is away, the shrine flames lit from it are only fire. When I hold it again, flames already lit from it, and any flame lit from those, serve again at once, without being lit anew. If the Keeper reads otherwise, I ask that it be written plainly.

### Read, not priced
Agreed. Not ruling by dreams buys nothing, and I do not ask it to.

## Round 2 reply

Confirmed. This is what I meant, and I ask for no change.

- Walk the Dream: confirmed as written.
- Keeping Sleep: confirmed as written, with both clarifications as the Keeper wrote them: the chain of flames serves, the seven hold for the whole chain, and flames lit from the lamp serve again at once when I hold it again.
- Rising: confirmed as written, with the nearest house, anyone's, where a lamp is kept burning through the night.
- The lamp as the instrument: confirmed. I know the weakness is that anyone who learns of it can take my people's kept sleep by taking the lamp, and I carry it knowingly.
- Keeper trait: accepted. Flames near me dipping when I am afraid fits what I am, and it shows my fear and nothing of its cause. I would not wish it hidden.
