# Power card: Vael

Bynames: none.

Confirmed on Night 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific), by the god's round 2 reply.

## Form

One body, as every god has in this campaign.

A half beast, half person. From the waist up a broad, heavy shouldered woman, middle aged, with a wide plain face, dark eyes set large and wet like a deer's, and long furred ears that turn toward sounds she is not looking at. From the waist down the body of a great dark hind: deep chested, thick legged, a short pale tail. The coat is the colour of a late sky, nearly black, scattered with small pale flecks along the flanks that show only in lamplight. The skin of the human half is brown and warm to touch. Her hair is long, coarse and unbound. She has no antlers. Standing, her head is the height of a tall man's head and a little above; the hind body is the size of a draft pony's, built for carrying and for standing a long time.

Senses are ordinary, with eyes that see well in dim light and ears sharper than a person's. She moves at a walking hind's pace, a steady walk that she can keep up without tiring, a short heavy run when startled. She does not leap fences. She cannot climb ladders or enter low doors without stooping and turning.

What breaks it: Ordinary harm to the body: spear, arrow, blade, fire, a fall, being crushed, drowning. Hunting nets, pens, ropes and a barred or walled enclosure can hold her. A gate or door she cannot pass (too narrow, too low, barred against her) shuts her out. A wound heals slowly, at the pace a deer's would. She cannot die. A broken body is scattered or ended and she must return by the power below.

God-sense: the god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing that this is one like itself. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.

When the body is broken: see the power Rising.

Keeper trait: When you are afraid, flames near you show it: every lamp and fire within a few paces dips low, and anyone watching sees it. Its reach: it dips flames and puts none out, your own lamp among them, so it ends no working and touches no price; it shows your fear and nothing of its cause.

### Definitions

- Her own people: anyone in a household that keeps her shrine.
- A kept shrine: a household shrine kept for her, with a flame lit from her lamp or from a flame down a chain of lights that began at the lamp.

### Things carried

- The lamp, of fired clay with a horn shade and a flax wick, on a cord at the waist, is not ordinary: it is the instrument of Keeping Sleep and is written under Weaknesses taken.

## Powers

### Walk the Dream
Status: main
Description: While one of her own people sleeps, the god may enter that sleeper's dream and walk in it: speak with the sleeper, hear them, comfort them, show them a place, and stay until they wake. One dream at a time. She may enter the last sleep of one who is dying, if they are asleep. She is a presence in the dream and cannot rewrite it, wake the sleeper, make them remember or compel them to anything. A sleeper may push her out by fear or refusal. What is said is remembered as a dream is, in pieces, and fades unless told.
Drawback: While she is in a dream her body stands where it was, dim, slow and nearly voiceless: it cannot walk more than a step, speak above a murmur, lift or carry, and is easy to overlook, move or hold. Harm that lands on it is felt and not resisted. If the body is broken she is thrown out of the dream and the break stands. The sleeper's fear in a nightmare reaches her as her own while she is there.
Limits settled at creation:
- Her own people: anyone in a household that keeps her shrine, wherever they sleep.
- Distance: any, among those.
- Not those who keep no shrine of hers, and not the unsleeping.
- A sleeper under Keeping Sleep that night has no dream to enter.
- She learns only what the sleeper says or shows; the power shows her nothing else that may be in a dream, and what she meets there that is not the sleeper is unruled until it happens.
- A mind: it rules none.
- Use across a year: one dream at a time, her body thin for as long.
- A scarcity: none.
- More than ordinary craft: a sleep made or kept by more than craft is unruled until it happens.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, being watched.
- First wording, kept as intent: "so that no one in her keeping dies alone."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Keeping Sleep
Status: main
Description: At dusk the god lights her lamp at a household shrine kept for her. That household sleeps the night through, unbroken: no waking fear, no night terror, and the rest is whole. It heals no wound and no illness. It holds no one against real danger: a broken door, a shout, a fire or a blow wakes them as it would anyone. The household has no dreams that night. Up to seven households at once, each lit separately and renewed each dusk.
Drawback: For each household kept, the fear of its night arrives in the god at dawn as her own, wherever she is, and stays until dusk of the following day. One or two, she is shaken. Three or more, she acts on first impulse, cannot hold a plan across an hour, and cannot enter a room that is shut or crowded. All seven, she cannot stand still and flees what frightens her if it comes near.
Limits settled at creation:
- Only a household that keeps her shrine.
- One night per lighting; it does not stack.
- A household may refuse it by putting out the shrine flame.
- It takes only the fear of the night's sleep, never the fear of a present danger.
- Carrying: the lamp.
- Other gods' peoples: it does not reach them.
- Use across a year: seven households a night at most.
- A scarcity: safe sleep is a thing people may want badly; the power gives seven households a night and no more.
- A mind: it quiets sleep and compels nothing.
- Not applicable: falling, water, being watched.
- By the trade, a keeper of a household shrine may light and tend that shrine's flame at dusk in her name, from a flame lit from the lamp or from any flame down a chain of lights that began at the lamp; the seven households hold for the whole chain, and the fear still reaches her at dawn.
- While the lamp is away those flames are only fire; when she holds it again they serve again at once, without being lit anew.
- First wording, kept as intent: "a coin of safe nights."
Rulings:
Trades: its instrument, the lamp, is the weakness that bought the keepers' lighting in her name (see Weaknesses taken).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Rising
Status: main (the return)
Description: When the god's body is broken, she returns whole after a season at the nearest house, anyone's, where a lamp is kept burning through the night. What she carries comes back with her, the lamp too if it was on her.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit at each return. The item is taken by a logged roll from the god's own record, and the god is not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- The standing scale's season rung.
- The season runs from the breaking.
- A body held or shut away is not broken, and no season starts until the body is broken.
- The walk from the ground is added.
- If another holds the lamp when the body breaks, it stays where it is.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Hidden powers
Two hidden powers exist. Discovered powers are added above as they surface.

### Watch-Wakeful
Status: knack (surfaced Year 1: winter, the night she sat beside the Night Sitter)
What it does: She stays attentive through a long watch and notices a change in a sleeper's breathing. A knack is small: it shades an outcome or a craft and never does what a power does.

## Weaknesses taken

### The lamp as the instrument
Keeping Sleep comes only through the god's lamp. If it is out of her hand and away from her person, by theft, trade, loss, force or her own free gift, the power is gone until she holds it again, and the shrine flames lit from it are only fire while it is away. In any other hand it is only a clay lamp, and no one else gains the power. A copy does nothing. If it is destroyed, the power is gone until her next return, when it comes back with the body. If another holds it when the body breaks, it stays where it is. Anyone who learns this can take her people's kept sleep by taking the lamp.
It bought: the keepers of household shrines lighting in her name, within the seven.

## Trades
Offered once, on Night 2. Taken: the lamp as the instrument, for the keepers' lighting in her name. Trades are closed for the rest of creation.
