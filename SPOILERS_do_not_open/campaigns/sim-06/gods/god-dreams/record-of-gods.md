# Record of other gods: Vael

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, or what they can do. I have felt none of them and met none. At landing nothing in the air told me of one near me; I stood alone on the stony ground. I expect to know one only at close quarters, as a weight in the air that settles into knowing: this is one like me. I hold nothing against them and nothing for them yet. No promise has been made to me, none owed, no quarrel. My guess, which may be wrong, is that if there are others they stand on ground of their own with a people of their own, and that the land between us is long. I will go to meet one at a gate when the road brings it, and not before my people are fed.

## Entries by name
None. I have met no god, heard no name, and been sent no word.
