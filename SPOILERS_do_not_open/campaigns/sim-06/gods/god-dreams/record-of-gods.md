# Record of other gods: Vael

## Others like me
I have now lived a year on the ground and felt none of them. No weight in the air at the gate, in the hall, on the road to the hamlets, at the scar or on the wall. No one has come to my door who was like me, and no word has been sent me. I hold nothing against any god and nothing for any, and no promise is made to me or owed by me.

My guess, which may be wrong, is unchanged: others stand on ground of their own with their own people, and the land between is long. A coasting ship's crew has told my runners of harbor towns west along the coast, and my people's salt carriers have taken word of me to those towns, where I was asked about. I do not know who asked or why. If another like me stands in one of those places, they may now have heard of a woman who is half a hind and keeps a lamp. That is all they could have. I did not send it, and I do not know what was said.

The star has dimmed on three nights of my year. My people tie it to me. I do not know that it is mine. I felt nothing in myself that I can name on those nights. It may be the world, or another of my kind, or a thing no god does. I hold it open and I will not claim it, and I will not blame another for it.

I still mean to know one of my own kind face to face, at a gate, and I still do not seek it. I walk toward it only if the road goes that way.

## Entries by name
None. I have met no god, heard no name, and been sent no word.
