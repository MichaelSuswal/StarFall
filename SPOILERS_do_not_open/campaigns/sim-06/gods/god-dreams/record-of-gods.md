# Record of other gods: Vael

## Others like me
I have now lived two years on the ground and felt none of them. No weight in the air at the gate, in the hall, on the wall, at the scar, on the hamlet roads or at the Night Sitter's work yard. No one like me has come to my door and no word has been sent me. I hold nothing against any god and nothing for any, and no promise is made to me or owed by me.

My guess, which may be wrong, is unchanged: others stand on ground of their own with their own people, and the land between is long. What is new this year is only what my court's party brought home. No ship lay at the river's mouth. The harbor town where the salt is boiled is a walking journey round the shore from the river, and its people now speak freely of "the guest at the bend who keeps a lamp." They asked, and the leader told them nothing of what the lamp does. A house there sets the price of salt for the bend and will set a fair standing price by the year, for stone cut at my people's scar to its order. None of this says that a god is there. It says only that word of me has gone along the coast and has been talked of, and that I do not know what has been added to it on the way. If another like me stands on that coast, they may have heard it. I did not send it beyond what I wrote, and I do not know what was said.

The western party of four found no one and no smoke in the hills. I do not take that to mean no one stands there. They walked what they walked, and the hills are wide.

The star dimmed on five nights of my second year, against three in my first: in the second, sixth, ninth, eleventh and twelfth months. Each time the blue deepened and the star stood dim about an hour in the middle of the night. The Keeper of the Count's cord has them with the dyings: on two of the five a person died in the town and on three no one did. I felt nothing in myself that I can name on those nights that I did not feel on others. It may be the world, or another of my kind, or a thing no god does. I hold it open. I do not claim it and I do not blame another for it.

I still mean to know one of my own kind face to face, at a gate, and I still do not seek it. I walk toward it only if the road goes that way.

## Entries by name
None. I have met no god, heard no name, and been sent no word.
