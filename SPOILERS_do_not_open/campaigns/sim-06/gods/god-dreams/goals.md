# Goals: Vael (Year 1, on the ground)

1. Be let in at the gate as a guest, and be known. I am not yet theirs and they are not yet mine. No office holder acts for me until he has heard me and taken my word. I will win that slowly and honestly, at the table, and not by showing off what I can do.
2. Get the town through its first winter. Grain in, fuel in, the old and the small fed first. I do not yet know what the store holds. Learning that from the Steward of the Store is the first thing I ask after the meal.
3. Make the nights safe, one door at a time. Light a flame only in a house that asks. Begin with the court's hearth, then the households with a deathbed, a newborn, a fever or a fresh grief. Take on the fear of two households a night at most this year, until I learn what the cost does to me in this place. Keep the seventh place for the heart's choosing, and hold it empty until I know whom I would put there.
4. Learn what the people fear in sleep, and what walks the long night here. Sit with the Night Sitter. Learn what she does and what it costs her. I will not take her work from her.
5. Learn the neighbours and the ground: the river, the scar where stone is cut, the hamlets, the road to the hill country, and where salt might come from. Knowledge before I commit my people to any quarrel.
6. Find one of my kind face to face and know them. I do not seek it this year. I walk toward it only if the road goes that way.
7. Keep my own rule: I give no orders in dreams, ever. The throne and the court stand between me and the people by day, and I will not go around them.
8. Hold the lamp. It is the whole of Keeping Sleep, and anyone who learns that can take my people's kept sleep by taking it. I do not tell it to strangers, I do not hand it to any hand, and I do not set it down out of reach.
9. Worship, only as shrines and lamps, kept by whoever keeps them. A companion who is not a god and who will tell me when I am wrong, if one comes. The empty chair at the long table, if I find where it stands.
