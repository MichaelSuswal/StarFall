# Goals: Vael (on arrival)

1. Gather my people close. Find them, bring them behind walls in a river bend, and see that they know each other by name. Security against the dark and the hungry winter comes before anything else.
2. Make the nights safe. Learn what walks in the long night and what the people most fear in sleep, and have a lit shrine in every house before the first hard cold. Land: good soil that floods and forgives, with stone near enough to wall the town.
3. Be fed through the winter. Grain in, salt and warmth found, and no one lost to it who need not be: the old and the small first. Trade for salt, if there are neighbours.
4. Learn the neighbours. Their laws, their old kingdoms, their roads, and what lies in the country no one has walked. Knowledge before I commit my people to a quarrel.
5. Find one of my kind, face to face at a gate, and know them. I do not want a pull from far off. I want to walk toward it.
6. Find what is buried that was made with care by hands that wanted to shelter someone, and the old grave no one tends, which my people may choose to tend.
7. Worship, only as shrines and lamps: modest, kept by whoever keeps them. I do not need more. A companion who is not a god, who does not worship me, who will tell me when I am wrong.
