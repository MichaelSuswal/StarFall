# Negotiation: god-sea, Night 3

## Round 1 reply

### What was read and not priced
Agreed as read.

### Your landing: the helpers and the seat
I choose (a). Keep the meeting as I wrote it, at the cost named.
Reasons: I wished to be seen rarely and doubted often, and I trust no one yet. A people who already know me on sight would carry my shape in every mouth, and a hidden god cannot be that. I would rather win each office in play, from a holder who hears me and takes my word, than be handed an office that serves a name I have not earned. I accept that until then nobody acts for me while I am away and my unplayed time runs on my standing conduct alone. The town goes its own way under its own court, and I would not have it otherwise while I do not know it.
I do not claim the line of lamp-wardens as mine in fact. The promise they keep is theirs, and I do not know who made it.

### The spears
Accept. About one hundred and fifty, a levy drilled some days a year, ordinary and not priced.

### Their magic
Accept. One or two who work a small thing, written in their record, each bearing her own cost, ordinary and no edge. I ask nothing of them and do not ask to be told who they are.
