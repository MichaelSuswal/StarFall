# Record of other gods

- I have met none, felt none, heard none. A year on the shore and the weight in the air has not come once.
- I do not know how many, what they are, or where. I feel one only close by, face to face, and it tells me nothing else.
- Trust: none given, none withheld. No grievance, no promise, no debt.
- The star dimmed on three nights this year, twice in the first month and once in the fifth, each time for about an hour in the middle of the night. My people have seen such nights in other years. This year many tied them to me. I did nothing on those nights that I know of, and I do not know the cause. I note it. I do not read it as another god, and I do not read it as not one.
- Brennis, a lamp warden of old, to whom the eldest say the promise was made. I do not know the name. I cannot say I am not the one who spoke to him, and I cannot say I am. A god before me, or another like me, could have made that promise. I hold it as a question.
- My guess, which may be wrong: any other will want a people as I do, and will not be gentle about it. I judge from close quarters only.
