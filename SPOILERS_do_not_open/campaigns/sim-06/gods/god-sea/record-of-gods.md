# Record of other gods

- Others like me came down when I did. I believe it only because I was told to expect it. I have met none, felt none, heard of none.
- I do not know how many, what they are, or where. I will feel one only close by, as a weight in the air, and it will tell me nothing else.
- Trust: none given, none withheld. No grievance, no promise, no debt.
- My guess, which may be wrong: any other will want a people as I do, and will not be gentle about it. I judge from close quarters only.
