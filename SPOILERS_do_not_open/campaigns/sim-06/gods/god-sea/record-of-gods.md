# Record of other gods

- I have met none, felt none, heard none. Two years on the shore and the weight in the air has not come once. I felt no other like myself this year either.
- I do not know how many, what they are, or where. I feel one only close by, face to face, and it tells me nothing else.
- Trust: none given, none withheld. No grievance, no promise, no debt.
- The star dimmed on five nights this year: the second, sixth, ninth, eleventh and twelfth months, each time about an hour in the middle of the night, the blue deepening. Last year it was three. The count is rising. My people tie the nights to the sea and the step. I did nothing on those nights that I know of, and I do not know the cause. I note it. I do not read it as another god, and I do not read it as not one. If it is one, it is not near, because I felt nothing.
- Brennis, a lamp warden of old, to whom the eldest say the promise was made: whoever keeps the lamp is kept count of. The eldest say it was made by something that came up the quay steps and went down them again, and that the bell was hung so it could be called. Most of the town says Brennis made it herself, to the town. The old woman at the east lamp had it from her grandmother that the thing on the step did not ask for a name, and Brennis did not give one. I do not know the name and I cannot say I am not the thing on the step, and I cannot say I am. I arrived in the autumn of two years ago, not before. A god before me, or another like me, or a thing from below, could have come up those steps. I hold it as a question, and I do not claim the promise.
- A net-mender tells of Low Garrow, a town the sea took in one night. The tellers do not agree whether the promise is older or younger than that town. If a god or a thing from below was in that, I do not know it.
- On the harbor bed inside the mouth I found the broken lower timbers of two boats of the town's own build, not long in the water. Nothing else made by hands, nothing kept. Beyond the skerries, sand and weed. I found no sign of any god's hand in the water near my people.
- My guess, which may be wrong: any other will want a people as I do, and will not be gentle about it. I judge from close quarters only. I will not hold the promise against any other nor claim it for myself.
