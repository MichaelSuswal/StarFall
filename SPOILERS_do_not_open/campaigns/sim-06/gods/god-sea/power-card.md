# Power card: Skerr

Bynames: none.

Confirmed on Night 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific), by the god's round 2 reply.

## Form

One body, as every god has in this campaign.

A half-thing. A man's body to the hips, broad in the chest and shoulders, grey-skinned and salt-cracked, with a long plain face, heavy brow, no beard, and dark wet eyes set a little wide, like a seal's. Below the hips it is a beast of the sea: a thick tapering body, grey and mottled, ending in two paired hind flippers. Roughly the length of two men laid end to end, and heavier than a big man. Its left hand is burned and fused to a club of scar, the fingers gone to knuckle. Its right hand is whole and broad, built for work. Its eyes see well in dim light and badly in hard sun. It hears very well, especially through water and stone. It smells salt, tar, rot and weather from far off.
On land it drags itself on its forearms and the flipper-heels, slowly, at about the pace of an old man with a stick, and it tires. In water it is fast and does not tire. It wears a heavy hooded cloak and sits low, and in a dim room, under the cloak, it can pass for a big hunched man.

What breaks it: Being held out of water for long, which saps its strength. A net or chains it cannot drag. Fire to the beast half, which burns badly. Being driven out to open sea and kept from shore, scattered by weight of storm and current. Being shut in dry stone. A blade or club can hurt it and can pin it, but cannot end it.

God-sense: the god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing that this is one like itself. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.

When the body is broken: see the power Return from the Deep.

Keeper trait: Where you have lain a night in the water, the sea goes quiet: gulls and seals leave that stretch for some days, and fishers notice the empty shore. Its reach: it marks the water where you rested and nothing else; it gives no one your shape or your name, and it touches none of your powers or their prices.

### Definitions

- A day's walk: twenty miles.

### Things carried

- A small iron hammer with a worn ash handle.
- The lamp, of clay glazed with salt, is not ordinary: it is the instrument of Calm Under the Lamp and is written under Weaknesses taken.

## Powers

### Water's Hearing
Status: main
Description: With its right hand in salt water and a night given to listening, the god hears what the sea has taken up in the last day along that coast, within about a day's walk (twenty miles) of where it listens: voices at the shore, hulls, oars, bodies falling in, things moving along the bottom. Sounds only, never faces or names, and never why a sound was made.
Drawback: For a day after a listening, the god hears nothing spoken on land. Mortal speech is a hum to it.
Limits settled at creation:
- Salt water only; nothing through fresh water or inland.
- Nothing from past the shelf.
- It cannot choose which sounds come.
- A storm drowns everything but the storm.
- Distance: twenty miles along the coast, no further.
- Nothing a watcher sees marks it.
- The god's own people: their sounds come as anyone's.
- A mind: it rules none.
- Use across a year: one night for one listening, and a deaf day after each.
- A scarcity: it touches none.
- More than ordinary craft: a sound made or hidden by more than craft may not come true; unruled until it happens.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying.
- First wording, kept as intent: "With its right hand in salt water, and a night given to listening, it hears what the water has carried."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Calm Under the Lamp
Status: main
Description: With the god's lamp lit by its own hand and set at the water's edge or on a boat's bow, the water of one bay or harbor lies calm as far as the lamp's light would show on a still night: no swell, no chop, no breaking surf. The lamp may be refilled and tended, and the calm holds while it burns, up to a night and a day; then it must go out. It ends when the lamp goes out for any reason.
Drawback: The water it stilled will not take the god for a season after. It cannot swim there, and its beast half goes stiff and heavy if it touches that water.
Limits settled at creation:
- It stops no storm, stills no open sea, and does nothing to what lives in the water.
- Anyone may snuff the lamp.
- Being watched: the lamp is seen; the calm reads as chance to those who do not know.
- Carrying: only the first lighting needs the god's own hand; anyone may refill and tend it after, and the god need not stay.
- The god's own people: the calm serves anyone on that water.
- A mind: none.
- A scarcity: it makes a harbor safe for a night and makes no harbor.
- Use across a year: each stretch of water stilled is closed to the god for a season.
- Not applicable: falling.
- First wording, kept as intent: "It wants a safe harbor and time before the storm." The night and a day is what the trade bought; before it the calm lasted one filling of oil.
Rulings:
Trades: its instrument, the lamp, is the weakness that bought the longer hold (see Weaknesses taken).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Sound Timber
Status: main
Description: Timber the god shapes and joins with its own right hand (a keel, a strake, a pile, a door-post) does not rot, split or take worm in salt water for a generation of ordinary use. The work goes at the pace of the craft, one piece at a time, and is ordinary craft in every other way.
Drawback: Each piece costs the god its right hand's strength for a day: it cannot hold a hammer or grip a rope, and the burned left hand cannot do the work.
Limits settled at creation:
- Only timber the god itself shaped.
- Not stone or iron.
- Lasting, not stronger; fire burns it.
- It looks well made and nothing more.
- Use across a year: one piece, then a day without the hand.
- A scarcity: sound timber may be short where the god lands; the power makes timber last and makes none.
- More than ordinary craft: none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, distance, falling, water, being watched, other gods, a mind.
- First wording, kept as intent: "It does not make timber strong, only lasting."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Return from the Deep
Status: main (the return)
Description: When the god's body is broken, it returns whole after a season at the nearest shore of the sea. What it carries comes back with it.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit at each return. The item is taken by a logged roll from the god's own record, and the god is not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- The standing scale's season rung.
- It returns to the sea's edge, not to its people; the walk is added.
- A body held or shut out is not broken, and nothing returns until the body is broken.
- If another holds the lamp when the body breaks, it stays where it is.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The lamp as the instrument
Calm Under the Lamp comes only through the god's lamp. If it is taken, lost or given, the power is gone until the god holds it again. In any other hand it is only a lamp, and no one else gains the power. If it is destroyed, the power is gone until the god's next return, when it comes back with the body. If another holds it when the body breaks, it stays where it is. Anyone who learns this can take the calm from the god by taking the lamp; no one gains it.
It bought: the longer hold of Calm Under the Lamp, a night and a day with the lamp refilled and tended.

## Trades
Offered once, on Night 2. Taken: the lamp as the instrument, for the longer hold. Not taken: the wider reach, a keeper lighting in its name. Trades are closed for the rest of creation.
