
## Round 1 reply

### Water's Hearing
Accept as drafted. Twenty miles is enough. The deaf day stands.

### Calm Under the Lamp
Accept as drafted. The season of refusal stands. I ask one clarification, in the write-up, not a new power: that lighting by my own hand is needed only to begin the calm, and that the calm reads as chance to those who do not know.

### Sound Timber
Accept as drafted. One piece, then a day without the hand. I want it to last, not to be quick.

### Return from the Deep
Accept as drafted, season rung, one item. I take the correction: held and shut out do not bring a return, only a broken body. I still wish it to be so.

### The trade: the lamp as the instrument
Accept the weakness as redrafted: the power comes only through my lamp, in any other hand it is only a lamp, and if it is destroyed the power waits for my next return. I take the correction on both points.
I choose (a), the longer hold: the lamp may be refilled and tended, and the calm holds while it burns, up to a night and a day, then it must go out.
Reasons: I bargain for duration and for what survives. A harbor calm for one filling is a night's grace; a night and a day lets a boat go out and come home. I do not take (c), because I trust no keeper yet and have named none. I do not take (b), because a small harbor held longer serves a small people better than a wide water held briefly. I do not ask for a second weakness.
One clarification, in the write-up, not a new power: under (a), whoever refills and tends the lamp is anyone, not only me; only the first lighting needs my hand. Without this, (a) would bind me to the shore for a day and a night.

### Read, not priced
Agreed as read.
