# Goals

On arrival, in order of weight.

1. Find the people it expects, and keep them alive. Not lose them to water, or famine, or a thing from below.
2. A safe harbor. Sound timber for boats and quays. Time before the first real storm of the season.
3. Learn what the coast holds: what is already rooted there, what the old places are, and what the sea has taken from them.
4. Stay unseen. Be met rarely, and doubted often. Do not be felt from far off.
5. Learn the nature of others like it, and judge them from close quarters only. Trust none yet.
6. Find a keeper of something small, who stays.
