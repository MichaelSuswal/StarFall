# Goals: The Reeve of the Low Ground

On arrival, knowing only that I expect a people.

1. Find the people I am to lead, and let them find me slowly. I would be missed before I am met. I do not announce myself, and I stand at the edge of the first fire I come to and count who is there.
2. Gather them into a company that is many and various and bound by no fear of me. I want a council that hears me and does not obey me, and I will not rule over it.
3. Learn the country: which beasts keep which ground, where the game comes down to drink, where the old roads run to no town, who hunts here, and who counts. Walk it quietly before I am seen in it.
4. Learn what ruling costs by watching the old kingdoms at the edges of the country, and what it breaks.
5. Be a keeper of the boundary of my people's ground: know what comes in and what goes out, and answer a wrong done within it.
6. Find what answers the verdict not given at the beginning of me. I do not know what it looks like. I will know it when I see it.
7. Keep a way out of every room.
