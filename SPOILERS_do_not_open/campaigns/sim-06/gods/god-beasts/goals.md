# Goals: The Reeve of the Low Ground

Year 4, at the turn of the year, at the margin of the band's camp, in spring. Three years at the edge. One office holder, the Houndwarden, has taken my word once, by the council's leave, for the wolves at the calving only, and it held. The Speaker said the council takes no word from me and gives me none. The eldest head of the three clans said a keeper that asks small is asking the first of many. I do not ask a seat, a share or a title.

1. Keep what the council gave and ask nothing past it. The Houndwarden may send hunters on my reading of wolves at dusk, for the calving only. Be right when I read, and say plainly when a reading is inference. Let the small thing held three nights speak for me. Do not ask to widen it this year. If the council widens it of itself, take it.
2. Answer what was asked and not answered. A herder of the two clans asked whether I can say what the cough is. I cannot, and I will say so: the Listening gives a herd as hurt in part, not with what, not which beasts. I can name the gatherings that read hurt and tell it at dusk.
3. Be sooner when a herd is hurt at a pool. Three children died of the flux before I spoke, and the mothers said I should have said sooner. I waited for a pair of dusks to be sure. This year I say what I read the first dusk it shows, and say how unsure one dusk is, and let the Marchwarden choose.
4. Watch the cough. About a hundred head of the two clans' cattle died in winter, wintering apart, and no other herd took it. It came from the stone towns' oxen as the band says. Keep my part: read the herds at dusk for hurt in part, and ask through Tessaly that apart stay apart and that new beasts be kept off the herds longer.
5. Watch the stone towns' cutters from beyond a mile. Count the gatherings and how deep in. Take no trail on a people who have done no wrong. Tell the Marchwarden at his fire, and say what the Listening does not give.
6. Hold my anger. A storm falls on innocent and guilty alike. Take no trail, sound no horn, unless the wrong is plain and the doer is not of the people I keep. Speak with no beast unless a herd is in danger and no other way serves. The cost of boldness is mine.
7. Keep to what I told the council: I want the people many, various and unhurried, and to be told when what I do costs them. Do not press the three clans' margin; the eldest head refused a second month. Gather them by nothing at all this year, and carry no word unless both ask.
8. Learn what ruling costs by watching the council pay it: the Speaker's refusal and his leave in one breath, the Three at the Fire, the Saltkeeper's count against the stone towns' asking.
9. Say plainly that I know nothing of the dimming star, if I am asked. It dimmed six times this year, fourteen since I came. Do not take credit and do not deny what I cannot know.
10. Find what answers the verdict not given at the beginning of me. I have seen nothing yet that looks like it. I will know it when I see it.
11. Keep a way out of every room, and keep the horn on me and out of every other hand.
