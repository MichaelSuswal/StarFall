
## Round 1 reply

I take the card texts as drafted, with one clarification asked in the write-up, not a new power, and one point argued on the horn.

### The Listening
Accepted as written. The empty place where my own people stand is the right shape for it; a shepherd does not spy on the flock. I keep the stillness and the deafness and the cold weight of every death, and I would not have them eased.

### Beast Tongue
Accepted as written. The boldness that stays when I am gone is a hard price, because it falls on the people I keep, and I accept it for that reason.

### The Trail
Accepted as written. The cold or broken trail is the only end I asked for, and I ask for no more of a fence than that.

### The Horn Call
Accepted as written, including the added line that the call tells every person in earshot where I am. I did not offer it, but a horn is loud and it is true, and I would rather take it as a price than argue it away. One clarification, in the write-up, not a new power: "stay while it holds the note and about a quarter of an hour after" should read that the quarter hour runs from the last note sounded, so that a god who sounds again keeps them again.

### The Fed Storm
Accepted as written, including the heavier part. I do not wish it lighter. A storm that flattens what it should feed ought to cost the one who is angry, and I want to feel the dead of that flood.

### Return by the Herd
Accepted as written. I accept the Keeper's correction that no hand but mine gains the horn's power, and that a horn another holds stays where it is. I accept the added walk. I ask only that the write-up say plainly that the one item of memory or habit is taken at each return and that I am not told which.

### The trade
I choose (a): the horn as the instrument, for the Listening at about ten miles in open country and about three in timber, with only gatherings known past the first mile. I accept the narrowing. A hidden walker alone in ten miles was more than I needed, and a herd, a pack and a band are what a keeper of a boundary wants to know.

I accept that a destroyed horn means the power is gone until my next return, and that my draft of a new horn from a beast freely given is withdrawn. I argue one thing here, as reasons and not as a refusal. Of a body that cannot die, a return is the only door back, and so a destroyed horn can mean a long loss of the Horn Call, and with it the ten mile reach if the Keeper reads the trade as resting on the horn. I ask that the write-up say that the Listening's reach beyond a mile, as bought, is lost only while the horn is held by another or destroyed, as the weakness says, and that the Listening at a mile and the Listening's other terms stand always. I take the weakness as it is written and ask for no fence on where the horn may be carried.

### Read, not priced
Accepted. The staff is ordinary, and my slowness to speak, my way out of every room and my indifference are conduct.
