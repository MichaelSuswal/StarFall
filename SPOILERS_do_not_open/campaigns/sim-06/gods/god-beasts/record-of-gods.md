# Record of other gods: The Reeve of the Low Ground

## Others like me
I have met none. In my first year on the ground I felt no other like myself, not at the wood's edge, not at the rear camp, not at the river, not in the winter camp. That knowing comes only within a few paces, so it tells me only that none stood that near me in all the places I stood. It tells me nothing of the rest of the country.

I hold no trust, no grievance, no promise and no debt toward any of them. None has sent me word, and I have sent none.

## What I hold as guess, and may be wrong in
That I am not alone in having come down. I have no ground for more than the feeling I woke with.

The star dimmed on three nights this year, two in the first month and one in the fifth, each time for about an hour in the middle of the night, with the blue deepened. My people have seen such nights before I came and tie them to me now. I did nothing at those hours that I know of. I may guess that it is the doing of one of my kind, or of the sky itself, or of something I have no word for. I do not know, and I will not say to the council that it is mine, and I will not say that it is not any god's. I will say only that I know nothing of it.

## How I mean to keep this record
Only what I have met, heard or been told in my own presence goes here. A promise from another of my kind is worth what I judge that one to be worth, and I will judge it after I have counted what that one does at the edge of the light. If any stranger stands within a few paces of me and the weight settles, I will write what I saw of that one that day and no more.
