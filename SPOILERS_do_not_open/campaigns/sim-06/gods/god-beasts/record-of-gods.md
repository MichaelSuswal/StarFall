# Record of other gods: The Reeve of the Low Ground

## Others like me
I have met none. In four years on the ground I felt no other like myself, not at the wood's edge, not at the rear camp, not at the river, not at the margin of the three clans, not in either winter camp, not at the hunt's edge, not along the timber in the eighth month, not at the margin of the winter camp this year. That knowing comes only within a few paces, so it tells me only that none stood that near me in all the places I stood. It tells me nothing of the rest of the country.

I hold no trust, no grievance, no promise and no debt toward any of them. None has sent me word, and I have sent none. No message came to me this year.

## What I hold as guess, and may be wrong in
That I am not alone in having come down. I have no ground for more than the feeling I woke with.

The star dimmed on seven nights this year: in the second, third, fourth, eighth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth months. It dimmed on three nights the first year, five the second, six the third, seven this fourth. My people count twenty-one since I came. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. I mark that the count has risen each year by one or two, and I mark no more than that it rises. Three of this year's nights fell in the winter while I stood at the margin of the camp, and I did nothing at those hours that I know of. My people no longer say that I hold it off. The three clans that camp apart say it is the band's being too many to walk as one, and that my not saying no is a yes. I did not say yes. I said I do not know. I hold the clans' reading as a thing they believe and not as a thing I have ground to call true or false: a count that rose by one each year while the band stayed about the same size does not follow the band's size, but I have counted only four years and may be wrong. If it is a god, it is one who does the same thing more often as the years go, and I have no ground to say whose. I will not say to the council that it is mine, and I will not say it is not any god's.

The cutters of the stone towns work a little deeper again, three gatherings with draught beasts, unafraid, read from more than a mile in the seventh and eighth months. I hold it as a weak guess only that a people so patient and counting may have a god behind it. I have felt no weight near them, for I have not gone within a mile of them, and the weight comes only close. The towns' oxen still cough, their iron came grey and soft, and the party's salt came at half what was wanted. I have no ground to name a god in that, and I do not.

## How I mean to keep this record
Only what I have met, heard or been told in my own presence goes here. A promise from another of my kind is worth what I judge that one to be worth, and I will judge it after I have counted what that one does at the edge of the light. If any stranger stands within a few paces of me and the weight settles, I will write what I saw of that one that day and no more.
