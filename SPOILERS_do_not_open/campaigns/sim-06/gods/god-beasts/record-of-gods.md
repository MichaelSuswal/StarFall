# Record of other gods: The Reeve of the Low Ground

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I have not met them, and I know nothing more: not how many, not what they are, not what they can do.

## Year 1, at the landing
I stood at the wood's edge in the hour before first light and felt no other like myself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, so it tells me only that none stood within a few paces of me. It tells me nothing of whether any stand elsewhere in the country. I hold no trust, no grievance, no promise and no debt toward any of them, because I have none to hold them against.

What I hold as guess, and may be wrong in: that I am not alone in having come down. I have no ground for more. I will not take any stranger for one of my kind or for a mortal until I am within a few paces of it and the weight settles.

## How I mean to keep this record
Only what I have met, heard or been told in my own presence goes here. A promise from another of my kind is worth what I judge that one to be worth, and I will judge it after I have counted what that one does at the edge of the light.
