# Power card: god-beasts (no name held)

Bynames: "the Reeve", "the Reeve of the Low Ground" (its own word for itself).

Confirmed on Night 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific), by the god's round 2 reply.

## Form

One body, as every god has in this campaign.

A person's torso, arms and hands rising from the body of a stag: four long hind and fore legs in dun hide, a deep chest, a narrow waist where the two kinds meet and the hide thins into skin. Standing, it is about two and a half paces long from chest to flank and a little over two paces tall to the crown of the head, antlers not counted. The face is a person's face, long and still, the skin weathered grey brown, the eyes wide set and dark with a beast's flat shine in low light, the ears turned and mobile. Antlers of six points a side rise from the brow, pale at the tips, one tine on the left side old and broken short. A dark stripe runs from the crown down the spine to the tail. The hands are a person's hands, large, with a hunter's calluses that it did not earn in any life it remembers.

It sees as well as a person by day and much better at dusk. It hears as a deer hears, and it smells where the wind carries. It moves at a stag's walk, a stag's trot and, for a short time, a stag's bound; it is not faster than a stag. It stands very still, and when it is still and low in cover a watcher will often pass it by. It speaks in a low even voice.

What breaks it: The body is flesh and bone and can be wounded by a spear, an arrow, a fall, fire, a drowning, a crushing, or a cold deep enough. It heals slowly, at the pace of a beast's healing, and a deep wound lames it for a season. It can be held by a pit, a net, a corral, a shut room, or bonds of rope and hide. It can be shut out by walls and gates it cannot lift or leap, and by fire laid across a way. A lung full of smoke, a long chase across open ground by many fresh hunters, or being driven into a dead end will break it. When the body is broken it is scattered, as the beast is scattered in a kill and not as a person is buried.

God-sense: the god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing that this is one like itself. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.

When the body is broken: see the power Return by the Herd.

Keeper trait: Tame beasts know you first: the dogs and herd beasts of any people fall silent and turn to face you when you are within their sight or scent, before any person has seen you. Its reach: it tells a watcher that something is there, and where the beasts are looking, never what you are; it does nothing to wild beasts and touches none of your powers or their prices.

### Definitions

- A gathering: a herd, a pack, or a band of a dozen or more.
- A day's walk: twenty miles.

### Things carried

- A walking staff of ash, shod with antler.
- The hunting horn, bound with sinew and bronze wire, is not ordinary: it is the instrument of The Horn Call and is written under Weaknesses taken.

## Powers

### The Listening
Status: main
Description: Standing still with its attention set outward, the god knows where living things are within about a mile of its body: where, how large, which way they move, and whether each is hunting, grazing, resting, afraid or hurt. It knows beast from person. It does not know a thought, a name, or what a thing intends beyond its present state. It holds for as long as the god stands still. In open country it reaches about ten miles, and in timber about three: beyond the first mile it knows only gatherings (a herd, a pack, a band of a dozen or more), where, which way they move and their state, and not a single beast or person.
Drawback: It never reads the god's own people: they are an empty place in the listening. While it listens the body is still and deaf as a stone, hearing no speech, step or shout until it breaks the listening with a breath of effort. Every kill or death within its reach is felt as a small cold weight, and it cannot choose not to feel it.
Limits settled at creation:
- Through trees, hills and walls; not through deep water or thick earth.
- It does not tell two persons of a size apart.
- One who stands as still as the god is not found, and one very still among beasts reads as a beast.
- Another god reads as a living thing of a size, no more.
- A mind: none.
- Use across a year: as often as it stands still.
- A scarcity: none.
- More than ordinary craft: a thing hidden by more than craft is unruled until it happens.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched.
- The reach beyond a mile was bought by the trade and does not rest on the horn: it stands whether or not the god holds the horn.
- The blind place for its own people was chosen by the god from a pair.
- First wording, kept as intent: "A people it leads is a flock it does not spy on."
Rulings:
Trades: its reach beyond a mile was bought by the weakness "the horn as the instrument".
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Beast Tongue
Status: main
Description: The god understands the voice and bearing of any beast it speaks to and is understood by it, in a beast's plain terms: hunger, fear, ground, young, water, a thing that hunts, a thing that is safe. It can ask where, how many, how long ago, and what the beast fears. It commands no beast, and no beast tells what it does not know.
Drawback: A beast it speaks with loses some of its fear of the god and, over days of company, of people near it. Such beasts come close, linger and grow bold, and stay so when the god is gone.
Limits settled at creation:
- Beasts only: not people, and not things that wear a beast's shape wrongly, which the god knows by their not answering.
- It does not speak through a beast to another place.
- Use across a year: no limit; each speaking leaves its boldness.
- A mind: it rules none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, water, being watched, a scarcity, more than ordinary craft.
- First wording, kept as intent: "A judge of the hunt must be able to hear the hunted."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Trail
Status: main
Description: Holding a thing that was of a wrong done (blood, a scrap, a thing the doer held, a footprint), the god can follow the doer by that trail across any ground, at the pace of its form, for as long as the trail holds, up to about a season after the wrong. It finds the doer. It does not learn the doer's name or reason.
Drawback: Once it takes a trail it cannot put it down until it finds the doer or the trail goes cold. It turns for nothing: no entreaty, no better trail, no need of its own people. And it cannot decide afterward that the wrong was less than it thought.
Limits settled at creation:
- Only what is a wrong in the god's own judgment.
- Deep water, a long burn or hard rain makes a trail go cold; a long stretch by cart or boat breaks it.
- One trail at a time.
- It may refuse a trail and may not give one back.
- A doer that is a god: unruled until it happens.
- A mind: none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched, a scarcity.
- First wording, kept as intent: "it finds the person, not the judgment."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Horn Call
Status: main
Description: When the god sounds its horn, every beast of the wild within hearing, about two miles on open ground and a mile in timber, turns and comes: herds, packs and singles, hunter and hunted together. They gather without attacking one another or the god, stay while it holds the note and about a quarter of an hour after the last note sounded, and then scatter. They are called, not commanded.
Drawback: The god cannot choose who comes. Those that answer are angered for about a day, as beasts pulled off their own ground, and they remember it. The call tells every person in earshot where the god is.
Limits settled at creation:
- Herd beasts of a people within hearing answer too and may break pens.
- Not through a mountain.
- Things no one has a name for do not answer.
- A mind: beasts are called, never ruled.
- Use across a year: no limit; the anger is the cost.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, water, a scarcity.
- A god that sounds again keeps them again, and each sounding carries the drawback anew.
- First wording, kept as intent: "They do not obey; they are called, and what they do after is their own."
Rulings:
Trades: its instrument, the horn, is the weakness that bought the longer Listening (see Weaknesses taken).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The Fed Storm
Status: main
Description: When the god is angry in earnest at a wrong it has judged and not yet answered, a storm gathers within about three days over the country a day's walk around it, and breaks. It waters the ground and fills the low places. It comes of the god's anger and not its will: it cannot be called for rain's sake or sent away.
Drawback: The storm falls on innocent and guilty alike, and on ground the god does not choose; it floods the low places and can drown the young of the herds. It does not end until the anger is answered. And, heavier than the power needs and written as asked: at the storm's height the god feels the dead of that flood as it feels a kill in The Listening, listening or not.
Limits settled at creation:
- Temper or vexation brings none; only a judged wrong.
- It strikes no one by name.
- Being watched: it is weather to any who do not know.
- The god's own people are not spared.
- Use across a year: only as often as the god is so angered.
- A mind: none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, a scarcity.
- The feeling of the flood's dead is a heavier price than the power needed, written as the god asked; nothing was given for it.
- First wording, kept as intent: "when the storm comes it feeds a people and does not flatten them."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Return by the Herd
Status: main (the return)
Description: When the god's body is broken, it stands up whole after a season among beasts at the nearest wild ground where game lives. What it carries comes back with it, the horn too if it was on the god when the body broke.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit at each return. The item is taken by a logged roll from the god's own record, and the god is not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- The standing scale's season rung.
- A body held is not broken.
- The walk is added.
- If another holds the horn when the body breaks, it stays where it is, and in that hand it is only a horn.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The horn as the instrument
The Horn Call comes only through the god's horn. If it is held or carried away by another, the power is gone until the god holds it again. In any other hand it is only a horn, and no one else gains the power. A copy, or any other horn, does nothing. If it is destroyed, the power is gone until the god's next return, when it comes back with the body. If another holds it when the body breaks, it stays where it is. Anyone who learns this can take the call by taking the horn.
It bought: The Listening's reach beyond a mile, to about ten miles in open country and three in timber, gatherings only.

## Trades
Offered once, on Night 2. Taken: the horn as the instrument, for the longer Listening. Not taken: the reach in full for a second weakness. Trades are closed for the rest of creation.
