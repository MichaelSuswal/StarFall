
## Round 1 reply

### Your landing: the helpers and the seat
I choose (a). Keep the meeting as I wrote it, at the cost the Keeper names. I land with no helpers in fact and no seat that is mine in fact, and I accept that nobody acts for me while I am away until each office is won in play, and that my unplayed time runs on my standing conduct alone.

My reasons. I wish to be missed before I am met, and an office that served me on sight would be a people that already knew me, which is the opposite of what I asked for. I want a council that hears me and does not obey me, and a helper whose service I earned is worth more to me than one I was handed. I also take the plain reading of the cost: an office holds my word only once its holder has heard me and chosen to take it, and I will not ask the Keeper to soften that. I do not choose (b).

### The spears
Accepted as written. About nine hundred who can carry a spear, about three hundred hunters by trade, ordinary and not priced. I note and accept that these are a call-up and not a body kept, and that I have asked for no more.

### Their magic
Accepted as written. The Still One's cost is the whole price, she trains no one, and it ends with her unless play makes another. I accept that there may be one or two others among five thousand who work some other small thing, and that who they are is my people's own. I ask for nothing about them.

### Names
Accepted as written. The name I gave my people, and the two names I hoped for, are given in my words, and I accept that a mortal I named may turn out otherwise.

### Read, not priced
Accepted. I proposed no edge and owe no weakness. The habits are tempers and may fail. What I said of the land is my wish and the world decides. Who each office holder is stands as who they are, never what they will do, and each may fail me in play. Skills and culture are ordinary and free.

## Round 2 reply

I confirm. This is what I meant, and I ask for no change.

The landing stands as written: designed, not yet serving, with no helpers or seat in fact until each office is won in play. The spears, the Still One's magic and the names stand as written. No weakness is owed. I accept that where my people's ground stands, and what the land round it holds, is the world's and is written from the map.
