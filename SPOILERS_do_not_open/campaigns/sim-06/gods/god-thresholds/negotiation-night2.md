# Negotiation: god-thresholds, Night 2

## Round 1 reply

I take most of the draft. Where I do not, I say why. I bargain hard because I mean to keep what I am given, and I accept what I accept because I judge the price fair, not to be done.

### The Latch
Choice: (a), the guest's law on herself. I refuse (b). Under (b) a single shut door anywhere closes every door to my own hand, and I am afraid of nothing so much as a door that will not open for me. I would live in dread of my own power. (a) is the cost of the guest I am: I do not walk uninvited into a house I have not made my own, and I would notice it every day. I take it gladly.

Reasons and asks:
- On the oath: I concede it is a Limit and buys nothing. I keep it as written. I note only that it already binds me daily at every table I sit at, so I will not be asked to call it twice.
- Clarification, in the write-up and not a new power: the act of blessing a threshold, standing in it to lay the salt and speak the oath, is not a crossing of a threshold I have not blessed. Without that the power cannot be begun. I ask that the write-up say so.
- Clarification, in the write-up: "one who keeps it bids her in" means any person who keeps that door in fact, host, steward or doorkeeper, by word or by plain gesture of welcome. A refusal by a keeper is a refusal; a door with no keeper is a door she may not cross unblessed unless she blesses it standing in it.
- A ford or pass cannot be shut: accepted. A gate set in a pass, with a frame and leaf, is a door and may be shut. I ask that this be written.
- The seven and the spent salt: accepted.

### The Step
Choice: (a). I take the cost that falls on me and not on my doors: after a step I may not go beyond sight of the door I arrived in until the dawn after a full day, and I may not shut or lift it. I do not take (b). A step once between new moons is a power I cannot use for my people on the move; (a) lets me step as often as I am willing to pay.
Clarifications, in the write-up and not a new power:
- "Beyond sight" means beyond what my own eyes can see of the door on the open ground, by day or night, with no allowance for distance I could walk. I may go anywhere within sight of it, inside the house, around it, and up to the limit of my own seeing.
- "The dawn after a full day" means the first dawn that falls at least a full day after the step. A step at noon frees me at the dawn of the day after next.
- The one I lead by the hand is held by the same limit only if I choose to stay with them; the limit is on me alone.
Reach: I ask nothing more. I would rather have a power with a cost I feel than a bigger power priced as a gift.

### The Breaker's Hand
Choice: (b). The breaking unbars me: for a day after, none of my blessed thresholds will shut. I refuse (a): an arm that hangs useless until dawn leaves me one body that can be taken by anyone while the arm is down, and a god afraid of being held should not be asked to be helpless after every stroke. (b) is a real cost to a god of doors. When I break a door, none of mine will keep for a day. I take it, and I will feel it.
- On the Keeper's remark that "she can mend nothing she has broken" restates what a breaker is: I accept that it is conduct and buys nothing, and I do not argue it as a price. I keep it written, since it is true of me. I ask that it not be counted a second drawback against me elsewhere.
- Clarification: "a thing held shut" includes a threshold a keeper has barred against a refused guest, a door held against a traveler, and a seal of a house. It does not include a door held shut by a living body leaning on it. I would like the write-up to say so, because that is the case I expect to meet first.
- I accept the Limits as written.

### The Reading of the Host
Accepted as drafted. The Limits are fair. I ask one clarification, in the write-up: "learns the same of her" means that the one read learns that I have read them, and learns whether I mean harm to a guest at this table or crossing, the same one answer I get. I do not ask for it to be narrower, and I do not accept it broader.

### The Return Home
Accepted as drafted: the month rung, two items at each return, the nearest standing doorway of a house where people live or gate of a wall, and the walk added.
Clarification, in the write-up: the doorway is the nearest to the place my body broke, measured in the plainest way, and I arrive in the threshold itself, not inside a room. I also ask that the write-up say that if the key was in another's hand when my body broke, it stays where it is, as the standing rule says, and I come back without it and without The Latch or The Step until I hold it again.
I note without asking for relief that this is the part of my card where I am least able to bargain, and I say plainly that I took the month because I fear to be away from my people longer.

### The trade: the key as the instrument
Choice: (a), as written. I take the weakness. I know it is heavy: two powers hang on one cord about my neck, and I will carry it as I carry the rest. I refuse (c) because I would lose the purchase, and I do not name a different weakness (b), because the key is a thing I would feel missing, which is what a weakness should be.

What it buys: the lesser marks of my priesthood. I counter on one point, and give my reasons.
- The Keeper writes that a lesser mark counts among my seven. I ask that the lesser marks have a count of their own, seven, apart from the seven I hold in full. My reason: a mark that crowds out my own is a mark I will never ask my priesthood to lay, and then the trade buys me nothing. A lesser mark is a thing known to me and cannot be shut and cannot be stepped to, so it does less than a full mark, and it should not cost me one of the full seven. The step bought is the same single improvement I asked for: helpers acting in my name. I am asking the improvement to be of use, not for a second one.
- If the Keeper will not move on that, I will accept the lesser marks counted among the seven as drafted, rather than lose the trade. I say so now so that the Keeper knows what the counter is worth to me: I would take it, but it would be a poorer purchase.
- Edge case to be written: a lesser mark laid on a doorway of a place my people hold, and then the place is lost to them, stays marked and known to me, though no longer held by my people. I would not have it vanish.
- The rule that a priest must have taken the oath from me in person: accepted.

### Read, not priced
Accepted as written. The salt is spent by The Latch; the cloak and staff are ordinary; my fear and my quick hand are who I am.
