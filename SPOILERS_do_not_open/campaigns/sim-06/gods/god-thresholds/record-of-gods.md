# Record of other gods: god-thresholds

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, where they stand, or what they can do. I will know one only when I stand near it, in the same room or across a doorway, and feel the weight of it settle into this is one like me, and that will tell me nothing more.

## What I have met
No one, in four years. I have stood at the ford, the city gate, the hall door, both passes, the farthest toll house, the old cutting at the high gates and the rite fires of the lower and the upper columns, and sat at the low quarter tables and at the tables of the warlord, the First Doorwarden, the senior Doorwardens, a river trader, a packer and a captain's cousin. In Year 4 I felt no weight in the air anywhere, at the ford, the hall, the farthest toll house door, the high grass, or the table where I sat with Kessan and Idris over the cords. I name no god, I hold no grievance, I am owed no promise and I owe none.

## What four years taught me about the watching
- A mortal at a table who reads clean tells me nothing about whether I stand near one of my own kind. The weight in the air is the only sign I trust, and I have felt none.
- My Reading does not work on a god. If I ever read across a table and get no answer where I should get one, that is a thing to note, not to trust. I have not met it yet.
- The strangers the Marshal brings are fewer each year and always ordinary. A god who wished to be found would be easier to find than I am, and one who wished not to be found would not come to a toll house. Neither is knowledge.
- My people and the far houses are most of what I have seen of this country. A god could stand a long way off, in a place with no door I have blessed, and I would not know it. Four years of standing at crossings is not a search of the world. That nothing has turned up is not that nothing is there.
- The chancery of the river kingdom wrote to whoever holds the ford. That is a mortal king's clerk, not a god. I sent my word to it in autumn of Year 4 by a master of a mixed train and have had no answer and no envoy. I do not know what stands behind the old power it names, and I will not guess it into a god because I want one.

## The river kingdom's trains, and the star
In the tenth, eleventh and twelfth months of Year 4 no train of the river kingdom crossed the road. Other peoples' trains crossed as before. In those same three months the star dimmed. I note the match and I do not trust it. It may be the same cause, it may be two causes, it may be a coincidence of three months out of twelve. The star also dimmed in the second, third, fourth and eighth months, when the river trains still came, so the star does not follow the trains.
The star's count since I came: three nights in the first year, five in the second, six in the third, seven in the fourth. Twenty-one in all. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. The number rises by one or two each year. It does not follow my marks: I laid three full blessings in the first year and three dimmings came, one in the second and five, none in the third and six, one in the fourth and seven. I have no power that touches the sky and I do not know whose it is. It may be another like me. It may be the world's own weather. I hold all of these as guesses. If a household asks me, I say I do not know.

## What I will watch for
- The weight in the air at a gate, a hall door, a toll house, a ford, anywhere strangers cross and I stand near. If it comes I will stand still, let it settle, and look at the face before I decide anything.
- A traveler odd in a way that is not mortal: too tall, no shadow, wrong joints.
- Whether a stranger turns up at a crossing of mine within days of a dimming.
- A reading that gives me nothing.
- An envoy of the river king, if one comes. He crosses my sill as a guest. I read him, and I note who stands behind him.
- What my riders hear, beyond the houses, of why the river trains stopped and of what is said of the star there. I will weigh what they bring as their telling.

Nothing else is in this record, because nothing else has happened.
