# Record of other gods: god-thresholds

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, where they stand, or what they can do. I will know one only when I stand near it, in the same room or across a doorway, and feel the weight of it settle into this is one like me, and that will tell me nothing more.

## What I have met
No one. I woke in the hour before first light on frozen turf beside a road and felt no weight in the air, no settling into knowing. That proves little: I would feel one only within a few paces, and the nearest roof is two miles off. I name no god, I hold no grievance, I am owed no promise and I owe none. I have guesses about no one, and I will not invent them to feel less alone.

## What I will watch for
- The weight in the air at a gate, a hall door, a toll house, a ford, anywhere strangers cross and I stand near. If it comes I will stand still, let it settle, and look at the face before I decide anything.
- Whether any traveler, toll-payer or stranger the Marshal's riders mention is odd in a way that is not mortal: too tall, no shadow, wrong joints. I do not know whether others are marked as I am. I would not mistake a mortal for a god, and I would not let a god pass for a mortal at my sill.
- Whether a guest at my table, once he has eaten, answers a question straight when a mortal would lie. I do not know what that proves.

Nothing else is in this record, because nothing else has happened.
