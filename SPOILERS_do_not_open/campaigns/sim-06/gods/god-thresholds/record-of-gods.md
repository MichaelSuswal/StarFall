# Record of other gods: god-thresholds

## Others like me
Others like me came down when I did. I do not know how many, who they are, where they stand, or what they can do. I will know one only when I stand near it, in the same room or across a doorway, and feel the weight of it settle into this is one like me, and that will tell me nothing more.

## What I have met
No one, in a year. I stood at the ford, the city gate, the hall door, both passes, the toll houses and the rite fires of two columns, and sat at tables with the warlord, the First Doorwarden, a river trader, a packer and a captain's cousin. I felt no weight in the air at any of them. Eleven strangers were brought to me by the toll watchers and each was ordinary. I name no god, I hold no grievance, I am owed no promise and I owe none.

## What the year taught me about the watching
- A mortal at a table who reads clean tells me nothing about whether I stand near one of my own kind. The weight in the air is the only sign I trust, and I felt none.
- My Reading does not work on a god. If I ever read across a table and get no answer where I should get one, that is a thing to note, not to trust. I have not met it yet.
- I do not know whether a god would come to a toll house at all, or would keep to the high places, or would be like me and stand where doors are.

## The star
The star dimmed on three nights this year, each for about an hour in the middle of the night, the blue deepening and then returning. My people have seen such nights before I came. This year many of them tied the nights to me. I did not do it. I have no power that touches the sky, and I do not know whose it is. It may be another like me. It may be the world's own weather and nothing more. I hold both as guesses and I will not tell anyone it is mine, or that it is not, in a voice that sounds sure. If a household asks me, I say I do not know.

## What I will watch for
- The weight in the air at a gate, a hall door, a toll house, a ford, anywhere strangers cross and I stand near. If it comes I will stand still, let it settle, and look at the face before I decide anything.
- A traveler odd in a way that is not mortal: too tall, no shadow, wrong joints.
- The nights the star dims: whether a stranger turns up at a crossing of mine within days of one.
- A reading that gives me nothing.

Nothing else is in this record, because nothing else has happened.
