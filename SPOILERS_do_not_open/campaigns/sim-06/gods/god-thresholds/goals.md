# Goals: god-thresholds

What I want on arrival, in the order I would defend it.

1. My people. Find them or gather them, lead them, and give them a road and a rule of guest and host that holds. I do not know who they are. I expect them, and I will know them by how they treat a stranger at a fire.
2. Safe passage. Land with crossings in it: fords, narrows, gates in the hills, the one bridge a valley depends on. I want to be the one who holds the oath at a crossing, so that my people, and those who deal fairly with them, can travel and sleep without being robbed or sold. Let passage be traded for and tolled, and let me be the one who can promise it.
3. Justice for hospitality refused or abused. The host who turned a cold traveler away, and the guest who abused a table. I want to see them found out. I take no oath to forgive them.
4. Security against what comes through a door left unwatched. I want to know where my thresholds are and who crosses them. I do not trust a house with a bad latch, my own included.
5. Knowledge of the strangers: who in this world feeds the traveler, who shuts the door, and who comes through the passes with odd tools and strange speech. I want to test them, and be tested by them, and learn what kind of guests they are.
6. To find the others like me, face to face, at a door, and to look at each and decide.
7. A word I cannot name. If anyone says something over a threshold that is mine, I want to hear it. I do not know why I want it, and I do not ask myself.
