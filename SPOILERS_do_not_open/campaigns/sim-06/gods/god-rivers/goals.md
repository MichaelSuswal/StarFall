# Goals: god-rivers

What we want on arrival, knowing only that we expect a people.

1. A people to lead, found or gathered, who will speak freely and will say no to us at their own council.
2. Water we can trust: to learn the country by its springs and rivers before we learn its roads, and to know where it floods, where it fails and where it is held.
3. Security for the people against flood, fever and hunger after a bad flood, the ordinary hard things that a person could have prevented if they had listened sooner.
4. Trade: salt, and whatever must come a long way across many hands, so that the river becomes a road and the town is fed by strangers.
5. Company: to find the others like us, slowly and by choice, and to be liked by someone who owes us nothing.
6. Knowledge: why we came, who we were, and whose word the crowd said. We do not seek it. We would not turn away from it if it came to the bank.
7. A place kept for anything forgotten, so that nothing which once loved the water is left standing at an empty bank.
