# Power card: god-rivers (no name held)

Bynames: "the Lady of the Ford", "the river" (words it lets mortals use).

Confirmed on Night 2, 2026-11-24 (Pacific), by the god's round 2 reply.

## Form

One body, as every god has in this campaign.

We are two shapes and one body, and we shift between them as a river shifts between its flood season and its still one. The change takes about the time of three slow breaths, in the open or in the dark, and we choose when.

The first shape is a tall woman of broad shoulders and long arms, heavy in the hip and plain in the dress, barefoot by habit. Skin the colour of wet slate in shade and of river clay in sun. Hair dark and always damp, worn down and uncombed, with a few pale threads in it like foam on an eddy. A wide, calm face with a long upper lip and grey green eyes set far apart, eyes that look at a speaker steadily and a little too long. Her hands are large and warm, warmer than a body should be. She stands about a head over most mortals, but she stoops, slouches and sits low when among them, and she walks a bank as if she had done it for centuries. Senses as a strong mortal's, with a hearing for running water that is finer than any sense of her own kind has a right to be. She moves at a walking pace, a little faster downhill.

The second shape is a body of living water: a low, braided, shallow stream about the breadth of a cart, lying along the ground and flowing over it, grey green and clear in the day and dark in the night. It follows the fall of the land and the line of channels as water does, and moves at the pace of a slow current downstream and a walking pace across level ground, slower uphill, slowest of all up a steep face. It can pass under a gate, through a reed bed, into a ditch, a well, a spring pool, and it can lie in a river unseen as one more thread of the flow. It sees and hears as the water around it does, dimly. It cannot speak in this shape, nor hold a thing, nor carry the stone, which it must leave at the bank or have held by the water against its bed.

What breaks it: In the woman's shape: being bound, pinned or caged and held fast, drowned in a place with no current, or struck hard enough to scatter the body into water that cannot gather itself. In the water's shape: being dammed, drained, dried in strong sun, spread thin across dry ground, poured into a vessel and sealed, or frozen solid in deep winter. A held body cannot shift. A scattered body is only water until it returns. Fire dries us; cold holds us; stone walls and sealed jars hold us. A spear or blade can hurt and slow the woman's shape but cannot end it.

God-sense: the god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing that this is one like itself. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.

When the body is broken: see the power Rise at the spring.

Keeper trait: Your face does not stay with people: a mortal who has not seen you within a season remembers that someone was there, and what was said and done, and cannot call your face to mind. Its reach: faces only; it takes no deed, word or name from anyone, does nothing to another god, and touches none of your powers or their prices.

### Definitions

- A day's walk: twenty miles.
- The same river: one river with its feeders and its outflow.
- A working beyond sight: any working of Calling the water on water the god cannot see from where it stands.

### Things carried

- A plain grey wool wrap and a hemp cord, worn in the woman's shape.
- The flat grey river stone is not ordinary: it is the instrument of Calling the water and is written under Weaknesses taken.

## Powers

### Listening water
Status: main
Description: Standing in, kneeling at or laying a hand on running water or a spring, the god holds still and hears what the water carries within a day's walk (twenty miles) of water up and down from that place, feeders included: words spoken on or near the banks within about a day, that someone crossed or drank and how many, what sickness runs in it, what the weather is doing upstream, what lies in a pool or under a bank. It comes as the water's own muddled telling, old and new together.
Drawback: While listening the god is still and helpless, deaf and blind to all that is not water, and cannot shift or stand. Afterward it sleeps twice as long as it listened, and no one can wake it.
Limits settled at creation:
- Water truly joined and running or rising; a sealed jar, a cistern with no inflow and a lake with no outflow tell only what is in them.
- Frozen water is silent.
- Ice, a dam or a long dry reach ends it there.
- It tells what is, never why or who caused it.
- Other gods: a god in the same water is not known by this power.
- The god's own people: heard as anyone.
- A mind: none.
- Use across a year: bounded by the sleep.
- A scarcity: none.
- More than ordinary craft: water changed by more than craft may tell false or nothing; unruled until it happens.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched.
- First wording, kept as intent: "to know a country by its waters before we know its people"; the god asked for no written end to the reach and a heavier price in its place, and the reach was written at twenty miles with the price it offered.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Calling the water
Status: main
Description: With the stone in hand, the god raises or lowers, speeds or stills, turns or parts running or standing water within its sight: swells a stream, drops a ford to a trickle, holds a current, pushes it against its fall for a short time, shifts a spring's course by a few paces, opens a spring stopped with a stone where water lies behind it. It moves what water is there and makes none. Beyond sight, it may work up to the whole of one river in one valley, to the height of that river's own greatest flood and no higher. Never a sea, and never a river turned into a new bed for good.
Drawback: Each working draws the water from the god's own body. It goes dry, brittle and slow, and cannot shift or work any power again until it has lain in running water a day for a small working and up to a month for the largest within sight. Worked in anger, the water goes where the god feels and not where it aims. For a working beyond sight the god lies in running water a full season, unable to shift or to work any power, and one item of memory or habit is taken from its record as a return's is.
Limits settled at creation:
- Not water in a sealed vessel, in a living body, falling as rain or standing as ice.
- Water held by a dam or built channel only once the hold is broken.
- No more water than is in reach.
- Being watched: it is seen as flood or ebb.
- The god's own people: the water does not know them; the god cannot aim a flood around a house or a person.
- It chooses how large it works, and the price runs with the size it chose.
- Use across a year: bounded by the lying in water.
- A mind: none.
- A scarcity: none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling.
- First wording, kept as intent: "a war at the water's edge should not be ours to lose"; the god asked for no cap and a heavy price, and a top was written.
Rulings:
Trades: its instrument, the stone, is the weakness that bought the far sense (see Weaknesses taken).
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Warm hands
Status: main
Description: With its hands on a hurt or fevered person, and water it has touched to drink or wash with, the god mends broken flesh, brings down fevers, and clears the sicknesses that move in water and the sicknesses of childbirth, one person at a time, giving of its own warmth.
Drawback: The god takes the chill. Its hands and then its whole body go cold, slow and clumsy for as many days as the healing would have taken in nature. It cannot heal itself.
Limits settled at creation:
- It does not raise the dead, restore a limb, undo age or cure what is not hurt.
- It cannot be sold or bought with another's suffering.
- The warmth will not come for anyone the god has deceived or knowingly wronged (a condition, not a price).
- Distance: touch.
- Use across a year: bounded by the cold.
- A mind: none.
- Other gods: unruled.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched, a scarcity.
- First wording, kept as intent: "We give of our own warmth, and nothing is taken from anyone else."
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Unsigned
Status: main
Description: The god's workings through water leave no sign that leads to it. A mortal who hears of one, or sees only its result, finds nothing in it that points to a hand.
Drawback: It is never off. The god cannot make a working show its hand, even when it wants to be known, and cannot claim one in words: when it tries to say "we did this", what it says is that the water did. The thanks for what it does go to the water, the ferryman or chance.
Limits settled at creation:
- It hides works, not the god's nature: a god at close quarters still knows it.
- It hides nothing from one who watched it work, or was told by one who watched.
- It hides no deed with no water in it.
- A mind: it rules none.
- No one is made unable to believe; they are given nothing to go on.
- The tongue binds only a claim of a working that went through water; the god may say what it did with its own hands, what it thinks and wants, and where it was.
- First wording, kept as intent: "We would rather work unnamed by choice than be forgotten without it." The first asking had mortals unable to believe when told; that was not written, as a rule over minds.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### Rise at the spring
Status: main (the return)
Description: When the god's body is broken or scattered, it gathers again after a season at the nearest spring or head of running water, in the woman's shape. What it carries comes back with it.
Drawback: One item of memory or habit at each return. The item is taken by a logged roll from the god's own record, and the god is not told which.
Limits settled at creation:
- The standing scale's season rung.
- A body held is not broken.
- The walk is added.
- If another holds the stone when the body breaks, it stays where it is.
Rulings:
Trades: none.
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

### The pull in the water
Status: main
Description: While the god stands in or touches running water, it feels another god that is standing in or on water of the same river (its feeders and its outflow included) as a pull, upstream or down. Nothing of who, what it can do, how far, or whether it means well. A pull is a pull: it does not tell one from several.
Drawback: The weakness that bought it: the stone as the instrument (see Weaknesses taken).
Limits settled at creation:
- Only while the god itself stands in or touches running water.
- One river, its feeders and outflow, no other water.
- Other gods: what the other feels, if anything, is unruled until it happens.
- A mind: none.
- Not applicable: light and dark, falling, carrying, being watched, a scarcity, more than ordinary craft.
Rulings:
Trades: bought by the weakness "the stone as the instrument".
Growth: Possible. Not shown.

## Weaknesses taken

### The stone as the instrument
Calling the water comes only through the flat grey stone. If it is carried off, won, lost or given, the power is gone until the god holds it again. In any other hand it is only a stone, and no one else gains the power, by gift or otherwise. It is not taken while it lies in the god's pouch or within its reach on the bank; the water shape cannot carry it. The god does not feel where the stone lies. If it is destroyed, the power is gone until the god's next return, when it comes back with the body. If another holds it when the body breaks, it stays where it is. Anyone who learns this can take the god's hand from the water by taking the stone.
It bought: The pull in the water.

## Trades
Offered once, on Night 2. Taken: the stone as the instrument, for The pull in the water. Trades are closed for the rest of creation.
