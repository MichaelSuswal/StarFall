# Record of other gods: god-rivers

## Others like us
Year 1, before the year begins. We have met none of them and felt none. At the spring head, in the hour before first light, we felt no other like ourselves, as the landing told us we would not unless one stood close. We have not yet stood in the stream long enough to know whether the pull in the water speaks of one on this river, and we will watch for it whenever we stand in running water.

We know nothing of their number, names, shapes, peoples or powers. We trust them by default and hope to be liked by them. We have no grievance, no promise and no debt. Whatever we guess, we guess from nothing, and we will not take a guess for a fact.
