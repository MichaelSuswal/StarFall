## L-0003
Date: 2026-11-24. Year: creation, Day 2. Card: the player's god. Power: The Seal.
Matter: the Seal's price.
Arguments: the DM first set the cap of twelve and a darkening of all three powers at each breaking. The player: it is priced twice; "a breaking darkens the Seal and the Reading until dawn and leaves the True Measure alone." The DM agreed that the cost had been spread over a power the Seal has nothing to do with.
Ruling: settled for the player. A breaking darkens The Seal and The Good-Faith Reading until the next dawn; The True Measure is untouched; the cap stays at twelve. Undisputed after the change.
Binds: the player's card.

## L-0006
Date: 2026-11-24. Year: creation, Night 2. Card: the player's god. Powers: The True Measure, The Good-Faith Reading, The Seal, The Scattering (its hearing).
Matter: the Keeper's review of the four information powers the DM priced by shape, against the mystery standard.
Arguments: for the terms as written: each gives what a thing is, or that a thing happened, and never a cause, a source, a place or a past; the hearing is bounded to scales checked in the god's own presence and kept only as drift. Against: The True Measure's "true purity" could be put to a thing that is not of ordinary making. The card already answers it: such a thing "may not read true. Unruled until it happens."
Ruling: confirmed unchanged. None shows a mechanism and none can take a central measurement. No Limit is added.
Binds: the player's card. The provisional mark is lifted.

## L-0008
Date: 2026-11-24. Year: creation, Night 2. Card: the player's god. Power: The Scattering.
Matter: the slower return, provisional: whether one item is its price, and whether "the nearest true scale" as the return ground gives an advantage the nearest ground of the god's kind would not.
Arguments: for: the ground exists only where the god or its presence has checked a scale, so it is harder to find than any standing kind of ground, and where none stands the Keeper rules the ground and the time is three years at the least; the time is never shorter than the first rung's. Against: true scales will stand among the god's own people, so the god tends to return among them. That is the god's own work standing, bought at a year or years when it does not, and the nearest ground of a kind (a market, a counting-house) would stand in more places.
Ruling: confirmed unchanged, at one item. No advantage. Rung logged: neither standing rung; a slower return, taken at the god's asking.
Binds: the player's card. The provisional mark is lifted.

## L-0009
Date: 2026-11-24. Year: creation, Night 2. Card: the player's god. Form: things carried.
Matter: possessions the DM could not check against the world's craft (a magnifying glass, wax and a seal-ring, ink, stones for testing metal, the word "coin").
Arguments: for leaving them: a god comes down with what it carries, and what a god knows beyond the world's level it may bring (DESIGN 4.7); each is an ordinary object that does only what such an object does. Against: an object beyond the peoples' craft could be read as a grant of that craft. It is not: nothing is granted by naming it, and more of a thing is made in play, by the people, from what the world has.
Ruling: confirmed unchanged. Each stands as an ordinary possession the god came down with. None gives the peoples below the craft to make another; a lost one is replaced only by work in play. "Coin" in the card's text is the god's own word for stamped metal; what the peoples use is found in play.
Binds: the player's card.

## L-0010
Date: 2026-11-24. Year: creation, Night 2. Card: the player's god. Sealed record.
Matter: the sealed line the DM filed as a want and flagged for the Keeper to weigh: whether it is also the player's request not to be told something.
Arguments: for a request: its plain words could be read as the player not wanting the thing found. For a want: it was given in answer to the question of what the god wants and would not say aloud, in the god's voice, in one breath with the god's belief about itself; the player made no request under either request heading, and asked on Day 2 that the past it left open be decided for it.
Ruling: it is the god's want and not a request not to be told. It is held as a want under the standing rule: applied to nothing until a dated confirmation stands beneath it. The secret past is written as the lore and the outline give it, neither toward the want nor against it.
Binds: the sealed record; the DM's filing stands.
