## L-0011

Arguments: three drafts gave the holder of an instrument the power, or let a free gift pass it (god-sea, god-rivers, god-secrets in one sentence, god-beasts at a breaking of the body); two gave a way to remake a destroyed one (god-sea over a season, god-beasts from a beast freely given); one a sense of where it lies (god-rivers). ## L-0029
Date: 2026-11-24. Year: creation, Night 3a. Card: the player's god. Sealed record, a request to the Keeper.
Matter: the sealed request of creation Day 3, that the vacant keeper's place be more than a cause of the market's quarrels; weighed beside the same day's sealed want about the old keeper's death, which the DM flagged as pulling the other way.
Arguments: for granting: Day 3 lets the player and the world work out its people's past together (DESIGN 3.17); the request asks for a thing in the world and not for an answer; the world draft and the placement draft already put old standard weights and the leavings of the vanished trading power at this seat, so the world can hold it without anything being made for a plot. Against: no people is made to fit a plot; the Keeper never plants a clue to serve a theory; the request does not say what the vacancy should point to, and must not be read as leave to choose an answer the player would like. On the want: it is a want, with no confirmation beneath it, and by the standing rule it is applied to nothing, neither served nor defied.
Ruling: granted in part. On Night 3b the old keeper's office, its standards and its vacancy are written into the deep history as a place where one of the short mysteries of this seat's ground can be met: a thing that was there at creation, findable from the first day, shown as effects and objects that admit more than one reading, with its truth fixed and its cost on the schedule like any other. What it points to, and whether the death itself was ordinary, are set from the deep history alone and are told to no one. Nothing is promised about what play finds. The want is held unapplied.
Binds: Night 3b (4 and 5); the sealed record. The player hears only "received and ruled".
