# Event schedule: sim-06

Written on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-24. Clocked events run on years no one controls. Triggered events fire on what gods and peoples do. Every mystery's cost stands here as a row, named by its mystery. Roll ids are in the keeper log (3b.6) and beside each rolled year.

How a row's time is read. Years are bare numbers. A month is counted from the waking season of the country the row names: month 1 is the first month of the season its god wakes into. The countries of the player's god, god-sea, god-dreams, god-secrets and god-thresholds open in autumn (months 1 to 3 autumn, 4 to 6 winter, 7 to 9 spring, 10 to 12 summer). The countries of god-rivers, god-healing and god-beasts open in spring (1 to 3 spring, 4 to 6 summer, 7 to 9 autumn, 10 to 12 winter). A row for a country with no god counts from the clock's `season_opens`, autumn, and says "clock count". An event that crosses countries takes the one season written on its row, in the year of the country named there.

No row herds the gods to one place: the one thing that visits every seat, the carriers' round, goes to each in turn and gathers no one. No path or hidden way exists for any row; every journey a row implies is priced with `tools/map.py travel` in the year it runs. No row names a thing the player left for play: the player's town, people and the dead keeper stay unnamed here.

## Random events

For each country, each year, one roll: `python3 tools/roll.py --campaign sim-06 --who keeper --what "random event, <country>, Year N" --base 30`, and in Years 1 to 7 with `--factor opening=-20`, so the opening gives one chance in ten a year and the base stands from Year 8. The country roll is no act and no choice among targets and takes no sealed factor. A roll for how a people meets what comes is a roll for their acts.
