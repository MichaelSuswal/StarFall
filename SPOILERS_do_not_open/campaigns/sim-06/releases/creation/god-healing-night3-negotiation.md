# Negotiation: god-healing, Night 3

## Round 1
### What was read and not priced
- You proposed no edge, so nothing is priced against a weakness and no weakness is owed.
- The habits and prides you wrote are taken as tempers: who your people are, able to fail. None is a guarantee.
- What you said of the land and its past is written as your wish or your people's belief. The world decides.
- Who each office holder is stands as who they are, never what they will do.
- Everything under Skills and culture is ordinary and free.

### Your landing: the helpers and the seat
Draft text: You come to a people that does not know you. The steadings are your people's own, the Hearth-Holder holds the throne for a Tender your people have not met, and the Reckoner of Bodies, the Warden of Waters, the Keeper of the Mounds, the Spearward and the Judge-Mother serve the court under its own custom. You wrote that they know whose they are but not that the Tender is you, and that you will not announce yourself. So the form is "designed, not yet serving": you land with no helpers in fact and no seat that is yours in fact. Until each office is won in play, nobody acts for you while you are away, your unplayed time runs on your standing conduct alone, and your people go their own way under their own court. That is the cost, the same for any god who lands so. It is not a refusal of a default, it bears on no edge, and it buys nothing. Where your people keep a seat or a place for one they await, they keep it for that one and do not know it is you: an office is won when its holder takes you for the one awaited and takes your word, and not before.
Choices: (a) Keep the meeting as you wrote it, at the cost above. The offices stand as you described them, each under your people's own custom, and each becomes your helper when its holder hears you and takes your word, in play. (b) Say that your people already know you when you land: that you have been among them, or that they know you on sight for whose they are. Then the offices you described serve you from landing, your word is carried while you are away, and the meeting is rewritten so, in your words. Neither way is priced and neither buys anything.
The Keeper's reasons: an office can carry the word only of one its holder knows. What you wrote has your people holding their offices under their own law, or for one they have not met. The cost is said here plainly so that you choose knowing it.
Accept, refuse or counter, and give your reasons.

### The spears

### Their magic
Draft text: The sitters you described, perhaps one in several steadings and none in many, each easing a fever or a hard labor through a night at her own cost. Fitted as you wrote it: ordinary in this world, and no edge. The cost each bears is the whole price. You cannot make another by telling; such a one comes as the world gives her.
The Keeper's reasons: how much of it there is comes from the world and is the same for every people.
