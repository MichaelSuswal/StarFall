# Day 3 questions: god-rivers

These go into your Night 3 release. They come from what you made of yourself on Night 2 and bear on the people you will lead. Answer each in your own voice.

1. Your works leave no sign and you cannot claim them. What do your people think the water is, and what do they think you are?
2. Your face does not stay with those who have not seen you within a season. How does the council know you when you come to it?
3. A working beyond sight lays you in the water for a season. Who speaks at council for the water while you lie there, if anyone?
