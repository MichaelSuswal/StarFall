# Release: god-dreams, Night 2

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## What is so of every god here
Knowing another god: a god does not feel another god from far off. Only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, does it feel the other as a weight in the air, and over a moment the weight settles into knowing: this is one like me. It tells nothing more: not who the other is, and not what it can do.
Bodies: one body.
Mortal magic, for pricing: it is simple, and it costs the worker.
You need no food, and sleep is a choice. You move at the pace of your form.
You cannot die. The peoples you will find are at about this level: before the
medieval era, no steel forges. That is where the world begins, not a
ceiling. You may bring in what you know, but it is built here by the people,
from what the world has, at the pace of the work, and you say how it is to be
made; nothing is granted by naming it. Every power carries a drawback you
would notice losing. There is no set number of powers. Nothing is over the
top.

## The people you lean toward
Rule: a sacred monarch behind a court. A mortal line holds the throne in the god's name and is held sacred for it; a court stands between the throne and the people, and the god is reached through it.
Worship: local shrines. Household and village shrines, kept by whoever keeps them; no order of priests.
You build your people within these on Night 3, or give your reasons not to.
Your people come with helpers who run things when you are away. Who your
people are, and who those helpers are, is settled on Night 3.

## What you said, and asked
No follow-up.

## The season you wake into
Autumn.

## Options
Each is offered once. None is expected of you.
- Anything past the three rules above (a sense that reaches further than a few paces, say, or a way to act where your body is not) would be a power of yours, and priced.
- A thing you carry may be had two ways, and you choose. As an ordinary possession, at no price: it can be stolen, copied or wanted for whose it is, and it does nothing an ordinary thing of its kind does not. Or as the instrument of one of your powers: the power comes only through it, and whoever takes it takes that power from you until you hold it again, though in any other hand it is only the ordinary thing. The second way is a weakness taken on, and a weakness buys something (trades, below).
- A price may be made heavier than its power needs, if you say so; it is then written as you ask.
- Where a drawback could go two ways, you are given the choice and pick one. As an example of such a pair, for a power that reads others: that it never reads anyone of your own people, or that whoever you read learns the same of you.
- Trades: a weakness taken on can buy one improvement elsewhere. Examples of the kind of thing a weakness could buy, each one choice among several: your helpers doing in your name a thing you do; more of a thing a power holds at once; a longer reach for a power; a faint sense, from far off, of a matter of your own kind. You need not take any. A flaw that is only who you are (a temper, a habit of mind) is written into how you conduct yourself and buys nothing.
- When your body is broken: you cannot die, but you can be hurt, held, scattered or shut out. Say what you wish to happen when the body is broken. Because a return has a cost and a trigger, it is written as a power and priced on one standing scale, the same for every god: a return that takes a season costs one item of memory or habit; a return that takes a month costs two items; nothing is faster. The faster return costs more. The price is always items of memory or habit, never a possession: what you carry comes back with you. You return at the nearest ground of your kind, or at a ground you name if that gives no advantage the nearest would not. A wish that fits neither rung is priced as you ask it.
- Wording: where all you need is clearer wording, ask for a clarification "in the write-up, not a new power".
