# Day 3 questions: god-healing

These go into your Night 3 release. They come from what you made of yourself on Night 2 and bear on the people you will lead. Answer each in your own voice.

1. You cannot read what ails your own people. Who among them tells you, and do they know why you ask?
2. Those you have mended know when you are near. Does the line that holds your throne include any you have mended, or mean to?
3. You want your people to need you less each year. What do they tend themselves, and what do they bring to you?
