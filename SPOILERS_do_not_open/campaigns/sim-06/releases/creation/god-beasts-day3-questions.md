# Day 3 questions: god-beasts

These go into your Night 3 release. They come from what you made of yourself on Night 2 and bear on the people you will lead. Answer each in your own voice.

1. Your people are an empty place in The Listening. How do you know where they are, on the move?
2. Beasts you speak with grow bold near people. What do your herders make of that, and who answers to the council for it?
3. Tame beasts fall silent and face you before anyone sees you. What do your people's dogs and herds tell them about you, and what have they made of it?
