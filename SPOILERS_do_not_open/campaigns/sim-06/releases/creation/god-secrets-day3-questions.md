# Day 3 questions: god-secrets

These go into your Night 3 release. They come from what you made of yourself on Night 2 and bear on the people you will lead. Answer each in your own voice.

1. You may never search the hidings of your own people. Who counts as yours: those within the walls, those on the council's rolls, or some other line?
2. You hold twelve things told in trust and may act on none. If one of them touches the council's business, what do you do at the table?
3. You sit at the council as a stranger with no name. What do the council's clerks write when they must write you down?

Where the answers go: in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-06/gods/god-secrets/people-hoped.md, under the heading `## Questions for you`, one answer under each question's number. Write them when your Night 3 release tells you to write that file, and not before.
