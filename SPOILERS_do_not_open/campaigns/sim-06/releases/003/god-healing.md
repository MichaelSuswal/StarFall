# Release: god-healing, Year 3, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 3, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 4, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 4 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- The Reckoner carried your asking to the Warden of Waters. Three test wells were dug on the rise with the elders' leave. You read each before any drank and again at the season's end: sound both times.
- The old elder's well, read before his steading: foul, as before. He named his father, Tolm, who dug it, and keeps it.
- Your summer walk, about fourteen days: up the river and its creeks and to the first trees at the fen's western edge. The river, every creek and every spring above the bank burials read clean. The ground under the bank burials read foul, and the wells below them. Nothing foul comes down from upstream. Old burial mounds on higher ground read sound. The foulness begins at the newest bank graves and stops where the low ground's wells stop.
- The moss of the mounds, read at midsummer and again after the first frost: living, sound, nothing ailing it that the Reading knows, and nothing in it that common moss lacks. The old women say it stopped biting the tongue in the second month and does nothing now that common moss does not do.
- You sat at the seat's hearth when the Hearth-Holder asked, and told the Spearward you would turn the rot on no store of the people, in war or out of it.
- Seven mendings, each with its fever and its shake after. All held. No carcass or spoiled store lay by a wellhead, and you called no rot.
- The Reckoner's winter count was lower again where the wells are closed.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
- The old sitter at the mounds: "Was it you that did this, or was it going already?"
- The sitters of the three nearest steadings, through Hedda: what they should brew for the wasting now the moss does nothing.

## What the Keeper ruled
- Nothing lay outside your card. The Reading told you where the foulness is and is not, and not what brought it.

## The sky
The star dimmed on six nights of your year, more again than last year: one in the first month, a pair in the fifth, a pair in the seventh and one in the eighth. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count fourteen such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
The Warden of Waters lets the nearest households draw from the rise. The tremor left your hands at the thaw.
