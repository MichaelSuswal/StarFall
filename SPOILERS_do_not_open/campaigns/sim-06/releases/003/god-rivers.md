# Release: god-rivers, Year 3, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 3, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 4, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 4 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- At the fire you answered the ferry child both questions. A small boy said a spring cannot walk.
- At the flood the Ferry Elder read the marks with you: the deep water lay a little farther from the ford stones again, on the side away from the weirs. The head herd keeper asked why you would search for their goats and what you would do that a boat and a dog cannot.
- The council gave leave for the going and set it at three days down, the turn at dusk of the third. You went by barge down the brook to the ford with a pair of ferry keepers and the cord walker, lay there a pair of days, and answered the ford keeper. A ford keeper asked after: "What is another like yourself?"
- Three days down the larger river, about ninety miles. After about twenty-five miles the hills fall away and the river goes into a wide low country of many channels, reed and standing water. Met there: reed cutters at their staithes, who gave news and a count for the cord, and at dusk on the second evening a band of the boat people, who traded reed work for a knife and answered nothing about themselves. What the reed cutters say of the carriers: the salt for the stone towns now comes up from the sea in the south, and does not need the ford. They named no place. Eight days back up.
- You stood in the larger river to the knee each dusk and each dawn. You felt no pull.
- At the ford you listened once, about an hour. What the water gave, for twenty miles up and down: the deep water lies farther again from the ford stones on the side away from the weirs; the bed behind each weir is shoaled; fewer laden beasts crossed than last year; nothing strange from the hills. Nothing like yourself is in that water. You slept about two hours and nothing came against you.
- In autumn you stood at the mark with the keepers and brought all of it to the council in one sitting.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
- A small boy at the fire: "A spring cannot walk."
- The head herd keeper: why you would search for their goats, and what you would do that a boat and a dog cannot.
- A ford keeper: "What is another like yourself?"

## What the Keeper ruled
- Your four readings stand as you stated them. The listening tells what is and never why. You felt no pull because there was none to feel.

## The sky
The star dimmed on six nights of your year, more again than last year: one in the first month, a pair in the fifth, a pair in the seventh and one in the eighth. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count fourteen such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
The town keeps its marks and its second cord. The ford's people call you Knee Deep.
