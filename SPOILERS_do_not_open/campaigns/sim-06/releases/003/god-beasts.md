# Release: god-beasts, Year 3, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 3, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 4, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 4 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- At the dusk council your request was said whole by Tessaly. The Speaker said the council takes no word from the keeper and gives it none, and that the Houndwarden may send her hunters on a reading she judges good, at dusk, for the calving only. The eldest head of the three clans said a keeper that asks small is asking the first of many.
- On three nights of the calving you told Tessaly where the wolves lay and which way, and she sent hunters and dogs the same dusk. The wolves were turned each time. About six calves were lost.
- In summer you read the herds at each pool the Marchwarden named. On your word of a herd hurt on a pair of dusks running, he moved the camps off the pool below the second ford. Three children had died of the flux there before the move. The mothers said to Tessaly that the keeper should have said sooner. No one asked you for rain.
- The eldest head refused you a second month at the three clans' margin. You did not go.
- The council's own word to the stone towns, that the cutters keep to the first stands, went with the trade party. It was heard and nothing was promised.
- Late summer, by night through the timber, never within a mile: three gatherings of people with draught beasts, working and unafraid, a little deeper in than last year. You told the Marchwarden so at his fire, and that the Listening past a mile gives no number.
- The trade party came home with half the salt wanted and iron greyer and softer than other years; the stone towns' oxen cough still. Its beasts were kept apart a month, as the Saltkeeper already meant.
- You stood at the edge of the hunt and the season was kept.
- In winter the cough came back hard in the two clans' cattle, which wintered apart on their own ground. About a hundred of theirs died. No other herd took it.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
- A herder of the two clans, through Tessaly: whether you can say what the cough is.

## What the Keeper ruled
- The Listening gives a gathering's place, which way it moves and its state. It does not give what a gathering is doing. That a herd at a pool is drinking, and anything about the water, is your own inference, as you told Tessaly.

## The sky
The star dimmed on six nights of your year, more again than last year: one in the first month, a pair in the fifth, a pair in the seventh and one in the eighth. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count fourteen such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
The band has once acted on your word, by the council's leave, and it held.
