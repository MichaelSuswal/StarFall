# Release: civ unnamed-people-dreams, Year 1, the year resolved

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 2 with your own Year 1 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The river party reached the river's mouth, found the crew of a coasting ship beached for the night, was fed, traded its linen and pots for salt, and came home in month 12. The sailors said the salt is boiled at a harbor town west along that coast, and that it fetches far less there than the carriers ask at your gate. The court has this word.
- The western party found no one in the hills, ran short of food and turned for home. It was not back at the year's end. It comes in early in Year 2, all four alive and thin.
- The households kept by lot slept the night through, as they say. The guest's fear at dawn is as she told it. What the dying dreamed is theirs; none was cured.
- A flame at the court's hearth is a shrine like any household's.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
