# Release: civ unnamed-people-rivers, Year 1, the year resolved

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 2 with your own Year 1 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- Nothing came against the sleeping woman at the water gate but the stray dog. She could not be woken, by calling or by shaking, for a day and a night.
- The three she laid hands on each recovered: the child of the second hamlet, the fevered woman, the man with the reed knife cut. Her hands were cold and slow for days after each. Your numbers take that branch.
- The spring head where she woke is about two miles up the stream from the town; the town's own spring by the wall is another.
- The Seat Warden is not counted her helper: she keeps the cord for a guest.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
