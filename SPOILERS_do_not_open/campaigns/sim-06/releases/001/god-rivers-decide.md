# Release: god-rivers, Year 1, the year's decisions

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## Where you are
You have landed. Your landing release, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-06/releases/000/god-rivers-landing.md`, says what you sense and see where you stand, at the hour before first light. This year you read it in place of a last year's release. Nothing has happened yet but the landing.

Year 1 runs from that hour, through your waking season, spring, and round the four seasons to the same season again. The whole year is yours to decide tonight. You will be told what came of it in your next release.

## What you read
`CLAUDE.md`, `engine/roles/npc-god.md`, everything in your own folder `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-06/gods/god-rivers/`, your landing release, and this file. Nothing else.

## What you do
1. Update `record-of-gods.md` from what you have experienced. Nothing goes in it from anywhere else.
2. Update `goals.md`: what you hold to now that you stand on the ground.
3. Write `decisions-001.md`: three to seven concrete actions for the year, in your character, each a verb with a place or a person and a reason, in the order you mean them, with the season you mean each for. Say where your body goes and what it does. Say what you ask or tell your people to do, and through whom. Your helpers' offices stand as your landing release says: no one acts for you until an office holder has heard you and taken your word, so say how you mean to be met.
4. A message to anyone not in front of you is carried by the world, by a messenger or by you, and takes real time. If you send one, write it in full in `decisions-001.md` under a heading `## Messages`: to whom, as you would name them; the words exactly; the carrier; the route as you or the carrier would choose it; anything sent with it; what the carrier is to do if the recipient cannot be found, is hostile, or asks questions.
5. If you use a power in a way your card does not plainly cover, say so under `## For the Keeper to rule` and state your own case. If you mean to try to grow a power, say what you try, where, and what you put at risk.
6. You know only what you have seen, heard and been told. You may be wrong.
