# Release: civ unnamed-people-healing, Year 1, the year resolved

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 2 with your own Year 1 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- Each of the stranger's sittings ended with the sufferer mending, the Hearth-Holder's granddaughter among them; after each grave one the stranger was fevered and shaking for days. Your numbers take the branch in which the mendings held.
- The court steading's well and grain store: the stranger said both were sound. What the Warden of Waters did was his own caution.
- The wells of the low steadings downstream of the bank burials: the stranger said plainly that they are foul, and that the ground of the bank burials is foul in the same way. Where elders heard it, wells were drawn off or closed.
- The heap in month 8 went to black slurry in an afternoon under its hands, and its hands smelled of it for a day and a night.
- On its late summer walk it went some days along the bank each way and toward the treeline and came back; it brought no one with it.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
