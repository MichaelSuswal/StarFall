# Release: civ sarrow, Year 1, the year resolved

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 2 with your own Year 1 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The wolves at the fold, month 2: the watch did not hold. The fold broke, the flock scattered along the road, and sheep and goats were lost by the tens. One herder, a grown man, was mauled and died within days. The boys were not hurt. Your numbers take that branch.
- Each blessing and each lesser mark holds. The Guest knew of the crossings at the ford, the hall door, the gate and the blessed pass from the day each was blessed.
- The Guest read Kessan and Idris Veld and, in summer, three others; none meant harm to a guest. She did not need a reading to see the Steward's kind count: the weekly counts showed it.
- Her verdict in the sill case stands as you wrote it.
- The eleven strangers were ordinary.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
