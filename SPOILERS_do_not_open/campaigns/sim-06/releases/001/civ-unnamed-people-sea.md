# Release: civ unnamed-people-sea, Year 1, the year resolved

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 2 with your own Year 1 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The late boat in the swell, month 4: it came in with all five aboard. The harbor mouth lay calm that night from the moment the figure's lamp was lit at the water's edge; the water outside the mouth did not. Your numbers take that branch: about 2,011.
- The pile at the lowest step and the keel of the point ferryman's boat: each is sound work, joined as a good wright joins, and neither has rotted, split or taken worm. Your people can know no more than that yet.
- What the Warden-Monarch was shown: an old, wet shape that is not a man's. The sticks left across the jar bore notches answering her count and one mark she had not seen before.
- The marks on the tideline rocks in spring are small cut marks at the height of the water, the kind a man makes to measure a tide.
- The figure did not answer her question about Brennis, nor the Master of Timber's two questions.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
