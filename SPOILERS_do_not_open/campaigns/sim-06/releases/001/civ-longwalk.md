# Release: civ longwalk, Year 1, the year resolved

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 2 with your own Year 1 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The spring wolves at the rear: turned on each of the four nights, little lost. The east flank in month 6: the pack was turned and no more than one calf was lost after the keeper took the flank. Your numbers take those branches; the third knot on the cord stays open or is tied as the Houndwarden judges.
- The trade party went and came as you wrote the plain case.
- The dogs fall silent and turn to the keeper because of what it is; it did nothing to them.
- The fox and the boar on the cord: the keeper says it spoke with neither.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
