# Release: god-healing, Year 4, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 4, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 5, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 5 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- You went to the Reckoner first and she carried your askings. You answered the old sitter at the mounds and the three steadings' sitters.
- In the third month the Keeper of the Mounds stood with you on the bank ground and said his records aloud. What you read: under the rows laid in the wasting winter and since, many in one place on the low bank, the soil is foul, and the wells below them; under the older rows it is sound; the rise where he means to lay the next is sound. He pegged a limit on rows laid in one place.
- The sitters sent for you twice beside a brew. The Reading was closed on each sick one, and you asked the sitter what ailed them and said what you could from her words.
- In summer you walked twelve days alone down the river bank, about 58 miles below the court steading and back, all of it fen. At the two emptied steadings the bank burials read foul in the same way, and the wells below them. The river reads clean all the way. Below them you found no steading, no burial ground and no other people within your reach.
- Four mendings, one at a time, each held, each with its days of shaking after.
- The test wells on the rise read sound at the end of the wet season.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
- While you were on your walk, a runner from a steading below the court, for a woman in a hard labor: "Will it come?" No answer could be given. By your return the labor was over.

## What the Keeper ruled
- The fen's steadings are your own people. The Reading is closed on their sick, before a brew and after. You may ask what ails them and you may mend them. Wells, soil, graves, moss and stores read as before.

## The sky
The star dimmed on seven nights of your year, more again than last year: in the second, third, fourth, eighth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth months. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-one such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About as last year, the winter count lower again where the wells are closed.
