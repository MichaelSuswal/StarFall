# Release: god-sea, Year 4, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 4, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 5, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 5 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- At the tide door, at the lowest tide of the first dusks, you gave the Warden-Monarch your answers for the Harbormaster and for the Eldest Midwife. She said each back whole and carried each by her own mouth in the autumn. You read the clerks' list of the dead.
- The night's listening at the point: the coast's ordinary day. No hull that is not the town's or a known coaster's. Nothing that is not tide.
- Sound Timber twice: a pile at the quay head in the fourth month, and in the eleventh the lowest strake of the east cove's long-liner. Both are sound. The pile held through the longest storm.
- No boat was caught in the winter and you lit no lamp.
- In spring you went west under the water by night to the old causeway's shore end, 66.5 miles by the shore, and gave one night to listening there. What you heard: that shore's ordinary day, surf on the stones, no hull, no voice, nothing moving among the footings but the tide. Nothing has moved there since you swam there last. Nothing is kept by anyone.
- While you were away, on the twentieth day of the eighth month, a decked boat of the point ran for the mouth on a long swell with four aboard. The Warden-Monarch had your lamp lit by the tenders at the water's edge, on her own word, and said before them that it was hers and not yours. It burned as a lamp does. The boat broke at the mouth. Her master and his elder son drowned; the younger son and the hired hand were hauled out by the Captains' boat. You heard the Warden tell it to the water at the tide door when you were in that water again, in the fourth week of the ninth month.
- In the tenth month the Midwife sent a house through the Warden, a widow's, and you came once at the lowest tide at dusk and heard what she asked.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
- The Harbormaster, through the Warden: "Where inside the mouth? Say which timbers and where, and I will put a lead there. I will not drag a harbor on a word."
- The Warden-Monarch, to the water at the tide door, after the boat was lost: that she lit your lamp on her own word with you away, and how it went. "I do not know whether I had the right. Did I?"
- At the widow's bed, the Midwife's two askings, which your people's telling below carries. Your decisions gave you no words for them.

## What the Keeper ruled
- The twenty miles of your listening are measured along the coast from where you listen, wherever that is.
- Two pieces of Sound Timber in the year, weeks apart, are inside your card.
- Your lamp gives its calm only when your own hand lights it. Lit by another hand it is a lamp. Nothing was spent by that lighting and the harbor water is not closed to you for it.

## The sky
The star dimmed on seven nights of your year, more again than last year: in the second, third, fourth, eighth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth months. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-one such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About 2,037. The point has lost a master and his son, and the town has seen that the lamp without you is a lamp.
