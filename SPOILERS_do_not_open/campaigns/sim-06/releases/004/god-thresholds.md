# Release: god-thresholds, Year 4, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 4, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 5, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 5 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- At the ford the older watcher gave you the old reckoning as his father had it: by the beast. No train has been met by anyone but a watcher.
- In the hall you sat with Kessan alone, and the next day gave your three answers aloud before the five.
- Your word for the river king left the ford in autumn with the master of a mixed train, the second master the watcher asked. No answer and no envoy came this year.
- Two counts of the stores; the ration cut from the first snow at the lower count; the seed sealed. The riders went to the far houses for the loan: the nearer house sent about two days' worth, the farthest words only. The stores lasted to within days of the thaw and the seed seal was whole at the sowing. About a hundred and fifty died over the ordinary, the old and the youngest. The names of last winter's dead were said at the rite fire at the first snow.
- At the thaw you walked to the farthest toll house, 68.2 miles from the winter city, and laid your sixth blessing on its door, the keeper told first. What the blessing has given you since: the watchmen and their households in and out many times a day; on the days a train stands at the house, more people carrying in at that door than the house's own number. It tells you nothing of what was carried. What you saw there: a new roof, good horses, a full smokehouse.
- From the thaw fewer trains came than last spring, by about a third, and the river kingdom's paid the raised toll with open complaint. In the eighth month the three of you sat with the cords and half the raise was given back from the ninth month.
- In the tenth, eleventh and twelfth months no train of the river kingdom came over the road at all. Trains of other peoples came as before.
- In summer you were with the upper columns on the high grass. Cairns were set and notches cut at the line of the grass; the line stands where the oldest say it stood last year. The blue flower did not come up.
- You felt no weight in the air all year.

## Asked of you and not answered
- Kessan, by a rider up to the column, while you were on the high grass: "May I send my riders beyond the houses to learn why the river's trains do not come?"
- The Steward, by the same rider: what the hall is to do if no river train comes before the first snow and the salt runs short.
- The Mother of the Hearth, through a keeper of rite: whether the households are to be told the salt will not last the winter at the ration.

## What the Keeper ruled
- Nothing outside your card was done. The sixth blessing stands; one place is left of seven.

## The sky
The star dimmed on seven nights of your year, more again than last year: in the second, third, fourth, eighth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth months. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-one such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About eighteen thousand, less the winter's dead. The salt is short at the year's turn.
