# Release: god-beasts, Year 4, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 4, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 5, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 5 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- At the dusk council Tessaly carried your answer to the Speaker and the herder, whole.
- At the calving you read wolves on three nights and told Tessaly at once, and she sent hunters and dogs the same dusk and had the calves penned. Your readings were true. The wolves still got in among the calves on the second and third nights. About thirty calves were lost. The eldest head said he had said so.
- At the pool below the third camp a herd read hurt in part at the first dusk, and you said so with how little one dusk is. The herders found a pair of beasts horned in a fight. The next dusk it read resting. The camp stayed.
- In the seventh and eighth months you read the cutters from more than a mile: three gatherings with draught beasts, working and unafraid, a little farther in again. You told the Marchwarden. The council sent no word this year.
- The trade party came home with half the salt wanted and grey soft iron. Its beasts were kept apart six weeks and read whole, and joined the herds.
- In the winter the cough stayed in the two clans' cattle, wintered apart: about forty dead, far fewer than last winter.
- A young herder of the three clans that camp apart, sent by his own clan, came to the margin in the eighth month to ask whether the star dimmed because the band was too many to walk as one. You were away along the timber, and he went home on the third morning with no answer. He came again in the tenth month, with a man of the main band beside him, and asked it to your face. You answered as you had decided: that you know nothing of the dimming, that you did nothing at those hours that you know of, and that you do not say it is not any god's, because you cannot say what you do not know. He told his clan you had not said no. His clan says that means yes.
- The herder of the two clans asked you in the tenth month to read his herd at dusk each few nights. You did, and he found coughing beasts in the gatherings you named and put them apart.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
- Nothing stands unanswered. The eldest head said in the ring, not to you, that a thing given twice is a custom.

## What the Keeper ruled
- Nothing was put to the Keeper.

## The sky
The star dimmed on seven nights of your year, more again than last year: in the second, third, fourth, eighth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth months. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-one such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About as last year, with thirty calves and forty cattle the fewer.
