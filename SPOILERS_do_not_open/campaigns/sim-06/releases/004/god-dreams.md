# Release: god-dreams, Year 4, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 4, and what your people report. You read this at the opening of Year 5, in place of a last year's release. It is your go for nothing by itself; your Year 5 release names your files.

## What you did and what came of it
- In the yard of the watch you answered the captain and the four at your door. At the second house you answered the father. Where what you said did not match what your people know, they told you so: the father's son came home two autumns past and not last year, and the Warden of the Door asked whether your word for the delta means the town lies four days down the river from its mouth, since it lies four days up.
- You gave the Keeper of the Count your list of afraid days. He set it by his cords and said a count shows how many and not why.
- In the third month the oldest of the barrow singers died, of her age. She was the last who had the third barrow song. Her house kept no flame and no one asked that her sleep be walked. A pair of young households hold two parts in three of it.
- Keeping Sleep as before, three households on three named nights of the deep winter, the last on the hardest night of a late frost. The wall's men saw at those dawns what you told them they would.
- The court sent the stone: nine in three boats with sixty blocks, linen, pots and oil, from the quay on the twenty-fourth day of the second month, the leader carrying your word by heart. A boat grounded on a bar and three days were lost. No ship lay at the river's mouth in the ten days he waited. The stone was stacked above the high water and he went round the shore on foot with a pair of runners. At the harbor the factor gave bread and three nights, said his house's ship lifts the stone at the first fair weather after the gales, since no day was set, and sold salt for the linen and oil at the standing price. He would not have your word carried on to the delta: his house does not carry a stranger's word to a market it sells to. The leader judged the coast road unsafe before the gales and turned for home, as his leave allowed. All nine were home in the second week of the fourth month with a little salt. Your word did not go on. On the quays he heard nothing new of the one at the delta market: it is still seated, by last year's talk.
- The town had a fourth lean winter for salt.
- In spring the four offices told you what the town would do if you were away, and you kept it as a rule.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
- The captain of the wall: "If a lying hind is not her in a dream, what has the wall been sitting?"
- The Warden of the Wall: which house is nearest to a broken body.
- A man of the young barrow singers' households, through the Warden of the Door: whether you will hear what they have of the third song, two parts in three, now the oldest singer is dead.

## What the Keeper ruled
- Nothing was put to the Keeper.

## The sky
The star dimmed on seven nights of your year, more again than last year: in the second, third, fourth, eighth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth months. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-one such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About as last year. The stone lies, or lay, at the river's mouth for the harbor house's ship, and the court does not yet know whether it was lifted.
