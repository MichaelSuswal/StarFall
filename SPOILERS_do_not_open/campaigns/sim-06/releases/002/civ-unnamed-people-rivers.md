- The strayed herd: it was missed in the morning. Eight searched four days and brought home about thirty-five goats and three cattle; the rest were lost to the reed. No searcher was hurt.
- Nothing came against the short listening. She slept about two hours.
- Both whom she warmed recovered.
- What she told the council in autumn: what the keepers measured, what the carriers said, what she saw at the ford, and that the deep water lies farther off on the side away from the weirs. The council's course is the one you wrote for a mark to read by.
