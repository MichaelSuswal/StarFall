# Release: civ unnamed-people-dreams, Year 2, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 3 with your own Year 2 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The salt party: it did not come home before the cold. It reached the harbor on foot round the shore, was given bread and three nights, the leader spoke the words on the third night to the factor of the house that sets the price, and they bought salt at the harbor's price from another house's pans. The gales shut the coast road. They wintered at the harbor and came up the river at high water, home in month 8, all six, with the salt and this answer: that house will set a fair standing price by the year, for stone of the scar cut to its order. Your numbers take the branch with no word all winter.
- The Night Sitter did not sleep on the kept night. The rest of her household did.
- A flame a household lights from its own kept flame is a shrine and not a third household kept; the cap of two was kept.
- On the three mornings after three households, the wall's men saw her shaken past the other mornings, quick to act and slow to hold a thought, and she would not enter a shut room. No more than that.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
