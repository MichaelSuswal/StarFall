# Release: civ longwalk, Year 2, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 3 with your own Year 2 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The wolves of the calving, months 1 to 3: the watch did not turn them. About thirty calves were lost over the three nights. Your numbers take that branch.
- The dry month: the turns at the river held. Your numbers take the lighter branch.
- The keeper's readings were as the Houndwarden passed them: hurt, afraid or resting, and no more. It told her a herd was hurt; it did not know with what.
- What it told the Marchwarden of the cutters: three gatherings of people with draught beasts, toward the winter woods, deeper in than the songs put them, working and unafraid, moving in a little by the month. It gave no number and said it had none. Where your year file says it told him how many, read this in its place.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing. The rain is not known to be anyone's doing.
