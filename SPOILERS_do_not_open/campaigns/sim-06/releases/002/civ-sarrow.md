# Release: civ sarrow, Year 2, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 3 with your own Year 2 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- At the two farthest toll houses the Guest and the Marshal left with the watchmen's word and the cords as shown. The thin toll stands as it stood. The two farthest houses stand on the road in the high country, short of the road's end and of the river kingdom; the walk fits where you put it.
- The blessing at the far pass holds. She knows of the crossings there from that day.
- She gave no verdict in the grass dispute and no answer to the herder at the rite fire; both wait on her.
- Whether she came to the seat on the night of the first snow is not on the record; nothing is added.
- The dimmings of the star and the missing flower are not known to be anyone's doing.
