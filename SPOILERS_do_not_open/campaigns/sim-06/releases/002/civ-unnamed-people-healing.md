# Release: civ unnamed-people-healing, Year 2, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. You read this at the opening of Year 3 with your own Year 2 files. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- All nine mendings held; your numbers take that branch.
- What it read: the moss, living and sound, nothing foul in it. The rise, sound. The wells kept open below the bank burials, foul as before; the wells closed last autumn, fainter and still foul at the bottom; the emptied steadings' wells, foul; the creek, clean.
- The ox in the weir went to slurry in an afternoon, as your people saw.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
