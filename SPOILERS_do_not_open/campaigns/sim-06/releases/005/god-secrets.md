# Release: god-secrets, Year 5, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 5, and what your people report. It is your go for nothing by itself.

## What you did and what came of it
- You kept from the granary door. The harvest was counted aloud from the first sack without you, a tally-keeper of each family cutting a stick, and the closing count was cried as the Warden's.
- At the council you spoke when asked and gave what the Clerk's sticks hold of the river's cost. The Clerk set dredging, groundings and who paid each on one stick.
- The Clerk and the Gate Warden took up the count of the star: the watch on the wall cut a stick for each of the three nights, with the hours by the candle. The three agree within a finger of candle. You were in the town on all three, and the watch marked that too.
- The Clerk set down the cellars that took water and the fevers by street, as his count.
- The crews sounded a fourth spring. The cut over the bar holds, shallower by about a hand than when it was cut. The first loaded boat crossed it.
- In the eleventh month you went down by a boat house's boat to the marsh holdings, sat at three tables and heard the boat families' side of the river's cost. You were back through the river gate on the fifteenth day, a day before the day you named, and went first to the Clerk.
- You felt no weight in the air.

## Asked of you and not answered
Nothing stands unanswered that reached you.

## What the Keeper ruled
Nothing was asked that your card does not plainly cover.

## The sky
The star dimmed on three nights of your year, fewer than last year: twice in the second month and once in the seventh. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-four such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About as last year. The count at the door is the town's own now.
