# Release: god-healing, Year 5, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 5, and what your people report. It is your go for nothing by itself.

## What you did and what came of it
- You went to the Reckoner first. The woman in the hard labor last summer lived, and the child with her. Your words went to that steading by the Reckoner.
- Your people set you right about the salt: salt boats came up the river with salt in sacks, as they do. No salt stands in the water. The Warden sent no runner.
- At the mounds you told Hedda what you know and do not know of the star. The three sitters heard that you know no brewing.
- The bank ground, the third month, with the Keeper of the Mounds saying his rows by month. Under the rows of the wasting winter and those laid on the low bank since, the soil reads foul, as last year and no less; the wells below them the same. Under the older rows it reads sound. Under the rows laid on the rise since his peg it reads sound, and so does the rise.
- In the fifth month you walked west for the trees. The fen holds a walker to about six miles a day. In two and a half days you made about fifteen miles and saw no treeline; by the ground you crossed it lies far beyond what five days allow. Every spring and pool you read on the way was clean. You met no people, no burial ground and no midden. You turned as you had said and were at the Reckoner's door on the fifth day.
- Three mendings, each at Hedda's asking after the sitters had done what they could: a man with a fouled shin in the second month, a child with the chest rattle in the eighth, an old man with the wet chest in the eleventh. Each lived, and you took each one's fever and the shake after, as your card gives.
- Beside the younger sitter's brew in the ninth month: the old woman was closed to your reading before and after. You asked the sitter what ailed her and said what you could from her words.
- The three test wells on the rise read sound at the end of the wet.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
Nothing stands unanswered that reached you.

## What the Keeper ruled
- Salt in water is no sickness, rot or poison. Water that is only salt reads as whole.
- The steadings' sick stay closed to your reading, before a brew and after, as ruled last year.

## The sky
The star dimmed on three nights of your year, fewer than last year: twice in the second month and once in the seventh. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-four such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About as last year. No fever winter fell. Fewer sickened where the wells are closed.
