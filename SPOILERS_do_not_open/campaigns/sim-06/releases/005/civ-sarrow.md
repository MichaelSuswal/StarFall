# Release: civ sarrow, Year 5, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. Where it differs from your own Year 5 files, this is what happened. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The riders: 117.6 miles and six days on horse from the western end of the road, a little beyond the farthest house, to the river king's town, which lies south-west of the pass whatever the old packers call the way; some three weeks waiting there; received as guests; the word given into a clerk's hand; a written answer given them; one beast's load of salt bought, dear; home on the twentieth day of the second month, before the snow. What they tell and what the letter says are in your god's own telling.
- The winter stores failed some weeks short of the thaw. The herd went, then the dried meat; in the last week the seal was broken before witnesses on part of the seed. About four hundred died over the ordinary. The sowing was short. Your year takes Branch B.
- The day at the farthest door: about one part in three of the toll taken that day went into the house's own store, under the name of the watchman's tenth; the cord knots only the hall's part, and knots it truly. Your year takes Branch D2, large.
- The night the Guest was at the far door, the ford stood open, as the older watcher could not know. The night after she stepped back, the far door stood open. At the ford she was guest again until the dawn after a full day.
- The river kingdom's beasts cough. None of your own herds showed it by the year's end.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
- A correction to the record: the farthest toll house stands on the western stretch of the road, toward the river kingdom. From the winter city to the road's western end is 148.1 miles by the road, eight days on foot, and the house lies a little short of that. The 68.2 miles and five days given in pass one and last year were measured to the road's other end and were wrong. No day in your year turns on it.
