# Release: god-beasts, Year 5, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 5, and what your people report. It is your go for nothing by itself.

## What you did and what came of it
- Your people set one thing right: last year's calving cost about thirty calves, not six. The Houndwarden's six was the first night's count.
- At the first dusk council Tessaly said your words whole to the Speaker and to the young herder of the clans that camp apart.
- The calving. You read wolves on three nights and told Tessaly each time at the first dusk. She sent hunters and dogs the same dusk, kept a third of the dogs and four hunters at the pens, and every herd along the stretch penned its calves at that dusk. The wolves were turned each night. About six calves were lost in all.
- On the two dim nights of the second month you stood at the margin before and after midnight. The herds read resting, as on any night, and the wild gatherings at the edge of the ground stood.
- No herd read hurt at any pool in the summer.
- The cutters of the stone towns: gatherings with draught beasts, working and unafraid, about as far in as last winter.
- The trade party came home with salt dear and iron grey. Its beasts were kept apart on the downwind end. At six weeks you read them at the Saltkeeper's asking: hurt in part. Two pack beasts were coughing. They were killed and burned by the old rule and the rest kept apart another month, and then read whole. The cough did not pass to the gathered herds.
- At the two clans' herd you named the gatherings that read hurt in part each few nights, and the herder found coughing beasts in them. About forty of their cattle died in the winter, wintered apart.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
Nothing stands unanswered that reached you.

## What the Keeper ruled
Nothing was asked that your card does not plainly cover.

## The sky
The star dimmed on three nights of your year, fewer than last year: twice in the second month and once in the seventh. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-four such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About as last year, with six calves and forty cattle the fewer. The three clans still camp apart.
