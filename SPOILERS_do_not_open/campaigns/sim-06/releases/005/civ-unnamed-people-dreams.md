# Release: civ unnamed-people-dreams, Year 5, pass two

What the Keeper settled of the things your report left open. Your people know these as people know things: by what they saw and were told. Where it differs from your own Year 5 files, this is what happened. It is your go for nothing by itself.

- The party: a boat grounded on a bar under its stone and three days were lost; no one hurt, the stone whole. A ship of the harbor house lay at the mouth within the ten days and took the forty blocks and the leader. The old stack had been lifted by that house in the eighth month of last year. The factor gave bread and three nights, heard the Guest's word to him, and gave salt at the standing price for last year's sixty blocks and this year's forty. His house sells no iron and no picks; the iron on his coast is the same grey iron. No carrier on the quay would take the Guest's word toward the gales. The party was home in the first days of the fourth month with two boats of salt. Your year takes Party A.
- The Guest's walk: fifteen days each way by the river road and the shore. It reached the harbor on the twentieth day of the tenth month, was a guest in the factor's yard three nights, and was at the wall on the ninth day of the eleventh month, before the day named. Your year takes Walk A. What it settled there is its own to tell.
- What the wall's men wrote of the lying nights stands as they wrote it.
- No singer holds the third part.
- The dimmings of the star are not known to be anyone's doing.
