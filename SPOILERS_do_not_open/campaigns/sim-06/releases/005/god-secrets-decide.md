# Decide: god-secrets, Year 5

## Why you come down
Gods come down here to lead a people: to find or gather them, to build, to
rule, and to spread. That is what the world below expects of you.

## The year
Year 5 begins at the turn of the year, in your waking season, autumn, and runs round the four seasons to the same season again. A year here is twelve months. Your fourth year on the ground is over. The whole of Year 5 is yours to decide tonight. You will be told what came of it in your next release.

- "We counted the harvest in at the door a third time, and the far bins were filled first by the sack with a stick from each house, and the whole store stood at thirty-eight thousand five hundred. - "The river opened on time. The bar was a little shallower again, and in the dry spell it was under the foot and a half, so by our own rule we dredged in the dry season, and it cost fourteen hundred and twenty days of hired work and two boats, and the bar is open. The farming houses say they paid for the boat houses' river, and the boat houses borrowed their share from the two houses at the table."
- "The winter fever was light among the old and hard among the small. The cellars took water again."
- "The star dimmed seven times, more than any year. We count twenty-one since the Counter came. We do not know what it is. We set the cups on the river steps and call it manners."
- "The Counter went up the river again in summer, on the cousin's boats, and said he would go on from the landing and be back before the harvest, by the twenty-second of the eleventh month."
