# Release: god-dreams, Year 5, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 5, and what your people report. It is your go for nothing by itself.

## What you did and what came of it
- In the yard of the watch you answered the captain and the Warden of the Wall, gave the Keeper of the Count your list of afraid days, and set right up for down through the Warden of the Door.
- At the barrow you heard the two parts of the third song to the end. The runners went round the hamlets and came home in the third month: old people remember that there was a third part, and no one holds its words.
- The court sent seven in two boats with forty blocks on the twentieth day of the second month, with your two words. A boat grounded on a bar under its stone and three days were lost; no one was hurt and the stone is whole. At the river's mouth the old stack was gone, the hides folded under a stone. A ship of the harbor house lay there within the ten days, and its master said his house lifted the sixty blocks in the eighth month of last year. It took the forty and carried the leader to the harbor.
- The factor gave bread and three nights and heard your word to him on the third. He said: his house holds the salt for last year's stone and gives salt for this year's at the standing price; his house sells no iron and no picks, and the iron on his coast is the same grey iron from the mining towns, soft these two winters; the delta market lies in the gulf beyond a great headland, a summer's run for a coaster that beaches at night, and no one walks it; coasters of several houses go in summer; in summer any master going in would carry a word for pay, and in autumn none. The leader asked on the quay for a carrier outside the house. None would take a word toward the gales. He brought your first word home unspoken and said so. The party was home in the first days of the fourth month with two boats of salt.
- Keeping Sleep as your card gives, two households a night, three on three named nights. You sat the last sleep in the kept houses that asked.
- In spring the four offices heard you and the throne gave leave. You set out on the fifth day of the tenth month with four of the watch and the leader as guide: 109.5 miles down the river road to the mouth and 97.9 miles round the shore, fifteen days. You stood the nights with the lamp on your waist.
- At the harbor you were a guest in the factor's yard, his door being too low for you: bread, three nights. On the third night you asked him to his face. He said yes. A coaster of his house going into the gulf this summer carries your word, by its master's mouth, to the one who reads the scales at the delta market. He said a guest who walks fifteen days to ask at the table is not a stranger's word. You gave the master your first word, with up where down had been. It has gone. You have no answer yet.
- What the harbor says of the one at the delta: a reader of scales who takes barley and no silver, seated as keeper of the town's weights for a term, who says before the yard that it is a god; and that the market there has weighed by its six old weights since last autumn. The quay looked at you and kept its distance. Nobody hindered you.
- You felt no weight in the air on the road or at the harbor. You were at the wall on the ninth day of the eleventh month, before the day you named.

## Asked of you and not answered
Nothing stands unanswered that reached you.

## What the Keeper ruled
- What the wall's men wrote of the nights your body lies still is what a watcher at your door sees: it lies with the lamp lit, breathes slow and even, does not stir to a hand, and at dawn is shaken. Nothing more is given.

## The sky
The star dimmed on three nights of your year, fewer than last year: twice in the second month and once in the seventh. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-four such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
The town kept two households a night while you were gone and met you at the gate. It has salt for a full salting, the first in five seasons.
