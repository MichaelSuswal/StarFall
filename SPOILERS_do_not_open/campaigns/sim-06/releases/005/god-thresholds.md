# Release: god-thresholds, Year 5, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 5, and what your people report. It is your go for nothing by itself.

## What you did and what came of it
- At the ford and then the hall you gave the three answers aloud and left your standing answers with Idris Veld and Kessan.
- Kessan's riders, seven with four laden beasts, left the farthest house on the twelfth day of the first month. By the road north-east and the river it is 239.4 miles from the road's far end to the river king's town, thirteen days on horse. They were received as guests. The captain gave your word into the hand of a clerk of the chancery and waited. A written answer was given him to carry. They bought one beast's load of salt in that town's market, dear, and were home on the twentieth day of the second month, before the snow.
- What the riders tell, as their telling. Why no train came in the summer: the raised toll, and the river asking less; and this year the kingdom's draught beasts cough and trains are short of beasts. Salt: it comes up the great river from a market town in its delta, and from the harbors of the south coast; it is dear everywhere inland this year. The star: it dims there on the same nights, and the people count as yours do. The king keeps his hall. The beer of that town is thin and its old barley has gone plain.
- The chancery's answer, read by the keeper of rite who has the river speech: "The king's chancery to the one who holds the ford. Your two words are entered. The king's traders go by the river because the river asks less of them, and by the road when the road asks no more than the river. The claim stands as written in the roll: a share of the tolls of the pass is the king's by the old grant. The king will send to be heard at the ford when the roads allow. Nothing is given up by this letter."
- The winter. Two counts, the ration at the lower from the first snow, the salt to the meat first, the seed under two marks. The stores failed some weeks short of the thaw. The herd went, then the dried meat. In the last week the seal was broken before witnesses on part of the seed. About four hundred died over the ordinary, the old and the youngest. The sowing was short.
- From the thaw the river kingdom's trains came again, about half as many as before the toll was raised, some with coughing beasts.
- The nineteenth day of the eighth month, a train day. You stepped from the ford to the farthest door and stood in it from first light. What you saw and counted: of what the train paid in toll that day, by the beast and the load, about one part in three was carried into the house's own store and smokehouse, and the rest set by for the hall. The keeper's cord for the day knots only what was set by for the hall, and knots that truly. Asked whose the rest was, he said: the watchman's tenth, as his father took it. Your hand moved once at the door; you said so aloud and put it down. You ate at his table. After the dawn after a full day you stepped back to the ford.
- It was judged at the sill, the keeper heard, Idris Veld and Kessan together, your view last, as your people's files tell it.
- You went up to the cairns once. The line of the grass stands at the first notch. The flower did not come.
- You felt no weight in the air.

## Asked of you and not answered
Nothing stands unanswered that reached you.

## What the Keeper ruled
- The step back is a step like the first. The door you leave stands open for the night after you leave it, so the farthest door stood open that night and told you nothing. The ford does not stand open a second night. At the ford you were guest again until the dawn after a full day.

## The sky
The star dimmed on three nights of your year, fewer than last year: twice in the second month and once in the seventh. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-four such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About four hundred fewer than the ordinary winter would have left. The seed is short. The far house's share is known to the hall.
