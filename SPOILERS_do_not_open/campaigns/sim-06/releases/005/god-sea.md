# Release: god-sea, Year 5, the year resolved

What you saw, heard, were told and felt in Year 5, and what your people report. It is your go for nothing by itself.

## What you did and what came of it
- At the tide door in the first dusks you answered the Warden-Monarch and gave her your words for the Harbormaster and the Eldest Midwife. She carried each by her own mouth in the autumn.
- The night's listening off the mouth: the home coast's ordinary day, and nothing that is not tide. You lay the deaf day under the rocks.
- You laid three pale stones by hand beside the broken timbers on the line of the chain. In the second month the Harbormaster had a lead dragged along that line. It rang on stone, and his grapnel brought up a strake and a rib. The Warden told it to the water at the tide door and you heard her.
- Sound Timber twice: one keel timber of the point's new boat in the fourth month, and in the eleventh the sheer strake of the ferryman's boat. Both are sound.
- In spring and the swell months you learned the shoal off the point with your own body and sent its shape to the Warden by the jar, in notches and a mark. The Harbormaster has sounded it on three mornings and drawn it.
- On the nineteenth day of the eleventh month a decked boat of the quay's households, four aboard and low in the water with her long line, ran for the mouth on a long swell. The bell was struck twice about an hour before dusk. You heard it below the quay and came at once to the water's edge below the lamp. The lamp the tenders had lit burned there as a light. You put it out and lit it with your own hand, and the mouth lay calm as far as its light would show. She came in, all four, on the Captains' line. The tenders and those on the quay head saw a hooded figure at the water's edge with the lamp.
- The harbor water is closed to you for a season from that night, as your lamp costs.
- The Midwife sent no house this year, and you went to none.
- You felt no other like yourself.

## Asked of you and not answered
Nothing stands unanswered that reached you.

## What the Keeper ruled
- A bell struck on the quay carries into the water by the town; where you lay this summer you heard it.
- Your lamp gives its calm only lit by your own hand. A flame another hand lit must be put out and lit again by yours.
- Two pieces of Sound Timber weeks apart are inside your card. Stones laid by hand are stones.

## The sky
The star dimmed on three nights of your year, fewer than last year: twice in the second month and once in the seventh. Each time the blue deepened for about an hour, the star stood dim for about an hour in the middle of the night, and then it was as before. Your people count twenty-four such nights since you came.

## Your people at the year's turn
About 2,045. A boat came in under the lamp with you at it, before the quay.
