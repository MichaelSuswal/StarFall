# Landing: god-thresholds

## The hour
The hour before first light. The season you wake into: autumn.

## What you sense
Rock and frozen turf under you. Thin hard air, and the sound of a fast river below. Snow on the high ground gives back the star's light. Woodsmoke comes up the valley.
You feel no other like yourself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, as a weight in the air that settles over a moment into knowing, and it tells nothing more than that the other is one like you.

## What is in view
What follows shows as the light comes.
You stand in a deep upland valley, about 1,900 feet up, beside a road.
- The road passes 150 yards to the north-east, about 6 miles of it in view, from the west to the north-east.
- A ford lies about a mile to the south-east.
- A river lies about a mile to the north-east, about 3 miles of it in view, running from the east to the north.
- A pass opens about a mile to the north-west, and another about 2 miles to the north-east.
- A city lies about 2 miles to the south-east.
- Mountains stand on the skyline to the south-west, their nearest edge about 14 miles off.
A level stretch of ground shows out to about 3 miles; things on the ground show within about 2.
The star stands in the south, about a fifth of the way up the sky, above the valley wall: a round blue light, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by.

## What was told you before, corrected
Nothing to correct. The ford, both passes, the cut steps, the road and the smoke are as you were told; the smoke is the city's, about 2 miles to the south-east. The heights stand on the skyline.

## Your helpers
Your people know you on sight for the one they awaited, and the offices you described serve you from this night, by your own account of the meeting. Each holder is who they are and can still fail you.
