# Landing: god-dreams

## The hour
The hour before first light. The season you wake into: autumn.

## What you sense
Stony ground under you, dry. Mist below, lying along the low land. The smell of stubble, turned earth and kiln smoke. The air is still and has the first cold in it.
You feel no other like yourself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, as a weight in the air that settles over a moment into knowing, and it tells nothing more than that the other is one like you.

## What is in view
What follows shows as the light comes.
You stand in a river valley, on stony ground about 180 feet up.
- A river lies about a mile to the south-west, about 3 miles of it in view, bending from the west to the south.
- A scar where stone has been cut lies about a mile to the north-west.
- A town lies about a mile to the south-east.
- Hills stand on the skyline to the west, their nearest edge about 37 miles off.
- Mountains stand on the skyline to the north, their nearest edge about 81 miles off.
A level stretch of ground shows out to about 3 miles; things on the ground show within about 2.
The star stands in the north-west, about a fifth of the way up the sky: a round blue light, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by.

## What was told you before, corrected
The river's bend, the scar and the smoke are as you were told; the smoke is the town's, about a mile to the south-east. Nothing you were told is missing. Hills stand on the skyline to the west and mountains far to the north.

## Your helpers
The offices of your people stand and are filled, and each runs on the people's own custom until its holder takes you for the one awaited and hears you. Until then no one acts for you while you are away.
