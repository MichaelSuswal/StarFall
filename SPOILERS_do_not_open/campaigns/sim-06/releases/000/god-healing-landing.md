# Landing: god-healing

## The hour
The hour before first light. The season you wake into: spring, the thaw.

## What you sense
Dark silt under you, soft and cold. The sound of a big slow river carrying ice. Wet air, the smell of mud, rot and the first green. Woodsmoke, faint.
You feel no other like yourself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, as a weight in the air that settles over a moment into knowing, and it tells nothing more than that the other is one like you.

## What is in view
What follows shows as the light comes.
You stand on flat bottom land beside a slow river, about 10 feet up.
- Low mounds lie about half a mile to the north-west.
- The river lies about half a mile to the west, about 4 miles of it in view, running from the north to the south.
- A village lies about 2 miles to the south-east.
- Forest stands as a line of trees on the skyline to the west, its nearest edge about 22 miles off.
A level stretch of ground shows out to about 3 miles; things on the ground show within about 2.
The star stands in the east, about a seventh of the way up the sky: a round blue light, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by.

## What was told you before, corrected
The mounds, the river and the smoke are as you were told; the smoke is a village's, about 2 miles to the south-east. The forest shows as a line of trees on the skyline. No mountain is in view.

## Your helpers
The offices of your people stand and are filled, and each runs on the people's own custom until its holder takes you for the one awaited and hears you. Until then no one acts for you while you are away.
