# Landing: god-sea

## The hour
The hour before first light. The season you wake into: autumn.

## What you sense
Shingle under you, wet and cold. A hard wind off the water, with salt and rotting weed in it. The sound of the sea breaking on rock close by. The days are shortening and the first of the winter storms is not far off.
You feel no other like yourself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, as a weight in the air that settles over a moment into knowing, and it tells nothing more than that the other is one like you.

## What is in view
What follows shows as the light comes.
You stand on a cold broken shore, about 6 feet above the water.
- The sea opens from the point where you stand, out to the skyline.
- Rocks stand in the sea at the point itself.
- A town lies about a mile to the east.
A level stretch of ground shows out to about 3 miles; things on the ground show within about 2.
The star stands in the south-west, about a seventh of the way up the sky: a round blue light, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by.

## What was told you before, corrected
Nothing to correct. The harbor wall and the smoke you were told of are the town about a mile to the east. The skerries are the rocks at the point where you stand.

## Your helpers
The offices of your people stand and are filled, and each runs on the people's own custom until its holder hears you and takes your word. Until then no one acts for you while you are away.
