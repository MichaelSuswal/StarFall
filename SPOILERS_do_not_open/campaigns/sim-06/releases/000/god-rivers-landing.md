# Landing: god-rivers

## The hour
The hour before first light. The season you wake into: spring.

## What you sense
Water coming out of the ground at your feet, cold, and running away downhill. Wet turf. The air is mild and full of the sound of birds waking on water somewhere below. The smell of woodsmoke comes up the slope.
You feel no other like yourself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, as a weight in the air that settles over a moment into knowing, and it tells nothing more than that the other is one like you.

## What is in view
What follows shows as the light comes.
You stand in low hills, about 420 feet up, at the head of a stream.
- The stream runs from the point where you stand, about half a mile of it in view, from the south to the south-east.
- A path runs with it from the same point, about half a mile of it in view.
- About 2 miles to the south-east there is a town, seen only as its smoke and its lights, not the place itself.
On this ground a level stretch shows out to about 3 miles; things on the ground show only within about half a mile.
The star stands in the north-west, about a seventh of the way up the sky: a round blue light, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by.

## What was told you before, corrected
The stream, the path worn down along it and the smoke are as you were told; the smoke is a town's, about 2 miles to the south-east. One thing differs. The wide lowland of many channels is not in view from the spring head.

## Your helpers
The offices of your people stand and are filled, and each runs on the people's own custom until its holder takes you for the one the seat is kept for and hears you. Until then no one acts for you while you are away.
