# Landing: god-secrets

## The hour
The hour before first light. The season you wake into: autumn.

## What you sense
Firm ground under you, a little raised, with wet ground somewhere below it. Cold mist. The smell of marsh gone brown, and of woodsmoke. Geese calling overhead in the dark.
You feel no other like yourself. That knowing comes only at close quarters, in the same room, across a doorway, within a few paces, as a weight in the air that settles over a moment into knowing, and it tells nothing more than that the other is one like you.

## What is in view
What follows shows as the light comes.
You stand on a low river plain, on a rise about 25 feet up, on a worn track.
- The track runs through the point where you stand, about 3 miles of it in view, from the south-west to the north-east.
- A town lies about a mile to the north-east.
- A river lies about a mile to the north-west, about 4 miles of it in view, running from the south to the north.
- Mountains stand on the skyline to the south, their nearest edge about 16 miles off.
- A plain country rises a little on the skyline to the south-west, its nearest edge about 28 miles off.
A level stretch of ground shows out to about 3 miles; things on the ground show within about 2.
The star stands in the south-west, about an eighth of the way up the sky, above the mountains' line: a round blue light, wider than the moon and steady. It gives light enough to walk by.

## What was told you before, corrected
The walled place, its smoke and the worn track are as you were told: the town about a mile to the north-east, and the track you stand on. The river is in view. Two things differ. Mountains stand on the skyline to the south, which you were not told. The wet ground at the bend's edges is not in view from the rise.

## Your helpers
The offices of your people stand and are filled, and each runs on the people's own custom until its holder hears you and takes your word. Until then no one acts for you while you are away.
