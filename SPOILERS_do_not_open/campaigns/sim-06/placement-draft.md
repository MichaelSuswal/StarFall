# Placement draft: sim-06 (Night 2)

Provisional. Working names only; ids come at tracing on Night 3a, and the plan written first on 3a.3 may move anything here except the landing spots and what the Day 3 brief has said. No distance, bearing or sight line in any release comes from this file; those come from the traced map.

## The region
One continent, wider than it is tall: about 1,100 miles east to west and 850 north to south at its broadest, about 650 miles across at its narrowest, about 1,000 across on average. Aim: 800 to 1,200. A cold sea to the north; a deep sheltered gulf biting into the south-west; open coasts east and south. High ground in the north-west and in a broken spine down the middle-east; lowlands west, south and south-east.

## The seats
A ring of seats round the continent, spaced evenly in rough terms, each in country its god asked for (Q2) from the terrain list. Going round from the north:
- god-sea, the north coast: a cold broken shore of skerries and small harbors.
- god-secrets, the north-east: firm ground on the bend of a river that runs to the north-east coast, marsh at its edges.
- god-beasts, the east: wooded uplands falling to broad grazing plains, rivers through both, marsh in the low places.
- god-rivers, the south-east: springs in low hills, a braided lowland, fords and floodplain, a shifting delta on the south-east coast.
- god-dreams, the south: river country with hills behind, flooding soil, stone near, a river bend.
- the player's god, the south-west: the head of the sheltered gulf, where several rivers reach it in a wide delta; salt flats, reed islands, low hills behind with ore and clay; ground hard to cross.
- god-healing, the west: a slow lowland river with marsh at its edges and forest behind; no mountains in sight.
- god-thresholds, the north-west: the high broken country of gorges, passes, fords and narrows, where the roads between west and north must go.
Each seat's river is its own system except that the gulf takes several. Six seats asked for river country, so the rivers are many and far apart.

## Landing spots
Set here, before any text says what is seen from where a god comes down. What "lies in reach of its eyes" is in kind only; the sight rule and the trace settle the rest.
- god-sea: on a shingle spit below a headland, at the water's edge. In reach: broken shore both ways, skerries, a small harbor's wall and smoke.
- god-secrets: on a low firm rise inside a river bend. In reach: the river on three sides, marsh beyond it, a walled place's smoke upstream, a worn track.
- god-beasts: at a wood's edge on a shoulder of upland. In reach: timber behind, open grass falling away, a river's line of trees, wet ground in a hollow.
- god-rivers: at a spring head in low hills. In reach: the stream running down to a braided plain, reed and standing water, far off the shine of many channels.
- god-dreams: on a stony knoll above a river bend. In reach: the bend, bottom land with flood marks, hills behind, a quarry scar, smoke.
- the player's god: on an old levee of firm clay at the head of the delta, where the channels part. In reach: reed islands and channels, pale salt flats toward open water, low hills behind, smoke from more than one fire, a worn track along the levee.
- god-healing: on a river bank of dark silt in flat country. In reach: the slow river, marsh at its edges, forest standing behind, a grave field's low mounds.
- god-thresholds: in the mouth of a pass, where a track comes down to a ford. In reach: the gorge, the ford, a second pass across the valley, cut steps, smoke from a camp.

## What is not placed here
- The center point and the ring of stones upon it: provisionally in open godless country near the middle of the continent, inside the ring of seats, because the middle favors no seat. Final on 3a.3.
- The warm stone that fell with them lies between a pair of seats, by the lore. It is not placed by the Keeper's preference: it is left to the placement roll in 3a.3, among the adjacent pairs of seats once the ring is set.
- The dozen fading places, the godless peoples (about half the land held), the non-human kind, the buried things and every rare candidate of the world draft: 3a.3 and 3b.

## What each land will carry of the deep history
One paragraph each, to be written in full on 3b.
- The north coast: harbors older than the people in them, sunken roads leading out under the water, bones in a sea cliff. A coast that has lost towns to the sea before.
- The north-east bend: a market town's ground many times rebuilt, cellars under cellars, the leavings of the vanished trading power thickest here or in the south-west, to be divided on 3b so that neither seat holds more.
- The eastern uplands and plains: herding country that was farmed once; a long curve of bones under a hill; old roads the grass has taken.
- The south-east lowland: channels that have moved many times, a buried dry bed, an offering place no one keeps.
- The southern river country: a walled place of the old kingdoms' middle age, quarried stone reused, careful graves forgotten.
- The gulf head: the old trading power's sea-door; weights, tokens and sealed vaults in the low hills; salt worked here for longer than anyone has counted.
- The western lowland: old grave fields, a people that tended their dead with great care and are gone; one of the fading places lies near, to be set on 3b.
- The north-west passes: gates in hillsides, cut roads, toll places of a rule long ended; the hardest crossings, where some of the nameless things keep.
