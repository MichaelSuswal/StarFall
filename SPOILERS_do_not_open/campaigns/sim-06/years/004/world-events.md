The godless peoples, by the Keeper:
- The Aldermark [r10]: the old king keeps his hall. The chancery sent its third letter, to the holds. The barley of the Kings' Acres goes plain in month 11; the brewers say the beer has been thin two harvests. Its traders paid the Sarrow's raised toll in spring with complaint and in summer sent more by river and stayed off the Pass Road [f65] for the season. Their draught beasts took the holds' cough on the iron road; it shows next year.
- The harbor league: an ordinary year. Its first letter from the chancery is still unanswered. House Corran lifts the bend town's stone if it is brought to the river's mouth. Captain Maud Sennet goes up to the unnamed town [f20] in summer.
- The mining holds: the cough still in the oxen; grey iron; cutters a little deeper in the Ossenwold [r21]; salt still short; the chancery's letter comes with the spring traders.
- The water folk: the two gatherings. At the thaw, month 7, the flood at the mouth of the River Ellow [f07] takes the winter rafts of the bands moored there.
