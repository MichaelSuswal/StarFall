## Year 3 again: the unnamed town [f20], from day 5 of month 1 to day 14 of month 7
Fixed by play (the DM's record, Year 4 played day; `P/player/decisions-004.md` items 1 to 6; `P/messages/m-0005.md`): at the gatepost on day 5 the fish seller's balance was read out, the buying pan heavy by about one part in sixty; the god took up the half-stick; it gave the word, mended twice, cut on a pair of sticks and split. The first stick is the town's word, taken by the Stallreeve for the eleven; the carrier's half is with the ship owner of the open water. The second stick went up to the eleven, each thing by itself. The carrier took three instructions and would not promise a pair. Nothing of it is changed.
- Lines the DM gave mortals beyond their written openings, each kept: the Stallreeve's sorting of what he took and what he carried; his "What they say to the rest I bring to this post"; the Assay-master's one speaking-up; the carrier's reasons. Small facts the DM set, each kept: the runner, the fresh sticks, the asking's half in the god's hand.
- The sealed act. On the night of day 5, at the landing, the god put its hand in the pocket it keeps empty. It is empty: nothing in it, as the card gives. No one saw anything but a hand in a coat. Done as an act the god said it does (`year-turn.md` section 5); what it showed is in the sealed part of the day sheet and nowhere public. The seventh weight is where it was: in the main channel of the River Alder [f03] below the hall. No hidden power's condition is touched: no pledge was pressed on it.
- The habit. Every night at the landing it ran its thumbnail down the nest cups, smallest first, before counting the day's readings. Conduct. The boatmen call it "running the cups" (the people's agent's telling).
- Month 1. From day 6 it asked owners and captains at its landing dawns to bring large stones to the beam. They brought them [r-0387; stated 50, rolled at 55 with the sealed factor; success either way]: thirty in the stretch, as the people's report tables them, each weighed with its owner watching and called as it read (The True Measure). Day 8: the fish seller's mended balance read level. Night of day 22: a dim night [month r-0303; day r-0343], seen from the landing. Day 23: a boy asked whether the star is its own; silence, pending.
- Month 2. The hill carriers and the old weigher of Ruddfold [f25]; his six stones weighed on day 8 as 48, 96, 193, 384, 768 and 1,536, and his saying, as the earlier resolution had them. Day 11: a young carrier asked whether it can be killed and was answered in the player's words; asked what would break it, no answer (the player's word). The same day it asked the Stallreeve for day 14, the lowest tide of autumn [r-0396]; he gave it, and had the household told the night before (the people's agent's). Day 14, a market day: the god walked to the Maun Flats [f22], 1.6 miles of marsh from the town, and back before dusk. The post stood empty with a pair of the watch at it, nobody was read, no notch was cut. At the pans the household turned out every basket; the basket it chose read clean salt [r-0388: the world's question, odds 10, failed; nothing is bulked this season]. A boy asked the empty post whether the star is its own. The water folk were gone from the channels from day 12 to day 16 (row PH-2).
- Month 3. Rain. Day 14: the potters' hall's own scale read level. Day 20: a stallholder asked whether it can tell who is lying, and then whether a man it read knows; answered by its standing words and by decision 11.
- Month 4. Day 18: an old net-maker's door-stone read 3,011 [r-0392, the world's question, weighed by the evidence, odds 40, success; r-0393 a door-stone; r-0394 month 4; r-0395 the figure]. The god's own reckoning for the seventh is 3,072. It said what the stone read and nothing more, told the Stallreeve and the Assay-master first and the ring second. The Assay-master went to look and said it was never cut. Four people tried to buy it; the Stallreeve forbade buying a stone at a landing for its weight. It is a door-stone, from the old quay as its owner believes. It is not the seventh weight. No clue of mystery S01 was moved, added or taken away; the yard says "sixty-one short". Day 20: the Assay-master's six scales, a pair level and four a hair out. Day 24: it stood witness to the lease of a boatyard and said what it saw and heard.
- Month 5. A hard spell; fever in the back houses, fewer than the year before. Two dim nights [r-0302, r-0304]. Day 11: an old woman of the back houses asked whether it can cure a fever and was answered in the player's words. The woman who asked a year before did not come [r-0390].
- Month 6. Day 15: the ring's winter sitting, door shut, the god not sent for. The second stick, thing by thing [r-0379 to r-0386, each the god's asking of its council, the sealed factor after the stated odds; none turned on it]. All eleven knew of the clerk, the punt and the loft when the empty seat was put; eight took it (the people's agent's sealed telling). The master of the smiths asked nothing [r-0389]. Day 17: the Stallreeve said at the post which were taken and which not, with the counts and one reason, and asked the god what it would now have cut for the carrier. Its decided words do not answer that: silence, pending. Nothing more was cut.
- Month 7. The thaw; the River Alder [f03] rising to an ordinary flood. Day 2: a netmaker asked whether it can tell the height of the water as the tide-reckoner does; silence, pending. ## god-sea and its people
Met the Warden-Monarch at the tide door at the lowest tide of the first dusks and gave its answers for the Harbormaster and the Eldest Midwife (m-0006, m-0007); she carried each by her own mouth in the autumn; delivered, no hazard within one town, not rolled. It read the clerks' list of the dead. Water's Hearing at the point the night after: the coast's ordinary day; nothing that is not tide. Sound Timber on a pile at the quay head, month 4, and on the garboard strake of the east cove's long-liner, month 11 (L-0042); both sound. No lamp was lit in winter. Spring: it went west under the water by night to the Drowned Causeway [f33], 66.5 miles by the shore, and gave one night to listening there. Ruled (L-0047): the twenty miles are measured from where it listens. What it heard: the shore's ordinary day, surf on the stones, no hull, no voice, nothing moving among the footings but tide. Nothing has moved there since it swam there; nothing is kept by anyone. Month 8, day 20, the god away: a decked boat of the point ran for the mouth on a long swell. The Warden-Monarch had the god's lamp lit by the tenders, on her own word. Ruled (L-0048): lit by another hand it is a lamp and gives no calm. The boat broke at the mouth [r-0404; odds 45; failed at 99]: her master and his elder son drowned; the younger son and the hired hand were hauled out by the Captains' boat. The god was back in the water by the town in the fourth week of month 9. It heard the Warden's telling at the tide door when it was again in that water (the Year 2 ruling). Month 10: it came once to the widow's house the Midwife sent, at the lowest tide at dusk, and heard what she asked; its decisions give it no words beyond those already carried, and what she asked there stands unanswered. The Banked Coal and Crew Count knacks were exercised. No hidden power's moment came: it took hold of no chain.

## god-dreams and its people
Answered the captain and the four in the yard of the watch, and the father at the second house; where its decisions rested on things its people know otherwise, they told it so. Gave the Keeper of the Count its list of afraid days. Row L05-e: the oldest barrow singer died in month 3; her house kept no flame and no one asked that her sleep be walked. Keeping Sleep as the card gives; three households on three named nights. The court sent the stone: nine in three boats with sixty blocks, linen, pots and oil, from the quay on day 24 of month 2, carrying the god's word for the one at the delta market (m-0008). A boat grounded on a low-water bar under its stone [r-0401; odds 65; failed]: three days lost getting her off, no one hurt, the stone whole. No ship of the house lay at the mouth in the ten days the leader waited [r-0402, the world's question, odds 40, failed]. The stone was stacked above the high water under hides, and the leader went round the shore on foot with a pair of runners, 97.9 miles, reaching Port Sennet [f75] late in month 3. The factor of House Corran gave bread and three nights, said the ship lifts at the mouth at the first fair weather after the gales since no day was set, sold salt for the linen and oil at the standing price, and would not have the god's word carried on [r-0403; odds 60; failed]: the house does not carry a stranger's word to a market it sells to, he said, and he keeps what he hears. The leader judged the coast road to the delta unsafe before the gales and turned for home, as his leave allowed: m-0008 returned. On the quays he heard nothing new: last year's talk, that the keeper of weights at the delta market is still seated. They came up the river in the boats and were home in the second week of month 4, all nine, with a little salt. The town had a fourth lean winter for salt. Row W-2: the flood at the river's mouth in month 7 went below the stack and did not reach it; the house's ship lifted the stone in month 8 and holds the salt for it at the harbor. The town does not know that yet. Spring: the four offices answered what the town would do if it were away. The Watch-Wakeful knack was exercised. No hidden power surfaced.

## god-rivers and its people
Answered the small boy at the fire and the head herd keeper at the council. The weir hands measured: every weir shoaled behind, the older the more. The council opened a gap in the oldest weir (the people's agent's). Summer: by barge to Wellen Ford [f47], then down the River Wellen [f08] five days, 133.6 miles to its mouth by the tool, 4.5 days: through the Wellen Hills [r25] and the Braidwater [r26] to the shifting delta on the Lome Sea [r32], where the water turned salt on the fifth day. Met: reed cutters at their staithes, and a band of the water folk at the delta. What was learned: no salt comes up this river; the reed cutters of the lower channels say the salt for the stone towns is landed at a harbor far to the west and goes up to the holds' south town by land (mystery S05, the second half of its truth, as a people's report, no place named). The ford keeper was answered at the ford. It stood in the river each dusk and dawn: no pull; no other like itself. The one listening, at the delta: within twenty miles, salt and fresh water meeting, the channels' ordinary life, boats of the water folk, nothing like itself. Nothing came against its sleep; not rolled, no threat in the record. About twelve days home. Month 10: Warm hands on a child of the lower town; the fever came down and the god took the chill, as the card gives. Autumn: it told the council all of it. No knack's moment came.

## god-healing and its people
Went to the Reckoner first. Ruled (L-0049): the fen's steadings are its own people, so the Reading is closed on their sick, before a sitter's brew and after; it asked the sitters what ailed each. Answered the old sitter and the three steadings' sitters. Month 3, the bank ground with the Keeper of the Mounds: under the rows laid in the wasting winter and since, many in one place on the low bank, the soil reads foul, and the wells below them; under the older rows it reads sound; the rise reads sound (mystery S07, as effects). Summer, twelve days alone downstream along the River Morrow [f02], about 58 miles at its own pace on fen ground and back: at the two emptied steadings the bank burials and the wells below them read foul in the same way; the river reads clean all the way; below them no steading, no burial ground and no other people within its reach. Four mendings as the card gives, each cost paid, not rolled. Test wells sound. No hidden power's moment came.

## god-beasts and the Longwalk
Gave the herder its answer through Tessaly at the dusk council. The wolves at the calving: its readings were true and were acted on the same dusk, and the wolves still got in among the calves on the second and third nights [r-0397; stated 90, rolled at 95 with the sealed factor; failed either way, 98]: about thirty calves lost. The eldest head said he had said so. Summer: at the pool below the third camp a herd read hurt in part at the first dusk (a pair of beasts horned in a fight, the herders found) and resting the next; the camp stayed. Months 7 and 8: the cutters, three gatherings with draught beasts, working and unafraid, a little farther in. Autumn: the trade party home with half the salt wanted and grey iron; its beasts had not taken the cough [r-0398, the world's question, odds 10, failed] and read whole at six weeks. Winter: the cough stayed in the two clans' cattle, about forty dead, wintered apart. A young herder of the three clans asked twice about the star and was answered in its decided words. The Windward Sense knack was exercised. No hidden power's moment came.

## god-secrets and its people
The harvest was counted aloud at the door from the first sack and the store squared; the closing count was cried as the Warden's. Winter: it told the Clerk of the Tally what the old stick says, at his wish. Ruled (L-0050): speech of what it once read is no new use. The Clerk has told no one. Spring: the channel a little shallower a third year; the council's rule fired and the bar was dredged in months 10 to 11 (the people's craft). Summer: up the River Iss [f11] from day 12 of month 10, 39.7 miles, four days; then on alone from the boat families' landing by the Iss Track [f37], 17.3 miles, and onto the Ansel Plain [r04] with drovers at their pace, about twelve days out and twelve back. It saw the plain and never came within many days of the pass. Its listening brought nothing new [r-0405; stated 50, rolled at 55 with the sealed factor; failed either way]: the drovers it met tell the same tale of the pass herders' Guest it had last year. The dim nights of months 10 and 11 found it on the plain. It was back on day 20 of month 11, before the day it named. The Figure-Memory knack was exercised. No hidden power's moment came.

## god-thresholds and the Sarrow
At Marle Ford [f61] the older watcher gave the old reckoning as his father had it: by the beast. In the hall she sat with the commander alone, then gave her three answers aloud. Her word for the river king's chancery (m-0009) left the ford in autumn with the master of a mixed train; a known road, no hazard, not rolled; 246.8 miles by the tool, 22 days on foot; given to a clerk of the chancery at Aldermoot [f70] in month 2; delivered. The chancery entered it and sent no answer and no envoy this year. Two counts of the stores; the ration cut from first snow; the seed sealed. The far houses' loan [r-0399; odds 50; failed]: the nearer house sent about two days' worth, the farthest words only. The stores lasted to within days of the thaw and the seed seal was whole [r-0400; odds 55; success]: about a hundred and fifty dead over the ordinary. Month 7: she walked to the farthest toll house, 68.2 miles by the tool, and laid her sixth blessing on its door (The Latch). From then she knows when anyone crosses that door and how many. What it gave her in the season: the watchmen and their households in and out many times a day; on train days more hands carrying in than the house's own number. It tells her nothing of what was carried. From the thaw the trains were fewer by about a third. Month 8: the three sat with the cords and gave back half the raise from month 9. Row S08-b: in months 10 to 12 no train of the Aldermark [r10] came; trains of other peoples came. Summer: with the columns on the high grass; cairns and notches at the line. The Table-Seating knack was exercised.
