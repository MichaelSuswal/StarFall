# Year 1: everything that happened, with causes

Resolved on the night turn of 2026-11-24. Rolls are the sealed log's unless written "DM r-". The world's own events are in `world-events.md` beside this file.

## The sky and the world
- The star sank by its yearly figure (row E01-a). No one could notice.
- Three dim nights (row PH-1; count 3 by `tools/phenomena.py`): two in month 1 and one in month 5 of every people's year [r-0244, r-0245, r-0246]. Every people saw them and most tied them to the newcomer among them. They are wrong: the cause is the star's own.
- The water folk gathered twice off the Whin Flats [r29] (row PH-2). Cranes gathered once on the Ansel Plain [r04] (row PH-3).
- Weather ordinary everywhere. One random event in twelve rolls [r-0230 to r-0241]: wolves at a Sarrow fold [r-0234, r-0242, r-0243].
- T-1: the coughing sickness went with traded beasts from the Longwalk to the mining holds of the Hask Ridge [r20], to show in Year 2 [r-0247]. Cause: the two clans' sick cattle (mystery S04) and the yearly beast trade.
- The godless peoples kept their ordinary year. The league captain's coaster did not come up to the unnamed town [f20] [r-0248].
- No message was sent by anyone. No god met another, and no god's people met another god.

## The player's god and the unnamed town [f20]
The played day (the DM's record): the god walked in by the Bank Way [f21] on a market day [DM r-0001], watched the bench weigh wrong [DM r-0002], weighed the salt and then the hall's ten pieces in front of the yard, was ruled right by the Assay-master, named itself the Weigher, read scales at the gate for fees in kind [DM r-0003 to DM r-0005], and went up to the hall at dusk.
- The meeting at dusk, resolved by the player's handed-off words (ledger L-0032). The Assay-master asked how it knew and was told, in the god's words; asked to be shown again and was: three things he set out, one a small fired weight he had marked himself twenty years before, each read as he knew it to be (The True Measure, as the card gives). Asked what it wanted and was given the offer in the god's words. Asked its name: none but the Weigher. Five asks the words do not answer met silence: why the eleven should take a stranger's scale; whether it is something more than a man with good brass; who sent it, and whether a king upriver wants his share again; why it asked how the old Weightward died ("He was my friend"); what it wants of the hall after the weighing. He took the silences as he took them (the people's report) and carried the offer to the eleven as his own on the ninth day, with his own hand on the beam as a term. He said nothing of the rack or the old weights' number; the god did not ask and has not seen the rack. Nothing in the people's account of the meeting contradicts mystery S01.
- The year at the gate. The god read scales at the foot of the gatepost through the year on its stated terms. Every reading was true (the card). The Stallreeve set two of the watch to keep the line, then brought every dispute of weight to the gate and ruled on the reading himself. The Tally-keeper began, unasked, a split stick of the gate's readings. The Assay-master tested the gate in winter with fourteen scales, some with faults he had set; all were read right; he told the Stallreeve only.
- The clay. From month 3 five of seven kiln-masters weighed a fixed basket of each digger's load and refused the heavy ones as gritty. It did not work, and was not rolled: the loads that crack are cut with clay from the old yards on the Pellow Downs [r15], which has no temper (mystery L05), and a basket of it weighs like any other. By summer the sorting kilns had cracked as many loads as before; the heavy digger, whose clay is from a lower, denser pit and sound, was wronged; some potters now say the gate's scale is no use for clay. The people's cause for the cracking (grit) is wrong.
- Month 6, the cup. A bonded man of a stallholder whose scale the gate had read out tried to lift a cup from the nest in the dark at the landing, where the god sits late by the channel folk's lamp. He failed [r-0255; stated odds 10, rolled at 5 with the sealed factor]. The god's hand closed over the nest before his did; it said nothing and did not strike him; Bren's people took hold of him and the watch held him. At first light the Stallreeve brought him to the gatepost and asked the stranger whether it asked a price of him. Not a question of weight: silence (r-001). The town went on by its own way: the council heard it in its ring and set a price in measure for a theft tried on a guest of the yard; the Tally-keeper holds the stick, the price unclaimed. The man gave his master's name. Whether the master sent him the council did not find.
- Asked of the god through the year: twenty things (the people's report, in full). Those its decisions answer were answered as decided (the fee, the order of the line, the readings). The rest met silence and stand as pending questions.
- Month 5: the tide-reckoner called the midwinter water four days ahead, right in height and half a day late in hour, inside her priced knack (L-0024); her hands were numb for the season.
- Month 8: the head of the hill goods, who voted against, sent a walker to Ruddfold [f25] by the Old Rudd Way [f23], 123.9 miles, 7 days on foot by the tool, with a split stick asking the hill folk to send a witness with their own weights. He arrived. Any witness comes down with the carriers in Year 2.
- Month 9: the eleven voted seven to three, one absent, to ask the stranger to weigh the old weights before them after the harvest, with the Assay-master's hand on the beam.
- Month 11, held. The Stallreeve brought the invitation to the gatepost on a market day, spoke it before the yard and laid the half-stick at the foot of the post. The god's decisions cover weighing the old weights before the council; the day, the hand on the beam and the setting are the council's terms, and an answer is a promise. Unsure is major: the thread stops at the laid stick. The day the council set is the first market of the new autumn.
- The year's numbers: the people's `state.md`. An ordinary year; the market less quarrelsome; the watch sixty-four.
- True scales (L-0031): Bren's balance since the landing day, and each scale the god tried and found level, each while it reads true.
- Hidden powers and knacks: none surfaced. Growth: nothing earned.

## god-sea and its people
Listened on the first night (the deaf day after), watched the quay for some dusks, met the Warden-Monarch at the tide-door in month 2 and was taken at its word; she named it to four of the court in month 3. The Master of Timber took its word after one pile was joined (Sound Timber, as the card). The Harbormaster did the same orders as the court's own. The boats were hauled early; no storm of the five did harm. Month 4: the late boat came in with all five [r-0249], the mouth lying calm under the god's lamp (L-0033); that water will not take the god until month 7. Banked Coal surfaced. Winter listening once more. Spring: it walked some miles of strand each way from the point by night, at an old man's pace, and set its own marks on rocks at the tideline. It found nothing made by hands that the town does not already know: the nearest old places of that coast lie days beyond where it walked. Summer: one keel joined.
What the Warden-Monarch was shown: what the god's sheet gives of its shape, and no more. The sticks it left: notches, the count answered and one mark of its own. It did not answer whether it was the one Brennis spoke to, nor the Master of Timber's two questions; its decisions give no words for them.

## god-dreams and its people
Came to the gate as a guest, was taken in by the Warden of the Door, kept the three nights, and was taken by him and then the seat for the one awaited. The court's hearth was lit; about 330 households asked for the flame (L-0034). Keeping Sleep two households a night; the fear arrived as the card gives. Walked the dreams of about a dozen dying in kept houses; what the households say of it is theirs. Watch-Wakeful surfaced. Spring: the hamlets. Summer: the scar and the wall; two parties sent in month 11. The river party reached the mouth of the River Ellow [f07], met a league coaster's crew beached there, traded linen and pots for salt, learned that the salt is boiled at a harbor town west along that coast and what it fetches there, far under what the carriers ask at the bend, and came home in month 12 [r-0251]. The western party found no one in the hills, ran short and turned for home; not back at the year's end [r-0250]. Salt carriers took word of the guest to the harbor towns.

## god-rivers and its people
Listened at the spring head (the town's ordinary morning; clean water; a ford downstream; no pull). Walked in, was fed at the first hearth, heard by the council, let stay as a guest who counts. No one takes it for a god. Warm hands on three: each recovered, and the chill was paid in days as the card gives. Flood marks cut, a flood store set aside, the lower well relined, all as the people's own. Autumn: the long listening at the water gate under watch; nothing came against it, and it could not be woken. What it heard: the river's muddle of a day, the salt carriers' talk at the ford of fewer crossings, the deep water lying farther from the ford; no pull.

## god-healing and its people
Read the mounds (old bone, quiet) and the bank beside them, where the newer graves lie: wet ground, foul with the newly dead, draining toward the low side. The village was the court steading [f55]; its well and store read sound. About thirty mendings through the year, each as the card gives, each cost paid; the Hearth-Holder's granddaughter lived. The Reckoner asked straight and was told; she took its word in month 9. On the late summer walk and through the autumn it read the wells of the low steadings downstream of the bank burials and found them fouled with the same foulness as the bank; it said so plainly and the wells were drawn off or closed where the elders would (the branch of S07 in which the wells are read: the fever thins there; row S07-a will meet what is then there). One heap rotted by its hand in month 8. A faint tremor stays in its hands until the thaw, which opens Year 2.

## god-beasts and the Longwalk
Read the country from the wood's edge (no band within reach: fixed fact), followed the Herd Road [f40], reached the rear camp on the fourth dusk, and was let keep the margin by the Houndwarden on her own word. Spring wolves turned [r-0252, with the sealed factor]; the summer pack turned [r-0253, with the sealed factor]. Heard at council in month 6; stood with the hunt in autumn; wintered at the margin of the main camp. No office has taken its word. Windward Sense surfaced. The three clans wintered apart after a knife cut over fuel wood. The trade party went to the holds and came back with dear salt and some iron; sick beasts went with it (T-1). L-0035.

## god-secrets and its people
Came in as a nameless trader, was entered as a guest, urged a count aloud; the count came a little under a twentieth short. Given a stool at the foot of the table, five to four. One telling taken in trust in winter (the cooper family's second tally: one kept thing). One night as the dog: Stray's Welcome surfaced; no one touched the key. Figure-Memory surfaced at the table. Month 8: open ground searched with the key; nothing lies hidden there. Month 10: down the Iss Track [f37] to its end and onto the Ansel Plain [r04] among the drovers' trails, and back inside the fortnight. L-0036. No office holder knows what it is.

## god-thresholds and the Sarrow
Blessed Marle Ford [f61], was bidden in at the gate, read the Warlord and the First Doorwarden (neither meant harm), and was served from the first day. Hall door and gate blessed; three lesser marks laid. Month 2: the fold on the road broke [r-0254]; the flock scattered, sheep and goats lost in tens, one herder mauled, who died within days [r-0256]. Weekly counts showed the Steward's autumn figure had been kind by a fortnight; stores shared by hearth in month 6. Spring: both passes walked, Stair Pass [f62] blessed. Summer: with two columns; three strangers read at toll tables, none meaning harm; eleven strangers brought to her, all ordinary. Four full blessings held, three lesser marks. The thin tolls stand as they stood (mystery S08).
