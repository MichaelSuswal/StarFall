The godless peoples, by the Keeper:
- The Aldermark [r10]: an ordinary year. The chancery sent its second letter, to the holders of Marle Ford [f61]. Its river traders came home from the delta in summer with the news that the market has a keeper of its weights again, and who.
- The harbor league: an ordinary year. The first letter is still unanswered. Captain Maud Sennet sold her news of the delta along the Strand. She did not go up to the unnamed town [f20] this summer.
- The mining holds: the cough still in the draught oxen; the black-band ore gone to common grey from late winter; cutters a little deeper in the Ossenwold [r21]; salt still short.
- The water folk: the two gatherings; an ordinary year.
