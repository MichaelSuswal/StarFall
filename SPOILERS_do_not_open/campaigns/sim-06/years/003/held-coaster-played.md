# The coaster's master at the gate, Year 2, month 10: as voiced by the Keeper

Captain Maud Sennet, from `S/years/002/held-coaster.md`, fixed before the player gave the god's words. Her balance read level, as fixed there. Her three asks were the three fixed there. She told none of her news: the god did not ask for news, and she tells none unasked.

- The god's first two questions asked what every deckhand on Sutter Strand [r28] knows; that is not news she can sell, and she answered them shortly. New fixed fact, set conservatively from the deep history (the league was founded from the old power's southern sea-door, and old standard weights are a scarcity of this world): each league house keeps its own weights; the council at Corrith [f74] keeps one set for quarrels, cast and recast from its own earlier sets, which a house takes when it must and no one swears to. There is no old master set on the coast.
- The third question asked her judgment, which she prices. She named her price: which is the town's weight. The god's words do not answer for the town. Price refused, nothing given.
- "Did you say you were a god?" She had it from the landing, where the whole water had it since the sixth month and the ninth. The god's words: asked plainly, it tells her. Her answer is the league's own saying, from its record.
- A league quay: answered by the god's words. The town's weights: answered by the god's words (the town's). No gift offered: her note gives none.
- She did not give her name, house or port; she was not asked. The boatman's guess that she is out of Corrith [f74] stands uncorrected in the god's knowledge. She is of Port Sennet [f75].

What she carries down the coast, as news she will sell in Year 3: a reader of scales at the delta market who takes barley and no silver, who was seated as keeper of the hall's weights for a term, and who says before the yard that it is a god; the hall's clay pieces a twentieth heavy; the old weights six of seven. It is common talk on the quays of Port Sennet [f75] by the winter of Year 3. Whoever winters there may hear it.

She did not come up to the unnamed town [f20] in Year 3 [r-0321].
