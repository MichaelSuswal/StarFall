# Held: the coaster's master at the gate, Year 2, month 10

For the Keeper, when the held moment is played. Captain Maud Sennet (`cast.md`), of House Sennet, Port Sennet [f75]. She carries news between the south coast and the delta, and prices it. She gives the god her own name if asked; she does not give her house's business.

Why she came to the gate: she heard at the landing of a stranger who reads scales true and takes no silver, and that the hall's weights were found heavy. A reader of scales who cannot be bought is worth knowing to anyone who buys and sells by other people's weights. She brings her own ship's balance to be read, and means to see what the stranger is. Her balance reads level.

What she will ask: who it works for; whether it would read a cargo at a league quay if a house paid its passage; what the town means to do about its weights.

News she holds, each for a price in kind or in news of the town, never given free and never false:
- A letter came to the league this year from the chancery of the river kingdom, claiming old dues on the league's sea-door. The houses have not answered it (r-0284; mystery S09).
- A guest has stayed a year at a river-bend town up the eastern river, half a woman and half a hind, who keeps a lamp; its people came for salt this winter and wintered at Port Sennet [f75] (m-0001).
- House Corran and House Sennet are near a feud over a lost cargo.
She does not know what the guest at the bend is, and does not know of any other.

She tells none of it unasked. If the god asks for news, she names a price first.
