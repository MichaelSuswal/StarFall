The godless peoples, by the Keeper:
- The Aldermark [r10]: an ordinary year. The chancery sent its first letter. No one was sent to the delta.
- The harbor league: an ordinary year. It received the letter, an old claim on the sea-door's dues, and has not answered it. Captain Maud Sennet took her coaster up to the unnamed town [f20] in the first month of the delta's summer.
- The mining holds: the cough in the draught oxen; cutters farther into the Ossenwold [r21]; salt still short.
- The water folk: gathered twice at the lowest tides.
