# Balance: sim-06

A weight from 1 to 10 for each god's total power. The measure, set once (keeper log, 2.3): what the card can do to the world in a year (force, reach, how many it falls on, what it lets the god know from afar), less what its drawbacks and weaknesses take from the god on an ordinary day. 5 is a god plainly more than any mortal worker whom a gathered people can still trouble, answer and hurt. Resemblance between cards counts for nothing. The band is 4 to 6. Roughly equal, not exactly.

## Weights from the confirmed cards
| God | Weight | Reasons |
|---|---|---|
| the player's god | 4.5 | Five powers, every one information; one reach without limit of distance (The Seal) and one use without limit (The True Measure). The scales can be stolen and a breaking forced by any party. A return slower than the standing rungs. The DM's lean note: about level, leaning slightly down. |
| god-sea | 4 | A hearing along twenty miles of coast, a harbor calmed for a night and a day, timber that lasts. Each is local and slow, and each costs it a day or a season. Slow on land. |
| god-dreams | 4.5 | A dream walked at any distance among her people, one at a time, with her body left thin; seven households' sleep kept, at the price of their fear. Nothing that forces. |
| god-rivers | 6.5 | A valley's flood, at a season's helplessness and an item; twenty miles of water heard in words; healing; works that leave no sign; a far sense along one river. Told in round 2 that it stood above the band; it kept the flood. |
| god-healing | 5 | Mending and knowing what ails, both at touch and both dearly paid; rot at a cartload a touch, with a growing cost. No reach beyond its hands. |
| god-beasts | 6 | Six powers, none large: gatherings known to ten miles, beasts spoken with and called, a trail it cannot leave, a storm it does not command. Wide but shallow. |
| god-secrets | 3.5 | A shape that costs it each change, twelve confidences that bind it more than they arm it, hiding places found within a market square. The lightest card. |
| god-thresholds | 7 | Seven doors known from anywhere and shut against any ram, a step between them across any distance, a breaking hand, a reading at table, a month's return. Paid for by the guest's law on herself, a day held at each arrival, and one key that carries two powers. Told in round 2 that she stood above the band; the counter on her trade was narrowed. |

## What stays out of line
god-thresholds stays noticeably stronger than the rest, and god-rivers somewhat. Both cards are frozen and neither is edited. It is a fact the world's politics will deal with (DESIGN 3.15): each is strong in a way that shows (a flood; doors that will not open), and each carries a thing that can be taken from it.
