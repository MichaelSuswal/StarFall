# Cast: sim-06

Written on creation Night 3b, 2026-11-24. The few world-level figures placed at creation, each with its nature and history fixed and its place by map id. Everyone else rises from play. A being with a Limit line answers questions, within that limit and no further; the line is copied from `mysteries.md`.

## The Whitmoor Ring
Place: the Whitmoor Ring [f76], Whitmoor [r09].
Nature: one living thing in the shape of about a hundred and fifty standing stones. Older than the last fall. It eats what the star rains at the center, is fed from above on dim nights, and wants the fall. It does not speak, bargain or answer; it only wears on those near it.
Mysteries: L01.

## The rim herders
Place: the rim of Whitmoor [r09]. About three hundred.
Nature: shepherds descended from toll-payers of the Kennoch rule. They have no names for one another and point. Plainsfolk call them the pointers. They are afraid of nothing they can see, trade wool at the heath's edge, and will not go in among the stones by day.
Mysteries: L01.

## The carriers
Place: on the road. In this country in Years 7 to 9 and from Year 43; the order of their round is rows L04-a to L04-i of `event-schedule.md`.
Nature: a line of about forty walkers of a people that carries names from country to country. The deep lore's name for them is the Longwalkers; no one here uses it. Led by whoever is eldest, called the eldest voice. They want to be told the names of things, and nothing else. Neither kind nor cruel.
Limit: The carriers know the names in their lists and what each was the name of, as it was told to them; they do not know what the gods are, how fast the star sinks or when it falls, and they have no list of what is to come; anyone who feeds them and sits the night may ask a few questions between lists; they answer plainly or say they do not know; and they sing nothing and answer nothing under compulsion.
Mysteries: L04.

## The voices that come down
Place: the star. They reach the ground only as the Limit says.
Nature: those who have risen, under this star mostly the very old, who want nothing and are weather. A few younger ones still want something. Maunath, risen from the Maun Stair [f27] in 416 BA, is the youngest. They do not agree with one another and cannot be made to.
Limit: The voices that come down know what was true of their own countries and of rising; they do not know the year of the fall, what a risen god becomes after it, or anything the land has done since each of them rose; they reach only a god whose standing has come to 58 or one asleep inside the Ring on a dim night; they speak in weather and old images of islands that move, never in plain words, at most once in a dim night; and to most askings they give nothing.
Mysteries: L03.

## The Mollen of Aldermoot
Place: the Mollen quarter of Aldermoot [f70]. About three hundred.
Nature: the slow people. Not human; each lives about two hundred and fifty years. They keep their own law of guest and debt and their own books, of which the Ninth Book is the oldest still read. Its keeper is Emmerin, who was a child when the Aldermark was young. They are exact, incurious and fair once a price is agreed.
Limit: The Mollen of Aldermoot know every bargain entered in their books since the Assize's founding, the parties, the terms and the day; they do not know why any party acted, anything never entered, or anything of the star, the gods or the fading; they answer only what is asked exactly, to one who pays the search price in advance, one question to a search; and of a matter not in the books they say it is not in the books.
Mysteries: E01, S09.

## The unnamed fallen stone
Place: the unnamed fallen stone [f77], Bren Hills [r05].
Nature: a piece of what the far fall threw that did not become a god. Warm, blue-grey, heavy. It dreams at whoever sleeps near and gives nothing in reply to asking.
Mysteries: L02.

## Brid of the hill
Place: the Bren Hills [r05], with her father's flock.
Nature: a shepherd's daughter, nine at the arrival. She saw the stone fall and has slept near it more than anyone. She tells her dreams plainly and is believed by other children. She is right about what she saw and wrong about what it is: she says it is a lamb of the star.
Mysteries: L02.

## The house of the Aldermark
Place: Aldermoot [f70].
Nature: the old king Hale Aldren, seventy-one, proud and tired; his daughter Marit, exact and disliked; his nephew Edric, liked and careless. The chancery clerk Peverel Stane, who finds the one number in a roll and believes the roll.
Mysteries: S01, S09, S10.

## Josse Tarn
Place: Port Sennet [f75].
Nature: a traveler from afar, wrecked on Sutter Strand [r28] in 12 BA, about fifty. A boat builder who remembers his hands' work and little else of where he came from. Content, and afraid of being asked.
Mysteries: none set.

## Captain Maud Sennet
Place: Port Sennet [f75]; in the Gulf of Maun [r31] a few times each summer.
Nature: master of a league coaster, of House Sennet, forty. She carries news between the south coast and the delta, and prices it.
Mysteries: none set.
