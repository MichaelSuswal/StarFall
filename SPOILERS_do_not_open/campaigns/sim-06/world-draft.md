# World draft: sim-06 (Night 1)

The first draft, from the star and the blend. Nothing here is placed; placement is Night 3. Vote counts and placements are in the keeper log only.

## Tone
Dusk and lamplight. Warm rooms, full tables and bargaining streets, with a dark outside that is watchful and not safe. Wet reflective stone, morning mist, the hour before rain. Settled peoples are close, talkative and given to counting what is owed. Palette within the house style: umber, ochre, gold and near-black, with cool wet accents.

## Climate and seasons
Climate 4 on the scale: on the harsh side of the middle. Four real seasons everywhere, varying by latitude, height and distance from the sea. Hard winters that close roads, and storms that close the seas for a stretch; a loud flooding thaw; summers long enough to feed a people that prepared; autumns of harvest and reckoning. Waking seasons are each god's own (on the sheets; the player's is early autumn).

## The star, as it bears on the land
- Late: large, low and bright. Fading is visible in about a dozen places, the oldest and most storied and the most-magicked first. The descent is measurable within forty years by marks and patience. No fall inside the campaign.
- The living country reaches 90 days' walk from the center; the Pale lies beyond.
- At the center point stands the hoarder: a ring of standing stones that is one organism. It must be paintable on the surface sheet. Unplaced.
- The carriers walk this world. The thinners are absent: no pale herds.
- The Remainder fell in godless country. Unplaced.
- The star's content: islands that moved. The ascended are mostly the very old; what comes down is weather and old images, with few offers, as often as a late star gives.
- Ceiling: in `S/star.md` only.

## Magic
Level 7, final: common. A worker is known in most valleys and most towns, never in all; no one lives far from one, and it is still not everywhere. Mortal magic stays simple.
Feel and cost, one system: the worker holds still, listens and takes something on; the effect comes; the cost is taken from the worker herself. It is countable and small each time (a night's sleep, warmth, a small dear memory) and large over a life, toward her name. Great workings leave ground where nothing particular grows.
Local understandings, which may be wrong (unplaced): that the cost is a debt owed to something that keeps the books; that a mender carries a share of what she mends; that the cost can never fall on another.

## The two rules
- God-sense: nothing at a distance. At close quarters (the same room, the two sides of a door, a few paces) a god feels another as a weight that settles into knowing over a moment. It tells that the other is a god, and nothing of who it is or what it can do. Never a count. Holding it in or disguising it is not innate; a god may propose it as a power.
- Bodies: one body. Acting at a distance by other means (agents, tokens, dreams) is a power to be proposed and priced.

## How much gods stand out
5: plainly more than any mortal worker, and still troubled, answered and hurt by a gathered people. Mortals meeting a god know they are before something greater than a sorcerer and are not struck dumb; they decide what to do about it.

## The wild
4: familiar animals and many legends with teeth in them. A few things with no name, kept to the edges, the deep water, the far marsh and timber, old ground, and the hardest crossings. The same rules for creatures everywhere.

## Held and open land
5: about half the land is held by godless peoples of some age, and half is open, by area and by count of regions. A few old kingdoms, proud and rooted; much country with room in it.

## Age of the human story
9: many thousands of years, ruins under ruins, with gaps. Peoples have forgotten themselves more than once; much has gone soft into the earth; records are torn and argued over.

## Terrain the world must contain
Several large river systems, far apart; a sheltered sea with a great delta or estuary, salt flats and reed islands; a second braided lowland with marsh; a cold broken coast with small harbors; low hills with ore, clay and springs; floodplain and marsh; forest behind slow lowland water; wooded uplands falling to grazing plains; a belt of gorges, passes, fords and narrows.

Placement wishes, one per god, all unplaced:
- player: a wide delta or estuary where several rivers reach a sheltered sea, salt flats and reed islands, low hills behind with ore and clay, land hard to cross so that routes matter.
- god-sea: cold coast, broken shore, small harbors.
- god-dreams: river country with hills behind, good flooding soil, stone near, a town in a river bend, morning mist.
- god-rivers: springs in the hills, braided channels, marsh, ford and floodplain, a shifting delta.
- god-healing: slow lowland river, marsh at its edges, forest behind; no mountains.
- god-beasts: wooded uplands falling to broad grazing plains, rivers through both, marsh in the low places.
- god-secrets: firm ground on a river bend, water to trade on, marsh at the edges.
- god-thresholds: river country breaking into fords and narrows; passes, gorges, gates in the hills.

## The underground
Underground: none
The buried things of this world are sites on the surface sheet: doors, vaults, cellars, graves, bones, and one stretch of buried dry riverbed within a single region. Nothing joins them below ground.

## Pools

### Fears
Widespread: hunger after a bad year; sickness that travels, by water, grain, beasts or a trade route; something old and patient in the far and deep places that does not care.
Common: the patient neighbor who counts, leverage and debt; a rumor that outruns its truth.
Rare candidates (unplaced): drowning and what comes up from below; a fear that comes in sleep and stays past morning; a pack that has lost its fear; a drought the stories said could not come; a currency that fails; a ruinous fashion; hospitality refused or abused, and what comes in by an unwatched door.

### Scarcity
World scarcities: salt; iron.
Tempers of the peoples, not things: trust, and with it honest measure and safe passage; rest and safe sleep; time.
Local shortages and rare candidates (unplaced): safe harbor; sound timber; draught beasts and unspoiled breeding stock; clean water in some lowlands; the knowing of medicine; old standard weights, prized because honest measure is rare; one particular good that grows or lies in only a few places and is worth fighting over, its kind to be set on Night 3.

### Buried
Widespread: old roads, some sunken; sealed doors.
Common: the leavings of a vanished trading power (weights, tokens, ledgers, vaults and cellars), with the question of why it ended; enormous bones; graves made with great care and forgotten.
Rare candidates (unplaced): bones in a sea cliff the tide uncovers; bones in a long curve under a hill the herds avoid; a sealed spring stopped with a great flat stone; a stretch of buried dry riverbed; a forgotten offering place; gates in hillsides; a grave it is not clear was made for the dead or against them; a place under the water still kept by someone; a thing made by hands that wanted to shelter someone.

### Non-human peoples
2: very few. One kind for certain, a second as a rare candidate, each small in number, each with its own history and reasons.
Candidates (unplaced, chosen on Night 3): a people of the water margin who own no land and give their dead to the water; a slow, long-lived people who remember every deal and keep their own law of guest and debt and their own books.
Colors that may attach to either (unplaced): close to the dark and dreaming; dealing by night and barter; of wood and herd; unreadable in grief.

### Travelers from afar
2: one or two in living memory, each remembered by name. They come lost, wrecked or sick, unsure how their own things work, with a bad memory of the road. Each from a plane entry (Night 3).
What they may have brought (unplaced): a tool nobody here made; a way of counting or keeping accounts; a crop; a ship design; a coin; a cure.

### Mortals worth following
Widespread kind: the keeper of something small who stays (ferryman, midwife, well keeper, gravedigger, gatekeeper, lamplighter).
Common: the one who keeps the books and finds the one number.
Rare candidates (unplaced): a tracker who becomes a judge; a child who dreams true; a ship's captain; a debtor who climbs out; one who opens the wrong door out of pity.

### Discoveries
Widespread: a companion who is no worshipper and will say no (for one god, a rival first).
Rare candidates (unplaced): why the old trading power fell; a market that opens only at certain tides or in certain years; a place where a lost name can be found for a price; one who knows a god by a name it does not remember; a country with no gods in it; a place under the water still kept; what the dead of the land feared; a truth about where the dead go; a place a god is homesick for without having seen it. As belief only: that every river is one spring.

### Undared wishes
All unplaced; none is to be set where its own god is certain to meet it.
- player: a promise with weight. Not a rule of the world; a custom of some people, or a place where bargains are witnessed and remembered.
- god-sea: a people kept alive this time.
- god-dreams: an ending that is a door; what dies returning in some form.
- god-rivers: a place kept for the forgotten.
- god-healing: something mended that stays mended.
- god-beasts: a company held without fear; an answer to the death at its beginning.
- god-secrets: to be missed.
- god-thresholds: its name said in a doorway.
