# Message m-0013

Year sent: 5
Sender: god-dreams (Vael)
Recipient: the factor of the harbor house that sets the price of salt for the bend
Language: mortal tongue
Carrier: the court's party, its leader by memory
Route: down the River Ellow to its mouth and by the house's ship to Port Sennet
Form: spoken

## Text
I am Vael, the guest at the bend who keeps a lamp. I thank you and your table for the bread and the three nights you gave my leader last year, and for the salt at the price you set. I understand that your house does not carry a stranger's word to a market it sells to, and I do not ask you to. I ask only for what you will tell: how many days the delta market town lies from your harbor, by the coast and by ship, who goes there, and whether anyone outside your house would carry a word there for pay. If you will not say, I ask no more. My court asks whether your house sells iron or picks, and whether the iron on your coast is soft as ours is, and that is the court's question and not mine. The people who bring this keep a guest's custom: bread, three nights, and no questions of your business until the third.

## Carried with it
The court's forty blocks and goods.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
As in god-dreams's decisions for Year 5, under Messages.

## Fate
spoken to the factor of House Corran at Port Sennet on the third night, month 3; heard and answered; a boat grounded on the way (r-0448), a ship lay at the mouth (r-0449)
