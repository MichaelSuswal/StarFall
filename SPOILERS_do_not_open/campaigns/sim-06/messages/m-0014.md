# Message m-0014

Year sent: 5
Sender: god-thresholds
Recipient: the king of the river kingdom, at his chancery
Language: mortal tongue
Carrier: the captain of the commander's riders
Route: from the farthest toll house by the western end of the road and on south-west, 117.6 miles from the road's first point, 6 days on horse by the tool (the sender's words: the road north-east and the river)
Form: spoken

## Text
To the one who wrote to whoever holds the ford. A word of the Sarrow went to you in the autumn of last year, by a train master, and no answer came. Since the tenth month no train of your kingdom has crossed the road. The toll was lowered at the ninth month, and the road is open to you as to any guest. I do not charge you with the absence. If it is the toll, say what would bring your trains. If it is another thing, say so. The sill is the same as it was. A claim is heard in person, at Marle Ford, and nothing is owed or refused until it is heard. I have no name to sign. They call me Guest, and Mother of the Door.

## Carried with it
Nothing.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
As in god-thresholds's decisions for Year 5, under Messages.

## Fate
given into the hand of a clerk of the chancery at Aldermoot late in month 1; a known road, no hazard in play, not rolled; a written answer given to the captain (r-0456, success at 50) and carried home on day 20 of month 2
