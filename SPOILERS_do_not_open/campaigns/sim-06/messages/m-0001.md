# m-0001

Sender: god-dreams (as it names itself in the message: Vael). Year 2, month 2 of its year.
Recipient: "whoever speaks for the harbor town west along the coast where the salt is boiled, as the salt carriers name it; a headman, a harbor master, the one who sets the price."
Carrier: a trade party of six of its people, led by the Steward's chief cord-keeper of the trade goods; spoken from memory.
Route: down the River Ellow [f07] to its mouth, then west along the coast, by ship or on foot as the leader judged.

## Text
"I am called Vael. I came down to a town in a river bend, four days up from the river's mouth, in the autumn of last year, and I stayed as a guest, and I light lamps in the houses that ask. Your salt carriers took word of me to you and you asked what I am. I am a guest who stayed, half a woman and half a hind, and I keep a lamp. I ask nothing of you but a fair trade in salt at the price you take at the boil. The people who bring this are guests at your table, and they keep a guest's custom: they ask for bread and three nights and do not ask your business until the third. If you will tell them what you want of us, they will carry it home."

## Carried with it
Twenty bolts of linen, a hundred pots, fifteen jars of flax oil. No flame, no lamp, no token.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
As `gods/god-dreams/decisions-002.md` gives them.

## Fate
Delivered at Port Sennet [f75] in month 3 of Year 2 to the factor of the house that sets the bend's salt price. Carriage roll r-0294 failed: delayed; the party wintered at the harbor and came home in month 8 with salt and a spoken answer.
