# Message m-0011

Year sent: 5
Sender: god-sea (Skerr)
Recipient: the Eldest Midwife, as the Warden-Monarch would name her
Language: mortal tongue
Carrier: the Warden-Monarch, by her own mouth
Route: within the unnamed harbor town
Form: spoken

## Text
You asked two things at the widow's bed. I heard both. I have no whole answer to either, and I will not give a half one. I count the widow among the kept. I took nothing from the house. I take your count of the year's dead as the only honest count and I read the list. I do not say Low Garrow and I do not say not. If you send me a house, I will come once, as before.

## Carried with it
Nothing.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
As in god-sea's decisions for Year 5, under Messages.

## Fate
delivered in the autumn; no hazard within one town, not rolled
