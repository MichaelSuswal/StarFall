# m-0004

Sender: god-dreams (Vael). Year 3, month 2 of its year. In answer to the spoken answer its party brought home in Year 2 (m-0001).
Recipient: "the factor of the house at the harbor town that sets the price of salt for the bend."
Carrier: the court's harbor party of eight, led by the Steward's chief cord-keeper; spoken from memory.
Route: down the River Ellow [f07] to its mouth, then west along the coast on foot.

## Text
As `gods/god-dreams/decisions-003.md` gives it under Messages.

## Carried with it
The court's goods: thirty cut blocks and a pair of dressed samples of the stone of the Ellow Scar [f51], twenty bolts of linen, sixty pots, twelve jars of flax oil. No flame, no lamp, no token.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
As the decisions file gives them.

## Fate
Delivered at Port Sennet [f75] in month 3 of Year 3 to the factor of House Corran, on the third night. Carriage roll r-0336 failed: delayed; a boat grounded under its stone, no ship lay at the mouth, the party went round the shore, wintered at the harbor and came home in month 8 with salt, the House's order for stone and a measuring cord.
