# Message m-0012

Year sent: 5
Sender: god-dreams (Vael)
Recipient: the one in the delta market town in the west who is seated as keeper of the town's weights and says it is a god
Language: mortal tongue
Carrier: first the court's party, its leader by memory; then the god in person to Port Sennet; then the master of a coaster of House Corran, by mouth
Route: down the River Ellow and round the shore to Port Sennet; from there by a coaster of House Corran into the Gulf of Maun
Form: spoken

## Text
I am Vael. I am half a woman and half a hind, and I keep a lamp in a town in a river bend, four days up the river from its mouth. If another word of mine has reached you, it said down where it should have said up, and I ask you to forgive that. I have heard that you say before the market that you are a god. I do not know whether you are. I do not ask you to prove it. If you are one like me, I would like to meet you face to face, at a gate, as a guest meets a house, and I will ask nothing of you but to know you. I tell you where I am so that you may come or not as you choose. I do not send anyone to bring you and I do not ask what you can do. If you are not one like me, I wish you a good market, and I ask you to forgive a stranger's question. The people who bring this are guests at your table, and they keep a guest's custom: bread, three nights, and no questions of your business until the third. If you will send me a word, they will carry it home.

## Carried with it
Nothing of the god's.

## Sender's instructions to the carrier
As in god-dreams's decisions for Year 5, under Messages.
