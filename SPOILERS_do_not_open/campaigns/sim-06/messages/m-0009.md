# Message m-0009

Year sent: 4
Sender: god-thresholds
Recipient: the king of the river kingdom, at his chancery
Language: mortal tongue
Carrier: the master of a mixed train leaving Marle Ford in autumn
Route: north-east by the road and the river to the chancery
Form: spoken

The text, what is carried with it and the sender's instructions are as the sender gave them in `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/sim-06/gods/god-thresholds/decisions-004.md`, under Messages, copied whole below.

## Messages

### To the king of the river kingdom, at his chancery
To: the king of the river kingdom, or the chancery that wrote to whoever holds the ford.
The words, exactly: "To the one who wrote to whoever holds the ford. Your letter was heard at a rite fire of the Sarrow. The Sarrow keep the sill. A claim made by letter is a claim not yet made, and the sill hears a claim only face to face. If the king, or one he trusts to speak for him, will come to Marle Ford, he will be met at the ford as a guest, fed and seated and left in peace until he has eaten and slept, and heard at the hall table. No share is owed by the Sarrow, and none is refused, until the claim has been heard. Bring the old measure you name, and the word of how it was kept. I have no name to sign. They call me Guest, and Mother of the Door."
The carrier: the older toll watcher of Marle Ford, who gives it, folded and tied with a pinch of salt, to the master of the first train leaving the ford for the river kingdom this autumn, with my toll for the train waived for carrying it. If no train leaves before the snow, the watcher keeps it dry and gives it to the first train after the thaw.
The route: the train master's own road to the chancery of the river kingdom, by the road north-east and the river, as he would choose it.
Sent with it: nothing else.
If the recipient cannot be found: the train master gives it to any clerk of the chancery or any officer of the king and asks that it be passed to the one who wrote. If the master will not carry it, the watcher does not press him, and brings it back to me. If the recipient is hostile or asks questions: the carrier says only that the Sarrow keep the sill and that a guest who comes in peace is fed. He does not say where I am, what I can do, or the size of the host, and he does not argue the claim.

## Fate
Delivered to a clerk of the chancery at Aldermoot in month 2 of Year 4. No answer this year.
