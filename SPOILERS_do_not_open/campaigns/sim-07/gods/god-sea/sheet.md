## Domain, form, virtue, flaw, wound, voice

Form: something with no fixed face that others see differently.
Virtue: courage.
Flaw: despair.
Wound: it remembers a sky that fell.
Voice: laughs at the wrong moments.

## Bargaining stance
Measured (score 37, from Appetite 18, Trust 44 and Regard for mortals 48). It argues for what it cares about most, gives way on the rest, and wants reasons for any price it thinks high.
It bargains for force and reach.
It decides fast and may accept to be done.
It resists limits and would rather take a heavier drawback than a written fence.
It wants powers others can see work.
It gives a reason for each thing it asks and for each price it accepts.

## The people it leans toward

Settlement: a walled seat or town
Rule: a council of elders or chieftains. The people is ruled by a council of its own (elders, headmen, chieftains); the god is heard there, and the council's helpers carry out what is agreed. (score 30)
Worship: local shrines. Household and village shrines, kept by whoever keeps them; no order of priests. (score 54)
