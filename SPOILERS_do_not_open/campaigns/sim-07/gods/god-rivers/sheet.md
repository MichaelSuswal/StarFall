## Bargaining stance
Measured (score 57, from Appetite 32, Trust 79 and Regard for mortals 59). It argues for what it cares about most, gives way on the rest, and wants reasons for any price it thinks high.
It argues from reasons and consistency with what was already agreed.
It bargains for duration and for what survives.
It is willing to spend all three rounds to get a thing right.
It wants every limit written exactly.
It wants powers others can see work.
It gives a reason for each thing it asks and for each price it accepts.
