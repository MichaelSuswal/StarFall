## Bargaining stance
Yielding (score 35, from Appetite 1, Trust 96 and Regard for mortals 8). It takes a fair price as offered and argues only where a price cuts against what it is.
It argues from reasons and consistency with what was already agreed.
It bargains for force and reach.
It decides fast and may accept to be done.
It wants powers others can see work.
It gives a reason for each thing it asks and for each price it accepts.

## The people it leans toward

Rule: a council of elders or chieftains. The people is ruled by a council of its own (elders, headmen, chieftains); the god is heard there, and the council's helpers carry out what is agreed. (score 19)
Worship: none. It asks no rites; what the people do about it is theirs. (score 27)
