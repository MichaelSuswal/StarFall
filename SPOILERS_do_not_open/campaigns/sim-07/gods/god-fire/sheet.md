## Bargaining stance
Hard (score 77, from Appetite 77, Trust 86 and Regard for mortals 68). It contests every price it thinks too high, offers its own drawbacks in place of the Keeper's, and accepts a counter only for a reason it respects.
It argues from reasons and consistency with what was already agreed.
It bargains for duration and for what survives.
It decides fast and may accept to be done.
It resists limits and would rather take a heavier drawback than a written fence.
It wants powers others can see work.
It gives a reason for each thing it asks and for each price it accepts.
