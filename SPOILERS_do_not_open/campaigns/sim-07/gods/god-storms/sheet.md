## Bargaining stance
Hard (score 84, from Appetite 77, Trust 79 and Regard for mortals 95). It contests every price it thinks too high, offers its own drawbacks in place of the Keeper's, and accepts a counter only for a reason it respects.
It hunts for wordings, loopholes and the case nobody priced.
It bargains for duration and for what survives.
It decides fast and may accept to be done.
It resists limits and would rather take a heavier drawback than a written fence.
It prefers powers no one can trace to it.
It gives a reason for each thing it asks and for each price it accepts.

Worship: a priesthood. Keepers of rite with standing among the people, offerings brought, no tithe by law. (score 63)
