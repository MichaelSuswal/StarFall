# The Keeper's year

The Keeper's sealed procedure for a play year, beside `engine/night/night-turn.md`, which it follows step for step. It holds what the public engine leaves to the Keeper: how a held moment stops the year, what the DM's day sheet holds, how the pass-one releases and the sealed side release are written, and the standing rulings of play. Read every night turn, with `engine/roles/keeper.md`, `engine/rules/encounters.md` and `sealed-factors.md`. Its rules come from run 06 (items 33, 60, 64, 65, 67, 69, 72, 76, 79, 81, 82, 83, 90, 92, 93, 94, 98, 99, 100 and 101) and Michael's decisions of 2026-11-25, applied in build 7.

Paths: `P/` is `campaigns/<name>/`, `S/` is `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/<name>/`, NNN is the year being resolved. The shorthand is the Keeper's alone: every release written for another agent spells out the full path, as the runbook's templates do.

## 1. Held moments and played scenes

Michael, 2026-11-25 (decision 1; run 06 items 81, 82, 90 and 94).

- **What is held.** A major encounter (`engine/rules/encounters.md`), a scene the player prepared for at the table (the decisions file names it, with its time), and an intent the player stated for later than the sitting (run 06 item 64): each is held at its opening moment, the moment of contact. A held moment with a world figure whose words are the Keeper's is held the same way and played live the same way; it is never voiced in the night.
- **The first held moment stops the player's thread.** In pass two the Keeper finds the first held moment of the player's god's year. The god's year and its people's year are resolved only up to it. Nothing of them past it is written as what happened, released, or told: not in `P/releases/NNN/dm.md`, not in the people's pass-two release, not in any public file. What the player's people's agent wrote past it stays in their sealed files (`S/civs/<people>/`), marked on its first line "Past the first held moment: not yet what happened", and is worked again on the night after the moment is played.
- **The world goes on.** The rest of the world (the NPC gods and their peoples, the godless peoples, the sky, the schedule) is resolved for the whole year, as `night-turn.md` gives. Whatever of it would reach the player's god or its people after the held moment (a trader's boat, a storm, a letter) is resolved as the world's and stands, and is not told: it reaches them on the night that resolves that stretch. Whatever turns on what the player's god or people do after the moment (an NPC people's answer to them) is held with the thread, logged under `Held past the first moment` in the keeper log, and resolved on that night.
- **Only the first is boarded.** The DM's release carries the first held moment as a board (section 2). A later moment in the unresolved stretch is not yet reached and carries no board.
- **A played scene holds.** The night after a held moment is played, the Keeper reads the transcript, the decisions file and what the DM and the player's people's agent feed up, and accepts the result as played. It never unmakes it: no reroll, no overrule of any roll made in it (`sealed-factors.md`, DM rolls in play; the sealed factors entered through the board), no new board laid under it, no told line taken back. Where the DM played outside a parameter the Keeper wrote, the played result still holds, and the departure is logged under `Rulings for review` as a finding, never corrected.
- **Working the answer in.** That night resolves the player's people's stretch from the moment on, with the played answer: the agent's sealed draft of it is set aside, the agent writes the stretch again from its release, and pass two resolves it with the world's events already fixed for those months.
- **A second held moment.** Where that stretch (the held year's tail) reaches another held moment, the player's thread stops at it, as at the first: nothing of the god or its people past it is resolved as what happened, released or told, the morning tells only up to it, and it is boarded as section 2 gives. At most one untold stretch of the player's thread is open at a time: the stretch past the new moment is the open one, and no later year's player thread is resolved until it is told. The world is still resolved whole, each night, as above. The clock, and how the decisions file carries the earlier year, follow `engine/night/night-turn.md` and `engine/rules/morning-story.md`.
- **Dating.** A held moment keeps its own date: its month in the one count, and its year. What was played sits under the year it was played in, as `night-turn.md` and `engine/rules/morning-story.md` give.
- **Superseded.** For every new campaign this replaces sim-06's L-0038 (a prepared event resolved in the night on the handed-off terms) and L-0046 (the told year reworked against the played moments): no prepared event is resolved in the night, and no told stretch is reworked, since nothing past the first held moment is told. Run 06 item 101 falls away.

## 2. The day sheet

Run 06 item 60, with decision 1, and items 65, 72, 83, 92, 93 and 94. The DM's source for small world facts in play. The Keeper writes it in pass two, after the year is resolved and before the releases are checked, as the `## Day sheet` section of `P/releases/NNN/dm.md`, with its sealed part in `S/releases/NNN/dm-sealed.md` (below); on Night 3b both are written for the first played day, in `releases/000/`. It is for the DM only. A fact the DM needs that is not on it is rolled by the DM, its odds stated to the player in (oog) before the roll, and logged for the Keeper, who reads the year's DM rolls in pass two.

What goes on it:
- What the next played day is likely to need, judged from the decisions file, the pending list, the conduct profile and the held moment: the days it may cover, the people the god is likely to meet, the places in reach. It is not the world's whole record.
- The cast the god is likely to meet, each with a name and sex (run 06 item 65), by the naming rule: the Keeper names what is its to name, never what the player left unnamed for play. A name the god has not heard is marked "not yet said", for the DM to use only when the mortal says it or is named in the god's hearing.
- Every figure with its true value where the god can test it (a weight's true figure, a scale's error, the price at market and the price asked of a stranger).
- For the first held moment, and for each scene the player has said it will prepare or play, the board: what the god can see, hear and know at its opening, in the public sheet; and its facts, its parameters and their reasons (decision 1), in the sealed part only, never in the public release (build 7 check, moderate 1). Parameters are what each figure wants, what it will and will not do, what it will and will not accept, its stance and its opening words, and the ranges the outcomes fall in. The reasons are for the DM only: the DM may share them, lie about them in a figure's voice, or not mention them.
- For each world question the board may raise, its odds with their factors, written as the DM will state them: whose attempt or whose question it is, and what success means for that party (run 06 item 72).

The sealed factors enter a board only as `sealed-factors.md` gives: a range that turns on chance for the god or its people is set with the god's steps before the board is written, logged in the keeper log only, and never named in the facts, parameters or reasons. The odds the sheet gives the DM to state leave it out.

Where it comes from: the sealed record. A fact the record does not hold is set before the sheet is written, conservatively, from the deep lore and the deep history, and logged as a new fixed fact (`engine/roles/keeper.md`, the world's fixity). Once written on the sheet it is fixed. A parameter never steers toward or away from a mystery's truth: a figure present carries what it knows and no more, with its rule 16 limit where it answers questions, and the world neither guides nor rescues (mystery standard rules 9 and 15).

What never goes on it: the sealed factor, by name or effect; another god, or a count of gods, beyond what the scene puts before the god's own senses; a mystery's truth beyond what a figure present knows; a phenomenon's count before it is seen; any hint of a change in powers before year 70 (section 8); a sealed term.

**The sealed part.** `S/releases/NNN/dm-sealed.md`, "Written by the Keeper. Read by the DM, the Keeper and the checker." It holds, for every held moment and prepared scene on the sheet, its facts, its parameters, its world-question odds and its reasons, in the template below; and anything else the sheet needs that may not stand in a public file (a sealed term, a truth a figure holds that the god has not witnessed, what an act in a sealed note shows the god, section 5). It is written whenever the sheet has a held moment or prepared scene, or such a need, and otherwise not. The public sheet points to it at each board ("Facts, parameters and reasons: in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/<name>/releases/NNN/dm-sealed.md"). No civ or NPC god agent is given it.

The template:

```
## Day sheet
For the DM only. Facts of the world for the next played day, from the Keeper.
A fact not here is rolled by you: state its odds to the player in (oog) before
the roll, say whose attempt or question it is and what success means for them,
and log it for the Keeper.

### The days
<the month in the one count and its season; the days the played day may
cover; market days, sittings, feasts; the weather of those days; the light>

### Figures
<weights, measures, prices, wages, tolls, in the people's own units, each with
its true value where the god can test it>

### The cast
- <name, or "not yet said: <name>", or office> (<sex>): <office; manner, one
  line; what they want this season; what they know that the god may ask; what
  they will not say>

### Places in reach
<from tools/map.py describe; what is there on those days>

### Held moment: <a plain title>
(and one section like it for each scene the player has said it will prepare)
Board: <where; its date, month and hour; who is present; what is in view, from
tools/map.py describe>
Facts, parameters and reasons: in
SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/<name>/releases/NNN/dm-sealed.md
```

The sealed part, `S/releases/NNN/dm-sealed.md`:

```

### Held moment: <the same title as on the public sheet>

- <figure>: wants <...>; will <...>; will not <...>; accepts <...>; will not
  accept <...>; stance <...>; opening words: "<...>"
```

## 3. Pass-one releases

### 3.1 The player's people

`P/releases/NNN/civ-<people>-pass1.md`, public. Every NPC people's pass-one release (`S/releases/NNN/civ-<people>-pass1.md`) takes the same form, sealed, without the held-moment line.

- **Months and counts (run 06 item 98).** Every phenomenon the people would see is given by their own word for it, with the months it was seen and how many times in all. The months come from `tools/phenomena.py SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/<name> NNN`, which prints them after the count when `S/state/phenomena.json` carries a `months` field for the year (months counted from the waking month). Where the year has none, each occurrence's month is rolled in pass one, one d12 each in the one count, before the release is written, logged, and written into the file's `months` for that year, so the tool prints them from then on and checks that they number the count. The count is the true figure from `tools/phenomena.py`, and is given only where the people would see every occurrence; where they would see only some, the release gives the months of those seen and no count (mystery standard rule 15). All the world counts one set of months (Michael, 2026-11-25), so the same months go to every people that would see them.
- **The held moment.** The release names the first held moment already known (a held moment carried from the morning, or a scene the decisions file prepares), by its month and day, and tells the agent to write nothing of its people past it in their public files; anything past it goes in their sealed files, marked as in section 1. A major encounter the agent meets in unplayed time is stopped at contact the same way.
- **What goes where (decision 7).** The people's public files hold only what the god could witness or be told. What they did, said and knew out of its sight and hearing goes in their sealed files, `SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/<name>/civs/<people>/`.

```
# Pass one: <people>, Year NNN

## The year
One count of months for the whole world. Month 1 is <the waking month>, the
same month in every country.
Write your people's year up to <month M, day D: the first held moment> and
no further in your public files. Anything past it goes in your sealed files,
under the line "Past the first held moment: not yet what happened".
(Or: "No moment is held yet. Stop any thread at a major encounter, at the
moment of contact.")

## Your god's decisions
<the full path of the decisions file; the conduct profile and standing words
by full path>

## What your people meet, by month
- Month N: <weather; a world event as they would meet it; who came; what was
  heard>

## Seen in the sky and the land
- <their word for it>: seen in months <a, b, c>; <n> times in all.

## When your god is away
<section 4, in the words the template there gives>

## What goes where
Your public files hold only what your god could witness or be told. What your
people did and knew out of its sight and hearing goes in
SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/<name>/civs/<people>/. Asking or telling
anything from your sealed side release is itself sealed: your public files
never say that such a thing was asked, told or known.

## Your report
<the report's full path, as night-turn.md names it>
```

### 3.2 The decide release

The same frame, in the same words, for all seven but the label and what is the god's own. It prompts nothing about growth (section 8) and nothing about prices, beyond a cost the Keeper puts back to the god for a gift it gave a mortal (section 6).

```
<the premise, runbook template 7.0>

year here is twelve months.

<the full path of last year's release for this god; at Year 1, its landing

<the section "What the people tell their god", copied whole from its civ

<each message delivered this year, by its file's full path, or "None.">
```

It ends with the release lines of runbook section 7.

### 3.3 The sealed side release

The player's people have three sealed things, each its own: the Keeper's world side of their past and customs, `S/civs/<people>-sealed.md`, written on Night 3a and given to no agent; their agent's sealed folder, `S/civs/<people>/`, for what the god did not witness (decision 7); and this yearly side release. The side release is the Keeper's, drawn from the sealed record as a whole, the world-side file among it, and carries only what the people's office holders and other mortals know; nothing of the world-side file goes in it that they do not (build 7 check, minor 8).

`S/releases/NNN/civ-<people>-sealed.md`, for the player's people's agent only, never the DM (run 06 item 76). What the people's office holders and other mortals know that the god does not, so the agent can play them.

The asking and the telling are themselves sealed content (run 06 item 100; the sim-06 Year 3 checker's ruling). The public files never say that a sealed question was asked, that a sealed thing was told, who told it, or that a sealed note exists. They say only what the god could witness or be told, and what the people then did in its sight or hearing. A meeting the god did not attend is told in public, if at all, only by what came out of it in the open.

```
# Sealed side release: <people>, Year NNN
Read by your people's agent only. Never by the DM.

## What they know that your god does not
- <office or name>: <what they know; where a search for it stops>

## How it is kept
Asking about anything in this file, and telling it, are sealed themselves.
Write them only in SPOILERS_do_not_open/campaigns/<name>/civs/<people>/. Your
public files never say that such a thing was asked, told or known, or that
this file exists.

## This file is not a prompt
Nothing here is a reason for anyone to act. A mortal acts on what it knows,
as it would have without this file.
```

## 4. When the god is away

Michael, 2026-11-25 (decisions 4 and 5), with run 06 item 69. The same for the player's god and every NPC god, read by the Keeper and given to every civ agent in its pass-one release.

- **Acting for the god.** A town acts on its god's behalf only where the god expressed that intention, or plainly took it for granted (its decisions, its standing words, its conduct profile, the orders its helpers carry). With no way to reach the god, the town has no answer from it: that is the price of not giving explicit permission. The civ agent may decide that someone in the town took it upon themselves to make the call; it is then that mortal's act, known as such, and never the god's.
- **No reply.** A question put to an absent god meets silence and stays pending, unless the god noted otherwise. A simple ask that the conduct profile's stated temper answers (refuse a bribe, name its fee) is answered by it, logged as conduct and not as a decision (item 69). A promise the god gave to answer later is such a note: the asking is held, a held moment, and never met with silence. People know only what happened: they were not answered. No agent writes what the god meant by its silence; "any other assumption is just an assumption" (Michael).

The template line for every civ pass-one release:

```
## When your god is away
Your people act in your god's name only where it said so, or plainly took it
for granted. Otherwise they have no answer from it, and may not act as if they
had one; someone among them may take it on themselves, and that is their act,
not the god's. A question put to your god while it is away meets silence and
stays pending, unless its standing words or conduct profile answer it. Your
people know only that they were not answered.
```

## 5. Acts in a sealed note

Michael, 2026-11-25 (decision 8). An act the god says it does is done, wherever it is said, a line in `S/player/unsaid.md` included: the Keeper resolves it in the night like any decision, needing no confirmation. What it shows the god goes to the DM in the sealed part of the day sheet (section 2), never in a public file. An ordinary habit the god states (checking a pocket) is conduct, carried out and never priced. Wants and fears stay feelings, applied only under the runbook's confirmation rule. This replaces the sim-06 Year 3 ruling that an act given only in the sealed record is not carried out.

## 6. Powers given to mortals

Michael, 2026-11-25 (decision 9; run 06 item 33). Giving a power to a mortal costs the god something. The DM does not price it at the table: it records the player's wish. The Keeper prices it that night on the god, by the measure of runbook 3a.1 (what the gift lets the mortal do in a year, and for how long), less for one that dies with the person, and puts it back to the player as a cost, in plain words, in the DM release. The gift is given when the player says yes; the DM enters it on the card as a dated entry with its "Ledger: pending" mark, and the Keeper writes the ledger id beside it on the next night, the one mechanical write it makes on the card in play (run 06 item 67). An NPC god's gift is priced the same way in pass two and put back to it in its next release, as a cost it takes or declines in its next decisions. The mortal's own cost of working it is the mortal's, and is not the price.

## 7. Clues and searches

Michael, 2026-11-25 (decision 3; `mystery-standard.md` rule 17). Before any search is resolved, the Keeper logs which the thing is:
- **Placed by the Keeper** (creation, the deep history, the mysteries, the schedule, the cast): it is there. A search that reaches its place by a means that can find it finds it, as its branch says, and is not rolled. A search that did not reach the place does not find it.
- **Came about in the game**: a roll with modifiers, weighed by the evidence. The odds start from how likely the thing is where the search looks, given what play produced; each piece of evidence moves them by how much more likely it is if the thing is there than if not. Start and modifiers are written before the roll, and the sealed factors follow after them (`sealed-factors.md`).

This replaces the sim-06 Year 3 ruling that rolled a search whose mystery branch says the thing is found.

## 8. Growth and hidden powers

Michael, 2026-11-25 (decision 2; run 06 items 79 and 99).

- **Slow, and earned.** Powers feel limited to what was given. A power or knack progresses slowly, only by being done a lot and by pushing at new boundaries; a god a little faster than a mortal. The Keeper judges each year's earning by that rule, the same for every god, with its reasons logged. Nothing prevents an attempt.
- **No prompt.** No release, decide release or round asks an NPC god to grow or to state an attempt. The run 06 question (item 99) is settled: growth arises unprompted or not at all.
- **No hint before year 70.** Until at least year 70, no hint of any kind about a change in powers reaches any god, the player or any NPC: no release line, no DM line, no felt change, no day sheet line, no prompt, and no card entry. The growth file records what the year earned; what grows shows only in use, as what happened. Its card entry is written no earlier than year 70 (build 7 check, minor 9).
- **Hidden powers.** A hidden power surfaces when its condition is met, and its condition was set on Night 2 to be rarely met in the early years (runbook 2.7). The Keeper never arranges the condition and never steers away from it. A surfacing is discovery, not growth: it is told as what the god meets.
- **Knack use** is counted as runbook template 7.10 gives: a line when the skill itself was exercised, in whole or in part; being near it is not use.
- **The knack figures** (`engine/rules/powers.md` section 4 leaves them here; run 06 item 79), the same for every god. Set by Agent K in build 7 and awaiting Michael's ruling; applied as written until he rules. A year counts as a year of use when a related skill was exercised at least once in a scene or in unplayed time. A year of use counts as a year that pushed when the skill was taken past anything it had done before (a new kind of work, a harder case, a farther reach), named in its `Use` line. A god's knack shades one step further after ten years of use, at least three of them years that pushed; a knack or power a god gave a mortal, after twelve years of use, at least four of them years that pushed (gods a little faster than mortals). Gaps do not reset the count. Each step is written `Grown` in the growth file with the years it rests on.
- **Standing, each year** (written in from sim-06's Year 1 ruling, run 06 item 79): the same rule for every god. No move where only a few hold the god's shape in mind; one point where most of its people have held its shape in mind through the year, as whatever they take it for; two where a large part hold it as the one they awaited; three where a people several times the usual size has held it so, and served it, all year. Each move a dated line in `S/state/standing.md` with its reason.
