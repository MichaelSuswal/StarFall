# NPC God Creation Rubric (draft 1)

> SPOILER WARNING: Keeper material. Players should not read this file.

All rolls are 1-100 unless stated. The agent for the god sees its rolls, then answers the world survey and creation questions from inside the character. Nothing from the archetype table is ever named in play.

## 1. The ten leans
Each is a lean between two ends. 1 = fully the first, 100 = fully the second.
1. Mind: intelligent (reasons it through) / clever (finds the trick)
2. Body: strong (overwhelming force) / enduring (outlasts anything)
3. Presence: revealed (wants to be seen) / hidden (works unseen)
4. Temper: patient (plays for centuries) / impulsive (acts now)
5. Regard for mortals: shepherd (serves its people) / sovereign (its people serve it)
6. Order: law (builds rules) / freedom (breaks or ignores them)
7. Making: builder (creates and keeps) / breaker (unmakes and clears)
8. Company: solitary / gathering. Roll +25 toward gathering, capped at 100.
9. Appetite: content / hungry
10. Trust: open / wary

## 2. Mythic archetype seed (roll d30, secret inspiration, never named)
Drawn from world mythologies. Used for flavor of being, not copied.
1 The devouring mother who is also the cradle (Tiamat, Kali)
2 The smith maimed for his craft (Hephaestus, Wayland, Ilmarinen)
3 The trickster who steals fire or light and pays for it (Prometheus, Raven, Maui)
4 The psychopomp, ferryman of the dead (Anubis, Charon, Xolotl)
5 The sleeping king under the hill who will wake (Arthur, Barbarossa, Kronos in Elysium)
6 The spinner of fates who cannot cut her own thread (Norns, Moirai, Spider Grandmother)
7 The storm-rider whose anger feeds the fields (Thor, Perun, Tlaloc, Indra)
8 The serpent that holds the world together by biting its tail (Jormungandr, Ouroboros, Rainbow Serpent)
9 The god who dies each year and returns (Osiris, Dumuzi, Baldr, Persephone)
10 The sun that must be fed or it will not rise (Huitzilopochtli, Amaterasu in the cave)
11 The mountain that was a giant (Ymir, Pangu, Atlas)
12 The jealous sibling who kills and is cursed (Set, Cain, Romulus)
13 The hearth-keeper who never leaves and holds everything together (Hestia, Brigid)
14 The hunter who becomes the hunted (Actaeon, Orion, Herne)
15 The drowned god of the deep who keeps what sinks (Ran, Manannan, Sedna)
16 The mad poet whose words come true (Odin on the tree, Bragi, Vainamoinen)
17 The judge who weighs hearts and cannot be bribed (Ma'at, Yama, Rhadamanthus)
18 The lord of flies and rot who is also medicine (Beelzebub, Nergal, Pazuzu)
19 The twin who is the shadow of the other (Castor/Pollux, Hunahpu/Xbalanque, Quetzalcoatl/Tezcatlipoca)
20 The one who built the labyrinth and was locked in it (Daedalus, Enki in the abzu)
21 The thief of names who grows by eating what others call themselves (Isis learning Ra's name, djinn lore)
22 The herd-god, counting beasts, drunk, generous, dangerous (Pan, Dionysus, Cernunnos)
23 The ice-giant who remembers the world before (Ymir's kin, Thrym, Chione)
24 The war-queen who washes the armor of those about to die (Morrigan, Valkyrie, Durga)
25 The child-god, ancient but small, cruel without malice (Krishna as infant, Eros, Horus the child)
26 The bound god whose venom drips and shakes the earth (Loki bound, Prometheus bound, Fenrir)
27 The gate-keeper with two faces, one for each direction (Janus, Heimdall, Legba)
28 The lightning-eyed scholar who trades an eye for knowing (Odin, Thoth, Vidar)
29 The sea-foam born, beauty as a weapon (Aphrodite, Oshun, Freyja)
30 The wandering stranger who tests hospitality and punishes its absence (Zeus Xenios, Odin Grimnir, the Elohim at Mamre)

## 3. Domain (roll d20; reroll duplicates within a campaign)
1 fire, 2 harvest, 3 war, 4 secrets, 5 roads and borders, 6 the dead, 7 the sea, 8 storms and sky, 9 stone and mountains, 10 craft and making, 11 beasts and the hunt, 12 healing and rot, 13 law and oaths, 14 trade and counting, 15 song and memory, 16 dreams and night, 17 rivers and springs, 18 ice and winter, 19 thresholds and doors, 20 fate and chance

## 4. Form (roll d12)
1 human-seeming, uncannily perfect; 2 human-seeming, visibly wrong in one way (too tall, no shadow, wrong joints); 3 a great beast; 4 a small beast; 5 a shape of weather or element; 6 a thing of many parts (a swarm, a flock, a court of voices); 7 a giant; 8 a child; 9 an old one; 10 a half-thing (half beast, half person); 11 shifting between two forms; 12 something with no fixed face that others see differently

## 5. Virtue and flaw that pull against each other (roll each, d10)
Virtue: 1 mercy, 2 patience, 3 generosity, 4 courage, 5 honesty, 6 loyalty, 7 curiosity, 8 restraint, 9 justice, 10 humor
Flaw: 1 pride, 2 wrath, 3 envy, 4 greed, 5 fear, 6 despair, 7 lust for being worshipped, 8 cruelty from indifference, 9 obsession, 10 cowardice dressed as caution

## 6. The wound (roll d12, from the foggy past; the god feels it but cannot explain it)
1 it was betrayed by one it made; 2 it failed to save a people once before; 3 it was once worshipped and then forgotten; 4 it killed a sibling; 5 it was bound for an age; 6 it lost its name; 7 it ate something it should not have; 8 it was the servant of a greater thing and fled; 9 it loved a mortal who died; 10 it broke an oath and the world broke with it; 11 it was born of a death; 12 it remembers a sky that fell

## 7. Voice (roll d10)
1 speaks in questions; 2 speaks in very few words; 3 speaks in proverbs and old sayings; 4 courtly, formal, elaborate; 5 blunt as a hammer; 6 warm and mothering, with iron under it; 7 laughs at the wrong moments; 8 never says a thing directly, always sideways; 9 speaks of itself in the third person or plural; 10 speaks like a merchant, everything a bargain

## 8. Traits
After seeing all rolls, the god's agent names two traits for itself (what it will be known for). The Keeper assigns one more, known to the god from arrival.

## 9. Rules
- No two gods in one campaign share an archetype seed or a domain.
- The agent must reconcile contradictions among its rolls rather than ignoring them; a contradiction is where the character lives.
- Nothing from the archetype table is ever named or quoted in play.
