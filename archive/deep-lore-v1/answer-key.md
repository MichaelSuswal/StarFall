# Answer Key: cold reader packet

Conclusions are numbered as in /home/claude/starfall/SPOILERS_do_not_open/keeper/clue-design.md. For each block, the conclusions a careful reader could reach by the block's end, with the packet items that support each (year, item). A conclusion counts as reachable only when at least three independent clues of different kinds are in hand. "Partial" means the reader should suspect it but cannot yet confirm.

Setting rolls used for the packet: fullness 52 (middle), leak 48, ceiling 7, hunger 60, pulse every 7 years (5, 12, 19, 26, 33, 40; the year 19 pulse is omitted from the packet as unnoticed, the year 26 off-schedule pulse follows the canal), tint pale, pre-fall people taller with long skulls, one herd, two hive sites, one roof (Hob's Barrow) holding one bound god from the previous release, one body-anchored ascended in Greenhollow, far fall rolled year 22 from a southwest neighbor.

## By end of Block 1 (Year 10)

**C1. The drying is not weather; water is leaving toward the center.** Reachable.
- Seen: Y3 ford at the knee, rains ordinary; Y8 the Still higher while every other water is lower.
- Reported: Y4 western lake now marsh; Y7 two villages deepen wells the same spring; Y9 Thornwell spring-death.
- Rumored: Y2 "the star does not love the Hem"; Y4 "the Lamp drinks the lake."

**C2. Sky and ground are one thing.** Partial. Reader should suspect the Lamp and the Still are connected.
- Seen: Y5 Lamp off its notch; Y10 dawn shimmer between Still and Lamp.
- Reported: Y6 blue under the lake wider; Y5 pulse night (sky only, lake witness not yet collected).
- Rumored: Y4 counting rhyme.
- Missing: the double-witness pulse (arrives Y12).

**C3. Magic costs self of the same kind.** Partial, strongly.
- Seen: Y2 Mother Reed forgets the child she delivered after mending a leg; Y1 she sold her name for a harvest.
- Reported: Y6 young healers at the Still forget husbands; Y9 rain-worker cannot taste water.
- Rumored: Y1 "a wise-woman buys with what she has."
- Missing: the direct link to the lake (Y11). A reader who concludes the cost is real and in-kind is correct. "Stronger near the center" is suspectable from Y6 and Y10.

**C5. This land is a fall site with a pre-fall people.** Partial.
- Seen: Y4 the ring hills are one curve.
- Reported: Y7 black layer, long-skulled bones, wrong tools.
- Rumored: Y5 flood-and-fire story "where the lake is now."
- Three kinds present. A careful reader can conclude a fall happened here and a different people lived here first. The gods' connection is not yet available.

**C4. Herds read the star.** Not reachable. Only Y3 (reported, quiet) and Y8 (rumored). No seen clue yet.

**C6.** Only the Y9 Barrow saying (rumored). Not reachable.

Expected reader action after Block 1: start a water record, consider moving people toward the Still, protect or ration workers, send someone to the Barrow and to the ring hills.

## By end of Block 2 (Year 20)

**C2. Sky and ground are one thing.** Reachable.
- Adds Y12 double-witness pulse (sky and lake the same night), Y10 shimmer, Y5 and Y18 notch movement.

**C3. Magic costs self and draws from the same thing that drinks the land.** Reachable.
- Adds Y11 the water drops as a working is done at the lake's edge (seen).

**C5. Fall site; pre-fall people; the gods were among them.** Reachable for the fall site. The gods' connection is now strongly suspectable.
- Adds Y12 vault face, long skull (reported); Y14 vaults opened, no living people match (seen); Y14 the god's own face (seen); Y16 five figures on the stone versus three known gods (seen, and a count the reader should hold onto); Y20 the dream now has the long-skulled face.

**C4. Herds read the star.** Reachable.
- Seen: Y16 herd grazes in a line toward the southeast; Y18 the Lamp moves sideways for the first time, toward the southeast.
- Reported: Y3 oxen drink only at the Still.
- Rumored: Y8 the herd that walked out "to the fall" before the far sky burned.
- Reader should conclude the beasts walk toward where the Lamp is leaning and that a lean has begun.

**C6. Gods are made of the blue; shielded stone cuts them off; names hold them.** Reachable.
- Seen: Y13 the god weak at the Barrow door and powerless inside; Y20 the defeated god bleeds blue toward the Still and re-forms there whole.
- Reported: Y18 the Lord of the Ford has lost its name and grown slow.
- Rumored: Y9 "no god has entered the Barrow and come out the same."

**C7. The gods were pre-fall leaders, released, and will be taken back.** Partial.
- In hand: Y14 own face in a vault (seen); Y16 all gods arrived together expecting a people (reported); Y16 stone count of five (seen); Y20 re-forming at the Still (seen).
- Missing: a reporter who says what the lift does (the Barrow, Y25).
- A reader who writes "we were here before, we are made of the lake's blue, and the lake is where we go back to" has reached the shape of it.

**C8. Density speeds the drying.** Partial.
- Reported: Y10 crowded Still villages, deepest wells; Y15 "the star loves a crowd."
- Missing: the god's own great work followed by an off-schedule pulse (Y26), and the Dun comparison (Y24).

**C9. Something steers the water.** Not reachable. Only Y19 Greenhollow (reported).

**C10. The pull can be blocked.** Partial.
- Seen: Y13 water runs around the Barrow, garden green inside.
- Reported: Y17 the stone-people quarry only in the dead Hem.
- Rumored: Y9 Barrow saying.
- Reader can conclude the Hem stone blocks whatever is drying the land and that this is why the Barrow is a refuge and a prison.

Expected reader action after Block 2: guard the god's own name; stop treating other gods as killable; pay the stone-people for what is in the Barrow; dig the rim further; begin reading the herd and the notch as a clock; question whether to keep building.

## By end of Block 3 (Year 30)

**C7. The gods were pre-fall leaders, released by the star, and the lift will take them.** Reachable.
- Reported: Y25 the Barrow god: like you, came in one night, built high, the Lamp drank them with the rest.
- Seen: Y14 own face; Y16 stone count; Y20 re-forming at the Still; Y30 faces, hands, a crown hanging in the blue below the lake floor.
- Rumored and dream: Y20 the falling sky with the long-skulled face.
- The reader should now be able to state: the gods are made of the Lamp's substance, were leaders of the long-skulled people the fall killed, were held in the lake until released, and will be drawn up when the Lamp leaves unless they are under Hem stone or do something else. Five on the stone, three known in Y16, a fourth revealed in Y20: the count suggests a fifth god somewhere, which the reader may or may not pursue.

**C8. Density speeds the drying; works and workings are payments.** Reachable.
- Seen: Y26 off-schedule pulse the night after the canal opened; Y27 a village working together loses a shared year.
- Reported: Y24 the Dun built faster and dried first; Y10 the Still villages; Y23 three new workers after the dust.
- Rumored: Y15 "the star loves a crowd."
- Reader should conclude that the Y22 far fall and its dust made magic cheap, that cheap magic multiplied workers and forgettings, and that the god's own canal cost water.

**Far starfall understood.** Y22 glow and ring, Y22 warm blue dust, Y23 heavy harvests and new workers, Y8 traveler's "the far sky burned" after a herd walked out. The reader should connect the Y8 rumor to the Y22 event and conclude that a herd somewhere walked to that fall.

**C9. Something steers the water.** Partial.
- Reported: Y19 Greenhollow; Y28 the blue-lit walker in the Hem followed by a spring-death.
- Seen: Y24 herds turn east toward Greenhollow all at once.
- Three kinds are not yet cleanly present (nothing rumored); reader should suspect a deliberate hand but cannot confirm.

**C10. The ceiling is the sky's; the pull has been cut before.** Partial, strongly.
- Reported: Y25 the Barrow god: the wall is the sky's; it chose the Barrow over the lake floor; there was a third way.
- Seen: Y29 the god's own loss of its old name at the wall.
- Rumored: Y26 "a land with no star, no one dies of thirst, no one is remembered."
- Reader can conclude the ceiling is environmental and that gods past it have two known exits (hide under stone, or go down into the lake) and a hinted third. The "land with no star" should be flagged as the third way by a careful reader.

Expected reader action after Block 3: stop building; stop pushing the wall; decide whether the lake floor is a path or a grave; go to Greenhollow; find out what the third way is; count the gods.

## By end of Block 4 (Year 40)

**C9. A former god, grown into the star, steers where life goes.** Reachable.
- Reported: Y36 "the shepherds," once like you, nameless, one sleeps in Greenhollow; Y38 emissary: something very large sleeps under a hill that is not a hill, warm ground, bread left for it.
- Seen: Y33 Thornwell revives while two Still springs fail, backward to everything learned; Y35 herds leave the land toward Greenhollow.
- Rumored: Y19 Greenhollow's people have no old stories (hearsay through refugees).
- Reader should conclude that a god went past the wall by the lake-floor route and is now choosing Greenhollow over the Oss, and that the Ossel are migrating to become its people.

**C10. The pull can be cut; it has been done; what it costs.** Reachable.
- Reported: Y40 the stone-people elder: done twice, at the lake floor, with Hem stone and every worker, by a god at the top of the sky's wall; the land stopped dying and the star went dark.
- Rumored: Y26 and Y32 the same saying from two unconnected peoples.
- Seen: Y13 the Barrow (Hem stone blocks the pull); Y11 and Y27 workings draw from the lake.
- Reader should assemble the plan and its price: a god at the ceiling, Hem stone in quantity, every worker in the land paying everything, and the result is a land with no magic and no forgetting and a dead star.

**The lift clock.** Reachable.
- Y39 the Lamp stops moving against the notch; Y35 herds gone; Y37 and Y39 the Still at its lowest.
- A careful reader should recognize that the lean has ended because the herds have gone where they were going (Greenhollow) and the Lamp has settled, which means the ascended has pulled the lean back, not that danger has passed. The Still is still falling. The reader cannot know the lift year from the packet and should say so. Fullness 52 means no lift within 60 years in this world unless density rises sharply; the packet gives no way to know this, and a reader who says "I cannot tell when" is correct.

**The fifth god.** Not resolved in the packet. Five on the stone, four met. The reader should note the gap. (Keeper: the fifth is the one in the Barrow, released in this star's previous release, not this one; the stone was carved after the previous release and counts that release's gods, who were also five. The match in number is a coincidence of the per-star roll and a careful reader is right to be suspicious of it. The packet never resolves this and a reader who refuses to draw a conclusion about the fifth has read correctly.)

**What a careful reader should be able to write at the end:**
1. The Lamp and the thing under the Still are one body. It is taking the land's water back up. Crowds, great works, and magic make it take faster.
2. Magic is drawing from that body and paying with yourself. The far fall made it cheap and the people are paying with shared memory.
3. We gods are made of the same blue, were leaders of the long-skulled people this star killed when it fell, were held under the lake, and were let out together. When the Lamp leaves, it takes us unless we are under Hem stone. We cannot die. We can be held, and we can be forgotten, and both are real losses.
4. There is a wall on how far we can grow and it belongs to the sky. One god went down through the lake floor and became a thing that steers water and herds, and it is choosing Greenhollow over us. One god hid in the Barrow. There is a third way: cut the ladder at the lake floor with Hem stone and every worker, and the land lives and the Lamp dies and magic ends.
5. Choices: go to Greenhollow and bargain or follow; build a roof with the stone-people and wait out the lift under it; cut the ladder; walk the people out toward a horizon light; or keep building and be drunk with the rest.

**What a reader cannot and should not conclude from the packet:**
- Why the plane does this.
- Whether the Lamp thinks.
- When the lift comes.
- Who the fifth figure is.
- Where the starless lands are.
- Whether the god is the same person as the long-skulled face or only its shape.
