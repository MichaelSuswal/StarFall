# StarFall

Version 1.0. Designed by Michael Suswal.

A single-player god game played in a chat with Claude, at most one year of the world per real day. You fall into a world that does not know you yet, land in country suited to you, and lead whoever you find there. Other gods have fallen too. You will never be told how many.

## How to start

You need a GitHub account, Claude with code sessions (Claude on the web or the desktop app, with GitHub connected), and an OpenAI API key for the paintings (the world map is painted, so creation needs it).

1. Download `starfall_v1_0.md` from this repository.
2. Open a fresh chat with Claude and attach that file. Say hello.
3. Claude walks you through the rest: your own private copy of this repository (the **Use this template** button), your OpenAI key saved as a GitHub secret, the nightly task that turns the world, and then Day 1.

To come back in a new chat, attach the same file and say "resume" with your repository's name.

## Creation

Creation takes three days, played like a tabletop. Speak in character in quotes; step out with (oog) when you want to negotiate. Each night the world is built a little further from what you said.

- Day 1 opens with one (oog) question: mirror mode or standard. In mirror mode a few of your god's smaller gifts are drawn from you as a person (never anything sensitive); in standard mode, nothing is. Then the world survey: questions about the world you hope to land in. Answer what speaks to you, skip the rest, and reject any premise you like. Your answers are one voice among the gods falling with you.
- Day 2, your god. Describe it freely: form, past, what it wants, what it can do. The DM asks, prices and rules. Every power comes with a drawback.
- Day 3, your people. Who they are, how many, their customs, their skills. Ordinary culture is free; anything that gives a real edge is priced.
- Day 4, 6:30am Pacific. The first morning story. Year 1 begins.

## A day of play

At 6:30am Pacific the morning story arrives: where you are, the state and mood of your people, a painting of the day's highlight, and a summary with hooks you can ask about. Then the day is yours. Play scenes, make god-scale decisions, ask about anything. Nothing resolves until you commit, and costs and odds are stated first. Ask for the map any time.

## The clock

- 1:30am Pacific: finish typing before this. The chat saves, and the world turns overnight.
- 6:30am Pacific: the morning story posts.
- A day you do not play, the world waits.
- When the morning stops at a moment held for you to play, the clock waits: you play the moment the next day. A day of play never covers more than one year.

## Costs

The game runs on your Claude plan. Each night runs a handful of agents and uses a real share of your usage. Each painting costs money on your OpenAI account; you choose at setup whether to paint everything or only the maps.

## The one rule

Never open `SPOILERS_do_not_open/`. It holds the other gods, the hidden history and every mystery's answer. The game runs on the honor system, and it is only a game if you do not look.
